I need help

Whichever of these sounds like you, you are welcome here. You do not need to be sure of anything.

I’m in a group and I have doubts

Having doubts is not disloyalty, and it does not mean something is wrong with you. You do not have to decide anything today. You can ask questions and take your time.

I have left a group

Leaving can bring relief, grief, anger and confusion, sometimes in the same hour. These reactions are common. We can listen, help you make sense of what happened and point you to people who understand.

Someone I love is involved

It is hard to watch someone you love change. Staying in contact and staying calm usually matters more than winning an argument. Write to us and we can talk through your situation.

I work with people

Teachers, clergy, counsellors, journalists and officials can ask us for information and for referral. We also run a Schools’ Programme.

What happens when you write

1

You write to us

A few lines are enough. You do not need to name the group.

2

We listen and reply

We help clarify whether the concern is about cultism or about something else.

3

We point you on

Where another kind of help fits, we refer you to people who understand.

Write to info@dialogueireland.ie

If someone is in immediate danger, call 112 or 999. Some people come back to us after months or even two years. That is fine.
