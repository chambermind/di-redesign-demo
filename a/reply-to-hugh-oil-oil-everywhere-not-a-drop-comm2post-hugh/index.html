For those who make investments they do so on qualified bone fide investment information taking in consideration risk, then the sign up to the company and its bylaws which govern investment. To all investors a company has a Corporate social responsibility (CSR), also known as corporate responsibility, corporate citizenship, responsible business, sustainable responsible business (SRB), or corporate social performance.

This is a form of corporate self-regulation integrated into a business model. This would function as a built-in, self-regulating mechanism whereby business would monitor and ensure its adherence to law, ethical standards, and international norms. Business would embrace responsibility for the impact of their activities on the environment, consumers, employees, communities, stakeholders and all other members of the public sphere. What clearly has happened in the case of INE / BNE is that this governance has been allowed to run riot and there is none at the moment.

Furthermore, business such as BNE / INE would proactively promote the public interest by encouraging community growth and development, and voluntarily eliminating practices that harm the public sphere, regardless of legality. Essentially, CSR is the deliberate inclusion of public interest into corporate decision-making, and the honouring of a triple bottom line: People, Planet, Profit. Instead what we have here is a deliberate attempt at constant mis-information.

This type of behaviour as demonstrated by Mr Quinn the Cult leader is not business it is self seeking cult activity that serves his only purpose. Ms Morrice, and her cousin Josh Stewart are equally as bad in what they are doing. As is the other Director Patti Usher. What has to be clear is that you have cases of international fraud and crimes and they will be held responsible for these activities. There is always an audit trail with this type of destruction.
