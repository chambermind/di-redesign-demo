The Naked Emperor

Transcendental Meditation

By Johannes Aagaard

When you first meet Maharishi Mahesh Yogi you are struck by his size. How can a Maharishi be that tiny? He does not only look little, but also very innocent and kind. His broad nose and his brown eyes have nothing of that expression of arrogance and “Besser-wissen” which characterises the attitude of some of his Indian and Western assistants. While they push people around he just sits there, witnessing what happens without interfering.

One is inevitably misled: If anything is wrong in Transcendental Meditation (TM) it must come from the people around him – or behind him. He is too good, most certainly. One feels an urge to touch him and become close to him, while the many soldiers in his army definitely do not evoke such sentiments.

What the TRUTH about Maharishi and his TM movement is we cannot know for sure – as yet. But we may within the near future. For something is happening in his empire. It is on the point of crumbling. The Goliath from Rishikesh – so small in stature and so great in armour – is hit by some stone from a David from Berkeley, the Spiritual Counterfeits Project, a handful of evangelicals and their supporters. It appears that those few stones are enough to change the balance within the TM; perhaps because the balance was already delicate before the stones found their mark.

The Davids of our era are operating not least significantly in court rooms. The Spiritual Counterfeits Project, together with the Coalition for Religious Integrity, and Americans United for Separation of Church and State, issued a lawsuit challenging the teaching of TM in schools. It so happened that New Jersey became the state for this law suit, and judge Curtis J. Meanor became world famous by rendering the following summary judgment:

“That the Science of Creative Intelligence / Transcendental Meditation and the teaching hereof, the concepts of the field of pure creative intelligence, creative intelligence and bliss-consciousness, the textbook entitled Science of Creative Intelligence for Secondary Education – First Year Course -Dawn of the First Year of the Age of Enlightenment, and the puja ceremony, are all religious in nature within the context of the Establishment Clause of the First Amendment of the United States Constitution, and the teaching hereof in the New Jersey public schools is therefore unconstitutional…”

TM has appealed to the Circuit Court of Appeals, and the question may go to the Supreme Court, but the possibility of a different conclusion is very unlikely. TM has lost the battle. It will have to withdraw from all educational and other governmentally supported institutions in the US, and its often repeated claim to be a neutral technique without religious connotations is now impossible to uphold any longer.

This is not the end of TM, but this is the beginning of a completely new period in the life of TM. Consequently, the leader of the movement until now, the American Jerry Jarvis, has been set aside and is a “dead man” in the movement, unless he comes forward with new miracles for TM. Maharishi will survive. Gurus always do. But he will most likely have to choose a very different future.

TM came to the West in 1959 and became the religion of the hippies. Maharishi became famous not least because he was made the guru of the Beatles and Mia Farrow. He was the symbol of the long-haired and flowery period of the youth revolution of the sixties. He was himself a drop-out and became the guru of the drop-outs. His movement was called the Spiritual Regeneration Movement, something made up totally by himself in 1958 and sold to western youth via the mass-media, who more or less created the sensational Maharishi and his first image.

The Spiritual Regeneration Movement had its time in the west, headed and governed first of all by low-keyed Charlie Lutes from the US, but he had to give in when Jerry Jarvis took over and launched the new image of TM as the solution to all the problems of the world, a solution ostensibly unrelated to religion and mysticism, but based on scientific research and improved psychological balance. The frontman for this latter trend was R. Keith Wallace, who was made the President of the Maharishi International “University” and whose smiling face is found in endless copies on the slick publications which are sent out in millions from the headquarters of TM. But the real power behind this development was the now dethroned Jerry Jarvis.

The movement’s radical change can be seen from the change in personality of its disciples. They are definitely not the hippie-type any longer. They are smart girls and tie-boys. They are efficient looking and smoothly smiling people. Flower-Power is given up, and Mind-Power is taking over. They still go high but not on dope. They go high on the Maharishi himself and his meditative techniques.

The radical change took place in 1971-72 and was created by two factors, one internal and one external. Reports from former TMleaders have come out stating that Maharishi fell in love with an 18 year old girl. Their time together ended when she left him. The disillusionment of the in-group, who knew about it, did not come from any sort of fear of the reality of love, but from the extreme difference between the doctrine of Bramacharya, which Maharishi has always propounded, and his blatant contradiction of it. How could a man who claimed to have given up all worldly pleasure behave as a normal human being? How could a Father Divine behave like a human brother?

The in-group often had a hard time explaining inconsistencies in the life of the Maharishi. He is famous for his temper and his fear and paranoia are legendary. Once one of his trusted messengers, who brought loads of dollars into Switzerland and India was caught in the customs. The way in which the liberated master responded to the crisis was to get rid of all incriminating material by dumping it in the sea. His instructions to the staff about his own role in the running of the organization: “I am only the spiritual leader, I know nothing about the running of the org.” – demonstrated that he was a man who was certainly not above petty human politics.

The radical change of TM was thus motivated by the inner development of the Maharishi himself. He started living a double life -also getting more money than one can imagine. He grew rich and he simply became worldly in a very normal way. He started buying; for instance 7 Mercedes Benz at a time, a jet helicopter, and similar things which changes not only the lives of men, but men. He was winning the whole world but loosing his soul. It is probably as simple as that, and as horrible.

He was never very trustworthy, but now he simply became a liar par excellence. The whole TM system became a lie, and the disciples were systematically drilled to become liars. They operated on the basis of a system with different levels of consciousness, and the higher up in the system the more divine one became. Maharishi was expected to be at the top -even if it was never said – and at that level he was Truth itself, God Himself, and thus the problem of truth and credibility could never be raised. What he said was per definition true. And those who spoke against him, were per definition mistaken.

The movement became a roaring success, first of all because of the scientific glamour it got from some experiments in some US institutes of psychology. The results published by TM were quite out of proportion compared with the factual research done when all the overstatement is taken away, however, something still remains. The destressing effect of TM was a fact but very little was done to explain the fact that the same effect is found in similar and simpler methods. TM claimed the whole honour and glory. But not forever. At this late hour the scientists are turning against TM and are redressing the balance, renouncing the cordial alliance which TM established so onesidedly.

Billed as the mind-pager of the 20th century, and “proved” by scientific experiments to be effective and harmless, TM sold well, very well indeed. Hundreds of thousands of people entered the movement, and Jerry Jarvis as the right hand of the Maharishi and public relations man had to change the organisation to cope with the situation.

A group of efficient young, Germans and Americans took over and formed a new elite. The old ones had to accept grudingly a secondary position, even if a small minority of them were given high honorary posts in the new hierarchy. Maharishi was eager to establish an order of monks and nuns, but with the new development this did not exactly fit in.

Instead he organized an in-group of 108 “brahmins”, i.e. young and rich people who could be relied upon, real yes-men and woman, complete with Maharishi’s body-guard of uniformed he-men.

The Age of Enlightenment was inaugurated from 1975 – 12th of January – with among others these words from Maharishi: “There has not been and there will not be place for the unfit. The fit will lead, and if the unfit are not coming along there is no place for them…The ignorant will be made enlightened by a few orderly, enlightened people moving around. Nature will not allow ignorance to prevail. It just can’t. Non-existence of the unfit has been the law of nature.”

Maharishi was very joyful at that day. He had launched the World-Plan. He had put forward the theory of the “Maharishi Effect” which was confirmed again and again “from chemistry, physiology, neurophysiology etc. Such a joy -” as he expressed himself on that day in the beginning of 1975, perhaps the peak of his life. But since then is has been going down, largely as a result of the New Jersey court case.

To counteract this, he started the program of levitation. Maharishi of course knew about the “siddhis” (occult powers) back in the Old days, but he now saw a new possibility in these powers. They represented the “peak of human possibilities”, the real transcendance of human limitations. He took the instructions from Patanjali’s Yoga Sutras, where anyone can find them, and as usual he made a sort of easy-reader programme out of it.

Of course, all of these thousands of TM students are not really flying, they are merely bouncing around (in more ways than one:). But how can it be that so many of them are able to testify to their own flying experience? First of all, they want to believe it. There is a very strong social pressure exerted upon them. In the future no one can have a position of leadership in TM unless he or she can levitate. Second, they are doing the bouncing together under a number of mental manipulations, which reminds one of the Hu-meditation of Shree Rajneesh. The mental effects of this auto-suggestion is considerable. They do get high. And levitation is nothing more and nothing less than this imagination.

Swami Vishnudevananda from the US (Shivananda-tradition) has publicly denounced TMs claims to teach levitation and asked Maharishi to demonstrate the supposed phenomena under laboratory conditions, yet TM is not willing to do so, even though they have been offered a lot of money for doing it.

Not only outsiders have reacted negatively. It is a much more serious situation for Maharishi that some of his own top-disciples have now had enough, more than enough. They have landed after the flight. They want to get out. It has been the game of our life,” they say, “to run around being world-governors and all that. But the old man is overdoing it.”

Usually when guru-disciples drop out, they remain in the reality of the ideology. They feel no responsibility for the many people trapped within the movement, even if these people have to a large degree been brought into it by the systematic lying of the former disciples themselves. They just want to get away and others may clean up after them.

The point of course is, that TM has always been, under the cover of its pompous pseudo-scientifical jargon, a solid piece of Hindu-religion with all the effects that has on human beings in relation to ethical relativism and cynical individualism.

The religious nature of TM was pushed into the background, but was at the same time firmly established. All attempts to reduce the religious basis was resolutely opposed by the Maharishi himself. When an important part of The Norwegian TM wanted to drop the puja with its blatantly hindu character, this group was immediately excommunicated by Maharishi himself. An independent TM organisation consequently arose in Norway – ACEM – under the able leadership of Are Holen, one of the former top leaders during Maharishis early times.

The puja ritual is not only hindu, but is through and through tantric. And the mantras given out as part of the ritual are as a whole tantric in origin. Once a disciple asked Maharishi about this, and the Maharishi answered: “Tantra, that is our tradition, and we are stepping into it one day.”

In fact Maharishi came from a tantric tradition, since the order to which he belonged – the holy order of the paramaham­saha – is tantric by nature. The name of his beloved guru called Guru Dev (Brahmananda Saraswati) indicates this tantric context. The successor in the ministry of the Shankaracharya, called Karpatraji will, however, have nothing to do with Maharishi.

The reason for this lack of connection is quite simple. Maharishi was not the heir apparent in Guru Dev’s lineage, as he pretends. He was a brahmacharya, i.e. a brother dedicated to live as a celibate and work for the order, which he did as a clerk and secretary. But he was never made a swami or monk. That is rather strange, but most probably means that he was never recognized as a spiritual man. Consequently, he never received the esoteric teaching and never became enlightened. It is a fact that Maharishi never talks about his enlightenment, or experience of samadhi, as is the rule for all spiritual leaders in India.

A story is told in TM circles about the spiritual authorisation which was given by Guru Dev to Maharishi in order that he could enlighten the average spiritual life in the world, but this is most probably a legend which has come into being because of the lack of a real and established authorisation by the guru.

Since Maharishi has no basis in the tradition from which he came, he is trying hard to achieve acceptance in India. He is not only giving his family enormous gifts, but also politicians and other gurus have been overwhelmed by his will to give – in order to get. It seems that his only success in this direction was his meeting in 1976 with the miracle guru-Muktananda, whom he seems to have allied with for some sort of mutual recognition. The special emphasis of Muktananda is on the siddhis, the program which Maharishi made famous after 1976, and which today contributes so much to his lack of credibility.

At any rate, Maharishi is running a world firm which pretends to sell something it in fact does not sell, but rather sells something else. It makes people into Hindus. They are, however, made into Hindus without knowing it. This is the problem. No one will attack the right of Hindus to proselytise their religion, no matter how false one may consider this religion. It is their right. But no-one has a right to propagate a religion under the cover of not being religious. That is insidous, unethical, and it ultimately invalidates the religion.

In fact, about 2 million people have been initiated into Hinduism via TM, for they have worshiped Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva by means of an offering of flowers, fruits, cloth and money. They have received some of it back again as “prasad”, which means part of the divinized offering, which the gods accepted and gave back. The guru, both Maharishi and his guru, are worshipped as part and parcel of the ritual, since the Divine Reality is seen and expressed in the Divine Guru. “Guru and God” is one, that is a fundamental confession of the religion in question.

These 2 million people have for years been meditating daily on the name of a Hindu god or goddess. They do not recognize that fact, but a fact it remains. They are told that the puja functions objectively without regard to their religious adherence or non-adherence. And that may be true. It may well function even if the disciples know nothing about it, as does the mantra, which they received from the offering.

The mantra which is most often given out is the mantra Aim (ainga, Aaing, Aaim, Ing, Im, Eng, Em)depending on how it is pronounced). That is simply the pseudonym for the goddess Ma Saraswati, the great Shakti of Shiva, which is his pleasure potency. She is the tantric goddess par excellence. During one’s development, more mantras are added, or the mantra grows.

As R.D. Scott has described in his book “Transcendental Misconceptions” (1977), it can happen by adding two words to Aim, thereby reaching the mantra “Shri Aim Namah” which simply means “I worship the most honourable Saraswati”.

The ritual is a tantric worship service, the mantra is a mini-edition of the same tantric ritual, and the doctrines which gradually are manipulated into the minds of the meditators are tantric doctrines. These are all fact’s, well established by objective research, but denied flatly by Maharishi and his people. The top-disciples who have now left him admit that they knew the truth all the time, but living in a world in which the words of Maharishi were Truth, it did not matter.

The former disciples quite clearly put all the blame upon Maharishi. They say he is the great liar, he is the deceiver. And no doubt he is. But that does not mean that he is the only liar and deceiver. Those who knowingly participated and continue to participate in the systematic lying which has taken place all over the world for nearly 20 years are also guilty. And they also bear some responsibility for the whole development.

A guru is made by the disciples just as much as the disciples are made by the guru. It is a mutual affair. Maharishi himself is trapped. He is never able to relax. As soon as he appears hundreds of people try to get to him for advice and consultation.

What he has gained in external power and prestige he has obviously lost in self-confidence and authentic stance. His utterances are becoming more and more bizarre. He can speak and speak without ever expressing anything. Any new phrases in his speeches, according to the former disciples, are added by his in-group and speechwriters.

He is most certainly responsible for the lack of authenticity which has been the mark of TM from the beginning, but the disciples are also responsible. Their wild and uncontrolled enthusiasm has contributed a great deal to the present TM madness.

What is the task of the churches in this situation? We cannot take responsibility for the TM people. Those Christians who have themselves been misled and have supported the TM farce will have to repent for their participation in this manipulation of millions, this worship of a “naked emperor” (H.C. Andersen)

The churches can do nothing better than to prepare a genuine form of Christian meditation, which without pomp and circumstance can be offered as a genuine means for establishing a right balance in mind and body “in the name of Jesus” and “the light of the Holy Spirit”, a meditation with open eyes, i.e. not forgetting and trying to escape from the world of our Creator, but going into it in order to save what it is possible to save, respecting the difference between man and God.
