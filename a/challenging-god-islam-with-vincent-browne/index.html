Challenging God: Islam with Vincent Browne

Religion

Which Abdul Haseeb turned up on Challenging God, Jekyll or Hyde?

Challenging God has shown how the study of Religion is very confused in this country. People who would be very tuned into political developments are totally at sea in regard to understanding the current religious landscape.

Vincent Browne introduces Oliver Scharbrodt as a lecturer in Islamic Theology at UCC. The caption  under his name says he is from the Theology Dept, UCC?

No such Department exists:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/10/28/dialogue-ireland-welcomes-study-of-religions-at-ucc/

Below you will see how we welcomed this faculty in 2009, but Challenging god does not seem to have understood that the teaching of Religion at the old Queen’s Colleges is precluded………

“We have come along way since the formation of the Queen’s Colleges in the C19 which excluded denominational religious education of any kind. Cardinal Newman had written his Book on the Idea of a University which viewed Theology as the queen of the Sciences. Here we have the study of religion which Dialogue Ireland welcomes and has campaigned for, for years. It is unfortunate that our history has precluded the study of World Religions, comparative religion and religious studies which other European states have had since the C18. We have faculties of denominational Theology at St Patrick’s College, Maynooth,  the Milltown Institute, All Hallows, Trinity College Dublin, Union College, Belfast, Baptist College, Moira, Co Down and Judaism Studies TCD.”

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/10/28/ucc-begins-survey-of-irish-muslims/

Abdul Haseeb comes over as a very gentle though very anti Western commentator. No research has been done on his extreme Islamist views and you would get the impression that butter would not melt in his mouth. He was connected to Liam Egan now based in Saudi Arabia who made virulent attacks on Jews and the Ahmadiyas. Also he has appeared as the spokesperson for the Clongriffen Mosque and has admitted when interviewed that the hype around this €40 m mosque project is pie in the sky.  There is currently no funding. However, below the links you find his real views on religious tolerance. Also by reading through the links you will find a real Jekyll and Hyde persona!

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/08/30/proposed-mosque-gets-approval-various-reports/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/08/30/clongriffin-mosque-a-mosque-too-far-planning-permission-yes-building-not-a-hope-in-hell/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/08/31/what-do-the-dublin-welfare-society-and-abdul-haseeb-project-co-ordinator-for-the-clongriffin-mosque-represent-cultism-or-communication/

http://www.a-haseeb.blogspot.ie/

“The Islamic celebrations are decent, clean and balanced. It is not an occasion for intensive acts of worship marked by strict rituals, as is the case some Jewish traditions. On the other hand, festive seasons such as, for example, Halloween or Christmas in Christianity, or Diwali and Holi in Hinduism, cause widespread drunkenness and harm to life and property. To the extent that some of these celebrations/festival seasons are based on fairy tales and myths – with no evidence whatsoever.”
