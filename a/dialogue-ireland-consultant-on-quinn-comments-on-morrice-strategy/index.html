Dialogue Ireland consultant on Quinn comments on Morrice strategy

Tony Quinn


Dialogue Ireland consultant on Quinn comments:

2010/09/22 at 1:06 am

QUINN’S PROBLEMS, NEWSPAPER EXPOSES, SUSAN MORRICE’S STATEMENTS

It is becoming blindingly obvious from Susan Morrice’s increasingly vituperative attacks on anybody that dares to disagree with her and Quinn and her increasingly desperate, incoherent and inconsistent statements in the media that she is unable to answer legitimate questions about her situation and problems of the oil company. According to her, the problems, including the very real problems of the company now being discussed in public, are all to be blamed on an unnamed “conspiracy” and have nothing to do with her, Quinn or anybody who is in her camp. This is one of the weakest strategies used to deflect criticism, defend a weak position, attempt to force blind allegiance to a leader and crush legitimate discussion – invent a “conspiracy. She and Quinn have consistently failed to produce any evidence of a “conspiracy” because there just isn’t any evidence of a malevolent group acting against the company. There are however those who disagree with her and Quinn and are justifiably they feel, dissatisfied with the state of affairs as they see it and want changes made to the benefit of the company and their own interests. This is entirely legal and even admirable.

Her references to a handful of newspaper articles as if they were favourable to her is bizarre as they simply quote her statements (contradicted by many other individuals), and give them no credibility other than that, while in reality there are several tongue in cheek statements that make her and Quinn look very odd at least and in other cases make absolutely critical mention about Quinn, his history, his lack of meaningful qualifications and his laughable claims. Newspaper reports raise many serious questions about the state and functioning of the company none of which she meaningfully addresses. Morrice’s inability or unwillingness to see any of this is worrying for a supposedly down to earth businesswoman. Her unwillingness to condemn attacks on individual privacy, her support, in public, for those who carry out these intimidating and persecutory tactics, her latest call to arms to create ‘good publicity’ for her cause and drifting from reality into fantasy is even more worrying.

The rambling diatribes against any legitimate opposition and the labelling of the many negative media reports about Quinn as just the work of a few troublesome individuals and Dialogue Ireland is clearly nonsense. Dialogue Ireland has archives of numerous newspaper exposes on Quinn going back nearly 40 years and many individual testimonies over the last two decades highly critical of the cultist Quinn. Morrice’s increasingly desperate attempts to defend Quinn, and to deny the reality of Quinn’s actions and history are very much in keeping with the mindset of the Quinn cult. Her public statements seem to be becoming increasingly ill-conceived, lacking in clarity or consistency and at times hardly literate. This seems very reminiscent of Quinn’s style and level of operation. Needless to say Quinn hides away from the trouble and says very little in public, apart from his stage-managed events controlled by his clique, but leaves the women to fight his battles as he has done for about 40 years.
