Deadline passes for long arranged visit of DI to the Congregation of Our Lady of Refuge

The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge

In a reply to Fr Michael Caulfield I gave a deadline to him for my visit scheduled for some time to visit and see for myself what was going on.

We have an appointment for tomorrow could you confirm by Friday 3 PM whether this visit is on?

Reply by DI to Fr Michael Caulfield of the Congregation of Our Lady of Refuge

Embedded media (opens the original source)

We have had no further contact from Michael Caulfield and so my visit has been cancelled.

I was hoping to meet his assistant a woman who apparently stays up in Roscommon while Michael returns to Dublin. She is supposed to be the person setting up their web site so it would have been good to see what she was up to and talk to her about her involvement with The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge. Michael says she is very devout and committed to the aims of the movement.

She is the youngest person connected to The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge. See her next to the woman with her head encircled.
