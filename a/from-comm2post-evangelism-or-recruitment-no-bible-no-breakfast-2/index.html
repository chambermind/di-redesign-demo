From comm2Post. Evangelism or recruitment? No Bible no Breakfast 2

Submitted on 2011/04/06 at 1:02 pm

See commentary under post.

I have laughed a little at the comments left on this site for the last few weeks as so many are obviously hearsay & have absolutely no grounds. But I cannot but respond at this point. I have been involved with Abundant Life over the last few years & although I don’t think they are perfect in every way, there is absolutely nothing which justifies this barrage of insinuations recently. The church does use St,Patricks to advertise Christianity as well as their events & there is nothing shameful in that. Many other Churches in Ireland & in Galway, do the same, just look at the parade every year & see the colours of the African churches & the other Irish Churches. The Church does not make money on its Kids Summer Camp. This is a terrible insult to all the camp workers over the past 10 years who have taken a weeks holiday to volunteer to run sports, teach art, run drama workshops, puppet shows & music classes for all the kids who have attended this event over the past 10 years. I still am aqainted personally with Kevin Sanford, & I have to say that anyone who has met him or dropped into a service in his Church would find him a gentleman. Every year he leaves room for kids to come to camp who cannot afford to pay, & in 10 years he has never put up the price of the camp from €55. Most years we had around 100 kids in attendance (some of whom were given free access) which would raise approx 5k (at 55 per child). These funds were used to run music classes, art & wood crafts, & to decorate the premises, turning office buildings into childrens themed jungles, pirate ships etc. The children are also entertained by dramas which all promote moral values & Christian principles i.e. Do not Lie, do not steal, do not bully, Do not be disrespectful of your parents. As it is an arts camp, the characters are always fully costumed & supplemented by puppets, & props which all create a fantastic atmosphere of fun & art amidst learning about basic Christian principles. If you think you can create a Kids camp in Galway which can cater for 100+ kids while only charging them 55€ per child & giving them 9am until 3pm day filled with Sports, Soccer tournaments, arts, wood crafts, children’s themed room decorations & fully costumed actors teaching them moral values, then I challenge you to try & do it for less than 5k. Whatever your issue is with this Church, please do not insult these people by saying that they are making money from a Kids event. And worse, to insinuate that all Kids are brainwashed is also very offensive. All parents who have attended this camp in Galway over the years have never found anything controversial taught to any of their kids except basic morality & Christian principles. Most years the workers would be over 25 people so the ratio of workers to kids is extremely high & their is always a volunteer nurse at the event. Kids are treated with the utmost respect & enjoy a completely safe environment. I for one, would like to say “Well done” to all the workers & all the leaders who have worked this camp for free over the last 10 years. who have taken some of their summer holidays to work for so many kids ni our city to entertain & instill in them the values which we hold so highly in the Christian faith. For all the music practices, all the drama practices, all the songs, the sports, the long hours spent preparing music, crafts, dramas & decorating venues, & then to be smeared throughout Ireland by Mike Garde & Dialogue Ireland because of some disgruntled people I think it is very unfair. I understand that the intent of Dialogue Ireland & Mike Garde is to expose extremeties & abuses within religious movements (& we all see the need for that in this country), however I believe in this case you are nit-picking through articles to try and create the appearance of financial unsoundness & abuse, which is not at all justified. It is quite obvious that none of you have seen the Churches premises at this moment in time becuase they are not lavish by any stretch of the imagination. You are also not aquainted with Kevin Sandford because if you were, you would be well aware that he is not making any money out of this Church or these events. He has previously raised funds in the USA to work in Ireland but is in no-way well paid, or paid much at all these days. Therefore I would caution your bloggers to reconsider some of their allegations & be absolutely correct in what they are asserting on this website. We have all tremedous things to learn from each other & we welcome the dialogue that this site has exposed. I imagine even Kevin Sandford himself will take away lots of wisdom from this site & realise how some of his fundraising or events are causing offence, and therefore reconsider his priorities & his convictions & convey them better to the general public. All this I commend Dialogue Ireland for doing, but in relation to smearing Abundant Life as some money-hungry, brainwashing entity with only self-interest, I happen to know that this is really not true. Its a pity that bloggers choose to anonymously blog instead of drop by the Church & see exactly what happens. Its very easy to imagine terrible things happening in a place you are completely ignorant of. Much like any of the Pentecostal Churches throughout this country, or the African or Brazilian Pentecostal Churches, Abundant Life is charismatic & lively & filled with much of the cultures that come from its multi-cultured origins. If this is not to your taste or spiritual preference then I would respectfully request that you show the same dignity towards Abundant Life that they show towards the traditional expressions of religion that surround them in Galway. Bear in mind also that many of our tradition Irish religions also have the worst record of spiritual & physical abuse of any organization, & many of us who have distanced ourselves from them are amazed at their general acceptance in society today while organisations like Abundant Life are lambasted with accusations. Is it perhaps becuase we are so unfamiliar with new expressions of pentecostalism in Ireland that they offend us so much.

1 last point. In my experience with them, Abundant Life tries to teach people that God is good, & therefore to pray for good things, & believe that God is a good God rather than a harsh judge. In the spiritual climate in which we are in, many people find it offensive to think that God would be good, or would answer prayers for provision of needs, but that does not seem to contradict the bible anywhere that I can see. There is no-one in Abundant Life who I would consider rich – NO-ONE!! So the idea that they preach that the Gospel equals prosperity, is in my opinion a mis-understanding of their belief that God is a good God. I had quite an involvement in this ministry & I can vouch that there is definitely no money tricks, or money grabbing going on in my experience. I reckon the ministers could probably take from this debate that their newsletter are extremely one-sided & they should just stop mentioning this issue as it obviously causes quite an offence, particularly in the climate we are in today.
