Dialogue Ireland received this testimony from a former member of the COG.

In Ireland we still have a remnant of the COG living near Castletroy, Co Limerick. When they first came to Dublin they lived in Haddon Road, Clontarf.

Una McManus was an early member and tells about her involvement in her book featured below. In the early 80’s they were in Newcastle, Co Dublin and were expecting a nuclear attack. In the 90’s they were in an isolated house near Duleek, Co Meath where I visited them. They tried to convince me of their open marriage concept. They allowed “voluntary” sex between marrieds and unmarrieds. They were also visited there by Gordon Melton.
