Is Ole Nydahl a shining star or a rough diamond??

Ole NydahlTibetan Buddhism = Lamaism

http://www.lama-ole-nydahl.org/

Ole Nydahl has been in Ireland often, but for over 20 years DI has been aware of his activities. First through Prof Johannes Aagaard from Aarhus University who had him as a participant at a Conference there in the 90’s where I was present.

Also with our awareness of abuse issues in regard to Rigpa and Sogyal we have received a number of requests in regard to his activities.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/04/07/briefing-document-on-sogyal-rinpoche/

 https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/rigpa/

JF Kennedy was a President for a short time and because he was assassinated we now have frozen him in time as a hero. However, it is becoming clear that his disorderd sexual life has cast a shadow on his character which is a lens we must look at his record with.

We have already studied how Lamaism enables and protects the Tibetan Lamas, but it seems that Ole Nydahl as a Westerner has also been excluded from being viewed as a serial abuser. We are here opening a dialogue on this subject and would invite people to use our comment section to leave their views to help us explore this topic.

We must stress that comments are only welcome which address the issues around the claims he is involved with abusive relationships with women and then we ask you comment on the video below and ask is this an appropriate relationship for HH 17th Gyalwa Karmapato be in? Also note the loss of critical reasoning which Johannes Aagaard referenced back in 1993?
