Taoiseach Enda Kenny receives book, “The Magic in You,” from Tony Quinn devotee Susan Morrice. What are we to make of it?

Emma Maree Fitzpatrick:

Dialogue Ireland received a package sent out to INE supporters and as it contained a photograph of the Taoiseach Enda Kenny we decided to call his Press Office and inform them of this information.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/01/03/susan-morrice-and-the-new-age-unwrap-yourself/

We obviously sent on material to brief him on the Quinn connection. “I am sending you the picture sent out by Susan Morrice Chairman of the Belize Oil Company where there are serious problems since Tony Quinn got involved. You will note we have material going back over 40 years in our archive.”

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/tony-quinn/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/30/judge-bannisters-judgement-read-it-for-yourself/

We wrote as follows, “I am attaching the Christmas Card sent out by Susan Morrice who is seriously under the influence of Quinn. The assumption of this crude propaganda tool is that Quinn is a kind of Merlin the Magician who found the oil in Belize!”

This package includes the Christmas Card attached, the book which the Taoiseach is holding, and the picture of the Taoiseach and was sent out to all their shareholders.


This picture could bring the office of the Taoiseach into serious disrepute.
Hence we decided to contact the Taoiseach before we publish it.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/27/cult-book-the-magic-in-you-makes-tony-quinn-into-merlin-the-magician-who-found-the-oil/

We are planning a protest outside Quinn’s next meeting at the Clontarf Castle this Sunday.

“I look forward to hearing back from you and am available to consult with the Taoiseach on this issue and on the cult issue in general as Ireland has no protection for the victims of cults in our legislation.
Also being from Mayo he is no doubt aware of the activities of Christina Gallagher. We have the most extensive archive on the House of Prayer in the country.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/house-of-prayer/
Cardinal Brady is totally refusing to address the activities of a priest who is in his diocese Fr Gerard McGinnity who flagrantly supports this fake visionary
Christina Gallagher by his endorsement and his weekly visits there. Elder abuse is the result and we have heard from so many families it is unbelievable.”

When we had again after 24 hours heard nothing from the Press Office we contacted them again:

“Further to my email 24 hours ago and my call to check you had received my mail, I can confirm that you have made no progress in regard to ascertaining whether the Taoiseach knowingly engaged with Susan Morrice and Patricia Fitzpatrick of the Quinn Cult.”

This photograph is being used to promote the idea that Quinn has the ear of government and was included in a Christmas mailing to his supporters by Susan Morrice.
This will no doubt be used on Sunday next at the Clontarf Castle to further entrap people in this group when Quinn hold his next seminar.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/11/26/tony-quinn-surrenders-after-the-helix-and-goes-behind-the-drawbridge-at-clontarf-castle/

“The Pope is also subject to this type of attempt to use his office to further particular interests  and it is a very common cult tactic to advance their cause.”

“As promised I attach a copy of another example of this phenomenon much more serious in its import.”


“Here the Dalai Lama is shanghaied by the founder of the Aum Cult in Japan. He was taken in, which is not a crime, but unless one clarifies the situation the cult uses it. The former Taoiseach Albert Reynolds took it a step further and actually gave lectures to the Moonies for a fee after he left office.”

BBC Spotlight highlighted the issues with Susan Morrice in a documentary last October. You will note we have attached a still from that programme indicating the influence Quinn has in this regard.

Susan Morrice is the sister of Jane Morrice who was involved with the Women’s Peace Coalition which led to the Good Friday Agreement.

“No doubt you will remember that Justice Hogan presented the Taoiseach with a copy of his work on the revision of the constitution in September last year.”


“This was just before he gave his landmark judgement which gave great support to our work in educating the public about cults. This was all in the context of a deposition by Susan Morrice the person he is photographed with.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/21/final-version-cornec-v-morrice-ors-judgment-by-hogan-j/
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/23/the-stunning-silence-of-the-irish-media-in-regard-to-the-landmark-hogan-judgement/
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/23/the-importance-of-justice-hogans-ruling-for-the-work-of-dialogue-ireland/

It is our intention to publish this photograph before the events on Sunday and if we have not received a reply from you by then will indicate we are still waiting to hear from you. However, we have a duty of care to those affected to show what lengths Cult groups go to push their agenda. We will also show the picture of the Dalai Lama to illustrate this point.”

A few hours later we got this cryptic reply?

Mike,

We are not able to confirm this.

Ger Butler
Government Press Office

Obviously if the we in fact do get confirmation of the exact circumstances of how the Taoiseach is in the company of Susan Morrice we will gladly publish it. If this photograph turns out to be a fake we will also publish that. But in the meantime we have to assume that the Taoiseach of Ireland is not fully aware of the import of this photograph.
