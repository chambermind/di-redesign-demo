Concerned Mennonite: Quinn and pollution

Tony Quinn

Concerned Mennonite: Quinn and pollution

I am reading all of the information that is being posted on this website about BNE and the oil company.  I would like to use the facilities of your website to make the following comments.  The BNE management team, INE Directors, CHx partnership and the GOB still continue to allow BNE to “flare off” the gas produced in the Spanish Outlook area.  This affects our community and our health and well being.  This is burning millions of much needed dollars that our country and communities need.

In relation to the Mr Quinn, for someone who is supposedly so into health and well being according to the press activity and his own publicity.  How can he be a supposed  Director of a company that is allowing this.  Would he like all his fancy people attending his expensive seminars to have to breath in those fumes day after day.  This man is not what he says he is, he is simply a man that I don’t believe ever stood in our community.  He is just happy to rip people off including short changing this community.

Susan Morrice is constantly in Spanish Lookout in her big fancy car and her private plane flying in for visits.  She arranged for one of the fancy Educo gyms to be brought to iguana creek offices for ‘everyone to use.  Well most of us Mennonites cannot access it and even if we do we are there breathing in the toxic fumes.  What is really hard for us to understand it that the GOB and the Ministers still allow this to happen??  It is an eyesore for the last five years.

It not good for da trees you know an ma grandchildren they swallow it –
