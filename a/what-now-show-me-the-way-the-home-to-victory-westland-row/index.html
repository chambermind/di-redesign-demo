What now? Show me the way home to Victory Westland Row

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church


http://www.property.ie

On the Sunday Victory used The Green Isle Hotel, a quick and sudden return to Firhouse was proclaimed. One week later a more subdued pastorate addressing a smaller crowd, spoke of a possible delay. But what really are the permutations?  Grant Thornton the receivers will be tasked by the Bank for a quick resolution. This is particularly so when said bank has by and large left the Irish market.  Before the bank can go after the trustees personally they will first have to establish the actual loss that is the shortfall between the loan amounts less funds raised from sale of assets.  It should be borne in mind that from a balance sheet or actual loss position the bank will have provided for this already and so distress sakes like this are already history from their perspective.

This could give rise to a “white knight” buyer who could put a revitalised Victory back in play. Other Evangelicals may ( or may not) feel that to purchase it for the kingdom would be too close to a Judas kiss and may shy away. Alternatively a large group of former members could seek to set a new king on the throne; I understand there are many with possible buying power.

Property Description

The property occupies a prominent position on Westland Row and comprises a terraced four storey over basement building. The Westland Cafe exploits the property’s road frontage successfully trading with ‘Seattles Best Coffee Shop’ coffee brand. The cafe can also be accessed internally through the original door which has been retained providing a traditional Georgian style entrance to the building, this is located to the right of the Cafe. The basement directly below the retail element of the ground floor has been extended and fitted out to provide additional seating for the cafe, it also provides stores, toilets and a small kitchenette. A hall / theatre fully equipped with a stage, lighting, camera, sound equipment and large television screens occupies the extended ground floor at the rear accommodating approximately 220 people. The first, second and third floors comprise of commercial space and the total property extends to approximately 655.3 sq m / 7,053.58 sq ft. 

Location: 

The subject property occupies a high profile location on the east side of Westland Row in close proximity to it’s junction with Lincoln Place. 35 Westland Row is situated approximately 1 km west of Grafton Street and close to Merrion Square. The immediate area is predominantly commercial in nature and the street benefits from high volumes of commuter traffic passing en route to Pearse Street DART and Train Station. Surrounding occupiers include The Trinity College Campus, The Royal Irish Academy of Music, Kennedys Bar and the O’Callaghan Davenport and Alexander Hotels.
