What is happening with the Palmarians?

Palmarian Church

We continue to receive distressed communications from a number of families and individuals who have family member or are themselves seeking help to exit this very secretive organisation. Unlike other Catholic Movements like the House of Prayer which claim to be Catholic but are not, this group claims to be THE Catholic Church and that it has supplanted the Rome based movement. You will find material about the Palmarians on our site:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/palmarian-church/

Whereas a few years ago it was nearly impossible to know what is going on now as one person wrote to us recently this changing:

.. So I went to the internet to investigate and when I saw what was written about Palmar, it struck a chord. Actually, there’s worse that you do not know about…

We recently heard of a woman who has tried to visit her sister in Dublin on many occasions, but had to fulfill a rigid dress code to be allowed in. Here is the letter she received with the following instructions,

The writing on the envelope suggests it was not written by the person who wrote the letter?

Even having done this it did not mean she would gain access. Often the curtains were drawn and no one answered. On one visit she arrived up to the house to see the curtains drawn and when she pushed the bell, was greeted by not her sister but the new owners of the house.

So younger people are not allowed to go into their parents houses and gradually they are being estranged. I believe it is time for the social services to be called as these vulnerable people are under such undue influence. Today we heard from a person who grew up in the Palmarians that when a partner dies it is suggested that widows then join another person in the same situation. We can only assume that the cash realised from the sale of these houses is going to the Church and not to the family members?

Recently the Director tried to see if there was any life at the Palmarian Church, at St Helen’s, 38 Haddon Road, Clontarf, Dublin 3?

Like all these groups who have something to hide there is no way to contact residents or to gain entry:

Various pictures of the house which was totally deserted, and no one came near it while I was there. I intend to have our Clontarf members maintain a watch on the property to see if anyone actually goes there? Please those of you concerned send us any photos you have!
