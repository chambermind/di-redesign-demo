Conferences & CommentariesHouse of Prayer

This latest report came out on the final day of the International Eucharistic Congress –

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/06/17/christinas-7th-heaven-by-jim-gallagher-sunday-world-june-17-2012/

Being present at the Statio Orbis Mass on June 17 was both a privilege and poignant. My reflections on the upcoming event were published in the Sunday Independent. 

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/05/13/questions-to-mike-garde-about-the-eucharistic-congress-from-the-sunday-independent-and-his-answers/

Outside were those that were trying to obtain justice as victims of abuse.  I was conscious of the great liturgical triumph going on but the simple steps of addressing the victims of Christina Gallagher are not being taken and are not viewed as  a priority. Many of those devotees of Christina Gallagher are not even aware yet they are victims but rather they believe that they are are a sign of a new Catholicism without the corrupting influences of the current Church.

We may expect that grand gestures and retirements will soon be forthcoming now that the Congress is finished but again these will simply deflect people from the reality that nothing is being done.

Fr McGinnity is not disciplined. He goes over to the House of Prayer each week bypassing the very Eucharist to be found in every parish which was highlighted for the past week at the Congress. They are not allowed to have a Eucharistic Celebration at the House as the place has no Church approval. The wishes of the Archbishop of Tuam are ignored and Cardinal Brady who is a Doctor of Canon Law sits on his hands while a priest from his diocese supports and encourages a schismatic Marian Scam. This is taken as an endorsement of the Parish Priest of Knockbridge, Co Louth by the supporters of  Christina Gallagher. Formal excommunication proceedings need to pursued to make clear where this issue stands. If currently no criminal or civil proceedings can proceed then a Canon Law response is necessary and the evidence showing that Fr McGinnity has received donations intended for the House of Prayer in his name need to be investigated.

 https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/08/05/let-us-draw-you-a-picture-of-the-house-of-prayer/

This an abuse of vulnerable adults here understood as those who under the mental manipulation of Christina who are handing over significant sums of money and whose families are emotionally destroyed and divided.
