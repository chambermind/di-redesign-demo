Legion closes seminary in Dublin!

Legionaries of Christ

DIRECTOR TERRITORIALIS

Below is a translation of an unsigned letter announcing the closing of the LC seminary in Dublin and the reassignment of Fr. Anthony Bannon. The complete Spanish language text was found on the Trastevere blog, where it was  posted today:

http://blogs.21rs.es/trastevere/2011/07/17/legionarios-de-cristo-cierra-el-seminario-de-dublin/#more-7583

Also we have had reports which suggest that Regnum Christi may be shifting its geographic location in Ireland.
Dublin, July 15, 2011

To the Legionaries of Christ and the consecrated members of Regnum Christi in the western and central European territory

Dearly beloved in Christ:

Please accept my cordial greetings from Dublin. I hope that you are well and that the various summer apostolic activities are yielding abundant spiritual fruits for all those taking part in them.

At this time I wish to tell you, as I have already done personally with the legionaries and consecrated in Ireland, that our Director General, having obtained the consent of his advisors and the approval of the Papal Delegate, has informed me that the Legion will not be able to assign novices to the Dublin Novitiate after September of this year. This novitiate will be closed and the brothers there will be transferred to other novitiates. The reasons which have brought about this painful decision are the scarcity of Irish vocations in recent decades, combined with the current difficulty of maintaining the novitiate with vocations from other countries, as well as the consolidation of efforts and resources that we are implementing throughout the world.

Since 1961 this novitiate has formed legionaries, among whom are more than 50 legionary priests who have offered and will continue offering their lives tirelessly in the service of God and the Church. Thanks to the presence of the Legion in Ireland we have also seen the emergence of a group of consecrated members of both branches who have committed themselves with great fervor to its mission in various parts of the world.

We retain the hope that in the future God may grant us numerous Irish vocations and the possibility of forming novices in this country which has provided so many and such exemplary vocations over the centuries. We will make every effort to maintain and foster our evangelical work in those lands where the Church is experiencing very great difficulties.

The apostolic community will continue to reside in the Leopardstown center in Dublin, and we will continue offering our services through the various apostolic works: The Dublin Oak and Woodlands academies, the retreat centre in Clonlost, the Faith and Family Centre in Dal Riada, and the collaboration that some priests offer in parishes on a regular or occasional basis.

At this time I would also like to inform you that Fr. Anthony Bannon, L.C. has been named superior of the Dublin community and will assume this post on July 18, 2011. Fr. Sergio Rosiles has been named director.

I invite you to keep hope and confidence in God alive as he guides us with His providential and loving hand through this phase in the life of our congregation. We continue strengthening our promise to lead a more holy life, both individually and as a congregation, in a way that we may give luminous testimony to the men and women we encounter along our path, and that we may be able to offer a greater service to the Church.

I ask you keep me in your prayers and offer my own for you. I remain yours in Christ,

cc:     Fr. Alvaro Corcuera, L.C., director general

Fr. José Alberto de la Garza, L.C., assistant to the territorial director for religious life
