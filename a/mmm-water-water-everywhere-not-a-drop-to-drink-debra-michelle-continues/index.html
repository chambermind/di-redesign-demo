MMM Water, water everywhere, not a drop to drink Debra? Michelle continues

Magnificat Meal Movement

We hope others of you will leave your stories and do not forget there is a section for comments. Phil Bloomfield told me about the attempt to encroach on Church lands and how they had to remove the obstructions at Fr Ryan’s request. I nearly got arrested at that point when I tried to seek an appointment with Debra she called the police and suggested I was “stalking”  her. Fr Ryan history will show was really a giant of a man in regard to taking on the MMM.

Michelle continues,

I often wonder how many people know about the well at the convent with the so called ‘Holy Water’. Debra gathered as many people together as possible on the day she said Our Lady would be appearing to select the spot where the bore was to be drilled. This was where there would be a well of miraculous holy water.

Every one followed her around the property praying the Stations of the Cross. I for one was thinking that Mary wasn’t going to turn up because we were running out of stations. We finished the last one and still nothing happened. Then Debra ran towards the convent laundry and proclaimed that Mary was standing near the bushes next to the building. She said that this was where the well would be.

The day the bore was drilled it was not an easy process. It took a few attempts and had to be bored deeper that expected to find water. Debra had the water pumped into the convent as the main water supply. She proclaimed the water was holy so we were all drinking it except her. People were coming regularly to fill containers for their own use. I didn’t know why she wouldn’t drink the water herself until after I was forced out of the movement. I found out that the DNR report showed the water was not fit for human consumption! To top that off, the well was illegally bored on the land that adjoined the property next door. This belonged to the Catholic Church. Father Ryan actually told me this.

It is a wonder that we didn’t all get sick from drinking the water. It’s amazing how she was able to get us all to believe that the water had the power to heal.

Another saga unfolded one day when I was working in the cafe that I owned in the main street. One of the ladies who regularly helped me had just arrived from the convent to tell me that there was some trouble over at the convent concerning Father Ryan and Debra. Debra ordered a group of the men to move the fence between the Catholic Church oval and the convent. She claimed that part of the land on the oval side belonged to the convent. So she just decided she was going to take it back. When Father Ryan found out what she was doing he confronted her on the spot and ordered her to immediately put the fence back where it was, or there would be legal action.

I went over to the convent to see what was happening. While the verbal altercation between Fr. And Debra was taking place, a group of the women ‘slaves’, (some from overseas), were dancing around in a circle and chanting like crazy people. I was so embarrassed! Debra was totally in the wrong. The fence was not in the wrong spot in the first place. Just another stunt that everyone blindly went along with making fools of themselves in the process.

Debra always seemed to enjoy causing trouble. Living in peace was not her style. She said that part of being a prophet was to cause division. Behaving like a brat is not something God would approve.

That poor priest next door endured a lot of harassment from Debra. She was very pleased with herself the day she told me that she was phoning him at two and three in the morning just to get him out of bed. She did this for weeks at Gods request. This information just gave me more proof that this woman was not genuine. Fr. Ryan confirmed the phone calls when I spoke to him after I left the movement.
