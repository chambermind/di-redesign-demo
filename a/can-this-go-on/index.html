Can this go on?

RIGPA

I keep hearing no one has been found guilty.

That is the best Rigpa can

come up with. There was an ashram

in Poona where the great Bhagvan

used to dispense wisdom. A sign outside

for those seeking enlightenment

read, “leave your mind at the door.”

Hear the sound of one hand clapping,

throw a stone into the lake with no

ripples. No Guru, no method-

there are nets set out to catch us

when we were born.

James Joyce, The Portrait.
