Reports on the 20th Anniversary of the House of Prayer

House of Prayer

World Youth Day in October 2011. The testimony of a young person!

Picture from the http://www.voiceofourladyspilgrims.com web site

I attended the House of Prayer, World Youth Day in October 2011. I went out of curiosity and a sense of trying to understand what this place was all about. We travelled on a bus from one of a lot of Irish Towns which stopped for people along that route as far as Limerick city. There was quite a large number on the bus between 20-25 people young and old alike. As I attempted to pay for the journey I was told by the person that it was a gift and I could attend again.  Generous as a way of bringing new people to be part of the project.

To start with, the every window of the bus had the ‘sacred’ picture of Our Lady with her eyes bleeding, the icon synonymous with the House of Prayer. This was very unnerving as it’s a frightening picture. On the bus we said several decades of the Rosary. The Rosary was said in the House of Prayer manner, very, very slowly with an over emphasis on each and every line. This reduced the prayers almost to a chant and was no ways similar to the everyday flow of speech. It made the Rosary seem hypnotic. I was told that this was to bring out the full meaning of the prayers. This was also re-iterated by Christine Gallagher on the day.

This journey took about four hours, without taking into account the stop in Knock. Then there is the four hours of prayers and the four hours back….. Mind altering exercises.

This praying continued until we reached Knock, where we stopped for Mass.

Note the significance of this matter of fact report. Why would you stop in Knock if your final destination is a Marian Shrine in Achill? Why would you stop at what is the official Marian Shrine of the Catholic Church in Ireland? It is the word Mass. Why is this important?

Because the House of Prayer is not recognised by the Catholic Church the strategy of Christina is to dupe the disciples to think they are part of the Catholic Church when they visit Achill. However, the place does not have the Eucharist so they stop for it from various starting points all around Ireland. The teaching given by McGinnity is all fire and brimstone and is part of the undermining of the people coming to Achill Catholicism. The fact they keep coming back there suggests it is the only place teaching the full message of the gospel. The brainwashing effect of listening to this priest from the Archdiocese of Armagh for long periods of time and the long journeys is the equivalent of indoctrination on a grand scale. Family members report people listening to a Robotic priest and their relatives becoming like the Stepford wives.

After that we headed for Achill. At this stage we were shown a promotional DVD of Christine Gallagher’s spiritual healing powers, her reasons for choosing Achill, all the miracles that she has performed for example healing people of cancer. Again as with the House of Prayer itself, everything was very dramatic. The people that Christine chose to heal were very much on death’s door with gory, harrowing descriptions of desperation and sickness.

Once we reached the House of Prayer, we entered. It is a very unassuming house from the outside. Inside the main door is the shop where you can buy your special Rosary beads, candles, holy pictures and all religious paraphernalia to do with the House of Prayer. Also there were three large scrapbooks filled with these so called testimonials ‘proving’ the healing powers of Christine Gallagher. Alongside this was a canteen.

What I did notice were the people who worked in the House of Prayer. These were fully suited ‘heavies’ for want of a better word. They were wearing a very smart uniform, had jackets with religious emblem on it, ties in gold and blue with a tie pin with the same emblem. Very noticeable was their ear pieces. All of these men were big and had a vague intimidating look about them. They had the aura of bouncers and gave the vibe that you were not to mess with them. Many stood outside guarding doors which required key codes to access.

 It is intimidating and they are afraid that someone like DI would arrive. We have already had a report of camera man being assaulted at the entrance. The priest has lost it big time and believes every shred of the stuff he recites, hook line and sinker.

We were encouraged to buy a single rose to place at the Altar in front of a very large image of Our Lady (again the House of Prayer version). It was stipulated that the children were to be separated from the adults. I found this rather disturbing as although I am certainly no child there were young children aged 9-10 separated from their parents. I witnessed an argument between and woman whose teenage children did not want to be separated from their mother. An official from the House of Prayer was dogmatic that they had to be separated from their mother and it was blatantly clear that this was causes both the teenagers and they’re mother much distress. They were separated.

This event was stage managed to show how this was a youth movement with a vibrant group wishing to be around Christina. In reality this is a set piece to promote her propaganda.

Inside the Church (for want of a better word), which had any aspects of a church with the exception of the bloody and frightening iconography. There was a very large picture of Our Lady, a bust of Jesus’s head where the crown of thorns was much emphasised along with the blood running from his head. Beside that, there was a large statue of the archangel St. Michael; with his sword drawn (he along with the pictures can be seen in Marcella’s house). The pamphlets left of the pews contained the words of the House of Prayer hymns. Each page in the pamphlet had the image of our lady printed on it. Within the hymns certain words were underline and printed in heavy black type. These include words such as blood, sacrifice and consecration. On the back was instructions on how to payer properly and slowly and that you should fast 2 days a week, Tuesday and Friday I believe. I destroyed all the documents when I reached home.

(A pity as we need the evidence for our archive.)

The House of Prayer officials were guarding the doors. Again you were free to come and go but the very presence of two suited burley men was enough to intimidate. People were coming and going (not many I may add) but I felt that you could not leave.

Note the official line that you are free to come or go, but the person who wrote this report felt so intimidated she did not feel free to do so.

The ‘service’ terribly long and draining. Fr. McGinnity spoke about Christine suffering, persecution and her miracles. Christine herself took centre stage. She spoke of her suffering and devotion to Our Lady, of the dangers of the modern world, tsunamis’, earthquakes all signs of the Devil of course. She spoke of the threats from other countries, of how other religions were gaining power of the dangers of Europe and the Euro, that the Euro would fall, and that Europe would take control. Christine compared herself to Padre Pio several times during her ‘sermon’, associating herself with Padre Pio both by her ‘stigmata’ and her persecution. She also spoke at length about sins. For example she stated that if you drank, took drugs or had sex before marriage that this would lead to suicide. Not only did she say this but proceeded to give an in-depth, very gory description of your soul bursting from your body and descending into hell. It was terrifying. The manner in which Christine spoke was in a low, throaty, impassioned voice which only added to the disturbing nature. Never once did she raise her voice. Christine also spoke out against the modern day church and essentially blackened it by saying that it wasn’t doing enough, that it was weak and gave the impression that we shouldn’t have faith in it. She also spoke of the dangers of tarot cards as it caused us to consort with the devil. Also she claimed that she spoke regularly to Our Lady and that Our Lady only resides in the House of Prayer. No-where else just in Achill.

Note the point about Knock earlier, sending them there has a mixed message. In reality it is part of their strategy to claim that they have exclusive hold on Mary the Mother of God.

Christine also claimed that Achill would be only safe place when terrifying natural disasters took place in Ireland and prophesied that many would try to reach her but not all would be saved.

This is her way to keep people coming to her every week:

1.      She is the only true messenger of Mary.

2.      Achill geographically is the only safe place.

3.      McGinnity acts as the enabler and gives her cover.

4.      The failure of the Catholic Church to respond hands her a vidctory and reinforces the idea she is the centre of action.

5.      Mary is only available through her. Forget about Knock.

6.      Her vulgar fortune telling natural religion binds them to her.

An emphasis was put on the special Matrix medal that our lady had Christine fashion and the holy picture. Only these particular items will protect and save you. She also spoke of how to pray properly in a slow fashion.

Matrix medal is another stunt to control her members.

After this, they stopped for more testimonials connected with the healing power of the water in Achill, cancers cured, tumours disappearing all of which occurred by drinking the water an Achill and praying. These testimonial were read by more House of Prayer officials, these were in the same garb as the ‘bouncers’ but were much thinner, weaker, less threatening looking. One young boy (roughly aged 17-19), dressed in his official House of Prayer suit, stood up and spoke of how he was cured by demons. A CD was also played; more testimonials were played on this CD.

Finally we were asked to consecrate ourselves to Our Lady. More praying ensued, again in the slow manner and the House of Prayer hymns were sung.

Finally after 3-4 hours we were back on the bus and on the way home we prayed and prayed and prayed.
