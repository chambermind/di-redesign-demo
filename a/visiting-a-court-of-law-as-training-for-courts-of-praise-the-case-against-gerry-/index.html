Visiting a court of law as training for courts of praise, the case against Gerry Byrne and Brendan Hade

EvangelicalismVictory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

When you enter the Dail as a TD you generally have a REGISTER OF INTERESTS where you must declare yours in a transparent way. When Gerry Byrne and Brendan Hade entered ministry their business interests seem to increase? Is there not a conflict of interest? The week before they both participated in a service on national TV they were again involved in a court case?

We note also that they keep changing their solicitors, why is this?

M & B Ceillier Solicitors in 12 Whitefriars, Peters Row, Aungier Street

We currently are watching this legal space. You will note that Victory is a Trust with one assumes a primary objective of the furtherance of religion? As a Trust they are required to present accounts to Revenue, but are not required to publish their accounts to the public as in the case of a company with directors.

One wonders if the commercial realities and profit making aspects of the Centre as distinct from the Church called Victory are purely used to further their primary religious aims?
