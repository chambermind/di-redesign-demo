Right of Attila the Hun and left of Genghis Khan – Yusuf al Qaradawi

Here is a scholar who appears to those to his right as sell out and a compromiser, and to those in the middle as moderate Islamist, but to those who have a sense of Dublin’s role as the HQ European Council for Fatwa and Research a dangerous precedent for human rights for women, children and civilians in Israel. Though this body has been in Ireland for 13 years there is not a record of any research material on this project. Rather everyone is amazed at the mosque where this organisation is based, not realising its origins as profits of Maktoum’s horse racing winnings.

Read the detailed account of Yusuf al Qaradawi and of the HQ European Council for Fatwa and Research and wonder why they have not received a summons under the incitement to racial hatred?
