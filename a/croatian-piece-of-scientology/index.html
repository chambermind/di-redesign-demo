Scientology

Members speak in support of Scientology, and those who have escaped say that this is a prison where they were brainwashed. What is the truth?  The Church of Scientology remains a controversial group since its foundation in the mid last century. Today it has about 8 million followers. Some experts claim that their beliefs are not controversial, because everyone has a right to believe whatever they want. It is a moot point! In many countries, the investigations have revealed that members were involved in numerous scams and that they manipulate people.

Informative magazine Checked (Nova TV) reveals all about Scientology – ‘religion’ which is only recognized in the United States, and which was established by American writer L. Ron Hubbard. Journalist Mato Barisic was talking with the President of Cisk  Branka  Dujmic Delcourt and with a few followers of this controversial Slovenian cult.

Although this is in Croatian it features our own John Duignan. Video courtesy of our fellow FECRIS member the Centar za informiranje o sektama i kultovima (CISK).

Everything these days we hear and see of Tom Cruise and Katie Holmes makes you wonder about Scientology. Are these reports true?! It begins benignly, as a self-help course, and ends up as a severe form of mind control where the individual loses their freedom. It began the 50-ies of the 20th century. Writer Science Fiction writer L.Ron Hubbard decided to share with the world of technology control and acceptance of the unconscious human mind. The developed technique called Dianetics is Scientology – a combination of religion and science.

Millions of followers worldwide. And millions of controversy. They themselves say they are victims of a conspiracy by pharmaceutical corporations, others argue for against and suggest that they are just Scientologists are Business corporations, not a real church. Its real and sole objective is money and profit. In Germany and Belgium over the last decade there have been investigations, and in France earlier this year a lawsuit  was succesfully concluded against the Scientology Organisation which found them guitly of fraud.

Branka Dujmic Delcourt president of the Center for information on sects and cults. ‘I read in a book that he bet with friends that will establish a religion and will earn bunch of money. So, the bet is won, ‘says Delcourt.

Church of Scientology  – robbery of the century or the path to happiness?

He won the bet and millions of followers. On the one hand there are those who are grateful for psyotheraphy techniques involved, like Joze and Mojca from Slovenia. But on the other hand, people like John, the Irishman who was in the organization spent 23 years. He fled because he says, because he could no longer endure its brainwashing.

He was involved with the elite organisation called the Sea Org and saw everything they did and said, most of the rumours about them are true. “You may not communicate with your family, nor anyone have a laugh or write. Any contact with the outsiders is with strict supervision. I was in solitary confinement for three weeks and they interrogated me for days and it is very serious and violent. This was my experience. They thought that I was working for the enemies of Scientology ‘, says John.

He says it all begins benignly, as a self-help course, and ends up as a severe form of mind control where the individual loses their freedom.
