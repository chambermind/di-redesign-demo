Tony Quinn’s paranoid claims come down to earth in a court room in the British Virgin islands

Tony Quinn

Following the report by Paul Cullen of the Irish Times which is a triumph for Irish journalism, as Tony Quinn has never been in a court before in his 40 years it is interesting to comment on some of his claims.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/06/22/tony-quinn-in-court-row-over-oil-firm-millions-by-paul-cullen-the-irish-times-friday-june-22-2012/

ALLEGATIONS OF covert surveillance, phone hacking and intimidation filled the thick tropical air as businessman and former lifestyle guru Tony Quinn began his evidence in a courtroom row over millions of Euro in oil revenues.

Perhaps this report would help clarify what happened here?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/06/11/dossier-sent-to-the-charities-section-of-the-revenue-commissioners-in-2009/

It has been noted before that Quinn is a technophobe and does not really use mobile phones or computers so one wonders how he has stolen the clothes of those who have been the victims of … covert surveillance, phone hacking and intimidation.

Mr Quinn, who flew to this tiny island by private jet from his home in the Bahamas for his court appearance, defended himself trenchantly against allegations of mismanagement by a former fellow director of International Natural Energy, Sheila McCaffrey.

INE has discovered extensive reserves of oil in Belize but has never paid a dividend to investors. Three of the individual directors and 74 of the 82 original investors were Irish and all had attended Mr Quinn’s mind power seminars. Those who founded the company have since fallen out and a succession of court actions has ensued, including Ms McCaffrey’s.

We are not concerned with the oil company but only with the former Educoists and victims of the behaviour of Quinn. Before there was oil DI was there and before a single drop of oil came out of the ground we had been hearing from victims.

http://www.nhssb.ni.nhs.uk/publications/social_services/Safeguarding_Vulnerable_Adults.pdf

The only conspiracy in reality is based on Quinn’s paranoia. “People are out to get him.”  No we are not out to get him but to bring what has happened to people, the break of families, alleged assaults of women, psychological trauma,  PTS  and financial ruin to the public domain. We sought to have him speak at a Conference over ten years and instead of responding he actually attempted to get his flunkies to misrepresent our intentions.

http://dialogueireland.org/dicontent/a2z/tonyquinn/index.html

http://dialogueireland.org/dicontent/resources/conferences/2001conference.html

http://dialogueireland.org/dicontent/resources/video/tv3tqpiece.html

The court heard that INE spent almost $2 million in 2008-2010 on security and surveillance. Mr Quinn said he asked the company for security protection after fearing that he might become a victim of a “tiger kidnapping”.

This claim is just bizarre and is totally delusional. In my opinion it represents a delusional figure clutching at straws. Do people read the forty years of facts we have archived?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/tony-quinn/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/01/18/the-tony-quinn-story-%E2%80%9Cfrom-butcher-to-oil-slick%E2%80%9D/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/02/16/summary-of-media-coverage/

 He claimed he was followed constantly for two years and subjected to intimidation and surveillance.

Followed by whom and for what purpose? It is well known he refuses to give interviews and was using ex Army Rangers to attend his seminars here in Ireland.

He said “things were being pushed up over the walls” of the house, a Martello tower in Malahide.

The facts are completely at variance with this claim. When people were trying to serve a summons on Quinn there was no post box at his residence and there is no intercom or door bell, so how is one supposed to not so much gain entrance but to be responded to?  Here is one example:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/01/19/nailed-to-the-courts-by-nicola-tallant-sunday-world-17-january-2010/

Also take a look at the photographs of his home in Ireland and answer how,

“things were being pushed up over the walls”

We were still able to deliver a card in 2009 but by the next year the mail opening was sealed to resist a summons being delivered. In fact in 2010 they had to be nailed to his door and with no other option some likely threw them over the wall, though it is very high as the photographs show.

 INE spent $130,000 on “IT penetration testing” while $192,000 was spent on “surveillance ops” in Dublin and London. Mr Quinn explained that its computers had been hacked and people were trying to listen in to board meetings at his home.

This is remarkable for someone who is a technophobe as mentioned above, it is more DR Who than Dr No! One can only wonder what planet this person is on with this wasteful use of funds. Chasing shadows and his desire to control was given free rein by his use of the company funds to avoid the consequences of his actions. He tried to hide his actions and his violation of many good peoples human rights by delaying tactics, stratagems hoping that  those who opposed HIM would be financially ruined while he remained in the lap of luxury.
