PRAY-PER-VIEW – By Jim Gallagher Sunday World, Sunday June 19, 2011

House of Prayer

Christina’s TV audience as cameras zoom in on UK mansion

pdf:

TV3 and Eng house

TENS of thousands of TV viewers will see the fabulous English mansion owned by House of Prayer guru Christina Gallagher this week. TV3’s current affairs programme Midweek travelled to the tiny village of Knockin, near the Welsh border, to film the astonishing home uncovered by the Sunday World in March. Money-mad ‘visionary’ Gallagher bought the magnificent property – it has seven bedrooms, seven bathrooms and a seven-person hot-tub – for a whopping £1.8m two years ago. It is now on the market for a massive £2,250,000, which would earn the 58-year-old grandmother a healthy £450,000 profit – well over €500,000. TV viewers on Wednesday night will be able to see for themselves the luxury in which this “humble housewife” likes to live. And the programme will ask just how can a woman with no formal income and no job afford such a palace. The millionaire mystic thought this astonishing property in the rolling hills of Shropshire would never come to light. She registered it in the name of her son Brendan, a humble plumber in Knockmore, Co Mayo, who could never afford such a home. The house brought to SIX the number of luxury private properties the Sunday World has linked to Gallagher, a woman who insists she has no interest in materials things and would happily live in a tent. Three years ago we shocked the nation when we uncovered the fabulous €4m mansion where Gallagher lives in plush Malahide, north Co Dublin. jim.gallagher@sundayworld.com
