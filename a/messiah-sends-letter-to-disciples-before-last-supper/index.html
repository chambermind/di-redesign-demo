Messiah sends letter to disciples before last supper?

Tony Quinn

A letter from Quinn has been sent to us concerning his visit to the RDS, Dublin this Sunday Jan 9, 2011.

From_Tony_January2011

We intend to write a commentary in bold to give the public a better understanding of the underlying values and philosophy of Quinn.

January 2011.

Dear Good Life Club Member,

Discover how: This could be the best of times for you.

I believe that just by us spending a few hours together you will have a better future.

THE SELF HELP SEMINAR

This year so many of you advised us to stay in the Bahamas. We were told the weather here in Ireland and England is frightful. You would be mad to come back.

( You may remember last year Quinn refused to accept a summons and in the end it had to be nailed to his door. How do you write to this fellow, he does not even have a mail box, or a bell to ring. The great communicator in deed. Also he has a big day in court to face in Nevis next week. Not a good idea to mess with the judge again!)

Yes, I did consider staying put. Then I thought about seeing all of you. About coming out on that stage and seeing a sea of bright, smiling faces. Then the standing ovation I always get from you, just for showing up. Is there any of us that would not come back for all that? To all of you a heartfelt thank you – but it also makes me all the more determined to make our day together worthy of your attendance. I am sitting here thinking ‘I know exactly what our day should be about but how do I do the theme justice in just a few sentences?’

(He needs to be worshipped and it is only the disciples who believe in his philosophy of UPM (Use other peoples’ money,) that know how to give him a high. He will be brought by high speed to the event and will come in like a thief in the night through a back entrance.)

WORLD CHANGE

‘The only thing constant is change.’ Clearly, everything today is changing faster than ever before. Most of us feel that we, our loved ones, and our lives are threatened by circumstances beyond our control. We may feel that we all face a future that’s uncertain, even frightening. We are no longer sure that either our leaders or we ourselves know how to make it all right again. People are panicking.

(This expresses his own thinking. I have escaped for so long from the law, can I make it to my 65 birthday?)

IS THIS THE PERFECT TIME FOR YOU?

This could be where you come into your own. Is this your time to shine? We have all attended the Seminars, listened to all this ‘Educo’ stuff. Now comes the time to put it all into practice. No, not just for you but for everyone.

GETTING REAL

How many times have you heard me quote ‘You and your world are composed of your thoughts. The wise person controls their thoughts.’ [Buddha] Maybe you did not put much store by it before but this one quote reveals to you the cause and solution to all the problems. The Truth is you are better prepared than anyone. You know what to do to be a mind master. This involves taking back control of the building blocks that form you and the world, THOUGHT.

(His belief system:

1. He is the Christ of the inner circle

2. He is an impersonalist, his believes in THOUGHT.)

OUR LIVES ARE AT STAKE

Surely with what you have learned you can see we have lost control of thought? One little word ‘Recession,’ and the thoughts of what it means, strikes fear into people’s hearts. Everywhere you hear ‘We are in the grip of recession’. Surely by now you realize the outcome when the recession program is so emotionally impressed on everyone’s mind? [Formative Learning].

Then our minds are set to run on the autopilot of recession. You know, I don’t think it’s too dramatic to say that it’s like an evil spell that has been cast over the world. You may have heard me say, ‘We are both the product and cause of the state of the world.’ Read this carefully, are you sure it’s good to educate people about recession?

(So that person in Malahide who lost their job, or that woman who was murdered is really the cause of the effect that occurred to them. Get it?)

NO CONTACT WITH REALITY

Because we live in the so-called information age (Misinformation?) it is easier to sway people’s minds almost instantly on a global level. Thought programs quickly spread to become the global way to do things. You are considered behind the times if you don’t get with the program. Look at this world-changing snapshot as an example of what has happened. In the Ireland in which I grew up, people had great resistance to buying things on what was called the ‘Never Never’. You got something like a TV and paid a small sum over a number of years. ‘Never never’ arose because it seemed like you were never done paying. Eventually you would hear ‘Everyone is doing it.’ More and more you heard the slogan ‘Buy now, pay later’. Soon we began hearing, ‘People are living beyond their means’. This was only the beginning where people were encouraged to live a lifestyle which in reality they could not afford. Credit cards were introduced; banks competed to give out loans etc. Some people were ‘Up to their eyes in debt.’ No longer was it for small sums. Part of the common program was that people believed and felt they were entitled (and were encouraged to do so), to have a certain standard of living. Countries took the same approach and borrowed vast sums [money was no longer linked to gold reserves.] On the subject of vast sums, I would ‘dearly’ like to know how much the world has spent on information technology such as the mobile phones, computers, big brother, etc. Have they helped the world and its economy? The bottom line is the more thought / information departs from reality the greater the danger. As you know, I call this ‘sheep think’ and as you know sheep lose their wool and their lives.

(Here Quinn tries to make his own fear of the modern world and his belief in the gold standard sound like critical and valid observations. Rather he talks about the old Ireland where we could not pay as the ‘Never Never.’  Rather this a summary of his philosophy of personally being a miser who never likes to spend or to pay for services he requires. As I wrote the other day he does not have the ability to enjoy wealth. He is a technophobe, apparently incapable of using the information technology such as the mobile phones, computers he decries. If you have an image of him with a laptop writing this, dismiss it from your mind. Think more of a person writing a few ideas down and others being paid or more likely  not paid to produce this broth. The image you should have of him is him ringing from an old rotary phone. Malahide 1947!)

NOW IS YOUR OPPORTUNITY

Like never before, people need your help, they need to know what you know. You always wanted to make a difference. Now is your time to shine, to be an example, inspiration and leader. Now is the time to put into practice what you know. Let us meet and put a plan in action. We can help everyone you know and love. Right now, make this your New Year / Life Resolution.

(His resolution is go to the RDS and then head for the airport so the Feds don’t get to him. Make it your New Years resolution to not wait for Paul Marriot ot Maire Lalor or anyone else to take action. Head for the RDS and make such a noise outside that like the walls of Jericho the building comes down.)

THE END OF THE SOCIAL MODEL (Sorry, but I called it.)

People are engulfed in dark thought. Even some of you may be finding it hard to overcome the darkness created by others. We accept society’s programs without question. Now we have the rude awakening of finding they are not working. People have been asleep in a false sense of security. For those of you who are familiar with my business structure you will remember that I called this the ‘social model’ and said it had to fail. And now it has.

(The social model is that where your neighbour has blocked pipe you give them water, where you have snow outside your house you move it, where litter is left you gather it and dispose of it. This model is like a honey comb, each cell is inter related and works for the good of all. The individual suppresses their ego to work for the common good. Quinn’s model is like a box of marbles as soon as you open the box they run wild all over the place. It is ego and self, self, not the other.)

In a nutshell, the social model is where you live in a cocoon looked after by social workers.

(Notice his disparagement of society. He confuses the workings of a genuine democracy with a kind of welfareism? He will claim he supports the poor in Belize with the  Charityfunction at Kilmainham etc. In reality he uses other peoples money and has nothing for his own people. He is leech on society and has destroyed many families, marriages and businesses.)

There is not the space to cover it all here but it is a very expensive, self-destructing model.

(There is no space, as there is nothing to show for 40 years of doing what he does. Please others use the comment section to tell us what you have experienced.)

A NEW AGE OR A WAKE UP CALL

People who consider themselves spiritual love to talk about the new age. Well, welcome to the new age. Times are not a-changing, they have changed. From now on everyone will have to be productive. In the social model you get to take out more than you put in, for a time (the welfare state, big brother, etc. — like a business or country where you take out more than you take in, then run on credit and bank loans). The so-called success people appeared to have was largely an illusion. An illusion based on extended credit and loans. You may be aware that I have had an input into hundreds of businesses [over 100 in the university study alone.]

(This is rich from someone who has never done a productive days work in his life. All this stuff about businesses. Usually successful people came to him, but had some weakness he exploited. Some even believe that it Quinn who made them successful when it was they who made Quinn successful. Could we see the peer reviewed material for this [over 100 in the university study alone.]? )

People continually show me business plans. When I apply my business structure to their plan, often the readout I get shows that the person can’t do business. They are doing what I called Creative business, in that the apparent success is based on credit and loans. Once this dries up what’s left is not a pretty picture. Quite simply, you may get the false notion that you are good at business when the credit tide is with you. Now that the tide has turned against you if you are still swimming strongly, then congratulations, you are a good businessperson.

(He so abused this type of person and used creative accounting of the tale?)

PRODUCTIVITY

You don’t need me to tell you that things are going to get worse. What can you do? Become more productive. This will involve growing as a person. You will no longer be able to leave your fate in the hands of others. You will have to learn and do new things. You will have to discover new resources within yourself, see and take new opportunities. Quite simply a family, business or country are all the same. The productive people can only carry so many. Then Self -Help must of necessity come into practice. We all must up our level of productivity to meet the challenge. The social model produces the opposite in learned helplessness and sheep think. The media often refer to me as the ‘Self-Help Guru’ and that’s exactly what everyone must now learn (if you haven’t already): to help your self.

(THIS SUMS UP HIS PHILOSOPHY -HELP YOURSELF TO OTHERS MONEY.)

SPIRITUAL GROWTH AND HUMANITY?

A few weeks ago I was going into London on a train. All around me people are on their computers and phones. Talk about fiddling while Rome burns. People really are disappearing into these things. Truly asleep in a thought-created unreality. Call a business on the phone: can you get to talk to a person? Indeed are you still talking to the people in your own house, even when out to dinner, with people on the phones and the kids playing video games?

(See how unhappy he is with the information world, he is totally unable to deal with it. His view of Children  shows he has no idea of how they operate. Also the internet provides one of the greatest tools to his opponents to defeat his 40 year reign. Will they buy his blarney or will they click on the mouse and play their part?)

Can’t you see that we are losing contact with and no longer participating in our own lives? Our lives are crying out for our attention and we are losing our ability for Self-Help. What is happening to our Evolution as Human Beings, Self -Expression, and our Spiritual Growth? Could this be the root of the problem? Is life giving us the feedback that we are going in the wrong direction?

MIND / LIFE MASTERY

The basic building blocks of you and the world are thought. Just by controlling thought you can be at any level. It’s what I call manipulating the variables. Even you need to ask yourself are you controlled by thought?

(Here is at this Thought stuff again. Just give the idea of thinking straight for yourself a try and give his thoughts a miss. They are costing you your autonomy.)

Then you will be the voice of thought and the means of spreading its programs. Thought is a tool. Your most vital self-help step is to know this to the point where you take control of thought. Then you can see through all the illusion and know which to do. You can dismantle the darkest thought to live in Reality as your Real Self. You can quickly begin to discover the Truth of this just by culling back your thoughts. In addition you can build the lifestyle you want by creatively controlling your thoughts. My aims are that by the end of our day together you will be more rooted in reality, truer to your self and know how to be productive.

The media have made much of the fact that I personally have been successful. That’s good news for you because as a friend of yours I am more than happy to meet and give you any tips or advice that I can.

(The media are not saying he has been a success but rather are questioning how he got his hands on the loot? They are gradually getting to understand that is because he is using mind games to overwhelm people who come under his spell.)

We may not have the addresses (or new addresses) of some people who attended the Seminars.

(Here he is actually not talking about pen and ink addresses, but email addresses and mobile phone contacts. He is asking his followers to try to make one last effort to bring in the old disciples. If not he could go west for good.)

Therefore please help and encourage all past participants that you know to attend this most vital Seminar day and provide us with their contact details. Together let’s make this our best year yet.

With lots of love,
