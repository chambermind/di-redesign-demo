A Christian reflection on the Prosperity Theology

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

Our story – from an anonymous blogger who came face to face with the prosperity teaching…..

Our experiences of abuse happened before we went to a prosperity teaching church otherwise known as P.T., so we actually went there with our eyes wide open and ready to do some classroom style learning. We were not emotionally connected to it as we weren’t there long enough and hadn’t been able to share in a way that allowed us to be connected which reveals the nature of how micro managed it was. We saw God was going to use this experience to teach us many things.

Regarding the abuse we did experience. For a while we were left reeling and hurt. We had left the relative safety of the Catholic Church in England and therefore any support with it and we knew absolutely no one outside of what we were going through here being foreigners in a foreign land so to speak. We can look back and see the blessing in it as we were forced to look only to God for answers. What we did with the experience was humble ourselves before a mighty God and allow Him to show us what biblical forgiveness is; what following Him and the joy of healing truly means. Since then I have learned that when you pray for people who cause you anger and pain, it has an amazing effect or your own faith and wellbeing.

We did learn after we left this place of abuse and before the P.T. church that the pastor had been reported previously to their hierarchy and like us, others had been advised to go to the P.T. church as an alternative but this time we went there with our eyes open. Looking back at the overall picture I can see that God has been teaching us that to be spiritually dead is to not know, reject or add to God’s truths through His scriptures. P.T. seemed to have the right motives and all the right smiles and temperament in place. It was organised to a fault, but something huge was wrong and it took the best part of a year to figure out what. That was because God was working on our understanding at the same time. It seemed confusing to us that whilst God was personally asking us to leave everything and come out of the world and follow Him, the opposite was actually been played out in the church right in front of us largely because of the prosperity teaching and the way it was presented. We had conversations with people explaining what God actually meant by prosperity and what His seed really was but they were lost in the explanation that the seed is God’s word and NOT our finances. We told someone on the team that God could actually be found outside those four walls and that with a step of faith and less homage to their leader they could bring Christ’s message to the streets. We had a polite talking to by the pastor for that one!

It would be prudent to explain at this point our story in coming to Ireland. In England we had security, a very nice house in a pretty village in England and a secure job at a large University which my husband had been in for 26 yrs. We went to the local Catholic Church. The children were in excellent schools and socially every need was met. BUT there was an itch in our faith walk that simply would not go away no matter how much we scratched it. One day we knelt before God and laid ourselves bare and poured out our hearts. To this day I marvel at what He said to us. “Leave, sell what you have and follow me” Us? We decided to test the waters and the following week we put our house on the market (during a house slump!) It sold the next day. My husband approached his boss about leaving and was immediately given a redundancy package. Ok Lord, so now we have the wherewithal we need direction. How we came to be looking at the west of Ireland is anyone’s guess. It wasn’t any more off the map for us than anywhere else and we were comfortable in England. We had a short time to find a place to live so my husband came over for a week, stayed in a b&b and did everything in his power and knowledge to find us a place to rent. It was summer and there was nothing and believe me the best of Irish hospitality did its best to help us. It was the day before he was due to come back home and he had one last look in the papers new release and saw two houses (only two!) somewhere in the middle of nowhere. He drove out and on the way to one got lost and stopped to ask an old man wearing a flat cap who was fixing his wall for directions. The man said “You don’t want that house you want mine” As it turned out the house my husband was looking for had been rented and the second one turned out to be this one belonging to Harry. God bless you Harry and all your cattle which tend to run amok every other day.

This was a huge step of faith to leave everything we had, all we knew to follow God into the unknown, literally.

To sum up the last 12 years. God’s first words to us on arrival were “What are earthly houses worth, there are dark hours when we will see nothing what better than to wait for the sun to shine” God began stripping us of all our carnal desires and taking the focus off of ourselves and putting it onto Him, He was taking us through the scriptures and stripping away the dross, bad teachings and faulty understandings. He was opening our eyes to the truth of His body, the “called out ones”, the ekklesia. We had stepped out of the relative comfort of our local Catholic Church but whose teachings we had had many problems with for a long time and stepped into the unknown. Now we were introduced to “church” systems that were foreign to us and had many problems of their own. The couple we attended were teaching about wealth and prosperity and telling all who would listen that God wanted to financially bless us all but privately God was showing us the futility of worldly wealth and the blessings of Him as our provider of just enough of anything and only when needed. These churches were consumed with the need to get numbers up and seats filled using a worldly system. God was showing us that He was leading many like us, all over the world in a different direction where the ekklesia was not necessarily confined to buildings, did not need mammon to sustain itself and today this has become a phenomenon of huge proportions and where many are willing to sell what they have to help each other without the glare of a prosperity teaching. The body of Christ is alive and well working in ways only He knows. The couple of churches we attended had various forms of ‘spiritual’ manifestations that were at odds with God’s teachings ranging from the more well known ones like hysterical laughter and twitching, compliments of Toronto blessing, to falling over at the snap or push of a finger ‘in the spirit’ which briefly I felt obliged to do so as not to offend the person doing the pushing but that was until God told me it offended Him so I stopped. We were invited to go on ‘spiritual maze walks’ and ‘pushing imaginary walls down weekend’ and meditation days in a Toronto blessing style in one place which we declined, all in the holy spirit of course but we could not find any biblical evidence for this and realised many were torturing scriptural verses to control the masses. Things took a turn for the worse when we came across some that were using power and authority in the name of “This is my church and you will do what I say” to control beyond what we knew was acceptable and at a time when God was showing us that the ekklesia was His and not man’s. We were learning that the Holy Spirit was more than capable of leading His church. That the verse was apt which said ‘See to it that no one takes you captive by philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the elemental spirits of the world, and not according to Christ.’ Col 2:8 That God’s house was being abused by people who were not who they said they were or were blind themselves. Unfortunately, the P.T. church was one of these examples for us. As we understood it they, like others were misusing the role of pastor as a tool to rule over people and bring in heresy like word/faith prosperity gospel messages but we weren’t hearing very much on the subject of Grace and repentance and neither were we being walked systematically through the scripture verse by verse. We were getting tired of hearing about the pastor and his wife’s mansion in heaven complete with a swimming pool, apple pie and a big car. Instead we were learning that the role of the pastor was as a shepherd. The pastor, teacher, healer or one who prophesies, the ones gifted with helps or interceding etc were no less or more important than the other. We were all equal before God and were gifted so as to serve the church. God is the one who has supremacy. This was becoming less evident the more we went on and in fact what was being built contrary to God was a Nicolaitan system which was straight out of the world system and didn’t resemble a biblical pattern at all. By Nicolaitan I mean a system that represents those who establish a ruling class over the rest of the people in the ‘congregation’ (ekklesia), by exalting fleshly leadership and developing a ‘pecking order’ something God clearly says he hates. So our time there came to an end and we quietly left. That was nine years ago. The rest of our story is in the process of still unfolding and I’m sure we still have much to learn and share. We are aware that many are hurt and are being hurt by their experiences in these churches and we pray God will put us in a position where we can all help each other. We have a desire to contend for the faith and use the medium available to us to do so. A word of hope to anyone who needs it is that God’s grace and Mercy extends beyond our experiences. Take comfort from this verse “I have told you these things, so that in me you may have peace. In this world you will have trouble. But take heart! I have overcome the world.” John 16:33We are comfortable in the knowledge that we are exactly where God wants us.By faith we live each day. The following website was timely for us and there are many good articles to help you. http://www.truthforfree.com/ (I haven’t read the whole site so be a good Berean)
