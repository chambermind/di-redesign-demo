THE DIFFICULTIES OF TONY QUINN’S DISCIPLE

Phoenix Articles1 click to open:

GURU Tony Quinn recently learned that the Free Spirit beauty salon chain, run by devoted follower Tina Fearon, is in serious difficulty. This is the latest bad news for Quinn, who is currently being sued for alleged sexual assault, battery and fraudulent misrepresentation by a former devotee of his, Maire Lawlor, who had worked for the Quinn organisation for over 25 years. The first Free Spirit store was set up in the IFSC in 2002 by Dundalk-based Fearon. The IFSC Free Spirit had accumulated profits of  140,000 by the end of 2007, while other salons opened in the Dundrum Town Centre, Sandyford, City West, Drogheda and Belfast. Fearon is also a franchisee of Tony Quinn’s Educo Gym, running three of these, and she explained to the Sunday Business Post in 2008 that she was “looking into” opening up Free Spirit salons in Dubai, London and Beverly Hills. Sadly, however, not even all the positive thinking that comes with being a Tony Quinn disciple could save Fearon’s businesses from the Revenue. Earlier this month, it sought court orders to wind up the Free Spirit operations in Citywest, Drogheda, Sandyford and Dundrum. The Free Spirit holding company, Free Spirit Beauty Group Holdings Ltd, was listed for striking off by the Companies Registration Office earlier this month, for failing to file accounts. It’s unclear if Tony Quinn is an investor in Free Spirit. (He is not listed as a shareholder in any of the companies’ annual returns.) However, in an “uncensored” interview on Educo’s website “Dr. Tony Quinn” does mention that he is “involved” with the Free Spirit companies, and Fearon gives Quinn a gushing testimonial explaining that his Educo training seminar was the key to her financial success. Goldhawk hopes that Fearon’s current difficulties won’t shake her faith in Ireland’s favourite multimillionaire lifestyle guru.
