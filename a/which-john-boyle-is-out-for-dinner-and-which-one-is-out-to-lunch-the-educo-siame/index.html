Helpful Comment from Angie added:

John may sound like he is coached as a lot of what he says is out of context in ordinary conversation. He repeats, verbatim, what Quinn incessantly repeats on his seminars. Added to these words is a feeling of euphoria, and like Pavlov’s dog, the action/verbalisation increases the feel good factor; the reward. Due to intensive brainwashing these words are embedded on his brain, repeated automatically and the worst of it is he thinks he’s sending a vital message to the world and Quinn hopes that others will want that vital difference in their lives. John’s previous thought pattern got hijacked and his cognitive faculties are out to lunch when confronted by information that requires processing.

I have met other quinnites whose conversation is very limited; usually connected to Tony and how great he is. In fact, a lot of them could not make life decisions unless it was approved. They were convinced that nothing could happen unless lots of ‘energy’ went into their request. It encouraged dependence on Quinn’s services whether they could afford it or not.

In other words, John and other followers, are enthralled by the ‘wisdom’ of the oracle, hang on it’s every word regardless of content and, with just a little ‘switch’ in their mind, swallow and automatically regurgitate when required. They are hypnotised into accepting this change in perception and believe it will ‘make this world a better place’. The ridicule they inevitably meet is brushed aside as ignorance of the masses who are there to be indoctrinated to fulfil the oracle’s desire for power and money.

Sadly, those who make the ‘vital difference’ are left high and dry to cope with their inevitable downfall.
