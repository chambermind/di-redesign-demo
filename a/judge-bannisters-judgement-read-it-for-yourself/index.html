JUDGE BANNISTER’S JUDGEMENT: READ IT FOR YOURSELF

Tony Quinn

Just around the time when the BVI Judgement would have been due to be published, that is a few days ago, mysteriously the www.eccourts.org   site has become unobtainable on the internet.  This is a crucial time when Susan Morrice is attempting to get the shareholders to ratify her and Quinn’s past actions which are condemned in the Judgement – allocating shares to Quinn without a payment given to the company, appointing him a director illegally, spending the company’s money as their own – and keep them in charge of the oil for the future.  The shareholders’ inducement for this is an immediate payout, in defiance of the Judge’s opinion that no dividends could properly be paid until it is determined whether Quinn’s shareholding is valid or not.  So that you can read the Judge’s own words on these important matters, as the East Caribbean Court’s website has become unavailable, we are putting up the Judgement here.  We will let you know when the Court’s website becomes available again. We will then link to the official judgement.

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments.html#recent_judgments

All we have is a pdf copy of the judgement currently but because we are familiar with a DOS attack as happened to us in 2009 we believe it is in the public interest to publish the judgement without delay:

Judgment July 16, 2012-3

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

DI is seeking permission from Justice Bannister to put up the judgement of the Commercial Court. We have temporally taken it down as we might be in breach of the Court’s rules. We are having difficulty getting through on any medium to the BVI. What we do know is that Susan Morrice and Sheila McCaffrey have both made statements following the judgement. Also that Susan Morrice has sent out a copy of the judgement. That means that all the shareholders have now got a copy. However, Ex Educoists who do not have shares are still in the dark in regard to the judgement, as are the general public with an interest not in the inner workings of an oil company but in the exercise of influence the proper focus of this site.

We have since spoken to our legal adviser here in Ireland and consulted a court official on BVI who indicated that there should not be any problem in publishing the document as the copy signed by Judge Bannister was sent out for publication on the court website for general pubic release more than 10 days ago. We would of course if contacted by the Court remove it if requested.
