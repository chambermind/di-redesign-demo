Report on the INE court case from our cult watch correspondent on Tortola, British Virgin Islands

Tony Quinn

The actions in Tortola in the last weeks have been boldly taken Tony Quinn to a pinnacle of exposure.  The raft of oil money that should be flowing down to Irish investors was shown instead to be clearly lining his pockets.

The presentation of Tony Quinn with his entourage of Eve ( Emile Hattiagh) and Mary Power arriving by private jet on the island set the scene for what was to come.  The entourage of Quinn also included Josh Stewart whisked though the emigration arrival with a team of security looking personnel.  Speed was important to the busy Guru; he had interrupted his plans to hold court at his seminar in Monte Carlo for this court appearance.

The sun-bleached hair, clearly dyed blond for the occasion seemed quite unruly at times.  Most striking was the appearance of the orange glow from him especially his hands.    Quinn clearly showing his 68 years appeared slightly stooped.

The deceit and the dishonestly of Tony Quinn and his manipulation of those closest to him was exposed to all. But riding above all of that is the control that he has over people.  Those concerned are so entrenched that they cannot see what is clearly in front of them.

The fact that Tony Quinn in his delusional world came to court in Tortola and tried to argue his case from the witness box after flaunting every rule in the court procedures was obvious.  The great Guru and his great business structure came tumbling down the more he tried to explain.

Asked about his seminar names- HE could not name the basic level in detail, the advanced levels name was also elusive.  It seems saying “Mind Master” in a court of law is difficult for him.   His lack of ability to describe the Business structure in any detail other than red, amber and green codes which been given to people to describe what level they had achieved.  It appeared as if these were badges given out to attendees at his seminars, it could even have been based on the number of seminars you attended.

As for basic reading skills the guru looked awkward and uneasy dealing with simple questions about his ability to understand an email communication.  It could be his lack of IT and telephone skills which is well known- he is a technophobe.  His best communication is done through seminars and on phones one could deduce.  Seminars give you the opportunity to talk at people and bore them into hypnotic trances.  Well he tried that in court as well, and got extremely frustrated when he the great Guru was not allowed to speak.

The evidence however did speak, it was loud and clear.  Millions to him for Educo seminars, carefully carved out between companies and individuals.  Millions more on security as he is endangered.  What those threats were seemed vague. No basic detail.  Nicola Tallant’s attempt to interview him in Monte Carlo back in 2010 clearly upset him.* ( Open link above.)

Then asked about Round Table and White Knights he admitted that he loved that story and told people about it on his seminars.  He went on to state that he had a model of the white knights on his round table in the Martello Tower at Malahide in Dublin.  This ‘French château like building’ as it was described by him in the witness box was where the knights stayed close to the round tower.  However, his memory cells did not stretch to him understanding that two companies White Knight and Round Table Management  received large consulting contracts and almost a million dollars between them.  He of course did not know any of this.  It was all quite shocking to him.

The legal indemnifications that he has in place from the Company appear very comprehensive. They include indemnification against legal actions from private parties in Ireland and newspapers.  Questioned hard about this he described it as ‘normal for a director’.  There were many other normal things that you would expect a director to know and understand that clearly eluded him.  The basic principles of reporting it appears in the Quinn rule book of corporate governance that giving information to members and reporting  accurate and timely information is not a requirement.  Also changing the rules that govern the company and not telling anyone makes it ok, as long as the majority of the board agree.

After three days on the stand, this guru that parades himself as being so knowledgeable about business clearly knew nothing.  This guru that is a ‘Dr’ was reduced to a Mr as his documents were false and unverifiable.  This guru has ripped millions from shareholders – his followers and his only answer is that.

Having money does not make you a bad person.

The case goes on live in Tortola, BVI
