When we receive well thought out and clear views from either side of an argument we make them into posts. We have had generally self serving misrepresentations of scripture and churchmen hiding behind the law. They have failed to view this from the perspective of the victim and from the standpoint of the gospel message which they claim to represent. DI is human rights organisation which tries to protect vulnerable adults, so we only reference scripture from the perspective of informing the debate not because we are advocating a particular view.

Elma O’Callaghan wrote:

For those of us who work with vulnerable adults or children the directive is clear in the statement of intent. It is to protect them from harm. This is from external as well as internal forces. This means that by intent, we are in a position of power and with that power comes a responsibility and a issue of trust. Any breaches of those essential qualities should result in forfeit of any rights to leniency. It is not about being Christian or about being sinless or about the act being a one off occasion or about doing the time for the crime. It is about the sacred breach of trust and the abuse of power. The victims are the ones who are violated not the one who did the violating. When someone signs on for such a path where vulnerability is paramount then the rules are explicit. Emotional outbursts are moot. This man should have put his hands up and declared his guilt instead of using his middle name to confuse people. He should not have allowed himself to be placed in a pastoral position again. By doing so he not only proved to be of low moral character but his ego spoke loudest by declaring himself above the vulnerable policies which seek to protect. His is a predator and he will continue to be so until he is caught, again. Until religious organisations implement strict; open, honest and clear guidelines that seek to protect the vulnerable from within as well as without, the faith in them will continue to erode and the result will be self destruction and extinction.

It is mind boggling to get a grip on media outlets so frightened by libel that they can still write that the case against Willi Stewart is ‘alleged,’ and seek to actually question if the case actually took place?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/11/08/bbc-norfolk-reports-resignation-of-willi-stewart/

“A Norfolk vicar has resigned after information about a past alleged offence was posted online.”

Even when you publish an eye witness account and have witnesses there on the day it does not seem to stand up? Libel rabbits caught in the lights. Also a newspaper in Beccles was approached in June of 2011 and sat on their hands. They have the cheek to print this when they have had all this information since last year and I phoned them a month ago. Give us a break!

“a campaign focused on a matter from his past”

What campaign? This is most supine deferential stuff I have ever read.
