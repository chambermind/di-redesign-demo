SELF-SUFFICIENCY AND SUSTAINABILITY ARE KEY TO EVERYTHING?

Tony Quinn

Susan Morrice:

We know you only too well and what you have written here demands correction – you’ve left out a few truths too.

It reads as though just you and Michael Usher were the ones who found the oil- not true Susan Morrice, there were a group of other well respected, professional people around to guide you in this venture, without whom the company would not be where it is today.   There’s no miracle here Susan Morrice, it’s good people doing their job, and you have flaunted this and are trying to take credit for others’ diligent work.

‘Self sufficiency and sustainability’ indeed are the key to everything, and you and your Guru Tony Quinn have become very ‘self sufficient’ through the benefit of shareholders’ money.   Your ‘sustainability’ is brought about by continuing to mislead not only the shareholders in the oil company (who gave you a job) but also the people of Belize.

The Never Delay field was kept a secret from shareholders, and if we are to believe the Hon Mike Espat, from the Belize Government too.

You are using the oil company as your private bank account – you claim we have made all these profits and that you have distributed $10 million in shares – and yet no shareholder has received any dividends – oh yes, but they have been allowed to borrow their own money, meanwhile, you, Guru Tony Quinn, Patricia Usher and Josh Steward have pocketed a few million dollars among you.   And let’s not leave Gilbert Canton out of this big hand out (if you are shy about disclosing the amounts, I can help you with them).

You spend corporate money as if it’s going out of style – we (the shareholders, without notice or approval) paid for your ‘all-expenses-paid’ jaunt to  Caye Chapel where you and Guru Tony Quinn hosted Said Musa and other Government officials – and what a bill that was.  Would you care to share this information – again, if you won’t I certainly can.

You don’t mention Guru Tony Quinn in your article – after all he is the one announcing in Ireland, heavily supported by you (remember your tearful, emotional presentation on January 10 in Dublin) that he was responsible for the oil find.

At least you two should get your ‘stories’ straight if you’re going to burst into print.

And yet again you are misleading the reader regarding the education benefit for students in Belize – these aren’t grants, they are loans.  If the children can’t afford to go to school, how on earth can they afford loans? Your ‘self sufficiency and sustainability’ doesn’t hold up here.

…and please don’t quote Guru Quinn to us (‘if you love life and get on with it, brilliant things will happen’).  The oil company has given enough to Guru Quinn (yet again without shareholder knowledge or approval) – shares to the value of $24,000,000 – yes you read that correctly – we’re talking millions.  And let’s not forget your nephew Josh Steward who walked away with a few million in shares and dividends – you took the cake of course.

The ‘energy resources within you’ Susan Morrice and your Guru Quinn are in the form of dollars out of the pockets of other people.

It would be really good if you could write an honest and correct report Susan.  Remember many shareholders have been with the company for quite a few years and we KNOW what is happening.   You don’t get it Susan Morrice and Guru Quinn do you, but it’s all catching up with you now and no matter how much ‘spin’ you put things, it’s all going to come to a full stop very soon.   That’s the way the cookie crumbles!

BNE/INE Shareholder

Upsides – Thesis – Susan Morrice

SELF-SUFFICIENCY AND SUSTAINABILITY

ARE KEY TO EVERYTHING

Upsides, January 2010

Realising a dream of striking oil in Belize represents the triumph of belief over scepticism and is transforming the economy of this small nation. Over the past 50 years, no less than 50 oil companies have spent millions of dollars unsuccessfully searching for oil in Belize. Nevertheless, my native Belizean business partner, Mike Usher, and I believed that oil existed. We set up Belize Natural Energy (BNE), with the support of Irish and US investors, and purchased the drilling rights to 500,000

acres of Belize land. Mike Usher sadly passed away before the project could reach fruition. His legacy lives on, however, as on 24 June 2005, the first anniversary of his death, we struck oil with our very first exploration well. As if this wasn’t miracle enough, the following four wells also produced oil. Since then, the total number of oil-producing wells in the Spanish Lookout field has increased to 10, together producing around 5,000 barrels of top-quality Light Sweet Crude a day. In addition, we’ve recently discovered a new field: Never Delay. This is particularly significant because it is logistically convenient to the country’s new capital city, Belmopan. Not

only will it provide cheaper electricity to the capital, but it will also create many new jobs within BNE   and its numerous surrounding service industries. The relationship between BNE and the   Belize government is a very special one,    in which self-sufficiency and sustainability are key to everything. BNE is now the country’s largest revenue generator. The Belize government receives a 7.5% royalty share of gross oil revenues, plus a production share component, a 10% working interest in the oil field and also a 40% tax rate. Up to 400 people work for BNE at present, 95% of whom are Belizean. In addition, new groups of entrepreneurs   have sprung up around the oil discoveries, providing services such as transportation, marketing and IT support.

Energy independence

A portion of the oil revenue generated is placed in a BNE Dream Trust: a joint venture between BNE and the government, gas. We’re also working on a simplified attracting international interest. The development of the country’s own propane and butane gas resources, as by-products, is very important to Belize; since most cooking is done using imported country’s ultimate aim is complete energy independence. One thing I’ve learnt over the years, apart from the value of a sense of humour, is that if you love life and get on with it, brilliant things will happen. If we   had listened to all the sceptics, we might never have dug that first well. We maintained

our belief and our discoveries have brought a renewed sense of optimism tothe whole country. We all have energy resources within us, in the form of our dreams. Like the oil, while it was always there, it takes the right mindset to draw it out.

Susan Morrice is a geologist, and co-founder and director of Belize Natural Energy (BNE). For the past 30 years, she has worked in the oil industry.
