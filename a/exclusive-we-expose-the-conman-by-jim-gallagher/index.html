EXCLUSIVE WE EXPOSE THE CONMAN BY Jim Gallagher

House of Prayer

Sunday World November 15, 09

EXCLUSIVE WE EXPOSE THE CONMAN BY Jim Gallagher

Man who runs American House of Prayer for Christina is a convicted fraudster who conned his own company out of millions.

Rooney1

Rooney2

FAKE visionary Christina Gallagher’s main American fundraiser is an ex-convict who took part in a $15million fraud, the Sunday World can sensationally reveal. John Rooney, who is the official owner of the self-proclaimed holy woman’s €4-million Malahide mansion, spent time behind bars for his part in a massive interstate scam in the US. The 65-year-old fraudster pleaded guilty in a Texas court to a con-trick which cost his own employers at the time a whopping $10 million. Rooney, a father of four, was sentenced to 18 months in prison when he appeared before the US Federal District Court in Galveston on April 4 1988. He was locked up in La Tuna prison – convict number 38220-079 – outside El Paso. He was released in June 1989 and then spent time in a half-way house. The conman subsequently declared bankruptcy and his family was forced to sell their home and cars while he was behind bars. Rooney spent the next few years living with his family in rented accommodation in Washington State before moving to Steubenville, Ohio, in 2001 where he now runs the House of Prayer Empire in America for Christina Gallagher. He met the millionaire mystic on a trip to Ireland in 1998 and has been her American link ever since. Former supporters of Gallagher’s controversial House of Prayer on Achill Island will be horrified to hear of Rooney’s criminal past.

Humble

The revelation comes just a fortnight after the release of an explosive new book, Immaculate Deception, by this reporter, which lifted the lid on Gallagher’s own money-making scams over the last 20 years. It revealed how she turned from a humble Mayo housewife with no formal income to the millionaire head of an international organisation – and how she had snapped up luxury properties for herself and her family. The book has sparked massive media attention in Ireland.

Now many former devotees will be shocked by the knowledge that they handed over

huge amounts of money to the control of Gallagher and ex-jailbird Rooney, allegedly

to build the chain of six houses of prayer across America and Mexico.

Many elderly donors in Ireland were brainwashed into handing over tens of thousands of euro but were asked to make out their cheques to John Rooney. Soon after, Gallagher’s magnificent home was bought Rooney’s name. One couple, Michael and Betty Morrissey from Co Waterford, signed a cheque for an incredible €50,000 to Rooney in June 2005 and now assume their money went straight into buying Gallagher’s house. Last night Mrs Morrissey said: “I am just so shocked by this news. We thought John Rooney had probably been hoodwinked by Christina like the rest of us. “This surely must be the final blow for the House of Prayer.”

The Sunday World can reveal today the full story of how John J. Rooney ended up behind bars. At the time he had been a senior vice president of the Guaranty Federal Savings and Loan Association in Galveston, Texas. In 1987 he was charged with two separate indictments in a month. One was for making false entries in his company’s books before he resigned to start his own consultancy business.

Bonuses

The court heard he aimed to defraud the firm of several hundred thousand dollars by making false entries to boost his own bonuses. He was also indicted with the two other men by an Illinois federal grand jury of falsely obtaining loans that resulted in losses of more than $15 million to savings and loans associations in Texas, Kansas and Louisiana, including a $10 million loss to his own employers. The men presented false documents to obtain loans for coal mine reclamation projects but used the money for their own purposes.

The court was told that Guaranty Federal announced losses of over $10 million in bad loans and named Rooney as the man responsible.

Rooney, who had since moved to Mercer Island, Seattle in Washington State, pleaded guilty to the fraud in a court appearance in February 1988 and was sentenced in April by Judge Hugh Gibson. Today Rooney works as a legal head hunter, signing up attorneys for law firms across the US. A Sunday World check into Rooney’s business shows that he could not have afforded to buy Gallagher’s stunning home on the luxurious Abington private estate in Malahide, even though he is named on the land register. His company, RFC Consulting, which is based at his home in Saint Joseph Drive, Steubenville, Ohio, employs just himself and has an annual turnover of €130,000 (€87,000). He set up the company 19 years ago, according to official records. His house is worth around €200,000.

Charity

He also runs the Confraternity of Our Lady Queen of Peace, Gallagher’s House of Prayer organisation in the US, from a Steubenville post box number. He is listed as president and secretary and latest figures show the charity had assets of $6.7 million and income of nearly $3 million. The Sunday World called to Rooney’s house last year to try to find out why his name was on the land register for Gallagher’s house and to ask him where the money for such a magnificent mansion came from.

But the Irish-American exploded in rage and threatened to call the sheriff if we did not get off his property. He has refused to talk about the finances of the House of Prayer ever since. One of his business associates told the Sunday World last night: “There is absolutely no doubt in my mind that he will be making money from his links with the House of Prayer. “He is real right wing Catholic and I know he was obsessed with Christina Gallagher at the beginning but there is no way he won’t be profiting.” A family friend explained yesterday how Rooney first met Gallagher while on a trip to Ireland in 1998. She said the whole Rooney family had become obsessed with Christina Gallagher. Rooney’s children had all married non- Catholics but the partners had all converted. “He has turned his whole family into a cult. He has got them all worshipping Christina Gallagher. “He is a brainwasher. He can bulls**t a bulls**tter.

“But he is a smart guy when it comes to money and has access to every attorney in America. “I think he believes she is the real deal. He certainly did at the beginning anyway although I don’t know if he has seen through her now.”

Devout

The friend said the Rooneys were comfortably off but would not be rich enough to buy a €4 million mansion for Gallagher. Rooney’s devout Catholic wife Joanne did not work, said the friend. “I knew them before they met Christina and they had a normal Catholic life. Then he met her and it was all about money. ‘We have to raise money for Christina!’ The kids were not particularly devout but they are now.
