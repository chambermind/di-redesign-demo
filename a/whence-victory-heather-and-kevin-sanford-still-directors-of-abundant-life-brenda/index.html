Whence Victory, Heather and Kevin Sanford still directors of abundant Life? Brendan Hade of the IMF.

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Victory, Heather and Kevin Sanford…………………………………………………………….. Brendan Hade?

Our recent exposés of the acquisition of Abundant Life by Brendan Hade and his Victory organisation has raised a lot of questions about churches in the prosperity movement and their roles as not only churches but as businesses and registered Limited companies. This dual representation of a church as both a religious charity and limited company has created a conflict in the internal mechanisms of how these churches operate. What we see with people like Brendan Hade is a move away from the biblical representation of a pastor as that of a shepherd to what we actually see in these churches which is the Pastor operating as CEO and Managing Director.

With friends like this who needs to fly Aer Lingus?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/11/19/brendan-hade-ceo-of-victory-in-take-over-of-abundant-life-ltd-galway/

In the Abundant Life example we see the members being treated as assets in a balance sheet, happily being traded by Kevin to Brendan. These unfortunate individuals are looked upon more like share options rather than people. Kevin was more than happy to transfer his “stock” portfolio to Brendan Hade but questions have to be asked, why? Much like company shares, dividends are paid to the owner of the shares out of company profits. In this case Brendan knows he is entitled to a guaranteed 10% dividend from his new shares in Abundant Life. By this we are referring to the tithe from his congregation. Of course many faithful members will give over and above the 10% dividend so we can be sure that Brendan will get to enjoy many bonus payments outside of his guaranteed dividend scheme.

This monetisation of the Gospel by Brendan Hade does not just stop at fleecing the sheep for dividends. Consumed with mergers and acquisitions to increase his revenue streams via his new Galway branch he neglects his duty bound biblical obligations to his own congregation. A further look at his Victory church raises further concerns about the commercialism he has instilled into what should be a church. In essence Victory is not run as a church by Brendan but as a corporation. In a business one of the most valuable assets is the property itself held by the company. In this case we see Victory focusing on their building as a revenue generating property rather than a sacrosanct tabernacle. Brendan Hade seems to ignore the bible passages that focus on this problem

Matthew 21:12 – “Jesus entered the temple area and drove out all who were buying and selling there. He overturned the tables of the money changers and the benches of those selling”

Of great concern is a website that Victory currently has to showcase the church property. http://vcc.ie/ This website focuses solely on the commercial aspect of the church. We are treated to descriptions of restaurants, recording studios, cafes, VIP rooms, theatres, etc. What we have here is something more akin to a shopping centre than a church.

http://vcc.ie/media/BROCHURE2011.pdf

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCQzInIB8Qc

To add further insult to injury, nowhere on the commercial website for victory is their premises referred to as a church. It is presented as merely a corporate venue fully available for all types of activity and its primary purpose as a church is completely and purposefully hidden from prospective customers. One has to ask why Brendan is so keen to hide that the Victory Centre is a church. Surely as a minister of the Gospel, his primary focus should be to propagate the Gospel by any and all means. Instead we see him hiding the fact that the Victory Centre is a church to increase his revenue streams. It is a shocking turn of events to see a preacher brush the Gospel under the carpet from his prospective business partners and customers. Brendan seems content to gloss over the scriptures that urge him to let his light shine before men and is very happy to turn off the light switch at the right times with his business partners. Many customers of the Victory Centre are simply unaware that it is a church and Brendan keeps it that way so as not to offend and drive off potential business. We even see from the past events listed that the church has hosted Jedward concerts! I wonder what God would make of 2 teeny boppers shaking their “booty” on stage in his holy temple to the screams of adoring girls? I’m sure Jesus would happily use the same whip he used on the money changers in the temple across John and Edward’s booty.

Brendan’s church might have the X-Factor now, but it is certainly missing the Jesus-factor.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/07/21/resurrecting-religion-by-alex-meehan-sunday-business-post/

Another case of not wanting to offend people with the inconvenience of a Gospel message so as to garner extra finances. One also has to question why an organisation like Spirit FM would allow an entity like Victory to infiltrate it and promote its own profane movement on the air.
