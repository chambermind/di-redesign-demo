11 December 2012

Silent Prayers for new Mosque by Siobhán Maguire ~ Sunday Times 9.12.12

Religion

Here as with most reports on this mosque it is not what is reports tells us but what it does not tell us? The issue of the call to prayer is a sub text for who is behind this? Is it developer led or are there real Muslims trying to integrate into Irish society? We will post further on this as it is important that people know who they are dealing with. Also the group behind it, Dublin Welfare Society Limited are based in Clondalkin but have no contact details other than a postal address. They seem to have no activities, no contact with other Muslims as Ali Selim from the Clonskeagh Mosque says in the article they had no knowledge of this group till they saw the planning proposal in September? Also in the planning proposal they claim the following:

http://www.dublincity.ie/AnitePublicDocs/00384346.pdf

Section 1.8:

1.8 Strategic Objectives of the Islamic Cultural Centre are listed below:

  • Providing quality services to the Muslim Community in Ireland.
  • Assist in stabilising the Islamic presence in Ireland to have a positive and acceptable effect.
  • Striving towards the development of a noble and active Muslim generation in Ireland.
  • Addressing all misconceptions that distort the image of Islam.
  • Building bridges with the wider society in order to portray the true understanding of Islam and its humane values.
  • Confirming the unity and universality of Islam through proper coordination with the local and European Islamic organisations.

Here follows our commentary:

Providing quality services to the Muslim Community in Ireland.

This is a laudable goal, but when no other Muslims know anything about it is that not strange? A Muslim friend of mine says it smacks more of an apartheid project than an integrationist one. Could it be as we suggested more to do with dealing with the Irish property crash than  the actual perceived needs of an imaginary Muslim community?

Assist in stabilising the Islamic presence in Ireland to have a positive and acceptable effect.

How does this assist the Muslim community to stabilise. They like all Irish people have experienced the shock waves of the crash and most would have established themselves with homes and mortgages etc. Only very rich people could buy into this project, but it clearly is not based on any needs that have been established. The only part of this project that would be of interest to Muslims would be the schools proposals.

Striving towards the development of a noble and active Muslim generation in Ireland.

            This is apple pie type stuff and is pious aspiration rather than researched needs.

Addressing all misconceptions that distort the image of Islam.

By not even explaining who the Dublin Welfare Society Limited are, by not having a spokesperson to outline this project, by not even telling their fellow Muslims what is going on they are certainly onto a loser. You can’t have a misconception when you do not have a conception.

Building bridges with the wider society in order to portray the true understanding of Islam and its humane values.

The only bridge likely to be built is by Gerry Gannon, these folks would not know what a bridge is.

Confirming the unity and universality of Islam through proper coordination with the local and European Islamic organisations.

Sorry another fail here, they have done nothing to inform their Muslim friends, nothing to inform Irish society and more importantly nothing to inform their neighbours once this project takes off?
