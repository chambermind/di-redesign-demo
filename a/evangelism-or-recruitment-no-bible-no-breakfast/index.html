World-famous evangelist Hinn draws hundreds

By Scott E. Williams

The Daily News

Published November 7, 2005

LA MARQUE — The appearance Sunday night of one of the world’s best known television preachers brought hundreds of people to a local worship center.

Abundant Life Christian Center’s annual “Campmeeting on the Coast” began Sunday, with a service conducted by world-famous televangelist Benny Hinn.

Hinn, born Toufik Benedictus Hinn in Jaffa, Israel, hosts the daily television program “This is Your Day.” Abundant Life regulars and newcomers said they looked forward to seeing Hinn, 52, perform miracles in person.

“When the power of God manifests itself, you want to be there,” said church visitor Sarah LeVrier.

LeVrier came to Sunday’s event with Bobbie McLaren, an Abundant Life member since 1997.

McLaren said she also came to the service because she had “seen Benny Hinn on TV,” but Hinn was not the only person she was looking forward to seeing. Her son, Kevin Sanford, would be one of many clergy members at Sunday’s service. McLaren said she had not seen him in months, as he had been involved in an international ministry that had taken him most recently to Ireland.

Abundant Life administrator Bob Story said exposing new people to the church was part of mission of the four-day “Campmeeting.”

“We’re here to edify and exhort the message of Jesus Christ,” he said. “People are bringing people who need to hear the message of Christ, and this is the church’s high season. It will energize the church for a whole year.”

Services continue today, starting with a 10 a.m. service by Pastor Walter Hallum. Tonight’s service begins at 7 p.m. and features another well-known evangelist, Jesse Duplantis.
