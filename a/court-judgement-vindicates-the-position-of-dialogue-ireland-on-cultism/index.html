Court judgement vindicates the position of Dialogue Ireland on Cultism:

Tony Quinn

Dialogue Ireland Statement following the judgement of Mr Justice Edward Bannister in a case brought by a founder of International Natural Energy (INE), Sheila McCaffrey, against Mr Quinn, the company and its chairwoman, Susan Morrice.

The Judge made a large number of detailed, balanced judgements essentially stating his judgement on the many disputed points laid before him – these statements are now no longer in dispute, his judgement stands on the public record.  The value of these judgements to those who are influenced by and/or fearful of the Educo leadership is in establishing that on the one hand the Educo teaching is that those trained in its methods have access to cosmic sources of wealth, whereas in practice the Educo leader Tony Quinn induces a company board to make commercially unjustifiable payments to him and to his co-Educo-ists and to grant him shares for which he made no payment.

The Judge’s comments on Tony Quinn’s business expertise and judgement are also extremely valuable from this point of view.

We welcome the Judgement from the British Virgin Islands High Court which gives the judgements of Judge Edward Bannister on the mass of evidence presented to him.

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

While our concern is not with the commercial matters of the oil company, we are committed as we have been since our inception to assisting those affected by Tony Quinn and his Educo organisation.  We consider the judgements which have been given on many points in the case will be helpful for those affected, giving an impartial and informed view from an experienced judge of the performance of Tony Quinn and some of his Educo colleagues in running an actual commercial enterprise in the real world.

We believe that this judgement vindicates the position taken by Dialogue Ireland which argued that cultism is at the heart of this case. We believe that undue influence has been exercised over people which has led them to be influenced by Quinn.

We have no interest in the competing claims of the directors of the oil company, but as our recent articles on Boylesports have shown there is a cultist thread which runs through this narrative.

We have great empathy for Sheila McCaffrey, Susan Morrice and John Boyle who we regard as having been the victims of this influence.

DI has been under continued harassment for over ten years. The link below gives the documentary evidence showing this.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/06/11/dossier-sent-to-the-charities-section-of-the-revenue-commissioners-in-2009/
