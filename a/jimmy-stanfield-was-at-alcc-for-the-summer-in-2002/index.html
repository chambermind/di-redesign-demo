Jimmy Stanfield was at ALCC for the summer in 2002

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

DI was approached by a former participant in Abundant Life. He had read our blog and was happy to post this testimony.

My name is Jimmy Stanfield and I was at ALCC for the summer in 2002.

What I remember most about the place are the young vibrant new Believers who characterized the church at that time.  I also remember the strong families in the church…….I recently learned that Kevin Sanford left Ireland and returned to Texas. This was a tremendous surprise to me.  I looked online and discovered this site and began to read the story of how all that came to be. Without going into events at that church in the past, I can only say that since I wasn’t there and haven’t heard the Sanford’s side of things so it would inappropriate for me to comment on any of those issues.

I am writing to express the tremendous burden I feel for people in abusive church situations, not only in Ireland, but worldwide. If what many express in the comments section of this website is true, then I want you to know how to overcome it. To be honest, I feel that ministering to people who have been abused by power hungry clergy can only be helped by the theology of grace.

Biblical grace teaches us that the abused need not feel guilty or feel like God is mad when power hungry church leaders punish them for merely “offending someone”.  Neither should they feel badly because they were kicked out of the church in an unscriptural manner by a perverse misuse of biblical church discipline.  Therefore, I want to address the subject of biblical discipline.

There are numerous comments on this website claiming that people were banned or kicked out or run off from the church for such vague reasons as talking against the church, or for having a wrong attitude. If this is true, then you need to know a few things. To begin with there is a legitimate use of biblical church discipline. We see one of best examples of this in the New Testament in 1 Corinthians chapter 5.  Briefly, there was a couple in the church in Corinth who not only were living together without being married but were in such an incestuous relationship that a man was sleeping with his father’s wife-most commentators say his stepmother! This is an example of gross and open immorality and how the church dealt with it.

1 Corinthians 5:1-5

1. It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and

such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that

one should have his father’s wife.

2 And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath

done this deed might be taken away from among you.

3 For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged

already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done

this deed,

4 In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together,

and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,

5 To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh,

that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

Notice the CHURCH was charged to cast the unrepentant member out AFTER giving him a chance to repent of gross and clear immorality (this responsibly or power was not to be wielded at the sole discretion of a pastor or presbytery, but the local church entire); the offenders were not kicked out of church for refusing to sell vitamins or being late to babysitting for the pastor’s wife or any such nonsense.  Legitimate church discipline is ONLY applicable in the case of actual and unrepentant sin, not simply the occasional, “oh well the Pastor’s wife doesn’t like this person’s vibe”. Again, I’m not saying those things happened at ALCC, but if it did, then that is not legitimate biblical church discipline. If that was really happening, as the comments on your articles suggest, then those people need to know that they were kicked out of the church by a severe misuse of the doctrine of church discipline. The Bible does NOT give the authority to a pastor to cast people, who have not committed an actual recognizable sin and been given a chance to confess and repent, out of the church. Finally, notice that in 2 Corinthians Paul commands the local church (that would include their Pastors) to go to that person and receive him back into the church lest he be “swallowed up with overmuch sorrow” (2 Cor.2:7) Biblical church discipline is always aimed at restoration to the fellowship of saints.

Did that happen in your case? Did anyone go to you out of concern for your sorrow? Did the church make any attempt to restore you to fellowship? I feel for people who have suffered such abuse. It does happen at times and if it did happen with this church, then they need to know that such a misuse of church discipline was NOT of God. Let me speak to you who may have been abused.  God is not mad at you because you got on the wrong side of a Pastor or didn’t show enough enthusiasm or anything like that. In fact, ALL of God’s “mad at you” ended at the cross and your faith in what Christ did there is more than enough to cover any sin you commit! Brethren, we don’t throw our fellow Christians away. Sometimes through ignorance or malice the church authorities can reject you but we, the biblical church, the universal body of Christ, following the example of the scriptures, do not reject our fellow Christians. If you have sin in your life then quit fooling around and repent of it; receive your heavenly father’s forgiveness and be restored to the fellowship of the saints!
