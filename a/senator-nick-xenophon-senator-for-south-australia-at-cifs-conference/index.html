Senator Nick Xenophon, Senator for South Australia at CIFS Conference

CIFS QLD

PO Box 4002

St Lucia 4067 Qld                                                                                                                              January 18th, 2010

RE: Reminder / Early Bird Registration closing for CIFS QLD National Conference.

Dear Sir or Madam,

Recognizing that Christmas and New Year holidays have been the focus for everyone in the past few weeks, I am sending out this reminder that the early bird registration will be closing on January 29th, 2010.  Should you need extra copies, the Conference Brochure and Registration Form are available on the following link: http://www.cifs.org.au/conference.php

The Cult Information and Family Support Group Qld have organized three professional speakers from the International Cultic Studies Association in America and several Australian speakers to address the subject of coercive persuasion and mind control.  We are also pleased to announce that Senator Nick Xenophon, Senator for South Australia will be speaking at the CIFS Qld National Conference on March 13th, 2010.  Senator Xenophon is an Independent member who has recently spoken in the Senate regarding the alleged abuses being perpetrated by Scientology.

The two-day conference will include lectures and ex-member workshops and will be relevant and informative for psychologists, health professionals, counsellors, educators and ex-members.

The conference is being held at Parliament House, Brisbane on March 12-13th, 2010 and we would urge anyone considering attending this important conference to register early, as places are limited.  If you have any questions, please feel free to call or email CIFS QLD on 0413 082 344 or cifsqldnatcon@gmail.com

Kind regards,

Helen Pomery

CIFS QLD Member
