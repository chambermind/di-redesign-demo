RTE Primetime on Irish missionary abuse

Abuse by Church and Irish institutions

Plentiful coverage has been given to the issue of clerical sexual abuse in Ireland, but until now the abuses that have been committed by Irish missionaries abroad has not been investigated. Until now. On October 6, between the RTE News and Prime Time, RTE  issued a lengthy apology to Fr Kevin Reynolds about this programme. They confess that they totally libelled the said priest and totally withdraw the unfounded claims they made. They also acknowledged that they should have awaited  evidence in regard to the claim that he had a child with a Kenyan woman.

Update April 13, 2012.

DI awaits the BAI report, but has been considering other aspects of this case. Did RTE get it all wrong in preparing this documentary? Fr Reynolds was not the only person referenced in this documentary. If the other cases were accurately reported then RTE has a duty to reinstate the material that has now been taken down. If however, they have slandered others beside Fr Kevin Reynolds then that needs to be addressed as part of the response to the BAI.
