MMM 2000

Magnificat Meal Movement

Reflections after hearing Debra on Fri eve.31/3/00 Dublin

Debra appeared on TV3 morning news and then on Pat Kenny Radio programme last Friday. She had already advertised in the Irish Catholic under the name “TWO PILLARS”. She was very evasive in both interviews, and claimed she did not know what PC or adherent meant, which is interesting for someone who trained as a teacher.

It was with some irony that in her talk in the evening she referred to herself as being “unpolitically correct”?

Arriving a little after 7pm, I thought I would just slip into a seat to hear what Debra had to say. As you entered the Gresham Hotel you saw the notice for the “TWO PILLARS FOR 2000 IN ASSOCIATION WITH THE MAGNIFICAT MEAL MOVEMENT”. Obviously the comments of Archbishop Connell  Irish Catholic 30/3/00 had holed her boat in the water, so they attempted to confuse people by the new name. When I arrived there at 7pm which was the time in the advert., there were about 20 people there.

At about 7:50 a person called Colm Dunne opened the proceedings without even a word of apology for the delay, and Debra then spoke non stop for 1hour ten minutes.

On TV and Radio she came over with a sickly innocence,as if you was a bit naive. Now she still kept to that script, but it was clear she was working from a very well worked out strategy. At this stage she had an audience of about 100 of which 2/3 were her supporters and the rest Marian Catholics and people looking for some answers to questions.

Her starting point for the evening is that JP2 is our pope. She did not use her usual strategy of attacking the local clergy, but piggy backed on the pope to show she is following him, and that the faithful are just not getting the full picture! Her second point was that true salvation can only be ours if we have the right eucharistic practice and know the blessed mother. Not once in her talk did she mention Jesus Christ the true reference point for true Mariology, but in my view she came close to Mariolatry.

She made reference to a statement made by the pope on May,2 1999 in which he is supposed to have warned the faithful to be aware of false bishops and clergy leading the church astray. She made quite a lot of references to the fact that we are in the end time.

She then commented that JP2 was brought up in the Eastern Rite and that he had said,”that the only hope for the church lay in the Eastern and Melkite rite.

She then talked about the deranged people who oppose us. People who do not follow her advice= (the pope’s advice are like the people of Sodom and Gomorrah!

She then went onto outline how she started having visions when she was 17 every evening after dinner for 13 months, but of course the local priest said it was not.

She lambasted anyone spending money on a holiday or doing something to their houses. Please support two projects

1 We need to consecrate Russia to the Immaculate heart. She is now in 73 countries and wanted people not to bother with TV which is only filled with the poison of communism, which was the poison which killed Russia.

2 Her second project was to support the building of a Basilica to the BVM in Hellidon before the end time hits us.

She then spoke about the Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament as the pinnacle of Christianity, but unfortunately it is so poorly honoured in Dublin because people do not have access to the Eastern Rite. She only gets a high when she attends that rite, which is not like the happy clappy worship in the other churches!

JP2 is her pin up boy, and “I don’t mind if he is not clever”. Whatever that means?

She seems to shifted to an emphasis on the right rite if you will pardon the pun and sees the new rite as a Protestant influenced meal, rather than the sacrifice of the mass, which is interesting considering the name of her movement!

She appears to appeal to people not too clear about the new rite and still wedded to the old rite. This she does with her Adoration practices, and when they are in she feeds them with her own visions which are in direct conflict with the Church she claims to follow. Also she has won over mainly the elite from existing Marian movements here in Ireland, and has given them quasi religious status which in another age would have been channeled into a religious order

Sect that Condemns Mass

The Magnificat Meal Movement arrives in Ireland on Friday. The breakaway Catholic cult, with its leader, Debra Geilesky, is likely to attract sizeable crowds on a nationwide tour, even trough it preaches that mass is invalid and Catholics risk their salvation by participation in it.

The group is based in Australia and was at the centre of a suicide scare last September, when some followers said Geilesky was having visions that she would die on 9/9/99. She claims to receive messages directly from God.

Catholic priests in Australia say the ingredients for tragedy exist because of her dominant personality, and the hierarchical nature of the group, including the existence of ‘slaves’. Queensland bishop have urged Catholics to leave.  Rev John Ryan said: ‘I think lots of good people are trapped in there and deluded.’

One member is a 40-year-old professional, who sold her house in Dublin two years ago and moved to Australia. Friends suspect she has put all her money into the cult. ‘She has been completely taken over by the group,’ said one.

Many other Irish people joined when she did, but have since left. She was once very sound and level-headed. Now she thinks she is a prophet and having apparitions.

‘Please don’t name her or she could get angry and break off all remaining contact with Ireland.

Church steps up war with cult

Courier Mail (Australia), May 19, 2000
By Amanda Gearing

MEMBERS of the Magnificat Meal Movement religious group had excommunicated themselves from the Catholic Church and could not receive communion, Toowoomba Bishop William Morris says.

“Any attendance by Magnificat Meal members at Mass can only be understood as a protest against the Church and not as a genuine desire to pray with the Church,” Bishop Morris said in a letter to parishioners.

“In those circumstances, they could not possibly expect to receive communion.”

The Queensland centre of MMM is at Helidon, between Brisbane and Toowoomba, and is led by Debra Geileskey.

Two Toowoomba-based Sisters of Mercy who are active members of the Magnificat Meal Movement are under pressure to withdraw from the cult. The sisters, twins aged 83 with more than 60 years’ in the Sisters of Mercy, have refused to comment on their predicament.

But since the Bishop’s letter was issued, both have continued to attend MMM activities in their habits.

Bishop Morris’s stand against the cult has been backed by all Queensland’s bishops, including Archbishop John Bathersby.

They have asked all Catholics to withdraw from the Movement and its teaching.

In a joint statement, the bishops said they would welcome all who had aligned themselves with the Movement back into full communion with the Church.
