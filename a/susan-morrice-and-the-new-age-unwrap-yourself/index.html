This helpful commentary on the poem? by Angie is added:

There you have it folks, a poem in black and white, written by an Educo member who clearly believes he is a product of the ‘new man’. The psychopathic Quinn’s interest in developing occult abilities and use of hypnotherapy have bestowed on him the great honour of being recognised as ‘Christ’ the Aquarian who brings ‘light’ and ‘wisdom’ into the world.

The ‘new man’ of Educo has the ‘amazing’ opportunity, while his emotional health is put in serious jeopardy, to being conned into believing he is accepted and equal to ‘Christ’/Tony Quinn , who sits on a throne. To arrive at such ‘amazing’ heights in the Educo cult, all barriers for self-preservation have to be destroyed; “Broken through that mental cage” is testimony to the total disrespect Tony Quinn has and the danger he poses to people who attend his seminars. The author, under the influence of Quinn, is under the dangerous illusion that the natural thought process is an obstruction to Life. What has been glaringly obvious to me for a long time is that Quinn “rids them of their nuts and bolts”/ mental health, to facilitate the induced hypnotic programme; “and puts a lid on Pandora’s Box”/hides the awful truth of what is really happening; the emotional breakdown and the removal of the essential human cognitive faculty that, he believes, poses a threat of rejecting his hypnotically induced mind-set.

“Round and round the merry go round” of conditioned thought till “The truth hits the ground”; Quinn’s indoctrinated version of the truth takes over the thought process. Quinn’s desperate attempts to succeed show a gross inability to understand the damage he inflicts on his ‘foolies’. In his acceptance of the occult he believes the ends justify the means. His “Golden Age” is about accumulating wealth, no matter what the cost.
