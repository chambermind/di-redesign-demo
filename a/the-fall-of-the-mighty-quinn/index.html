THE FALL OF THE MIGHTY QUINN

Tony Quinn

THE CONTINUING  FALL OF THE SELF-PROCLAIMED MIGHTY QUINN 

How far are the “mighty” (in this case Quinn) fallen?  Quinn’s predictions from around 2008 that his troubles would be far in the past by now have once again failed to materialize and 2012 was something of an end of the present world for Quinn.

In particular, a string of disastrous courtroom ventures has led to his humiliation and the further destruction of any “reputation” he was trying to promote.  In court last July, Quinn was reduced to a rambling, incoherent figure with apparent major memory loss that, it seems, his Educo methods haven’t helped.  Quinn’s inability and unwillingness to respond meaningfully to court questioning and court procedures emphasized the image of the ageing leader caught like a frightened rabbit in the headlights of the court (and later in the Spotlight of the BBC).  The self-proclaimed messiah, had to endure hearing in court and subsequent reports in the Press of many revelations of serious misdeeds by him in relation to the oil company and its finances (using his version of OPM – other peoples’ money, his recommended strategy not that of Napoleon Hill from whom he took and twisted it) and towards its shareholders.  Quinn had to endure an assessment of his Educo business philosophy and techniques as trivial and vacuous by a learned Judge and many other humiliations including being revealed to be illegally gifted a seat on the board of the oil company and a huge gift of shares for no apparent consideration by Quinn.  Quinn had no answers as his pretence at a “reputation” was shredded in court.

It was a PR disaster of huge proportions for Quinn and a vindication of what Quinn’s critics have been saying for 40 years.  There had been previous court disasters for Quinn and many major critical exposes in the Press, which he has never managed to get retracted in his 4 decades of the cult business (see Dialogue Ireland archives on Quinn), but 2012 was really the crowning disaster.  Small wonder he’s now settled his outstanding cases (with OPM ? – other peoples money, we hope not).  The settlements all come with gagging orders as standard so he hopes there’s no more devastating revelations waiting for him in court or in the Press.  His guilt about further revelations and lack of confidence in his own cases when confronted with real opposition have caused the change of policy from attack, attack and attack your critics with lies and legal threats to a “cut your losses” and run for cover.  In hiding in the darkness of the shadows, he can get on with cult business as usual as the puppet master who runs things through his besotted women and broken men at the core of the cult.  His strategy to destroy Dialogue Ireland, previous methods having failed, by endless legal strategies (using his OPM strategy ?) had now backfired, as he slid on one banana skin after another in crises of his own making though obviously his “foolies” were given the blame for these.  Morrice’s frantic attempts to fight back with bizarre propaganda on behalf of Quinn led her to seriously propose, in writing, that the troubles were all because of alleged “lies by the Press, Dialogue Ireland and Spotlight” (the BBC programme that shone a very critical light on her and Quinn’s activities in the oil company).  Her own reputation and credibility were severely damaged in the process but somehow it’s all supposed to be due to nasty and “negative” people in Dialogue Ireland and the media.  She continues to spiral further into the cult mindset Quinn has induced in her and it seems nobody can enlighten her as to how she’s being played like a violin by Quinn.

In defiance of Judge Bannister’s advice Quinn still tries his cult manipulation methods to get on the Board of the oil company and through his besotted minions continue to run (ruin?) the oil company, as revealed by judge Bannister when he savaged Quinn and Morrice about they way they mismanaged oil company affairs.
