Katie clashed with Cruise over whether their daughter would be raised a Scientologist.

Scientology

Religious beliefs blamed as Tom Cruise

and Katie Holmes head for divorce

By Philip Sherwell in Los Angeles
Monday Jul 2 2012

A long-running conflict between Tom Cruise and his wife Katie Holmes over which religious upbringing was best for their daughter may have lead to the breakdown of Hollywood’s most curious relationship.

Even by the standards of celebrity effusiveness, Mr Cruise’s original on-air declaration of love was cringingly embarrassing.

Mr Cruise jumped up and down on the famous sofa of talk show queen Oprah Winfrey and professed his devotion for his new girlfriend, Katie Holmes.

That was seven years and countless red carpets ago — a lifetime in showbusiness circles, and also, it turns out, for the marriage of Hollywood’s most curious couple, sometimes known as ‘TomKat’ for short.

Last week, it emerged that Ms Holmes had filed for divorce — the final act for a union that had been as widely mocked as it was obsessively monitored.

Mr Cruise was apparently oblivious to her plans until shortly before her lawyers lodged the paperwork.

It was an unwanted early birthday present for Mr Cruise, who turns 50 tomorrow.

The couple have not been seen together for months, prompting speculation about the marriage. His refusal to leave the remote set where he is filming to celebrate his milestone birthday with his family was said to have been the final straw for his 33-year-old wife, with whom he has a daughter, Suri (6).

But an underlying cause of the split seems to have been a crisis of faith for Ms Holmes over her husband’s devotion to Scientology.

Ms Holmes, who was raised in a Roman Catholic, Mass-attending family in Ohio, had embraced the church’s strictures to marry Mr Cruise, her heart-throb as a teenager.

Daughter

But she is said to have clashed with him over whether their daughter would be raised a Scientologist. Indeed, Ms Holmes has reportedly applied for “primary residential custody” of the six-year-old.

Prior to the couple’s lavish 2006 wedding, critics of Scientology ran a ‘Free Katie’ campaign.

In the intervening years, friends said that Ms Holmes had become increasingly disillusioned with Scientology. In 2009, she insisted that Suri attend a Roman Catholic kindergarten.

TMZ, the gossip news website, said: “Our sources say Katie has never been fully committed to Scientology, even though she has had ties with the church. We’re told the couple had been arguing over Suri — that she’s now of an age when Scientology becomes a significant part of her life.”

It is telling that Ms Holmes chose to file for divorce in New York, where the couple have an apartment, rather than California, where their marriage was registered.

The East Coast state does not tend to award joint custody to parents squabbling over how to raise a child. Their home state, on the other hand, favours joint legal custody.

There have been other signs that all was not well. Just two weeks ago, Mr Cruise was lauded as an entertainment icon by his peers at the Manhattan showbusiness haunt, the Friars Club. The actor’s date for the night was Suri, not Katie.

Married bliss may not be forever. But for a Hollywood star, couch-jumping displays of love and adoring quotes live on in perpetuity. (© Daily Telegraph, London)

– Philip Sherwell in Los Angeles
