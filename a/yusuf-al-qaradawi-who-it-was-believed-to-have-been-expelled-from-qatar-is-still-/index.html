Egypt is ruled by thugs and killers: Senior Muslim scholar

By

Al-Masry Al-Youm

Yusuf al-Qaradawi, pro-Muslim Brotherhood figure and head of the International Union for Muslim Scholars, delivered Friday sermon in Qatar at Omar Ibn al-Khattab Mosque, where he said that the military rule is evil.

Qaradawi said that Egypt is currently under military rule.

 

“There is no longer democracy in Egypt,” he said.

 “Egyptians have been patient for 60 years and when they gained their freedom, they chose Mohamed Morsy to be their first elected president,” he added.

Qaradawi described Morsy as being the “first president who gave people freedom and democracy, allowing them to say and do what they wanted.”

 Qaradawi described the military as “oppressors who have no limits.”

 “Look at what they are doing in Egypt, Syria, Iraq, various countries,” he said.

 “Those oppressors have killed worshipers, fasters, pious people and readers of Quran who did not harm anybody.”he said.

 “The military, police, thugs, and snipers killed thousands in Rabaa al-Adaweya which was obvious injustice,”he said.

 “I swear to God I saw a sniper on a rooftop who chewed gum while shooting people like cats and dogs,” he added.

 Qaradawi described the current regime in Egypt as “thugs who kill people and steal their money.”

 “Their rule would not last,” he added.

 Qaradawi defended Al-Azhar University students saying they were defending their university, looted homeland, and democracy.
