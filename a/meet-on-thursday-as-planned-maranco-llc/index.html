Meet on Thursday as planned MARANCO LLC

Tony Quinn

“Meet on Thursday as planned.”

We already reported the short notice and the contempt in which the current leadership treat their shareholders. When they are clearly not doing their job they make unwarranted attacks on unnamed individuals. Hopefully now Ireland has cleared the election hurdle business commentators will begin to look at this issue. Ed

Please urgently circulate the below statement to as many INE Members as possible.

MARANCO LLC

PO Box 556, Main Street, Charlestown, Nevis

28th February 2011-02-28

QUOTE

I refer to the message issued in the name of INE on Friday evening in which the “Board of Directors” purports to call off the AGM which is currently scheduled to take place in Dublin on Thursday.

The message refers to alleged threats and intimidation as the basis for postponing the AGM.  No details of the alleged threats are given.  The message seeks to imply, however, that these alleged threats have been made by Maranco or on behalf of Maranco in the context of the on-going litigation in Nevis.  If that is the intended implication, then it is (a) totally baseless and (b) defamatory.  In the absence of any evidence of the alleged threats (for example, when, where and to whom were the alleged threats made and what is alleged to have been said), I suggest that shareholders should treat these allegations (and the context in which they are made) with deep suspicion.

I have travelled back to the UK from Belize specifically to be present at the AGM.  I understand that Sheila McCaffrey has also made arrangements to be in Dublin on Thursday.  In the circumstances, even if we as shareholders are to be denied the opportunity to question those who are in control of the company as to, for example, the recently released accounting data (which give rise to a significant number of issues), I suggest that we should nevertheless meet on Thursday as planned, as notice of a meeting has already been given.  I will be very happy at that time to report on the present status of the litigation in Nevis.

Regards

Paul Marriott

END QUOTE
