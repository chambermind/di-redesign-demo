Subject: Feedback on the recent INE communication
From: ineshareholder@gmail.com

To: ineshareholder@gmail.com

 Dear Members,

Good news from our Board at last. They have come to a settlement with Sheila! And we are to get our share allocation from the returned Maranco shares.

Unfortunately, as so often, the communication raises more questions than it answers. Let’s go through it paragraph by paragraph…

NOT all the legal issues have been settled. Rob Henshall is still being sued, and they have just taken out an action against the BBC!

Current asset value; This we still do not know, despite many requests. The real value of a company is in its actual assets, in our case proven reserves. We have no more prospecting rights, so what ‘potential’ extra value do we have?

Increase in shareholding; Good, it looks as though it is on its way at last. However, what about the McCaffrey shares- has the company bought them back, or is Sheila still a shareholder?

No mention has been made of the Quinn shares, which have been judged to have been allocated illegally. Have they been recovered, if so was payment made? Should they not also be distributed among Members?

Jean Cornec’s shares have, we believe, been bought personally by Susan Morrice. This, of course, gives her a very strong voting position on the Board.

Dividends; We were all very pleased to get our dividend last year, which presumably was for 2011. Or was it for 2012? Or is it going to be every September? The gross inequity of the non- payment of LRP to about a third of the Membership has still not been mentioned, and must be addressed before another dividend is released!

Harnessing the potential; The horizontal drilling sounds interesting, and might assist the extraction rate from Spanish Lookout. New World Oil and Gas have just completed horizontal drilling in Belize and it was unsuccessful. We have, of course, a finite amount of oil in our fields, the rate at which it is extracted is perhaps not as important as preserving the sandstone and reducing water ingress. Current high volume extraction has led to a water content of some 32%, I have been informed. This means there is degradation of the oil bearing strata.

We could certainly do with some positive PR, but that has nothing to do with the efficiency of the company.

Trading of shares; Good news indeed! Of course, any changes to the 2002 O/A must be ratified by the Membership, so we will be all be responsible for ensuring that any changes are appropriate to our common interest.

Investors; If and when our company becomes ‘clean’ with proper, unconsolidated accounts, regular AGM’s and unencumbered by legal actions, it may well look attractive to outside investors. We have a cap on the amount of shares that can be released without general agreement of the Membership, as decided at the 2005 AGM, so new investors would provide a welcome market for those of us wishing to sell our stock. We are aware of attempts in the recent past to sell off part of the company, and of offers by potential buyers, so we must keep a close eye on options for the best possible outcome.

Positive Public Relations. Yes, we have a good little oil production company in BNE, and we have to live down some atrocious management history by INE. It is in all our interests to get the company rehabilitated in the eyes of the public, and the business community. However, how that might open up increased value beyond proven oil reserves is an interesting question.

Creating the best outcome possible. We all have a vested interest in this, and we would all like to see our initial miracle become a benchmark for business in the petroleum industry. If we create a fair and democratic company with good lines of communication we can make that happen.

IN ADDITION

The AGM elected Class B members to the Board, but there seems to have been no response to this by the current incumbents, and Class B still have no voice.

The EGM, which was run strictly in accordance with the Operating Agreement and Robert’s Rules, seems to have also been completely ignored. A position statement on these matters would be very welcome.

We currently have one original Director out of five. We have an un-ratified replacement for Mike Usher, we have Josh who is still un-ratified. We have three Class B members ratified by Members but unacknowledged by the Board. This is a very bizarre and messy situation, not one with which potential investors would be happy. If we are to have a clean and marketable company, this situation must be addressed immediately.

Legal expenses must by now be in excess of at least two years dividend for Members. New actions are still being initiated! Compensation for Cornec, Lalor and McCaffrey must run to many millions. The company has to date never won an action.

Why was all this allowed to happen? We are being asked to stop the noise, but from past experience we have to scream to make ourselves heard, and even then we are ignored.

Susan’s five years as ‘Sole Managing Member’ has now expired, so we need to elect a new Chair.
