The House of Prayer in Achill has no Church approval……………

House of Prayer

Yesterday someone brought a little leaflet to our attention. It is about the Marian Shrine in Knock which is recognised by the Tuam Archdiocese.

A little card with prayers was found in a Church in Achill sound. It would be clear that if there were two sites in the same diocese who were both claiming to be Catholic a little rivalry would be normal. However, here we have a group claiming to be Catholic who are not, namely the H of P.

H of Pnot recognised

People it is true in smaller numbers are travelling miles to the place and bypassing the Real Presence in countless Churches en route? These are generally were nice people who have been hoodwinked by the Marian Mafia at the H of P.

When they get there they find a pseudo Catholic Priest Fr Holy Joe McGinnity who has removed the Blessed Sacrament from the H of P in 1998 in fragrant disregard of the Archbishop of the area. Consequently as it has no approval they can’t celebrate the central sacrament of the Catholic Church.

Talk about an own goal.

In other words good people are being led astray by a priest who is under the discipline of the Cardinal in Armagh. He has flagrantly broken his word with countless people that he would act to address the Elder abuse and the use of Marian and apocalyptic threats to get monies with menaces.
