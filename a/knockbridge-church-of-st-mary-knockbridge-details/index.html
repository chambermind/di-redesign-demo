Knockbridge Church of St Mary, Knockbridge Details.

http://www.armagharchdiocese.org/parishes/87

As it is impossible to reach Fr Gerard McGinnity those who wish to contact the Church can find all the details here. Most of the people don’t seem to know what is going on, and they have a real problem holding onto their phones. They seem to just go onto to the base automatically when you call.

So if you need to get your money back, it might be best to try to local TD Mr Seamus Kirk who is on the Finance Committee of the parish.

If you need to get some pastoral help you may have a problem in so far as they do not seem to know whether they are on the council or not. When DI contacted the Pastoral Council Chairperson,  Mr Eugene Caulfield, he was blissfully unaware where the Lone Ranger=GM was. Whenever Christina is mentioned it is like a firewall goes up. She must be ok if GM goes over there so much, after all we do not mind that he goes to a heretical and sectarian Marian Pyramid Scheme has the gift of bilocation and basically is getting subsidised by real Catholics to work with a dodgy number with houses everywhere. Don’t mention the war. Don’t mention the Elder abuse. Also do you not know if you disagree with him they send legal threats and like the Red Sea the seas open up for Holy Moses. How many hundreds of thousands of good peoples’ money has been spent on keeping the law in business. Use the big names, but get the smaller guys to do the threats. One person I talked to in August said in general people were sick of it all, but no one wants to rock the boat in this small country idyll. He has been double jobbing with Christina in Texas and giving his approval to her nightmarish apocalyptic messages. He should make an appearance at Knockbridge Church after another fix in Achill this Sunday.   Fr Gerard McGinnity  seems to have a very broad understanding of the role of the Parish Pastoral Council, and some of them do not seem know what actions he is taking on their behalf according to one parishioner DI talked to tonight. We would not say that the Parish Pastoral Council   =   Fr Gerard McGinnity  but it might not be to far off? I would ask the protection officer of the Armagh Diocese to consider GM as a case of a vulnerable adult. This what we wrote about it. In Northern Ireland there is a category in law which acknowledges the reality of vulnerable adults. That is not just minors, young people or people with disabilities, but anyone subject to undue influence, mind control and mental manipulation or mystical manipulation where a person is brought under control due to the misuse of Marian, or other theological criteria for the purposes of removing the critical function of the mind and rendering the individual senseless. I believe for the sake of his sanity the Archdiocese of Armagh should have an intervention done to GM to assist him to break his addiction to CG. He needs the fix of being a big fish in a small pond for his every day reality is that he was dealt some terrible cards of being a small fish in a big pond. His only relieve is that he is a Msgr. He was trampled on by the official church and he has never recovered, and CG has become his adoring mother in the absence of Mother Church.
