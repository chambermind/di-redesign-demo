4 November 2009

National Australian Conference in Brisbane. Letter from CIFS

Conferences & CommentariesCultism

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/10/07/national-conference-on-treating-victims-of-coercive-groups-and-destructive-cults/

Dear Mike,

Thank you for your email sent last month.  I am sorry that I have not replied sooner.

CIFS Qld is an associated group to CIFS NSW (Sydney), so we are just a different group of
people who are working in Queensland.  At present, we are very focused on the National
Conference that you have included in your information on your website.  Thank you.

We have two ICSA (International Cultic Studies Association) speakers coming from America
as well as several Australian speakers, so we are very pleased to have this opportunity to
address the cultic problem here in Australia.

I will read your thesis when I have time so thank you for attaching it to your email.  This past
weekend I have written letters to every politician here in Australia, informing them of the
National Conference and making some points about the situation here in Australia.  In my
letter I quoted the responsible work of the French Government to address these issues and
I was particularly interested to hear of the recent ruling against Scientology.

Once again, I apologize for the delayed reply to your email.  Thank you so much for writing.

Kind Regards,
Helen Pomery

CIFS Qld Member
