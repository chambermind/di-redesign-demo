Cardinal Brady and the House of Prayer: How long O Lord?

House of Prayer

The Catholic Church in Ireland is currently reeling from the affects of child abuse, youth abuse, and historical abuse cases. However, there is another issue that does not receive much public attention. DI has been privately bringing up the subject of Cardinal Brady’s inaction in regard to a priest in his Archdiocese. Fr Gerard McGinnity. This documentary aired in 2008 raises very serious questions about the role of Cardinal Brady in this regard.

We have received reports that he has received endless delegations from a cohort of family members of parents and grand children of members of the House of Prayer. He has promised action but has delivered nothing. What we are hearing is that many older people involved are experiencing elder abuse. Using magical manipulation Our Lady and Jesus Christ are referenced as the source of what is a Marian Pyramid Scheme. If the Archbishop had only heard about this a few weeks ago it would be one thing, but to refuse to act after such evidence has been shown and for such a long time is very serious. The personal enrichment of Christina Gallagher, the evidence that cheques were made out to a priest of the Archdiocese of Armagh raise very serious moral issues.

Archbishop Neary of the Archdiocese of Tuam has been quite clear that the House of Prayer is not recognised as an authentic Catholic organisation and that it has no standing in the Archdiocese.

Armagh on the other hand knows this, but has allowed a priest in good standing to collude with and to actively support a schismatic web site this so called House of Prey. We understand that Fr McGinnity was a courageous priest who brought to the surface the fact that the President of Maynooth, Michael Ledwith’s behaviour was at variance with Christian morality. https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/11/03/the-connection-of-mcginnity-and-ledwith-on-the-pat-kenny-show/

Instead of being commended for his courage he was first exiled to Rome, then to a role in St Patrick’s School Armagh, and finally to a backwater in Knockbridge, Co. Louth from where he acts as a mentor and supporter of this fake visionary.

http://www.voiceofourladyspilgrims.com/FrMcGinnity.html

The continued lack of discipline and fragrant breech of canon law are leading the Catholic Church into a greater compromise and the faithful are becoming sickened.

We can only conclude that though Archbishop Brady has made a formal apology to Fr McGinnity for the wrongs done to him in regard to the Ledwith case, McGinnity must hold information that makes it hard for him to be treated with the same accountability that any other person would have received.
