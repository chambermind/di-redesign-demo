An Irish visit – November 2005

Unification Church

Report for Dialogue Ireland of an observer who obtained a pass to join the event, but wishes to remain anonymous: Comments follow from Mike Garde of DI on this text, also in bold

After a performance of ‘My Lagan Love’ (for the north) and ‘Pleasant and Delightful’ (for the south) by three late-teen/early adult girls, proceedings began with a ‘Holy Juice’ ceremony led by speaker Tom Walsh,

Who is Dr Tom Walsh?

Additional comments in italics by the observer 7/11/05:

Dr Tom Walsh was billed as the Secretary General (whether this is of the Unification Church, the Inter-religious International body, or the Universal Peace Foundation, I don’t know), and I talked with him briefly (he was a bit cold and kind of rude to me actually!) at the VIP reception. Said he is in Moon’s entourage and has travelled the 57 cities with him. I said that you must be very tired. Got no response. Maybe I deserved it! He said he’d known Moon for over 30 years (I think). He gave a brief welcome speech at the VIP reception and then took the stage upstairs where he conducted the ‘Holy Juice ceremony’. He’s pretty tall and balding – looks a bit like Jack Charlton or Farmer Hoggett from ‘Babe’!!!

Who led a couple on stage in the ritual. Drink the first half of the shot-sized cup thinking of your wife/husband, and the second half thinking of yourself. This ceremony was enacted by all standing, and accompanied by dramatic chorus music which finished with a big ‘Amen’ and applause.

Sheikh Sitthardhi ….- Is this Sheikh Dr Shaheed Satardien who is on the programme or SiraqZaidi? The former is a moderate. The latter is a radical Shia who was at the meeting till the bitter end.

Additional comments in italics by the observer 7/11/05:

The Muslim speaker was an Arab and did not have long hair – under the white head scarf it almost looked like he didn’t have any hair. He had been sitting in the middle of the front row. I am 99% sure it was Sheikh Dr Shaheed Satardien (I wasn’t sure about the spelling of his surname at first, got it right after checking the name list on the programme. I am practically sure that it was Shaheed Satardien, now that I think of it again. As for him being on the invitational list in error, he and Tom Walsh, and then Dr Song, embraced very warmly and publicly after Shaheed’s speech! He obviously knows them well enough!

… took the stage, and delivered a speech praising Moon and the movement’s efforts towards “establishing a society characterised by world peace”. He said society is “practicing spiritual apartheid because we don’t want to accept certain faiths”. Made a big deal of the considerably moot point, in an attempt to be locally relevant, that “it is wrong to assume that all Catholic priests are paedophiles, that all people who go to Mass condone it… It is not true”…..

This statement makes me think it was Shaheed as he made the same point to me when I suggested on Thursday that he would compromise himself if he took part in this venture. He told me his name was on the Invitational Committee in error, however, when we saw the programme for the night on Friday 4; he was listed as a VIP speaker.

He repeated this later. Stipulated briefly that ‘Moonie’ is a derogatory term. “What other faith today is spending all of its resources… in the Middle East?” “Who bought a Garden of Peace (for conflict in the North) but the Unification Church?” Said that the best two visionary quotes he had heard were from Dr Tom Walsh: “Dr Walsh emphasises the culture of heart”

and Rev Quirke. Who is he?

Additional comments in italics by the observer 7/11/05:

Just realised that Shaheed was referring to “Rev Kwak”, not Rev Quirke as I had thought previously. Rev. Chung Hwan Kwak is chairman of the IIFWP and the Interreligious and International Peace Council. He is also chairman and president of News World Communications, which owns The Washington Times and United Press International.

Rev Song then spoke, commenting that we were at a “turning point in human history… a crossroads in God’s providence”. The world is “in a situation filled with bitter suffering… God will not tolerate this any longer”. “When we meet Rev Moon tonight, we meet truly the King of Peace… (Who) gives of himself 100% for God and humanity… the embodiment of the principle: ‘live for others’”. “Fr Rev Moon is the central point of the global revolution of heart”.

Then followed a video, billed as documenting Rev Moon’s life and work.

-Universal Peace Federation, founded 12/9/2005

-A mechanism for co-operative peace-binding efforts

-One global family under God

-‘creating dialogue… a movement of God being directed by a man’

-String music, dramatic voice over

-‘can be likened to the dream of Martin Luther King’

-‘people who changed the world have not been reformers, but crusaders’

-3 stills: Gandhi, Martin Luther King, and Rev Moon: “all are crusaders for peace”

-“God is sitting back, waiting for humanity to… mature”

-Not a single sound-bite from Moon himself

Reverend Sun Myung Moon, addressing the Inaugural Convocation of the Universal Peace Federation in the Anna Livia Suite, Gresham Hotel, O’Connell St, Dublin.

4th November 2005.

Four pages into his speech, he begins on a tangent – spoken in Korean and occasionally some very broken English “…when we love our enemies more than we love our own children.”

Adam and Eve were the true parents through which everything is connected in the world. However, because of the Fall, everything in the world was put under Satan’s blood lineage. Adam and Eve fell, so they cannot get to the Kingdom of Heaven; to get into heaven you have to love Satan, and then your family can be complete.

“We are conflicted on many levels, beginning with the war between body and mind…”

Mind and body are fighting. Because God’s not there, the mind can’t win.

“They recognize only their own interests while ignoring the misery of tens of thousands who die of starvation every day.”

They are all our brothers and sisters…

“Who can untie this ancient knot of Cain and Abel?”

This is the fundamental problem; the first killing. All wars and conflict stemmed from this.

…

The following was translated by interpreter on headset.

“Peace among nations can never come when those entrusted with the task have not resolved the Cain-Abel relationship between their own mind and body.”

True parents come only once in history. Adam was the model for all men to be born like; Eve was the model for all women to be born. (thumps lectern) Their task was to follow God’s true lineage – it was their responsibility and duty from the beginning – they were in a position to build the ideal family (applause). Look at your own family now. Is it a model? Is it God’s ideal family? And what about you as man or woman? Are you a model for all men or women? The Fall happened and then Satan took over everything. In your nation, do you see peace all the time? No. You need a model family to resemble. Jesus came as the second perfected Adam. Then the True Parent comes as the third perfected Adam. Are you with me? (YES!) There is no owner of this world. Therefore it is time to launch a United Nations that is united by God’s will. We need an Abel-type peace council. We have peace ambassadors, now we need a peace congress; when God is supervising everything, do you think we need election system? Can you change your father and mother through election system? (Laughter, ‘no!’) Through election system, you can choose your family. This is Satan’s cheeky tricks. An Abel-United Nations would help you with this.

Here we notice a subtle attack on democracy. Basically Moon is about the abolition of democracy and the removal of the division between church and state and the replacing of it by his theocratic Godism project. This was demonstrated last year when he was crowned as Messiah in the US Senate by a bi- partisan group of representatives. He also allies himself with those who are very close to fascist principles. One of his former aides who has left the movement worked for Le Pen as a Moon appointee at the Brussels office of the European Parliament

…

“the first time in history a man has succeeded in establishing the position of Adam…”

… the lost position…

…

I have the Bering Strait Tunnel project. Some say I’m crazy (laughter), but God is with me. God is why I am here. (Applause). Great Britain tried to deny me a visa several weeks ago. People said that Fr Moon would come and destroy England (laughter). Do you think I am here to destroy your country? (NO!) I am 86 years old. I have toured 57 cities, a different city every day. An ordinary man would give up by now… maybe I will die in the duration of this tour, but that would be my honour. (applause).

I was born as a farming boy. Before the age of 10, I could see things in advance. People used to bring me photos of their marriage, and I could see from the photos if the marriage would be a disaster or would work. If what I saw was bad, I threw the picture away; if it was good, I placed my hands on the photo. I told fortunes. Sometime when someone walked into our home I would chase the person out of the house because I could see his criminal past. People were afraid of me then. So when I come to a nation, I can perceive the future of the nation, the same as I do with persons. Many things happen like this – not because I’m a great man, but because I’ve been told by God… This isn’t my own talk, my mouth is being used by someone from above, and it’s an instrument. Sometimes I say harsh things, but the words aren’t mine. My father and old friends used to bow down to me because they could not understand how I could see things in advance. My obligation is to teach you as much of the truth as I can before I pass into the spiritual world. I was told by God to do this, it is not my choice.

…

The blood from Abel has been crying throughout the world. The Lord of Advent comes as the third perfected Adam. The first Adam failed on a family level, with Eve and the Fall. What about Jesus? Did God send his only begotten Son into the world to be betrayed by his disciples and be killed on a cross? No. Jesus came to find his true bride so he could build the true family, and have children. Without this, he couldn’t leave his true seed behind. If you want to harvest fruits, you need to plant seeds. The first true seed was Adam and Eve born as twins’ position. Therefore I question, who was born between Adam and Eve? I ask you Irish people, who was born first, man or woman? (Man!) Who came first? Adam; man was created first. Why did he create man? For Adam’s own sake? (No!) Then for whom? (Eve!) Who does God need? He needs his spouse. As a precondition, God thought of Eve first, and then he created Adam to fit Eve (great applause).

When we say ‘above’, as a precondition we think of ‘below’. Back and front. Up and down. Man and woman. Look at the shape of woman. You can draw her easy – round head, triangle and legs (laughter). Why is woman shaped this way? To balance man, her husband. Let’s talk about love organ shape. Man’s love organ is convex shape (gestures with hands, forming a convex oval shape). This shape is to give easily, to a near or far person; it’s long and stick-like. For whom? What about woman’s shape? As a wife you have to hang on to your husband’s love organ, whether you live or die.

When you go to a party, you can expect delicacy food; but what is yours, what truly belongs to me? Mother? Father? Children? Husband?Are they truly yours? What is most precious for you? In society there is a trend to send parents to an old folks’ home, alone. Think of the ocean. The ocean doesn’t just appear – it is there. So is lineage. Think about the baby in the womb – it is living in water, salty water.

People misunderstood me, didn’t know me, and labelled me with crazy names. But look at Church leaders now, selling positions with money! I received 1,200 crown of King of Peace. The entire 270 tribes in Korea offered crowns to me as the King of Peace. Maybe I need a museum some day (laughter).

If you sign a paper with my signature on it, spiritually you are with me. If the Queen of England or the Prime Minister signs papers saying they’re sorry for being wrong about me, then it’s OK.

You need a mediator, that’s what I came for. Without the thumb, how can four fingers function? The thumb is the centre. (Holds up his left hand, palm facing the audience, with his fingers extended as he demonstrates the point.) The thumb is God. The fourth and fifth fingers are son and daughter, and the middle two fingers – the second and third – are parents, Adam and Eve (applause). If two hands come together and cross fingers together, each finger has three joints. Two points create a line, but three joints create a space. Above, centre, below – three is an important number. If anyone makes a mistake they must repent. In recent history, we had the Cold War between two powers: Communistic regime and free-world democracy. But three; right, middle, left. Man fits woman – eyes, nose, mouth; then chest, stomach, love-organs. You must live there for eternity. You should say, “I am born for the sake of my wife” and vice versa. Try divorce and you’ll be more miserable. When you got to bed and you’ve had an argument or a fight, you should be naked: you are only half a person, put together you form a wholesome human being (applause). If you feel like dying, then die yourself. But divorce only makes it worse. Absolutely no divorce! Out there in the world there are people, they are like insects – worse than insects, out there practising free sex and homosexuality.

Nature is full of mystery, we should believe in it – otherwise life is dull, and it is no fun (laughter).

I predict by 2013, I will launch a spiritual revolution. As the filial son, as the patron saint and as the divine son of God, I will launch a spiritual revolution, whether I am here or in the spirit world. I have four children in the spirit world already, and they are sending messages, ‘amazing reports’ already. My life has been straight with God’s mission, even in prison.

When I came here tonight, you might have though that I had ten eyes (laughter), you might have thought that I have a big eye in my forehead – well, maybe I do spiritually – but I’m an ordinary man. People oppose me, but they don’t beat me. They all died and I am still here.

(Growls) Why do we need national borders? The absolute value – which is one, not two – for any minister, priest, rabbi or imam, is that no person, no ideal is above God.

Do dogs eat their own dung? Do dogs eat babies’ dung? I’m asking because I don’t know! (nervous laughter). You see, I am a religious founder, but I can talk about normal things too. Reverend Moon is a religious founder, but I drink water when I am thirsty. I am a religious founder, but I am a man too!

I say to Catholics, if you like Reverend Moon more than your Catholic fellows, then Catholicism will prosper.

1,700 US daily newspapers tried to destroy me with slander. But I created the Washington Times, so that the voice of God could be heard. I can get anything through my media. I can get any information from the White House any time I want, through my media. Even within a few house of secret Congress meetings of three or four Congressmen, I can get the supposed ‘unleakable’ information (laughter)!

When I was in prison, I gave half of my prison meal portion to my fellow prisoners – I lived on one-third of the food, and did three times the amount of work as others. After this suffering and discipline, look at me now aged 86; I am strong (sustained applause, cheering).

Who gives you absolute value? God can’t give it all by himself, even if he claims he can. Where does it begin from? Through sperm and eggs, that’s how new life is created. What about God’s sperm? Which eggs should God’s sperm go to? Only one woman in the Garden of Eden – Eve. It is about marriage and children… divorce splits the child! The absolute value comes from your partner. You must surrender and not boast yourselves.

Reverend Moon knows the truth, that’s why I am teaching you. Even in the spirit world I’ll look down on humanity and if it’s not good, I’ll talk to God and maybe we can create a new earth. It’s no problem in the spirit world; the problem is in the physical world.

There are three points in the Bible, nobody knows them, maybe God will show you some day.

If Reverend Moon needs money, suddenly lots of money flies to me (laughter)! If God tells a person directly to give Reverend Moon money for the sake of world peace, how can they resist? Think of the fish. Some fish live at the bottom of the sea, thousands of feet below the surface, where the water pressure is so great – and yet somehow they survive. Many fish do not have teeth. They simply swallow smaller fish. Reverend Moon can swallow anything. If he loves Ireland more than the Irish people, Ireland is his! I can send angels and ancestors to give answers.

If you are determined to follow me, even if you lose your life, you go to a better place – I’ll be there (laughter). It’s not the point – it is a matter of time (“Yeah”! Cheering.)

If someone does God’s will, I will give them this (takes off his watch). This is my 85th birthday present from my wife, it’s probably worth $10,000 (gasps from audience). I will give it to someone who does God’s will. But obviously I cannot give it to everyone here as there is only one watch (laughter). (Tries unsuccessfully to put watch back on his wrist) My wife knows how to put it on (laughter).

You absolutely need a husband or wife, not for yourself, but because they give you absolute value. Filial piety means you must resemble your parents. But we were talking about ‘seed’. Everybody say that: ‘seed’. (Crowd repeats the word ‘seed’ a couple of times). Even if you read this speech a thousand times, you won’t grasp the depth of it, you need my explanation.

Do you think I have the qualification to launch an Abel-United Nations? (Yes!)

Even when I was a student, the teachers followed me, were afraid of me. So you see, leaders have always been afraid of me. The British government have banned me from getting a visa, and the EU leaders applied Schengen against me. I can cast a net and I will catch nations. Ireland and England have fought for 800 years… but let’s not get into that. We should exchange Irish children with English children in marriage to reunite Ireland and England. These two nations can become one, united. Within seven generations, the world will be changed completely. Do you believe that? (Yes!).

Repeats himself from earlier regarding how the ‘whites’ are descended from ‘the Polar Bear race in the north, near the Arctic’, who did much hunting. This is the third time he has said this, using almost exactly all the same words and phrasing.

We can liberate God. I have been living the life of a humble man, laying foundations to better the world. Isn’t that right? (Yes!) Some rich people need to be educated by me. For the Bering Strait project, maybe I have to educate wealthy people to raise the $2-3 billion in funds. Once people come to my classes to be educated by me for a week, they will give their support.

In the USA, I did 113 conferences in one year. (applause)

Logbook notes at the time:

Moon’s speech started about 7.10 pm. By 8.40 pm, many people around the hall were getting restless and tired: shifting in their seats, slumped forward, and looking around distractedly. By 9 pm, an Indian woman got up and left from her seat in the middle of the chamber, and by 9.05 pm, three men from the same general seating area got up and left together too. A couple of 6 or 7 year old boys sitting beside me had fallen asleep.

It is clear that part of the audience was brought over from Birmingham. An Indian lady who was a Hindu was an example of this. I asked her where she was from. Some of the African members looked like they may have come from the UK as well to make up the numbers. There were about 300 in the Anna Livia Suite.

Notes while in hall during speech:

Mind-numbing, boring, rambling, repetitive, endless metaphors; feel close to ‘snapping’! Feel like I can leave but don’t want to miss anything either.

Notes after leaving:

Left around 10 pm. Four and a half hours of it. Excruciatingly bored and tired; like being stuck in a boring class – except for 3 times as long and with nothing to learn, nothing meaningful being taught!

We heard that the talk went on till at least 11 PM when they had some other items. Additional comments in italics by the observer 7/11/05:

Can’t believe it went on for another hour, surely this counts as cruel and unusual punishment?! So much of it was nonsense cloaked in metaphor and supposedly ‘logical’ language (plenty of ‘therefores’ and rhetorical questions)- if my transcript of his ‘out-takes’ seems a bit disjointed, I can say that it is due more to the lack of any logical connection to what he pronounced than my shorthand! As much I would have preferred to ideally, I just couldn’t stick out

the rest of it.

Met Mike Garde sitting in the foyer on way out – he and two friends of Rachel Matthews of Gerry Ryan Show fame – who said I looked very shaken; was I okay? Ended up just the self-aggrandising ramblings of an old man, returning to and reiterating the same points, the same dead-end tangents, and the same pseudo-Biblical metaphors. So boring, draining and circular; non-directional, intellectually frustrating – felt close to snapping, although perhaps not in the ‘cult terminology’ sense. Just had to get out of there, was going psychologically numb! Mind-numbing and deadening. Other people – proper members – had left before me, children dozed off, and there were teenage Unificationists sitting outside in the hallway. Questionable conviction to their leader, true parent, “the filial son, patron saint and divine son of God”? A serious sense of preaching to the converted; obedient laughter, cheers, agreements – all on cue despite the obvious fatigue of the crowd.

We have a copy of the speech, which would have taken about 30 minutes to read, but as it is just a restatement of the same old tired lines we will not bother sending it on.
