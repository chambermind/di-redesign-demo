Newsletters from Abundant Life- The archive

ChristianVictory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

We received these Newsletters from a correspondent who has been observing the this Church for a number of years. We understand whereas it was up on the web site it has now been removed. The content we believe gives some insight as to why. We publish it without comment:

February 2008
Issue #135

Hello Everyone,

Heather and I trust that all is well with you and yours as we head into to a New Year and a New Season. January has already been so busy! We have had our ’round-the-clock’ prayer going on for the last three weeks. We feel it is so important to spend time in God’s presence before launching into the New Year. In addition to this we have also had our annual ‘Jumble Sale’ (kind of like a garage sale where we raise money for our nurseries and children’s departments). I have also just returned from an important ministry trip to the U.S., and I’m leaving for a ministry trip to Australia in about thirty minutes!! The trip is all ministry focused without time for anything else. Just to let you know these trips are never paid for by our ministry or church. They are funded by whoever invited us.

Heather is in her twelfth week with our fourth–and final!–baby…we are so excited and she is green at the gills! She’s been fighting nausea for the entire time. I’m feeling pretty good, though.

We would really appreciate your prayers on a particular front. We were advised last year to check on our tax situation in Ireland. We have always drawn our salary entirely from America, paid taxes only in America, and have our 501C3 based only in America. Upon arriving in Ireland we were told that as long we paid US taxes and only drew money from the US we would owe no Irish taxes. However, upon following the recent advise given us, we discovered just how precious the counsel was. Though Ireland and the US have a tax treaty (which was thought to exclude us from paying taxes here if they were paid there), the new accountant discovered we are actually still responsible for taxes on this side as well. Since most of our salary is attributed to either parsonage, pastoral care and transportation, etc., we haven’t paid too much in taxes over the years and were indeed several years actually below the poverty line (though we have felt very blessed). Ireland, however, doesn’t allow similar exemptions and requires full payment on anything unpaid. For example, there normal tax bracket here runs at 40%. So whatever bracket we paid in the states, we would be required to fork over the remaining amt. to the Irish government for EACH YEAR plus pay 40% on everything that the U.S. considered tax deductible plus another 40% on the money we use to pay the taxes! So, since we went to them as soon as we found out we were at fault, we are not in danger of exposure: and they place tax cheats on the front page of every local newspaper in the country! Can you imagine?…Thank God for good advice. Anyway our tax bill for nine of the last eleven years works out to a whopping $75,000!!!!!!!!!! Ouch. But we consider ourselves very fortunate as the government seems to have allowed more deductions for us then they normally would. We are not panicked; we are trusting in the Lord and would very much value your prayers. Since we truly work for God, and have worked with integrity in all that we knew, we know that He will bless us.

Its gonna’ be a great year: Increase in every area, joy abounding on every side, dreams coming true! Don’t leave anything out this year! Fully enjoy every blessing, maximize every opportunity and love our God with your whole heart and soul and mind and body.

We love you dearly and look forward to all the blessings God has in store…for you and for us.

Kevin and Heather

May 2008
Issue #138

Hello Everyone and Greetings from Ireland,

Heather is doing well and is in her 26 th week of pregnancy! The church is better than ever….we had 240 people in service last Sunday! This month we hosted a wonderful First Communion service for a group of second graders including my own second grader, Abbey. It was so special…all the kids were great and highlighted the service with their songs, scripture memory and prayers. There wasn’t a dry eye in the place. We love having a special service where we do First Communion for the children because we find they really understand the importance of the event from then on.

Also, we were privileged to minister to a humble Filipino man after the service. He once walked with God and studied to be a priest. He fell in love, married and drifted away from the Lord. God gave me a word for him that, like Peter, Satan wanted to sift him. After Peter’s denial of Jesus, he was restored to his faith and filled with the Holy Spirit and became tremendous preacher in the move of God. God would do the same for the Filipino man! I then laid hands on the Filipino man and he was gloriously filled with the Holy Ghost and began speaking in tongues and weeping. (Acts 19:1-6) God nudged me to hug him while I prayed for him. To him it was as if the Father Himself was hugging him like the prodigal son returning home. (Lk 15:20) Isn’t God beautiful.

We also had Ivan Tait from the United States with us this month. Those of you who have never ‘experienced’ Ivan’s ministry are truly missing one of the finer things of life….Ivan’s amazing! Along with his powerful preaching, inspired prophecies and outlandish humour, he is a prince with God and is filled with true Christian compassion for the widow and the orphan. Our church spent the last five months collecting money for his new orphanage in Kenya. We have some serious needs ourselves and have been looking for a place to plant a serious seed in the Father’s vineyard to that end. (James 1:27) What better seeds than widows and orphans! When you have a need – just plant a seed!

Now, for a little business: Please allow me to speak freely concerning partners, needs and our current situation. As you may know, the Euro peaked against the Dollar last week. We once earned a Euro for only 89 cents…it now costs us $1.60 for the same Euro. I’ve heard everybody complaining about the price of gas in America. It costs us about TEN DOLLARS PER GALLON!…consider that next time you’re at the pump. It’s about $30 to dry clean a suit!!!!…and I’m a preacher!!!!

We’ve made some financial progress with our Irish tax situation but we still have a very long way to go. We have, however, found a fantastic tax attorney/CPA. He is a true help and we are confident in his ability to work affectively with the international tax treaty. Please keep praying with us as we pray for complete resolution and as we pray in all the finances needed. Also, we are researching tax attorneys in the states to help us refine the international process and define our liabilities in the US correctly. It’s been difficult to find somebody with experience in this area and we seem to be the only Americans in Ireland even dealing with the issue. Well, trailblazers go first!

We are praying for 100 monthly partners that can support us faithfully at $50.00 per month or more. We currently have about SIX of those. We need Churches that will support us with at least $200.00 per month. We have TWO (one of those is new and not yet consistent). That is what we need just to pay the bills and, as you can see, we are well short of the mark.

Ireland is a country where less than 0.4 % of the population is born again (‘06 national census)! Catholics appear to be quite different in Ireland than in the States. Here in Ireland, if you ask them if they are Christian, they say “No, I’m Catholic”. Almost none profess to have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. In 1973, 90% of Catholics attended Mass on Sunday in Ireland, today in the cities, only a small percentage do. They desperately need to be touched by the Lord’s redeeming love. Help us reach them…and I believe God will bless you for it, richly.

We will be redesigning the newsletter over the next few months to keep everyone posted on where we are every month. I trust it will make a difference.

April 2009
Issue #149

Hello Everyone!

March was, of course, wonderful. With St. Paddy’s Day and the big parade in town, we always have a terrific time and a terrific witness for the Lord Jesus. It is a natural time to share the Gospel with the city without being marginalized by any religious bias.

This year was extra special. We had a team from Burleson, Texas, that helped us with everything… and we really needed the help, too. Jennifer Huffman, who has visited us a couple of times on mission trips, and her husband, John, organised the trip with the new church they’ve been attending. While I was in Texas back in January, I met up with them as well as their Pastors. They had expressed a desire to bring folks from their church, Lifegate, for their very first mission trip. The church is only about two years old, and they brought a team of 17 people… isn’t that great! Pastors Chad and Amber were such a blessing. They and their team worked so hard for us and for the church here. The theme for the parade was “The Sea”. Together we built our biggest float to date replete with loads of jelly fish, a sea with waves and a huge, big fish.

We had 10,000 amazing Gospel tracts printed up entitled “Charting Your Course Through the Storms” to hand out along the parade route. The idea was first to convey the thought that God has a plan to get them through life. Second, that we’re offering a class starting next Sunday to help them discover that plan. Thirdly, it included a prayer for salvation that we pulled out of a book entitled The Confessions of St. Patrick.
The heavens cooperated with us by giving the best spring day we’ve seen in many years. We had sooo much fun and all ended up at my house after the parade for a BBQ. Our crew broke out an accordion and a few spoons to play a bit of Irish traditional music. Hannah and Abbey showed off some of their Irish dancing, and everybody sang a bit, in between rounds on the trampoline. It was a great time and a great day! The burgers didn’t hurt any, either.
As a fun footnote, we noticed a video clip that someone put up on http://www.youtube.com that has some good footage of our float. In case you’re interested, you can simply search for “Galway St. Patrick’s Day Parade 2009” on the YouTube website. Though there are several clips, this one is just over 5 minutes and has the tag “HQ” for high quality. We start about halfway through the clip. You can hear our music and will hear the kids saying, “Hey, look at the big fish!” Also, you can actually see Heather and the baby in one of the church vans. Be sure to use the high quality (HQ) button on the video so you will be able to see them clearly.

Thanks so much for all of your love and support! We temporarily solved the immediate tax problem here in Ireland with a family loan. We do, however, have to pay this back at a rate of about $1,000 per month. If you would like to contribute toward this, we would truly appreciate it. If you have any prayer requests, be sure to include them in your correspondence… we love praying over you all.

September 2009
Issue #154

Hello Everyone!

We had a terrific summer and now have all the kids back to school. I travelled to the States for a couple of weeks in August. I was in 10 cities and preached 13 times in two weeks or so, whew! We had wonderful services and got to see a lot of friends and meet many new ones.

Amongst all the cool things we did over the summer (youth camp, street teams, etc.), I got to do one of the coolest things I’ve ever done in my twenty years of ministry: a wedding. Yes, I’ve performed weddings before, but this was no ordinary wedding. This one was for two people who don’t go to church here. What made it so special? Well, I’ll tell you. You see before, in Ireland, the government never licensed the minister… only the building. Now the laws have changed and ministers are licensed. This couple approached me about doing a “garden wedding in an Irish manor house.” They had asked their priest if he would do it first. But, moored to tradition, he refused and told them to do it in the church like everybody else. Well, times have changed and people aren’t will to just “do what they’re told” anymore. There are other options now. Anyway, the bride-to-be asked her father, a member of my church, if his “priest” would do it. You just can’t imagine how glorious or how unique it was! The setting was spectacular: in the garden, white chairs, roses and flowers in full bloom, and the lakes and mountains of Ireland’s wild West behind me at the altar like God’s great canvas. What you must also keep in mind is that no one in the congregation, no one, had ever been in an outdoor wedding. It was truly amazing. Even more wonderful was that I got to minister to the couple for the months before the wedding, and then to the entire congregation (most of whom were medical doctors, as was the groom) the blessed Gospel. It was so special. I even got two ovations during the toasts over the meal… my gosh!! Unforgettable.

120:
Now for something very important: Heather and I have been praying for something for over a dozen years. We’ve been believing for 120 partners to support us every month… 100 individuals and 20 churches. We have fallen well short of the mark for all that time. Now, I’m going to ask every single person on our mailing list, which is not very large, to consider being one of the 120. We need 100 people to support us for $50 per month. If you love us and believe in us, then please help us to do what we do. What other missionaries do you support? What better soil to sow in? Who do you know that works harder than we do and has stayed as clean as we have? Just look at all of the photos on our web page over the course of four years in Nicaragua and twelve years in Ireland. With this number of partners, we will hit every goal and pay every bill. We believe that, like on the Day of Pentecost, when there were a 120 gathered together for one cause, God poured out His power and His blessing (Acts 2:1-4). We’re not asking for much, and never have. Please consider this humble request. My family and I would truly appreciate it. Plus, our partners receive a few special blessings from the ministry and, we believe, a lot of special blessing from above! We include the number of ministry partners on the newsletter every month so you can know how close we are to our goal. Help us to achieve it! We believe it will be a powerful seed you sow toward achieving your own goals.

October 2009
Issue #155

Hello everyone!

Heather and I remain very blessed with many more tales of adventure every month. God is certainly fun to know and to serve!

I went back to Nicaragua this past month for the first time in many years. Before going, God spoke to me through 2 Kings 8:1-6, where the Shunamite woman (who had helped Elisha) had to leave her land for 7 years because of the coming drought. Upon returning, she sought the king’s help in restoring her land. As God would miraculously have it, she entered the king’s court at the very moment Gehazi was telling how Elisha had raised her son from the dead. Such was the blessing of the Lord that the king gave her land back, plus “all the fruit of the land from the day she left until today.” God blessed her with such restoration that He actually brought her up to speed!! He restored her to where she would have been!!

Since I haven’t been able to minister in Nicaragua for the past 7 years, I knew that God was speaking to me about restoring the years of harvest and relationships that we had to ‘leave’ for awhile. The result? AMAZING!!! One of the young men who was in our church over 15 years ago (now not such a young man as he was when Heather and I performed his wedding ceremony) is now pastoring a terrific church of his own. The trip was blessed from the start. I received a FREE bump to First Class on the flight to and fro. This was very special to me as I had never flown First Class in all the years we lived and pastored down there.

Day One:
• I was on the only Christian TV channel for an entire hour… live!
• Interviews with 4 radio stations and 1 newspaper
• Evening crusade at Pastor Eddy Salmeron’s church, The Father’s House. The crowd was so big we had to have the service outside in the open air! About 40
people received the Holy Spirit!
Day Two:
• We were invited back to the Christian channel, where we received calls from all over the country including many, many people that had known us.
• We were invited on to the morning show of one of the main public TV channels!
• I did radio segments for Christian and secular stations
(The radio programs were outstanding! The secular station ‘La Buenisima’ was particularly powerful. As we were ministering, the female DJ broke down and began to weep as the incredible anointing in the room flowed with God’s presence. She even asked me to pray for Nicaraguan families on her show and played a Christian song as we left the airwaves.)
• I was interviewed by the largest newspaper in the country!
• Evening crusade continued to grow; we held them in the studios of TV Channel 21 (The evenings were highlighted by the presence of many friends and many old members of our Nicaraguan church, many of whom are now in ministry themselves! About 50 people were filled with the Holy Spirit every single night, and the experience was unforgettable for them and for me.)
Day Three:
• We were invited back to the Christian channel for a 3rd morning as the host was so hungry for the truths we ministered. They even allowed us to use their studios for the Thursday and Friday night crusades, filming it and putting it all on the air.
• We had more segments on various Christian radio stations, including the #1 station in the country.
• The last night of evening crusades was a blow-out! With prophecies, dancing, and the laying on of hands to the whole crowd, the place was ignited with the fire of God and the excitement that only the presence of the King can bring.

Needless to say, my 3 days in Nicaragua were a blur of activity! I was privileged to be accompanied by a young protégé, Cecil Robles. He was a very instrumental and pleasant companion in the work of the Lord. Our church here in Ireland is excited about connecting to the work in Nicaragua, but nonetheless are glad to have us back… and it was great to be back!

With much love, Kevin, Heather and Family

February 2010
Issue #159

Blessings to you all!

By the time you receive this, you will be well into the New Year and I trust that 2010 will prove to be a remarkable year for us all. One of the ‘Words’ that God gave us for 2010 was “a fresh start”! Isaiah 1:18 says, “Come now, let us reason together, says the Lord: Though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow.” Imagine! When the snow falls, you have a fresh, clean and beautiful covering over the ground. You can’t tell what yesterday looked like because it covers all the tracks, the footsteps – even a bloody battlefield would look new and clean. What a blessing that only Heaven can bestow: a New Start!!

Well, as for our church in Ireland, we are taking that literally!! We’re starting the New Year off in a brand new place. Due to circumstances out of our control we’ve had to leave our facility (one of the finest in the country) and move to a hotel for our services. We were believing to find a new location and were so concerned about the effect of the transition on the church. WOW! What a relief! The whole church became so excited about the change and simply rose to the challenge. Last Sunday was one of the largest services we’ve ever had… and with no advertisement. Everyone was just happy to ‘move’ out and trust the Lord to be with us, guide us and provide for us. What a blessing as well that our overhead is dramatically reduced and we’ll be able to take care of some old church debts and, in Jesus’ name, save up some money for relocating. Honestly, I wish we had done it sooner and not have been so worried about getting out of the boat! Much pressure is gone and the church is so surprisingly happy about the whole thing. Actually, it feels stronger than ever!! And it has always been strong, as any of you that have visited could attest. Thank God! We’ve found the new start already working for us in a beautiful way. God has said that even despite our own failures and sins, He would wipe them away and help us start again with a clean slate. What a great God! I’m truly excited to see what 2010 brings. We have solidly placed ourselves into the mighty Hand of God. We have found Him to be carrying us forward! Praise the Lord, for He is good. He is faithful! He alone is God, our God! Amen!

We are also making plans to bring our family home this summer! We’ve not been home to Texas as a family for several years now and are all looking forward to seeing family, sun, Mexican food, etc.! We wanted to let all our partners and friends know, in case some of you are interested in helping us with expenses. The family will be in Texas for about a month… I’ll have to fly back to Ireland so that I only miss one Sunday or so. The tickets alone will run to about $6,500. (There are many benefits to having a beautiful family of six… this ain’t one of them!) So if you’d like bless us, we’d really appreciate it. Your donations could go toward tickets or perhaps “buy a day” for us. One day, calculating airfare, car hire, food, etc., would be around $300. And we’d love to see you if we’re close by. Thanks so much for your love and prayers. Until next time, remember that we are praying for you all.

Love, the Sanfords

May 2010
Issue #162

Please Read This Newsletter!!

Hello, everyone! I’m reconfiguring our mail-out and wanted to let everyone know what we were doing. Everything is going well, but we are, along with many others, trying to cut back on some of our ministry expenses. Our newsletter costs us about $500.00 per month to send out. We have needed 100 partners at $50.00 per month to pay bills here, but we have currently only 12 partners (along with a few others that give less). As you can see, that barely covers the newsletter cost, and it provides no salary for our family. Though we thoroughly enjoy sending each month’s letter with new stories and new pictures of recent events, we feel we need to trim it in order to be good stewards. So if you desire to remain on the mailing list, please consider becoming a monthly partner. We feel it would be irresponsible of us to continue sowing seed into ground that yields no fruit to the ministry here.

Most people—Christians—do not ‘get the need’ in Ireland. Funny enough, it was much easier to raise funds for Nicaragua, where we planted our first church, since it is a poor Third World country. Of Ireland, however, where the spiritual need is drastically more desperate, there is an incredible ignorance of the need. To contrast, in Nicaragua, there are now literally thousands of Bible-believing churches of various sizes. In Ireland, there are tens…with only four or five congregations of more than 200 people! In Nicaragua, and many other mission fields, the youth are on fire for God! In Ireland, this generation is filled with godless hedonism and nearly every single young person has no knowledge of God! That is not an exaggeration. Many will support a once-off gospel project or a short-term mission trip of sorts to another country. We, though, are called to stay in a foreign country and build a church. The Bible says that when Jesus “saw the multitudes, He was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd” (Matt. 9:36). We pray that our friends will also be moved with compassion to help us stay and shepherd these sheep, that the wolf devour them not (John 10:12).

Our church is currently on a 21-day fast. We are desperate to see God move in this land, that the church would grow, and that we would see real and lasting breakthroughs financially. This has been our hardest year ever. Though the work is strong, as any visitor can attest, we have been stretched ‘beyond measure’ on the financial front. I know that many of you have also been struggling. But we truly believe that if you provide for the ministry out of your scarcity, God will make sure that your barrel doesn’t run dry and your oil will not fail (1 Kings 17: 9-16). Thanks so much for your love and partnership. We believe we can make a difference! We believe you can, too!

Together in the Harvest,
Kevin, Heather and family
