Behold the lamb of God that taketh away the cash of the world=sheep

Tony Quinn

When I went on a seminar I was asked by Caroline McDonagh to take with me some meat.  Quinn eats very specifically & with great attention to where the food comes from.  His meat used to be bought from a Dublin butcher.  Caroline wanted me to take out the some meat with me in my own luggage.  (I was flying out to a foreign location such as Monaco, Capri, Egypt.)  My understanding meant that this would be a breaking of customs regulations.  I felt both honoured & pressured to be asked to do this.  With hindsight I think it was extremely wrong of McDonagh to ask me: Quinn can afford to pay for his own transportation; she was asking me to break the law and we were paying to go out to  him.  People going on seminars were routinely asked to take both meat & duck eggs to him.

Well that’s living the “Good Life” in the Bahamas TQ style.
What about the people who are given the brown envelopes to take out to TQ, what an honour that must be, to be allowed to launder for Tony, I bet that brings your vibes up a bit!!!!

I brought meat also – very heavy so had to get one of the guys to carry it as I was asked to take it as hand luggage. I was told that, to do the connections, Tony needed “high vibe food”, The clearer the food, the clearer he was and he needed to be clear to be able to connect people to their spirit. By the way I was also told that I was privileged to be asked to do such a thing and that doing it would immediately start bringing up my own vibes. I also carried eggs and applies, supplements and even a water distiller. Also all the pens, books and paperwork for the seminars. Also all Jim Fitzgerald’s electrics. I was also with a guy once who had been asked to carry one of Tony’s girlfriends bags. He musn’t like paying for extra luggage.
