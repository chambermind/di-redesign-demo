In order to understand the poor financial accounts of the Dublin mission, it is necessary to understand two key pieces of background. First, the Dublin mission was sued by ex-member Mary Johnston. This case was settled out of court for an amount believed to be in the region of €2 million. Second, any excess money made by the Dublin is used to send its staff members for Scientology training in larger Scientology structures in the US and the UK. This ensures that money is constantly flowing uplines to the higher Scientology facilities. These two factors combine to explain the dreary accounts of the Dublin mission.
