TM Mantras

Transcendental Meditation

By Johannes Aagaard

Transcendental Meditation officially maintains that a mantra is given by the TM teachers according to specific insights and in accordance with the individualities of the meditators.

In reality the TM mantras are given according to very mechanic norms, namely according to age-groups. From the above mentioned German book “Die Macht der Süssen Worte” we publish a list of TM mantras indicating the age-groups. If any of our readers know about similar or different lists or are able to correct this list from personal observation, we will be most grateful to get information on this point.
