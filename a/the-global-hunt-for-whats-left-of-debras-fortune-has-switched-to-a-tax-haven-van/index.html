THE global hunt for what’s left of Debra’s fortune has switched to a tax haven Vanuatu

Magnificat Meal Movement

THE global hunt for what’s left of Debra’s fortune has switched to a tax haven chain of tropical islands in the South Pacific.

With apologies to the Sunday Independent for rewriting their story

http://www.independent.ie/national-news/hunt-starts-for-sean-quinns-funds-in-paradise-island-3161628.html

Financial investigators hired by the former members of the MMM were dispatched to Vanuatu, in what could prove to be a “critical stage” in its efforts to track down the fallen Fake visionaries foreign assets.

The archipelago of islands north of Fiji offers strict secrecy and no taxes for investors to put their “surplus funds”. In this case funds were taken using a Marian scam and transcendental trickery a form of magical manipulation!

The team of investigators, led by a former detective with a grudge, have been working around the clock to establish whether the former MMM control freak concealed funds before the list of her disciples who have broken from her spell moved in to recover outstanding loans of an unknown amount.

It was alleged that Debra, her chauffeur John Tonner now acting as a personal assistant conspired to put  a lot of dosh– out of reach, rather than repay her disciples.

A source close to the financial investigation says their inquiries have now reached a “critical” stage.

The investigation is headed by Dialogue Ireland investigative International, a private investigation firm, in conjunction with an agency in Brisbane.

The team are working in Australia, and it is believed the link to Vanuatu was unearthed during investigations in Melbourne.

A source close to the investigation told DI they are working around the clock to track down the ill gotten gains that are now owed to her creditors. “We have reached a critical stage,” the source said. “Debra has made some very stupid decisions where she has allowed her emotions to cloud her thinking.”

The former detective heading up the investigation is believed to have led a number of successful investigations into corporate fraud and irregularities.

– Niamh Horan and Maeve Sheehan
