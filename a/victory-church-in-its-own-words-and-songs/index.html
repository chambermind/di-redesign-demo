HISTORY

Victory Centre Is located close to the foothills of the Dublin Mountains in Firhouse, Tallaght the origin of the place-name may derive from the Irish word “fir”, which means “of the man”. It may also come from the “Fir tree” as there was Fir trees in abundance stretching from the Dublin mountains right into the now Suburb .  It is situated on the eastern bank of the River Dodder, 2 km downstream from Tallaght village and a little upriver from Templeouge Victory Centre is located on the site of the old Killiney house the meaning of which means “Church on the river” quite remarkable as this name was given hundreds of years ago and only in the last 3 years the “Church on the river” or Victory Christian Centre has been completed.

ABOUT

Victory Christian Centre is a Bible believing full Gospel non denominational Church, people from every country, every creed, and every tradition including an increasing number of Irish people attend Victory Church each Sunday, they come to celebrate what Jesus is doing in their lives. They come to be built up mind, soul and spirit. They come to share the many miracles that Jesus is still performing today through the Holy Spirit in people, from headings in their bodies to restored lives from addictions, to marriages restored. We invite all people, from every back round to come and experience the love and Joy in our service. The Church is headed up by Pastor Brendan and Sheila, they believe in the word of God which can transform and change people, Services on Sunday start at 11:00 am , there is a Children’s Church downstairs, together with crèche’s and play area.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

THE BIBLE
We believe the entire Bible is inspired by the Holy Spirit

THE TRINITY
We believe in one God who exists in three distinct persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God born of the Virgin Mary who came to this earth as Saviour of the world

SALVATION
We believe Jesus died on the cross and shed His blood for our sins. We believe that salvation is found by placing our faith in Jesus and what He did for us on the cross. We believe Jesus rose from the dead and is coming again

WATER BAPTISM
We believe water baptism is an act of identification with the death and resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ

COMMUNION
We believe in taking communion as an act of remembering what the Lord Jesus did for us on the cross. The broken body is for our healing and the shed blood for our redemption

GROWING RELATIONSHIP
We believe every believer should be in a growing relationship with Jesus by obeying God’s Word, yielding to the Holy Spirit and by being conformed to the image of Christ
