A Course in Mind Control Tullycoll lives on!

Tullycoll Trust Foundation

There’s A Course in Miracles group that meet in Belfast and their website is:

www.miraclesni.org.uk

Several of the members of this group are ex members of Tullycoll and we feel they should be made aware of the information that Dialogue Ireland has uncovered about this organisation. We know that on occasion Sandra Heaney has in fact attended these meetings and in the past Joe Heaney has delivered talks to the group. Also the facilitator has been very much guided by Joe Heaney in the past and we believe still has contact with him.

Welcome to the Belfast Study Group

for

A Course in Miracles

(ACIM)

A Course in Miracles is a self-study system of spiritual psychology that helps us to change our thoughts from those of fear to those of love. It has a very practical application in our everyday lives fostering inner peace, spiritual growth and better relationships.

We are a group of individuals from all walks of life, from all religions and none, who come together once a week to share and study the teachings of A Course in Miracles in an informal setting.

We meet every Monday evening from 7.30pm to 9.30pm in the Belmont Tower, 82 Belmont Church Road, Belfast, BT4 3FG.

Meetings begin with a few minutes of quiet and a reading from the Course followed by an opportunity to share or simply listen to the message of the Course.

Meetings are open to all. We are a non-profit making organisation.

(Voluntary contributions of £3 gratefully accepted.)
