23 December 1978

The French Inquisition

Scientology

By Peter Jensen, Church Of Scientology, Denmark

The Church of Scientology, founded 1954 by the American author and researcher L. Ron Hubbard, has during the past quarter of a century followed the tradition that it is the religious minded individuals who have created and continuously create important changes and reforms in society. The scientologists have fought the deprivation of the individuals’ human rights and the increasing lack of regard for the spiritual wellbeing of man, which. characterises our modern time.

In many countries throughout the world, the scientologists ‘have worked to reform the violent methods of institutional psychiatry, disclosed threaths against the individuals’ freedom and privacy, and has worked for a better care for the aged and mental weak and the rehabilitation of alcoholics and criminals. Such reform projects have gained broad public support due to a rising dissatisfaction with various private and government controlled offices.

In a world where extinction of all life of this planet is well within the reach of the technical capacities, the need for an active and effective effort to protect the public cannot be overlooked.

The purpose and motive of the social reform work is the appreciation of the aims of the Church of Scientology: A civilization without insanity, without criminals and without war, where the able can prosper and honest beings can have rights, and where Man is free to rise to greater heights.

Through the years the Church has been subjected to persecution of various kind, and in 1958 it was revealed that the Church of Scientology was one out of the 99 groups in the American society, which were listed on Nixons and Internal Revenue service enemy-list. Through the last two decades numerous false reports have been spread about the Church from intelligence services such as FBI and CIA. This information stems from a twenty month long investigation undertaken by an investigatory Committee set up by the Church.

Every religion has had rough times and a number of examples of persecution is known from history. The Inquisition was one of the most grave examples of intolerance and hate against religions. In our time the inquisitional methods of punishment is carried out through phone-tapping, espionage, circulation of false reports through official channels and razzia on private property with the subsequent depravation of belongings etc.

One of the latest attacks against the Church and its Founder was the French court-decision against the Founder and the leading persons from the French Church of Scientology.

During the hearings the Church’s lawyer presented a long row of witnesses, who witnessed about the basis for the Church and the creed, biographical data and official acknowledgements of the Founder as a humanitarian, the independency of the French Church organisation, the spiritual quality of Scientology, belief in the Supreme Being and immortality of the spirit, the religious recognition in many countries, social reform activities and the financial and other aspects such as the E-meter and the OCA personal analysis.

However the entire courtcase was a result of the obsolete totalitarian French judicial system, where the law permits to accuse and judge a person in absentia, and at no time any proof was given for the accusations.

Apparently, L.Ron Hubbard has been judged because he has written books, which with their content of knowledge and practical methods has created the basis for the new world religion. Hubbard has never set his feet in France and as early as 1966 he retired as administrative leader of the Church in order to concentrate on his research concerning spiritual awareness.

The Church of Scientology does not concern itself with the treatment of physical illnesses, but seeks through the practice of spiritual councelling to help the individual member to an understanding of himself as a spiritual being in his relation to physical universe and God.
