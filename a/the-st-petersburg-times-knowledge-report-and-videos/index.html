The St. Petersburg Times – Knowledge Report and Videos

Scientology

The St. Petersburg Times continue their excellent investigative reporting into Scientology. As well as providing more video testimony from ex-members (viewable below the fold), they have unearthed this insightful knowledge report (in Scientology a ‘knowledge report’, or ‘KR’,  is a write-up of another Scientologist’s supposed ‘crimes’. Scientologists are encouraged to write KRs on each other and to send them to the ‘ethics’ division.):

I have had two comm cycles with Burt and Lynn on this last leg of making an Ideal Org in Seattle. Both Burt and Lynn did a lot to make the building purchase cycle happen and donated over $160,000, which is much appreciated and a commendable. My reason for writing this report is that in my last visit with them they told me that they were not going to donate anything else to the building cycle as it would put them further into debt and it would take them about 1½ years to pay off their debts if they added the $90,000 that I have asked them for. The fact that it would only take them 1½ years to handle their debts if they donated this amount to the building tells me that they can do more if they were willing to, as 1½ years is not a long time to handle a substantial debt. A lot of people in Seattle have given a lot of time and effort and energy into making an Ideal Org, and their sacrifice for Clearing Seattle is much greater than being in debt for a year. I have communicated to them the urgency of the situation and that every Scientologist in Seattle needed to pull together to make this go right, and that this is the way we are going to Clear Seattle for real. Their response to this was the above.

Burt and Lynn have a successful business and are certainly able to do more to the building, and a substantial amount at that. I understand the point of not wanting to go further into debt, and 1 don’t disagree with them on this at all, however I do disagree with them not being theta the solver and looking at what they could do to come up with the funds needed to forward the expansion of Scientology in their own city.
