Confirmation of the Quinn’s B shareholding making him a member of INE.

Tony Quinn

DI has received a report from a well placed person in the organisation that in fact…

Quinn is a B shareholder as this person told me he had taken shares in payment for his seminars.

As far as the litigation in the High Court is concerned it is of little relevance but it does answer those who say he could not be added to court cases involving disparaging the oil company as he is not a Director. All that is required is that he is a member or that the INE in general was disparaged. As Justice Hogan has pointed out during the hearings it is irrelevant what Mike Garde, DI or Nicola say about the company, what is relevant is whether we relied on Katrina Skinner for these views. What we are clear about is that Susan Morrice in early 2009 was involved in disparaging B Shareholders which should be discussed at the AGM.

Earlier in the post on the last day of the court case we reported:

Brian O’Moore SC had raised the fact that the Morrice camp had claimed that the International media was at the heart of a campaign to disparage the oil company.
