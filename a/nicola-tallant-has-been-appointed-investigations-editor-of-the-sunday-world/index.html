Dialogue Ireland has been aware of the cultist attitudes and mentalities present in regard to Quinn. We are actively involved in providing advice and information showing how Quinn has dis-empowered good people, wrecked families, made entrepreneurs who were focussed on tasks lose their direction and in some cases their business. On a recent Frontline programme there was a very successful business person who had sold his company for €15 m. A number of years ago there was a person involved with him who had great gifts, but he left after he had been on a Educo Seminar. These people saw through Quinn’s influence and succeeded, no one knows if they had bought into his nightmare what would have been the result? Our experience is likely failure.

NICOLA TALLANT has been appointed Investigations Editor of the Sunday World.

She is a qualified criminologist and has worked as an investigative journalist for 16 years with the Star, the Irish Mirror and the Sunday World. Her exposés of mind guru Tony Quinn, Ireland’s sex trafficking gangs and the sex ring at the centre of the shocking Dalkey House of Horrors case have cast light on some of Ireland’s darkest secrets. Her investigations into the mysterious Church of Scientology became an internationally acclaimed book, The Complex. She recently co-hosted the hit TV3 crime show, Crime Capitals, with award-winning investigative journalist Donal McIntyre.
