COMMENT ON LATEST SUNDAY WORLD EXPOSE OF QUINN September 20, 09

Tony Quinn

– LEGAL ISSUES MENTIONED BY QUINN AT THE  MEETING

Quinn attempts to avoid justice:

Quinn says he can’t go into a court.  He’s really saying “I won’t go to court” to face the music, unless of course he’s forced to go there by law.  Facing any kind of justice is the last thing he wants to do and any of his legal people (and now oil legal people) will definitely advise him against going to court.  He would have a terrifying and disastrous time in court trying to defend his indefensible actions over the years.

The facts also are that he hasn’t even been able to successfully refute even one of the damning allegations and charges made against him in numerous media exposes over nearly 40 years.  It’s not that he hasn’t tried to muster various devotees from the cult to lie on his behalf and to get “evidence” to support his case but in each instance QUINN still hasn’t been able to refute even one of the charges made against him.

We have already alluded to Quinn’s problem in relation to taking a case against people. Some of his former high level members have informed us that his barrister/supporter told him that his major problem is that most of the people who are criticising him are telling the truth. Libel is only possible when you do not tell the truth. We also reported that the attack on our web site in January and a death threat made in the presence of witnesses has now been sent to the DPP for adjudication. These witnesses were high level members of the Quinn group and heard him directly discuss the taking down of the DI web site and talk about taking out the Director. They too have made statements to the Gardai. You can now understand why Quinn has so much security. He just wants to avoid that summons which will be served sooner rather than later.
