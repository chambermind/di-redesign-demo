Correspondence from Jeanette Rigney and John Sherwin of TABOR

Tabor Institute

Correspondence from Jeanette Rigney and John Sherwin of TABOR:

Tabor Corr

Dialogue Ireland received a forwarded registered letter to our former Chairperson who died earlier this year. It was addressed to Veritas House and they forwarded it to us. Unfortunately, Fr Tierney died in May of this year and was not involved with Dialogue Ireland for over 10 years.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/05/14/the-death-has-occurred-of-fr-martin-tierney/

We were approached by a number of people to post material on our blog in regard to their experiences with the Tabor Institute. We invited the Tabor House to respond and would have given them the opportunity to not just to comment but to make a post to answer their critics. They never took up this option.

Subsequently the Sunday Times published an article which saw our site receive the most visits since we began, with students using this forum to address their experiences.

We received some comments from people who wished to censor free speech, and who confused concerns about privacy and data protection with what was in the public domain and publicly available on the Tabor site. It was natural that after they began to be the subject of public scrutiny they would wish to withdraw what was up there. This was not an invasion of their privacy, but rather an unprofessional attempt to shoot the messenger and cover up the evidence. If there were concerns about the posts why was a letter not written in early September rather than wait till the actual day when comments were reduced to a trickle?

These assumptions are to be found in the correspondence which we have now decided to publish. Obviously Fr Tierney can’t reply, but the above is by way of a reply from Dialogue Ireland. We again repeat we would be delighted to host any statement from Tabor. Though separate letters were sent by Jeanette Rigney and John Sherwin the content of all the letters are exactly the same and the writing on the envelopes suggest one sender.

Dear Fr Tierney
Your publication of a Blog on Dialogue Ireland concerning my business TABOR Institute has caused me great distress.
I am herewith requesting the original computer files and paper ones as well which have
been used as source material for this defamation. Further I require you to provide details of all email correspondence concerned with this matter including IP addresses of all contributors to this matter.
Finally i require that you cease and desist from all use of my name and business names in any publication, blog, computer press, Email or any other means of communication.
A copy of this letter has been sent to the Data protection Commissioner.
Yours Sincerely
Jeanette Rigney

Dear Sirs
I am making a request under section 6 of the Data Protection act, to have my information (including my businesses and photographs copyright to me on other websites) erased and removed from your website. I have not given my consent to personal data about me being published. A copy of this request will also be submitted to the data Protection Commissioners Office.
Yours Sincerely
John Sherwin
