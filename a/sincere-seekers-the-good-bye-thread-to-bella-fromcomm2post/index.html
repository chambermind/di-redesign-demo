Sincere Seeker’s-The Good bye Thread to Bella. Fromcomm2post

RIGPA

DI takes a significant comment and raises it to a post when an important point is being made. We also give contrary views exposure if they add to the debate. Recently we were approached by some supporters of Rigpa, but after establishing a protocol for facilitating a dialogue they vanished?

We believe the reports in this post are accurate from earlier information we received. Ed

Bella,

Don’t you understand? We’ve been to Rigpa, met Sogyal-I even introduced by best friend to him and Sogyal screwed his partner, bringing their relationship to an end and sending my colleague spiralling into a lengthy period of psychiatric disturbances leading to him abandoning Buddhism. Please explain at what point you believe alarm bells should start ringing?(If you can read this mail from so deep in the sand)

I suggest you stop the literalism and consult your teacher on what constitutes valid reasoning. Personal experience is only one way of proving the existence or non -existence of phenomena according to the Buddha’s teaching. Consider inferential reasoning, the testimony of (many) others. These two are valid ways of establishing truths. You on the other hand seem to believe that unless you’ve seen it, it doesnt exist (Have you ever been to Mongolia? If not, it doesn’t exist according to your reasoning). Your alternative response is it was all a long time ago and Sogyal now regrets what he did. This is an admission that abuse did take place (which you alternately deny and confirm) and that all that is required to make the world a better place is for the Golden Child to think he made a mistake. Its a start but I’m afraid worldlings amongst us don’t feel its enough. Even for Buddhists, its not enough-read up on the four opponent powers, in particular the power of the vow not to repeat ones negative acts. It’s no use saying I wish I hadn’t done that then just doing it again, and again and again.

You strike me as one of these people that cannot bear to let others have the last word. i am afraid he last word is ‘guilty’. Beyond that, there is nothing more to say.

PS Reality is not just around the corner-it’s staring you in the face but your projections and prejudices are preventing you from seeing it.
