Diversion and Deflection the name of the game:TQ strategy in Dublin while BVI gives Quinn a headache

Tony Quinn

Why make an ex-parte Application to have Mr. Mike Garde deposed in
relation to proceedings which are ongoing in Denver, Colorado?

DI analysis of the Tony Quinn strategy:
On Wednesday July 25, the same day as the proceedings in Dublin, Justice Bannister was giving
direction to the conflicting parties in regard to his judgement:
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/18/quinn-payout-of-23m-on-oil-
firm-shares-in-doubt-by-paul-cullen-the-irish-times-july-18-2012/

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

It was not going to be good news here in Ireland so why not deflect from the main
issue by putting the cat amongst the pigeons in Ireland. Never defend always attack.
Logically why would you seek an ex – parte application in the high Court in Dublin
when early in June I was asked whether I was willing to give a deposition?
I had replied that I was willing and had asked a few questions.
I never received a reply from Sarah Watson.
Today I note she is the same lawyer who delivered this grounding Affidavit
and letters rogatory to my solicitor for my information.
