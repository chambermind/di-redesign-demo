There are even some unofficial drinking game rules for this broadcast:

  1. Every time a someone stays silent at a question = 1 shot
  2. Every time John Sweeney says “Hi! I’m John Sweeney from the BBC. Who are you?” = 1 shot
  3. Mentions of a prominent celebrity such as Tom Cruise or John Travolta = 1 shot
  4. Every shot of a camera filming Sweeney = 1 shot
  5. Every time someone Sweeney’s interviewing, has interviewed, is associated with, references or Sweeney himself is accused of some crime (eg. an ex or critic is a peadophile) = 1 shot
  6. Every time Tommy Davis refers to Sweeney’s RAEG last time = 1 shot

WARNING: Attempting to strictly follow the above drinking game rules will almost certainly result in paralysis if alcoholic beverages are used.
