NO NEED TO BE QUINN’S VICTIMS

3. Picketing protests at one or more of Quinn’s location (see Anonymous – the campaign group who organise highly successful anonymous protests against Scientology on the streets and on the internet)

5. Coordinating current information about Quinn with the reported and unchallenged information about him from the Seventies onwards.

His fear and intimidation tactics aren’t working so well anymore which must surprise him as he’s one of the world’s great cowards. He’s relied also on sheep-like behaviour from his devotees but many are now breaking ranks and discovering some strength. Quinn is VERY vulnerable and HE knows it which is one of the reasons he needs so many puppets and stooges to hide behind who will lie on his behalf.
