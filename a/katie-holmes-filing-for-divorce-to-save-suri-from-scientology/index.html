Katie Holmes filing for divorce ‘to save Suri from Scientology’

Scientology

By Guy Adams

Tuesday Jul 3 2012

http://www.independent.ie/lifestyle/independent-woman/celebrity-news-gossip/katie-holmes-filing-for-divorce-to-save-suri-from-scientology-3155643.html

KATIE Holmes filed for divorce from Tom Cruise in an attempt to prevent her six-year-old daughter being educated at schools with links to the Church of Scientology, multiple sources claimed yesterday.The blockbuster legal battle became public on Friday amid disagreement between Ms Holmes and Mr Cruise over their daughter Suri’s attendance at the New Village Leadership Academy in Calabasas, California.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Village_Leadership_Academy

Ms Holmes (33) was raised a Catholic and is reported to have concerns about Suri’s religious upbringing. The academy, which the child joined in 2009, is officially secular and employs “study technology”, a technique created by Scientology’s founder, L Ron Hubbard, as one of a range of educational methods. Some of its teaching staff are also Scientologists.

The school is an unofficial feeder to the Delphian School in Oregon, a boarding school that counts Mr Cruise and his ex-wife Nicole Kidman’s adopted children Connor (17) and Isabella (19) among its alumni.

“Suri is coming to an age where she gets educated enough to get locked into the faith,” Marty Rathbun, a former senior executive in Scientology, said. “That’s why there’s almost certainly truth in the consideration that schools have started coming into play in all this.”

Mr Cruise is Scientology’s most celebrated member. He and ms Holmes were married in 2006 at a ceremony presided over by the church’s leader, David Miscavige. But they are said to have had growing disagreements over how the faith should be applied to rearing their child.

“Scientologists believe in reincarnation, that infants are essentially ancient adults being in children’s bodies,” a source familiar with the dispute said. “Tom treats Suri as a little adult. Katie takes the opposite view: that she deserves a childhood.”

Importantly, Suri has reached the age at which church members begin being “audited”, a form of counselling at the centre of Scientology.

A “security check” devised by Mr Hubbard for use on six-to-12-year-olds requires them to be asked personal questions while attached to an “e-meter”, measuring electrical charges carried by their body.

The list of questions includes: “Have you ever gotten yourself dirty on purpose?” And: “Have you ever told bad stories about someone?”

‘Check’

Ms Holmes is apparently anxious to prevent her child from being subjected to any such “check”. She is therefore reported to be seeking sole custody of Suri, a move that would give her the right to determine her religious upbringing.

The actress filed for divorce in New York, while Mr Cruise (49) has counter-filed in Los Angeles. Both jurisdictions will take a similar view regarding the distribution of the couple’s $275m (€218m) fortune, but New York courts tend to grant sole custody, while joint custody arrangements are more common in California.

Ms Holmes is perhaps also anxious to prevent history from repeating itself. When Mr Cruise divorced Ms Kidman, he secured a joint-custody agreement. The couple’s children grew up as members of the church and today have a relatively distant relationship with their mother, who is not a Scientologist.

Mr Rathbun says he “audited” Mr Cruise at Scientology’s headquarters in Los Angeles between 2001 and 2003, when the actor was divorcing Ms Kidman. He said he saw Connor and Isabella, who were six and eight years old at the time, being introduced to auditing.

“Everyone was thrilled to see Connor playing on an e-meter,” he said. (© Independent News Service)
