The cult of Quinn: Memories of the early days from a definite non Educoist with cop on.

Tony Quinn

Having visited the Templeogue house around 1973 was an experience. Tony sat in a chair while all his devotees sat on the concrete patio around him. I remember one girl being very sick and my friend whom I was visiting there told me she had been on a forty day fast and wasn’t able to keep water down now. Tony was ‘teaching’ them about diets and fasting. One wonders what the long term effects this had on all the people he experimented on.

We then visited where she slept and what a surprise that was, a large room with perhaps 7 or 8 sets of bunk beds. No privacy whatsoever. The price she was paying was the price of a single in town, yet she was sharing with possibly 14 other people.

Some of the devotees were fluctuating in weight from very thin to fat and thin again. They paraded about on stage in bikinis and made every effort to look their best for Tony.

Hamhaugh island, well a box room had a set of bunk beds sleeping two people. No sense of sharing when it came to meals. Everyone got their own and sat on their own in the grounds [summer 1981]. They didn’t seem to give any vibes of care or friendship there. Tony had his own chalet more like the size of a barn, and of course he needed that for his study as he was doing his PhD.

The house there had many boxes of Quinn yoga leaflets and my friend having come back from work tired felt it was her duty to go out distributing them, I was horrified seeing he had no respect for others.

It was so important to keep Tony happy.

This really amazed me when my friend in the Quinn organisation told me that some of the ‘clients ‘ on one of his courses were absolutely amazed to see Tony needed to go to the toilet. They thought he was God!

Hicks Tower is cold and has a dark feel about it. In the kitchen, only the best exotic fruits are prepared for the guru and the best meals prepared too. Funny thinking about fruit met some of the local kids who have long since grown up. They used to climb over the wall to steal apples, and Quinn would try to put a spell on them.

They thought he was a joke  and ran off. Actually have since met someone who used to visit the Dutch family that started Wavin Pipes who owned it before Tony got it.  They used go all the way up to the attic room which I understand now has a poet living there.  Also it seemed that the property purchase was quite complex and Revenue were involved. Also that guy Derek Lawlor had some experience in property. No one seems to know where he is now.

He has a chalet there in the grounds and his gym.

Over the years only very few visits from my friend were made to family and none to friends, and these were kept very short and eventually fizzled out. I heard that whenever people rang looking for people living there the call would be cut short as Tony’s immediate needs had to be catered for.
