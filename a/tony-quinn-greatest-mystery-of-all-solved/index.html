From Tony Quinn

Man, Woman, the Unknown

the Greatest Mystery of All

SOLVED!

(Each time Quinn sends out an invitation it is couched in enigmatic esoteric terms t create a sense of mystery. One has the feeling he is going to produce a great revelation. The reason it is kept secret is obvious to not let the information get out as most people would regard it as quite amusing and repetitive. Tony is going to unlock the secrets of the universe. It has that Life of Brian feel to it.)

Know Yourself – Sing Your Song

(With this new approach it is now easier than ever before)

 (Note with Quinn it is always easy, effortless. Easy Rider, not real effort, just an effortless opening of the pocket and the cash flows…… Tony is now doing the X factor.)

Drop everything, this is more important! Come and meet with me on Sunday June 9th in Clontarf Castle for just a few hours. I would like to sing to you our song of life. After which you will know yourself and can leave singing your song. Please make this opportunity available to us. This meeting is not just to hear about, but instead together, us making it come about.

 (This is the usual stuff, no added reflection just repetition?)

 We will explore the possibilities of a new you, with a new life, a new society and a new world. All of which can begin for you immediately, if you so choose.

 (Would it be nice to have a new Tony helping those around him? This is not about helping, but helping oneself. It is another variation of an old theme. Using other people’s money – OPM!)

 The Key To It Is You

 Once you know yourself, you will see how it all becomes possible. This gives you the means and the power. Now it is more effortlessly possible than ever before.

 (Yes you are the key to break from this conditioning, but it is not effortless and will require help to break a life time of brainwashing.)

 The Truth

 If the truth of you ever becomes common knowledge it will change the world. This is THE Breakthrough – Be there to Pioneer it.

 (Unfortunately, if Tony met the Truth he could not cope with it. He deals in evasion and avoidance of the this precious commodity.)

SPECIAL NOTES:

I want you to see my New Video on “Know Yourself” at 12 noon sharp.

 (Could this be the end of the Seminar circuit and the beginning of Tony’s new meetings online? Now I doubt Tony will be there at 12 sharp, but he may feel comfortable this time to start on time because there is no protest going on? However, we decided to visit the Clontarf Castle and to observe this possible exit meeting.)

 Kindly bring your notebook.

 (It is quite remarkable to see people bringing note books sometimes quite expensive for these sessions. Like new students on the first day of school.)

At present I have no plans for further meetings.

 (Note the sting in the tail. A bald statement which is not very clear, and leaves you having to come to see if this is just another sales pitch or for real.)

 

With Love,

Tony.
