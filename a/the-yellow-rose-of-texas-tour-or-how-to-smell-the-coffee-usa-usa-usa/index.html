Why are so many leaving? The Ohio house of prayer is no longer mentioned on their website, and Mexico also seems to be missing. Yet the duo continues unabated.

Over twenty years now, they have gone from one fundraising venture to another, both here and abroad. Fundraising to such an extent that many have been left almost destitute. Their heart rending stories of how the money was extracted and how they were to tell nobody. A regular phrase used by Fr. McGinnity,

“Do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing”. The works/projects were always so important that they had to be kept very confidential, even as far as not discussing it with family members as they may not understand.

The house of prayer in Ireland lost its charitable status some years ago. Yet the houses in America continue to enjoy the privilege.

Will the duo bring home some of the loot to Ireland? Or will they fill the vault with gold bars? It will be interesting when the revenue commissioners start to investigate those gold dealers and who their best clients are!

Let’s hope they all have a good holiday and not endure too much suffering. We await their return and the meeting we are seeking for such a long time near the Liffey.
