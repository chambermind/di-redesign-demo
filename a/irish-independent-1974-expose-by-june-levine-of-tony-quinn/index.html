Irish Independent 1974 expose by June Levine of Tony Quinn

Tony Quinn

The Irish Independent 1974 expose by June Levine on the Tony Quinn Cult (The Tony Quinn Yoga Group as he called it then).  The article was written after a visit by her to the Templeogue commune where Quinn ruled his approx. 50 and manipulated their thinking and lives.  Some of this comes across clearly in this article.

A very hard hitting expose report on Quinn was written in the News of the World in 1978 which reported as it said the false claims by Quinn about his healing system. It show the long standing pattern of Quinn mentally manipulating people, many of whom had lives wrecked by Quinn and are long since swept under the carpet.  The victims defend the indefensible (Quinn’s character and behaviour) only to find out later at first hand how badly this sociopathic, power and money obsessed false messiah can wreck a person’s mind and life. Notice also the ezrly biography and his father’s comments. Also the continuity with the present. Similar patterns come through to the present. Notice the early emphasis on Quinn as a kind of Christ figure. QUINN_1974 ARTICLE BY JUNE LEVINE
