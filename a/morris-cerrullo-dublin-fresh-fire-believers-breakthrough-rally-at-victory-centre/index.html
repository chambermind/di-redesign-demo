Morris Cerrullo

The current cry of “don’t judge, don’t judge” has for too long been the gag used to prevent the expression of concern in church circles. So first things first , I would ask you to consider the words of the Apostle Paul in Galatians “If any man preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached let him be accursed”. Very strong words, but Paul was dealing with a teaching which sought to bring the new faith back under the law and away from the doctrine of grace. Paul deals with this time and again, so it is no surprise that we deal with it still today. Sadly however few will come out and call these teachings what they in fact are “false gospels”. They are not just teachings which emphasis aspects of the real gospel, but are errant and dangerous, and most often the purpose is that which the Apostle Peter wrote “to make merchandise of you”.

It has come to my attention that one such false teacher Morris Cerrullo, is due to speak at the Victory Centre in Firhouse/Tallaght at the end of May this year.

May 29, 2012

Dublin Fresh Fire Believers’ Breakthrough Rally

| 7:00 PM | Dublin  Ireland

http://www.mcwe.com/events.php?m=5&y=2012

This event like last years’ visit by Benny Hinn is not advertised on Victory’s web page, but leaflets are available in their lobby. Perhaps this is intended to keep a little distance, to maybe just stay beneath the radar as this ministry has been the subject of legal action in the United Kingdom in relation to false advertising claims.

The promo leaflet “fresh fire, a believers breakthrough rally” promises to go beyond any other event in healing, power, teaching, blessing anointing and worship”. One wonders against what background Cerrullo makes this assessment. Interestingly the following month he is advertising “The Last great anointing”.

So who is this man, and what does he teach? Much of what he teaches comes from a mixture of Latter Rain, name it and claim it theology and from Word of Faith, so he draws from a very mixed bag of tricks. But let me share from his current pod cast on the upcoming Passover fast which he is plugging on his site.

Speaking to born again believers, Cerrullo tells his listener “God has commanded us to celebrate the Passover every year”. Really? Surely the very context of the final meal Jesus shares with His disciples is the opposite of this and we now celebrate the death and resurrection of Jesus.

Speaking of his preparation for Passover Cerrullo tells us “God has commanded us and I have prepared a Passover altar”. The Exodus makes no mention of an altar. By sending your prayer requests for Cerrullo to place them on his altar and cover them with a special oil prepared for the occasion , your prayers for all known requests will be answered. The prayers of some believers evidently need his participation for success and answer.

At this special Passover Cerrullo promises nine special angel revelations. However he quotes what are a collection of promises made to Israel in the text of Exodus, putting a word of faith spin on them. He therefore is ascribing to Angels the words of God, which need no revelation as they are written in the scriptures. Anyone who has read the book of Exodus has had the promised revelations already, but from God who according to Paul “inspired all Scripture”

Having promoted his Passover event Cerrullo asks “ so what must you do”? . No need to wonder because God has already told Cerrullo the answer. He says “I asked God what should they do and God command that you lay before Him in worship and make a Passover offering.” So here we see it, to get favour from God I must make an offering, but not just an offering but a specific offering. This offering will release the Angels revelations a book and a bottle of oil. It all seems a bit strange but Cerrullo has also spoken to God on your behalf. “I asked God, why $92 why such a strange amount? I have asked for $1000 and even bigger sums, why $92”.

Why indeed? I already have the text of Exodus and I can research angels myself for free on the internet, and do I really want a bottle of non-descript oil? But wait God can explain, now bear with us, “ God told me there are 9 secret revelations so that’s where the 90 comes from”. OK so about $10 per revelation, “but Lord why the two dollars? Why such a small amount, and He told me this is 2012, so it’s a strategic time and season” Well there you have it, it appears that God so loves us that he will squeeze out that last $2. Utter lunacy, a false gospel of absolute mixture.

At a time when our nation is on its knees from the effects of corruption do we really want Christ represented by this sort of stuff? This man and this ministry is interested in only one strategic thing and that is the advancement of their own wealth and they do not care who gets hurt in the process. The leaflet is looking for the sick the weak and the needy to come, why so that they can feed on them.

I am asking all who know the scriptures to discern that this is not a ministry of the true gospel and join with me in telling those you are under your care to stay away from this event. I would further call on Victory Centre leadership to reconsider the staging of this event.
