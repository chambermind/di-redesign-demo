Kay comm2post Belfast Telegraph: Baroness of the Belize oil fields

2010/01/19 at 7:35pm

I’m not an oil investor but am hearing many accounts of lack of usual business procedure, lack of accountability to shareholders, lack of transparency in decision making, trying to appoint a notorious cult leader to the board, apparently a lack of published audited accounts, delayed or non-existent dividends, very ‘unusual’ activities regarding loans (subject to approval of certain individuals), etc etc. Even in a ‘non-conventional’ set up, a company can’t just make up its own rules.My advice is keep investigating, keep building the dossier on the activities of those in question, get more advice, build connections with trustworthy individuals who are in the same predicament. Don’t be terrified by flimsy legal threats and recognise the culprits are not that clever even if they’ve bought off or intimidated opposition or those who would scrutinise them in depth. It takes time to win the battle and things aren’t going very well for the mucky messiah, Quinn which means that his puppets are having to cope with increasing difficulties. They are getting increasingly mucky and increasingly discredited by association with the “messiah”.
