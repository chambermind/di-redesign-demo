Who are Michael Caulfield and Bishop Ralph Napierski?

The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge

 The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge

The first person, Michael Caulfield  is difficult to identify. He is a businessman  who formerly was involved with the William Kamm, also known as The Little Pebble. Kamm is the leader of a religious group called the Order of St Charbel which claims to be part of the Roman Catholic Church and due to be approved by the hierarchy. The Holy See, however, does not regard the group as being part of Roman Catholicism.

 http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Kamm

Recently Michael Caulfield seems to have dropped any reference to Kamm and has built his own Church in Ballaghaderreen, Co. Roscommon. It is called, The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge.

We asked the Bishop of that Diocese which is Achonry whether they had even heard about Michael Caulfield or The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge? However, they had not. We would have not heard anything about them either if it was not for a Bishop Ralph Napierski.

In a document concerning his defence of his apparent attempt to crash the Papal conclave we come across two photographs linking him to the The Congregation Of Our Lady of Refuge.

http://napierski.files.wordpress.com/2013/03/conclavebuster-bishop-napierski.pdf

We have been receiving reports about Michael Caulfield for a number of years, first linking him with William Kamm, then we also received reports that he was connecting with Joe Coleman and with the House of Prayer.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/house-of-prayer/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/joe-coleman/

Very recently we have heard about the connection with Bishop Ralph Napierski.

DI is not concerned about the beliefs of this group, but rather the undue influence exercised over individuals through the group. In other words our remit is the abuse of the human rights of vulnerable adults, not whether this is an Orthodox Catholic community. We invite comments to get more background on this group.

We will explore some of the themes of authority in the Catholic Church in commenting on Bishop Ralph Napierski. We will post material on the Bishop to expand these issues.
