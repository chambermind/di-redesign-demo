Sunday Times September 2005

Scientology

Sunday Times September 2005

Having listened to the Church of Scientology’s views on adult literacy in prisons, the Oireachtas education committee will soon, presumably, be inviting Satanists to outline their thoughts on discipline among preschoolers.

Labour’s Jan O’SuIlivan, a Committee member, explained her indulgence of the Scientologists’ publicity stunt by saying she knew little about the controversial cult or its reputation for mind control.

0’Sullivan should glance at the 1998 prison chaplain’s report on attempts by Criminon Ireland, a Scientology-backed group, to recruit prisoners through a correspondence course. She might then realise why Scientologists are so keen that convicts can read.

Liam Fay
