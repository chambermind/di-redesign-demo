These phrases I mentioned that are part of Tony Quinn’s ‘philosophy’ are abusive in the sense he uses them to break down any barriers to gain access to their unconscious minds. Phrases I can remember being used to hypnotically induce indoctrination are ‘no thought’ ‘let go’ ‘no effort’ ‘do nothing’ and ‘trying is lying’. I’m sure there are others.

The suggestion for ‘no thought’ is to bypass the critical mind so there is no interference in taking on what he says. The explanation given is they have accessed a higher state of mind; in other words, if a person agrees to allow this process they are held in high regard to the rest of the group and encouraged to allow the same process to occur. They have opened themselves up to being manipulated and conditioned by Quinn with no real understanding of what this involves or the outcome he has in mind for them.

‘No effort’ is exactly that. They prefer it when people make no effort to do anything or make any changes in their lives without input from the cult leader or influence by ‘top brass’, someone high up in the organisation.

‘Do nothing’ fosters the idea that changes will happen by totally depending on the ‘energy’ regardless of the request and if you don’t get it you are likely to hear something along the lines of ‘it was not meant to be’ or ‘it is not the right time’ or ‘you must not really want it’ or ‘you need to stop interfering’. The great ‘results’ published were given by indoctrinated people who allowed Aideen Cowman to change/exaggerate the results of Tony Quinn’s ‘prayer’ on their behalf.

‘Trying is lying’; the indoctrinated have no difficulty in repeating what Quinn has programmed them to say. They do not have to ‘try’ or ‘think’ about it and so will not say anything other than what comes to their programmed minds when talking about Quinn and his ‘philosophy’ or results and can become part of general conversation. In other words, many of them don’t realise what is occurring and repeat what was planted in their minds. Since they are not ‘trying’ they are not ‘lying’. However, it is interesting to note that Quinn tended to focus (trying) on particular people on his seminars and deliberately appealing (lying) to some aspect of their character purely to draw them into his cult. Those who had Christian beliefs were told what he did would enhance their beliefs. Religious/spiritually aware people who attended seminars and Eccles Street did not realise, at that time, that Quinn is opposed to any Godly religion/higher spiritual awareness that curtails his personal aims or actions. Why else would Aideen Cowman refer to him as God? It demonstrates Quinn’s need to have their belief in God or Jesus transferred to him. This aspect of the conditioning process was obvious when I witnessed the group showing signs of worship towards him.

When I see the above phrases together they give the impression that the less active cult/ex-cult members are in making decisions in their personal lives the more powerful the cult leader is. The cult’s constant use of such phrases explains the inertia and automatic trance state that occurs when in Quinn’s or ’top brass’ company and the holding on to the educo belief system of ex-cult members. I very much doubt that I was the only one who realised how evil they are when there is resistance to their plans. Fear and inertia have kept people bound to Quinn’s influence and are the biggest hurdles to overcome when wanting to gain back control of their lives. They need to accept that the biggest favour they can do for themselves is to think about the experience itself and make an effort to do something about lessening the effects and, if at all possible, become active in curtailing the activities of Tony Quinn and everyone associated with him. Ask yourself these questions:

Are you still attracted to being with people who went through the same process?

How would you feel you would cope if you were to disconnect from them?

Do you hold on to his ‘philosophy’ to prevent feelings of depression or loss?

Can you believe in yourself without his ‘philosophy’?

Are you attracted to other groups that use ‘energy’?

Tony Quinn’s conditioning methods can change people’s feelings and attitudes in a negative way. How does it manifest in your life?

Do you use ‘energy’ to attract, gain control, and manipulate/abuse others?

Have your relationships with your family/friends suffered/suffering because of Tony Quinn’s influence on your life?

Do you still believe in the abilities you had before his mind-set changes and are you capable of using these abilities in the future?

It is also known that many people left their regular jobs after attending a seminar during the time of the Celtic Tiger. How has leaving your job benefited or hindered your future prospects?

What personality changes have you noticed in particular and how have they impacted on your personal/spiritual life and work?

The increasing need for educators to work in this area should be a concern for the government. Cult awareness, as in the outcome of indoctrination and the truth about the people who lead these cults, should be part of the educational curriculum. People who are damaged by the likes of Quinn need to break through their inertia and, at least, post about what happened to them so as to increase our understanding of the effects of mind control. You are instrumental in putting a stop to the cycle of cult abuse.
