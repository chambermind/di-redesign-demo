Sheila: The difference between exiting Educo and being thrown out

Tony Quinn

Having had opportunity to work with people now who have been involved with Tony Quinn for over twenty years I would suggest some issues are now becoming clear.
Those who have copped onto his various stratagems usually under go a deep sense of shame, then depression and then a great anger and even rage. I remember the partner of one person who saw Tony outside the GPO and wanted to strangle him. His wife had been conned into a seminar. An earlier generation of Quinnites were more spiritually inclined and had the sense of like Judas betraying their Lord. He was not quite the Jesus of Christian tradition but rather an occult Jesus á la Aleister Crowley the beast 666. Many women who approached us have had a psychiatric lifestyle after meeting up with the Messiah.

Quinn was ever the miser and he developed a very coherent way to use this view point to his advantage. It was called UPM, USE other People’s MONEY. This created a following who wished to live the 5 star lifestyle but in reality they were living on empty and were drained of all their resources, body, mind and spirit.

The early Educo was a pyramid scam and it was based on repeating the mistake of going on the seminar till you used up all you had. However, once the person realised they had been scammed they were with help able to rebuild their lives. Most in this category move on and in fact Dialogue Ireland is no longer on their horizon as they get on with their lives.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/10/15/the-spotlight-on-quinn-the-bbc-goes-to-town/

At 1min 48 in the Spotlight programme (1) Glen O’Callaghan one of his most dedicated member’s talks in terms of his business going through the roof while in reality it has gone through the floor. Not good when you sell carpets. When you have a positive construct that nothing negative can affect you reality is very difficult to face. Programming has been so intensive and deep rooted it would take a massive amount of competence and skill to address this.

There is major shift with EDUCO/INE. Here initially Quinn is totally opposed to this as he sees it as a direct threat to his Educo business. However Sheila is the storm trooper for this and she swings into action.

Her audience are not your usual shareholders but those drawn from a wide background across the sectarian divide in N.Ireland.

Suddenly the oil is discovered and Sheila now becomes the star of the show as she is the answer to Quinn’s great dream to become a billionaire. From sources close to Quinn he already has around €100m, this then became his relentless aim and he used every trick in his arsenal to make this happen. Sheila had the where withal to organise things, but once the oil was found she gradually became less important as she had no expertise in running oil companies, as Justice Bannister observed it was just a matter of time before she was gone. More importantly she had no cash and this is where Susan Morrice came in. Coming from Protestant Unionist stock she had married a conservative Republican Jew Alec Cranberg.

He was mighty big hitter cash wise as we see from the early financing of the enterprise. However, after indulging his wife for some years he realised he was no longer dealing with a Protestant realist but with Merlquinn gobbledy Gook , the power within and It or was it ET? At 8 min 22 in the Spotlight programme (1) you can what Alec had to live with?

Now there was a deep synergy between Educo and INE, and gradually very ordinary people were being drawn into Eldorado. They kept close to Quinn and as the Cult leader he began to bind them to him. Selling shares at the Educo meetings, getting people to pay for seminars with shares. Giving people like Vincent Hartford who had worked for him all his life shares instead of a proper pension. Then being behind the attempt to shut out the very people who had brought him there. He got his two women to hand him the keys of the kingdom and shares to give a nightmare to the B shareholders but for him there was gold in them hills. No dividends and then giving people loans of their own money and him milking it for himself. The lowest of the low.

Now he had to get rid of Sheila she was after all the architect of the project, but he really needed to stay close to Morrice where the cash would come from. Also he wanted to be at her side when her marriage came to an end. First he would use the oil money to attack his enemy and claim he was under attack, then her own assets to assist him. What Justice Bannister called a private army? Johnson’s were employed to use every legal stratagem to come after DI, The Sunday World, and individuals who he wished to destroy before they got to him. Even after the judgement on  deposing Mike Garde to give evidence at the Denver Court case were thrown out an appeal was lodged with the Irish Supreme court. More money wasted with our friend in the background. Sheila was thrown out of heaven and as lost soul tried to fight back, but they tried to destroy her. At a meeting in Dublin last year Paul Marriot indicated he was going to do some heavy lifting and take on Quinn and Morrice.

Sheila was not addressing her cult involvement but trying to get her position and her share back. She was part of the dream and had been forced into a nightmare. She had broken from Quinn but seemed unable to break from his conditioning. Paul too went in and found the Quinn/ Morrice strategy when dealing with him was to withdraw, and avoid entering the legal arena. In the end they settled with him. It is clear from the judgement that Sheila felt she could emulate Marriot’s strategy. However, she had not realised now she was Judas, unlike Paul who was just a guy who drilled wells. This was personal and every legal stratagem was thrown at her. She was the anti-Christ  and worse she was no longer a believer.

Her weakest link was that she was not able to make the judge understand

the before and after.

She was under influence before and now she is not.

ST Sheila on oil

It is as simple as that. Some strange quirk made her refuse to face this fact and it may cost her, her fortune. Also she like Paul Marriot went in and left the B shareholders outside the door. The only thing that the Judge clarified in regard to her was that she was and is a director in a hostile company.

 Conclusion

[100] I will grant a declaration that the 2007 Operating Agreement is invalid and that Mr Quinn was never validly appointed to the board of INE. I will also declare that Ms McCaffrey has never been validly suspended from the INE board. All of the other relief claimed in this action is refused.

Otherwise he totally rejected her case:

[97] The second reason why I decline to exercise the power to dissolve on the application of SM Life/Ms McCaffrey is because Ms McCaffrey was complicit in many of the steps which have, in my judgment, given rise to a seriously arguable case that INE should be dissolved. She was party to the purported resolution of 7 August 2006, which was designed seriously to undermine the protection available to the Class B members and, whether reluctantly or not, she was party to the 12 December 2007 resolution which was designed to complete the process. She was party to the questionable allotment of 64,000 shares to Mr Quinn in October 2006 and to his invalid appointment as a director of INE. It seems to me that it would be quite wrong to permit her now to take advantage of these matters in support of a claim to have INE dissolved.

At the battle of Bewley’s where a bra was the place to stop the show, Sheila reacted with fury when the picture showing her signing over Quinn’s shares was shown.

At 12min 20 in the Spotlight programme (2)

Rather it was a time to stand and say folks at one time I was under his influence, but now I understand what happened to me, and I need you B shareholders to assist me, as far as Justice Bannister is concerned I am a busted flush. Before the meeting she was like a deluded Educoist trying to press the flesh and get every vote for the meeting.

Instead she tried to take over the room when Susan Morrice left the building and tried to become the saviour of the B shareholders at the EGM. She refuses to listen to the Judge who has spelled it out so clearly.

 95] Secondly, I would not consider dissolving INE without having heard representations from the Class B membership or, at least, without having given the Class B membership the opportunity to be heard on the question.

How is this done do not ask me,  but has it been done? No. The judge has already read the riot act to Quinn and Morrice and has told McCaffrey to stand back. The B shareholders can take her, but she can’t take them to his court.

Where are the shareholders now? It is quite clear that that meeting was the equivalent of the D Day landing for a lot of them. Yes there were still a cohort of members singing the glories of Quinn.

You are our leader as he is in the bunker in Malahide, but in reality there was a major shift that day.

This culminated with the Spotlight programme.

Seeing Quinn with Mary like a hen with her little chick was the equivalent of seeing the execution of Ceausescu. However, after his death they had a body, here Quinn or as the BBC for some reason called him DR Quinn went in and took more money off people. Rather as John McGhee put it at our  Protest,

‘Tony you snake oil salesman.’

However, they have gone a long way but they are still under influence and it is not that of Justice Bannister. Is that why the BBC at the end shows that photo of Sheila signing over the shares?
