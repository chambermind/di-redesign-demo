Modern Mystery School – Podcast featuring Dialogue Ireland’s MMS researcher

CultismThe Modern Mystery School

In December 2022 Dialogue Ireland’s researcher Ian O’Grady (under a pseudonym “Damion”) was interviewed by the “That’s Effin Weird!” podcast, where he talked about the Modern Mystery School (MMS).

⚠️ Given the name of the podcast, expect strong language. ⚠️

Listen to the podcast by pressing the “Play” button above, or visit this link and play it there. If you do not see the player click here.

Ian has an incredible knowledge of the Modern Mystery School and speaks kindly of Dialogue Ireland. He has worked with survivors of this group.

Ian wrote an incredibly detailed article about the Modern Mystery School with many of his findings in May 2021.

Last month Dialogue Ireland revealed how Vice had published material failing to mention Dialogue Ireland leading survivors away from a helpful resource for their recovery from their experience with the Modern Mystery School.

In May 2021 Vice released an article online about the Modern Mystery School that did not mention Dialogue Ireland but did refer to two other cult-websites that have not published any material on the subject of the MMS. This was admitted as an oversight by the author.

In April 2022 Vice released their Modern Mystery School documentary which again omitted any mention of Dialogue Ireland.
