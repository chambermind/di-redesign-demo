THE SWORD OF DAMOCLES FROM JUDGE BANNISTER HANGING OVER TONY QUINN

Tony Quinn

We have managed to obtain a copy of the BVI judgment on the case against Tony Quinn and Susan Morrice and the INE oil company. Every part of it makes very educational reading for those interested in cults – but we would like to start at the end. Judge Bannister sums up his opinion of how the oil company has been run since Tony Quinn became involved in a devastating paragraph – Clause 91 on page 22 of his 100-Clause, 24-page judgment.

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

These are the points which you won’t read in Susan Morrice’s PR handouts:

The company has been running on an illegal Operating Agreement since 2007, this having illegally replaced the proper Operating Agreement of 2002.

Tony Quinn has never legally been a member of the Board.

His share allocation is very probably not legal either – the only reason Judge Bannister did not issue a judgment on this point is that nobody asked him to – it was not part of the demands that Sheila McCaffrey brought to the Court, but he stated that he believed there was a “serious question” about it (also Clauses 24 and 28).

Without a judgment on the legality of Tony Quinn’s share allocation, no distribution of dividends can be made – because dividends have to be made to all shareholders (a principle called “pari passu distribution” i.e. you can’t not pay dividends to some shareholders because you don’t like them) – so you can’t pay a dividend without a complete legal list of shareholders.

The company has not held an Annual General Meeting each year as its legal Operating Agreement lays down – in fact only one, or at most two in the whole nine and a half years of its existence.

 Until Paul Marriott brought his case to Court, “a section of the membership” – from the details in the Judgment this seems mostly Tony Quinn and Susan Morrice, “felt entitled to treat company funds much as if they were their personal property” and “there is evidence that INE money is being used to fund litigation by Ms Morrice and Mr. Quinn in a manner not authorized by the 2002 Operating Agreement” – this is ongoing, did not stop when Paul Marriott brought his case.

This judgment hasn’t made either of the parties very happy, because Judge Bannister is clearly disgusted with how the company has been run and is not prepared to issue any resolution to the mess the company is in without considering the Class B membership. He doesn’t hold out much hope for the company’s future under the present management:

 “In this case the company’s true Operating Agreement has been flouted since August 2006 and has been completely disregarded since December 2007. The present Board shows no appetite for doing anything other than continuing to ignore the rights of the Class B membership under the 2002 Operating Agreement and its track record offers no grounds for believing its behaviour will change.” (Clause 91)

It seems to me that these considerations make it seriously arguable that it is not reasonably practicable for the business of INE to be continued in conformity with the 2002 Operating Agreement and thus seriously arguable that the power of the Court to decree a dissolution of the company under Section 52 [a reference to BVI company law] has arisen.” [Clause 92]

He has therefore strongly indicated his willingness to consider a petition to the court to

1. Rule on whether Tony Quinn’s shares are valid or not (Clauses 24, 28)

2. Order the company to be wound up (Clause 92]

Who does Judge Bannister want to hear this petition from? Basically, any shareholders who are dissatisfied with how they have been treated by the company. What he has made very clear is that this has to include Class B shareholders,

 “I would not consider dissolving INE without having heard from the Class B membership, or at least, without having given the Class B membership the opportunity to be heard on the question.”

An order to dissolve the company, as far as we are aware, would mean that a Liquidator would be appointed who could

  1. Sell the company’s oil reserves and other assets on the market
  1. Determine by examining and auditing the company’s records how much of the company’s expenditure was legitimate and how much was personal expenditure by members, Directors etc.
  1. Distribute the money raised from the sale of the reserves and other assets to the shareholders in proportion to their shareholding – money which had been spent on personal or unwarrantable expenditure, such as personal “jaunts” [a description used in the Judgment], legal expenses etc could be deducted at source from the payments made to the shareholder(s) in question, and the increase in profits would benefit the other shareholders.

The Class B members are therefore the key to letting the Sword of Damocles fall or giving another chance to the people who trampled on their rights, illegally discriminated against people they didn’t like, spent the company’s money as their own, and are still using it to fund litigation which is not authorized by the company’s true constitution, the 2002 Operating Agreement.

So, if you’re a Class B member, you’ve probably had more wooing since this Judgment came out than you’ve had since you parted from your money. Suddenly, everybody wants to be your friend! Riches will be flowing your way very shortly! You are the most important person on the planet!

Now you know why.
