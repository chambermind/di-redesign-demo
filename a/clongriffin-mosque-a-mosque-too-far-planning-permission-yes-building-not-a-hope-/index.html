Clongriffin Mosque a mosque too far! Planning permission yes, building not a hope in hell.

Conferences & CommentariesReligion

Abdul Haseeb in a phone interview with DI answered in a few moments the question we have been asking for a year. Where is the €40 million coming  from? He was very clear that they have no major funding or patronage source. So a lot of time could have been saved if they were asked some pertinent questions as to their funding. As it is it looks like they do have the finance to even put in the foundations. In reality the planning permission was the easy bit.

This project is pie in the sky. Up till now the rumours were that the funding was coming from Saudi or Qatar sources. No one has asked these basic questions and no one was available to answer these questions about funding.  The Dublin Welfare Society had no  contact details available  so genuine questions were greeted by cries of Islamophobia on our blog.  Also to have such a large mosque and other buildings flies in the face of the fact that whereas, they have answered the planning issues, they have shown no evidence that their is a community of Muslims to even make use of one tenth of the proposed space. 

http://dublinwelfaresociety.ie/

So this is really not a big bang but a whimper as, “The Dublin Welfare Society who are behind the development of the project stated that they are very pleased that permission has been granted with a statement on their website saying:

 “The project is expected to start as soon as sufficient funding (restored.)” are found. This is all a pipe dream with no solid funding to back it up. How much alone was spent on the architects and the planning process?

Earlier this month we reported that in reality there was no mosque but a store front in Clongriffin with an aspiration to see a mosque in the area , but with no obvious available funds to build a massivemosque as the fundraising dinner indicated.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/08/08/clongriffin-update-the-multi-million-mosque-turns-out-to-be-a-store-front/

The Director noted these further claims:

Speaking to TheJournal.ie, Abdul Haseeb, the project co-ordinator for the Clongriffin Mosque Project said “we are very excited about this development” adding “the project will come in around €40 million, so now that we have approval we are trying to see what is the best way forward. The way it works in Muslim community is we put out the word about fundraising to the local community and to other mosques, so we just have to keep working at it.”

This seems to be a Deobandi* project and with the changes at Clonskeagh and the fact that all these mosques represent totally different and competing directions in Islam it is highly unlikely that there will be a major funding stream to reach €40,000 not to mind €40 million?

* http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deobandi
