Life Improvement Ireland = Trojan Horse Scientology

Scientology

Life Improvement Ireland

https://www.facebook.com/LifeImprovementIreland?fref=ts

www.smarttest.co

The Scientology Dublin has come up with a new way to fool people by hosting a Facebook community called Life Improvement Ireland.

http://www.anonireland.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=287&p=1656

 There is no mention at all of Scientology, Dianetics or L. Ron Hubbard but if you link to the personality test you will discover in the small print on the last page*, once you have completed the 200 questions, that it is Scientology after all.

 People need to be warned about this deception.

http://xscn.ie/wp/recruiting/

Contact Pete Griffiths, ex Scientologist who is available for media comments on 086 3703811

 * The Personality Test was developed for the Church of Scientology. The idea was to create a simple way for people to identify areas of their lives which might be improved by different Scientology services. The test has proven to be very popular in its own right, and has been successfully taken by many thousands of people. Its accuracy is for you to judge, but a lot of those who have taken it have reported finding it highly accurate.
