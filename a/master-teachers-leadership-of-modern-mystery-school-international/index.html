Advanced Spiritual Training

http://mms.new-paradigm.net/instructors

The Modern Mystery School prides itself on being the true “Harvard of Metaphysics”. We provide training in all fields of the metaphysical world – from alchemy and astral travel to sacred geometry and ceremonial rites. We work intensively to provide instruction on not just the theory of cosmological order but also to show students, through experience and practice, how the mysteries impact our everyday lives.

Our students learn the truth about the world, the universe, and themselves in the same manner as the best metaphysical thinkers and practitioners throughout history.

Keeping in line with this method, the Modern Mystery School provides certification for students when they reach a point in their own practice and study to begin teaching certain course materials. The equivalent of a traditional college degree, the people who receive certification are at the forefront of the metaphysical world in understanding and knowledge.

They are truly the most proficient instructors and practitioners in the world today. In our organization there are several different types of instructors, each trained to provide instruction in specific fields – including specialty fields. They offer a variety of life transforming programs.

Master Teachers

The leading experts in their various fields of study, our Master Teachers are the most knowledgeable individuals and often teach specialty courses. In their experience and schooling they’ve earned the certification and equivalent of a PhD or Doctoral degree in metaphysics.

In the Modern Mystery School, all over the world, we provide international training programs. These are usually two weeks long and the coursework available in generally taught by our Master Teachers. The international programs – including the Know Thyself Program – are only available through these instructors. They travel to teach and instruct dependant on the need for instruction.

Gudni Gudnason

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/11/24/dr-who-gudni-gudnason-more-on-the-the-modern-mystery-school-cult-or-just-blarney/

Gudni is a native of Iceland. He is a humorous man who helps his students reach deep in their study of themselves to answer the question “Who Am I?” much faster than they would on their own. He watches with patience as the students evolve and in time go out into the world to teach and become masters of their own destiny and provide service to humanity. Gudni has been serving humanity now for over 30 years in this capacity – a long and honorable career that has touched many lives and given hope to the world. Gudni lives in Tokyo with his wife and two cats. He does not serve in any leadership within the Modern Mystery School, allowing the crew to captain the ship.  He watches as they each take their watch at the helm and steer through calm and rough waters alike.

*Hideto Nakagome

Hideto, as everyone knows him (except in Mexico where they call him Senor Herberto) is our funniest teacher, always able to get the students to laugh. He is also a deep-thinking Japanese who unifies western and eastern ways of thought. He learned firsthand about western ways when he lived in the US, in New Mexico, for fifteen years. He is a trained Tibetan Buddhist Priest and has served as a bodyguard for His Holiness the Dalai Lama. He is also a Japanese Exorcist. He lives in Tokyo, Japan, with his wife Luisa who is from the Philippines, their son Raijin, and their daughter Isis. He is a member of the MMS leadership of Japan.
