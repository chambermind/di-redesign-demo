The Irish Mail on Sunday 31/07/11 – Clarification on the House of Prayer

Conferences & CommentariesHouse of Prayer

 The Irish Mail on Sunday Correction 31/07/11. (4” col. on page 18)

 HOUSE OF PRAYER

On July 10, we reported that Mr Eric O’Prey had left his estate to the House of prayer and Christina Gallagher. The House of Prayer and Miss Gallagher have asked us to point out, and we accept that they have not accepted the bequest, as it is not their policy to do so where there is a dispute or controversy regarding it. No transfer of the property has, accordingly, taken place.

We are happy to make this clear.

end of quote.

1: Who did Mr O’Prey leave the estate to in the first instance, the H of P or Christina Gallagher? There is a big difference!

2.  It is not their policy to accept estates where there is a dispute or controversy!

Doest this mean they accept them when the media do not find out about them!

No doubt Tuesday we will be entertained again by the latest missive from http://www.christinagallagher.org/en/ along the lines of Tabloid admits lies about our beloved and esteemed visionary.
