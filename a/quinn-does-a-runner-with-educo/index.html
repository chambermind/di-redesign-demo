“It looks like Quinn is leaving Ireland as you will see below he does not have contact details for Ireland but only for the UK and the USA. I had the strange experience of the same English woman’s voice being on the answering machine voice message for both locations

It seems very odd that the Educo organisation no longer seems to have an obvious base in their home location, Dublin. Doubtless there will be devotees keeping the “faith” alive but have things really reached that stage of decline so quickly that it’s difficult to find a contact for Quinn’s operations in Ireland. Especially after the recent rally one might expect him to able to throw in some more cannon fodder into the battle to conquer Ireland even if just to say “it’s business as usual and the media exposes and other criticisms aren’t getting to me”. One might hope the messiah would stand up and counter his critics even for the sake of the people of Ireland who he’s been dedicated to saving for the last 40 years, at least according to him.
Nope, he’s away as quickly as possible out of Ireland and the devotees can deal with the fallout as usual. Absolutely typical of the “messiah”. He couldn’t escape the summons though no matter how much he hid behind others. It’s nailed in place now and he’s gradually getting nailed for his abuses. Enjoy the sun, Tony as long as you can while the devotees battle on in the cold and rain but you may be back sooner than you’d like to face justice and more dissatisfied customers here in Ireland. Spare a thought, Tony for the devotees who will need to work overtime papering over the cracks, churning out more of the same old spiel and outright lies than ever. They’ll be burning up the keyboards late into the nights on your behalf . They’ll have to work twice as hard to get new recruits in the gyms and elsewhere. Do you ever consider their mental and spiritual wellbeing? For the most hardened cronies of yours, the tiny number that have remained over the years, they’re deeply mired in the mess but for those just beginning to be programmed and conditioned into your system have you no mercy at all? I suppose the answer is pretty obvious. A man’s character is known by his actions. Enjoy the Bahamas while you can, justice comes to all finally.
