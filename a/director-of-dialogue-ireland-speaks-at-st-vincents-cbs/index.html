A former student of the school Paul Lennon was one of the people who brought out the truth about what was really happening in this so called Catholic Religious Order.

He is originally from Cabra and attended St. Vincent’s CBS Secondary School. He was one of the first Irish people to be recruited into the order along with Declan Murphy the brother of Mike Murphy of RTE fame.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/03/07/paul-lennon-the-misuse-of-power-in-religious-organisations-the-legionaries-of-christ/

He is an expert in the field of how you can have a cuckoo in the nest. Generally people do not believe you can have a sect inside the Church, Paul shows how this is the case.

He wrote a book about his experiences and do not be put off the title as it is more a than a tale of woe, but a moving story of a missionary priest who does not so much lose his faith, but restores our faith in humanity by his self giving defence of human rights.
