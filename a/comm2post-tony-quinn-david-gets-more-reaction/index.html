Comm2Post: Tony Quinn – David gets more reaction

Tony Quinn

We publish pro Quinn comments if they are at all relevant. We stress we are not going to censor any view even if it totally contradicts our position. We look forward to having David reply to those who commented on his piece.

David,

1. Why are there people who do talk about TQ as more than human ? And who act accordingly.
This is not merely 1 or 2 untypical individuals in Ireland, or something that happened at only one time, or happened a long time ago and has now been totally eradicated.
Also this kind of pattern has been exposed in many different settings, in various cultures and societies.

2. What is there about the approach of TQ himself , over very many years, and his whole organisation and their methods, which creates or facilitates that reaction ?

3. What has TQ himself ever done
[a] to counteract such reactions when they appear among some of his *followers*,
[b] to prevent their re-occurence among other *followers*, or [c] to educate the public generally on this kind of deplorable development ?

If there are not clear and credible answers to simple, direct and relevant questions like that, and they are certainly not known to me, I for one can only wonder what the real agenda and approach – and impact – of the whole TQ phenomenon may be.

There is a Gospel line which reads – *By their fruits shall you know then* -a piece of wisdom which also seems to me to speak to people of any or no belief.

Tom Carew
