Open letter to Cult Leader Tony Quinn

Tony Quinn

We are republishing this document posted to us last year again. In light of the revolutionary changes taking place in the Middle East in oil rich countries, there seems to a desire to throw off 40 year regimes that have oppressed and mentally destroyed generations. However, we see that when the people reach a point of no return they lose their fear. One senses that Quinn is getting close to that tipping point after he has enriched himself at the expense of so many. Ireland is waking up to this point as a country as we have reached rock bottom. It would not surprise me if Quinn would be getting close to this point in his reign…..Dublin Airport? Who will do it, and how? Remember Tony is so out of it he does not even know how to use a computer. Will he see the way he is walking close to the edge. He is trying to spin out the legal processes thinking he will side step this one. Beware of the Ides of March,  April is the cruellest month I know!

Open letter to Cult Leader Tony Quinn

Dear cult leader Quinn,

(Please note we address you as cult leader, not Dr., since we have determined that all of the degrees that attach to your name are fraudulent – none have been earned from accredited academic institutions – read on). Be advised that this letter will bullet point the frauds and injuries that you have caused to happen and now, like all dangerous cult leaders, you are finally exposed.

The harm you have caused cannot be overstated.  To minimize your influence on your followers could lead to the tragedies that we have so often seen when unsuspecting and trusting followers are trapped in lies and cult leaders like you, have their backs to the wall.  We have only to look at the horror inflicted in Guyana on the 900 innocent followers of Jim Jones.  Or consider Charles Dederich and his Synanon cult who ruined the lives of thousands.  Lately, in Arizona, USA, the cult leader and life style guru, James Arthur Ray (who, like you, teaches his followers to “live in the moment” and “reject negativity”) and his Angel Valley Spiritual Retreat Center sweat lodge caused the death of 3 followers and put 18 in the hospital.  Or the followers of Warren Steed Jeffs, head of the 10,000 member FLDS cult in the American South West who now resides in an American prison convicted of rape and incest.  The list goes on and on. Cult leaders like you will resort to unimaginable evil to achieve their diabolical ambitions.    Is it too much of a stretch to picture cult leader Quinn exhorting the faithful to drink his Kool-Aid?

INE/BNE

What started out as one man’s dream (Mike Usher) to create a source of prosperity for a poor people by tapping into Belize’s valuable oil reserves, and with two good friends to help realize this dream, has now turned into a disaster verging on a criminal enterprise where friendships have been destroyed and profits that should be dedicated to the welfare of poor Belizeans and divided among shareholders (many of whom are ordinary, working class Irish who, in these tough economic times clearly cannot afford to see their investment dream wiped out), are being drained from the corporation to satisfy cult leader Quinn’s insatiable appetite for harvesting profits and power.

This is your treacherous history:

  • You have infiltrated INE/BNE surreptitiously and illegally by acquiring a substantial shareholding and inserting yourself into INE/BNE’s management to make certain your ambitions are realized.  No one can determine the extent of your equity or how you acquired your shares.
  • You have caused INE/BNE to loan you a substantial amount of money, perhaps as much as $400,000 USD, to benefit your EDUCO enterprise, then induced INE/BNE to write off the loan.  (Page 9 of International Natural Energy, LLC Consolidated Financial Statements for the Years Ended December 31, 2008 and 2009).   (INE/BNE shareholders please take note: Standard practice in a privately held, closely held corporation: loans to officers must have the approval of 75% of shareholders (not directors) and the shares of the beneficiary of the loan do not enter into the calculation.)
  • In order to protect and grow your interest in INE/BNE you recognized the history of Belizean government officials to be corrupted and took advantage of this by conducting an EDUCO “seminar” at Cay Chapell Resort two years ago.  This was nothing more than an overt strategy to buy the influence of Government of Belize officials – all paid for by INE/BNE.
  • Requests by INE/BNE shareholders to have Belize’s Attorney General investigate the many improprieties of INE/BNE’s conduct are to this day stone-walled.
  • In order to bullet proof your activities from legal action you have engaged the services of many of the leading attorneys in Belize for various minor purposes – lease hold contracts, vendor contracts, etc.  Should shareholders or other interested parties wish to pursue legal action in Belize they will be met with attorneys’ inability to represent them due to a conflict of interest.
  • There are testimonials from INE/BNE members that they have been improperly detained behind locked doors for hours so they could be indoctrinated and brain washed using your own brand of mind control (your weapon of choice) to further your power and influence.
  • In a sordid and arrogant plan it appears that you are now attempting to illegally acquire shareholder’s original certificates, telling the bald face lie that the prestigious accounting firm of KPMG (who has not provided accounting services to INE/BNE since early 2009) has recommended that shareholders surrender their original share certificates in order to effect what you euphemistically call a “reconciliation” of shares to better arrive at the true value of INE/BNE.  (KPMG has responded with the following statement: KPMG’s Executive Director of Global Security, Charles  Steadman states “…..I do not know of a business reason for any KPMG member firm personnel to request a stock certificate or copy thereof directly from any shareholder since the company (INE) or its registrar/transfer agent would have this information.” He further states “Please be advised that KPMG LLP made no recommendations for INE shareholders to submit their original stock certificates as part of this reconciliation since that is a legal issue.” What happens now is clear – if unsuspecting shareholders tender their shares as you request, they will never be returned – shareholders will be left with a worthless receipt – and they will be denied any claim they might put forward to have their ownership recognized.  Why else would you require original certificates and not certified true copies if your “reconciliation” was in fact a bona fide registration plan?

EDUCO Blueprint for Successful Living

  • The testimonial of a muscle bound freak, an alluring young woman with large breasts and anecdotal evidence as to the efficacy of your supplements all make for snake oil pseudo-science.  Please enlighten us to the methodology you used with your “….university research conditions where mind-over-matter was applied to fat loss” (page 12 – Blueprint for Successful Living).  Enlighten us on these “research conditions….” cult leader Quinn.  Inquiring minds need to know.  Did you conduct a double blind clinical study?  Has your data been subjected to peer review?  How many subjects participated in your study?  In what recognized scientific journals (perhaps Lancet, Clinical Chemistry, JAMA, Nutritional Science, Journal of The Institute of Food Technology??)?  Can we review your data?  By the way an alumni search of the University of East London reveals no “Tony Quinn”, no “Dr. Tony Quinn” or any other type of “Quinn”.  Also, Brian R. Clifford who allegedly supervised cult leader Quinn’s doctoral thesis is not a professor at UEL.  He’s an undergraduate advisor.
  • None of your health food supplements have undergone a clinical study for safety and efficacy.  Most are simply plain L-amino acids or nucleic acids in a fancy package available in any reputable health food store or chemists for a lot less money.
  • You teach your followers to “live in the moment” and reject “negativity” and you do this for 30,000 Euros a seminar.  I feel so very fortunate to have saved my Euros – in fact I have already learned these attributes you teach from my dog.

The “noise” you hear cult leader Quinn is your victims’ groundswell of outrage.

A final thought for your consideration cult leader Quinn – in over 50 years of operating successful businesses and exposure to the rough and tumble world of business, I have seen businesses run by arrogant individuals that have become successful – I have seen businesses run by stupid individuals that have become successful – but a sure formula for failure is that of a business run by an individual who is both arrogant and stupid.

Cult leader Quinn you are arrogant and stupid.

Member INE/BNE
