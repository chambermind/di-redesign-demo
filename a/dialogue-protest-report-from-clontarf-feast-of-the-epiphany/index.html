Dialogue Protest Report from Clontarf, Feast of the Epiphany

Tony Quinn

Dialogue Protest Report from Clontarf, Feast of the Epiphany

QUINN GREETS THE SHRUNK FAITHFUL FOR A RALLY IN CLONTARF CASTLE   – SUNDAY 6th JANUARY 2013


We were there when they came……

Again Quinn had the same security group present added to by a man with a Trilby hat who seemed to think his attempts to take photographs of us protesting would add to our discomfort.  He also had his events team present at  Clontarf Castle. They were the same people that guided Tony in the back entrance at the Helix and who escorted  Susan after her rejection by the shareholders at the AGM last year at the Bewley’s Airport Hotel. Here they too they were a waste of space as Tony was already in the hotel or had come in by a back way as he started on time around 2 PM from our inside contacts. We had no more interest this time whether Quinn came in with DR Who or by space ship. The security team had not been able to secure the perimeter of the hotel like say at the RDS or the Helix where they had control. Here we had a hotel where the public was coming to have lunch, coffee or use the accommodation. His security detail was totally redundant as they were trying to do the impossible work out who were Quinn’s enemies =anyone who is not in the cult. They were not needed as again it was paranoia rather than any threat which was evident. The hotel could have dealt with any threat and the Gardai were just down the road. Most of the guests thought they were at the cross between an Anyway meeting and the feeding of the five thousand.

Some Educoists were camera shy!

The Mucky Messiah, Quinn, still tries to mix oil and (cultist) muck.  The latest stage-managed outing for the aging cult leader in Clontarf Castle today was a far cry, however, from the Educo heyday when hundreds were fleeced by Quinn by attending his Educo “philosophy” seminars.  In those days at the RDS he could motivate his flock to convince hundreds to attend his mind-expanding / mind-bending sessions.  The gathering today to give support to the unwell cult leader was composed of the remnants of the old guard from the 70s and 80s and some younger ones though overall the low numbers and enforced positivity couldn’t hide the fact of the main Educo event of the year, the January blessing from the messiah, was being held in a room which gave the impression there were more there than there really were. RDS to Helix and it is going down. However, because of the location it was difficult to measure the exact numbers.

So in January 2013, the messiah appears to the faithful flock (many of them now thoroughly sheared of money, normal relationships, mental independence, etc) for the yearly blessing and main pep talk of the year.  The motivational speech with the usual crowd included those drafted in to have enough numbers so Quinn’s ego wasn’t completely deflated, had an element of anxiety about it.  This wasn’t just because Quinn and Co are so easily spooked by seeing protestors and people associated with Dialogue Ireland in the same building as themselves.

When the Director of DI sat down about 2 metres from the entrance to the Conference Room it became clear that this strategy of using the Clontarf Castle had down sides for them.  Here once anyone sat anywhere they could not be touched. I proved the point by sitting as close to them as possible. We knew Susan was familiar with Clontarf Castle as a location as she used it last September according to our sources to promote her position for the AGM.

The move from the Helix to Clontarf Castle showed they recognized our logistical victory last September.

We had moved on from that. We were not concerned what they did we wanted to make a public protest and had no interest in trying to interfere with his mind games seminar. The management were advised of my presence and suggested I should leave. I asked to speak to the General Manager and reminded his agent I had already written to them explaining I had no desire to engage with the Educoists as we would respect private property and would be using the facilities like all other patrons. We would only protest outside the hotel. Talking to the staff and having had lunch at the hotel 2 weeks earlier it was clear that patrons felt intimidated at the front entrance to the hotel when they were being asked their business. The restaurant was quite quiet as a result.

Normally you have a ‘security presence’ not an ‘security absence’ when there is no one to protect?

I never heard from the management and after an inordinate delay got my coffee.

Patricia Fitzpatrick came close and saw me and nearly passed out with shock?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/01/05/taoiseach-enda-kenny-receives-book-the-magic-in-you/

At one stage Susan Morrice had her back to me and was about a metre away. She had stayed the night in the hotel and an English group who were over for the weekend saw the Educo label on her and started looking it up on Google?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/01/03/susan-morrice-and-the-new-age-unwrap-yourself/

Our English Visitors, and the cup of coffee…should have called room service!

I explained what was happening and they went into town and saw the Tony Quinn Shop in the Jervis Centre. They were somewhat confused at the attire of some of the women. The English specially found it bizarre how the people coming had brought their own water and food for the presentation. Some left their refuse on the tables in the foyer. Some came with gifts for the Messiah as it was the Feast of the Epiphany or Little Christmas. Others again as we had noticed at the Helix event looked like painted Jezebels, with provocative skin tight clothes. One in a golden number looked totally out of place as if she was still up from the night before. Brash and truthfully lacking in taste. Next to them what looked like pilgrims to Knock on a day out with Rev Tony. Then the mark of the beast they all had these expensive journals with a hardback. I noticed Susan had hers tightly clasped.

There was a desperation to put on a brave face and put the best “spin” (denial and delusion here) on the state of things in Educo world.  The year 2012 is a year that Quinn and closest Educoists want to try and forget as far as possible, though deep down they know the revelations about Quinn and his misdeeds will not go away.   Quinn and his closest associates just don’t understand the digital and internet age and they think they can manipulate people and the flow of information as effectively as they could in the past.  They are seriously wrong and Quinn’s cult misdeeds are available for all to see in many areas of the internet.  Quinn and his close associates would like to be able to manipulate even the internet but it just doesn’t work they way they think it does.  The backing down in the court cases, which were promised by Quinn to demolish his critics, have increased the feeling that things are not going well.  This underlying feeling is something that a motivational speech cannot change nor can the wild declarations of undying loyalty and hysterical promises and outpourings from the more unstable members of the crowd paper over the cracks.  A simulated party atmosphere with ladies in party dresses for their hero /love object wasn’t enough to lift the overall atmosphere and leant the feeling of uncertainty as to what the tone of the gathering was about.  Quinn is long known for his predilection for his closest women to dress very tartily, until, of course, they are too old for this.  We know he isn’t partial to women who are deemed to be ugly and they don’t get to be “close” to the messiah, unless they have some other function that makes them useful in the cult.

The old guard still turn up at the yearly blessing, like lost sheep still hoping that the messiah will finally deliver the goods after 40 years of waiting.  They can’t finally accept, especially investing their all in Quinn for decades, that this “emperor had no clothes”.  The younger ones at the meeting were more of the “business” side of the Educo cult.  Quinn mixes the glittering promise of acquiring remarkable money-making ability by practicing his “methods” for them with the possibility of spiritual enlightenment thrown in for those who may be lured by this noble idea.

Morrice, one of his most besotted women, a member of his “handbag brigade” as he dismissively call them, tries to buy the loyalty of wavering Educoists, using their own money (OPM – other peoples’ money, as Quinn calls his twisted version of the strategy).  The promise of better days ahead for all, and great oil riches to come, are meant to buy off any rebellion of the kind she experienced when she tried to stage-manage an oil AGM.   Mixing oil with cultism now seems necessary for Quinn and Morrice as perhaps there’s not enough interest in Quinn’s superficial “philosophy” to fill an average conference room of the kind the faithful were shepherded into in Clontarf Castle today.

DI Protest Team retired for lunch and then we were on duty to inform the residents of Clontarf and be there when the pilgrims dispersed. We got various reactions including someone who stopped in the middle of the roundabout to tell us we might cause an accident!

Glen O’Callaghan was charming as ever and waved. May he sell many carpets this year. May he receive his money back from all he has given loans to. Then the V for not victory sign and then a very emotional “You f_ _k__g  C_ _t which was a load of old b_ _ _ o _ _s as I am a man. The mass was over and like coming out of a Church car park some came out and nearly killed themselves coming out the entrance instead of the exit!

We were there when they left…..

Notice the star in the East illuminating our holy mission to bring to earth the light that is one way, “THE TRUTH.”

     We thank the management and staff of the Clontarf Castle for taking this most bizarre Conference in good humour and hopefully your local patrons can touch the spot where St Tony stood and delivered the Sermon on the Mount of  Olive Oil. As one member of staff said to me, “it is some kind of oil business meeting.”
