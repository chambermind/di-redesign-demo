4 lawyers?

Mr Moye whom Morrice assigned to parliament the meeting is lead counsel in a court case that INE have taken against Mc Caffrey, Lalor, Fennelly and Cornec. The previous legal firm had agreed a deal to pull INE out of the litigation in Denver but for some reason Morrice saw fit to hire Moye White, a new firm to start it up again.

There was also a lawyer there who had been with Josh Stewart at a meeting the night before. Stewart said he was his personal counsel.

There were also two more lawyers i.e. John Mc Mahon and Sarah Watson for Johnson and Johnson. They are the firm that represent Tony Quinn and Caroline Williams. They also were representing Susan Morrice in the High Court Hearing that tried to force Mike Garde and Nicola Tallant into a desposition.

So how many is that? 8 in total I think?

The other ‘director who never was’ (not a cent paid by him for Cornec’s shares and no investment reflected in the accounts) proved to be most arrogant telling the members they were stupid and not business people, as he was trying to distract shareholders from the illegality and oppression of recent years with an announcement of ‘good news’( a Quinn tactic). But the investors were not having any of it and were constantly telling him to leave that he wasn’t a director of our company. A farce indeed!

Justice Bannister had decried the failure of the INE to hold an AGM. No agenda was sent out in advance and the attempts to rail road through Morrice’s agenda failed.
