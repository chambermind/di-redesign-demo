Court of Law not turning out to be a Court of Praise!

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

Victory Case adjourned till October

The case was adjourned today Wednesday, July 24 till October. The injunction is to stay in place pending outcome of full hearing of action in regard the Victory and other properties.

Its a move to save costs, fight the same issues in one case.  A tactic engaged by those with no funds and no hope of success.

 

Our Legal Opinion:

 

Likely implications for Victory:

Weakening of the cohesion of the Fellowship as now they are informed of the financial activities that have being going on under the guise of a Charitable Trust. It is likely the enterprise will lose members. Our sense is that after the initial resistance reality is setting in as people evaluate their future.

They have also got a problem as they have cried wolf too many times. They suggested they would be back in the Victory building shortly in June. Now they have a pig in a poke or is it a Red Faced Cow?

DI has been surprised by the level of comment and the growing recognition that serious problems have been covered up for a long time.

The Prosperity Gospel:

Unlike other commercial ventures or other Churches affected by the down turn Victory was claiming that by making financial contributions in the form of tithes one could be prosperous and achieve success. However, Victory  loaded their messages with the notion that following  Jesus  their way was a road to fame and fortune. While the Celtic Tiger was at its peak this notion seemed to make sense, but with its collapse their project was devoured by a Roaring Lion

This was a theological heresy* and a scam.  *(Not the concern of DI.)

One of the noted visitors who was a pedlar of this prosperity gospel was Jesse Duplantis. Here his plane is parked in Galway as he attempted to get people to pay for his “ministry.”

Now that they are bankrupt and total failures their foundation is shown to be sand.
