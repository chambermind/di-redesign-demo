Losing our religion: Ireland nearly tops poll

Religion

11:14, 7 August 2012 by Post Reporter

http://www.businesspost.ie/#!story/Home/News/Losing+our+religion%3A+Ireland+nearly+tops+poll/id/19410615-5218-5020-e706-e42044931291

Since 2005, there has been a 22 per cent drop in the number of Irish people who say they are religious

Ireland is becoming one of the least religious countries in the world, according to a new global poll.

Since 2005, there has been a 22 per cent drop in the number of Irish people who say they are religious, the second largest drop in the world.

WIN-Gallup International, a world-wide network of leading opinion pollsters, has found that less than half of all Irish people consider themselves to be religious.

Globally, 59 per cent of the world said that they think of themselves as religious person, 23 per cent think of themselves as not religious whereas 13 per cent think of themselves as convinced atheists.

In Ireland, only 47 per cent consider themselves religious, placing Ireland in position 43 out of 57 countries.

While just ten per cent of Irish people said they were convinced atheists, the rest said they were not religious.

Since 2005 those claiming to be religious dropped by 9 per cent globally. This compared to a drop of 22 per cent among the Irish population claiming to be religious.
