How not to make friends? Jehovah’s Witnesses in the spotlight

Jehovah's Witnesses

Hi My name is Brian O’Donnell.  I was a Jehovah Witness for 17 years and a Elder. I discovered a lot about the history of cover ups etc., I was recently disfellowshiped but not given any reason why refused to let me have any independent witness present when asked to put the reason in writing again refused the is a lot more I could say want to know more you can contact me @ bodireland@yahoo.com

We hope to have a full statement by Brian  O’Donnell telling his story about this totalitarian organisation. This is the organisation which wanted to allow a Congolese woman die for for a doctrine forbidding her to have a blood transfusion when she nearly died after losing 80% of her blood.

Fortunately, a brave consultant stood up for common sense and kept her alive. The High Court upheld this decision in April 2008.

Here we have another horrendous teaching which the JW’ uniquely call “disfellowshiping.”

It is not a real word. It is part of a vicious control mechanism to isolate, and disassociate people who they can’t answer intellectually, morally or on the supposed teachings of the bible. It also has terrible social consequences as the person who experiences this  is not even allowed to eat with family members who are members, talk to friends who remain in, or even look at the person in case they may be drawn into their ungodly activities.

A person in Belgian has taken a case to the courts for the loss of social intercourse, we will have to test this issue in the Irish courts.

You must remember these are no ordinary Christians these people believe they are God’s organisation on earth and all other groups off line.

Most Christians are aware of the idea of excommunication. Generally this can result from heresy, denying the fundamentals of the faith. This generally follows extensive enquiry and a full process. Then there is excommunication for being a member of a violent group, or for moral failure like adultery or fornication. Its purpose is restorative, not punitive. Here it is like a religious fatwa

Here “disfellowshiping”  is used as a blunt instrument if you question any teaching, and it is used without any process.

Brian has not had a chance to present his view and he has been cut off from all his friends.

In Ireland we need to be aware that this organisation does not allow dissent and when another child has to be rescued by the High Court concerning blood transfusion do not allow your usual balance to be swayed by only allowing them to ramble on about non blood products. Did you ever hear anyone else giving an alternative view about it on the media in Ireland?

In fact JW’ will not be in the same room as an apostate, the condition you attain on becoming- “disfellowshiped.”

The next time a child or adult is put at risk by this group I will be leading a protest at their Bethel=HQ in Newcastle, Co. Wicklow.

I visited their centre in Patterson N.Y. in 2006. I was astounded to see that they had no theological courses, and that a group of untrained elders known as the Governing Body were making life and death decisions with  encyclopaedias?
