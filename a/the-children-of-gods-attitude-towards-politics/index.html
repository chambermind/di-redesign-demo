13 April 1978

The Children Of God’s Attitude Towards Politics

The Family COG

By Pernille Aagaard

“We are politically and socially conscious and want to have an influence in the world for good, and we believe in a social gospel as well as a personal gospel. We are also interested in social justice, economic justice, political justice, racial justice, ect.”

(Moletter “The Political System No.333, 1974)

In this quotation the Children of God (CoG) clearly demonstrate their political commitment. They seek to engage in politics, to take part in political discussions, to vote, to exercise influence. All these things sound very attractive, and there is nothing wrong in taking active part in politics. The question is what kind of politics they support, what systems and ideologies they are interested in.

CoG have a very special understanding of the political situation in the world.

“Right now the world is divided into three camps:

  1. The Fascists, of whom America is the chief and helps finance, arm, and train all other Fascist, anti-communist governments. (….)
    The Fascist, but so-called democratic governments, are actually the socialism of the rich, in which the rich get together and cooperate to protect and keep their wealth and enforce their form of government on the poor at the point of a gun. (….)
  2. On the other side of the fence is the totally communist world – marxist, leninist, maoist – all of which believe according to their own doctrines that there is no such thing as a peaceful revolution. It has to be by force: (….)
  3. But on the fence in between, all these years there has been developing the as-yet undecided or  uncomitted third world. In fellowship and doctrine and common cause of the poor and independent, they range from liberals and socialists to the so-called peaceful and legal communists found in such countries as France, Italy, Yugoslavia, Scandinavia, Britain, Africa, Asia, ect.

All these more moderate Third world people have been hoping to bring Socialism or some form of independent Communism by the ballot rather than the bullet.”

(MOletter “The Political System” No. 333, 1974)

This division of the world is the basis of the political thinking and engagement of the CoG. The most decisive part of this political argument is their NO to the US-system. The CoG is first of all a NO to the American way of life.

“In the 1960’s the kids in America were still rooting and rioting for revolution and they still had hopes they could save their country and its freedoms from destruction, war, pollution and death caused by the diabolical Systems of the older generations and their sins!

But the parents became frightened that their own children were going to wreck the country and go Communist and the older generation would loose its wealth, security and control, and socialize the Nation. Blindly failing to see the handwriting on the wall, the parents desparately got together and quickly elected the would-be Fascist dictators, Nitler, Agony, and their Nitzies to try to save them from their inevitable doom!

Until this awful election in 1968 of these Nazistic Republicans the kids  were actually beginning to win some of the battles, and, for a little while it looked like the country might even awaken and help its youth win their war against oppression and economic, educational, religious and social slavery! It looked like youth might even begin to have a voice in the Government of America and stop its stupid wars and exploitation of the poor of the world!

But the enemies of change, youth, and revolution, by hook and crook, managed to kill and discredit youth’s  only candidates, and the reactionary older generation, the enemies of peace and freedom who always resist change, won this tragic election by a very narrow margin and the kids were licked! In 1969, Nitler and his Nitzies began to crush their youthful rebellion by the most fiendish, brutal, violent, and repressive police power ever seen used in America since the days of the horrible slaughter of the Civil War of just 100 years before!”

(MOletter “Bye, bye Miss American Pie”)

In the end of the 1960’s the youth-rebellion broke out in America, and the CoG must be understood as a part of this rebellion. The young people fought against the same problems:
against an America which was considered to be forfeit to Fascism, a nation which was exploiting and oppressing other countries, against war, against social injustice, etc. They were fighting for some sort of socialism in America. But this rebellion was put down very quickly. When Nitler (Nixon) was elected president in 1963 the youth rebellion declined, and in 1972 it was nearly finished. The CoG however did not stop fighting, but since they could not work in America, they decided to leave.

When the CoG left America, they settled in many parts of the world, but especially in Europe. Their view of Europe is highly coloured by their attitude towards America. Europe is the contrary of America. America is considered a whore, whereas Europe is considered a sparrow.

Europe is very weak and very divided (no common language, no common monetary system, no common government) and this weakness is considered a force which makes Europe unable to go to war. The two major sections of the world, the Fascist and the Communist will fight one another, but Europe will live in peace, according to Moses David;

“Europe’s very weakness through its division is going to preserve it because it is weak.”
“So Europe’s very weakness is going to be her strength.”
“I never saw it so clearly before, why God has brought us here: – neither to Russia nor to America, but in the Middle, where we’re now safer than anywhere else in the world!”

(MOletter “The Meek” No. 205)

The CoG has a very positive view towards Europe, but they are even more enthusiastic about England.

England is considered God’s special country, “Even Englands position geographically is like an angel hovering over Europe -” but poor England has been raped by the rest of Europe.

“It’s as though England has been seduced into joining Europe, and, as a result, she’s going to be ravaged thereby.”

(MOletter “The Rape of England” No. 204)

But England, too, has become polluted by listening to Darwin’s “anti-God evolutionary theories”. The power of Darwins doctrine increased and consequently “the great Christian power of Britain’s rule waned and faded.”

But no matter what England is doing “Nevertheless, God is going to spare England according to how she  treats His Children: As long as she’s good to people like the Asians, us and Howard Hughes, God’s going to protect England and her interests.”

(MOletter “The Rape of England No. 204)

The CoG consider Europe to be a form of Capitalistic-Socialism, but they believe that one day Europe will come under “the final domination of world communism”.

(MOletter “The Meek” No. 205)

The CoG is working against communism as it is in e.g. Russia. But they are fighting for “a Godly socialism”.

This kind of Godly socialism they find in Libya under the leadership of Gadaffi, for Gadaffi is against America, against Capitalism and Materialism, as are the CoG.

“He is the only world political leader of any importance who is gloryfying God and saying that God is the only answer to its problems, and is offering the only Godly alternative to the Godless materialism of both Capitalism and Communism.”

(MOletter “Gadaffi’s Third World” No. 245, 1973)

But the CoG also have another interest in Gadaffi. In the spring of 1965 Moses David’s mother (Grandmother as she is called in the CoG) received a “warning prophecy” of the great confusion and of its author which were to arise out of Egypt:

“Turn your eyes towards Memphis, for out of it shall come the Great confusion. The Author of confusion is even now marshalling his forces for this Great Confusion. He is gathering his forces from a great nation and Eastern nations, friends that will join with him. So sudden will be the Great Confusion that it will cause a mighty widening of the eyes of those who have not discerned the signs of the times. But be yet not deceived. Be prepared: And be not deceived by the Great Society, for it will come to travail and then bring forth the Great Confusion. Be prepared: Even now the skyes are red, red with warning and black, black with clouds gathering for the Great Confusion which is almost upon you:”

(MOletter “Gadaffi’s Magic Lamp” No. 226)

And on February 5, 1962 the American mystic and prophetess Jeane Dixon foresaw a young man arising out of Egypt to rule the world.

“A child, born somewhere in the middle east shortly after 7 A.M. (EST) on February 5, 1962, will revolutionize the world. Before the close of the century he will bring together all mankind in one all-embracing faith. This will be the foundation of a new Christianity, which every sect and creed united through this man who will walk among the people to spread the wisdom of the Almighty Power.”

(“A Gift of Prophecy”, the phenomenal Jeane Dixon, by Ruth Montgomery, New York 1966)

These two “prophecies” are very important to the CoG. The CoG believe in a young man coming from Egypt (Anti-Christ) who is to start the Great Confusion, a third world-war, and after this God’s Kingdom will be on earth.

The CoG consider Gadaffi as a possible Anti-Christ (or a precursor for Anti-Christ, because Gadaffi is not from Egypt but from Libya), and they believe that he will be the author of the Great Confusion.

The CoG therefore is on one hand working together with Gadaffi in order to create a Godly Socialism, but on the other hand they are warning people against him be-cause of his being a possible Anti-Christ.

This connection between Gadaffi and the CoG also means that the CoG are using Gadaffi as a bridgehead to the Arab world. Friendship with Gadaffi also gives the CoG freedom to evangelize among those Arab people, who are the friends of Gadaffi.
