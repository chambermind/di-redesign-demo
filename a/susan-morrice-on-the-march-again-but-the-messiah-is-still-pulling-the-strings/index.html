Susan Morrice on the march again, but the Messiah is still pulling the strings

Tony Quinn

It has been quiet since this time last year. We are approaching the anniversary of the High Court decision by Justice Desmond Hogan which had two effects on the Quinn organisation. First he realised that a number of court cases he was embarked on were going to be viewed by judges through the lens of cultism. Secondly DI was helped in its work by a landmark judgement which was quite amazing. We were protected by the Constitution in educating the public on Cults.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/21/final-version-cornec-v-morrice-ors-judgment-by-hogan-j/

Earlier in July 2012r Justice Bannister had really laid into Quinn and Morrice about the total disregard they had for their shareholders.
Also he believed the company was being treated as a personal fiefdom by Susan Morrice.

The scene has gone silent and many of the B Shareholders have done side deals with the INE. No one knows what is going on. We can only assume there is a divide and rule strategy going on.

As we will see Susan Morrice is presenting herself as a model entrepreneur and is supporting golf tournaments etc. In her latest venture Clannah she is still putting our the Educo babble of Tony Quinn.

The media in this republic have still not got to the bottom of this story
