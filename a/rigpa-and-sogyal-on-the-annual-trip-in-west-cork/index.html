The summer retreat is our main event of the year, a unique opportunity to receive these teachings from Sogyal Rinpoche in the extraordinary environment of Dzogchen Beara. Everyone is welcome!

SOGYAL RINPOCHE is a world-renowned Buddhist teacher from Tibet and author of the highly acclaimed The Tibetan Book of Living and Dying. With his remarkable gift for presenting the essence of Tibetan Buddhism in a way that is both authentic and profoundly relevant to the modern mind, Sogyal Rinpoche is one of the most renowned teachers of our time. The atmosphere Rinpoche creates is one of incredible warmth, love, and penetrating insight, as if he were speaking directly to the heart of each and everyone present.

On Facebook

He is often called an incredible communicator, and people around the world remark on the power his teachings have to reveal a glimpse of the innermost nature of mind and bring lasting transformation and confidence in how to face the challenges of daily life.

More information and “Summer Retreat 2013” booking forms are available for download below.

9-day Retreat Fee: €490

To help make this 9-day retreat more affordable for all we have organised a bus transfer to/from Cork Airport (Friday 28 June and Sunday 7 July only), negotiated group rates for single- and twin-room accommodation in self-catering cottages in Allihies and Castletownbere, and a daily bus shuttle from Castletownbere/Allihies. For more details see the documents below and contact the Dzogchen Beara office.

Lunch and supper is included in the retreat fee (beginning with supper on Friday 28th June, ending with lunch on Sunday 7th July). All meals are vegetarian.
