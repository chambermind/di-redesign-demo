Phoenix  (July 16-29) 04

King-Size Problems

WHILE self-described cult-buster,Mike Garde, has expressed concerns over the Galway-based pentecostal group, King of Kings Fellowship (KoK) – claiming it has “cultist tendencies”, KoK had some other problems earlier this year when King of Kings Church Ltd was listed for strike-off by the Companies Office.

Happily, for the KoK followers the company provided the necessary accounts last month. Accounts for the year ended June 2001 show that the company received tithes and donations worth €44,000. The pastor received a salary of €25,000 in addition to €8,000 in expenses. King of Kings Church Ltd – which is limited by guarantee – was established for “the promotion of the Christian faith” and was formerly called Tuam Christian Fellowship Ltd.

Although Garde has accused the KoK founder, Mary Sheridan, of exercising a controlling influence over the group’s members – who tend to be mainly women – she has recently decided to take a back seat in the company, and resigned as company director earlier this year. These days the company directors include her husband, Oswald Sheridan, who is described as an “engineer”, Ita Walsh, “a pre-school assistant”, and Ruth McHugh a “ mature student” – all with addresses in Tuam, Co Galway.

The KoK also has charitable status which means that all donations made to the body are tax free.
