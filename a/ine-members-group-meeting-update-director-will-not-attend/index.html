Tony Quinn

INE Members Group meeting Update: Director will not attend.

Disclaimer: Dialogue Ireland Trust wants to reiterate that it has no interest in the oil dispute per se. However, we are of the opinion that it is the undue influence of Quinn that has led to the conflicts between Quinn and shareholders. It is only as our research shows that the connection to Quinn’s mental manipulation is clarified that those under his influence can free themselves and make the necessary recovery.

The Director of DI was unaware till approximately 18:00 Wednesday 2, March that this was a private meeting. Unfortunately the organiser Rob Henshall only contacted him by phone after he had left a comment on our blog. We had no intention of going to a private meeting uninvited. The Director of DI had been in touch with a number of people before he posted who suggested that he could attend. The Director of DI had already declared in the disclaimer above that he had no interest in the oil business. All he wished to do was to hear an account of the process which will bring this sorry saga to a conclusion. After all until an AGM takes place nothing can happen.

Dear Mike
I know tomorrow would be a great opportunity to meet you for the first time, however that will have to wait for another day!
I regret to inform you that as an organiser of the INE Members Group meeting that only Shareholders of INE will be permitted to attend the meeting.
I know you have an interest in the affairs of Quinn, but this meeting is a private affair for members only seeking ways to rescue their investment.
This meeting is not directly about Quinn, it is about OUR investment.
I hope you understand my position as an organiser of the meeting and don’t cause a scene or place me in an awkward situation.
Thanking you in advance for your understanding in this matter.
Rob Henshall.
