Educo Seminar 2010 The R.D.S. Concert Hall, Merrion Road, Ballsbridge Jan 10.

Tony Quinn

Jan 2010 ©2009 Human Potential Research Ltd.

The Good Life Club

Educo®: To Draw out Your Life and Lifestyle from Within.

December 2009.

Dear Good Life Club Member,

A Happy and Successful New Year to each and every one of you. You may hear that a lot at this time of year and in my case it’s sincerely meant. However, why leave your fate to chance?

Tonys_Letter-Jan-2010-1

Instead let’s get together and set our minds to making it happen. A few hours spent installing the right mindset will make THE vital difference to your year. In the process we will have good fun and I will fill you in on all that’s happening.

• Firstly I would like to give each of you on the day a copy of our new updated brochure. In it you will find truly vital guidelines that for many of you will be something of an eye and mind opener.

• I believe that I have the knack of how to get the right mindset in place and I very much want to cover that with you.

• The last Song of Life was possibly the best and most successful seminar I was ever on. The input from the group made the difference. Since then, people have requested to explore further the difference between knowledge and intelligence. I maintain that  everyone has in common a higher intelligence that they may not be using. Could we explore this subject together?

• In everything from successful business to relationships how much do your opinions count? For example, could they be getting in the way of your best interests? Covering this subject should be fun and possibly even life-changing. Do you like the new approach I am using of bringing a group of people on stage who examine the subjects in greater depth? This approach of exploring together really seemed to work on the Song of Life Seminar.

I am really excited about this meeting and so hope you can make it.

Lots of Love,

Tony.

Date: Sunday January 10th 2010.

Venue: The R.D.S. Concert Hall, Merrion Road, Ballsbridge, Dublin 4.

Time: Noon until 6pm approximately.

Please be sure to bring a notebook and pen.

For any further information please contact +353-1-8304211.
