Tony Quinn

Guru/Cult Leader Quinn is now Captain of the oil business

Pre Quinn’s appearance on the oil business scene, we had a healthy and progressive company.  Post Quinn, it’s been a tortuous route and it has been tolerated because we have inadequate leadership in BNE – Susan Morrice is totally incapable of saying ‘no’ to Guru Cult/Leader Quinn, even when he claims to be the person who found the oil in Belize.

So readers, this is not an oil story, but the story of another of Guru/Cult Leader Quinn’s victims, only in this case the victim happens to be our oil company.   Guru/Cult Leader Quinn has insidiously slimed his way into our business.  At the last AGM he announced himself as a director and at the same time said only people who have taken his Educo courses would be eligible to obtain shares in BNE.

Obviously this is when he saw that there were bigger fish to fry, rather than focusing on his declining Educo business, in one fell swoop he could grasp bigger profits with much less effort – after all he had to do was sway Susan Morrice, a more than willing victim.

Sheila McCaffrey was the only Board Member who strenuously objected to his being gifted shares valuing US$24,000,000 and was suspended for her efforts.

So readers, this is a Cult story, oil just happens to be the victim, as opposed to the many individuals who have lost their money because of Guru/Cult Leader Quinn’s schemes in Educo.

He’s seen that he can manipulate the oil company in the form of Susan Morrice, and there’s no-one to stop him.

The last AGM was in September 09 – no minutes were distributed, but many people took recordings – so the evidence exists that Guru Quinn is running the show.

Keep on reading about the ‘soon to be convicted’ scam operator.   Remember, it’s not only an oil story;  it’s a cult story of an enormously arrogant man with no qualifications, except greed and manipulation.
