DI post on some comments by Joanna on the blog.

House of Prayer

Joanna or who ever you are you originally responded to my review of the programme entitled the following:

Review of TV3: Ireland’s Secret Cults Programme by the Director of Dialogue Ireland Trust

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/09/28/review-of-tv3-irelands-secret-cults-programme-by-the-director-of-dialogue-ireland-trust/

Joanna:

Here is what I said, on September 28, 2011 at 1:27 pm said:

“When I saw this programme coming up I asked a number of people to watch it and would they give me their views after seeing it. Those I asked were all people who had no connection with the House of Prayer and had in their disinterest an impartiality free from self bias or self interest. I should say though that these people were all Catholics.

(Do send us the details of this impartial group so we can evaluate if they were independent?)

 Overall, they noted that the focus of the programme was undeniably the House of Prayer as revealed in its sequencing. ie. opening with the House of Prayer, then small section on a cult movement, back to the House of Prayer, then small section on another cult, then back to the House of Prayer etc. In doing so they felt the programme betrayed its own agenda and those involved in making it. Allowing them less time, it also gave no real information on the 2 cult movements, leaving the overall impression that as a documentary it lacked in depth and research, which was evident too in the programme’s presentation of The House of Prayer. It was thought that the 2 cult movements were used only as the context in which to cast the shadow of ‘cult’ on the House of Prayer, the justification of which was not demonstrated in any way.

(You got it in one, that is exactly what the programme was trying to do and succeeded!)

The people in the House of Prayer appeared to be normal people, the same as any who would gather to pray at a place of pilgrimage, which did not back-up Michael Ryan’s commentary. The hidden camera work was obviously edited to ridicule and misrepresent the messages of Our Lady Queen of Peace. The supposed conflict with the Church and the House of Prayer, they said, appeared to be – all just Michael Ryan’s comment, just opinion, based on no evidence whatsoever. If anything the people I spoke to thought that Christina Gallagher had it right. They highlighted a number of points eg “The heads of our governments would be no more than puppets” ‘ring any bells’, they said; And about the reference to a “one world currency” they remarked, ‘governments around the world are talking about this now’; and they were interested in the comments made about the “the chip” We are all hearing about that now but Christina has been talking about these things for a long time!

However, the programme they thought had no balance and no redress and it hinged on connecting the House of Prayer with the 2 cults under the fact that the presenter Michael Ryan was unable to make contact with any of the people in these organisations! Yet at the end, the programme contradicted itself stating “TV3 have been in ongoing discussions with the House of Prayer and hope to be able to broadcast their view on the matters raised on tonight’s programme in the near future”

The House of Prayer themselves have a statement on their website, which was to be read and presented on the TV3 programme at the conclusion of the segment dealing with the House of Prayer and we can only wonder why it was not included.

(Do you think that TV 3 would publish self serving commentary by the House of Prayer on Monday? You must be joking! It only appeared on your site on Tuesday when you realised you were snookered, and tried to catch up. Note no date or time. Many contacted us stressing this point. )

 “The campaign of persecution against Christina Gallagher, her spiritual director, The House of Prayer, its good work and those who pray there, continues relentlessly. It is based on repetition of false allegations, misleading remarks and innuendo. This damaging attack implies and tries to create an impression of conflict between The House of Prayer and the Church where none exists. Christina Gallagher has always been obedient to the Catholic Church and all who choose to visit and pray at The House of Prayer (and who have every right to do so) are sincere, active and loyal members of the Catholic Church. They freely come and go and there is no pressure of any description to purchase religious artefacts. Failure to appear on this TV programme or to make a detailed statement is not the fault of or due to non-co-operation of The House of Prayer and furthermore, describing the activities at The House of Prayer as those of a ‘cult’ are totally false and defamatory and invite incitement to hatred.” http://www.christinagallagher.org

It would appear ‘dialogue ireland’ that your opening comments are somewhat self-congratulatory. It is true that this is certainly one of very few documentaries done on cultism in Ireland but as documentaries go, it is probably not among the best.”

I was not interested in responding to your comments in that regard, but used some of your comments in regard to my view that all of the groups mentioned were given the opportunity to give their perspective.

This was posted as:

Knock knocking on Heaven’s door – trying to speak to the deaf?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/09/29/knock-knocking-on-heaven’s-door-–-trying-to-speak-to-the-deaf/

The H of P refused to do so and all that TV 3 told me was that they were in discussions with you as you could not agree on a statement. Hence they placed the following notice up on their site:

 “TV3 have been in ongoing discussions with the House of Prayer and hope to be able to broadcast their view on the matters raised on tonight’s programme in the near future”

It is simple as soon as you agree with them Christina and Fr McGinnity will be interviewed on the TV3. Currently you do not agree so there will be no programme.

May I suggest you actually use your own site to make these points?

http://www.christinagallagher.org/en/

Today you wrote as following:

dialogueireland, you have lifted my comments out of context; (no doubt deliberately)

This the additional piece from the comments on our site:

——————————————————————

This piece reflects, as I said, the impartial comments made by a group of people unconnected to the house of prayer and not as you suggest, without evidence, “mouthpieces for the H of P”. As always with you dialogueireland, there is never any evidence for your accusations.

 

You also said in your opening statement; “All the groups were approached on a number of occasions but they refused to communicate.”  However the programme states at the end that TV3 were in communication with the House of Prayer. To use your words ‘Look at the documentary are you blind?’, it is there in black and white.

(Again this not relevant. The intention of the H of P was to not partake. The communication was about not agreeing about the terms of engagement. The H of P did not want to participate and that is what communications were about. They were sending legal threats all the time. As I wrote earlier if they want to take part in a documentary I am sure that TV 3 would arrange it. They had to use material from Would You Believe to get any idea what Christina stood for. So your statement was disingenuous and put up to mislead.)

 

You also said in your opening statement; ‘Even if they could agree with you about a statement to publish, they would not have published the one you tried to pretend they had agreed to” DialogueIreland, The House of Prayer’s statement is their statement, they do not need to agree it with anyone. TV3 received their statement and did not include it in the programme! while Michael Ryan’s commentary states that the House of Prayer could not be contacted! and as you state here “refused to communicate”!!.

(Let us spell it out: 1. They phoned you, they posted a letter into the H of P in Achill. Meanwhile they were subjected to a barrage of calls not to give the other side but to get the programme stopped. Also I was in Knockbridge to give an interview and again Fr McGinnity did not want to give an interview. He too did not answer calls, or reply to letters left in his post box. Nothing new:)

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/07/31/house-of-prayer-rte-prime-time-april-03-2008-now-on-youtube/  )

This is not true dialogue ireland. Your accusations are not true – again and again!!! (I am afraid it is!)

 I am not a spokesperson for TV3 and my views on the programme are mine and you do have a right to leave any comment you like but I do not need to reply to every one of your blessed thoughts. I suggest you as a proxy, Sage or any of you involved with the H of P should contact the station yourselves.

Your reply to my post was out of context. You replied to the wrong one!

You will note I will defend your right to comment and will not allow others to misuse your name or pretend they are ‘you.’

In summary please do not try to fight your battles with TV 3 on our site, leave any comment you like on this site, but we do not need to respond every time you don’t like what we say.
