Text of the letter featured in the program:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/10/15/the-spotlight-on-quinn-the-bbc-goes-to-town/

20 June 2006

Dear Tony,

We the directors of Belize Natural Energy, BNE, would be honoured and delighted if you would join and be a key part of BNE. Words cannot express the appreciation and gratitude we all feel towards you. We would like to offer you an originating share holding in the company equal to our own.

This is a class A stock position and an equal portion of the royalty.

Estimated value today of this share position is 16 million dollars. We project that within one to two years the value could be ten times that based on a discovery of 100 million barrels from additional oil fields.

This company was born from a mutual belief that we can make a difference in this world. When we put our whole open mind on the goal the force and source of the universe come together, within and without us to bring it about. The first miracle was discovering the oil in Belize against such odds. With this door opened the chance of finding additional major oil reserves is enhanced greatly. The challenge is to develop this truly significant resource in a totally holistic way and to bring the understanding that you opened in us to all the people of Belize. Belize will then be the most amazing shining beacon for the world to see; the gateway for humanity to enter LIFE.

We need your total involvement, this is impossible without you.

With all our love and life,

Susan Morrice, Jean Cornec, Sheila McCaffrey, Paul Marriot, Patti Usher
