These are not official court transcripts but notes by attendees (concerned B class members).

-Dear Shareholders,

Attached are a series of transcripts from the Tortola trial, for you to MAKE UP YOUR OWN MIND about the issues raised. Due to size, the files will be sent singly.

We have been biding our time waiting for the judgement in Sheila’s case against INE and/or its directors. It has already has been circulated to Members known to the INE Shareholder  Responses to the case from Susan and Sheila are, not surprisingly, poles apart, and do little to show us a way forward.

Three Class B shareholders- Ralph McCutcheon, Michael O’Brien and Patricia Murphy, went to Tortola to listen to the case. It was an amazing time- a chance to see the Directors under oath in the witness box, under cross examination. The first two days Sheila was examined by John McDonald QC for the defendants. He did not seem to be able to score one single point.

Then Tony Quinn was examined for another two and a half days. Next came Susan for another two days, winding up with Caroline Williams and Gilly Canton, and a policeman from Belize.

Having listened to Prime Minister David Cameron giving evidence to the Leveson Inquiry, it was obvious that both Tony and all the INE witnesses had the same problems with amnesia. Susan could not even remember how long she had been a director of INE! Tony managed to forget most of what he was asked about over the whole 21/2 days.

We have been busy transcribing the dialogue for the entire proceedings, which is quite a job, but I am told saves some $35,000 to buy an official transcript! The transcripts are very revealing of the way the Company has been managed, or rather mis-managed, since 2006.   They will repay careful reading two or three times over to get the full meaning of what was going on. The Judgement is one thing, but the text of the case allows a personal understanding of many of the issues ignored or sidelined by the Judge in his judgement, which was relevant only to the case brought by SM Life Ventures.

The figures in the margin refer to the bundle number, page and paragraph referred to. The 17 bundles of evidence totalled over 10,000 pages.

An important tit bit was from the amended operating agreement, in which Susan, Tony & Co. eliminated all Class B voting rights. Thankfully that document was demonstrated to have been changed after signing by the then directors, and signatures forged, so it has been declared invalid.

Another document demonstrated to have been forged was a letter from the Directors confirming Tony’s position as an ‘originating member’ and director.

The Forensic Accounts run to about 180 pages, and analyse the cash flow patterns in detail. This is probably the document which is of particular concern to us as investors.

INE employed various auditors simultaneously. They came up with greatly differing profit figures. The question put to Susan was that this might indicate the keeping of more than one set of books…

In a three month period alone in 2008/9 INE paid some $1.4 Million to security and hacking companies!

We are told that the court case in Tortola has cost some $10 Million. The Board had indemnified themselves against paying any of this themselves, presumably we the owners are expected to pay.

Some 90 ‘Profit Centre’ companies were identified by the forensic accountants. These were owned by the directors and others close to them, and large amounts of money were paid to them for ‘consultancy services’ etc. until it abruptly terminated as Paul’s case was about to be heard. Interesting!

Susan continues her action against Rob in the Belfast High Court. He is quite rightly counter suing. The evidence revealed in Tortola totally justifies all the comments he made, which are being stated by Susan to be libellous.
