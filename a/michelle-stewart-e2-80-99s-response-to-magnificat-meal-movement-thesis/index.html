Michelle Stewart’s response to MAGNIFICAT MEAL MOVEMENT Thesis

Magnificat Meal Movement

Michelle Stewart’s response to SPIRITUALITY AND CULTISM:
A CASE STUDY OF A NEW RELIGIOUS MOVEMENT –
THE MAGNIFICAT MEAL MOVEMENT (C) 2006

Mike Garde MA

03/07/2007 22:09

I received this response to my thesis from Michelle Stewart who was the assistant to Debra for many years. Her responses indicate that some items of fact need to be amended if the thesis ever is turned into a book. Unfortunately when I was in Australia in 2003 and Michelle and Gordon were not willing to give me an interview for the thesis. I am very willing if any other items of fact that people become aware of to amend the text. Here a picture is painted of a woman who is evil, now my thesis allows for a period of nobility, decline and fall. Does the wounding these people experienced force them to remove that divine element, or have we not yet faced the reality that darkness reigned, we just did not know it? Now it has become mundane and tacky, gold and exterior things, with a blinded membership still believing, or possibly not knowing how to move on or out. Some like Wal and Beth turned on the light and saw the darkness, others may not know how to deal with the realisation they have been conned. As someone put it last weekend, it is like being under a roller with a diamond cutting into you as the road is tarred.

Dear Mike,

Thank you for sending your thesis.  I found it interesting and mostly factual.  There are a couple of things that are not correct.  The first comment I want to bring to your attention is the one you mentioned that Gordon and Debra were involved in Real Estate in Helidon.  Gordon never allowed Debra to share his business here, in fact Debra used Gordon to sell properties to people she convinced to purchase a house that was supposedly chosen for them by Our Lady.  Gordon was coerced by Debra to donate all commission from these sales to the Magnificat Meal Movement.  In fact, Debra was the only person who profited from these sales the figure was definitely nowhere near $50000.   During this time Gordon was led to believe that Debra was speaking to God and Our Lady.*

Secondly, Debra’s life changed after she was dismissed from the school where she was acting principal. She went into depression for months according to Gordon and never really snapped out of it. Debra helped Gordon in his Real Estate business for a short period, but eventually she moved on to something else because she flits from one whim to another.

Thirdly, when Debra started introducing SDA beliefs into her talks Gordon and I were appalled. She tried so hard to convince us that this was what God was leading her into.  Most of the group were like sheep, ready to go along. In a private meeting with three SDA leaders, Gordon and Myself Debra confessed that she was fabricating all the visions and messages she was supposedly receiving from God and Our Lady.  I was not at all surprised, for a very long time I believed that she was a fake. I felt so relieved as though a huge weight had been removed from my shoulders.   I looked at her without fear. The feeling of oppression was gone and all I felt for her was disgust. I told her to tell the truth for once to the rest of the group. She asked me to give her six months to disperse the people living in the convent and the men’s house and then our two families (hers and mine) would be the only ones living on the property.  I should have known that she would not keep her word. She was in too deep she wanted the power, money and attention more than anything else. Gordon and I tried to convince her to do the right thing but she turned on us in the worst way.  She threatened me emotionally and physically and told me if I didn’t shut my mouth and keep the secret she would make up a story that Gordon and I were having an affair. I was not at all afraid because I knew it was not true, so I called her bluff and told her to go right ahead.  She did exactly that, because she knew that her stupid followers would believe whatever she said.  She blamed Gordon and Me for all the SDA rubbish, not Gordon and Marion Morgan, as you stated.

Debra had to get rid of me, so she broke into my private cottage and removed my belongings.  She changed the locks so I could not get back in.  I was advised by police not to go back, for my own safety. My son and I were literally on the street.  We were given a place to stay for a couple of weeks with people we hardly knew.  Would a prophet of God treat people like this?  People are so blind, just this behaviour alone should have had people doubting.  Gordon was forced out of the convent weeks after me.  He had no intentions of leaving, he wanted to get Debra to tell the truth but finally she made it impossible for him to stay and the police advised him also that it was not safe for him to try and stay.+

Your thesis had some interesting things that I didn’t know as they happened after I left.  Everything came flooding back, all the dreadful memories and feelings of mental and emotional abuse.  The fear that this woman instils in people is incredible.  My life has bitterly changed from having met this evil, vindictive, manipulate con artist.  I have lost my home and my security.  I cannot go into detail yet, but things are about to come to a head for Ms Burslem and I can only hope that people will take the blinders from their eyes and realise she is a fraud in every way.

It is a shame that you didn’t speak to me one on one I could have given you a very good account of situations and events inside the group itself.  I watched Debra very closely and I believe that I was given a very strong discernment. Her personality is very complex, she has multiple facets. In an instant she can be extremely vindictive and ruthless in the next she can pretend sweetness and innocence.  I found her behaviour to be very scary at times, bordering on madness.  I honestly believe that Debra never experienced anything spiritual.  You’d think that her husband would notice something early in their relationship but she did not say or do anything to make anyone think that she was experiencing anything supernatural until much later. She is such a good actress and extremely good at convincing people of her stories.  She has an overactive imagination even delusional.  She has the ability to read books and remember the information almost word for word.  She had books on every seer and visionary from around the world.  She read everything she could about Padre Pio and the stigmata and anyone else who claimed they were experiencing the same. She read books on cults and even commented on how she could have her own cult.  I have heard her tell the same stories many times and each time there would be something new added.  I confronted her many times about her lies but she always turned things around and said that I had a bad memory.

She is the most cunning person I have ever had the misfortune of knowing.  She convinced all of us that she could read our souls.  Everyone was afraid of what she might know.  She had sneaky members who were quite willing to tell tales on others just to score brownie points.  The fools didn’t realise that she was using the information to back up her claim that she could read souls. The strange thing is that she said she could not read mine and I asked her why, but she didn’t really give me an answer.  She now regularly proclaims that I am not human.  I am a shape shifting reptilian without a soul.

One of the worst things she did was have a locked box with a slit in the top for members to offer personal prayers and promises to Our Lady.  Little did they know that Debra opened the letters and read them to use the information against them at a later date thus proving that she was reading souls.  She told everyone that Our Lady didn’t need to open the envelopes and that she had given Debra the job of destroying them.  I was not stupid enough to ever put an envelope in that box.~

I travelled to seven different countries with Debra, including Ireland.  Not one of the places we visited had groups of more than 20 people listening to her talks.  She claims to have thousands of followers, this is utterly ridiculous. She is a liar and a thief and I can say this with conviction through personal experience. I believe the most disturbing thing is the power that this woman has over people.  Her personality is so strong and oppressive that she is terrifying if she cannot get her own way with everything.  I have never met anyone like her in my life and hope I never do.  She has the most insatiable appetite for attention and control that I have ever experienced in any human except in the movies!  If you want to know more just let me know.   I just wish there was something I could do or say to get these people away from her.

Notes added within a few days by Michelle:

06/07/2007

*Dear Mike,

I spoke to Gordon about the Real Estate comments and I was not accurate so it needs to be amended as follows:-  Gordon never allowed Debra to share his business here, in fact Debra used Gordon to sell properties to people she convinced to purchase a house that was supposedly chosen for them by Our Lady. There were properties in the main street that Debra convinced people to give 1/3 to the Magnificat Meal Movement and also to Our Lady’s Mount Pty Ltd.  At the time Gordon, Debra, Phillip and I were Directors of O.L.M. Pty Ltd.  The main street properties were to be used for commercial reasons and whatever profits were gained from these properties the Movement was to receive 1/3. Our Lady’s Mount never received any money from any of these properties and as far as I know neither did the movement because none of her schemes for the main street ever came to fruition. When Gordon sold these properties to the buyers he did not receive any commission for the ones involving O.L.!

M Pty Ltd. It was illegal for him to do so due to the fact that he was a director of the company involved in some of the sales.  The other properties Debra asked Gordon to sell followers were in the residential part of Helidon.  Gordon had the right to the commission from these sales, some of which was donated to the movement.  The movement profited from the donated commission.  The figure was nowhere near $500.00. During this time Gordon was led to believe that Debra was speaking to God and Our Lady.

+The next change is: – Gordon was forced out of the convent weeks after me. He had no intentions of leaving, he wanted to get Debra to follow through with her plan to remove all the men and women from the property, this would then lead to the truth being revealed. I told her that I would expose her. Gordon was hoping I would then be able to return to my home (the convent).  The convent was purchased by the two families as a home. It was never meant to be taken over by the Magnificat Meal Movement. While Debra had the mob around her it was impossible to remove her power base, because without it she is nothing. Finally she made it impossible for Gordon to stay and the police advised him that it was not safe for him to try.

~The next change is: – One of the worst things she did was have a medium sized, covered box at the foot of the altar in the convent Oratory. It was there for members to place prayer offerings to Our Lady. Most of the contents of the letters were very personal, so personal that one slave confided to me that she wanted to retrieve the letter because she regretted exposing her personal secret in her letter to Our Lady. I don’t know whether she did retrieve it or not. Little did followers know…………? I spoke to Wal about the box and he couldn’t remember if it was locked so then I started to doubt my memory as to whether it was locked so for factual reasons I think it is better to leave that out. I want to make sure that everything I’ve written is as truthful as possible.

Thanks and God Bless,

Michelle.

Wal Maggs:

23/07/2007

*Dear Mike, You are fortunate to have this testimony from Michelle. She was intimately involved in the founding of the MMM in Toowoomba, and later in Helidon. Has Michelle become too emotionally involved in her testimony? Of course! But the facts she quotes are known only to Debra, herself and Gordon. I am fairly certain that most of what she says here is close to the truth. Although, even on this point, Debra said in my presence that Gordon had given her money from the sales to help her at one stage. But Debra’s testimony is always suspect.

Another point of Michelle’s testimony that appeals to me is that from the very beginning Debra was not involved in any spiritual visions etc, and her mission was monetary. In other words, a rogue from the start. I get the impression you seem to think Debra was genuine at first and later fell from grace. I have never held this opinion and Michelle seems to reinforce this. I think Michelle’s testimony, come so late, is nevertheless priceless and should be given prominence by you. With regards, Wal

I find Michelle very honest when speaking with her. She seems fearless of Debra and probably most of what she wrote could be taken as true.
