My nine-year-old daughter is having Scientology lessons by John Meagher

EducationScientology

Irish Independent

Saturday July 07 2012

http://www.independent.ie/lifestyle/my-nineyearold-daughter-is-having-scientology-lessons-3161077.html

Despite its Hollywood links and boasts of millions of members worldwide, it is thought that fewer than 100 Irish people are practising Scientologists.  One of them is 34-year-old Donegal native Zabrina Collins, who works at the church’s Irish branch in Dublin’s Middle Abbey Street.The married mother-of-one was reluctant to be interviewed by Weekend Review this week because of the “Tom and Katie recent happenings” but she discusses her devotion to Scientology in the media section of the organisation’s official website.  As well as an interview, a video featuring husband Ger — who is also a Scientologist — and their nine-year-old daughter, Navarra, has been posted at: scientologynews.org


Collins speaks of her tough upbringing and family upheavals. “I started taking drugs when I was 13,” she says.  It was while travelling in Australia that she discovered Scientology.  “I saw a sign for a free personality test. I stopped in and asked if I could take it. You can imagine what the results looked like.  “I took a couple of Scientology courses before I moved on.  “Nine months later it occurred to me how much better I was doing when I was studying Scientology.  “I moved to Dublin, went into the Church of Scientology Mission there and joined staff. That was the best thing I’ve ever done.”  It was at the Mission that she met Ger. The couple married and had Navarra — who she describes as a “trouble-free child” within a year.In keeping with the ideals of Scientology, Zabrina had a “silent birth”.  “All of your painful experiences are recorded in a previously unknown part of the mind. It’s called the reactive mind.  “And it throws those experiences back at you in an irrational attempt to get you to avoid the same painful thing from happening to you again.”  In an interview with the Sunday Independent last year, Collins denied that Scientology was a cult and said Navarra had already studied some of the child-specific programmes including ‘Learning How to Learn’ and ‘The Children’s Communication Course’.

“I would like my little girl to get the best out of her education and these courses have done that,” she said.

“When she is older, she can decide if she would like to do more in Scientology or not, but that is her decision to make, not mine.”

John Meagher
