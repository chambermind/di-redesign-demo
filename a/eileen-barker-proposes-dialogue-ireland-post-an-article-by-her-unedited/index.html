Eileen Barker proposes Dialogue Ireland post an article by her unedited

Conferences & Commentaries

Prior to the FECRIS Conference in April, Dialogue Ireland proposed a debate between Eileen Barker and John Butler. She declined and so we posted some of the ideas that John Butler would have advanced if the debate had gone ahead.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/05/12/eileen-barker-declines-debate-with-john-butler-in-london-at-the-lse/

We also proposed to publish some of Eileen Barker’s articles for people to debate with or agree with. We again offer the opportunity for anyone to reply to Eileen’s position by way of a comment or in a fuller post which we will publish.

We received the following email from Eileen Barker on Friday 28th May:

——– Original Message ——–

Subject:            RE: Eileen Barker declined to debate with John Butler in London at the LSE.

Date:    Fri, 28 May 2010 18:46:07 +0100

From:   E.Barker@lse.ac.uk

To:       info@dialogueireland.org

If you are really interested in my views on value-free sociology, I suggest you post my Presidential Address, “The Scientific Study of Religion? You Must be Joking!” But please post it in its entirety rather than taking bits out of context.

You could find a copy in any of the following.

The Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion, Vol.34 no.3, September 1995: pp. 287-310.

The ‘Cult’ Controversy: A Reader (RST2255), edited by The University of South Africa. Muckleneuk, Pretoria: University of South Africa, 2009, pp 1-24.

Lorne L. Dawson (ed.) Cults and New Religious Movements: A Reader, Oxford: Blackwell, 2003: pp 7-25.

Eileen Barker
