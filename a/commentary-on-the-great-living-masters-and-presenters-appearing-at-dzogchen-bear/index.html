 1.      Ringu Tulku Rinpoche

http://www.dzogchenbeara.org/index.php?pid=97&aid=214

Here is what they have on the Dzogchen Beara web site about Ringu.

Ringu Tulku Rinpoche was born in Kham, Lingtsang, in 1952, and was recognised by the 16th Gyalwang Karmapa as the incarnation of one of the tulkus of the Kagyu monastery of Rigul, in Lingtsang. He received teachings from the Gyalwang Karmapa, Kyabje Dilgo Khyentse Rinpoche, Kyabje Dodrupchen Rinpoche, and many eminent Khenpos. He was awarded the title of Khenpo by the 16th Gyalwang Karmapa, and Lopen Chenpo (PhD) by the International Nyingmapa Society in 1983.

Rinpoche’s fluent English and warm, friendly teaching style is appreciated at Dharma centres around the world. Since 1990 he has taught Buddhism and meditation at more than 50 universities, institutes and Buddhist centres in Europe, USA, Canada, Australia and Asia

It’s amazing how many of them are the ‘greatest living masters of ‘Tibet.’  They all seem to be now?

 This is our assessment of Ringu Tulku. He is a very old fashioned, and conservative, old school, i.e. medieval autocrat, from Tibet.   He is heavily endorsed by Dalai Lama, and tied into Sogyal.  He is coming, in our opinion, to ‘herd the sheep,’ into a deeper conditioned conformity.

His teaching schedule is  extensive throughout Europe, but it is the ‘carrot and stick” and hell realms,  and his role is to repress even a ‘negative thought’ about these lamas.

http://www.bodhicharya.org/ringu-tulku/schedule/

He appears to be heavily tied into  the celebrity lama  circuit, i.e. Dalai Lama, the Eco Karmapa and of course Sogyal.  He has a number of centres world wide.  He is one of the ‘second tier’ players but ‘connected.’

He is called in to Sogyal’s centre to teach, to do the heavy repression reinforcement, in case there are ‘rumblings” of independent thinking in the Kagyu sanghas, and because Rigpa has gotten so much negative press. It seems his main ‘asset’ is teaching on the importance of absolutely all repression of ‘anger, criticism, and complaints, and accusations”, of any kind and how that would erase all ‘practice benefits’ even an instant of anger would tumble you to the hell realms!
He would be called in to quell dissent or even any negative ‘thoughts’ about  Sogyal within the ranks of the western students. He teaches from the most old fashioned, patriarchal priestly hierarchical view, i.e. “how to keep them devoted and uncritical  members.  Herding them like sheep.

 Rigpa and Sogyal has brought in a ‘heavy hitter’ with Ringu. He would be a busy propagandist and cult-reinforcer, to keep themselves on the thrones, beyond reproach.  In summary he is very traditional exponent of “Lamaism.”
