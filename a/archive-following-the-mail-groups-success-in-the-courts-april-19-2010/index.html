Archive following the Mail groups success in the courts April 19, 2010

Icross Charity

Dialogue Ireland was threatened with damages at the end of January by Michael Meegan for republishing articles in the public domain for as long as four years. As a Charity we could not have made payment had he succeeded in his attempt to silence us. The Mail Group – Associated Newspapers persisted and under the threat of taking a massive financial hit won in the High Court. We salute them and all the journalists who were able to use the judgement to tell the truth about Meegan and ICROSS. Here we have been given permission to host the articles from the Mail articles and the judgements of the court. These will become part of our archive, and will give the public the opportunity to see how Meegan used funds to defend his privileged lifestyle. He had started with a vision to help the poor and most depressed. Instead he became a predatory monster who drained the life out of the Kenyan people. As a South African born Irish citizen my blood boils at this terrible deception.
