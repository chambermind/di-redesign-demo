In response to your comments and claims

You say, “In response to your claim ‘Fitness expert?’, on January 10th, 2010 that Quinn “Returned to study at the Dalton Academy, Dublin, but failed to pass the Intermediate Certificate and gave up education….His poor literacy skills and lack of scholastic abilities mean that he has avoided any major courses of study and has failed dramatically when attempting even minor academic tasks. 1. civic responsibility,
2. on behalf of other women who have alledgedly suffered the same treatment from Quinn,
3. something called “serving justice” for herself and the justice system
4. if the court decides she’s due financial compensation, then she’s very welcome to it. Sexual abuse cases and many kinds of crime have fair compensation awarded to victims

You say, “she stated in the Sunday World 3rd March 2009 that she thought he was Jesus Christ incarnate for 25 years, before coming back to reality in time for the recession) hasn’t even been proven”.

Answer: Completely wrong again ‘Eileen’. As in your other posts, you just don’t do the research. You don’t even look into the archives in this website. Don’t be so expectant that other people should do the work for you. You may just be in denial of reality. Others, including many ex-cult members verify Quinn claiming to be the messiah on numerous occasions. Journalists have reported people as eminent as film director Jim Sheridan saying Quinn made the same claim.

Eileen or whoever, you probably won’t accept the obvious truth but there it is. You’re pushing yourself into an extreme state of denial.

You say “Patricia(January 11th, 2010 ), you say that “Its great to see Tony Quinn exposed after all the years of abusing and taking advantage of vulnerable people”, I have to say this is laughable. It’s not elderly, infirm or even incapable people, or to use your word “vulnerable” people who were “taken advantage of”. It’s those who had more money than sense and who are only coming to realise it. I don’t buy a big mac and then demand my money back afterwards saying that I’ve now decided I don’t agree with eating such things. This is essentially the same thing, people with an axe to grind who don’t want to take responsibility for themselves and their actions”.

Answer: Very weird “reasoning” and even weirder version of ethics. Many Quinnites WERE young and naive and vulnerable in these and other ways. Others, including mature people, believed the lies, half-truths, exaggerations and other deceptions and other statements told by Quinn and cronies and now YOU attack the victims without a criticism of Quinn. The “big mac” analogy you make is misleading because the people concerned do not appear to be saying they disagreed with the principle of what they were told they were going to learn or of what they could be expected to gain from the seminars but they are criticising what was actually delivered. They are exercising their consumer rights here but obviously you have a big problem with this.

You say, “This is essentially the same thing, people with an axe to grind who don’t want to take responsibility for themselves and their actions”.

Answer: You haven’t established the link you claim between your statement (a claim) in the “big mac” analogy and your argument that the real problem here is a failure by these critics of Quinn’s “to take responsibility for themselves and their actions”. The people responsible for making claims for the seminars and delivering them are Quinn and his colleagues. The critics were responsible for listening to the claims, accepting them enough to go on seminars and paying their money for this. The critics now claim that Quinn and colleagues WERE responsible for lies, distortions, exaggerations, etc and failed to deliver on much / most of what was promised. They have a clear right to make this complaint. Whatever the exact state of mind including elements of vulnerability, naiveté, highly trusting, etc of the participants, this complaint, in itself, is no evidence of dereliction of personal responsibility by them. They are claiming a form of breach of contract. They ARE taking a perfectly responsible action in exercising their consumer rights which you seem to deny.

You say they have an axe to grind but fail to show what the “axe to grind” is. These people actually believed in Quinn and his promises enough to go on seminars and now want to make a complaint. You don’t need “an axe to grind” to make a complaint. You hurl your despicable accusations at people, absolve Quinn of responsibility for lies and failure to deliver, insult Quinn’s critics including their intelligence and integrity by claiming that, en masse, they are a crowd of responsibility shirkers because they dare to complain about the self-proclaimed messiah and those that lie on his behalf.

It’s your choice who you want to believe, that’s your responsibility. You’ve made your position clear. Save yourself from deception. It’s not possible for Quinn at the moment, he’s not into repentance or restitution.

Let the phoney messiah take the responsibility of defending himself.
