Scientology Uncovered: On MidWeek Mike Rinder, former spokesperson for Scientology

Scientology

On Midweek:Live Wed 26 Oct: 10pm
Mike Rinder, a former spokesperson for Scientology in the US joins us to talk about being labeled a traitor. He is a Independent Scientologist who believes in the tech, but views the current Church leadership as totalitarian.

This week on MidWeek… It’s said to be the religion of the stars. But is Scientology a force for good or a sinister cult? For the first time on Irish television we speak to the man who used to be the church’s official spokesman in America. He’s now seen as a traitor. Scientology Uncovered: MidWeek
