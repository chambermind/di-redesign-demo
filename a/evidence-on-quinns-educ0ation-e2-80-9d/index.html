EVIDENCE ON QUINN’S “EDUC(0)ATION”

EducationTony Quinn

This record of Quinn’s own signature shows the elusive Mucky ‘Messiah’ was training in a hypnotherapy course in August 1990.

Quinn in hypnotherapy class in 1990

This evidence is very embarrassing for the self-proclaimed genius and divinity, who claims to have received his revelations from his supreme form of meditation (on the mountain tops) and who as the self-proclaimed messiah for this age was already enlightening mankind for nearly 20 years previously according to his own publicity from way back in his 1970s Yoga cult and communes.  Drawing on courses in hypnotherapy and NLP in the late 1980s and 1990s, the “mucky messiah” has been combining and slipping between roles as a Life coach and self-help guru, business coach and messiah for the new age as the opportunity presents without extensive training or achievement in any field as research reveals.  The claims of being the messiah are nowadays more often kept to the inner circle of the cult and revealed to the Educo followers after they have been conditioned enough.
