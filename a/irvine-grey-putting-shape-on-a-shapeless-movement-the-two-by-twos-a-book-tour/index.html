Irvine Grey putting shape on a shapeless movement the – Two by Twos – a book tour.

Two by Twos

You will find the venues below. We are happy to support any attempt to study this movement and invite members and Workers to send us their comments when they have had a chance to read the book. I intend to represent DI at the book launch in Dublin Thursday June 20th  and look forward to meeting anyone who has concerns about this movement. I am also reliably informed by a friend that they are holding a Convention in the Midlands this coming week end and early next week.

Irvine with his wife and then after his graduation. In light of events at Victory his future study will be of interest.
