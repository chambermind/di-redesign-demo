MORE OF THE HISTORY OF QUINN, THE FALSE MESSIAH, THE CULT AND HIS ACTIVITIES

Very useful to let people know of the business history of Quinn. There were some exposes of Quinn going right back to the early 1970s but the extent of the rip-offs is only now being fully revealed. The authorities, as with the child sexual abuse scandal, were not properly monitoring and addressing cultist groups like Quinn’s “Yoga” and “Educo” so it’s been left to a few whistle-blowers and diligent journalists to lift the lid on the mucky messiah. The muscling in on businesses, as the newspaper described it regarding the oil company, was atrocious and without conscience and was added to by the systematic muscling in on the work, reputations and relationships of dozens of individuals.

Muscling in on Doctor Jack Gibson’s work meant that Quinn tried to promote the few surgery cases using hypnosis instead of drugs that he set up with four devotees as a great breakthrough of his Educo mind power method on the Late Late Show. The reality was that this was the long established use of hypnosis as an anaesthetic which he copied, badly, from Doctor Gibson who used the method in over 4000 cases and helped Quinn during Quinn’s four cases. Doctor Gibson who was not an egotistical publicity seeker was airbrushed out of history in Quinn’s version of events. Doctor Gibson, with the support of his family later severed any connection with Quinn. Muscling in on Steve Collins meant Quinn tried to steal the media attention and publicity as much as he could for himself and tried to claim most of the credit for Collin’s success for himself. Attempts to muscle in on the reputations and successes of Jim Sheridan, Gabriel Byrne and other celebrities were luckily for them and the Irish people very unsuccessful. Many members of Quinn’s cult were not so lucky as he muscled in on their finances, careers, and marriages and wrecked them as he ruthlessly exploited them mentally, financially and sexually. The list here is huge and many cases are very serious. Many individuals are still trying to recover from the damage and many are emerging and telling their experiences in harrowing detail.
