Healing in USA attributed to House of Prayer

House of Prayer

Following the interview with Dialogue Ireland on the House of Prayer last week:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/06/20/the-house-of-prayer-by-mike-garde-on-seamus-martin-on-tipp-fm/

a woman called Maureen Cunningham contacted the show from the State of Georgia in the USA. She was a devotee of our Lady and seemed to equate this devotion to Mary with her devotion to Our Lady Queen of Peace/ House of Prayer Achill or their house  in Florida.

http://www.voiceofourladyspilgrims.com/FrMcGinnity.html

They are just other names for the House of Prayer. In summary people are so under influence they believe the Grace of God is only mediated by involvement with the House of Prayer.

She attributes the fact that her granddaughter Margaret Mary was healed having received a diagnosis of genetic deformity from doctors in NYC to Our Lady. Now that is not normally something a Catholic would think twice about. But listening carefully it was clear like all those involved in the H of P in Ireland  there is slightly different twist from your usual Catholic healing story. Here it is not the direct role of Mary the Mother of God in healing but here the agency and intermediary role of Christina / Our Lady Queen of Peace/ H of P is viewed as the effective cause of the healing. It is a subtle shift but it leaves the person totally dependent on the House of Prayer for their salvation and for all the benefits usually associated with the Catholic Faith?

1. Our Lady Queen of Peace = 2. The House of Prayer = 3. Catholicism

You only get to 3 by 1 and 2!

Like H of P members here in Ireland who travel vast distances to go Achill she does not see the irony of going from Georgia all the way to Florida to experience God’s presence.  Also she could not see how like the members in Ireland who have an obvious devotion to the Eucharist, (she mentioned the Tabernacle,) is not catered for as the place she attends has no recognition and the Mass can’t be celebrated.

The Eucharist was removed by Fr McGinitty, but the consequence is that those are not in full communion with the Catholic Church. She raised the point that the Church is slow to recognise these private revelations.  It is not a question of waiting  till they they have already dismissed them but have not yet seemed ready to take the next step and condemn them. Just like in Ireland she does not get it that she is bypassing hundreds of Catholic Churches to get there. Because the Catholic Church is not clearly condemning this outrage, the faithful can’t be blamed for walking into an ecclesiastical ambush. Also this woman was a most sincere witness and likely of deep faith and commitment. Seamus was very empathetic in his interview.

When Seamus asked her about the cash and Christina’s lavish lifestyle it was clear she was not prepared for that and was very hesitant and confused.

Finally he asked her how she came to give an interview? It floored her and she clearly had been approached by people in the House of Prayer or associated with it to counteract the interview the week before on the programme. She did not answer the Direct Question, ‘Did someone from the House of Prayer or its agents approach you?’  Are we to believe she got a message from our lady to be waiting on line to tell her story at 5:30 AM?

She diverted the issue by stating that she had been in touch with Our Lady Queen of Peace about her grand daughter’s story long before the interview came up. However, that was not the question that was asked. She then said, she did it voluntarily, which was not in doubt. A lovely lady but clearly under influence.

DI is so happy to hear about the health of the baby and wish her well. A chat with her local Catholic Parish might sort out a lot of her issues.
