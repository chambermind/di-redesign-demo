Alternative Spiritualities, the New Age and New Religious Movements in Ireland

Interdisciplinary conference at the National University of Ireland, Maynooth.

(October 30th – 31st, 2009)

Guidelines for Presenters
Participants will have a maximum of 20 minutes to present their paper.
Papers will be distributed to all participants on a CD-ROM at the conference, and we are working on a publication of selected papers from the conference.

An electronic copy of your paper should be submitted to Laurence Cox at laurence.cox@nuim.ie by October 15th for inclusion in the CD-ROM and consideration for subsequent publication.

It should be no longer than 9,000 words (including footnotes, bibliography, etc) and be in .doc (Word) or .rtf (general) format only.

Should there be any queries on this matter, please do not hesitate to contact Laurence Cox laurence.cox@nuim.ie
