Thanks for donating to The Dialogue Ireland Trust

Conferences & Commentaries

We are grateful to those of you who have made a donation to Dialogue Ireland Trust using the donate button to the left of the home page. Also some of you have used the standing order form which gives a steady income which we can count on.

http://www.dialogueireland.org/dicontent/about/index.html

This assists us with our research and helps us provide free advice and information to the public worldwide.

This is done by email and through calls to our clients around the world but mainly in Ireland.

Fees and Support:

Dialogue Ireland as a charity does not charge for advice and information on the phone or through email anywhere in the world. We charge for private consultations in person or with organisations and terms will be worked out prior to commencement of the work.  We also charge for specific research projects on behalf of families, individuals or organisations. However, as a charity we will provide a service to those who have an inability to pay. We welcome donations to support the work of Dialogue Ireland and if you have found this site helpful know that a donation YOU make helps us to continue providing this service. Details of how you can donate  can be found on our website. You can also click on the donate button on our home page. A standing order form for Ireland and the UK is available on request. Cheques from the Ireland, USA, South Africa and the UK can be received without bank charges.
