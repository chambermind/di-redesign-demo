Published on May 24, 2013

Everyone now knows about the atrocities which happened outside the Woolwich Arsenal, in London, on May 22nd. Yet, there is much confusion concerning the motive behind their attack. Why did these two young Muslims target a British soldier, why did they cut his head off, why did they then stand around and wait for the police while talking and asking to be photographed by the onlooking public? These actions all seemed odd, if not bizarre. Yet, once you look into the Qur’an you will find support for much of what they did, and why they did it. Jay Smith, who personally knows Sheikh Omar Bakri Mohammed and Anjem Choudary, the Muslim leaders who converted and taught one of these men, opens up these Qur’anic passages and shows you that they were following directives from within the Qur’an itself. He then contrasts their actions with those of a Catholic Christian woman, Ingrid Loyau Kennett, who, using Biblical verses confronts the two Muslims to lay down their knives, telling them that they won’t win using violence. Jay shows the contrasting messages we can find when comparing the violence within the Qur’an, with that of peace, even with one’s enemies, an injunction which is unique to the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

 

May 25, 2013

MANY OF YOU ARE ASKING WHY THESE TWO YOUNG MUSLIMS, HERE IN LONDON, KILLED THE SOLDIER SO BRUTALLY. THERE IS A REASON TO WHAT THEY DID. TAKE A LOOK AT MY YOUTUBE VIDEO, AND SEE WHERE THEY GOT THEIR MOTIVATION FROM, AND WHAT OUR RESPONSE SHOULD BE TO THESE KILLINGS.

This is the image you have seen on your television screens, and in your news print, of the killer Michael Adebolajo. He is talking here, and being filmed by a witness, with blood on his hands and on the knives he holds. He claims that he killed the soldier, Drummer Lee Rigby, because the British Soldiers were killing ‘His people’ in Muslim countries (i.e. Afghanistan & Iraq). What does he mean by ‘his people’? To understand that you will need to look at the references he was quoting to the crowd, taken from Sura 5:32, which he interpreted as ‘he who kills one [Muslim] (i.e. in Afghanistan, or Iraq) kills all [Muslims] (i.e. all of the Umma), therefore he who saves the life of one [Muslim] saves the lives of all [Muslims].

This is the same Michael Adebolajo, who participated in a public campaign a few years ago against the UK, and against Tony Blair, claiming that those in the UK government were the real terrorists.

Michael Adebolajo used to be a Christian, whose parents (from Nigeria) raised him up in a strict Christian home, in Northeast London. But back in 2003 he was converted by Sheikh Omar Bakri Mohammed, the founder and then leader of the ‘Muhajiroun’ Party.

He then came under the influence of Anjem Choudary, the current leader of the group, now known as ‘Islam4UK’ (see the picture above with Anjem speaking in the microphone in the foreground, and Michael in the background on the left)

 

Anjem taught Michael to go to the Qur’an to find out how to live and work. Thus, it was verses in the Qur’an which motivated Michael to ‘cut off the head’ of the young soldier (see Sura 47:4). He and his accomplice, Michael Adebowale, a new convert to Islam, then remained at the scene. They did not run away, nor attack anyone else, but waited for the police to arrive, so that they could fulfill the verses which followed, which promised the two entrance into paradise if they were killed while participating in ‘Jihad’ (Sura 47:4-6).

Observe how a man who used to be a fine Christian has now changed into a brutal killer, following the references in his Qur’an to behead those who fight him!

But that’s not the whole story. For in to this scene came a woman, who got off her bus to go to the body of the soldier lying on the ground. Her name is Ingrid Loyau Kennett. She is a Catholic Christian.

She went to the body, and found the soldier was already dead. And then she did a curious thing. Rather than walk away, or join the onlooking crowd, she went over to Michael Adebowale, the 2nd killer, and asked him for his knives, almost reflecting what Christ said to Peter in Matthew 26:52, telling Peter to ‘Put away your sword, for he who lives by the sword will surely die by the sword’. When Michael refused and said that they were going to start a war, she replied that they would lose, because the greater good would stand against them.

 

During an interview the next day, she was asked why she went to the two men and asked them to desist. Her response was “I live my life as a Christian, and I believe in loving my Neighbour”

 

What a contrast between the actions of these two Muslim men, and the one Christian woman; one who had been a Christian, but had now become a Muslim, and thus now killed because his revelation supported it; while the other, a Christian woman, asking the men to put away their ‘swords’, because her revelation supported it.
