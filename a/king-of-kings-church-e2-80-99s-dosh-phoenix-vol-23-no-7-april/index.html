King of Kings church’s dosh

PHOENIX VOL. 23, NO. 7   APRIL 7-21, 2005

Although self-described cult buster, Mike Garde, has expressed concerns in the past over the Galway based Penecostal group, King of Kings Fellowship (KoK) – claiming it has ‘cultist tendencies’ – KoK has been downscaling its operations according to accounts just filed for the King of Kings Church Ltd.

It will be recalled (see The Phoenix 16/07/04) the company was listed for strike off by the Companies Office last year for failure to file accounts, however, happily for the KoK followers it has now provided all the necessary documents. Accounts for the year ended June 2003, refer to various cut backs within the organisation, including the closure of a cafe and preschool in 2004,however, the operations were incidental to the main business of the Church.

The business of the Church is to ‘promote the Christian Faith’ and on the strength of this, the company, which has charitable status and has no share capital, received tithes valued at 50,000 euro and donations and gifts worth 26,428 euro, bringing contributions to a total of 77,300 in 2003. This figure however, was less than half of the previous year’s 208,000 euro.

KoK’s pastor also received a gift of a car valued at 40,000 euro in 2002, although the pastor’s expenses took a dip from 14,000 euro in that year to just 1,000 euro in 2003. Meanwhile, the accounts show meagre accumulated profits of 14,000 euro at the end of June, 2003.

These days the company¹s directors include Oswald Sheridan, who is the husband of KoK founder, Mary Sheridan. Although Garde has accused Mary Sheridan of exercising a controlling influence over the group¹s members – who tend mainly to be women – she resigned as a director last year. Other directors include a pre-school assistant, Ita Walsh, a mature student, Ruth McHugh along with Ann Campion and Catherine Costello
