Yoga Pair Lost & Yoga Group ‘trying to reach perfection’

Tony Quinn

Yoga Pair Lost was published in the Evening Herald on 21/3/1977. It tells the story of the disappearance of two young men. Then in Yoga Group ‘trying to reach perfection’ printed in the Evening Press on 23/3/77 again the theme of the loss of the two men. There is picture of Quinn and his quote… “Everyone thinks I’m making a fortune.” There is a picture of Martin Forde one of Quinn’s strongest supporters who is formerly from Raheny and an Economist. His addiction to vintage cars is already in evidence. What makes it easier to accept is that is ten years old and got for him by the “group.”  Heard that one before. Also listen to the altruism.  Any donations given are given back. Here is the commune Jesus style group reincarnated.

We would love to hear from those who gave donations and got them back, and if anyone ever heard what happened to the young men?
