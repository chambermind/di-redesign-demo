Justice Bannister received NO expert testimony on the significance of Educo.

Tony Quinn

The trial did not receive evidence about Educo instruction, so that I cannot say anything about its subject matter and content.


https://www.eccourts.org/judgment/sm-life-ventures-v-susan-morrice-et-al

 P 6.

(b) Mr Quinn

[18] Ms McCaffrey introduced Ms Morrice to Mr Quinn in around 2002. Mr Quinn, who gave evidence at the trial, is a highly controversial figure both in the Republic of Ireland, of which he is native, and further a field. He runs what he calls Educo seminars, which people are persuaded to attend at very high cost. The trial did not receive evidence about Educo instruction, so that I cannot say anything about its subject matter and content. Mr Quinn told me that he promotes what he called a business structure which, on explanation, seemed to amount to no more than advice to promote better employees in preference to inferior ones, although to be fair to Mr Quinn he was not given an opportunity to set out the principles of his business structure in any detail. Mr Quinn also franchises what he calls Educo gyms and has a chain of health food stores. He claims to have a doctorate and degree in hypnotherapy and to have a Master’s degree from the University of East London. Since he has been associated with the Belize project very considerable sums have been paid by INE and BNE for personnel to attend his seminars and to use (and staff) his gyms. Holders of the Educo gym franchise pay royalty to one of Mr Quinn’s Jersey companies.

Ms McCaffrey introduced Ms Morrice to Mr Quinn in around 2002.

Here is the nub of the problem for Sheila McCaffrey. It was she herself who introduced Ms Morrice her then friend to Tony Quinn. Because she was under the influence of Quinn she was in the vanguard of those who tried to get others to go on seminars and subsequently to buy oil shares. She was also the person most associated with the notion that it was Quinn himself who’s Mind Technology had located the oil.

Here is how she expressed it in 2006:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/09/20/quinn%E2%80%99s-mind-games-given-credit-for-oil-strike-in-belize-enda-leahy/

 Enniskillen-born Sheila McCaffrey, a director of BNE, said the company’s success was not pot luck: “Seven years ago I trained with Tony Quinn; it was an absolute transformation. It completely changed my life and it’s the only reason I’m in Belize today,” she said last week.

Quinn’s philosophy, which promotes what he calls “mind technology”, has been criticised as brainwashing but is defended by adherents as positive and life-changing.

Later in the judgement we read that she was part of the decision to gift TQ shares. Now when Sheila was illegally and savagely set upon and her life reduced to misery did she vacate the space where she was located, namely being under the influence of Quinn? It is one thing to become aware of a movement being a bad place to be and leave, it is entirely another thing to be thrown out and where the issue is not Educo but money.

The former person who leaves goes through anger, loss, and blind hatred as they realise they have been victims of a pyramid scheme. They often languish in the long grass and feel total idiots as they lick their wounds. They have been told if they read our blog or the newspapers bad things can happen. So they retreat into depression and turn in on themselves. Very few even have the ability to even leave a comment other than calling Quinn names on our blog. They have been so overwhelmed they do not have the vocabulary to articulate what has happened to them.

The latter are either passive former Educoists or now only concerned to get their money back and consequently are in an even worse state as their only preoccupation is getting the MONEY! This state could go on for 10, 20 or 30 years unless it is addressed.

This is the significance of Angie and disappointed Educoist entering the fray. They show the significance of the blog under two headings:

1. They are using the blog and reading the material on TQ and other groups in their own recovery and it costs them nothing. They may need professional help in this journey but it is using this blog that feeds that therapeutic interaction with the professional.

2. Thought Reform is what most Educoists are experiencing. They have had their mind taken over by processes they can’t understand. Here they find the information that breaks the control of seeing Quinn as Messiah, Jesus or the IT at the centre of being. The break from the insidious self centred philosophy of UPM, Use Other People’s Money. This self centred philosophy destroys the persons ethical fibre and allows them to target the weak for their advantage. They glory in not paying tax and taking responsibility for their activities. This selfist philosophy leaves people as a material shell without a spiritual core. Quinn uses the rhetoric of spirituality but the Educo phase of his seminars are based on pure materialism. We call this control the ‘idiot effect.’  Certainly the people affected are not idiots, but are generally sensitive people who have been affected when highly suggestive or vulnerable.

As a loyal supporter Sheila has been turned on and used as a scapegoat in regard to the problems of the company. To hide the avarice and illegalities of removing her from the board, Quinn in a typical cult manner concocted a conspiracy.

The problem logically for the judge was because Sheila did not show that she was a victim of undue influence and thought reform she acted as if she was always the way she is now and her case made no sense to the judge. She seemed to be gilding the lily and the judge threw out her legal requests. If Sheila had gone to a major media outlet and told her story after the initial embarrassment she would have been able to give, ‘expert testimony on the significance of Educo.’ She would also have been able to make the difficult case about cult influence and she would have over 350 posts on our blog going back 40 years to assist her.

 Mr Quinn, who gave evidence at the trial, is a highly controversial figure both in the Republic of Ireland, of which he is native, and further a field.

We just leave for you to read what the Judge says here. He can see that Quinn is leaving a trail of controversy behind him.

He runs what he calls Educo seminars, which people are persuaded to attend at very high cost.

Note the factual conclusion at very high cost. Again note ‘persuaded.’

Not encouraged but a heavy sell, which involves the disabling of the person’s rational faculties in order to get the money off them. Mind Masters, masterful mind control and the more you charge the more it is worth. It is always the Four Seasons, never Bewley’s Hotel.

Mr Quinn told me that he promotes what he called a business structure which, on explanation, seemed to amount to no more than advice to promote better employees in preference to inferior ones.

When Quinn is out of his controlled environment he reverts to rote learning like a kid in Senior Infants.

However, in fairness the Judge continues,

although to be fair to Mr Quinn he was not given an opportunity to set out the principles of his business structure in any detail.

Just as well he did not have to explain in open court how he is helping to destroy businesses, marriages, families and leave a trail of emotional damage around the world.

Mr Quinn also franchises what he calls Educo gyms and has a chain of health food stores.

The Gyms except for a few seem to be on the way out or funded by funds unknown. We reported just over a week ago that the Health Food Shops are in poor shape.

He claims to have a doctorate and degree in hypnotherapy and to have a Master’s degree from the University of East London.

Note the judge reports he ‘claims.’ We have shown this is actually something if he claims it in the US could have negative consequences for him. To repeat a bit of logic. If you do not have even a Leaving Certificate, and have obtained a Doctorate, why do you then do an MA on the same subject? In other words go backwards to go forwards? Also he is still saying up to the present he is working on a Doctorate.

Finally, the Judge writes,

‘Since he has been associated with the Belize project very considerable sums have been paid by INE and BNE for personnel to attend his seminars and to use (and staff) his gyms. Holders of the Educo gym franchise pay royalty to one of Mr Quinn’s Jersey companies.’

In other words is Quinn a kind of spiritual leader, a play acter businessman or a drain on the B Shareholders? More to follow.
