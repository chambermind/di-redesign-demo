A bad dream- The Hick’s Tower Green Zone Post- False Alarm

Tony Quinn

“When Jones town was about to fall Jim Jones ordered everyone to die so they could reach enlightenment. TQ educo castle is about to come crashing down around him very soon. Maybe the next new years eve party in hicks tower, as all the educoist travel from far and wide to attend might be the scene of Irish Jones town. If the champagne taste bitter DON’T drink run away…………………………………”

Someone left This comment on our blog, which got me thinking. They thought we might have a Jonestown scenario at Hick’s Tower. It is doubtful as TQ’s operation does not involve him hiding in the jungle rather living it up with Eve. The last thing he is going to do is commit suicide. Rather it is likely it will be his disciples who can’t live with the consequences of their actions.

We have been reflecting on the nature of the Eduocist recently. Why are they so passive. Why is there not a sesnse of burning anger at the rip off they have experienced?

Some ideas, because many had accepted the premise that Quinn had put forward namely material success.

He had long since given up the idea of Yogic spirituality, or he disguised his real interest which was to take as much dosh as he could at the expense of others.

Many who were screwed by Quinn shared his materialist assumptions. So when they saw the light they wanted to hide under a rock. Even when they sought assistance their main concern is how they could make money from their own experience.

Very few are willing to give time to help others who are affected, because leaving Quinn has not changed their assumptions merely their bank balance. The recession is gradually changing the perception of celebrity, but whether many survivors will truly become concerned about building a more just Ireland or helping other victims only time will tell
