BBC Spotlight programme on Quinn, Morrice and McCaffrey no longer available.

Tony Quinn

The Spotlight on Quinn ~ The BBC goes to town
We are no longer able to show this programme.  Here you can read the BBC’s explanation and DI’s responses.

They kindly explained their current policy.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/10/15/the-spotlight-on-quinn-the-bbc-goes-to-town/

DI: Are you willing to consider the case as an educational Trust dedicated to educating the public who were very actively involved in the creation of the programme to reverse your decision and to allow us to use it help those affected by this organisation? I would be happy to discuss this with you?

The BBC does not allow the BBC or BBC Worldwide to license identifiable BBC

content to non-BBC websites as a matter of policy. I’m afraid that it is not possible to

permit you to host this material on either your website or YouTube,

please note that this is the policy that is applied to all requests and

there has been no decision made which could be reconsidered as you request..

The BBC allows websites to link to material on the BBC website, the guidelines can

be found here;

http://www.bbc.co.uk/terms/business.shtml

However, recordings of programmes are only made available on the BBC website

for a limited period after transmission so this programme may no longer be available

DI:

Thank you for that clarification. Are you saying to me then that all your great research and material is now lost to a world audience after a certain period has elapsed?

It is like an author publishing a book and then shredding it after the first print run.

In the digital age with so many different options available does that make sense?

I know you are only carrying out directives and I really appreciate you getting back to me. Surely no one is going to pay to purchase a programme like this?

Surely the BBC getting its image up there is what counts, rather than lying in a basement somewhere?

All we are trying to be is a public library!
Best Wishes
Mike
