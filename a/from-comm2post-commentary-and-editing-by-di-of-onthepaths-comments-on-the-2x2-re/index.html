From comm2Post: Commentary and editing by DI of “onthepath’s” comments on the 2×2 “Religion.”

Two by Twos

Hello, I am new to the site. I was born into the 2×2 religion.

Thank you for your comments they are very welcome and we are going to respond to them and then hopefully we will also get the Two by Two’s or the Nameless One’s to respond.

Note your opening comment that you were born into the 2×2 religion.

This suggests at once a notion of uniqueness, that it is a religion in its own right and that it’s a birth rite religion. Not just another denomination of Christianity, but that it = Christianity in its own right? From being a reform movement it has become an established religion which you are born into. Of course, genuine Christianity you have to elect to join you can’t be born into it.

http://www.ebible.org/bible/kjv/John.htm

1:13 Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

In my experience, every (geographical) area has different rules. I think this explains people’s different perspectives here on this thread.

As a child I was sexual abused for many years by a church member (also a relative). My family hid this as they didn’t want upheaval in the church. I was told in around about way that it was the lesser of two evils for me to be a sacrifice for the family and the church. It was more important that his family not leave the church. It would be better for him to be thrown into the sea with a millstone tied around his neck than for him to cause one of these little ones to sin. Luke 17:2

This is the objective standard by which this behaviour should have been viewed from; however, it is clear that this person was sacrificed for the good of the organisation and family unity. Criminal behaviour was not addressed and the victim was not heard.

In my early teens the worker of my area, (also a head worker in the province) was told.

What would the Apostle have said about it,

1 Corinthians 5

Sexual Immorality Defiles the Church

5 It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and of a kind that is not tolerated even among pagans, for a man has his father’s wife. 2 And you are arrogant! Ought you not rather to mourn? Let him who has done this be removed from among you.

3 For though absent in body, I am present in spirit; and as if present, I have already pronounced judgment on the one who did such a thing. 4 When you are assembled in the name of the Lord Jesus and my spirit is present, with the power of our Lord Jesus, 5 you are to deliver this man to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.1

6 Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump? 7 Cleanse out the old leaven that you may be a new lump, as you really are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed. 8 Let us therefore celebrate the festival, not with the old leaven, the leaven of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

9 I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people—10 not at all meaning the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would need to go out of the world. 11 But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother if he is guilty of sexual immorality or greed, or is an idolater, reviler, drunkard, or swindler—not even to eat with such a one. 12 For what have I to do with judging outsiders? Is it not those inside the church2 whom you are to judge? 13 God judges those outside. “Purge the evil person from among you.”

Note instead of the abuser being put out and treated as a non believer, the innocent is revictimised and treated to censure?

He talked to the person alone and asked him to destroy evidence. The elders were told but were asked to keep it secret. He said, Hey sorry about that, hope we can still be friends. This man continued to attend church and speak. He was still allowed around children without supervision and no authorities were informed.

Here we see the destruction of criminal evidence and a criminal conspiracy based on the assumption that their group’s law trumps the criminal law

He was asked to apologize to me alone.

A controlled empty apology is given and there was no sign of repentance or any form of excommunication to make public the evil crime perpetrated on a child.

In my early twenties I decided to make a statement and have him charged because I felt he was harming his children and felt by not doing anything I was no better than the others who had done nothing. Eventually he pleaded guilty to numerous sexual abuse charges and given a year house arrest. That day in court, he pleaded guilty and sat in the front row with his wife, parents and other members of the church. I sat with my father and a court official.

Do you know how that feels? To be alone again? Since I was a child I had issues with my immediate family for letting this happen to me, so I was already an the outsider as far as they were concerned. However, the final straw was when I reported him and had him charged almost all of my family have disowned me. The friends in the area and workers have exiled me. They do not speak to me and are very rude if they happen to come upon me. He is still a member and attends all church gatherings. No worker has spoken to the area about this issue so it has been left to rumours. They have been told by the perpetrator and his wife he plead guilty to it because he was tired of it and didn’t want to drag the whole family through anything else. He claimed as well that I got money out of it…even though it was criminal not civil case. I didn’t receive a penny! I would like to know why I am the problem?

Here we see the reversal of biblical discipline. The victim is victimised and the victimiser is granted immunity?

The result of not following this path leads to the results described below. The person can’t see God as they are blocked because black has become white and vice versa.

Why hasn’t the workers talked to the area and cleared this up? People are allowing him around their children unsupervised because they don’t know the truth. I tried what I thought would correct the situation but it hasn’t. I am not here to protest the religion, choose whatever path you like. I just feel hurt that because of this, it has changed my life. I have to try and get healthy from not only the sexual abuse but the betrayal of family and so called friends, as well as the fight with God I have. I am so confused about God. It’s hard once you get away from those rules that constantly indicate what status you have with God to find your own happiness. I hope one day I can find that.

I enjoyed reading everyone’s posts!

One thing I forgot that is important is the idea to “not offend thy brother.”

This is a way of saying you should cover up criminal activity under the guise of Christian love. A horrific view of Christianity.

They seem to also not be aware of the fact that by not addressing the abuse they are putting other vulnerable children at risk. She has an uncle who does not agree in the way the 2×2’s dealt with but is not willing to rock the boat. They are willing to put the eternal destiny of children and vulnerable adults at risk to preserve the unity of the group. Any attempts to educate yourself about these issues is frowned on where I live.

I was born into the 2×2 and most of my family still remains in the “Truth.” I am excommunicated. I am not against 2×2 as a faith commitment, it is everyone’s personal choice. I know that different geographical area have different expectations. In the area I grew up in they were extremely strict, whereas as down south was much more lenient. This was quite evident at conventions we attend down south were the kids especially girls were allowed to wear and do many more things. Here are some rules or things I experienced as a kid.

#1 We were not suppose to associate or play with “outsider” kids. So parents were more or less strict on this.

#2 When children or others questioned our faith my siblings or I never knew what really to say.

#3 As a teen in my area we had rules such as:
no make up, dyed hair, bangs, cut hair, nail polish, short skirts, pants, shorts, jewellery, engagement rings until recently were really frowned upon. They preferred watches. Now it is more common. No TV, internet (until the last 8 or so years), radio, attending movies, Christmas, Easter, music(unless hymns) Drinking, smoking, premarital sex or cohabitation.

#4 There were definitely different classes of people(status). People were considered on the edge…like not hearty followers. This could be because you had a divided family, divorced, single mother, not following rules or expectations. My siblings and I were considered problematic because we didn’t conform. Like I was told by a head worker I would not be allowed to speak if he seen me wearing toe nail polish again.

#5 You weren’t forced to profess but I felt pressured. I was 14 years old and most kids my age had professed. I never wanted to but they had talks with me etc and I felt pressured into it

#6 It seems they had an emphasis on young marriage. Teens in my area get married usually quite young

#7 Career choices for women. I applied for college as a corrections officer. My parents were embarrassed and asked me not to tell the workers. When I did they said it was not a good idea as a job for a woman. Male based jobs were definitely discouraged…Like wearing pants for a job as a required outfit.

#8 Do not ask certain questions! This is absolutely my experience. If an adult member started asking questions they deemed that as showing a lack of faith and there would be consequences.

#9 Absolute denial of William Irvine!! My aunt and uncle decided to quit going to chuch in the late 90′s. It seemed quite a few left around the time this information came out. There was a meeting held in my area because they originated from there and also have relatives there. A member at the meeting said they had new information on the origins of the Truth concerning William Irvine. The head worker denied this and said that if you accepted this new evidence showed that you lacked faith. To this day it is not accepted in my area that he started the “Religion.”

#10 The elder goes to the worker with any problems. The civil authorities are never informed in my area. It is dealt as an internal matter, and the civil authorities are not recognised.

#11 People are favoured. Like those who host conventions!!

#12 When the workers are in your home you treat them like gods. This is so true!! They were made special meals!! I remember one worker said to me “Oh I just want a hotdog or a burger, because everyone makes this huge effort to make elaborate meals”. Kids must behave perfectly when they are around…even hide things. You must be quiet while they are reading and praying. It was all about them. It was understood to be the equivalent of as having Jesus in your house.

#13 They always drove other members cars and they were always very nice! They always had top of the line clothing and toys (laptop, cell phones, printers, ipads etc) They had keys to peoples houses.

#14 you had a better status if you donated more money

#15 The head workers must accept you as a worker. Usually this means you spent your childhood (if you grew up in it) very obedient. If you had a family of workers or a child that becomes a worker you are revered

If you exit or are excommunicated they do gossip a lot and say they just weren’t strong enough in your faith or strong enough to deny temptations.

This is the way it is taught all the time in regards to the rules. The party line was…The harder path leads you to God

After I left it was hard to get the rules out of my mind and the guilt that came with following them. They would say thing is the flesh tempting you I remember even playing cards (with a poker deck) I felt bad. Or being around a worker made me still feel inferior. It takes years to feel normal again.

You question God…Why would he put us here just to watch us suffer…doesn’t really make sense

Or you meet amazing “outsiders” who are great loving people and the truth teaches you they are bad and sinful just because they don’t go to church.
