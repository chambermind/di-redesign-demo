ALCC: The Trials of internment 1 by Kenneth Sabourin

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

My story at Abundant Life…

I have recently discovered Dialogue Ireland and its forum topic of Abundant Life Christian Centre in Galway, Ireland. As so many people I too have suffered at the hands of the tyrants and the cultism of this church. I would like to share my story and hope to bring some form of healing to those who are hurt as well. In addition I hope my story sheds light on the darkness that this so called church is and that is it brought down and Galway can be saved. I was an intern there from 2007-2008 from America. Most of their interns come directly from Ireland or Texas, where the Sanford’s originate. Before I unveil my story Id like to give everyone some perspective of who I am. On the forum my name was SSJ2GOHAN but my real name is Kenny Sabourin of Seattle, Washington. It’s located on the west coast of the United States of America. I am 22 years old but my time at ALCC was right out of high school so my age was 18 and by the time I left I was 19. I am a junior at Eastern Washington University majoring in social work and getting certified in addiction studies. I love Jesus with all my heart and Jesus loved his church. ALCC is not a Jesus church and it has done so much damage to marriages, families and individuals. So as a Christian I also believe it’s our job to stop those who proclaim the name of Jesus but do the exact opposite for their kingdom. As a young adult I have always had a huge heart for Ireland and so after I graduated high school I search online for a church in Ireland that accepts interns who want to give their time to serve God and get an international experience. I corresponded with ALCC in the spring of 2007 and when I graduated high school I was on a plane to Ireland 6 days later.


I immediately got plugged in to helping with their most recent “community outreach” Christian Camp of the Arts. It was downhill from day 1. I could write a book on the inequities and depravity of this church but because we are time sensitive in dealing with ALCC I will try to remain brief but give as much detail. Note I will refer to Pastor Kevin and Pastor Heather as Kevin and Heather or the Sanford’s. I will not be referring to anything directly negative about their children but I will talk about their parenting skills and how others had to babysit them. I will speak only for myself but I will also speak for what I observed in others. I didn’t have time to outline this story so I hope it will flow well enough for readers to grasp and understand. Whether you have been hurt by this church or are a member I encourage you to read this fully as I was a major cornerstone of ALCC’s labor and inner workings. I was full time staff with all the other ladies who worked there so I know what went down at this establishment. After my plane had landed I was transported back to the church by a member and was instantly thrown into work by Heather. This was June 22nd, 2007 during ALCC’s prep for Christian Camp of the Arts or also known as CCA. CCA is their summer vacation bible school. I assisted with everything including singing, dancing, building, and transition between scenes. On Sundays the church had 1 to 2 services, with the first one being some form of class of seminar that Kevin ran and the 2nd being the main service. Along with child care provided there was the kid’s church that I was involved with. Putting on dramas, songs and bible teaching for the kids. Monday through Friday I worked 50-70 hours a week for the church. I did several jobs including janitorial work such as vacuuming, sweeping, cleaning kitchen, dishes, cleaning both gender bathrooms, restocking the waters in Kevin’s office, emptying trash, organizing closets and spaces and other cleaning projects as well. I also spent a lot of my time doing errands like getting copies and prep for Sunday’s sermon, getting lunch orders making phone calls, sending emails but representing myself as other staff members of the church. My work day was a typical 9-5 but during seasons of kids camp or holidays or other events I would work a night shift that could and would very well bleed into 1am or later. A majority of my focus besides all these jobs was focusing on the Sunday school ministry because that year’s theme was star wars and me being a big star wars fan, I contributed a great deal to writing Sunday school dramas every week for the kids. Half of my time was spent doing the kids ministry while the other half was spent doing misc work for the Sanford’s. On Saturdays I had to go to evangelism training or work some more depending on what the Sanford’s wanted. Sundays was game day and I had 2 jobs. I’d get there bright and early just after the worship team got there and I had to make the church look good. Get rid of any excess trash on the ground, stock 1 regular and 1 sparking water underneath the Sanford’s chairs, make sure the crèche’s were clean, bathrooms were clean, lights turned on, chairs aligned properly, plants looked straight and much more. Once service time came to, the other children’s church workers and I had to go across the hall and suit up for the star wars children’s church show. Once it was over we cleaned everything up I was exhausted from the week and the performance I had to put on that morning. Sunday evenings were the only time I had “off” but more times then others I’d get stuck working again.
