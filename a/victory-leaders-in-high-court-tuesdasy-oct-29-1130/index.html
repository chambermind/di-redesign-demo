Victory leaders in High Court Tuesday Oct 29 @ 11:30

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

The Victory leadership is in court with a new legal team, we will be able to give you an update later on the results of this legal action. We can officially report that the hearing relates to issues concerning the receivership.

It will likely take at least 3 days and Victory are purely challenging the manner in which the Receivers acted last June. It has no bearing on any substantial issue related to the ownership of the properties. The judgement will be reserved and likely it will be well into November before judgement will be delivered. The logic of this seems to suggest that it might be premature for the receiver to grant Victory rental possibilities if they are in court at each others throats? However, that is purely speculation.

You may remember when Brian Hade appeared in court followed by his mother and father as well as Gerry Byrne they seemed to using the language and style of the Freeman movement.* They soon seemed to reject this and accepted the authority of the court.

* https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/06/13/hades-or-heaven-friday-june-14-the-four-courts/

Then Brian was brought forward and seemed to have some kind of coaching. This correspondent was informed that some American with a ‘Freeman’ disposition was coaching him. He seemed like a rabbit in the lights and spoke in a  hackneyed manner. Brian Hade spoke about not breaking any law and being a law abiding citizen. The judge tried to make clear to him the dimensions of our democratic system and tried to give him a kind of abc to assist him to be able to see that he was flagrantly breaking the law. He did not seem to wish to be represented and so the judge gave him an extended lunch break to reach a decision as to his response.
This summary of the Freeman position gives an insight into his thinking.

http://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Freeman_on_the_land

“Freemen hold that we are all subject to a massive international legal conspiracy perpetrated for the profit of the elites, but you can hack the system if you just use the right form of words. They believe only in their version of natural law, which they call “common law.” In practical terms, they believe this means they do not have to pay taxes, debts, mortgages, etc. because we were all deceived and if you say the right form of words this fact will be accepted.

Freemen believe they can declare themselves independent of government jurisdiction using the concept of “lawful rebellion”: that all statute law is contractual and therefore only applicable if an individual consents to it. They assert that what everyone else regards as “the law” doesn’t apply to them as they have not consented to a contract with the state, even going so far as to claim they have a lawful right to refuse arrest if they do not consent.”
He did not appear to have any religious convictions and never framed his response with reference to God. It would have seemed logical to suggest he was obeying a higher law. However, in this case the mundane nature of his resistance including ‘breaking into’ his former premises after the security personnel had taken over the building seemed to suggest he was under some form of influence. It might have been Brian’s strategy to be imprisoned so he could bring a case under Article 40 of the Constitution for unlawful arrest?

The kind of religious world view shown by the resistance to lawful authority has not fortunately led to any violence, but people under religious influence will not act logically as in other scenarios.
