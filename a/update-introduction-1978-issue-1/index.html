New religious movements and politics

What are the political consequences of the new religious movements? A number of answers have been given to this question, but these answers often tend to be over-simplified and too general interpretations.

In this issue of Up-date we document part of the diversity. The different movements point in different directions. And it is very difficult – if not impossible – to find a common denominator in this variety.

In the first article “Meditation and action” an attempt is made to penetrate into the Yogic way of life in order to find some of the operative concepts which determine the attitude to the world and its realities. The notion of deconditioning man is central and from it comes a general tendency towards enstasis, i.e. a movement into the interior away from the exterior world. The author of this presentation is the Director of the Institute for Missiology and Ecumenics, Univeristy of Aarhus, Denmark, dr. Johannes Aagaard.

The second article “Political implications in Neo-Buddhism” points first of all to the pragmatic attitude of Buddhism to society: that society is good which can guarantee the existence of the Sangha and the Dhamma, i.e. the order of monks and the teaching. The author is a Swedish scholar who is teaching at the above mentioned institute, lic.phil. Thomas Hultberg.

Transcendental Meditation is dealt with in the following articles: “Transcendental Meditation and Politics” and “TM is a religious practice, court rules“, written by the

Danish teacher Peter Maarbjerg. The author first of all describes “The World Plan” upon which TM bases “The Age of Enlightenment” and its main instrument “The. World Government”. Interesting in thin connection is the ruling of the court in Newark, New Jersey, USA, that the use of tax-money in order to promote TM is a violation of the first amendment to the Constitution.

How big the difference can be between two religious movements concerning their political attitudes is made clear by Pernille Aagaard in her articles “The Children of God’s Attitude towards Politics” and “Sun Myung Moon and the Third World War“. While the USA for Moses David and his “family” is a lost nation, the chief of all evil powers, the same USA is for Moon the chosen nation and the instrument par excellence in God’s holy war against communism. And while godly socialism for Moses is the hoped for aim, socialism and communism for Moon are negative and demonic forces.

Finally Flemming Falkenberg Olsen, a student of theology from Aarhus, introduces the AAO, Action Analytic Organisation, as an Anarchistic Organisation, describing its attempt to realize a socialist style of life as praxis.
