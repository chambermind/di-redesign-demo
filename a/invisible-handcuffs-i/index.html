Invisible Handcuffs I

Cultism

British police investigating a human trafficking case have spoken of the “invisible handcuffs” of emotional and psychological coercion involved. But why are they so surprised? Jonny Jacobsen argues that, for those involved in following cultism, little of this has come as a shock. He writes that “Anyone working in the field of cultic abuse could tell them that it is perfect possible to reduce people to virtual slavery through emotional and psychological abuse“.
