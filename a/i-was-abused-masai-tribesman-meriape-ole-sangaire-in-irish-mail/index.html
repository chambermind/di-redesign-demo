‘I WAS ABUSED’ Masai tribesman Meriape Ole Sangaire in Irish Mail

Icross Charity

‘I WAS ABUSED

In his first statement to The Irish Mail on Sunday, Masai tribesman Meriape Ole Sangaire claimed that Meegan began abusing him physically and psychologically when he was in his mid teens. The Kenyan first met Meegan in 1983, when he was 15 and living with his parents in the family’s hut. Meegan took the teenager under his wing, employed him and promised him an education. Meriape’s parents let the charity boss borrow some of their land on which to build an Icross compound. Almost immediately, however, Meriape began to notice some misleading behaviour on the part of Meegan. The Irishman would encourage Meriape and other locals to play up their image as ‘savages’ to visitors and donors. He would also exaggerate the primitiveness of his own living conditions to them. Then the abuse started. ‘In 1986, he began harassing me by touching me continually,’ Meriape said. ‘I asked him not to, but he continued, telling me I had sexy buttocks and telling me to touch his secret parts,’ he added. The behaviour progressed. ‘I became Meegan’s first victim, I realise now,’ the Kenyan said. ‘One night he pulled me into his bedroom and tried to force me to take off my trousers, trying to rip them off me. ‘I resisted him but although I was strong he was stronger. ‘He raped me that night. I didn’t shout but I cried. I cried a lot. ‘Even now I have problems with sex, problems I do not even discuss with my wife. ‘Once he put some Valium pills in my drink and that night I woke up to find him on top of me, trying to penetrate me.’ He explained: ‘After that I found the courage to tell my parents. They went to the District Commissioner to report him. ‘The DC wrote him a letter instructing him to report to his office.

‘As a result Meegan decided to leave my parents’ piece of land and left me desperate and ashamed, with poor education and no career.’
