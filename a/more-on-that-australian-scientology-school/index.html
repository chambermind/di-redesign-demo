More on that Australian Scientology school

Scientology

The following is from a KR (Knowledge Report) concerning the Yarralinda School in Australia. A KR in Scientology is a method of reporting out-ethics. The full 1.2MB PDF scans of this can be obtained here.

To:  John Sullivan
Flora Sullivan
Neridz James

CC:    MAA Flag Ship Service Org
MAA APS Cont
APS Consultant ABLE INT via APS INT
ED ABLE ANZO
CO OSA ANZO for info
MAA AOSH ANZO
Yarralinda School Ethics Section
D/DSA Melb D Emmanuel Foundas via DSA MelDay
HES Melb D Errol Schmidt

From Yarralinda School Board

KNOWLEDGE REPORT
JOHN SULLIVAN
FLORA SULLIVAN
NERIDA JAMES

5,5.08

The three above-mentioned individuals originated responsibility for the Ideal Org fundraising cycle that involved using the Yarralinda School property as security for a group of individuals who wished to donate funds to the org but did not have the funds available at the time of need. They brought this proposal to the Board of the school and assured the school of their involvement. They also originated that a ‘Friends of  Yarralinda’ should be formed and the individuals who had pledged money as well as the above-mentioned three would be part of this group. The intention of the group was to help Yarralinda School flourish and prosper. John Sullivan also said that he would formally get this association drawn up.

This has never been done.

The Board was also reassured that they would not have to follow up on this loan cycle, i.e. not have to chase the pledged money repayments.

This ended up not being the case (see attached time line of Board meetings re the cycle.)

When the Board was presented with this cycle we were told that the exchange for our involvement and risk was that our school loan would be paid out This figure at the time was S340,000. This amount was to be fundraised by the terminals involved in the ideal

Org. But at the Special meeting that we called in November 2007 we found out that this was not the case. (See attached minutes)

John Sullivan stated that the intention was that the school was to be “no worse off” for having been involved in this cycle and that  all was well.

This being said we are actually worse off.

1. Our mortgage was not paid out.

2. Interest on our mortgage has now accrued and has added to our debt-  if we had the understanding that our loan portion %Pas still ours we would have endeavored to handle regular payments.

3. Also our previous loan was at a lower interest rate and a better deal than the Ideal Org loan – had we understood that our loan was not to be paid out we would have kept our loan cycle separate and not had it combine into the ideal Org cycle.

4. Another aspect that we as a Board were not aware of until recently is that because the school building was used as security for the loan it entered into our financial accounts and set up a red flag with the Federal Government who are threatening to withdraw their funding to our school. This would effectively close the school down. This is currently being addressed but as the loan was due on the 3lst March 2008 and DID NOT HAVE ALL PLEDGES PAID we are in danger.

5. Our Board spent considerable time and effort chasing up all the legal papers that the pledges had to sign instead of working on programs to expand the school as that is our Board hat.

6. We also spent many hours working out the exact payments that each pledge owed as that had not been done by Emmanuel Found as.

7. At this date there is still $905,000.00 outstanding.

We called the special meeting to find out where all the pledged monies were at. We were assured that all was going well by Emmanuel Found as (D/DSA Melb) and Errol Schmidt (HE S Melb D) and that anyone who was having difficulty would he helped by these two. (See attached)

We followed this up again to make sure everything was going well for the payout date of 31st of March 08 and were again assured it was by the D/DSA and HES.

TI-IIS DID NOT HAPPEN. The loan was not paid out by the due date.

The above-mentioned individuals did not follow through and direct the cycle or get individuals to wear their hats – we attempted to get John Sullivan to follow through with the Association cycle several times but it did not happen.

The Board saw this cycle as an opportunity to help the Ideal Org and the above-mentioned individuals originated to the Board paying out the school’s mortgage (see attached Board minutes) to be helped in return. Only then to be told – “We never meant to pay out your loan”.

This is true.

The Board of the Yarralinda School

John Xenophontos – Chairman

Connie Curato

John Ford

Christel Duffy

Joe Curato (past member)

Paul Schofield (new member)
