A Special New Year Reflection from INE – Kissing the Blarney stone Morrice style

Tony Quinn

—– Original Message —–

From: International Natural Energy LLC

To: info@ine-energy.com

Sent: Friday, January 01, 2010 11:29 PM

Subject: A Special New Year Reflection from INE

January 1, 2010

Dear Members,

As we enter a new decade, 2010, I wanted to reflect on how far the company has come in such a short period of time. Leaders of countries, companies and other investors have heard the incredible story of BNE and its holistic discovery and development of oil in Belize, and how it is indeed transforming the country. You can be very proud of your membership in this company and your belief and alignment in this dream.

This unheard of success and global reputation of the company has all been possible due to the critical involvement of Dr. Tony Quinn and the Educo seminars. Over 90% of the shares are held by 330 Educoists, who have been on the Educo seminar, and who believed in the importance of the Holistic model in making their investment. In his seminars, Dr. Tony Quinn was able to introduce attendees to this holistic model, which in turn inspired them to invest in INE/BNE, which then facilitated the sales and profits for existing members. This is a rare and extraordinary benefit to members in a private company.

The rewards have been very significant. Over the past 7 years the membership has realized over 19 million dollars in profits, through transactions, from an initial 5 million dollar investment. These transactions were assisted by the company, with no brokerage fee involved. In addition the Loan Release Program provided almost 10 million dollars to the members during this Christmas holiday period. This return on investment is unheard of – especially in a company so young as INE/BNE and at a time of economic difficulties throughout the world.

Many of the members have expressed their gratitude to Tony already and the Board of Directors would like to join them in thanking him for all he has done in the creation and his undaunted commitment to the company.

Have an amazing start to 2010 and I look forward to seeing you on the 10 January in Dublin.

Susan Morrice
INE/BNE Chairperson
