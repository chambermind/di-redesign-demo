Freedom Ministries, Mylerstown House, Robertstown, Co. Kildare

Freedom Ministries

DI received a report that Freedom Ministries is now operating a Men’s Home out of the house below.

Sean Rogers, his wife and Stuart Murphy are continuing what they did in VO. According to our correspondent it is alleged that they “manipulate addicts and alcoholics into paying their dole and selling 40 cases of cupcakes each day.

“Apparently these are the new means to keep Andy and Marie Valdez living at the standard they have come accustomed to from illegal soliciting of money from people.” Our correspondent believes “they are a disgrace to Christianity.”

There are a couple of families there also that run the day to day operation. “Prayer, cooking, sending men out selling cup cakes and window cleaning.” “Greyhounds are kept there also and trained for races…” It is likely that unemployment benefits would be addressed to the house from the local exchange. If anyone has evidence of illegality please make a statement at your local Garda station or if you believe Welfare payments are going astray please phone the Department of Social Protection. Apparently, they search people for iPhones and stuff and ban photographs because of the exposé done on them by RTE. They are paranoid and trying to operate below the radar.
