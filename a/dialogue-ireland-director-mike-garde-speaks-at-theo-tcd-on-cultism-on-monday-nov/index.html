Dialogue Ireland Director Mike Garde speaks at “Theo,” TCD on Cultism on Monday Nov 12 @ 7 PM

Conferences & CommentariesCultismReligion

TCD Theological Society, home of interesting talks and ‘refreshments’

http://trinitytheologicalsociety.com/events/mike-garde-combating-cults-dialogue-ireland-and-freedom-of-choice/

Theos primary aim is to promote inter-religious dialogue with a focus on topical issues, inviting guests ranging from prestigious academics to celebrated authors to successful politicians to organisational figures speaking on a wide range of subjects, such as Human Rights, Current Affairs and Social and Environmental Responsibility, including the role religion has played in helping or hindering the development of our society. Each event is followed by a relaxed reception. Theo also provides free stress reducing Meditations for members, undertakes an annual experiential trip and has an academic Library and promotes the exploration of all aspects of interfaith experience and dialogue.

He will be speaking about Cultism rather than Cults. What are the attitudes and tendencies that combine to create this phenomenon?
