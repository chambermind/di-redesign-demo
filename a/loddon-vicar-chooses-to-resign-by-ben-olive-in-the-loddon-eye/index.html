Loddon Vicar Chooses To Resign by Ben Olive in the Loddon Eye

Abuse by Church and Irish institutionsConferences & Commentaries

Posted on 6 November, 2012 by Ben

http://loddoneye.com/2012/11/06/loddon-vicar-chooses-to-resign/#more-1558

The Rev William Stewart has resigned from his position as vicar at the Chet Valley Benefice in Loddon after details of an incident from his past have placed his family under pressure.

Rev William Stewart (left) and Rt Rev Graham James

Director of Communications for the Diocese of Norwich, the Ven Jan McFarlane, confirmed that Willi Stewart (pictured) has resigned from his post as of last Sunday: “Mr Stewart has chosen to resign following increased pressure on him and his family after an incident from his past was made public on the internet.”

It is believed that details of the “incident” from several years ago were posted online in 2011, close to the time Mr Stewart came to Loddon. The reverend, who hails from Northern Ireland and has spent many years working in the Republic of Ireland, has now decided that at this time it is in the interests of him and his family to step down.

Mr Stewart was inducted, installed and licensed in the Holy Trinity Church, Loddon, on 16th June 2011 by the Bishop of Norwich, the Rt Rev Graham James.

Jan McFarlane assured The Loddon Eye that at the time of Mr Stewart’s appointment “all the appropriate background checks had been carried out and Willi was appointed in good faith from the Church of Ireland.”

Steps are being made to replace Mr Stewart and all services, including the Remembrance Day service this Sunday, will continue as normal.
