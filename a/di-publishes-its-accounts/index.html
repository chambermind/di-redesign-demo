Dialogue Ireland Trust* publishes its accounts.

Conferences & Commentaries

You will hear people talk about being a registered charity. That is in fact incorrect there is no register of charities in Ireland.
There is only a charity number which is below our signature as following: Charity number: CHY 14004
This entitles us to register to obtain relief from Revenue for donations of €250 or more.
As a Trust having an income of less than €100,000 we are not required to publish accounts, but do pass them onto Revenue with a report every year.
So to answer to those who request us whether we should publish our accounts. The answer is not according to the current law.
We are very happy to publish our accounts for the public to inspect and we would be delighted to receive a donation of any sum to assist us in our work.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/donations-fees/

* Note we are a Trust, not a company and as a result do not have directors.

We have three Trustees and The Director of Dialogue Ireland is one of these.
