Fri, Feb 2, 07 The INdependent Reformer Page 13 Background on the oil

Tony Quinn

Some interesting comments on the oil find in Belize. DI would be delighted to host any comments from Belize about Tony Quinn’s involvement there, the effects on the Mennonites etc.  Also Shareholders who do not feel they are getting through to the company are welcome to share views in confidence on this site. Certainly we know nothing about the oil business and are only interested to trace what happened to the Educo business when the downturn came.

It seems that Tony Quinn realised he was on a loser as a seminar leader and managed to make a move to cash in on the oil programme.

We have no real evidence but there are a lot of people out there who are wondering what has happened to their shares. Let us hope it is better news than the cultist tricks we have read about the Irish banks.
