The North Korean Cult: How it is so closed to view that the only means of entry is fiction.

BooksCultism

Today with Pat Kenny Wed June 6, 2012

Adam Johnston joins Pat to discuss his book Orphan Masters Son published by Transworld. However here the fiction is faction. We have used N Korea to describe the inner workings of groups as diverse as Tony Quinn, Scientology or the House of Prayer. People are transformed by group indoctrination to lose their individuality and identity. The N. Koreans are still the off shoot of a Stalinist production line.
