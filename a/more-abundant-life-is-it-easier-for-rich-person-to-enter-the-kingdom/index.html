More Abundant Life.

As a public service we are continuing to post material on the Church in Galway called Abundant Life. We are getting reports that there is confusion concerning the connection between wealth and the gospel. Prosperity as a direct result of becoming a Christian. We will just put up documents from the public domain to allow the Christian Community and the general public to form their own opinion. We will also give an opportunity for people to use our blog to leave confidential comments to help them address issues like:

  1. Does the Church practice spiritual adultery? That is does it recommend its members to pay attention to their non Christian partner or do they in fact neglect them? 1 Cor 7? Are they so involved with Church activities that they neglect the home front? We have already observed these tendencies and attitudes in Churches of the same category in Dublin, Greystones, Tuam and now in Galway for over 20 years doing these things. Why is this pattern generally found among women neglecting non Christian men. My earliest case was in Courts in the early 90’s. Why does it not happen in other Evangelical Churches?
  2. Do you feel free to discuss these issues, or do you feel controlled?

Is their a feminisation of power in the Church which leaves men feeling castrated? We invite comments as already many are in engaged on our site.

3. We would be delighted to come to Galway and take part in a public debate on these issues if a civic group or Abundant Life would like to host such an event.  A Topic like, “The Gospel good news for the poor or greater riches for the rich?” Can you serve as a pastor and at the same time be heavily involved in business? Paul did not take money from anyone as he was a tent maker by profession. However, can you get supported by home churches and take a salary as well as being in business?
