Landmark

After a significant comment we often make a post out of it so more people will see it.
Submitted on 2011/06/08 at 11:02 am

I listened to the Live-Line programme and the to the people who shared their experiences of Landmark and it struck a cord. Many did agree, as do I, that what they are trying to do has some merit and Landmark does use some effective techniques. But it is the manner in which they couduct their programmes that gives cause for concern.

We are all guilty of living in the past and indeed for some of us the past stops us from realising our full potiential in life. But the way to over come that is not to pour you heart out to a roomfull of strangers and a bunch of amateur physicologists, headed by a self styled guru who also lacks any professional qualifications of experience in this area. If you listen to the individuals who called into live line, this is what they experienced and this is what I experienced at every landmark course and event I have attended.

Yes I have attended more than one event. Like many who attend landmark courses and events, I was encouraged (other adjectives come to mind but I will stick with this one) by a close friend to attend.

I thought that the Live Line programme got it spot on. I certainly could relate to the stories which the listeners related. I know that any landmark people will dismiss these individuals and their ‘stories’ (more landmark garjon) but they did paint a fairly accurate picture of the forum.

You are locked in a room for long periods of time…FACT
Your meal breaks are controlled…FACT
The temperatue in the room is controlled throughout the sessions….FACT
Your time between sessions is filled with landmark homework….FACT
You are given the hard sell to undertake further programmes…FACT
You are put under pressure to bring family and friends to meeting with a view to signing up for a programme…FACT
You will recieve advice on dealing with complex life issues by unqualified individuals …FACT and so incredibly dangerous.

If you still have any doubts, take a moment to read the three page disclaimer which every participant must sign before undertaking a landmark programme. Very few people ever read it. If you are considering signing up for a landmark programme, I urge you to read it carefully. And do it when you have time to digest it, not at a Landmark event with someone standing over you asking for your debit card details.

The final point I wish to address is the cost, about which there is always some debate. The money paid to Landmark Education for any of the programme is not overly expensive compared with other such programmes. However the forum is a four day programme, Friday, Saturday, Sunday and Tuesday evening, which for a working professional person means at least two days off work. If you have to travel to the city hosting the course, you may need to book in to accomodation. Staying with a friend may prove difficult as you may not get home til after midnight and then you have your ‘homework’ to do. And believe me you may not be the best company after such a gruelling day. You will also have to pay for all your meals and snacks. Some programmes such as the advanced programme may only be available in London, which is where I did my advanced course. The total cost of any programme can be considerable unless you are fortunate to live in a city which is hosting a programme.

There are two other facts that I will sign off on. If you are interested in attending a programme, ask yourself why Ireland and the UK are are almost the only countries in Europe that Landmark still offer courses.

Also, check out Landmark’s links with Scientology!
