HE USED CHARITY CASH TO FUND LIFESTYLE

Volunteer Brion Ó Loinsigh expressed his contempt for the way in which Mr Meegan allegedly ‘ran’ ICROSS.

http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-1267387/Charity-boss-loses-bid-gag-media-sex-claims.html

Mr Ó Loinsigh graduated from NUI Maynooth with a psychology degree in 2004, and planned to do a PhD in clinical psychology – which required working in a clinical setting. Work experience through ICROSS fitted this requirement, so he travelled to Kenya in July 2005. The next month, he identified problems with the running of the ICROSS Bondo office, in western Kenya. Meegan instructed him to take charge. ‘I didn’t believe I was qualified to do this, but I also thought it was my chance to make a difference so I dived right in. Through the drug stock book I created I found that 20,000 tablets were unaccounted for by the end of the month. They could only have been stolen by an ICROSS member.’ Meegan instructed him to fire all staff. But Mr Ó Loinsigh discovered other problems; community-based organisations had not been receiving the drugs they should have from ICROSS.

He wasn’t even sure some of the groups existed. It also became apparent an ICROSS staffer had been charging groups for training already paid for by the charity. The psychology graduate found there was no evidence Meegan had ever obtained a PhD, as he had previously claimed – so wasn’t qualified to supervise his own studies. But it was Meegan’s personal behaviour that truly appalled Mr Ó Loinsigh. He used charity money for personal expenses and spent a long time either abroad or on the internet searching for men.  And he made unwanted sexual advances. ‘One night, Mike came in drunk and sat on the couch and we were watching a movie. At the end he asked me if I was OK in my room by myself. I said I was fine. ‘He started to massage my shoulders, and asked if I was sure, that I could always sleep in Mike’s bed with himself and Elle. I said, I was fine. He said, “We have to look after the baby now.” Mr Ó Loinsigh expressed disbelief at Meegan’s treatment of staff. ‘The way Mike speaks to the people is terrible – like an unquestionable dictator.’
