The Song of Life Seminar

All you need to live a better – or even the ultimate life – is just a small number

of actual understandings.

There are two types of understanding: Intellectual understanding and actual

understanding. Intellectual understanding is where you understand the words.

Actual understanding results in a shift in your perception of life. Consequently

you now live from that understanding. In contrast intellectual understanding

will not result in permanent change. There are certain key foundational

understandings that result in the ultimate life. One of these key

understandings could be offered to someone in the form of a sentence. On

hearing this sentence a person could actually understand it immediately, or it

could take them a day, a week, a lifetime – or even never. The sentence

could be expanded into a talk, a seminar, a book etc. The person desiring to

understand this sentence would need to meditate on it until it became their

actual understanding. Then the person lives from this new understanding.

Living from New Understandings

The aim of the Song of Life seminar is that we explore together the key areas

in all of our lives, to discover how best they should be lived. The goal is that

the person gains a new understanding that will cause a fundamental

shift/change in the way they live and as a result they become more

successful at the business of living. The truth is that most of our lives are

lived unconsciously. That is to say that at some time in our lives we came to

certain conclusions / acceptances / beliefs (often from society and others).

These are now stored in our unconscious and they are the understandings

from which we live. Unfortunately some of those understandings may not be

the best and indeed may be actively working against us. Therefore a major

part of the seminar will involve bringing those unconscious understandings

back into consciousness to see if they actually work. If they are found not to

work we discard them. In turn when we have consciously decided what does

work, we can then install these new understandings in our unconscious and

obtain a higher standard of living and self-expression.
