ALCC: The Trials of internment 3 by Kenneth Sabourin

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

There is a section on racist attitudes in the ALCC which precedes the following section. We are seeking clarification from Kenny before publishing it here……“They use scripture way out of context to support their mistreatment of people. In fact Kevin and Heather have racist attitudes in the way the church is run. The children’s ministry had about 40 kids on a Sunday. We had 35 black and 4 or 5 White Irish kids. After rehearsals on Thursday we had to prepare for Sunday’s service by praying for a multitude of things. One being “praying in the Irish”. The Sanford’s explained it’s a “spiritual disease” that we had an unbalanced ethnic group in our kid’s church. In other words there were too many black kids* in proportion to the white Irish.   Their reasoning was that they run a church in Ireland so therefore there should be a predominance of white Irish kids  in children’s church. We had to count every Sunday how many came to kids church and how many were white. Here’s the thing. A pastor doesn’t dictate who comes into the church. Jesus decides that. If we are being selective instead of being thankful we are discriminating. ALCC attracted many black people because of the Charismatic and expressive nature of its worship, something which they are attracted to. There’s nothing wrong with having a majority of people of colour at your church and to view a 7:1 ratio of black:white children in children’s church as bad is discriminative, conditional and I feel is racist! You should except and love and pray for anyone and everyone who comes through your doors. The Sanfords are very selective in who has roles, leads and even attends their church. Look at their numbers. Its now low and He as a pastor should be thankful for who God’s sends them regardless of colour. Kevin is from Texas and they have a lot of racist history. They border Mexico and Kevin made an inappropriate joke about Mexicans to me one time when we were back in the States. Yeah I m sure he didn’t mean it literally, it still shows a wrong attitude. Considering I* am supposed to pray in the “Irish” because we have a spiritual disease, leads me to feel that he has a little racism in him.”

*black kids here does not refer to Black Irish, but generally to immigrants. The term white and black Irish are now used in the Irish census.

*Kenny has Irish roots. Ed.

The story of how Kenny tried to take some time off and his sacking from the intern programme:

While I was there, there were many conferences such as their 10-year anniversary where some people were supposed to be cured by Kevin or these big shot preachers but days, weeks later they hadn’t changed at all. Kevin’s response to all these people’s illness is “they lost their blessing” and as Kevin picks up their money that they gave hoping for a miracle. I spend more time with Heather who made me feel inadequate but the thing with Kevin is that I never felt that he truly cared. Any gift had a string attached to it. Here’s a,b and c but when you have a chance do x,y, and z. A pastor is supposed to be a shepherded and be in the muck but he was never getting dirty. The Sanford’s have their private bathroom at the church. Heather never worked at the church and Kevin was in his office looking at youtube videos all day. He never stopped by to ask how I was doing or ask if I needed anything. He was cold and the only time he wanted to talk to me was if he needed something or if I was in trouble. Honestly it’s hard to tell which one is more evil because Kevin paints himself as the good guy and that he’s in control and taking care of everything. Heather is painted as a proverbs 31 wife yet both have so many abusive traits that the only way to survive at the church is to be completely obedient. They expect you to give your time and money and they treat you depending how much you contribute. They even give people the option to put their tithe or offering in an envelope so they can keep track for tax reasons. (So they can claim more money and know who’s giving really.) And then there are the financial blunders of ALCC. Every Sermon started with a tithing or giving sermon that was about 15 minutes long then his real sermon would start. Kevin and Heather always have side projects trying to raise money. Whether it be Heathers vitamin business or Kevin’s coffee business or maybe a new book the Sanford’s are always trying to gain more and more income. If you notice Kevin’s newsletter’s he’s always asking for X amount of people or churches to “partner” with him financially. Sewing seeds and reaping harvests and so on and so forth. The truth is Abundant life is in serious trouble financially. I was at ALCC when it was at its peak in numbers. Around the 10-year conference we had a strong crowd but Kevin spent thousands of dollars on the conference putting them somewhere 40-45 thousand Euros’ in debt. (or $60,000 in us dollars is what he told me). Kevin personally told me they were in significant debt. One thing you half to understand is after all of Kevin and Heather’s prophecies don’t come true, its never because of them being wrong. No, it’s because a person involved in the prophecy got out of Gods favor or sinned. Or merely it was an attack from Satan and we didn’t pray hard enough or sew enough seeds. Going back to the debt, Kevin also told some of us that there was a miscommunication with his landlord and he was 10 years behind in taxes. I don’t know the legal terms but something to do with the church building being used as a charity/church and business and not having the appropriate title or disclosure. Something along those lines that got Kevin further in debt. Yet still they make their trips to Spain every summer or so often because southerners can’t take excessive cold for long periods of time. It’s a medical condition Heather has supposedly. I just think she should pray in Jesus name that she’d be healed. That’s what they tell everybody else? Everything is about presentation to the outside. When they send newsletters out to the community with pictures of the Sanford’s, those pictures are actually airbrushed before they are sent out because, “we need to make sure our pastors look good and represent Jesus well”. That was what my supervisor that worked under Heather told me. Look at Jesus though. He was dirty, unshaven and wore shepherd’s cloths. The Sanford’s are selfish. They see themselves as a beacon of hope. The light of Ireland. The sober super hero’s. They can do no wrong and yet they have no idea why people leave. Well I left and it wasn’t a pretty story. I invited my brother who is also named Kevin to come visit me. So we will call him Chael instead for brevity reasons. Now first off we got 2 weeks off for Christmas but we agreed that I would work 1 of those weeks so in January I could have 1 week off for when my brother Chael comes to visit I could show him around and have a good time. When Chael finally arrived the church welcomed him and were very warm as they are to most people. Kevin was actually out in the States and wasn’t coming back till later in the week. I was excited not only for my brother to be there but also because I got a whole week of not working. But it was short lived because I was requested to go to an “optional Easter play audition” by Heather. I declined because I wanted to spend time with my brother and honor my Sabbath by resting. Plus it was optional, right? And by Sabbath I mean rest week because we didn’t get regular Sabbaths. We worked 7 days a week. A few phone calls later I get a voicemail from Kevin saying I either need to shape up or ship out if I am not going to be obedient. My brother who is a Christian sees me go through this and he starts seeing red flag after red flag. He immediately calls Kevin back and tells him that we need to sit down and talk about this. Next day comes and Kevin has my supervisor and the person I am staying with come with him into the office along with my brother and me so there are 5 of us total. Kevin goes on to say the reason why they are there is to have many counselors for wisdom even though they didn’t speak one word. Kevin asked me what’s up and why am I acting the way I am. I told him I just wanted to spend time with my brother on my vacation that I negotiated with you guy’s earlier last year. I was stopped there and Kevin goes on to explain all these misused biblical words out of context and double talk jargon to show that I am a defiant disobedient person. He used the verse a righteous man falls 7 times but he gets back up, “so get up Kenny.” So his ultimatum stayed they same. Shape up or ship out. When my brother Chael politely asked Kevin what I really did wrong, Kevin had no valid answer except that he was the pastor and it’s his job to keep me in his covering of protection and if I leave his covering than he can’t help me. Hmmm didn’t Jesus go after the sheep when they left their flock? From that point on we were escorted out and Kevin threatened to call the police because we just wanted to talk about this diplomatically. My brother showed Kevin’s true colors, a self-righteous Narcissist who’s power hungry and trying to build his own kingdom, not God’s. Most importantly he revealed himself as a coward who really can’t deal with authentic truth. He is prideful, racist, uses the bible out of context and treats people and God as a commodity for his convenience.

His wife Heather supports him and carry’s her own ministry of making mindless drones who work for her and her family, and she also keeps women away from healthy relationships and her views lead to separation and divorce in marriages. Abundant Life is anything  but abundant and it will suck the life out of you. Its not the Gospel, Its not Jesus and this establishment has hurt Ireland so much. When I was kicked out I got a few emails from my female co-workers and they tried to justify everything that happened by saying, “Kenny if you would of just stayed you would of gotten so many more gifts and blessings…ect.” I encourage anyone who’s felt hurt or betrayed or wonders why they can’t confront the Sanford’s, its because they have brainwashed you into being able to take beatings and come back for more at the cost of their empty promises. They speak on prosperity. Let me ask you this. Take an inventory of your life at ALCC and what have you gained besides the Sanford’s favor. Actually no, the better question is what have you lost? Have you become another isolated member of the church where all and only of your friends are ALCC people? How much money are you giving and starving you and you family financially. How much time are you giving to serve there and not serving your family or spouse. And most importantly, whom are you serving? The Sanford’s? Or are you serving the one true God Jesus Christ who loves you unconditionally.

I apologize for the fact that I wish I had more time to really articulate this story further. I am nothing but a poor college student who has a heart to serve Jesus and people in need. I am available for any further detailed questions or if you’d like to share your story personally with me that would be great too. Together I hope we can get the darkness out of Galway and bring in the real light.

Kenneth.Sabourin@gmail.com
