Hades or Heaven? Friday June 14 ~ the Four Courts

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

We are in court 3 at the Four Courts where two sons of Brendan Hade are brought in as prisoners having resisted the High Court Orders made by Justice Sean Ryan on May 31.
Members of Victory Christian Fellowship/Centre have ignored court orders and are continuing to obstruct bank-appointed receivers from taking possession of three of their properties in Dublin, the High Court has heard.

Embedded media (opens the original source)


Chartered accountants Paul McCann and Patrick Dillon were appointed as receivers over three properties of the Victory Christian Fellowship by Bank of Scotland after the church failed to satisfy a demand for €18 million in respect of unpaid loans.

The receivers claim they are being prevented from taking possession of the properties at Kilmacud House, Kilmacud Road, Upper Stillorgan, Co Dublin; Westland Row; and Firhouse Road, Tallaght.

Mr Justice Gilligan  supported the action taken by Ms Justice Mary Laffoy last week directing several church members should attend court yesterday after being informed the congregation are staging a sit-in and had been told the receiver’s actions are “not legal in the name of Jesus”. He was very simple in his support of Mr Justice Séan Ryan  granting temporary injunctions against the Trustees of the Victory Christian Fellowship requiring them to hand over possession of the premises to the receivers and restraining interference with the receivers carrying out their duties. The orders were against the Trustees, Gerry Byrne, Brendan Hade, his wife Shelia and their sons Niall and Brian Hade. This was granted on May 31.

James Doherty, for the receivers, told Mr Justice Gilligan his clients remained unable to take possession of the properties and the defendants were ignoring the orders. There was no appearance by the defendants who are Trustees, Brendan and Sheila Hade, and Gerry Byrne counsel added.

Embedded media (opens the original source)

Counsel said security staff hired by the receivers recorded one of the trustees telling a congregation at one of the properties the receivers actions were “not legal in the name of Jesus”. A rota was also organised for members to sit in at the premises, he said. Unlike last Friday he stated that the Gardai had arrested two of the Hade’s sons Niall and Brian. They were both in court

Members of the public were still attending services at the premises, which raised health and safety issues and insurance on the buildings was also in question, counsel said. The receivers were now seeking the defendants attachment and committal to prison and while such a step was “draconian”, there was no other way to get the defendants to come to court, he said. he described in great detail the attempts to deliver papers to all the those concerned. He reported that Brendan Hade had moved his letter box from the house to a an outside box. There was a total refusal to  cooperate with the court. He described how 24 hour rotas were being organised to thwart  the receivers. They were operating their own mini state with walkie talkies to resist the Gardai. This included having a blockade by up to 40 cars.

Justice Gilligan then requested Niall Hade to address the court. He claimed he had nothing to do with Victory and is not a Trustee. He had only appointed counsel and he gave 15 minutes to allow for Niall to react to to the seriousness of the situation he was in. He was able to purge his contempt and was released when clearly stated he was not involved in any actions against the receivers. He also claimed he had no idea about all these activities even though he was seen with his father, had received countless letters and he claimed being in France until Monday 10th on a family holiday. Asked whether he usually ignored court documents he was evasive.
Of course the issue is not whether he is a Trustee or not but whether he was involved in resisting the receivers. The Judge took him and his views on face value.

Then Brian was brought forward and seemed to have some kind of coaching. This correspondent was informed that some American with a ‘Freeman’ disposition was coaching him. He seemed like a rabbit in the lights and spoke in a  hackneyed manner. Brian Hade spoke about not breaking any law and being a law abiding citizen. The judge tried to make clear to him the dimensions of our democratic system and tried to give him a kind of abc to assist him to be able to see that he was flagrantly breaking the law. He did not seem to wish to be represented and so the judge gave him an extended lunch break to reach a decision as to his response.
This summary of the Freeman position gives an insight into his thinking.

http://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Freeman_on_the_land

“Freemen hold that we are all subject to a massive international legal conspiracy perpetrated for the profit of the elites, but you can hack the system if you just use the right form of words. They believe only in their version of natural law, which they call “common law.” In practical terms, they believe this means they do not have to pay taxes, debts, mortgages, etc. because we were all deceived and if you say the right form of words this fact will be accepted.

Freemen believe they can declare themselves independent of government jurisdiction using the concept of “lawful rebellion”: that all statute law is contractual and therefore only applicable if an individual consents to it. They assert that what everyone else regards as “the law” doesn’t apply to them as they have not consented to a contract with the state, even going so far as to claim they have a lawful right to refuse arrest if they do not consent.”
He did not appear to have any religious convictions and never framed his response with reference to God. It would have seemed logical to suggest he was obeying a higher law. However, in this case the mundane nature of his resistance including ‘breaking into’ his former premises after the security personnel had taken over the building seemed to suggest he was under some form of influence. It might have been Brian’s strategy to be imprisoned so he could bring a case under Article 40 of the Constitution for unlawful arrest?

The kind of religious world view shown by the resistance to lawful authority has not fortunately led to any violence, but people under religious influence will not act logically as in other scenarios.

Finally Brian Hade after a great deal of gentle explanation by Justice Gilligan agreed to to accept the decision of Justice Ryan. He was hardly enthusiastic, but in the end he in open court agreed to call off the Alsatians and obey the law.
The Gardai described how the had tried to arrest the three Trustees at close to 6 AM yesterday without success. They were the elephants in the court. Their flagrant disregard of the order of the court shows that they have now lost the run of themselves and they have convinced themselves and many of their members that they are not subject to Caesar.
However, rather than facing the lions they are facing the hubris brought about by a gospel of prosperity which is no less delusional than that of  the Babel to be built by the Baron of Ballsbridge Sean Dunne.
They have now till Friday to repent and to humble themselves before the mighty hand of God. Whether they do will be the barometer of how they start the long journey to recovery. There is a narrow road that leads to heaven and a broad road that leads to destruction.
Will they wear the mark of Christ or the Beast?
