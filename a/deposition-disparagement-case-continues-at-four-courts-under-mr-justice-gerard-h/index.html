Deposition & Disparagement case continues at Four Courts under Mr Justice Gerard Hogan

Tony Quinn

Mr Justice Gerard Hogan continued hearing submissions on Monday September 10 at 11:35 from counsel for Ms Tallant, Brian O’Moore SC who had began a pretty devastating evaluation of the concept of journalistic privilege and looking at the rules governing depositions in Denver contained in the statement by Jim O’Callaghan last Friday.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/07/report-of-the-high-court-case-in-regard-international-natural-energy-ine-sept-7th-2012/

He addressed in the fullest way possible the issue at the heart of this case namely, “disparagement.” Quinn he acknowledged was not a Director of the INE, but at least a member. He showed that this case was not defamatory but about breach of contract.

Mr Justice Gerard Hogan noted how Quinn, “was a slightly controversial figure,” in comments which showed a lot of similarities to those uttered by Justice Bannister about Quinn. He seemed to pop up at every corner did this Quinn. Brian O’Moore SC did not accept that this was an accident. He believed the attempt to depose Ms Tallant was in reality an attempt to use Denver law to get information in regard to two defamation cases Quinn has taken against the Sunday World. Under the defamation act they have a certain time limit to bring cases to court, yet Quinn continually threatens but does not carry through. Interesting enough Justice Bannister could not believe his ears when Quinn told him he used the law in this manner. We hope to have some transcripts of that trial soon.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/30/judge-bannisters-judgement-read-it-for-yourself/

Mention was made of Paul Tweed who seems to have a role in another case where Maire Lalor is taking a case against Quinn in this jurisdiction for sexual assault, battery and fraudulent misrepresentation.

http://www.independent.ie/entertainment/books/review-the-law-privacy-and-libel-law-by-paul-tweed-3073753.html

http://www.thedetail.tv/issues/6/brian-carron/naming-suspects-trial-by-media-or-the-right-to-a-free-press

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/01/10/fitness-expert-quinn-accused-of-sex-assault/

Quinn has never in the 41 years of his activities ever been held to account and as Justice Bannister has suggested it is clear that oil company funds have been used to fund a lot of legal activity. Hence the B Shareholders have never received a dividend.

Brian O’Moore SC unpacked the expert testimony on Denver law and showed the expert witness had really little background in the field related to this case. He concluded his submission around 15.45.

Counsel for Mike Garde, The Director of the Dialogue Ireland Trust, Séamus Ó Tuathail SC first outlined Mike Garde’s credentials:

Mike Garde MA

Séamus Ó Tuathail SC then raised the issue as whether hacking was at the heart of the emails which had come forward through discovery?  Justice Gerard Hogan immediately sought clarification of this issue and there was some confusion between hacking and DOS attacks which two servers used by DI were the subject of a DOS attack, but the hacked emails were the result of someone going near Sheila Caffrey’s hotel room and hacking into her computer around the same time in early January 2009. Séamus Ó Tuathail SC then withdrew this claim and as noted in an earlier post is seeking to introduce the remaining emails in a correspondence of the Director with Katrina Skinner attorney for Jean Cornec.* He gave great prominence to the judgement of Justice Bannister which is part of the court system of the Eastern Caribbean which shares a lot in common with this jurisdiction. The commercial court sits on the BVI, but the company is registered in Nevis.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/10/publication-of-emails-between-katrina-skinner-attorney-to-jean-cornec-and-mike-garde-director-of-di/

Looking at the affidavit it is indeed appropriate to ask why is there a series of emails which includes a document which has the personal testimony of a woman who alleges she was the victim of sexual assault by Quinn?  This in the affidavit presented on behalf of Morrice+

by Jim O’Callaghan. Why did they release this document? Mr Justice Gerard Hogan again realised after thirty minutes that more time would be needed so we will reassemble at 10:30 AM on Tuesday 11, September.

He hopes to have a decision in the case by the same day next week the 18th September. He might have to revise this, but he is aware this case may be taken to the Supreme Court by one of the parties? He will then reserve judgement till October.
