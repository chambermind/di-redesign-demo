Bernie Purcell has walked away from Ghost nursing home

Therapy Groups

It looks like Bernie Purcell has walked away from this nursing home. Local newspapers have been unable to contact her. There seems to be very little activity at their premises on Rathgar Road. The gates have been shut recently.

Ghost nursing home

Thursday, April 15, 2010

The Enniscorthy and District Echo

http://www.enniscorthyecho.ie/news/story/?trs=mhojqlmhid&cat=news

ALL PATIENTS at Carrig Túr nursing home have now left the building, with only a skeleton staff remaining. The private nursing home’s 51 staff still have not been notified as to whether their jobs have been terminated, even though it is now two months since they have received a pay cheque. A meeting took place between staff representatives and their solicitor John Garahy and management on Thursday.

The owner of the nursing home was not present.

The staff members sought payment of wages and some inkling about their jobs, but no information was forthcoming from the matron, who has not heard details from the owner Bernie Purcell. All of the nursing home’s 37 patients have been moved on to other nursing homes, the last one leaving mid-last week. More than fifty staff, full time and part-time, are on the books of the nursing home in The Shannon area of Enniscorthy, in what was formerly the House of Missions. Many staff have stopped turning up for work as they feel they have been left “in limbo” by management.
One employee told The Echo that staff are angry with management as they have not been notified about whether their jobs are gone or not. No P45s have been sent out so staff cannot claim money from the labour exchange.
