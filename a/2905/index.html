Dialogue Ireland’s Islamophobic Whiff

Posted on الثلاثاء 02 ربيع الثاني 1431 by Admin

Dialogue Ireland’s website promotes itself as ‘an independent trust promoting awareness and understanding new religious movements and cultism at the service of the churches, religions and Irish society’, however a cursory glance at its front page reveals an inordinate attention to and what many perceive as an attack on Islam.

They repeat what others have glibly said, that ‘the cure to radical Islam is moderate Islam’, without explicitly revealing what ‘moderate Islam’ is, and at the same time passionately denouncing everything they consider it not to be. What we have in effect is a Christian organization trying to tell Irish Muslims what kind of Islam they consider acceptable in Ireland, regardless of whether it is correct or not.

And rather like the Centre for Social Cohesion in the UK, headed by avowed Islamophobe, Douglas Murray, one must wonder what purpose such an organisation serves in developing ‘understanding’.

If understanding is really sought, then discussion and dialogue are paramount, not denouncements based on ignorance, scaremongering and sensationalism. That’s the challenge!

DI responds: We hope our Islamist consultant will provide a more in depth evaluation of the MPAC and its Wahhabist roots.  We are delighted that Liam Egan has finally surfaced. We are not a Christian Trust but a group whose concern is cultism. Here we see good examples of incitement to racial hatred, sectarianism in regard to Shias as represented in the comments below, and pure anti- Semitism. We look forward to analysing the links to Saudi Arabia, and look forward to Egan sending us his biography with its history of his work as a Christian minister and his conversion to Islam.

We wish him well in regard to Islam, it is not our concern but he is obviously an Islamist as a cursory reading of his site reveals.
