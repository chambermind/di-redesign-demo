Health guru ‘did not back up’ weight-loss claims

Tony Quinn

Health guru ‘did not back up’ weight-loss claims

By Juno McEnroe Irish Examiner

http://www.irishexaminer.com/ireland/health-guru-did-not-back-up-weight-loss-claims-118578.html

Friday, April 30, 2010

CLAIMS that products sold by health guru Tony Quinn could help achieve weight loss and could treat serious ailments have been criticised by the Advertising Standards Authority of Ireland. The regulatory body of the advertising industry upheld a complaint against the fitness therapist because he had not backed claims over a slimming product with substantial evidence.  The use of testimonials in selling a “weight loss pack” including supplements was not sufficient alone, it was found.  The authority was also critical of advertising that said supplements were available for arthritis, eczema and other health problems.  It noted that marketing should not offer any product or treatment for serious or prolonged ailments or for conditions requiring the attention of a registered medical or other qualified practitioner.  Tony Quinn’s representatives responded that testimonials featured on the slimming product were “genuine”. A separate statement on a product “did not refer to them offering products to prevent, cure or treat such conditions”, they added.  Marketing for the stimulant drink Red Bull also contravened advertising rules. The final scene, where a young boy was in a gentlemen’s club, was found to be to be in conflict with the spirit of the code.
