Japa: “Members Past and Present, Please Read”

CultismHinduJapa Yoga

A Dialogue Ireland contributor sent us the following, addressing all members. past and present, of Japa Ireland.

For those of us who have seen the truth of their actions, we want to pretend it never happened and move on with our lives. Unfortunately, they are still operating, others are still at risk, and you could have the potential to stop someone else from being abused.

Those who left, usually left suddenly in shock and confusion, and carried the opinion that they were an isolated incident and had to make sense of it. We who suffered, couldn’t do enough to stop them. Those who spoke out, were not heard. The traumatised didn’t know what to do, were not supported, and had to pull their lives back together.

Cult?

During my time in this group, I found the thought of it being a cult, laughable. How could so many amazing people be a cult? Good-intentioned people from all backgrounds and areas. They were vulnerable, brave, and happy to support each other. They were willing to face their fears, share their experiences, work on themselves, and help others along the way. The idea of it being a cult was impossible to me. The problem was not with the members, but with the ones at the top who were manipulating everyone under them. They had control of us all because we chose to trust them, because we were looking for answers in our own lives.

The members made the experience beautiful. It was truly heart-warming to see so many people support each other. Unfortunately, the actions of a few sullied the great work of many. The cult was not the members, it was the leaders who took control and manipulated for their personal gain.

They kept us ridiculously busy with meditation, disciplines, study, and projects so we didn’t have time to understand what was happening. People vanished and we didn’t ask questions.

The truth

The ones at the top were constantly manipulating for their benefit. They were several steps ahead of us, always. If they realised you knew too much, they began to discredit you by targeting you in groups with others watching so you would feel devalued. When they realised they were depleting your resources (finances, time, energy, etc), they took measures to distance you from the group and cut off contact. They might ask the impossible of you and cause unnecessary stress and peer pressure until you break away. Or they would do terrible things to you, and make sure you’re surrounded by people that would not believe you, or would lead you into the next trap. Cruel mind games without humanity. And they had their members taking part and playing roles.

The pantomime

They hid behind spirituality, ancient wisdom, and various cultural traditions to make it look like they were highly evolved and trying to help you. They did help some people, but usually because they knew they would get back their investment, or so they could tell everyone what good Samaritans they are. What I noticed over time was that their help had a high price that was never discussed.

They had us share our secrets, our trauma, and our shame. If something positive happened in our lives, they took credit. If something negative happened, it was our fault for not following their instructions. They programmed us, and we let them.

We were being brainwashed, some more than others. The ones that were not completely committed to them, still desperately wanted to believe them, because they had become dependent on the support of their group.

Their game

These people are dangerous. I had no idea how dangerous and that more than my life was at risk. I was willingly blind because I thought they were supporting me and trying to make my life better. By the time I realised, it was far too late. What they did to me was horrendous. What they did to some others was much worse. Years later I’m still hearing from former members about their experiences of being misled, and incidents of sexual assault.

At first, I thought it was only Dennis Curran and Shashi Dubey who were manipulating and abusing. After hearing from other ex-members over the past few years, I’ve realised other senior members were aware. They knew about the abuse and allowed it to continue. They made the choice to take no action and allow vulnerable people to be manipulated and used. They had a support network of people whose focus is to brainwash and take from you. It might look like they are helping you, but they are only helping themselves in the long term.

A message for anyone still with Japa Ireland

Please watch the behaviours of those around you. If you are travelling to India this April on the next organised trip, please do not let women be separated or put in vulnerable positions. They should not be left alone as they may be targeted. It is not safe, and you may be able to prevent abuse. Do not let them change your idea of what is acceptable behaviour.

Physical abuse is only one part of what they do. The emotional and psychological games leave scars we cannot heal. Do not be afraid to say no.

Please ask yourself why others disappeared. What were you promised for your obedience? What were you told would happen for you and has it happened? Did others get what they were promised, or did they just disappear also?

If you have any doubts, please take a break from the group, and from contact with other members for 6 months. Let your mind settle so you can see clearly without someone else clouding your judgement. The truth is always in actions, not words. If they are not controlling you, they will not be concerned about you experiencing some distance.

Cult means control. A cult is when others change your thinking, so you’ll do what they want. Don’t be left like so many of us, isolated and broken. It wasn’t just our lives they destroyed, but also the lives of those around us.

Left with the guilt

I was in this group for years. I swore by it. I told others it was wonderful and tried to get them involved. I became part of the problem. I feel guilty for telling others that it was a safe place. My intentions were genuine and based on my experience at that time. Unfortunately, the reality is that I promoted a group that destroys lives. I carry the guilt from this. I carry the knowledge that my words may have led someone into the group, and they may have been abused. This contributed to my silence for a long time. But silence will not prevent others from being abused.

These people are dangerous. Do not engage with them as you will not win. If you are a victim of this group, please report the crime to the authorities. If you just need to talk to someone first and need an impartial ear, Dialogue Ireland is a registered charity and Independent Trust. They specialise in helping those affected by these groups. They will listen. They will not judge. They will give you options. You are not alone, so don’t let anyone convince you that you are.
