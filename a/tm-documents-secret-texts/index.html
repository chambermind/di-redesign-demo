TM-documents – Secret Texts

BooksTranscendental Meditation

TM-documents – Secret Texts, Aarhus 1977. English.

Introduced by Vagn Folkerman

The Dialogue Centre in Aarhus now publishes this small selection of texts from Transcendental Meditation showing the Hindu background of TM.

Transcendental Meditation presents its system of meditation as a purely neutral technique combined with the Science of Creative Intelligence (S.C.I.). It is officially denied to be a sort of mystery religion with Hindu background.

The purpose of this booklet is to reveal the esoteric core of the Hindu mythology and philosophy that is found under the surface of TM. By means of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi’s own teaching this selection of documents reveals what is the real context, meaning and goal of Transcendental Meditation.

The booklet brings The Ritual in Sanscrit, a transcription, and two different TM-translations of the ritual. In addition you can read “The Holy Tradition”, the understanding of God and Guru Dev, the understanding of Mantra, and the understanding of suffering by Maharishi Mahesh Yogi.

The “TM-Documents” offers in this way a unique presentation of secret TM-texts, not available to the public before.

Please note this review was published in 1977, and the booklet is no longer available for sale.
