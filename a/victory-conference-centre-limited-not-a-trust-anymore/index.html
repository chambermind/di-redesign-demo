Victory Conference Centre Limited, not a Trust anymore

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

Victory Conference Centre Limited

Thomas Cooney is the pastor of the Galway branch of VCF, Victory Galway.
Victory Galway, Liosban Business Park, Tuam Road, Galway, Phone: 091 759840, Email: office@victorygalway.ie

http://www.victorygalway.ie 

This looks like an attempt by Hade to register a new entity with a clean credit history, possibly in an attempt to start up a new church once VCF has been wound down by the receivers. This is exactly what happened in Galway when abundant life Christian centre ltd was wound down by the receivers and Brendan Hade took the church and moved it to a new premises under the Victory Galway entity with a debt free status while the Abundant Life debtors were left to burn. An interesting company has appeared in the CRO register. Is it a restart or failed attempt to hold on to the buildings? The documents were filed on the 30/05/13 with a Thomas O’Leary and Thomas Cooney as directors.

Submitted on 2013/06/21 at 11:32 pm

‘Banks call in €20 million debt after Revenue axes cult church’s charitable status’ http://www.sundayworld.com/top-stories/news/no-escape-to-victory

“And now Revenue will also be demanding hundreds of thousands of euro in unpaid taxes after stripping away Victory’s charity status.

The taxman will demand the return of nearly €1 million it handed the ‘charity’ in tax refunds. For every taxpayer who donates to the church, Victory was able to claim back tax on their PAYE/PRSI.

Victory was getting €150,000 to €250,000 in refunds from Revenue every year. In 2010 the figure was €208,914 while in 2009 it was €220,574.”

One can only assume it is backdated, as according to Gerry in the Indo interview Revenue still had not paid them 2010s tax and Jim Gallagher’s article says Revenue is looking for almost 1M back, 4 to 5 years worth.

From: http://www.revenue.ie/en/business/faqs-charities.html
“Can charitable tax exemption be withdrawn?

Yes, tax exemption may be withdrawn (retrospectively if necessary) if the body does not comply with its charitable objects or to the conditions associated with the charitable tax exemption. In such circumstances a charge to tax as determined by the Revenue Commissioners may be raised.”

The whole thing is a sorry mess for those who still attend. I believe many sponsored a seat for €750 and some paid for more than one. I don’t think they will get far with the Receivers looking for them back.

I attended on an off from 02 to around 06 and over those years their preaching changed from working hard, investing your earning and giving, to more and more giving. I used to give my wife a nudge when it was time to tithe, telling her it was pastor Brendan’s favourite part of the service. She was not amused. When they started flashing a tithing mantra up on the screens and getting everyone to repeat it, it was time to go.
