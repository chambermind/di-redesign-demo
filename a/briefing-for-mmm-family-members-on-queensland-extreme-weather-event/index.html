Briefing for MMM family members on Queensland extreme weather event.

ChristianMagnificat Meal Movement

At the best of times it is difficult to get through to members of the MMM so there must be a lot of concern about loved ones. This report was not very hopeful. I sent out an email to Judi & Phil Bloomfield who I stayed with in Helidon in 2003 to get a report. Any of you locally who have updates do leave a comment to that effect on the blog.

Over the years all I heard was that there was no rain for years, and that it was difficult to feed the cattle, and maintain agriculture.

The MMM building is on Mary’s Mount. As the name suggests it is high up but there are sections which are in the valley.
