To provide advice and information on cultism to those affected and to the media.

Tony Quinn

Our primary mission to provide advice and information on cultism to those affected…

We are an archive on cults with our original web site
Web site http://www.dialogueireland.org

and our blog

Blog site  http://www.dialogueireland.wordpress.com

It would be impossible for us to assist people without having the archive we do. At the time when an article is published people affected would not dream of looking at items about a group which has no relevance to them. However, when they are affected by involvement in a group suddenly articles in our archive are very relevant. We are in fact about protecting vulnerable adults, and only in a derivative way about protecting sources for articles. http://www.nhssb.n-i.nhs.uk/publications/social_services/Safeguarding_Vulnerable_Adults.pdf

Obviously the average reader of a newspaper sees TQ as just another news item they consider on the day. Generally they read it as an item of news and move on. It then disappears and goes down the tube. However, on our site it remains as a part of the evidence which would otherwise vanish. With certain newspapers like the Sunday World it would disappear completely if we did not hold it as they have no web site, so unless we have a relationship with the journalist it is lost by Monday. Generally with folks from Newspapers behind a pay wall we can get their text but to get a pdf is nearly impossible. In working with them they support us where they can but they find it increasingly difficult to get us copy or a pdf. So often we have to scan the paper. Some understand our educational and charitable remit but others actually have no idea how the internet works and think we are a rival media outlet. No we are an educational and therapeutic charity and the more hits we get the more work we get and in fact the fewer funds we receive. The only funds we receive are donations. This unlike a commercial business where the more hits you receive the more income that flows in. We offer advice and information to the public and assist the media in regard to the issue of cultism.

To provide advice and information on cultism to the media.

We have briefed nearly every journalist who has done a cult story. We often brief them for hours and provide them stories and people to interview.  https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/about/
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/services-we-offer/
The people who look at our blog do so not because they are coming to find the latest news on TQ but to assist them in their recovery. So the media need to see us as an ally and as the experts to facilitate their stories.  We are often misunderstood as being in competition with them. We have the feeling of being drained of our energy as we are used for a story, but the expertise and the human rights work of Dialogue has only been the story itself twice in the last twenty years.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/02/the-cult-buster-by-by-nicola-tallant-november-8-2009-sunday-world/

We often find the IT Department of the major papers have been very helpful in assisting us with old articles no longer accessible as they understand our need to archive material and our educational mission.

To show how this archive works look at this link. We have articles going back to the 70’s on Quinn, which is a great research tool for journalists and for victims.
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/02/16/summary-of-media-coverage/
https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/01/18/the-tony-quinn-story-%E2%80%9Cfrom-butcher-to-oil-slick%E2%80%9D/

Here are some of the ways this works:

The process by which we publish and circulate information, as well as the reasons why, are fairly easy to understand. We are often approached by people who have concerns, whether they be current members of a group, ex-members of a group or family members of those involved in a given group. I will grant that trying to balance this information is always a difficult task, but in many instances we find the information provided to us to be credible.
Let me illustrate what I mean by this by giving two examples. Many ex-members of the Scientology organisation have described the same patterns of manipulation and financial exploitation from the group. Some members have also provided documentary evidence for these claims. Publishing this information, and referencing the relevant policies of the organisation where relevant, seems highly warranted.
A second example concerns the House of Prayer organisation on Achill Island. Evidence such as cheque copies and deeds for various mansions seem to indicate something is very wrong with how the operation operates with regard to funds. This type of evidence, combined with both testimony and undercover footage of House of Prayer sermons, raises serious questions over their fund-raising activities.
Both of those examples show that the combination of testimony from ex-members and other documentary evidence that underpin any claims we make about these groups. We also try to archive any media articles that discuss these issues.
