Policy, policy, policy

Scientology

At present there is a court case occurring in Florida between the Estate of Kyle Brennan and the Church of Scientology (and some of its members in particular). The estate is alleging that “the death of Kyle Brennan was due to Defendants intentionally imposing Scientology policies upon a mentally disabled adult.”

You can read the defendants’ arguments here (1.2 Mb PDF).

You can read the plaintiff’s arguments here (80Kb PDF).

This article is not about the case itself, but rather about a declaration filed by Lance Marcor, an ex-member of Scientology’s Sea Organisation, as part of this case that gives us a look into the world of Scientology policy.

His declaration can be found here (4Mb PDF).

What is most interesting here is not the description he gives, but it is the way he references the relevant policy letters that give rise to that description.
