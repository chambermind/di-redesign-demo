Foreign Evidence Tribunal Act 1856 application

Yours faithfully,

Johnsons

Dialogue Ireland got confirmation from within the Quinn camp days after the Case of disparagement between Susan Morrice and Jean Cornec had been settled.

Also we received confirmation from those that DI had formerly helped that they had reached a settlement which confirmed our view that the game changer was in fact the judgement of Justice Hogan at the end of September last.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/21/final-version-cornec-v-morrice-ors-judgment-by-hogan-j/

This aspect of the Judgement was the final nail in the coffin for Quinn and Morrice.

 Since Mr. Garde’s activities fall squarely within the education of public opinion, there is a high constitutional value in ensuring that his right to voice these views in relation to the actions of religious cults is protected. It does not require much imagination to accept that critical information in relation to the actions of those bodies would dry up if Mr. Garde could be compelled to reveal this information, whether in the course of litigation or otherwise. It is obvious from the very text of Article 40.6.1 that the right to educate (and influence) public opinion is at the very heart of the rightful liberty of expression. That rightful liberty would be compromised – perhaps even completely jeopardised – if disclosure of sources and discussions with sources could readily be compelled through litigation.

However, Morrice decided to play the long game and her solicitors in Ireland gave the impression that the Supreme Court Appeal was still proceeding even though the case was moot! So they only confirmed that the case was over in the following words:

We refer to your recent correspondence in relation to the above matter. We confirm the proceedings in the US have been concluded.

The following section ended the statement:

“We are taking our Clients instructions in relation to proceeding with the Appeal on the costs issue.”

Already at the end of October 2012 we had heard that they intended to do this, but by refusing to answer the simple question is the case settled put to Johnsons by our solicitor, they have prolonged the agony by six months and tried to divert from the failure of Morrice to put up the white flag. She is now in danger of not only getting a further judgement for costs for time wasting but also the additional costs for the Supreme Court appeal on costs.

I got through to the District Court in Denver and they have acknowledged that the case was dismissed on November 30, by Justice Bronfin. We would be grateful to anyone in the US who could send us a copy of this dismissal for the record. We do not have locus standi to approach the Court for a copy.

We are relieved at the withdrawal of the case in question which was based largely on Quinn’s and Morrice’s charges of a mysterious alleged conspiracy, which they absolutely failed to substantiate.  This has to do with the misuse of legal processes, and the finances spent on this misuse of law, for the harassment of individuals, including DI people, who are deemed by Quinn and Co to be a threat to them.

The relief of not having to be involved with legal harassment which has gone for years is palpable.

The climate for all these changes was set by the Judgement of Justice Bannister last year who concluded there was a dirty tricks campaign against a number of named people including DI. His judgement really helped those of us without a clue about the oil business to see how Shareholders had been disrespected and denied access to their dividends. Also it was clear that Quinn had got into a position of power by undue influence.

All these cases can be looked at by going to the section of our blog dealing with Tony Quinn.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/tony-quinn/

However, we reference Justice Bannister’s ruling as it will be the gold standard for resolving the issues the B Shareholders concerns.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/30/judge-bannisters-judgement-read-it-for-yourself/

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

From earlier in the year you will note that that we had very strange events around a group of people seeking to assist the B shareholders.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/02/09/the-mysterious-saviour-of-the-b-shareholders/

We have noted a general silence around that issue since we could not substantiate the bone fides of the parties concerned. We did note that those who were concerned with Quinn and his activities became demented in the pursuit of their cash and broke off contact with DI to pursue that end. Also those we advised who had sought our assistance have now taken off into the sunset and obviously see their settlement as the end of the story and do not see the need to assist other victims.

A further isue that needs to be addressed is that it marks a different stage in the life of the Quinn cult (and DI’s continuing reporting on it), a shift in emphasis, as the main focus of attention for many visitors to the DI website in the last 5 years revolved around court cases involving oil money or personal injury claims of sexual and financial exploitation taken against Quinn, the mucky ‘messiah’ as he became known.  The cases are now settled complete with payouts and gagging orders as Quinn has been scurrying for cover frantically since the exposure and personal thrashings he’s received in the courts.  He’s now back to cult business-as-usual, hiding in the Bahamas, behind layers of devotees, where he feels much safer and less exposed to the light.  The legal cases were never at the centre of DI’s concerns and reporting on Quinn and his cultist group. There were many aggressive attempts to drag DI into these legal battles with which DI had no substantive connection.  The legal cases, however, did serve to highlight some of the typical attitudes and behaviours of a cultist leader and his subservient followers and for this reason and DI’s duty of care to the public it drew attention to the cult issues which were very much in evidence but were poorly referenced, if at all, by many of the Irish media.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/23/the-stunning-silence-of-the-irish-media-in-regard-to-the-landmark-hogan-judgement/

Now it is difficult to see how this will play itself out. Currently the media is not yet ready to deal with the cult story and has found it difficult to address Quinn Cult-Oil issue unlike their dealings with the complexities of the Sean Quinn, Anglo Irish Bank and other cultist issues.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/01/18/the-tony-quinn-story-%E2%80%9Cfrom-butcher-to-oil-slick%E2%80%9D/

Books by leading journalists have been written by our leading journalists, but when Susan Morrice sends them press releases and good news stories while oppressing her shareholders they publish them without reservation.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/02/08/news-release-in-regard-to-the-ine-b-shareholders/

The political class is at a similar game as we noted at the New Year when even our Taoiseach feels no need to explain his connection to Quinn with a photo of himself with Susan Morrice with the Children’s book lionising TONY.

We gave his press office time to give an answer, but none was forthcoming.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/27/cult-book-the-magic-in-you-makes-tony-quinn-into-merlin-the-magician-who-found-the-oil/

Connecting to Susan Morrice brings the Office of the Taoiseach into disrepute. We will be writing further about this once the high of St Patrick’s Day is past.
