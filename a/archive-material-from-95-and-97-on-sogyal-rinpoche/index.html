Archive material from ’95 and ’97 on Sogyal Rinpoche

RIGPA

The Irish Times, Southern Correspondent, Dick Hogan covered the abuse charges involving Sogyal Rinpoche in the paper on Feb. 23, 1995.

When the then Field Worker attended a Conference in London on May 3, 1997 at the LSE on NRMs and Violence, a woman who had been an assistant to Sogyal told of her alleged experiences of violent abuse. She explained that often the abuse took place in different locations. I returned home to Ireland and tried to engage the Sunday Tribune about this. The woman who was named on the Conference Programme reversed her decision to give an interview. We have left her name out now in the PDF we have on this site at her request.

A few months ago I was in touch with this woman again and it looked like she would tell her story to Richard Oakley for the Sunday Times article.

She decided against doing this and is now considering writing her own book about it. She also hopes to contact the Mivelude in the Office of the PM in France which addresses cultist issues.

I had done a series on the various NRMs with Ailin Quinlan then a feature writer with Cork Examiner, and thought she should be advised about what I had heard in London. See the fax to her dug up from my files.

By pure coincidence the day after the Sunday Times article on July 5,  Ailin Quinlan had an article in Irish Independent about retreat centres which featured Dzogchen Beara Tibetan Buddhist Retreat Centre at Allihies in West Cork.

http://www.independent.ie/health/case-studies/real-life-retreat-into-relaxation-1807335.html

In light of the conference theme of violence it was not surprising that we reported  we were concerned about the death of a man who died from a terrible accident back in 1998. The first reports suggested suspicious circumstances. However, with hindsight we are all now aware that nothing untoward had happened. But back then due to what had been heard in London we felt we had the duty of care to the public to make them aware of possible dangers. That was a snap shot in time, and has no bearing on the current issues which are resurfacing.
