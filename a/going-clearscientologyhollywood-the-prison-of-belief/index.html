Going Clear:Scientology,Hollywood & the Prison of Belief

Scientology

Going Clear: Scientology, Hollywood & the Prison of Belief by Lawrence Wright.

The review by Tony Allen-Mills raises the the important issue of libel. The fact is like a lot of  books it had to be published in the USA. Our special friend Cruise has the lawyers crawling all over the book. This review appeared in the Culture Section of the Irish Edition of the Sunday Times on January 27, 2013. In other words along with other authors Wright has written an objective account but Scientology really does not like free speech and again has managed to silence Britain again. One wonders whether Ireland’s revised Defamation Act 2009 would fare any better?
