The travails of a true Catholic in Helidon. A story of survival and healing and exit from the MMM.

Magnificat Meal Movement

I would like to begin with the video on the MMM dated in 1993.  Debra is telling listeners that God has given her a mission of starting prayer groups of adoration of the Blessed Sacrament.  She talks about a vision of the Eucharist rotating over the top of Australia.  She says Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament is really, truly and completely present.  She sees a vision of Mary profoundly adoring Christ in the Blessed Sacrament.  She talks about giving her “yes” to Jesus for spreading devotion of the Blessed Sacrament and how sweet it is to magnify him in the Blessed Sacrament, humbled in the form of bread on the altar.  She says He is asking us to adore him in His Eucharistic presence hidden from the world.  Claire Birchley says Jesus longs for us to be with him, and love him in communal worship in Mass.  She goes on to say that the Eucharistic is in the living heart of each church.  (This is very Catholic)

So what happened?  Debra has now taken herself fully away from the Catholic Church and has started her own religion.  The first step was to deny that Christ was present in the Eucharist in a Catholic Church consecrated by a Catholic priest.

My life living for five months with Debra in Helidon, Australia is only a few threads weaved into the tapestry and ongoing saga of the MMM.  Debra is not her real name.  She says Jesus gave her that name.    The Bible says you will know a person like Debra by their fruits.  Former MMM members who were Catholic or just believed in God and have lost either faith in God or in the Catholic Church are credible witnesses.   Debra is an actress who loves playing the role of a prophet.  Debra’s personality is one that feeds on the good opinion of her followers.

Referring to Dialogue Ireland’s video on the MMM, certain comments stand out in my mind as truth:

1)         Fr. Ryan lived in the backyard of Debra’s community residence in Helidon.  He said he counselled some former MMM members and witnessed crippled personalities, that there was a certain vagueness to them and their thinking process was disabled.

2)         Another lady who was interviewed said that anyone who opposes or questions Debra is accused of being possessed by demons,  (or mad)

3)         Wal Maggs testifies to the fact that it took him 12 months to recover after being separated from Debra’s community.  He says some people never recover.

4)         Another lady who was interviewed says she heard Debra say that she loves publicity bad or good as she knows how to work the media.

In the 1993 video of the MMM there is an insert which was done many years later than the video.  Debra is asking people for money and telling people they don’t have to be Catholic to be a part of the MMM and that the Catholic Church is a mess and out of control.   (Debra in black, cutting red roses.  Obviously, she has abandoned the Catholic Church at this point.)   In the interview with Gordon, her husband for 25 years, he confirms she has started her own religion.  This process takes time.   It starts with one lie, then another lie to cover up that lie and then more lies to cover up subsequent lies and so on.  As Michele Stewart testifies, Debra herself confessed to living in a puddle of deception.

My encounter with Debra is as follows:

In Los Angeles Claire Mansour introduced Debra to a Catholic faith community.  She testified to a couple of events.  First, that Debra had stayed with her for a long enough period of time that she could witness to the fact that Debra was not eating, and was indeed living on the Eucharist.  Then, there was an occurrence that one day (in this time period she stayed with Claire) Debra became ill.   Claire then took Debra in her Mercedes to a local church, “Our Lady of Lebanon” and when they arrived at the Church, Claire went to the rectory to ask the priest to come and deliver the Eucharist to Debra who was sick in the car because she was kept alive with the Eucharist.  The priest told Claire that she needed to bring Debra into the Church and then he would give her the Eucharist.  From Claire’s description, it sounded as if Debra might die.   However, they did manage to get Debra into the Church and after she received the Eucharist, she miraculously recovered (supposedly as reported by Claire).

There was another story from Claire that testified to Debra’s genuineness which took place at the L.A. International airport, but I don’t remember the details.

Another time when Debra visited Claire’s house and I was present, I remember Debra was happily and contently sitting on a couch with her feet up.   However, I was told that her feet were up on the couch because she was experiencing the wounds of Christ.

Claire scheduled quite a few talks for Debra.  I believe I attended around four and I was present twice at Claire’s house when Debra was visiting.   One talk was given at a Dominican cloistered order of nun’s convent that had 24 hour Eucharistic adoration.  There was a wall that separated the nuns.  On our side it was like a church and on the other side were the nuns.  This was my favorite talk given by Debra.  Claire tried to schedule another talk at their convent, but the nuns never allowed her to speak there again.

I believe the first time I saw Debra was the day that she talked about blessings while eating on her birthday.  .  When she only supposedly lived on the Eucharist.

What drew me to Debra was when she started talking about the property she had bought in Helidon, where she had a community of MMM members all living together and praying together.  I believed she was Catholic at the time and that she would stay Catholic.  Like the 1993 video on the MMM, Debra presented herself as totally Catholic.  At all Debra’s talk she told us that Jesus had been asking her to spread one hour of Eucharistic adoration a week.  I thought to myself that we did not need to join the MMM to adore Christ in the Blessed Sacrament, but I liked her message.  I had not thought much about it before, and ever since that time, I have been devoted to Eucharistic Adoration.  I had always wanted to live in a faith community, similar to a nun’s life and that was what mostly drew me to Helidon.

Ann Cooper from our faith community scheduled a pilgrimage to Australia.  Claire Mansour and I went on that pilgrimage also.  While staying there I ran after Debra one day to catch up with her to ask her if I could stay.  She agreed to let me stay.  Towards the end of my stay in Helidon, Debra told me that I was Jesus’ choice to live in Helidon, but I was not a person she would choose.  I never felt comfortable around Debra my whole stay in Helidon.  I could never figure out what was going on, but looking back, I believe that it’s possible my guardian angel was protecting me.

In the beginning, Debra said that she was assigning Judy to me as sort of a mentor.  I liked Judy very much, but the sequence of events, when Debra seemed to get upset with me, always kept everyone at a distance

During my first few days there I was not positive if I was in the right place.  I took a bus into Toowoomba one day to visit a MMM member who had a house.   I liked her very much and wanted to talk to her about the MMM.  I didn’t come away with any answer.  The lady was very friendly and gracious and gave me a ride back to the convent.  When I got back to the convent, Debra got angry at me and said that I was not allowed to leave the convent without her permission.  She accused me of greatly inconveniencing the lady.  She went and talked to the lady and the lady was never friendly to me ever again.

I remember someone had taken a picture that looked like the Eucharist was hovering over the convent.  We were told that we had protection at the convent but when we left that protection also left.

Our daily schedule was to wake at 5:00 a.m., pray the Stations of the Cross around the convent where they were strategically placed, afterwards we were to go to St. Joseph’s Church for daily Mass, one hour of adoration, group prayers and we had weekly confession.  This all suited me very well.  After at least three months of this routine I was beginning to feel very blessed.  Wherever grace abounds, the devil works hard at destroying and he accomplished the task to which he set out.

We also had cleaning/working duties in the convent.

One morning I was having a difficult time breathing so I stayed in bed.  It was a cleaning day.  When one has asthma the more you exert yourself, the harder it is to breath.  Well, to my surprise, all of a sudden I heard Debra coming down the hall screaming on top of her lungs (I had a room upstairs next to Christine).  Everyone must have heard her and they all must of have been stunned themselves.  Debra came knocking on my door very loudly screaming at me.  I was so stunned myself, I didn’t hear what she said, and I just remember that she was accusing me of being lazy and not helping with the cleaning.  I told her I did not know what my job was and she said that I was to report to Therese Kent.  I managed to tell her I had difficult time breathing but I ended up slamming the door in her face.  From then on I had the reputation of being lazy.

(Towards the end of my stay in Helidon, Judy came up to me accusing me of being lazy when I had just finished vacuuming the chapel and didn’t know what else to do.)

There was another day when Debra was sitting on the veranda and I looked at her, she had changed her hair style, and I told her she looked like someone (I don’t remember who).   This was yet another occasion for her to get angry.  (It seemed to be routine.)

Debra scheduled regular meetings.  At one meeting she told us that she had an encounter with Jesus and got angry and threw her diary in the furnace.  “What God Might Say to Me Today?”  I remember afterwards she was sitting at the furnace all by herself contemplating the burnt up diary and whatever meaning it had to her.  It was my job to clean out the furnace that day.  I thought naively to myself, well if this is what Jesus has dictated then maybe I should save it.  I found a few scrapes that had not been burned so I kept them but everything else I just swept into a bucket.  I put that bucket on the back porch of the kitchen.  When Debra found it, it was another incident for her to get angry.  She immediately ordered the whole thing to be thrown in the dirt in the paddock.  (in front of the convent.)  When I got to the United States I threw away the pieces I had saved.  I was sure she was not who she said she was by that time.

During my stay in Helidon, I recall three purported visions of Mary, once in the chapel when we first arrived, another where the Basilica was suppose to be built and the last one was in someone’s house in Helidon.  The last vision I was not interested in and I remember playing with a little boy in the kitchen instead which seemed a lot more fun to me.  I don’t think I believed in her vision at the time or at least I was doubtful, which she picked up on.   At that point, visions didn’t seem to me to be important.  Kindness seemed to be more important than the importance of Debra having visions.

There was another purported vision of Jesus in the paddock.  When word spread that Debra was having a vision of Jesus, a lot of people ran out to witness it.  I, however, stayed in the kitchen continuing with my kitchen duties.  When someone came back, I asked (I don’t remember who it was) what was the message Jesus gave to Debra. The person said, “Jesus asked Debra to change her bed sheets.”  I didn’t get it and was confused.

Once after the fact, MMM members were talking about it, Debra supposedly had a vision of angels in the paddock.  I don’t remember their specific comments about it, but they seemed to get excited about it.  (I certainly don’t believe she ever had such visions today.)

There was an incident wherein a near neighbor in Helidon came to the convent asking Debra to come out to her property and see if she could heal a cow who was dying, (I believe it was delivering a new baby).  I went also and I saw Debra praying over the cow.  However, unfortunately the poor cow died anyway.

After the day that Debra sprang to her feet at a Mass Fr. Ryan was giving during his homily and yelling on top her lungs, “Satan has entered this Church” and started telling us that demons lived in the Church, that was when everything went from bad to worse.  That was the point when I became disenchanted with the whole business.  I remember saying to myself, “I did not come all the way from Los Angeles to Australia to attend communion services.”  Debra started telling everyone that a priest was sending consecrated hosts to the convent and that she was not able to reveal its source to protect the priest.  She told us that Gordon was a Eucharistic Minister and that he would distribute Communion.

I didn’t understand what was happening, especially since Debra had previously even scheduled all night vigils in the Church wherein we members were to sign up for different times at St. Joseph’s Church.  I remember thinking to myself that it was very unusual for a Church to be open all night long.  Fr. Ryan had ministered to us and I thought he was a real gift from God.

Debra had told us that Fr. Ryan’s illness was caused by his sinfulness.  She accused him of always being inebriated, (drunk).   One day a week Mass was celebrated some distance away from the convent wherein we had to drive a car.  It should have been a big clue when Debra didn’t show up for Mass or adoration.  She didn’t show up for that Mass or adoration and sometimes at St. Joseph’s she did not show up.  However, she came barging in the door (of the Mass not at St. Joseph’s) and went into a side room where Fr. Ryan was and started reprimanding him about his sinfulness.  This was innate to Debra’s personality, reprimanding, as if God almighty himself were speaking.   I asked her why she didn’t have compassion on him and she just got upset again, and said she did have compassion on him.   In what way I couldn’t figure out.

That was the beginning of the end of my stay in Helidon.  From that point on the longer I was there, the more discontented I was with the whole business.

Then Debra started telling us that Jesus wanted us to picket the Bishop’s House because first Saturday Eucharistic devotions were being eliminated.  (Fr. Ryan said she wanted to use First Saturdays to prove to everyone that the Church supported her.)  I did not like the idea of picketing the Bishop’s House but I was told, “everyone is going”.  Debra asked me to type up a letter she was going to deliver to the Bishop.  In the letter she described all the evils in the Catholic Church.  I imagine the Bishop may have it on file if she gave it to him the day of the picketing.  Why does the Bishop need to come to Helidon, when Helidon came to him and to Fr. Ryan in such an offensive way?  Debra herself delivered most of the evidence.

What I found most alarming was that she had hidden a tabernacle in a room off the altar in her chapel.  I never knew it was there until my last few days in Helidon.    It was accessible to her in her residence.  She could go to the tabernacle in such a private way that no one could see her.  I found all that most alarming being she never had permission to do so.  Any Catholic knows that is a sacrilege without the specific permission of the Church.   The Catholic Church is the Eucharist and the Eucharist is the center and source of life in the Church.  We believe it is the true body and blood of Christ.  I believe that and every true Catholic does the same.  Yelling, “Satan had entered the Church”, is denying the real presence of Christ in the Eucharist, the opposite of her original message.

To cover it all up, she had to come up with excuses, the interview in Ireland where she states she is a “Traditional Catholic”, is totally deceiving.  She says she has the support of many priests.  I don’t believe she has the support of priests anymore.  By a traditional Catholic she means only eastern and Latin masses?   That means Christ is not present in most Masses around the world???

She started busing us on Sundays into Brisbane to attend a Melkite Church.   I’m sure it didn’t take that priest long to figure out who Debra really was.  She did not approve of us staying in Helidon on Sunday and going into town (Toowoomba) to Mass which is what I did at least once.

To boost morale, (I suppose) Debra had a meeting wherein she started telling us about her wonderful encounters with Jesus (visual) and how sweet it was to be with Jesus.  That was an obvious lie.  But, I suppose most everyone believed her at the time.

Around this time, after Debra banned us from St. Joseph’s, she told me I was no longer a guest, now I was a member of the community and the room I was in was a guest room so she moved me downstairs to rooms that only had partitions. (Not as nice)

My room was next to a girl named Ginny. I know Ginny was unhappy with the fact that Debra said she could not stay at the convent unless she sold her house in Toowoomba.  I wonder what became of her.

I know Debra was concerned about money at this point, because she started to send MMM members out to look for work.  Judy and Bernadette started work at new jobs.  Therese Kent was a nurse and already worked.

That was when strange occurrences started happening at the convent (at least with me).  One day Debra sat me next to her (which was something she had never done before and never did afterwards) when we were at table for supper.  A thought process of communication began happening in my mind, as if we were having conversations mentally and not verbally.  I can’t prove such things, so I don’t care if one believes it or not, I know what happened to me.  It never happened to me ever again, that was the one and only time I ever experienced anything of the kind.  Other strange things started happening to me which I cannot describe.   In reading a book about angels, it says that angels communicate through a thought process and not words.

I’m entitled to my opinion, and my opinion is that Debra wanted to understand things that were not from God.   There is some knowledge that God does not want us to understand because it will hurt us, like giving a child a gun to play with.

One day Debra said we were to all go into the paddock while she conducted a prayer service.  However the prayer service was not a prayer service.  Debra started identifying all this demonology that I did not know was even available in books.   Like there are nine choirs of angels, apparently there are different levels of demons too!  She identified all these different types of demons that she was binding up and sending them away from us into the abyss.  I would prefer to learn about good angels.

When I recently spoke to the priest who gave a talk on angels (a priest I have the greatest respect for), I asked him if we can do that, bind up demons and send them into the abyss.  He said we have no power to do such a thing.  He said that we need to call upon the good angels and ask them to protect us from the bad angels, like the St. Michael prayer.  God has won the victory over demons, we have not.  I went to Leighan and told her that I disapproved of such a prayer service as we can’t do that, only God can.  She didn’t seem to agree.

I didn’t understand what was happening but God knows the truth.   That was why after I left the convent I always stayed close to him at Mass and adoration and never gave up.   What drew me to Debra, is what healed me.

Eventually, she then told me I was to work every day with Michele in the local town market in Helidon.  She came checking up on me at the market one day as if she was very suspicious.  And then one day she called me at the store and got angry about something (again), I didn’t get it.   I just hung up on her.   When I came back to the convent she searched me out and when she found me she asked me why I wasn’t in the adoration chapel.  Truthfully, I was just trying to avoid her.  That was when she started accusing me of lies.  She told me that she was going to call Claire Mansour and have her pay for my free ride home.  I didn’t like her control and I didn’t think that Claire Mansour had any responsibility in paying for my plane fare.  She and others started pursuing me.  I went into my room and locked the door.  Debra and a couple of others started yelling at me, that was when I called her wicked.  I had another door in my room, so I ran out of my room out of the convent down the road to a couple’s house in the town crying.  Debra sent Gordon, Therese Kent, and I believe Michele (I don’t remember for sure if Michele was there).   They talked me into coming back to the convent.  When I got back to the convent Debra had called Wal Maggs.  She began telling everyone that since Wal Maggs had a son with needs, so that they would know how to deal with me and banned me to their home.  Wal and Beth Maggs were very nice.   We talked briefly and then it was time to go to bed.  They showed me to my room and they went to their room for the night.  I went into the bathroom and had an asthma attack and passed out.

The next thing I knew was when I woke up in the hospital.  I remembered hearing voices and not being able to open my eyes to see who was there.  I could not move my limbs and I started to panic a little, but little by little, one hand, then another started moving, one leg and then another leg started moving and then I was able to open my eyes.  [I would like to see the medical records.  My sister is a Nurse Practitioner and she could read the files.  I’m very curious, but I’m not sure they still have them.]  My sister said they told her I was in a comatose.

There was a doctor sitting next to me when I woke up.  He looked very attentive and seemed relieved, but then he left and I never saw him again.  (I had wished I had known who he was so that I could have thanked him).

I was told later that Debra came into the Emergency Room or Intensive Care room to conduct some kind of a healing on me.  She wasn’t able to heal the cow, so how could she heal me.

Then Debra, Gordon and Therese Kent came to the recovery room to visit me.  Debra insisted that I get a psychiatric evaluation and medication.  I believe she only came out of curiosity and certainly not out of any concern for me.  She was not a nice person to me.  Someone came into my room asking questions for an evaluation and I remember not answering any questions with any sincerity.  I don’t believe I even listened to this person. (I didn’t like the control Debra was trying to have over me.)

I remember wondering where I was going to go when they released me.  Rebecca came to pick me up and took me to Helidon.  Debra came out and started getting angry again and said, “I did not give you permission to come into the convent.”  A couple who had visited me in the hospital said that if I needed anything to let them know.  So I tried to call them.  Claire Birchley had the phone and I went to her to get the phone.  I had remembered their phone number.   Debra started yelling at me telling me that I was not allowed to call anyone and that I should go to a hotel.  I had no money, so I could not go to a hotel.  Claire Birchley was totally confused as to what to do, but she did end up giving me the phone and I called them and went to stay at their house.

I stayed at their house for about a week.  Debra had Anthony pick up all my things in my room and threw everything on their back porch of their house.  We found it when we came home one day.   They agreed with me about my feelings regarding Debra.  After a week another couple offered to take me in.  I was totally confused as to what to do, but God in his goodness takes care of all things.  I’m not unhappy the Immigration Police came and escorted me out of the country.  I’d rather have that happen than have Claire Mansour pay for my plane fare home.

During my stay at the second couple’s house in Helidon, we attended St. Joseph’s Church for Mass and I even accompanied them to a house where Mass was being celebrated by a priest from the Phillipines where Debra and the MMM members were in attendance and they also took me to Mass in the convent.   (The priest was staying at the convent at the time.) That was the day I saw the tabernacle.  I remember Debra getting mad again that I had come into the convent that last day.  I wouldn’t be surprised if she was the one to call the Immigration Police, although she told others that it was the hospital that alerted the Immigration Police.  I don’t know which the truth was (about my expired VISA), but it doesn’t really matter.
