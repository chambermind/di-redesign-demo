The High Court has passed a ‘motion’ in favour of the defendants against Pastor Kevin Sanford.

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

You can see from the courts website that a ‘motion’ has been passed in favour of the defendants against Pastor Kevin Sanford. This means that pastor Kevin is going to have to pay the defendants a large sum of money.

This case relates to a dispute in relation to rent on a former property where the Church was based. There earlier was a misunderstanding that the members of the family in dispute with Sandford were actually members of Abundant Life. This not in fact the case.

This will probably lead to further pressure on an ever shrinking flock or the organising of much larger scale events to raise the funds like the recent Jesse Duplantis event in Galway.
