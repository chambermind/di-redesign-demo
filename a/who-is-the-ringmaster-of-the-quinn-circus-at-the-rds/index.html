Who is the ringmaster of the Quinn Circus at the RDS?

Dialogue Ireland has been raising for two decades the influence of Tony Quinn in the fields of Yoga, Massage, Psychotherapy, Health Foods, Nutrients, Educo Seminars and Gyms. More recently we have shown that though he was totally opposed to mixing up the oil business with his seminar business, he has mysteriously been able to become a share holder in an oil company. At that time one of his most supportive members was Sheila McCaffrey. She was the one who attributed the oil find to Quinn. She also showed the same persistence in Belize in successfully finding the oil as she did in being one of his most successful recruiters for his seminars.

“Enniskillen-born Sheila McCaffrey, a director of BNE, said the company’s success was not pot luck: “Seven years ago I trained with Tony Quinn; it was an absolute transformation. It completely changed my life and it’s the only reason I’m in Belize today,” she said last week.”

So how do we now find she is out in the cold, viewed as an enemy by Quinn and her former friend Susan Morrice? Now we find Susan Morrice making the same comments as Sheila used to make, what is going on?

Generally, the general public will make comments like these people are adults and so if they want to hand over money fair dues to Quinn. They are treated as idiots and so they move into the shadows like the victims of rape. They brought in on themselves! Instead of trying to understand the processes which lead intelligent people to lose their autonomy they are blamed rather than understood. This use of undue influence is not generally recognised in our courts. It is part of the whole brainwashing debate, and the notion of mind control. It has been my experience that some of Ireland’s leading industrialists, businessmen and women have been totally overcome and reduced to an infantile state of dependence on Quinn. Also Quinn has taught them to do what he does namely, use other people’s money. When they come out of this nightmare they are still minded to let others do it for them. Rebuilding confidence and reclaiming their lives and responsibility can take years. Many try to do this unaided and without proper counselling. The result is depression, silence and despair.

They may have taken on board Quinn’s views that they will be zapped if they turn against the Messiah and Christ like person he represents. He is the Light and they are now in darkness. Some are coming close to their 1916 rising. So instead of cowering and by their silence giving the total floor for Quinn to say he is on the right track, it is time for every one of his meetings to be picketed. Not out of revenge, anger or despair, but as the way to draw public attention to the 40 years of his reign of psychic terror.

Going back to Sheila she had her email, and her life thrown into turmoil in early 2009 when her email was compromised. She became the subject of a campaign of vilification just before Tony Quinn’s Meeting in January of that year by as yet people unknown. Dialogue Ireland also had its website destroyed and had to migrate twice after it was taken out by half a GB attacks. The Gardai have not yet been able to locate the persons or person behind these attacks. Ironically, one of the business persons closely associated with Quinn was hit by friendly fire and his web site was compromised. In January 2009 I made a statement at Mountjoy Garda Station after two very close associates of Quinn also made a statement to the effect that in their presence they had heard Quinn say he would like to have our web site www.dialogueireland.org and myself taken out. The DPP as in other attacks from Scientologists on us would not proceed with a case.

Sheila also had a property of hers broken into in Belize and her partner’s business was exposed to unfair business attacks to undermine it. Sheila has gone though a terrible number of years. It is a reign of terror. Her involvement in a company which she helped succeed has been unfairly wrested from her. What is more she does not know what happened as the AGM’s are more like Stalinist Show Trials than proper orderly events to explain the progress of the company. They rather are mixed up with Educo seminars and the Oil business. They have the cheek to threaten people for disparaging the company if they complain they can’t understand what is going on in the company. The boundaries are totally broken, and there is no governance. I received a letter from a solicitor in Dublin telling me to not attack the director of an oil company, and when I enquired from the solicitor as to what it was about, he asked me to explain it to him, as he was just paid to send out the letter. What planet are they on other than Hick’s Tower?

So if you are going to the event on Sunday September 12 ask yourself honestly, did you not have gifts which you thought you would enhance by joining the Quinn team? You doubted your own abilities which were considerable. I was looking at Lorraine Nolan’s career. She was trying to go for the Eurovision over 10 years ago. I assume she went to Quinn like so many thinking he could enhance her career. Tomorrow she will perform, but what has he given her she did not have before paying through the nose?
