Tony hits town. Looking at sparklers with Eve on Grafton Street.

Tony Quinn

Tony has been back in Ireland a few days now. He was in town as usual. He is a great man for been seen in Grafton Street around Christmas. Also it is easier to be not noticed. As you know a lot of people would like to make an appointment to have that summons I talked about earlier in September delivered. People not sure about the oil money, and the so called loans. Others seeing Quinn as a dangerous person when he gets close to cash.

A journalist friend remarked that he had seen him in September and he looked like he had http://www.bodybenefits.biz/page.asp?Page=35&Menu=24

Facial veins – often called thread veins, split capillaries or broken veins – or those on the body can be reduced significantly, and in many instances cleared completely, by a variety of salon treatments.

Now people who drink in excess have this look, but Tony has it for other reasons, could that fatty diet cause the problem?

Then to my surprise while wondering down Grafton street this week- there was Tony in Weirs trying to find Eve….

Could it be he is finally going to get married, or are we going to be disappointed in that he will only get a bit of flashy jewellery to make Eve look good as he bathes in her reflected glory?

I meant to go to the party in Malahide but did not want to be jumped by that hot security detail Tony now has.

Those photos of the missile attack on Hick’s Tower were a spoof. You can be sure you do not need to go to such expense to have an army of people guarding you.
