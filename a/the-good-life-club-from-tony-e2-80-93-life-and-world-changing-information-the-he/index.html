BACKGROUND TO LATEST QUINN BROCHURE AND MESSAGE:

By now many of you will have received the latest sales pitch from one of Ireland’s most notorious cult leaders and snake-oil salesman, Dr. (not genuine qualification of course) Tony Quinn.  We give below a comment on this latest piece of self-glorifying and unsubstantiated propagandist claims.  The ageing self-proclaimed “messiah” increasingly uses legal threats to try and silence any opposition.  He now copyrights his leaflet which is why we don’t include the full text of his sales pitch here but use the principle of “fair use” to make comments in the interest of public information and debate.  These concepts of freedom of expression are not ideas that Quinn, in his cultist bubble world, is familiar with or happy with.

In common with the previous versions of Quinn’s “system” for success and personal achievement what is revealed here shows that it is not only again about a third rate reworking (plagiarisation as Quinn claims basic concepts of positive thinking, mind power and spiritual power are his discoveries) of long-existing ideas but the “system”, as many knowledgeable people have commented, is inaccurate and flawed to such a degree that it not only fails to deliver on the “empty promises” but has seriously damaged the finances, the minds, the relationships and the lives of many people over the decades.  Check Dialogue Ireland archives for pointers on this history, including many serious media reports.  Quinn has always worked on the old principle of “there’s one born every minute” i.e. there’s always suckers out there who have not done their research on Quinn’s 40 year old history of cultist exploitation and are therefore potential victims.  Quinn’s seminars should surely carry a financial and mental health warning from the government.  Small wonder with all the negative publicity that Quinn hides out in the Bahamas and slinks in and out of Ireland for the orchestrated rallies of the diminished faithful who are always pressured to drag along anybody and everybody they can to make up a big crowd “for Tony”.

Along with legal threats against critics and false propaganda against ex-followers one of Quinn’s other main strategies to protect and bolster himself is to attach himself parasitically to an oil company project which he initially rejected.  An additional strategy is, because the small coterie of cult devotees living with him in the Bahamas doesn’t provide enough worship for his narcissistic ego, he needs a fresh fix of new approval every so often.

Rinse and repeat – a message from Tony Quinn

Anyone selling the same thing for a period of time to the same market faces the problem of how to get the punters to part with their bucks when the last purchase didn’t do what it said on the box.  The problem is even more difficult when what it originally said on the first box was that this box contains everything you need to make all your wishes come true.  Yet further problems are caused when since the issue of that first box, a series of other boxes have been on offer each stating that this time, your wishes really will come true, that didn’t quite come true with the previous boxes.  The catch being, that if the contents of any of these boxes had done what they said they would, the customer wouldn’t be reading advertisements for new ones, being much too preoccupied sailing in yachts, cavorting in villas in exotic resorts, driving sports cars, finding new more desirable sexual partners than your old spouse/partner and similar pastimes that, judging from the promotional materials and lifestyles of the box-sellers, are considered sure to be priorities on the life-wishes list of any mature, deep-thinking individual.  So we look with interest to see what veteran wishes-come-true seller, Tony Quinn, has got on the label of the latest all-your-wishes-really-will-come-true-this-time box on offer.

“More a happening than a meeting.

You know by now that each time I invite you to meet with me, I believe that it will be the best ever. Okay, but this one is unmissable.”

First up, disappointingly, is a rinse and repeat of “it really will do it this time, folks”, prefaced with an “aw, shucks” type admission that he says this every time – “You know by now that each time I invite you to meet with me, I believe that it will be the best ever”.  And then, here we go again, “Okay, but this one is unmissable … I have discovered THE pattern of both Successful Living and Self Realisation”.  So what had he discovered the last [insert number here] times?

Given that he first made this claim nearly 40 years ago when starting his Yoga classes (followed by (rinse and repeat) Blueprint, followed by (rinse and repeat) Educo etc?)  Given that numerous people who bought and tried to use the product over these 40 years seem mysteriously lacking in yachts, villas, sports cars etc not to mention appearing at best average for their age health-wise?

A further claim is made that Quinn has now found a single pattern that makes both Successful Living and Self Realisation possible, and he assures that once learned and understood, “you will be able to start using it immediately”.  This begs the question of why there are two separate seminars in which this apparently identical formula will be taught, each lasting two weeks, although the cost is not specified on the promotional materials.

The “core” of the message consists of a series of vague, bland statements that could be culled at random from a New Age self-help book down on its luck, attempting to spice itself up with a reference to quantum physics.  Take the following example of these pearls of wisdom,

Quinn:  I always see you like the atom, when you split it a great power is released. The real you is trapped inside personal and global mindsets. Split these and we benefit from the power and potential of – You.

Actual atomic science:  splitting the atom releases a tremendous force that, left to itself, will act with terrible destructive power, leaving in its wake almost irreversible damage to the natural cell structures that promote and sustain organic life.

Suggested translation:  I see you as an atomic unit, you and your money and your energy and hopes for your life, when I split you, then you are free from your money, which is safely in my offshore bank accounts, releasing a great power to me, to spend on yachts, villas, sports cars, new girlfriends and other natural priorities of right-minded people.  This splitting may well cause you damage to your financial status and self-belief which you will not repair within your lifetime, but you’ll be too poor/broken/ashamed of being so stupid to do anything about it.  Quinn:  This is the area where even the ‘greats’ of our world have failed.

What “greats”, exactly?  Is Tony Quinn really saying that 14 days in a hotel in the Bahamas listening to him talking ill-informed platitudes about atomic science is going to outstrip the achievements of – to take a random sampling – Socrates, Jesus, Louis Pasteur, Gregor Mendel, Michelangelo, Leonardo da Vinci and Johann Sebastian Bach who tragically did not have the opportunity to take this seminar which would really have made all the difference to their contributions to human history?

He ends this section with a tempting offer – “When you come along I want to give you my new brochure”.   So he has something in writing, but it is not to be published openly for benefit of the suffering masses of humanity, apparently, despite the fact that it “reveals the pattern and you can use it as your textbook”.  This is certainly a change from the obviously hopelessly amateurish policy of Jesus of Nazareth, for example, who recommended “sowing the seed” as widely as possible to maximise the chances of it landing on good ground.  If only he had realised that spending 40 years keeping your core teachings as secret as possible, staying out of the way of everyone who isn’t carefully vetted, giving seminars in remote locations only to people who have already been filtered through previous layers of your organisation, and hiring security guards when you make a rare outing in public in your native land, was the way to go.

There is another curious little addition in the latest “rinse and repeat” publicity, an attempt to present himself as an academic research of “modelling”.  He makes a reference to an article written by the “lady who supervised my University Research in Modelling”.  Curiously, he makes no further reference to the article she apparently wrote, and does not even mention her name, so we may assume the sole purpose of dropping this reference in is to point out that he researched “modelling” in a university.  It is standard academic practice, when you mention an article, to give a reference stating the name of the author, the title of the article, the publication and the date, so that readers can look it up and read it for themselves if they wish.  If they did, of course, they would discover that there is a great deal of intelligent and interesting research on real modelling of human thinking and behaviour being carried out in many parts of the world, and they might decide to read that instead of going to the Bahamas to listen to Tony Quinn.  Importantly, the University of East London, lowly ranked in the league table of universities, has not validated Quinn’s thesis but rejected his claims to new discoveries and insisted they are simply aspects of hypnosis.

He also refers to the work of Dr. Jack Gibson, who used hypnosis to achieve pain control without anaesthetics. Quinn claims that Dr. Gibson was unable to teach anyone else to do this, while it is clear from Dr. Gibson’s own book, “Relax and Live”, that he taught many patients to do this in his long career.  Dr. Gibson, an authority on hypnosis, unlike Quinn, actually taught many doctors and other individuals in-depth to use his methods both in Ireland and abroad and there has been much successful use of Dr. Gibson’s methods of using hypnosis for pain control.  Quinn makes the claim that only he, Quinn, cracked the “code” with his modelling research, and “caused quite a sensation at the time”.  This presumably refers to Quinn’s use of hypnosis for pain control, in the company of and at times assisted by Dr. Gibson, with a few selected members of his cult who had operations using hypnosis as the anaesthetic.  The operations were used by Quinn to generate publicity for himself and his “system”.  Dr. Gibson later distanced himself from the publicity-hungry Quinn and avoided contact and any association with Quinn.  Fortunately, we now have the internet where anyone can look up “hypnosis, pain control”, and find out the truth – that for 200 years thousands of people have taught, learned and used this skill to give themselves and others relief from pain, and this continues to this day.  It is used even routinely in various hospitals throughout the world and by thousands of therapists in their private practices.
