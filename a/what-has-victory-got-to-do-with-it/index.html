What has Victory got to do with it?

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

The Sanfords are gone, and Hade and Victory are to the fore. Issues around non payment of tax, court cases and  a theology of prosperity are coming home to roost. A take over and church members being treated as commodities to be traded on the open market. Also members unable to detach long enough to see where this going, leaves many family members who had hoped the exit of the Sanfords would see an end to their nightmare realising they now must regroup and find ways to assist their families. So the systematic programming of the members using the high octane music and the now redundant money agenda has not evolved to critical reflection for those remaining.

The Abundant Life CC has closed down though its web site is still operating.
