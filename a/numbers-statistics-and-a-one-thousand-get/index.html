Numbers, statistics and a one thousand get

Scientology

One of the most common claims that adorn Scientology-related press releases is that of millions of members. Relevant data, however, casts strong doubts on this including:

The American Religious Identification Survey gave the number of Scientologists in the US at 25,000 in 2008.

The 2007 Australian census gave a figure of 2,507.

British census data from 2001 put 1,525 in Canada, 282 in New Zealand and 1,781 in England & Wales.

These figures are needed to properly contextualise the list presented below. Whenever victim testimony regarding the Scientology organisation is presented a common retort is to claim the existence of millions of Scientologists who are not similarly disaffected. The above data serve to show just how significant a proportion victim testimony constitutes within Scientology involvement.
