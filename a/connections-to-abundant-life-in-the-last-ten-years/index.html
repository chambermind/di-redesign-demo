Connections to Abundant Life in the last ten years:

The following link is a 22 minute YouTube featuring Perry Stone interviewing (Pastor) Walter Hallam.

Walter Hallam is the Pastor of Abundant life Texas where Kevin Sanford hails from. Seven or eight years ago Hallam visited Galway and apparently their financial support for the Sanford’s came to an end and they needed to find new ways of raising financial support.

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYTqi9NP2u8&feature=player_embedded

In the video Hallam talks about a light air plane crash in which his daughter dies along with 2 other people. Only two survive and one of them is from Abundant Life bible school Texas and is called Jason.

Jason came over to Ireland and Married one of Mark O’Mahony’s sisters, Rebecca. They both now have a business linked below. The O’Mahony’s originally led a faith based congregation in Greystones, Co. Wicklow. They moved to Galway and left Abundant Life some years ago now. There son Mark remained in Abundant Life and we now gather he is trying to form a church in Athlone.
