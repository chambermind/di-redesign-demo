The Good Life Club – New letter from Quinn with translation from Educoese

Tony Quinn

The Good Life Club

Educo®: To Draw out Your Life and Lifestyle from Within.

January 2010

Tonys_Letter-Jan-2010-1

Dear Good Life Club Member,

Let me begin by wishing each of you the happiest, healthiest and most successful year ever for 2010. In particular I would like to thank all of you who made your way to the last Sunday meeting in the RDS.

Normally people will say ‘I have come from Australia, Belize, USA etc.’ which is so awesome.

This time because of the horrible weather it was so moving to hear people say they came from Cork, Belfast, and Waterford etc. Indeed you continued to arrive throughout the day and by the second half the crowd was up to full strength. I know each and every one of you made a special effort to be there. No one could ever accuse any of you of being as the saying goes ‘fair weather friends’. Don’t you think it was one of our best meetings ever? The fact that we all wanted so much to be there made for such a great atmosphere.

Thanks also for all those wonderful, kind and supportive emails from those who could not make the meeting. It was the best response I ever had. And while I have the chance, thanks too for the Christmas cards and presents. One of my reasons for writing is to give you a copy of our new brochure. Hopefully it will inspire you to continue to put the material into practice. A point worth noting is that if you have

attended or are sending someone on a seminar, the cost may be offset against tax (check with your accountant). In the new brochure the business sections are now more clearly outlined to highlight the answers offered to these challenging financial times.

I have taken the liberty of enclosing a letter read out by Susan at the Sunday meeting. I wanted to take this opportunity to remind you that our small group in a very real and practical way has made a vital difference to Belize. Proving that ‘impossible dreams’ are possible to us. With this kind of track record as part of our mindset, what will we manifest next? Please stop and consider. Make these new year resolutions really life- and world-changing. Finally, since meeting you I have had so many great adventures and I hope you can say the same. Here’s to many more.

Lots of Love,

Tony.

Translation from Educoese:

Dear Oily Cartel Disciples,

Let me begin by wishing each of you the happiest, healthiest and most successful year ever for 2010. In particular I would like to thank all of you who made your way to the last Sunday meeting in the RDS. As you know I pushed the homing button and you came in the melting snow. You know I have the power to do this.

Normally people will say ‘I have come from Russia, Cairns, Cuba or Alaska etc.’ which is so awesome. This time because of the horrible weather it was so moving to hear people say they came from Cork, Belfast, and Waterford etc. Indeed you continued to arrive throughout the day and by the second half the crowd was up to full strength. I know each and every one of you made a special effort to be there. No one could ever accuse any of you of being as the saying goes ‘fair weather friends’. Don’t you think it was one of our best meetings ever? The fact that we all wanted so much to be there made for such a great atmosphere. Mostly sweat for I needed to have my full security detail there to avoid the summons from that woman who is trying to ruin me. I feel persecuted and I had to avoid being nailed to the cross in Malahide. Like Judas I did a runner after I saw my future nailed to my gate.

Thanks also for all those wonderful, kind and supportive emails from those who could not make the meeting. I could not read my mail as I do not know how to open an email or use a mobile phone, or for those from Alaska a cell-oops!

It was the best response I ever had. And while I have the chance, thanks too for the Christmas cards and presents. I had to get Mary to collect all these from the Post Office in Malahide as I had sealed my mailbox so I could not receive any summons!  The Duck Eggs and the leg of lamb had gone off, but it is the pong that counts.

One of my reasons for writing is to give you a copy of our new brochure.

EducoBrochure

Hopefully it will inspire you to continue to put the material into practice. It is basically a rehash of all the stuff I have used over the years, with the same failed testimonies and I had to wheel out Dr Clifford who will not say in public what I claim he has written. If it looks like something written last week, don’t worry the piece of paper it is written on is turning yellow with age.

A point worth noting is that if you have attended or are sending someone on a seminar, the cost may be offset against tax (check with your accountant). I would also advise you to contact your local tax office as you might find they are checking whether those that sold you the ride have declared it for tax. Whatever, do not claim relief if the person who sold it to you is not letting the revenue know.

In the new brochure the business sections are now more clearly outlined to highlight the answers offered to these challenging financial times. As you know when I originally started this gig I provided the flights and the hotels as part of the cost. But in these recessionary times it best you learn how to produce wealth by paying these charges yourself. Yvonne Sherry just was drinking too much at Dublin airport when people did not show up for flights. Now they pay and we make extra money- remember what I taught you. Use other peoples money!

I have taken the liberty of enclosing a letter read out by Susan at the Sunday meeting.

Susan-letter1

I wanted to take this opportunity to remind you that our small group in a very real and practical way has made a vital difference to Belize. As you know Susan is writing fiction to newspapers in Ireland and they are getting wise to the fantasies about the oil. All kinds of people are being totally taken out of the real story. As you know I was totally against the linking oil with Educo, but now I try to give the impression that like the prayer requests I sit in a dark room playing black Sabbath music while I divine where the oil is to be found. I then speak in Auntie Susan’s ear, and stare into my new cousin Josh’s third eye when I see the new find. Because I feel guilty I attack all those who oppose me by telling them they are attacking the oil company.

Proving that ‘impossible dreams’ are possible to us. With this kind of track record as part of our mindset, what will we manifest next? Please stop and consider. Make these new year resolutions really life- and world-changing. Finally, since meeting you I have had so many great adventures and I hope you can say the same. Here’s to many more. My next adventure is going to be a visit to a court in Ireland. I have never been to one or if I was it is so long ago I can’t remember.

Lots of Love,

Anthony Patrick Quinn.
