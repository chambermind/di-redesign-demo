From St Francis of Kenya to Media Gold digging in Ireland

Icross Charity

Dialogue Ireland has been aware of the activities of Mike Meegan for over a decade. Here again we see the lengths to which he is willing to go to amass money. The Charitable Trust he helped establish is now in a legal no man’s land of political correctness. As there is no real charitable accountability in this state as the legislation going back to the 90’s of the last century have never really been implemented leaving Meegan in a very powerful position as the trustees seem to be frozen into inaction. They feel no need to redirect the massive cash reserves at stake in limbo to a genuine charity working with the the people of Kenya.

No real audit seems to have taken place in light of the clear evidence that came out of the Mail on Sunday Court case.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/05/01/archive-following-the-mail-groups-success-in-the-courts-april-19-2010/].
