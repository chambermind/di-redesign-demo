“INE Members Group” Notice of meeting

Tony Quinn

Subject: INE Members Group. Notice of Meeting.

“INE Members Group”

Notice of meeting

The INE AGM has been officially postponed without a revised date being given and therefore it could be assumed that it has been cancelled.

The “Members Group” is therefore holding a meeting at the

Radisson Blu Hotel, Dublin Airport, at 2.00pm

on Thursday 3rd March, 2011

Registration at 1pm

All Members are invited including the Board.

Invitations have also been sent to Paul Marriott and Sheila McCaffrey.

Members are requested to please provide at the registration desk, proof of membership ownership, or the appropriate Proxy and valid ID.

We look forward to seeing you there.

(Please forward this invitation to any Member that you suspect may not be on the Members Group email list.)
