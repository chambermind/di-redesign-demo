Predictions – The end game for ALCC and the Sanfords

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

So far DI has been pretty much on the money in calling what will or would happen with the Sanfords and Abundant Life. We just wanted to share our latest thoughts in advance.

Hades of Glory – Brendan & Sheila Hade are very aware of the unrest in ALCC right now, rather than sending one of their trusted pastors to Galway to sort out the mess, they are going to take a ‘very hands’ on approach. Very soon the reigns will be handed over to the Hades with Kevin and Heather reduced to members of the congregation well in advance of their return to the USA. The Hades will come to town with all their own staff and ALCC will soon be a thing of the past, soon to be replaced by Victory Galway, a new beginning! Now of course they realise that this might prove to be a poisoned chalice and reports like this will be a rarity when the media starts digging a bit deeper:  https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/07/21/resurrecting-religion-by-alex-meehan-sunday-business-post/

The reports about prison like conditions in the following report were unfounded: PDF

Phoenix 08-37-41

The issue with the Victory outfit is not the lack of ethics in business, but the real concern is the monetarisation of the gospel as a system of financial rewards or wealth at the ideological level.

Christmas in Texas – It can only be a matter of time before Heather announces that she needs to leave ahead of Kevin. Finding a house, getting the children settled in a new school before the new term….. No matter what the reason, we are fairly confident that Heather and Kevin will want to get out of Ireland as quickly as possible. Right now every place they look, people are staring at them, some out of pity, the majority out of disgust. Jokes will be made by the pastors of how nice it will be to get some sunshine away from the wind and rain. Trying very hard to put a different slant on just how dire the situation really is.

Get together – Heather is going to announce a get together maybe at the family home. At this gathering – Heather will ensure to go around to staff and the inner circle one by one, offering advice on what to do next, letting them know how special they are, etc. This is a very deliberate ploy so that the lasting memory for all involved is one of the softer Heather, the caring one. She will of course save the best announcements for the group. Apologising for pushing people so hard with CCA and the likes but will go on to tell them that they are a gifted bunch of people and that she was merely a channel of God’s work in order to get the best out of them. There will be a tear in the eye just to add to the drama of it. The party will end with hugs and kisses and some people failing to come to terms with it.

Kevin will start ringing old church members/friends to say goodbye and it will be all smiles and happiness. He might even try to meet up with a few people in advance of going. Bury the hatchet type of stuff when really it is about trying to mop up any further mess before he leaves and leave as many people as possible thinking’ well they were not that bad after all’

So now we have warned people in advance……. Will they still fall into the trap of the latest round of manipulation? The answer unfortunately is an overwhelming yes.
