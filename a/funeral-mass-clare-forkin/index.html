FORKIN, Clare, (Killiney, Co. Dublin, formerly Sr. Francis O.C.D., Malahide Carmelite and Sr. Clare O.S.B. Beloved sister of Michael, Margaret Mary, Deirdre, Colman, Kieran, Patrick, Andrew, Garrett and the late Fr. Dermot Forkin.
