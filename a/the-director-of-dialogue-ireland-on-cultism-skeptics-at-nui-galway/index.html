The Director of Dialogue Ireland on Cultism – Skeptics at NUI Galway

Conferences & CommentariesCultism

The Director of Dialogue Ireland, Mike Garde MA will be speaking on the relevance of cultism for Skeptics at NUI Galway on Wednesday Dec 1, 2010

Critical analysis and a sceptical attitude are a necessary precondition to breaking the hold that cultist groups, states or individuals have over people. This includes states like North Korea, or Marian apparitions or individual control.

More info Ireland, like most European countries, has in recent decades been experiencing the presence of New Religious Movements (NRMs), sometimes referred to as “cults” or (in some continental countries) “sects”. The term NRM can be understood to include spiritual as well as religious movements properly so called. “New” may refer to movements that have been founded mainly over the past fifty years, though older movements are sometimes included.
Mike Garde (Dialogue Ireland) will give a talk on the phenomenon of NRMs in Ireland and highlight many of the tricks leaders employ to maintain power over their followers
