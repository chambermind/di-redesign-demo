Vows of Silence: Documentary examining the Vatican justice system Sunday 11 April RTE 1

Abuse by Church and Irish institutionsLegionaries of Christ

Since 2004 DI has been covering the issue of  the Legionnaires of Christ. We highly recommend a

book by Paul Lennon and the DVD of the production to be shown on Sunday 11 April – details below.

RTE One

22:25 Vows of Silence: Documentary examining the Vatican justice system that hid allegations of paedophilia against Fr Marcial Maciel, the founder of the Legionnaires of Christ, who was accused of sexual abuse and tyranny but allowed to retain his role as the head of the $650million fund. The programme traces Maciel’s rise to influence and interviews one former Vatican official, who lays the blame at the feet of Pope Benedict XVI.
