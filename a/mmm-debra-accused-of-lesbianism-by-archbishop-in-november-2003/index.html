MMM Debra accused of lesbianism by Archbishop in November 2003

Magnificat Meal Movement

This relates to a visit by Debra in relation to the Fatima visions which see the Conversion of Russia. Debra was going there to help achieve this aim. She linked up with what Cult experts regards as a most dangerous person Archbishop John Bereslavsky. She was with Claire Murphy, Fr Forkin and others mentioned in my thesis. The report from Amabilis Commentucci who was Debra’s agent in the East  Coast of the USA. The Director of  Dialogue Ireland received a commission to try to retrieve an incriminating video made by agents of Archbishop John Bereslavsky. He used secret video recordings of Debra. He had formerly worked for the KGB according to Dr Alexander Dvorkin a Russian cult expert. He advised against such mission, but it proved totally impossible to make contact with him.

Allegations have also been made that Debra was ejected from a Moscow conference organised by Archbishop John Bereslavsky having been caught in a compromising situation.[1] She was there in November 2003 after she had visited Ireland. Amy Commentucci, like Claire Mansour a onetime devoted member of the MMM, began to have doubts after this incident, but it really came home to her when Debra requested she give her a diamond ring received on her tenth wedding anniversary.[2]
