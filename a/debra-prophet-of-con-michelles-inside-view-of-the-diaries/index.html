Debra Prophet of Con – Michelle’s inside view of the Diaries

Magnificat Meal Movement

Regarding the Diaries…..It tends to be a bit of a mystery. As I have said before, I typed up Diary no 5 which was the first one published. I vaguely remember Debra saying that she didn’t want to publish the first four because she was convinced that the devil had tricked her. I have also said before that whilst typing no 5, I came across many messages with missing lines and dates. She just told me to fill in the blanks…….which I refused to do!

I believe that the first 4 diaries were too evident of plagiarized messages that she took from other seers and visionaries she had thoroughly studied. She was obviously afraid that it would be easily detected. She had become more skilled at putting her own flavour to the messages by the time she wrote no 5.

The diaries are full of repetitious writings that anyone could write with a bit of practice. Wal Mags can also attest to the fact that many messages were repeated, just with different dates and years. Debra had us all reading and referring to those diaries as though they were copies of the bible. She used the diaries as a tool for instilling the fear of God and herself in people. If someone questioned her she would tell them to read diary number whatever. Also if someone dared to oppose her she would give that person a message supposedly from God, always full of wrath on the person and protectiveness towards Debra. I know, I have been in the firing line many, many times.

Frankly I found the diaries very boring, the repetition of the content always lost my attention in the first few minutes. I still can’t believe I actually helped sell those things! If these messages were from God there would be no changing the words and dates after He had given them to her. Referring back to the message God supposedly gave Debra for me: I stated before that I was the one who wrote the message down as ‘God’ was giving it to Debra. I kept the original copy. When it was published in one of the diaries, the words were changed to the extent that the message was not written to me but Debra. The meaning behind the message was also changed. This was just another tick in the box on the negative side of my list.

I kept the copy of that message in the front cover of one of my mass books. When I finally had my belongings returned to me after I was forced out of my home, that book, along with a large portion of my other belongings went mysteriously missing!

Debra is a very skilled writer. She would have been better off using her skill to write fantasy novels for children. Her ability to read something and remember it was an attribute she used for the wrong reasons especially when it came to the Bible. Her use of scripture was a weapon of fear. Her memory of scripture was so good that she would quote lines and spit them at anyone who undermined her authority.

I have witnessed her on the phone giving advice to some poor person in trouble. While she listened to the person she would be flicking through the bible until she found a scripture that fit in to the moment. The problem was her interpretation of the scripture would many times be totally different to that of the book itself. Sometimes she would only read one or two lines which really did not explain the meaning fully. I was always of the belief that the bible was written clear enough for everyone to understand. I don’t believe we have to find double meanings in the scriptures and that you have to have some sort of gift to be able to understand. I was also of the belief that changing ‘The Word’ and making it your own was a no no.
