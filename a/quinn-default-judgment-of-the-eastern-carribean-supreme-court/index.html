Quinn Default -Judgment of the Eastern Carribbean Supreme Court

Tony Quinn

QUINN DEFAULT JUDGMENT – 31 JANUARY 2011

YOGA GURU Tony Quinn is in breach of his fiduciary duties to the Central American oil company of which he is a director, a court in the Caribbean has found.

JUDGMENT

As the 2nd Defendant Tony Quinn (“Quinn”) has not filed an Acknowledgment of Service or a Defence,

ON READING the Application for Request for Entry of Default Judgment filed 5 January 2011, the Notice of Application filed 5 January 2011, the Affidavit of Lineta Williams affirmed 5 January 2011, the Affidavit of Service of Stanley Michael Knowles affirmed 11 November 2010, the Order for Service out of the Jurisdiction of Mr Justice Belle dated 9 July 2010, the Claim Form and Statement of Claim filed herein,

AND ON HEARING the submissions of Counsel for the Claimant,

THIS COURT HEREBY ORDERS AND DECLARES THAT Judgment is entered for the Claimant, Maranco LLC, against Quinn for damages or equitable compensation for losses incurred by the Claimant in an amount to be determined by this Honourable Court on an Application for Assessment of Damages.

IT IS ORDERED THAT Quinn is hereby ordered to purchase the Claimant’s shares in International Natural Energy LLC (“INE”) without a minority discount in accordance with an independent valuation of INE.

IT IS HEREBY DECLARED THAT Quinn has breached his fiduciary duties to INE.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED THAT Quinn shall pay to the Claimant pre­judgment and post-judgment interest on any amount found owing to the Claimant at the rate of 5% per annum.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED THAT Quinn shall pay to the Claimant its costs of this action to be assessed or fixed by this Honourable Court.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED THAT any additional relief to which the
Claimant may be entitled pursuant to the Statement of Claim that is not covered by this Judgment in Default is reserved to a further Order of this Court.

IT IS FURTHER ORDERED THAT service of this Judgment and any other documents in this matter shall be served on Tony Quinn by delivering the same to No. 5 Flamingo Court, Paradise Island, Bahamas, and by mailing a copy to the same address by regular mail.

BY ORDER OF THE COURT
