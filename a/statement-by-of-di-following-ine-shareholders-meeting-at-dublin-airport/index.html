Statement by DI following (INE) shareholders meeting at Dublin Airport

Tony Quinn

Statement by Director of Dialogue Ireland Trust following International Natural Energy (INE) shareholders meeting on March 3.

Many were confused by what seemed to be the contradictory statements we offered on Wednesday. From the report by Paul Cullen in today’s Irish Times you can get a sense of why. Our function was not to interfere in the private business of shareholders but to assist in making people aware of the Quinn connection. All the information I need is found in Cullen’s report. By the way this represents the first report from within the oil debate here in Ireland.  Also some wavering Educoists still under the influence of Quinn would have been told I was a malign influence on the proceedings. Similar views were expressed when Anonymous started protests in 2008 against Scientology. Members were told that I was behind these protests funded by the chemical Industry. This created panic hence the contradictory communications.

So let me put on the record I am glad I was not there, and this point has been made to me by concerned people I was in touch with. There was a cock up rather than a conspiracy! It is not surprising considering that all the leaders of the meeting were being hit with legal threats nearly up to the beginning of the meeting.

“YOGA GURU Tony Quinn’s role as director of an oil company in Central America has come under fresh pressure after 70 shareholders backed a former director who is trying to oust him.”

The first point to make is that the numbers present represent a major breakthrough. It is people getting off their knees and taking ownership of their own problem. Also they have recognised who rather than what the problem is. Not Anthony Quinn, but the Guru Quinn.

The cult tactic of trying to spook members was employed. Here is the report:

“Before the meeting, an associate of Mr Quinn’s, Patricia Fitzpatrick, urged arriving shareholders not to attend. She handed them a note from the board which said their attendance would have significant legal and financial implications for the company. Some left but the majority entered the meeting room.”

Perhaps this was the intimidation the Board had referred to earlier. Her very presence to some would have been a very difficult thing to deal with. She represents her master by saying to Eduoists by going into this meeting you are playing Judas. When she was not able to undermine the meeting, she obviously had some success, she was brazen enough to try to act as a spy. Also it is the smoking gun which shows the connection between a hard core Educoists and the oil company. Why were there not members of the company there to advise the shareholders? Simply the reason is they would have not had a leg to stand on. This was a private meeting, how could it be a problem. So Quinn sends one of his Eduocists to try to influence wavering members. That says it all.

Ms Fitzpatrick then sought to attend herself, saying she was a shareholder, but was told to leave. A brief stand off ensued, after which she consulted with a lawyer and then left the room.

This was a significant moment according to my informants. The decision to ban her and her legal representatives was an empowering decision. They claimed their space and their private meeting for themselves. She showed the very thing that Quinn had accused them of, namely there is conspiracy between Quinn’s Educo operation and the INE. Also by trying to get people not to go in she contradicted herself by herself  going in. This shows the mindset of cultism. Who was she representing? All the time the current directors of INE say that people are conspiring against them. Here we see how an agent of Quinn’s and a fanatical supporter of his Educo programme makes a fool of herself. She had to be removed from the room not by the members of the private meeting who were incensed by her presence, but by the INE legal team who were worried she was going to really mess up!  She still stayed around and tried to ear wig what was going on. Those who had been accosted by her in the forecourt of the hotel were the one’s really enraged as they were told not to go in as they would be reported, wink wink, and yet Patricia did not even see the contradiction in her her own attempted gate crashing of the meeting. I assume this will feature along with the petition as an expression of a totally dysfunctional company in future legal action.

“The shareholders yesterday signed a petition calling for the appointment of a receiver to the company, International Natural Energy (INE). The petition will be used by lawyers for a former director, Paul Marriott, in his legal moves to remove Mr Quinn from the board and open up the books of INE to legal scrutiny.”

Paul Marriot was the first to break the control of the Guru by his principled court action and courage in the face of horrendous spin, the ducking and diving INE. Here we see the beginnings of a shareholder group coming of age rather than rage. They are now using democratic methods to take back their company from a takeover.

INE has earned more than $750 million (€537 million) in revenue from oil wells in the Central American state of Belize since 2005, but has never paid a dividend and shareholders are unable to trade their shares, the meeting heard yesterday.

This is the outrageous situation which continues to persist. The influence of Quinn is so strong that it is mind boggling even for those of us in Ireland dealing with corruption of developers, banks and the mismanagement of governance.

“Last Friday, the company abruptly postponed its agm planned for yesterday, claiming members had been subjected to threats and intimidation. “

You will note my earlier view of where the intimidation came from!

The dissident group called an egm but was warned by the company this would be illegal. An informal meeting that went ahead yesterday lasted more than three hours and heard contributions by Mr Marriott, another disaffected former director, Sheila McCaffrey, and many shareholders.

The important development here was the fact that Sheila McCaffrey came out as she really along with the early founders has been the subject of character assassination, cyber terrorism, and false rumours. Also she was intimidated out of Belize and subject to false reports to the police. Her private communications were spread around the globe and false deductions made about these communications by doctoring her documents and emails. Her democratic right of assembly and communication were impugned as evidence of conspiracy, when all she was trying to do was fulfil her fiduciary duty as a director. When these issues reach court she will no doubt have to have a multi million award as compensation for the ordeal she has been forced to endure.

“Ms Fitzpatrick then sought to attend herself, saying she was a shareholder, but was told to leave. A brief stand off ensued, after which she consulted with a lawyer and then left the room.”

This is very much a good example of the cultist mindset and tendency. In this case it did not intimidate or work, but that was its intention.

“Many of INE’s shareholders are Irish people who at one time attended Mr Quinn’s mind training seminars and invested in what was then a speculative oil venture. Mr Quinn was made a director several years after oil was struck and his involvement has become a source of contention between two rival groups of shareholders.”

It should be pointed out that at the beginning he was totally opposed to the oil company believing it would deflect from his seminars. When he saw the potential to cash in he changed his tune, and turned on those who misattributed the oil success to him.

“Last month, a court in the Caribbean found that Mr Quinn was in breach of his fiduciary duties to INE and ordered him to purchase Mr Marriott’s shareholding in accordance with an independent valuation of the company’s shares. The company has since obtained a stay on that order.”

Here is the key to the whole issue. There is now a legal process where the judge has got the big picture, namely all we have is delay and prevarication and using the shareholders as hostages like Saddam Hussein against the ultimate fall of the regime. They are clinging onto straws, but as it was on D Day it is hard to see the wood from the trees as you are in the middle of the battle. The general is Paul Marriott whose single minded strategy is beginning to achieve results. The INE gang has more cash, but the tide is turning because the mental control of the regime is about to snap. Then we will hope to see Susan and others join their old friends as victims.


‘“Oil is forecast to go up another $30-$40 a barrel and yet we can’t sell our shares or get a dividend – it’s crazy,” one investor, Rob Henshall, told the meeting.’


“Mr Marriott said his legal action against the company was about principles rather than money. He said he would “keep going until the money runs out”, but warned shareholders they had to stand up for themselves.”

Paul has put his finger on the weakness of the former Educoists. They have been taught to use other peoples’ money. They have unconsciously transferred this strategy to Paul. They want him to be the sugar daddy who takes away their pain. The lesson he has put to them is that he is involved with principle even to the point of the money running out. I can’t carry you on my shoulders; you need to get up of your knees. The indications are from a text I received late last night that this is what this meeting brought home to people. Also a source told me today that there is palpable sense of purpose as those present have rediscovered the original vision of what he oil company could be.

“Ms McCaffrey described herself as “practically destitute and penniless” after she was removed as a director. She claimed the company had a policy of keeping shareholders in the dark and said the majority of $10 million in shareholder loans by the company went to Mr Quinn and other directors. INE’s accounts for 2009 show spending of $1.1 million on “investigative and security expenses” and $1.3 million on legal expenses, she said. The company, which last held an agm in 2009, said it looked forward to having a “constructive meeting of members” at an agm on a new date.

This recognition of weakness by Sheila will no doubt give her greater energy and power. It is very difficult to say in a public forum the opposite of what you told the world a number of years ago.

The level of abuse she received and making her the centre of attacks over the years was well founded. She has the knowledge of the beginnings of the company and its operations so when a real AGM is called she will be able to deliver a devastating coup de grace to this clique.
