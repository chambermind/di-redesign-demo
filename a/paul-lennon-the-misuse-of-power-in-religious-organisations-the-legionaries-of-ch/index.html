Paul Lennon: The misuse of power in religious organisations (The Legionaries of Christ.)

Legionaries of Christ

Can cult like features be present in a

mainstream religious order?

The case of the Legion of Christ.

The issue:

A quiet but powerful presence in Ireland, beginning in the early 1960s, the Legion of Christ both as an order and a lay “movement” (Regnum Christi), became the most vibrant and successful Catholic institution in Europe and the Americas through the beginning of the 21st century. Strong and silent, the elite Legion troops often went unnoticed in the media and among the faithful. What was its Achilles heel and how did it begin to show signs of weakness? Did the Catholic hierarchy, Pope and Vatican ever get involved? Could the term “cult” ever be applied to the Legion and similar groups? Can aberrations exist inside respectable institutions and mainstream churches? How to prevent, discern and cure such evils?

It will be on in the GMB (Graduates Memorial Building), in Trinity College at 7pm running till about 8.20pm with a reception to follow.

The speaker:

 Dubliner Paul Lennon, presently a licensed professional counsellor in Virginia, USA, was a member of the Legion of Christ from 1961-1984 and an active priest through 1989. Over 20 years ago be joined other Irish, Mexican and Spanish former members on a crusade to tell about their experiences with the founder, Fr. Marcial Maciel, and his “divine work”. A thorn in the order’s side, Lennon and the tiny organization he created, were sued in civil court by the Legion in 2007 and somehow survived undaunted. In 2008 he published a memoir called “Our Father Maciel, who art in bed” and he has been contributing to a webpage, www.regainnetwork.org   for the past decade. He blogs at http://www.irishmexican43.blogspot.com/   where the crusade continues,  although he no longer feels like a lonely voice crying in the wilderness.

Cult-like Aberrations inside the Church?

The Case of the Legionaries of Christ

By J. Paul Lennon, MA, M.Div.

Abstract:

From the beginning, Mexico 1941, the Legionaries of Christ and their Founder, Fr. Marcial Maciel, appeared on the world stage as “signs of contradiction”. They were met by extreme reactions of allegiance and aversion within and without the Catholic Church. Nevertheless, for decades favorable winds had been propelling the Legion along as it gained members, money and “monuments”. Then, just a few years ago, strong winds of adversity began to buffet the vessel. Out of nowhere, it seems, its protective mother, the Vatican, began to doubt her darling, to question and to censure, first the founder and then the order. Beyond the scandals regarding the founder’s personal life, covered by the media, could there be more serious flaws in this “Work of God” approved by the Catholic hierarchy? Paul Lennon, a member from 1961-19841, and an observer, researcher and activist2 since then will help to unravel the mystery surrounding the Legion of Christ. But he will not stop there. In the process he will explore how excessive control, consideration and conformity can exist inside any institution. What are the effects on the members of any such “high-demand group”, be it secular or religious? Can they be brainwashed? How to discover the warning signs and prevent these aberrations and abuses?

1 Lennon published Our Father Maciel who art in bed, a Naïve and Sentimental Dubliner in the Legion of Christ in 2008, available on Amazon softcover and Kindle: http://www.amazon.com/Father-Maciel-Sentimental-Dubliner ebook/dp/B00519TUQU/ref=sr_1_1_title_1_ke?s=books&ie=UTF8&qid=1318729658&sr=1-1
