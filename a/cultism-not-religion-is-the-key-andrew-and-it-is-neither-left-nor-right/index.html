Cultism not Religion is key and it is neither Left nor Right!

CultismIslamismReligion

 Commentary on Andrew Sullivan’s article,

“Religion, the motive the left refuses to see amid the carnage of Boston.”

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/04/30/left-refuses-to-see-motive-amid-the-carnage-of-boston/

There are several things we cannot confidently say about the motives of Tameran Tsarnaev in bombing the crowd at the finish of the Boston marathon. We do not know all the details of his psychological profile; we do not know the identity of a figure called Misha, who was apparently instrumental in Tsarnaev’s radicalisation; we do not know why he chose the marathon as his target; we do not fully know the role of his younger brother or his wife, who claims total surprise at the events. Any act as violent as his is “multi-determined”, as my shrink unhelpfully puts it. Every individual is a unique blend of DNA, experience and psychological complexity. It’s worth keeping that in mind before we reduce someone to a caricature.

We can agree with this section as in all things there are many factors in a decision and the course of action taken.

But we do know this: Tsarnaev was a fanatical, extremist Muslim – and his brother has testified that “defending Islam” was the motive. Tsarnaev’s YouTube page is crammed with Islamist conspiracy theories, apocalyptic myths and violent Islamist imagery. He disrupted his own mosque’s services by ranting that it was wrong to honour Martin Luther King Jr because he wasn’t a Muslim.

Here Andrew conflates Islam with Islamism which is its cultist version. Already he points to the fact that Tameran Tsarnaev as an Islamist cultist was at variance with his own Muslim faith community. Later he seems to confirm this point:

“Stigmatising the 99% of US Muslims who are appalled by terrorism and far better  integrated than in Europe is another way to turn a tragedy into a catastrophe.”

I would say his classification as an issue of Religion aids that stigmata! By the way as a Cult Expert one of the most important lessons to learn is that as a theologian I tended to misdiagnose the phenomenon.

The Russian intelligence services alerted the CIA and FBI to what they detected as dangerous Islamist extremism even before he had spent six a months in the country. His uncle described Tsarnaev’s shift toward extreme devotion.  “I was shocked when I heard his words, his phrases, when every other word he starts sticking in words of God.”

As a rejection of the Bush project it is likely that the Obama administration has weakened the analysis of Islamism by confusing the issue with that of freedom of Religion. There is politically correct fear of Islamophobia which weakens Islamism awareness. Obama’s extra judicial killing of Osama Bin Laden and his drone attacks makes him unassailable from the right, and keeps the left at bay. The recent action on Israel and the stage managed rapprochement with Turkey is showing his goal which is withdrawal the USA from being a frontline defender internationally.

Tsarnaev gave up the sport he won prizes in – boxing because of Islam. He gave up drinking. He complained about the loss of values in modern society. He hit his girlfriend and persuaded his American wife to become a Muslim – now covered from head to toe. Of course the Boston bombings were an act of jihad.

We would note similar reactions to people joining New Age, Scientology, Evangelical or Catholic groups. Jim Jones had very violent results. Also Timothy McVeigh shows the violent streak still found in Christianity and the day after the attacks on Boston we had the prospect of a connection to the twentieth anniversary of Waco. There is currently cultist Buddhist violence against Muslims in Burma. These characteristics are not an expression of a religion but of cultism. In Africa we have had the Lord’s Resistance Army and the mass murder or suicide of the Ten Commandments of God in Uganda. Fortunately a lot of the New Age Groups give end of world warnings like the recent one about the December 21 last and the Mayan Calendar which turn out quite benign except for you bank balance. I visited Bugurach in Southern France where such an event was to take place, but the only ‘aliens’ the mayor commented on were those of us visiting to study the phenomenon. On the day itself it was an international joke. In fact to quote TS Elliott the world ends not with a bang but with a whimper. However those that benefit from these scams merely rearrange the story and continue to gouge an unsettled, floating and vulnerable public. Others like the Solar Temple, The Children of God, Jonestown or Heaven’s Gate end in violence.

History is crammed with violence committed in the name of God. From the Crusades to the Reformation to the religious conflicts of the 17th century in Europe, we have seen how Christianity although founded by a radically nonviolent guru – can become murderous. How much more plausible is the idea of violence coming from a religion founded by an explicitly political conqueror.

Here it is inter-religious as between Christian and Muslim and intra Christian as between Orthodox and Catholic.
