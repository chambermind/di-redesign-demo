PART 2 – DOES AN OIL COMPANY NEED A REPUTATION?

Tony Quinn

While we wait for the Judgment from the East Caribbean Court, here at DI we have received notice of yet another legal action, this time at Denver, Colorado, USA, involving Educoist Susan Morrice.

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments.html

We have been “invited” to be interrogated by her lawyers prior to the Court hearing in a US process called a “deposition”.  It is vague as to what we could possibly have to say that would be relevant to the case; however, it seems that one of the items that Susan Morrice is suing a number of people for is “conspiracy”.  We hope it is clear that here at DI we do not condone illegal actions, we have no plans to travel to Denver to overturn bins, would not dream of threatening a dog, and do not know where Tony Quinn shops and even if we did, would not lurk around there in the hope of “bumping into” him.

So what can the lawyers want?  It seems that saying what you believe to be true about important matters that seriously affect people, counts as “conspiracy” in the mind of Educoist Susan Morrice.  She is concerned, as she stated in the Court hearing in the British Virgin Islands, about “negative stories”.  Let us say it here

THE VALUE OF AN OIL COMPANY DEPENDS OVERWHELMINGLY ON ITS OIL RESERVES

http://www.belizenaturalenergy.bz/about_bne/visionmission.html

Our Vision:

To be a visionary leader in the oil and gas industry creating opportunities to mine the potential within.

Our Mission:

To positively impact the Belizean people by efficiently finding, producing and marketing oil and gas in a healthy, safe, environmentally and socially conscious manner that fosters teamwork, creativity and professionalism.

IT DOESN’T  USUALLY AFFECT THE VALUE OF THE COMPANY IF PEOPLE THINK THE DIRECTORS OF AN OIL COMPANY ARE NOT VERY NICE

(We have received a book for review which suggests that Quinn is like Merlin a magician and it was his spiritual powers that led to finding the oil. I guess that Justice Bannister might have other ideas. One wonders why the magician could not have used his powers to give evidence in court. They say Merlin only got results because he was on the side of good.)

So if we publish stories based on well-founded reports that Tony Quinn does things that have a bad effect on some people, THIS HAS NO EFFECT ON THE VALUE OF THE OIL IN THE GROUND IN BELIZE – THIS WILL STILL BE PROFIT MAKING IN THE MARKET

On the other hand, if an oil company is found in Court to be run by people who are so unprofessional that they have been operating under an illegal agreement for five years, and have been running up large bills for commercially unjustifiable expenses during that period, that might affect the value of an oil company, because even if you are not very nice you are still expected to be able to run a company competently.

DI DID NOT SAY THESE THINGS.

BUT JUDGE BANNISTER DID.

So why is the company paying for lawyers to interrogate people about a “reputation” that has no effect on the company, running up very expensive costs and reducing the profits which belong to the company’s shareholders?
