What is really going on? Do you speak Arabic?

Islamism

What is really going on? Do you speak Arabic?

Niamh Horan in “Behind the Veil” in conversation with Mike Garde:

I tell Mike Garde, Director of Dialogue Ireland, that during my interviews most of what I’m being presented with is a series of conflicting arguments as to whether or not there are extremist Muslims operating in Ireland to which he replies, “My concern is that (until now) he is giving you information you want to hear so that you will write about it and then other people will read it and believe it too. But who are you going to believe? The Imam or Shaheed, who spent three years attending the mosques and studying with principal Muslim characters and who is telling us that the Muslim community is preaching one thing and doing another. What has he to gain for telling us that? All he is getting from it is threats from Muslim leaders. The key thing for Irish people to understand us that a lot of the personal conversations between Muslims that contain extremist views are spoken through the Arabic language so if a journalist goes and interviews a Muslim leader they mightn’t get the full extent of their views but a sanitised version of his beliefs.”
