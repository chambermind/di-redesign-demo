Qatar strips Qaradawi of citizenship. Muslim IRB reaches its end game

Islamism

http://israelmatzav.blogspot.ie/2013/07/qatar-strips-qaradawi-of-citizenship.html

Thursday, July 04, 2013

Qatar has stripped prominent Muslim Brotherhood cleric Yusuf al-Qaradawi of his Qatari citizenship, has ordered Hamas politburo Chief Khaled Meshaal (who took refuge in Qatar after it was no longer palatable to be sheltered by Bashar al-Assad) out of the country, and has withdrawn support from the Muslim Brotherhood as a result of Wednesday’s events in Egypt (link in Arabic).

 

Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad bin Khalifa second, the Emir of Qatar, on Tuesday evening, ordered Sheikh Yusuf al-Qaradawi to leave the country, and the withdrawal of his Qatari nationality. They also ordered the closure of all offices of the Muslim Brotherhood in order to remove any doubt of support for their political aims. Prince Tamim confirmed in an interview, we are all Muslims, but not the Muslim Brotherhood. We are dealing not with a true representative  of a State, but with a sectarian political faction. Al-Nahar reported that Prince Tamim gave Hamas leader Khaled Meshaal, 48 hours to leave the country.
