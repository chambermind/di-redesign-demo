Tony Quinn

Publication of emails in 2010 between Katrina Skinner attorney to Jean Cornec and Mike Garde Director of Dialogue Ireland.

Mr Justice Gerard Hogan continued hearing submissions in regard  to case involving International Natural Energy (INE.) Former director Jean Cornec is suing for $15m (€12m) for shares he claims he sold to a fellow director, Susan Morrice, and was never paid. A number of parties are suing and counter-suing each other in the US case. It arises from the sale of shares in International Natural Energy (INE), an oil company to which Mr Quinn was appointed a director. Former director Jean Cornec has brought the action against the company, claiming he wasn’t paid for $15 million in shares he sold to a fellow director, Susan Morrice. The company claims he broke the contract of sale by engaging in a campaign of disparagement of the directors, including Mr Quinn. My Counsel Seamus Tuathail sought to have these introduced today. They were given to Morrice’s counsel Jim O’Callaghan who will consider overnight whether he is willing to accept them into evidence. Justice Hogan has given back the papers unread to to Seamus Tuathail and will adjudicate on them tomorrow. Justice Hogan was quite surprised to say the least at the way emails which are protected by the Data Protection Act 1988 are allowed into the public domain in Denver.
