Why is the Church of Scientology expanding in Ireland? | Claire Byrne Live

Conor gives a very lucid commentary on what is going on in the world of Scientology. He clearly shows that though here in Ireland for over 60 years The non Church Scientology Organisation do not meet the criteria for obtaining charitable status. They are fixated on being given a religious status so that they can avoid the normal rules of emplyment and taxation. Also their claim that Narconon is a secular programme is correct in that they do not have a religious impulse at all. However, it is a series of front groups. All one can say is the following. It is a NAR “CON” ON. When Hubbard started Scientology he used his E Meter which was questioned as being quack science. He came up with the idea of starting a religion as a way to put themselves beyond scrutiny.
