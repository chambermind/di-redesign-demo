Comm2post: Developments in the oil dispute with reference to undue influence.

We have taken the latest letter from Susan Morrice and a very cogent reply by an anonymous shareholder. Below you will find earlier posts which show how Susan has been able to bypass scrutiny by Irish newspapers as they did not understand the cult connection. Paul Cullen’s article was the first to put the oil aspect together with high intensity programming that occurs in the closed world of the Quinnites. Also there has been an unwritten assumption that only Quinn and his associates have the right to comment, while his opponents are disparaging when they use their right to put forward their perspective. Paul Marriott’s legal challenge has shifted the goal posts, and this week end it looks like Quinn is trying to put his finger in the dyke. How long Rasputin can hold out it is hard to know.

A letter from Susan Morrice
September 6, 2010

Dear Members:

I want to bring an important matter to your attention. The Company is being sued by Paul Marriott (Maranco LLC) in the Nevis High Court. He is claiming several orders from the court, amongst which are the following:

· An order to prevent the Company from making further loans to members without his express written consent,

· An order for a receiver and manager to be appointed over the Company,

· An order requiring the Company to purchase his shares at full market value and pay his legal fees, or in the alternative for the Company to be dissolved,

· An order for the Company to pay him compensation for his alleged losses.

By seeking these orders Marriott is attempting to take over control of the Company’s Loan Release Program. If he is successful in obtaining an order for the appointment of a receiver/manager of the Company or for the dissolution of the Company, it would mean that we the members would lose everything.

Over the years Mr. Marriott or his company has received at least US$42 million for services to our Company and he has received about US$1.9 million on the sale of his shares.

We consider his claim to be wholly without merit and part of the orchestrated campaign against the Company. We intend to provide a vigorous defense to it. In all of this our aim is to ensure that the Company continues in business and the interests of the members are protected and preserved.

This is just a further example of “meet our unreasonable demands or else” that the Board has been continually subjected. Here is just the tip of the iceberg. These demands have included “Pay me $30 million or else”. Then Maire Lawlor and her son “If you do not pay me I will spearhead a 14 month negative media campaign”. A small number of members have also chosen to be involved in this campaign. The most vocal of which are Rob Henshall, Ralph McCutcheon, Kieran McGuigan and George Hamilton. All these people are just puppets in the hands of those who cannot be named for the moment.

The easiest way to mount a company takeover is to start a negative media campaign. This serves two purposes to which there is no downside. Shares can be bought at rock bottom prices, way under asset value:

1- If enough shares are bought the Company can be taken over, then the negative campaign is stopped. Shares go up = huge profit, but only for the few who have orchestrated this negative campaign.

2- Should the Company survive and shares go back to their true value, that too is very profitable but again only for the few that have orchestrated this negative campaign. Others not knowing what is really going down would be too scared to buy.

I ask that you consider the following carefully. There is the asset and the potential. The asset is the oil coming out of the ground daily. Here it is the potential that is being manipulated, i.e. when confidence goes down, share prices go down. Confidence is already at a low ebb because of the present economic climate. Can you really be shouting from the rooftops “The Shares are Worthless”, and then expect someone to buy them from you? If there were any truth in this, would world renowned banks who have done all their Due Diligence, and let me assure you they are experts in this area, have agreed to a Loan Release of many millions in 2009, based on the asset if it were worthless?

Please further consider that the initial investors (members) invested $5 million US Dollars, and this has returned to the members approximately $28 million USD, $19 million USD in the sale of shares (units) and $9 million USD in the Loan Release Program. In December we plan to release another $10 million USD through the Loan Release Program (unless prevented by Mr. Paul Marriott) making a total benefit of $38 million USD to investors.

In the present economic climate, where else can anyone get that rate of return?

In the world, throughout history, the tail always seems to wag the dog. Similarly here, a small minority are loud and aggressive about their position while the silent majority just looks on.

Sincerely,

Susan Morrice

Chairperson

Shareholder
