Memorandum of Association for Abundant Life Galway

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Back in 2003 when Dialogue Ireland was assisting people in Tuam we became aware that there was some kind of body that included Victory in Dublin, King of King’s, Tuam and Abundant Life Galway. However, when enquiries were made the organisation seemed to evaporate. We show here again the Memorandum of Association. These show that at the beginning Pastor Hade from Victory was a director of the company. He subsequently resigned. Memorandum of Association 1998
