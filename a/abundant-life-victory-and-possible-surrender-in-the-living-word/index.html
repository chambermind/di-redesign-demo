Abundant Life, Victory and possible surrender in the Living Word!

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Now that the Victory takeover of Abundant Life is well underway, we have to ask ourselves the question what lies in wait for Brendan Hade and more importantly the current congregation of Abundant Life?

Currently we are well aware of the precarious financial situation of ALCC. Kevin and Heather Sanford have run the church over the last few years in a manner that makes the Fianna Fáil handling of the Celtic boom and subsequent collapse seem quite successful in comparison. It does seem unfair however to draw comparisons between an elected Government like Fianna Fáil and a self appointed dictatorship like the Sanfords. Unlike the Sanfords, Fianna Fáil were ultimately held to account by the body politic and democracy prevailed as a newly elected Government was given the reigns to fix the mess of their predecessors. We know that democracy does not exist in ALCC, as the Sanfords have shown contempt time and time again for the thoughts, wishes and opinions of their congregation. Their stance that democracy has no place in Christianity as theocracy should reign within a church structure is an ideology they consistently hammered into people to ensure they were never accountable to anyone and to tighten their control on all aspects of their members lives. With this in mind we see that the Sanfords have appointed their new Commander in Chief over Abundant Life, Brendan Hade without the input of church members. The church itself had no idea of the changeover until he was recently presented by the Sanfords to the church one Sunday morning, as its new leader.

In a democratic system the electorate get to analyze the policies and manifesto of the potential candidates before deciding their new allegiances. In this case we know that the members of Abundant Life have no say or idea of the policies or manifesto of the incumbent Brendan Hade or his Victory brigade. With this mind we wish to throw some light on current facts and potential outcomes for the church in keeping with Dialogue Ireland’s record of actually keeping ALCC members more informed than their own leaders who habitually operate in a clandestine fashion towards their congregation.

The church is struggling from a cash flow perspective. Brendan, being the experienced businessman that he is, will start to address this straight away. The excessiveness that the Sanfords employed in the church will be culled. We expect to see the church staff completely dissolved by the end of the year. Brendan may keep one of the current administrators on for continuity purposes but that should be it. The rest of the unusually large employed staff will be heading for the dole queue. Quite possibly we may see some of the staff emigrate to El Paso with Heather rather join the dole. You will note that we specified Heather and not Kevin as all involved with Abundant Life will know that the staff is employed primarily to meet the needs and requirements of Heather and not Kevin. Heather will be keen to take her well trained teachers, childminders, homemakers and Reliv operators* with her. Brendan Hade has no need of such redundant staffing and so the salary base will be reduced dramatically.

The next issue is the residence and asset base for the church. We know that the church is reported to be way behind in rent as well as owing excessive amounts to creditors so will Brendan assume liability for the outstanding credit? The answer to those with any sense of financial common sense is NO. Brendan will no doubt take the opportunity to nullify the existing credit lines and debts of Abundant Life by dissolving it as an entity. Abundant Life has no reason to stay afloat as an independent charity considering the operating structures of its now parent umbrella organisation VCF run by Brendan. We expect that the Abundant Life church will operate under Victory banner going forward.  This consolidation will also be a seamless transition for Brendan allowing his own staff to take the reins without complication and all revenues to be included in his current ledgers. This will help him prop up his own finances with Victory as he can now enjoy the tithes and offerings of at least 100 new members, a nice little earner for him!

Assuming that Abundant Life is dissolved in the near future, their creditors will easily eat up the asset base of the church. This would require the liquidators to be called in to satisfy the demands of creditors and many creditors will still not see a penny as there is simply not enough available in assets to pay the creditors. Owen Heffernan himself as been recently quoted as saying he has not received even a cent from Kevin despite the high court ruling. We expect there are many other creditors in the same boat.

Brendan Hade will be required to replenish the assets of the church once the migration to Victory is completed and most importantly he will need to provide for new premises. This will create a financial conundrum for Brendan as he will be required to provide serious up front financial commitment to the church to stabilise it from an asset and residency perspective. He will of course reap the dividends from the overly generous ex-ALCC crowd over a longer period but short time he will run a heavy deficit. We know he is experiencing financial difficulties at the moment within his Victory operation so the cash available is somewhat limited. This leaves us with 2 possible initial outcomes.

Firstly, the church may relocate to an even smaller and probably insufficient premises. This premises would lack the necessary assets required such as computers, lighting and music equipment, crèche equipment etc which in itself would be very expensive to replenish. The church would struggle for a long time operating in a barebones fashion which would be very discouraging to the current congregation who most probably already have itchy feet due to their current plight.

Another potential step could be the partnership with another local church. We know that Brendan Hade has close ministerial ties to Thomas Cooney, Pastor of another Pentecostal church in Galway called Living Word. Thomas shares the same ministerial roots and theological views of Brendan. Thomas and Brendan have enjoyed a good working relationship over the years and it is reported that surprisingly, Living Word has much closer connections to Victory than Abundant Life ever did. It is also reported that Living Word is undergoing its own struggles albeit on a much smaller scale to those of Abundant Life so a potential partnership between the newly branded Victory Church in Galway helmed by Brendan Hade and Living Word could be mutually beneficial. This would possibly involved the initial shared use of Living Word’s facility until Brendan could source his own facility and furnish it to a required standard. The question remains though would Thomas Cooney be willing to dilute his congregation with the toxic influence of Abundant Life members who still have not been vaccinated from the indoctrination of the Sanfords? It would be a risky move on Cooney’s part or indeed any other minister in Galway who would potentially entertain a contaminant like Abundant Life into their church, polluting and infecting their own congregation much like the Sanfords polluted and infected their followers…..Monkey See, Monkey Do!

All of this is just speculation however as no one, not even Brendan Hade can foresee how this is all going to play out in reality. We have merely constructed some potential outcomes considering the facts and evidence currently available. Dialogue Ireland has thus far been right on the money with its recent predications related to ALCC so it will be interesting to see looking back months from now, how prophetic these thoughts really are. Finally, why would Hade wish to take on this poisoned chalice? Surely, his own financial problems would keep him out of town? He is a venture capitalist who sees the distressed assets and wishes to build his empire from the failure of the Sanfords. Mutton dressed up as lamb. During the boom years the prosperity theology seemed to be true, Bertie Ahern’s codology with a spiritual veneer. Now it looks like a busted flush. Christians who advocate this view point seem totally off the wall in this current situation. This Nama or Celtic Tiger ideology is now shown for what it was all the time a diseased patient in the final stages of rigor mortis. Looking at Living Word’s web site it does not give the impression of being up to the challenge. Have we here a take over as well as an amalgamation? It is my intelligence from Galway that Living Word is also losing members. As I said they also represent the prosperity trajectory but with a quieter, Irish flavour and without the extreme control of members. Some from ALCC will find the transition to Living Word  easy. However, they will not know how to cope with the freedom and might go native and not need to use Covenant Eyes*.  The result will be that people will be able to find the truth of what has being going on by looking at our site or just talking to people about their experience!
