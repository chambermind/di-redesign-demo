Tony Quinn, Educo and INE ~ Appeasement and Settlement the order of the day.

Tony Quinn

A programme showing the charismatic style of leadership of Adolf  Hitler on BBC concluded not with a bang but with a whimper. Its summary of Hitler’s influence was as follows:

“Hitler had not hypnotised these people into supporting him, they had chosen to follow a leader.”

Possibly using hypnosis as criteria of influence in regard to Hitler was not a good choice of language. Then the bald statement that they had chosen to follow him revealed a naïve view of conditioning and brainwashing. Throughout the series one felt the story was undermining the commentary.

These reviews give insight into the phenomenon of cultism and a lens to look at Quinn through.

Though Quinn can’t be compared with Hitler there are certain traits like a lack of empathy that are comparable. After Hitler’s charisma failed he still believed his own propaganda when in reality he was living in a bubble in his bunker. Now Quinn does not have the same deprivations he is also living in his little bunker pronouncing on the next breakthrough while his former disciples are in a terrible state spiritually, ethically and materially.

The relevance here is that Quinn has been a Charismatic leader for over forty years.

This was his worst year from the point of view of influence. It is clear that his influence over his Educoists is declining. In 2009 at the RDS there was a significant audience and they were very cocky as they passed our picket and that we were just a joke.

http://dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/video/tq%20protest.html

The events of September at the Helix revealed that the Great Leader could not even arrive at the venue and was humbled by a small group of protesters.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/16/tony-quinn-educo-protest-pictures/

His SS members were still there but they were a forlorn group leaving the meeting and some had to be restrained from coming over to us. There had been a shift of power and Quinn was on the defensive.

He had been destroyed by Justice Bannister and his reputation was in tatters.

(Go to the category Tony Quinn and then click on the search button and put in Justice Bannister.)

The oil meeting where Susan valiantly tried to be masterful became a case of psychic collapse as she attempted to be her master’s voice. However, he was not there to hold her hand. She ducked and dived and exited.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/29/the-ine-agm-and-the-movement-towards-insanity-and-company-dissolution/

The question does arise  –  what is happening with all of the Quinn-Morrice court actions?  Are the cases now seen as so weak and without any real basis to pursue that even financially-minded lawyers might advise against them?  Is there such increasing objection from previously intimidated shareholders who are now finding their voices to object to more of their money being squandered by Quinn-Morrice for Quinn-Morrice’s benefits.  Are Quinn-Morrice now getting so worried about further rebellions from shareholders especially after Justice Bannister’s scathing comments about Quinn’s and Morrice’s and William’s behaviours?  Is there a sign of normality and non-cultist thinking taking hold in Morrice that she might question her Messiah’s strategy after so many criticisms and defeats in the courts and so much negative exposure as a result of having the guru and his actions revealed in the cold light of the courtroom outside of his cult bubble-world?  Is capitulation on the cards again or is the Quinn-Morrice legal campaign against Quinn’s enemies going to continue and will it be with using their own money for a change?

The oil story had been where the whole TQ EDUCO Empire had been for nearly a decade. We all know he was against it, but as soon as the oil flowed in he saw this as what one person told me, his opportunity to become a billionaire. The shift took place last year at the non AGM at Dublin Airport. Paul Marriot possibly the least Educoised of all decided to take Quinn and Morrice on. They were in denial and before they could wake up he had his settlement and was gone. He was an oil driller but refused to take on the role of bank rolling Cult Busters International. Some were annoyed with him but the B Shareholders were either attached still to Quinn or the strategy of the one shafted by him Sheila McCaffrey. She elected to follow Marriott’s example but got a hammering from Justice Bannister. She was left with a horrendous bill and because she could not detach herself from the conditioning of Quinn and seek a remedy due to undue influence her case was dead in the water.

All justice Bannister agreed with her on was that she was a Director and Quinn was not.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/07/30/judge-bannisters-judgement-read-it-for-yourself/

Both she and Morrice began a propaganda effort that would do justice to Goebbels. They interpreted the Bannister judgement to say the opposite of what it said. They refused to get off the stage and were pressing the flesh up to the last minute to get people to support them even though Bannister had told them both to vacate the joint and allow the B Shareholders a place in the sun.

The Battle of Bewley’s was the equivalent of Stalingrad for Susan but Sheila came in as the EGM saviour. She had managed to gate crash a B Shareholder meeting at Dublin Airport and become the official representative to such an extent that on the Spotlight programme we got a clear message that the main leaders of the B Resistance were joining forces with Sheila. Because they had been TQ Party members they could still not take the clear advice of Justice Bannister and go it alone. They were dependent Co dependants unwilling to grow up.

Regardless of that The Battle of Bewley’s represented the final events of the TQ Educo Cult. Yes some of the old guard tried to pack the room and shout out the praise of Tony, but you could see in their faces the game was up.

We had hoped that the Spotlight programme would represent a similar moment to the piece on Scientology from Panorama but it turned out to be about a cat fight between Susan and Sheila and the way in which these two educated women handed over shares and their minds to their Guru, and not about cultism.

The Judgement of Justice Hogan had set the tone by denying all this deposition bunkum, but more importantly he framed Quinn in the cult category and expressed the constitutional support of Educating the Public undertaken by Dialogue Ireland. So when the day comes for him to enter an Irish Court where he can not duck and dive as he has done outside Ireland he would be referenced clearly for what he is in reality a destroyer of souls of the Foolies and for the modern Cashies a bankrupter of dreams.

So there was silence and a shift as the protagonists regrouped after the AGM. DI felt the cold air coming from Siberia as we were seen as meddling in oil affairs rather than what we were doing which was showing the cultist dimensions of the oil story without fear or favour.  They have all lost the will to live and as a result the fire had gone out of all of this legal waffle. DI is now awaiting a Supreme Court decision to still try to depose Mike Garde to give evidence in this Denver case which was so urgent we were told Morrice needed a quick judgement from Desmond Hogan to facilitate an appeal in August. Instead of this after the Judgement in mid September they then waited as long as was possible before putting in the appeal at the end of October and it is now December and nothing is happening. Already all the deposition stuff has been dropped for a number of people and sanity is breaking out,  and so this Denver case looks like it is waiting for OAP bus pass.

This is our analysis, but now we go onto predict which is a very different animal and as we are not prophets will likely get the detail wrong:

  1. We believe the Cornec vs Morrice case will end in a settlement. Quinn has too many skeletons in the cupboard to be brought into the light if they continue to resist Justice Bannister. Maire Lalor and John Fennelly will likely be part of this case. Quinn will get Susan to get Maire’s claim for alleged sexual assault dealt with so he can come home to Ireland and not get a touch on the shoulder. The Master of SC’s Jim O’Callaghan and Paul Tweed will pull this together. My guess is that Sheila will be left out of this deal. It is remarkable that both Jim and Paul Tweed have been busy, Tweed with Louis Walsh and O’Callaghan with this case which must mark a new concept in representation?

  1. Quinn will get further shares from Susan and might have already got a pay out of the shares which Justice Bannister said are not his. Likely from intelligence sources he will try to do some deals with people around him who know where the bodies are buried. He already had a clean up like this in the early ‘80’s.

  2. If as reports suggest he has married Eve, he has to be careful to keep her sweet to hold onto the candy.

  3. The B Shareholders will continue for a bit longer like Godot waiting …. They will finally get themselves organised and seek professional help to address the influence they have been under. Hopefully as a group seek good legal advice without the A team and approach the bench in the person of Justice Edward Bannister.

  4. It currently looks like Quinn has got away with murder but finally the patience of the Eastern Caribbean Commercial Court  will be exhausted and the INE will be wound up.

  5. DI will continue to assist the victim’s of Cultism and hopefully we will find the oil episode becomes a strange chapter that dominated 2009 to 2012. We hope that all the parties who do settle might consider setting up a Trust fund to assist the victims of Cultism. We wish all concerned recovery and a life freed from this terrible shadow.
