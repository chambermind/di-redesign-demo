Fingalistan, Immigration, Islamicisation, Islamism or just helping Gerry out of Nama?

Conferences & Commentaries

This is a reply to the article published in the Sunday Times by Kevin Meyers

 Here Kevin Meyers poses a question in his essay on Sunday May 13 last :

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/05/13/liberal-elites-double-standard-hard-to-bare-kevin-meyers/

Let us examine his argument concerning  Clongriffin.

 

 

Ireland could have learnt from the British experience but, with a sublimely idiotic moral superiority, chose not to. Thus, north county Dublin is possibly about to undergo an irreversible demographic, cultural, racial and religious transformation, with the creation of a huge, Arab-funded mosque and Islamic centre at Clongriffin. The history of Islamic migration to Europe has usually followed the same cycle of small communities being established, which soon justify the construction of a physically dominant mosque, often with outside funding, followed by Islamicisation of the immediate neighbourhood. You may argue this is a good thing for what is currently a ghost estate, or a bad thing, or a mixed thing, but what it is not is something to be ignored. Instead, the proposed Clongriffin project was approved almost on the nod a first step, perhaps, to Fingalistan. Alarmist? Maybe. But consider what has happened in England. Birmingham is about to become a majority-Asian city, with Leicester soon to follow, and the first public burning of Rushdie’s Satanic Verses anywhere was before a 7,000-strong mob in Bolton.

Though we would agree with the fact that Meyers has actually given some attention to the issue of Clongriffin, let us unpack his argument a bit.

We do have some precedents to look at. First we have a post on the history of Islam in Ireland.*

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/12/09/islam-and-muslims-in-ireland/

We have already noted that with the Arab Spring the power balance in Islam in Ireland is shifting.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/04/24/appeal-against-clongriffin-mosque-tommy-broughan-td/

The original students who arrived from South Africa at the same time as Kadar Asmal one of the founder’s of the Irish Anti apartheid Movement set up a mosque next to the Garda Club on Harrington Street.* The next development was the move to the former Presbyterian Church on the SCR. The Muslim community that arrived was generally more educated than in Britain and were not part of end of Empire migrations experienced in Britain. So the analogy with the UK does not work. Also they moved into the former area inhabited by Jews like Clanbrassil St and the SCR. However, the next major shift was with the opening of the Clonskeagh Mosque around 20 years ago. There was a major discontinuity between the Muslim community in the inner city and the suburban mosque. In other words it did not respond to a felt need but was a trophy project like the one proposed for Clongriffin. Yes it had a primary school, but the secondary area was not addressed and most of the young Muslims were integrated into Irish life by attending Catholic secondary schools. The Convent schools suited Muslims as they were single sex. There is no evidence that there is a major transformation of the area as Kevin suggests. It is more like a Bantustan than South Dublinistan.

Significantly, there is no balancing countermovement of Christians into Muslim countries; no flowering of ecclesiastical architecture in Pakistan or Indonesia to cope with the religious needs of immigrants.

The savage persecution of Christians continues in these lands. In Iraq many Christians were murdered and the EU did very little to protect them. In fact Turkey was more welcoming of Christian refugees than European countries. Why would there be a counter movement of Christians to these lands? The reason people move is based on their religious affiliation but rather to do with economic survival. I really feel this sentence is really quite strange.

 The only societies that permanently admit huge numbers of outsiders of entirely different racial, religious and cultural backgrounds are of Western European Christian stock, either in the EU or the US, where the power of liberal elites is deeply entrenched. China doesn’t do it. Nor Japan. Nor Russia. Nor India, Iran, nor any African or Arabic countries.

Christianity is based on the Abrahamic idea that we are all at some stage exiles and outsiders who are allowed to join the story and become insiders. However, in regard to Clongriffin I see the real issue is not Islamisation but rather developer greed and the need for Gerry Gannon to off load a property going no where. Why DCC has not got information about who is behind this project is remarkable.

So Kevin there is little to worry about in Clongriffin.  However, as this comes to fruition it is important  to remember we all started as people who needed to be brought in from the periphery to the centre.
