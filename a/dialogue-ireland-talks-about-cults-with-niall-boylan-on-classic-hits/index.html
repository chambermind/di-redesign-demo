Dialogue Ireland talks about “Cults” with Niall Boylan on Classic Hits.

CultismMaoist CultScientologyScientology Community CentreThe Burke family

The Director of Dialogue Ireland, Mike Garde, appeared on Ireland’s Classic Hits Radio’s Niall Boylan programme last night to talk about Cultism.

Dialogue Ireland prefers to talk about cultism, and the behaviours within nefarious groups. Readers interested in the use of the word “Cult” and how it refers to cultism should refer to Garde’s 2006 Dissertation Study which goes into detail in Chapter 2.

Boylan is the winner of multiple IMRO Irish Radio awards for his “Interactive Speech” programming.

Topics discussed include “What is a Cult?”, “is it ”weak-minded” people who join cults?” (which Garde vehemently disputes citing examples) and cultism within modern-day life.

In the interview Garde refers to the Burkes, who have been covered on Dialogue Ireland recently.

Garde refers to the Lambeth (South London) Workers’ Institute of Marxism–Leninism–Mao Zedong Thought commune Cult, also known as “Jackie”, headed by Aravindan Balakrishnan.

In November 2023 RTÉ’s Prime Time broadcast a programme covering this group, and also addresses the cultic phenomena in Ireland.

Dialogue Ireland participated in this programme.
