Could she be telling porkies? Christina and the US Houses.

House of Prayer

The revelations in recent years regarding the House of Prayer raise issues about the truth claims they make.

In June of this year the House of Prayer stated:

“People who chose to respond to Our Lady’s request are the ones who, as with the Achill House of Prayer,  put in place the houses for the mission in the US and obtained a charitable trust, and Christina Gallagher has absolutely nothing to do with the houses of prayer in America, nor has she at any time ever benefited from them. On invitation, she will go there to speak about the messages, where her fare and upkeep are taken care of, but she gains nothing financially from her time there.”

June statement “Christina Gallagher has never sought to raise money. She has not gone about raising money, as is still alleged, nor did she ever approach anyone asking for money”

D.I recently uncovered an official document in which it stated the names of the Directors of the chain of houses in USA. Here it states the opposite of the above, namely Christina is a Director?
