The esoteric and exoteric dimensions of Tabor.

Tabor Institute

Following the article in the Sunday Times by Colin Coyle we feel that a few pictures are worth a thousand words.  These are taken from Tabor Photos of the Course found on the public site for Tabor on Face Book.   We show them to illustrate the esoteric and exoteric dimensions in Tabor. To protect the privacy of those who no longer wish to be associated with Tabor their images have been removed.

The two faces of Jeanette Rigney and John Sherwin of Tabor

Tabor Psychotherapy Course is unlike any other psychotherapy course in that it celebrates two Graduation Days.  Take a few moments to contrast and compare the two days. The first picture shows the Graduation that is held in private, which means that it is closed to outsiders or family members, and takes place in a Cairn on the grounds of  Dunderry House.  Everybody is dressed in white ceremonial clothes for the ceremony, and a blend of Shamanic and  Corandero  rituals are carried out by Jeanette Rigney with the students  (just like a High Priestess), and she is attended by John Sherwin in a lesser role.

The second Graduation Day is an open affair that may be attended by the families of  the graduates.  This ceremony takes place in the main room of Dunderry House.  The faculty and students are all dressed in the regular attire of cap and gowns, like regular academics.  Jeanette Rigney looking more like the course Director than High Priestess.

The altar for the graduation day for 4th year students.  Students had to wear clothes that were white with red accessories for this graduation day (which was closed to families, a second separate day was arranged for that, with cap & gowns).  She knew that it would be seen as a cult if it was witnessed by outsiders.

Students waiting to have their feet washed by Jeanette Rigney (an act of humility like that performed by Jesus Christ at the last Supper?)  Yet look how elaborate the altar in the middle is…. humility?
