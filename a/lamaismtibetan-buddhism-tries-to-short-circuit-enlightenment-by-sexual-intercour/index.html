Explanation: In Buddhist thought:

“Realisation” refers to the fact that when Hinayana practitioners attain the highest level of enlightenment, which is the fourth level, they have attained liberation; in other word, they have attained the final level by going through all the stages, (via the first, second, third levels.) That’s the proper sequence for small vehicle practitioners, via the first, second, etc. to attain “realisation”.
