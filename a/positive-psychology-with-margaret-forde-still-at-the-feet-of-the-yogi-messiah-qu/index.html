Subject: Positive Psychology with Margaret Forde
Date: Wed, 3 Feb 2010 14:55:44 +0000
Are you fed up with all the talk of gloom and doom? Like a new beginning in 2010? Would you like to become part of the solution instead of hearing constantly about the problems?
This is precisely the focus of new courses in Positive Psychology and Relaxation with psychologist Margaret Forde. Positive Psychology is a whole new movement that builds on what is right with a person. So expect topics such as becoming more resilient, maximising existing strengths, developing an optimistic thinking style, how to create more happiness, utilising flow in work or leisure, using the law of attraction.
“Most of the people who come to my workshops can recite the material from all the self-help books they have read” says Margaret, “but it doesn’t actually change the way they operate. The participatory nature of this course enables people to change their actual way of operating on a day to day basis. Add in relaxation and meditation and you have a complete stress-busting package. ”
Margaret runs six week courses in Dublin city centre and one-day workshops throughout the country, starting with Athlone on 6th Feb.
The Dublin venue is Ely House, 8 Ely Place, Dublin 2. Date: Wed. 24 February 8.30pm. Fee: €120

YOGA

“I found it in a very unexpected direction. I took up yoga as a form of physical exercise and was lucky enough that my first yoga teacher was Tony Quinn, who thro meditation had experienced the state of yoga, and the energy flow that comes from it. I was delighted to learn that the“ peak states” that Maslow and Rogers talked about were actual and experienced by all the great yogis, and that traditional yoga  was a step by step system to calmthe whirlpools of the mind and achieve inner freedom. As I immersed myself in the philosophy and practice of yoga, I was amazed to discover that when the mind became still, I could come in touch with a higher power  and there was a movement, a flow of life , an intelligence way beyond normal thinking from which you could draw inspiration and guidance.”
