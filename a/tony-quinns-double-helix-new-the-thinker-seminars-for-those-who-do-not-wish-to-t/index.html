Dear Cliff,

Your next Good Life Club Meeting with Tony is on Sunday January 6th 2013 at the Helix, Dublin.

Tony has just completed his two New ‘The Thinker’ Seminars. You will be able to meet and hear the people who were on these  in various meetings, and if not you will most certainly see them there on the Sunday Meeting.

Never before has anyone ever fully drawn out ‘The Thinker’, this is a whole new evolutionary step. Do you fully realise the life and world changing power of ‘The Thinker’? It is a complete alternative approach to living, a whole new virgin field. Truly the most exciting breakthrough ever in the history of mankind.

Everyone up to now has tried to change the circumstances and situations around them, instead here we ask that as a first step you control your thoughts, swapping the negative ones for the positive ones, unhappy for happy, and so on. Then of course you need to stay awake to the thoughts, so you don’t lapse back into them.

We all need to update ourselves and get on the same page now, where we sing the same song, the Song of Life, and others will begin to see and recognise it. This is where we can inspire everyone we know for a better life effortlessly, and it’s great fun.

Tony continues to put everything he has into this, in order to make the biggest difference for us all, it is his whole life’s passion. He will send you a letter closer to the day.

Make sure to come along and participate in this life and world changing day.

Lots of love,

Mary
