23 February 2011

Marcia Fox INE/BNE shareholder writes to Prime Minister Barrow

Tony Quinn

Dear Prime Minister Barrow:

You are of course aware of the great concern of stakeholders in INE/BNE;  representing a 10% stakeholder you have to be as equally concerned, if not more, as are other stakeholders about the total absence of fiduciary responsibility and lack of transparency in the operation of this company.   Although of course you may not have any legal standing to address this matter, you do have a moral one to protect the GOB’s interest by taking overt action and thereby not appearing to endorse this illegal behavior.

Also, something distressful for me to write, and no doubt for you to hear, is that rumors are rampant that the GOB is part of this cover up.   Situations such as government members attending guru cult leader Tony Quinn’s seminars on a private island, high-end wining and dining, gifts galore all paid for by INE/BNE, but never disclosed to members or found on financial reports serve only to compound these rumors.  This all adds up to great concern for stakeholders and the GOB (Government of Belize,) as a10% stakeholder in this company must surely feel similarly.

The stakeholders are those who realized this windfall in Belize but it seems that every one other than these investors are benefiting from the malfeasance of the company.

The Board had one ratified member, Susan Morrice.   It appears that Susan Morrice is besotted by this cult leader Tony Quinn, to whom she gifted $26 million in shares and brought him onto the Board without consultation or ratification by stakeholders.  No benefit passed to the company.

The Operating Agreement itself has been changed over the years without ratification by stakeholders (as required by the Agreement itself).   None of Susan Morris’s board appointments have ever been ratified (also required by the Operating Agreement).  This and her actions generally have raised questions about her stability and more importantly her ability to run the company which she appears to operate as a private bank.  In the best interest of the company, the majority of stakeholders believe she should step aside so the company, and indeed she herself, can recover.

To say that this operation is outrageous in its total disregard for its stakeholders (GOB included), its Operating Agreement and the laws of incorporation of the country of Nevis (where the company is registered) is too easy.   In the best interest of GOB’s place in all of this, action does need to be taken by the GOB –and indeed we stakeholders need to know where the GOB stands on this issue.     To this end we would urge the GOB to send a representative to the AGM in Ireland (3rd March at the Radisson Blu Hotel, Dublin Airport at 2 p.m.)  to hear and indeed demand clarification of the many concerns of and by the stakeholders.   Incidentally, every stakeholder should have received notice of this meeting, but in fact only selective stakeholders have been advised.

In the past these meetings have been a farce, no agenda, no question time allowed, no explanation of finances and members have even been thrown out of a meeting if they dared to ask any questions.  In truth they serve only as a platform for cult leader Tony Quinn’s enterprises.    Your representative can ask questions without fear of being thrown out of the meeting – Mr. Prime Minister we need the GOB to step up and fully represent and protect the people’s 10% stake in our company and demonstrate to all of its stakeholders that the GOB will not tolerate such gross malfeasance.

As for dividends, they don’t exist.  Yes, there have been payouts (maybe the better word is payoffs) for a small number of special people.  In this context Mr. Prime Minister what payments has the GOB received in relation to its shares?   You see, stakeholders have had to borrow their own money (called a Loan Release Program) while monies have been flying out of the company, most noticeably to Susan Morris, Tony Quinn, Patricia Usher and Josh Stewart (Susan Morrice’s nephew).    And, adding insult to injury, a stakeholder had to be deemed ‘worthy’ by cult leader Quinn  to be eligible for this program.   In addition, the stakeholder had to release their original share certificates.  In my considerable business experience, I have never seen such egregious behavior as this ‘money grabbing’ entity.

Stakeholders question why they have to ‘borrow’ their own money from a company that is purported to be doing better than ever.   This is fast approaching a ¾ billion USD in sales organization – so what is happening to all of this money.

It is difficult to appreciate how the GOB cannot be concerned about the operation of this company; enough information has been brought to your attention from various sources to instigate  an immediate investigation by the Attorney General  – in the absence of any earlier action on the part of the GOB, it is important now to demonstrate where the GOB stands on this fraudulent company. This is not a matter of taking sides Mr. Prime Minister, but of diligent stewardship due by all stakeholders in INE/BNE

Most sincerely

Marcia Fox

INE/BNE shareholder
