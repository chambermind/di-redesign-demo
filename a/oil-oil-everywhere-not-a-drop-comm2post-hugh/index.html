Tony Quinn

Oil, Oil everywhere not a drop Comm2post Hugh

2010/01/19 at 8:33pm

Anonymous, you say –

How can so many people hold one guy responsible for so much? It’s not like a dirty politician who swindles the system and steals from public funds, normal people seem to be handing Quinn their money and then blaming him for taking it. All this talk of “enlightenment”, I don’t know what ye thought ye were in for but if you just hand someone your money you can’t expect to ask for it back.

Reply – Yes! he’s more of a swindler than a dirty politician. He’s done even worse than fiddle his expenses from the public purse and taken bribes. He’s taken relatively large sums of money from trusting individuals for very little benefits according to many. Deception and false claims, manipulation of people financially, emotionally and sexually that’s wrecked minds and lives for over 40 years is disgusting behaviour. Noticeably you fail to condemn Quinn and in the process you exonerate him – that’s shameful. Regarding the talk of “enlightenment” which you raise, what’s wrong with expecting someone to deliver on their promise to teach a spiritual or philosophical system with some substance. Clearly, people have realised that Quinn’s teachings are very lacking in depth and effectiveness. Good on them and good on them for speaking up about it. You say they shouldn’t ask for their money back. Why not? Clearly again they disagree and feel they’re entitled to a refund or at least to register their objections and complaints.

Anonymous, you say –

As for this shares business, I have little to no sympathy for it. Investing in stock is no different than gambling and it’s just your own bad luck if you never knew it. You take the hand that’s dealt to you and then get over it. Ye are all responsible for what you did with your own money, nobody else.

Reply – The problems have nothing to do with share prices going up and down. That’s another red herring and way off topic. The real central arguments here include the following – the way the company is being ‘run’, bringing a notorious cult leader into the company’s operations, the lack of due financial rewards, etc.

You say you have no sympathy for Quinn’s victims. That’s obvious. It’s also obvious where you’re sympathies lie.

Ye could ask Quinn to take responsibility for ripping people off and to change his wicked ways. I doubt that you will do it but Quinn’s big ripping-off days are likely to be very few now outside of the oil business.
