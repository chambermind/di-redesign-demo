Report from the other side

The Oily Guru has indeed offered his “services” to reverse the recession in Ireland. In his great “wisdom” he has decided the recession was essentially due to the Irish people’s wrong mindset which he has been struggling to change for nearly 40 years. He says they changed their “negative” mindset for a while thereby causing the Celtic Tiger which, believe it or not, he’s also tried to take much of the credit for because he says they were thinking along the lines he’s suggested in the Blueprint over the years but the “foolies” (anybody except him) have now slipped back into their old “negative” thinking. Not much consideration or understanding here about banking collapses worldwide based on faulty risk-calculation models with excessive risk-taking by banks that lost huge sums of money facilitated by a climate of lack of monitoring / regulation of banks in an atmosphere of easy credit and greed (the mucky one should know about these), unwise purchasing and speculation among the public and inflated house prices. No mention of these from the “cult business” guru; Quinn’s analysis (and solution) is brilliant IF you’ve had a lobotomy, especially as he’s sent at least hundreds into their own private recessions over the decades (which many are still too embarrassed to mention), so he’s a real expert on greed, easy credit, crazy business expansion, etc – in short he’s much more of a recession maker than a recession breaker.

The guru (spiritual, business, relationships, etc – take your pick) said “mindset” means you’re mind is set. The crude implication is you should abandon your “negative” thinking and adopt his “positive” methods (“surrender” your mind to me – one of his favourite pieces of advice). He hasn’t understood the “set” refers to a set of assumptions, methods, etc but then academic achievements weren’t exactly his strength either. So far, the only substantial result he has achieved is running a cult which it turns out, according to psychology, is one of the easiest tricks in the world. The cult guru is now being kept afloat on a wave of OIL as the Educo “success” seminars have been dramatically downsizing even when people are being coerced by several seriously abusive methods to attend the jaw-droppingly self-indulgent, half-baked speeches.

It’s soon to be the 40th birthday of the Quinn-Yoga-Educo cult. “Messiah”, you’ll be at pensioner age then in 2011 and perhaps the youngster, Eve, will have an extra large cheesy cake for this double celebration if you’re both still there in the old hideaway in the Bahamas. First though come back to cold and wet Dublin and try to explain away some of your secret sexual misdeeds to the court. I doubt that hardly anybody other than a handful of old trusties will believe your version of events, the majority of these know the truth anyway and have been trying to cover up for you for years.

Happy Birthday, O Mucky, Oily “Messiah” at 64.
