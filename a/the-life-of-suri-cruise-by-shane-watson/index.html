The life of Suri Cruise

Shane Watson

Suri Cruise, daughter of Katie Holmes and Tom Cruise, is the most fascinating figure on the celebrity circuit right now. She’s got it all — the cute looks, the Hollywood pedigree, the jet-set lifestyle, the £2m designer wardrobe, the weird culty background. Best of all, Suri is our favourite contender in the “How will the story pan out?” competition, beating Jennifer Aniston hands down. We look at her and all we can think (apart from, “Can those shoes be legal?”) is, “What in God’s name will she be like when she’s 18?” Because Miss Cruise, in case you’d forgotten, is three years old. And something about her little-lady manner, and a life lived in the reality-challenged bubbles of celebrity and Scientology have made her a kid whose future we can’t help wondering about. Here’s how we think the next 30 years might look.

Suri at eight Is starring alongside Dad — Dakota Fanning style — in his blockbuster directorial debut, Tsunami: 2014. Tom allowed his daughter to take part because the story has a heavy-handed moral message and she is a natural in front of the camera. (Katie, however, is not permitted to be in the movie on account of the bikini scenes.) Designers are clamouring to give Suri her own label (Little Miss Ralph Lauren). Tom Kat compromise with a Vanity Fair shoot with the Obama girls (“The three most influential under 20”) and have agreed to Suri being the face of Hubbard Health Drinks.

Suri at 14 Has not been seen in public for a year, since she was a presenter at the Oscars. Rumours are, variously, that she has been placed in isolation during puberty, that her weight has ballooned, that she and Katie were caught dancing uninhibitedly to gangsta rap and have been sent to a Scientology reprogramming unit. Prior to this, Katie and Suri had taken to wearing veils in public.

Suri at 18 Now at Yale, Suri lives separately from the other students and is rumoured to have specially purified air pumped into her living quarters. Since she started college (where she is to be seen wearing the preppy uniform of kilt and Fair Isle cardigan, plus antiviral mask), the campus has been designated a no-fly zone, and boys rated “attractive to very attractive” have been tagged. Suri (who has forsworn all stimulants, according to her father) does a Chloe Madeley towards the end of her first term and is filmed lying in a gutter wearing a squirrel suit and fake breasts.

Suri at 25 Has secretly married Blanket Jackson in a Las Vegas ceremony and been outed as the anonymous blogger of My Dad Thinks He’s a Thetan. In a move that is now referred to as Britney’s law, Tom has assumed control of her finances and all her affairs, including banning a one-woman art show said to contain nude images. This is being contested by her lawyers.

Suri at 33 No longer married to Blanket (which lasted only weeks), Suri is living at an animal sanctuary with all the Jolie-Pitt children, Romeo Beckham and other celebrity offspring who call themselves the “La-La survivors”. She has rejected her inheritance and penned a critically acclaimed autobiographical novel called The Tot in the Trotters, which has been optioned by Oprah’s estate. Tom and Kat are quite old by now, so there are no hard feelings.
