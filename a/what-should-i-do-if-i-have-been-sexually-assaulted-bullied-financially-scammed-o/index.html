What should I do if I have been sexually assaulted, bullied, financially scammed or brought under undue influence in Japa?

HinduJapa YogaTherapyUncategorized

As many of you are aware we have been for nearly 20 years making people aware of the sexual abuse and violence of the Cult of Tibetan Buddhism, which we prefer to call Lamaism. In fact, it is unreformed Tantric sexual Hinduism. From reports we have been receiving over the past 6 months, what appears to be happening in Japa has many of the characteristics of this type of exploitive group.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/buddhist/tibetan-buddhism-lamaism/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/buddhist/rigpa/

This centre run by Sogyal Rinpoche in West Cork had the support of the former President Mary McAleese who opened it in 2007. This Lama has totally disappeared from the centre leaving a white elephant of a temple project and those leading the place act as if he was never there. He has been removed from their web site.

https://www.dzogchenbeara.org/

Now as the article above shows he is in total disgrace. However, you must understand these Lamas will bounce back and so as you come to terms with the fact you have been brought under undue influence you must recognise you are normal. Nothing that happened to you is normal. You did not bring this on yourself and you must get the strength to recognise you have been wronged and realise the use of magical manipulation to make you think they can get into your head is part of the brainwashing system you have been subject to.

You will note that Dennis is trying to rebrand Japa and has taken down the Japa web site. It is an empty shell. They are now on the run and I will in due course respond to the latest reports about sexual assault. We have been aware of them since day one, but now the floodgates are beginning to open.

When the Nazis lost power it is clear that some in leadership tried to pretend they had nothing to do with Hitler as there was chaos in the retreat.

We will need to go through every teacher and check their story. Do not accept any protestations of innocence or on the other hand do not point the finger at any of them. Everyone is innocent until proved guilty. Please do not tell me if you were in leadership you did not know what was going on? Reading the reports of our eyewitnesses who have written about what happened, if you wanted to see what was going on you would, should and could.

Some suggestions to assist men or women who have been the victims of sexual assault, common assault, violence or bullying.

  1. If the Japa teacher you were with has left do not continue with them until we have a clear idea what their role was and did they, in fact, benefit financially or sexually from involvement with Japa. Many will claim they had no involvement whatsoever, check that out.

  2. Seek professional help. That is not with any of the therapists or psychotherapists who were connected to Japa.

    Get help from people who have independent training which is secular and not connected to any of these Japa style practitioners. In fact, the evidence I am getting is that most of them succumbed to the undue influence of Dennis and Shashi who were deified and believed to have magic powers.

    This will help you to understand how influence works and will weaken the magical influence you are under.
    https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/about/cultism/

  3. Realise if you have been violated in the very core of your being this is not your fault. Have a read of this document which will help you improve your boundaries and give you a better sense of who you are.
    https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/05/31/is-sex-between-a-spiritual-teacher-and-students-harmful/

  4. Garda HQ and the community police are fully aware of the Japa situation. Be very alert that no one tries to induce victims to make statements as a group.

    Each victim must make a statement at their own local Garda Station, but once that is done some connection can be made to link these cases, but not in a way that would be regarded as a conspiracy.

    Visit the Rape Crisis Centre nearest you to receive assistance with a personal recovery plan.

    It is imperative that any therapist who was involved with Japa immediately withdraws from gathering names or approaching victims.  It is very important for victims to keep their distance from them. Boundaries are completely confused, so develop new ones.

  5. The Protection of Vulnerable Adults is a service of the HSE which is very pertinent to your situation.

    Each region of the country has people who can help.

    https://www.hse.ie/eng/services/publications/corporate/vulnerablepersonsfaq.pdf

    “What alleged abuse situations are covered by this policy?

    Types of Abuse Physical abuse includes hitting, slapping, pushing, kicking, misuse of medication, restraint or inappropriate sanctions.

    Sexual abuse includes rape and sexual assault, or sexual acts to which the vulnerable person has not consented, or could not consent, or into which he or she was compelled to consent.

    Psychological abuse includes emotional abuse, threats of harm or abandonment, deprivation of contact, humiliation, blaming, controlling, intimidation, coercion, harassment, verbal abuse, isolation or withdrawal from services or supportive networks.

    Financial or material abuse includes theft, fraud, exploitation, pressure in connection with wills property, inheritance or financial transactions, or the misuse or misappropriation of property, possessions or benefits.

    Neglect and acts of omission include ignoring medical or physical care needs, failure to provide access to appropriate health, social care or educational services, the withholding of the necessities of life such as medication, adequate nutrition, and heating.

    Discriminatory abuse includes ageism, racism, sexism, that based on a person’s disability, and other forms of harassment, slurs or similar treatment.”

  6. I would suggest that once people are clear in their own minds we should seek the assistance of a university or other such location and hold a day conference which would consist of victims only and their family members. Here sharing of their stories in a safe environment might process the elements needed to address this major calamity in your lives.

    Dialogue Ireland would be willing to make the arrangements but we would have to find a way to fund such an event, but we would have contact with a couple of therapists who would be willing to share with you over the day. A loose committee could be formed and issues of a legal nature could be discussed.

    One can’t take a group action in this jurisdiction but hopefully, legal advice might clarify what can be done. We might be able to attract some legal and psychological support pro bono.

    We can dream, but one thing is sure Dialogue Ireland will not try to recruit you to anything other than your own freedom. Furthermore, as some of these sexual assaults took place in India we would have to see what the Gardai suggest in regard to an international warrant for the arrest of Shashi.

    Obviously, if he enters any part of Europe you would have no problem, but in India, he is likely protected by people with very serious power. Remember though this all depends on individuals making statements themselves. Nothing can happen till that takes place.
