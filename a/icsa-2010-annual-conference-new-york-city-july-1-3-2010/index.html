ICSA 2010 Annual Conference – New York City – July 1-3, 2010

With the Collaboration of Info-Cult/Info-Secte, Montreal, Canada

This conference is for:

o        former group members

o        people born or raised in cultic groups

o        families of group-involved persons

o        helping professionals

o        researchers

o        other people interested in cults and psychological manipulation

More than 90 speakers from 14 countries.  Speaker Biographical Sketches (if the preceding and subsequent links do not work for you, copy and paste the Web address into your browser: http://icsahome.com/infoserv_conferences/conference_speakers_2010_ny.asp

Location: Doubletree at George Washington Bridge (2117 Route 4 East, Fort Lee, NJ) – across New York’s Hudson River

Special sleeping room rate: $99/night (available 2 days before and after conference for those wishing to tour New York).  Hotel provides a shuttle to and from Manhattan.
