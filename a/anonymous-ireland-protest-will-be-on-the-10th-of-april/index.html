ANONYMOUS Ireland protest will be on the 10th of April

EducationHuman RightsScientology

The emergence of Anonymous Ireland has given a massive boost to those who are victims of Scientology and their families, by their dogged persistence in raising awareness about cultist groups. Not only have they raised the profile of those seeking to protect the human rights of our citizens, they have by implication given a massive injection of energy to the work of those of us who are working in the field of cultism. It is interesting that the Times of London saw the significance of this movement in defence of freedom of expression and human rights. Irish society has nearly mirrored a cultist group in that our banking, governmental structures and political system have failed to protect our citizens. That is the reason that the work of Anonymous has remained nearly hidden within Irish society.
