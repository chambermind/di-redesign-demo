The feminisation of power and clustering in Craughwell – Abundant Life

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

For sometime Dialogue Ireland has become aware of a little commune forming near Craughwell in Co. Galway. Members of Abundant life Church are moving to be close to their pastors. This is a very significant development for any group with high intensity group dynamics.

The picture above shows Joyce Meyer and her family compound in the US. Is this what Abundant life hopes to achieve someday from teaching the prosperity gospel?? Joyce Meyer is a favourite for the ladies in Abundant life who are having the annual ‘Ladies Conference’ with Judy Jacobs this week. As with all such groups, even though Kevin Sandford appears as the Leader in reality there is dominant female who is usually the leader. Are we seeing this same trend develop with Heather Sanford at abundant life? This is a possible reaction to Churches where women were not allowed leadership roles. This too was very much the case in Tuam with Mary Sheridan, or in Hillsong in Australia.

Here’s the extended photo caption for this picture by Robert Cohen of the St. Louis Post-Dispatch: “Joyce Meyer Ministries bought these 5 homes for Meyer and her family. The Ministry pays all expenses, including landscaping and lawn care, property taxes and rehab work. Meyer, her husband and each of their four married children live in the homes, free of charge.”
