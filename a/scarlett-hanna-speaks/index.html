Scarlett Hanna speaks

Scientology

Scarlett Hanna, an ex-member of Scientology’s Cadet Org (think the Sea Org but for children), tells her story to ABC Lateline. She is the daughter of the current president of Scientology in Australia.

Embedded media (opens the original source)

For the Four Corners documentary click here

The official Scientology spokesperson in the above piece is Virginia Stewart. Her father just recently posted this to the Ex-Scientology Message Board:

>>Virginia Stewart from the Church of Scientology in Sydney denies the church routinely separates family members.

“I don’t actually agree it’s been routinely done and I think in that instance in that family you would have to speak to the father and the mother about why they lived like that, why they chose to take that action,” she said<<

This is a complete crock. I am Virginia’s father, and she phoned me on the day she was joining the SO, and said she was sorry, but she could not longer communicate with me as she was joining the SO.

And she has kept that decision, that was in 1988 and I haven’t had any communication from her since – 22 years later!

I even sent her a great gift for her 40th birthday, but not even a thank you card.

I was granting her her wish to do what she wanted, but this pisses me off. If my statement is wanted for the Oz enquiry, I’m now willing to speak up.

REgards, Allen
