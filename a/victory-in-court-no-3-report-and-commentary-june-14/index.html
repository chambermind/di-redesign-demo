Victory in Court No. 3: Report and Commentary June 14

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Disclaimer: Dialogue Ireland is not concerned about the beliefs of Victory or does it have concerns about these. Our only concern is undue influence and we only reference theological issues in relation to cultism.

The Trustees of Victory Christian Fellowship http://www.victory.ie/
Victory Christian Fellowship
Firhouse Road, Tallaght, Dublin 24
Tel – +353 1 461 0056 …… and the Victory Centre http://www.vcc.ie/

have avoided being sent to prison after agreeing to obey courts orders not to obstruct bank appointed receivers from taking possession of three properties in Dublin.
Yesterday at the High Court Brendan Hade and his wife Shelia and Gerry Byrne, who are trustees of the Victory Christian Fellowship, told Mr Justice Paul Gilligan they would not obstruct receivers from taking possession of properties at Kilmacud House, Kilmacud Road, Upper Stillorgan; Westland Row and Firhouse Road, Tallaght.



The three were ordered to appear before the Court over an alleged organised campaign to prevent receivers-chartered accountants Paul McCann

http://www.grantthornton.ie/Ourteam/PaulMcCann

and Patrick Dillon from gaining access and taking possession of the premises.

http://www.grantthornton.ie/OurTeam/Patrick-Dillon

The receivers claimed those orders were ignored and the trustees and unnamed others continued to prevent them gaining possession.
Arising out of the trustee’s alleged failure to comply or to appear at court to deal with the allegations the High Court made orders directing that the three be brought before the court over alleged contempt.
Earlier this week Gardai, in order to enforce the court’s attachment orders, made a series of dawn raids at the trustees’ homes, but were unable to locate them. Yesterday the three, who the court heard were not in Garda custody, appeared before Mr Justice Gilligan.

This a remarkable story and it is interesting that the very people that Victory needed to be in touch with to work out an agreement to continue to use the facilities were the very ones they refused to cooperate with!

The three, who were not legally represented, gave undertakings to Mr Justice Gilligan to obey the orders the Judge who said he was satisfied they “had purged their contempt.” Mr Brendan Hade denied that they had broken any laws. He expressed concerns about being able to conduct church services can be conducted, and for the employment of the 30 people working in the premises. They intended to get legal representation, Mr Hade said.

They started to use the same legal jargon as Brian Hade as if they had found some magic way to represent themselves. They were out of their depth within minutes. We have broken no law, is this criminal or civil contempt as if that made any difference. Gerry Byrne kept making statements contesting counsel for the receivers by stating it is alleged. Again the irony was lost on them that they had not contested the receivers so had no grounds to resist. They did not seem to understand that the Justice Sean Ryan’s judgement was in fact Ex Parte. It was brought by the Receivers without the notification of Victory Centre/Church. In order to overturn it they had to challenge it. Instead of doing this they fled…… The Judge did suggest they might consider representation and Brendan Hade told him they had instructed Gerard Black Solicitors. However, having explained that they could come back to the court in a few weeks the Trustees thought the order relating to the Receiver would be suspended. No this order was an interim one, and would become final on July 2. When Brendan Hade understood the import of this he asked then if he could cancel his challenge?

The Judge also directed the three properties be handed over next Monday afternoon.  The receivers were appointed last month over the Church’s properties by Bank of Scotland over failure to repay some €18m loans but have been prevented access to the buildings.

Gerry Byrne tried to use his members needs to get a sympathy vote, and to request that the Judge arrange for them to obtain the use of the buildings, but Justice Gilligan stated that was not his remit. He said, that was in the gift of the Receivers, the very people they were resisting.

They then obtained temporary injunctions against the trustees, and two of the Hade’s sons, Niall and Brian, requiring them hand over possession of the premises and restraining interference with the receivers.

Note that the injunctions are temporary against the trustees but unless contested become final on July 2.  On Wednesday the brothers, following their arrest by Gardai, agreed to comply with the orders.

This was reported on Wednesday last.

The receivers proceedings against Mr Niall Hade have been discontinued.

It is the understanding of  DI that Mr Niall Hade has no involvement with Victory in any manner.

Yesterday Brendan and Shelia Hade, Fountain Head, Rockbrook, Rathfarnham Dublin or Mr Byrne, of Woodstown, Knocklyon, Dublin said they would comply with the orders and agreed that the premises should be handed over peacefully  to the receivers.

 The resistance crumbled faced with the reality of going to prison. Suddenly the consequences of their actions came home to them.

What had happened has caused great upset to church members- some 1000 people, the court heard. Concerns were also expressed about future pray services and events going ahead. Gerry Byrne said the organisation was “not a business but a church.”

This of course begs the question what they are? The Revenue Commissioners took the view that they had breached the terms of their charitable status and revoked it. In other words under the guise of being a church they were in fact running businesses which had no charitable impulse. When a Trust obtains charitable status it is not required to publish accounts but only has to make a report to the Revenue Commissioners. These are not available to the public. So my observation is that they were running a business not a church.

Mr Byrne said the trustees wanted to cooperate with BOS. They had been working with it to resolve matters. A two year plan aimed at paying off the debt where church would sell the properties in Kilmacud and Westland Row and keep its facility in Firhouse, had been entered into.  However on May 29 last, some nine months into the plan, BOS “pulled the plug”. This lead to the current difficulties, Mr Byrne said.

There is no surprise here. When you lose your charitable status all the donations and expenses on which tax relief could be obtained dried up. so that refinancing plan was no longer a runner. When you consider the 1000 people and the expenses claimed for under the charity the show had to come to an end.

In reply James Doherty Bl, for the receivers, rejected claims his clients had acted unreasonably. The trustees had deliberately ignored court orders, he said. Counsel added the church had lost is charitable status due to its business activities and had operated a Starbucks in one of its properties, counsel added.

Starbucks is only one example but if you check the web site you can see how much business was operating there. http://www.vcc.ie/
Added to that were the conflict of interest between the pastoral and business activities of Byrne and Hade which we have evidenced over the years.

During the hearings I was approached by a man who handed over €50k for a business venture to Gerry Byrne when he was involved with the church. Jim Gallagher and Michael O’Farrell outlined some of these issues last Sunday. These are serious concerns.https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/06/09/banks-e18m-battle-with-the-pastor-preneurs/

These articles outline all kind of business interests little to do with the proclamation of the gospel and there are more legal battles and issues than most people have holidays in a lifetime. Business ventures, property disputes, asylum hostels and hostel accommodation with the Dublin City Council and South Dublin County Council. Hotels and internet companies etc.

“We can talk about a bible in one hand and a banknote in the other.”

Counsel expressed the receiver’s concerns about an event advertised by the Church for this weekend which invited people to attend at their premises. However the trustees said the event, which was planned to coincide with next weeks G8 summit in Co Fermanagh, was arranged some time ago.

Here we see the denial and hubris that was manifested. Calls after the receivers went still suggested that everything was going fine. Imagine when you know the Receivers have gone in not cancelling a major event. The statesman like comments about the G8 summit were meant to impress us. What they show is a trio in a delusional state brought about by their flagrant gambling with money and property. Unlike the Sean Dunne’s of this world they connected wealth to God’s blessing and so the resulting chaos is a direct result of a delusional theology based on a pyramid scheme which was sucking its members dry and history just happened to catch up with them and bite them. Here is what was inside the entrance at Firhouse:”A frosted glass wall is inscribed with phrases such as ‘Give freely and become wealthy’ and ‘Be stingy and lose everything.’

Mr Justice Gilligan, in adjourning the receiver’s application for the injunctions to remain in place pending the full hearing of the to early July said the receivers are to be allowed take possession at 2pm next Monday. Any issues the trustee may have concerning the receivership could be dealt with on that date, the Judge added.

We wish all the members well but this drama is not yet concluded as the legal, ethical and resulting psychological collapse of many will result as it has in other Churches in failure as happened to Icarus who tried to fly too close to the sun.
From a legal perspective serious consequences will follow, from a human point of view a number of people are going to have to face a bleak future of coming to terms with this failure in their lives. A lot as our comment section shows members and supporters are in total denial and view the mirror which they are looking at as too difficult to look at. This will pass and then anger will be directed at those that have led them up the hill and down again.
From a Christian point of view we can now answer the question of whether they represent the image of Christ or of the Beast. I posed that question in relation to their failure to turn up in court.
Now that they have, I have not noted any sign of  humility or recognition of the massive moral failure their behaviours represent. I note much talk about grace, but little walk of grace. We did nothing wrong. A man went up to the temple to pray and said, I am not like others etc. You get my drift. Here there is denial and more denial. There will be a search for a miracle exit. a purchase by some others so that the reopening of the the church will be be seen as divine intervention. However, before that happens do not be surprised if the law has to visit again. Now they do not lose the image of Christ, but will the mark of the beast now be revealed or rejected?
