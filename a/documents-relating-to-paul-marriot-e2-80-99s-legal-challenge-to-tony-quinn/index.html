Tony Quinn

Documents and summaries relating to Paul Marriott’s legal challenge to Tony Quinn.

Marriott – Claim Form and Statement of Claim

Statement by Paul Marriott

As noted before Dialogue Ireland has no interest in the oil issue per se.

However, due to the clarity expressed in these court documents which have come into our possession we believe it is in the public interest to publish them.  They further demonstrate the material we have on our site relating to Quinn. There is no need for further commentary, other than I suggest below that Quinn might try to run for cover?

On November 28, 2010 Nicola Tallant published an excellent summary of the issues involved in the case. Entitled, “Tony’s oil ‘Rigged.’”

Sunday World Rigged 42

Sunday World Rigged 43

We know that Quinn was totally against the oil project when it started but when he found he could follow his philosophy of using other peoples’ money he jumped on board and to pardon the pun found himself on the board.

He used every opportunity to connect his seminars to the oil business, and even allowed Susan Morrice to promote this business when doing his Educo seminars.

The real joke was that he got lawyers to write to Dialogue Ireland and accused us of disparaging the oil company. In other words he was trying to stop free association, which is a right in any democratic society from happening. This attempt to intimidate is totally bypassed in Paul Marriott’s clear legal challenge. This is not some legalese waffle about disparagement but a clear statement of the undue influence Quinn is exercising.

Quinn likes to get other people to fight his battles while he crawls under a stone, but the shape of his current problems involve a case concerning sexual assault and this new front in regard to the oil company. He now has to come out and face the music, as there will be no compromise. So his success in keeping out of the courts for nearly 40 years might be coming to an end. That is unless he can find a new fall guy or gal.

As you know he is planning a seminar for January 2011.

Dear Good Life Club Member

This is just a note to confirm that the next Good Life Club meeting will be held on Sunday January 9th 2011 at the RDS, Dublin, so you can go ahead and make your travel arrangements.

Later on Tony will send you a letter outlining the day. I really want you to
know that this meeting will be the most spectacular ever, and I truly mean that.
Not to be missed!

Make sure you bring a note pad and pen with you also.

Looking forward to seeing you there and I wish you a really happy Christmas.

Lots of love,

Mary.

VENUE: RDS, BALLSBRIDGE, DUBLIN 4
12 noon sharp to 6 pm. approx.
For further information contact: + 353-1-830-4211

Could he possibly be planning an about turn? Suggest he was always opposed to the oil business but Susan has got out of control and he is just not interested? Or will he fight? His record is flight, but January will be interesting. Watch this space!
