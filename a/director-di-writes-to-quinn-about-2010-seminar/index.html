Divide and rule: man is the new machine.

A landmark new book suggests we are thinking more and more like machines, and risk losing what makes us human BY

Iain McGilchrist?    Divide and Rule

Here is a quote from Bryan Appleyard’s Sunday Times review.

“First, let me make something clear. This is not new-age babble. Research into split-brain patients in the 1960s led to the discovery that the two sides of our brain performed radically different functions. Roughly, the left dealt with rationality, rule-following and exact ordering of information, the right with more subtle matters such as context, meaning and the nuances of our interactions with the world. New-agers, management theorists, self-help snake-oilers and others seized on this, and the left-brain/right-brain conflict became a pop-culture cliché. It became routine to hear people say “That’s so left brain”, meaning it was uncool, like wearing a tie or joining Rotary.”

You might be able to use it with your doctorate. By the way if you would like a participant observer at your seminars I would be delighted to attend?

Regards
