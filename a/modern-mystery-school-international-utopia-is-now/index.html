Modern Mystery School International UTOPIA is NOW:

The Modern Mystery School

MMS May 5th effort brings wondrous change to the world.

Below is Gudni’s writing to his followers in explanation of the happenings on May 5th after many of them left wondering what was all that about. If it reads a bit too much like a FAIRYTALE then maybe that’s because it is a ………

This was sent to us by one of the members that is now questioning how they ever became involved in the MMS and how they allowed themselves to be taken in by their tales.

Modern Mystery School International

THE DREAM OF UTOPIA!

by

Gudni GED Gudnason RM.

There once was a dream, a fantasy or a legend born midst the people, a legend that was supposed to ‘fix’ everything that was wrong or bad in our world. A little girl and a little boy started a journey to find this magickal thing, not really knowing what it was but they felt compelled to search for it! It was a scary journey as they had to venture far and long in order to awaken this FANTASY, they had to LEAVE THEIR PARENTS, FAMILY AND FRIENDS, but they were ready and willing to make that sacrifice. They packed their things and told family and friends about their trip, but they did not accept their journey. They said to the little boy and girl: “There is no such place” or “there is no need to find that place” or “we don’t need that, we are doing fine”, or worse yet, “That’s silly, you are being stupid – stop wasting your time with silly dreams!” but the little girl and boy knew that they had to go.

They left in the middle of the night, scared but determined to go as far as they needed to go in order to find this magick that was so needed. They had seen their families and friends suffering for years and complaining about how hard their hard were, BUT they knew there was a solution, a hope of change, but they had to find it!

They had walked for some time away from their village they came to a deep forest and at the entrance to it there was a large being, half man and half bull. They saw him from afar and were scared. “Should we go?” asked the little girl and the boy answered “I don’t know,it’s scary!” Slowly they moved closer and closer to the weird creature and at some point, because of a good feeling inside themselves they had about this creature, they had moved almost all the way to it… and in turn had arrived at the gate to the forest!

The large being stood high and tall and as the little girl and boy approached, it looked down on them and said: “What does thou seek?” and the children said: “We seek a better world for our people and for us”! The large being smiled and said: “Enter with Joy and come back when you find what you seek!”

They slowly walked past the large creature, a little bit scared but ready to explore this forest of many possibilities. It was an exciting time for the little ones and they were feeling happy inside, they walked down the path holding hands and chippering like chip monks, singing songs from home. Soon they were met by many other strange beings of the forest and these beings started telling them about the wonders of the Universe, teaching them tools of healing, how to understand the world better and how to re-connect with their inner divinity. They learned that they had been chosen for this path. That it was a sacred lineage and that their calling had been no coincidence! Mostly they learned lessons about JOY and how to live in perfect harmony with all things, how all things they need are hidden inside of them and that they just need to remember. They learned that they could not die, because they are spirit eternal and they felt content and peace and were happy that they came!

But after a while they realized they had not found what they were looking for and they started asking: “But what about the world we are seeking? A world of peace and love, where is it?” At this point they had gathered around a little fire in the forest and with them were many beings both animals and other mixtures of animal and human. When they asked this question the beings went silent and the old wise owl flew down from a tree and said to them: “Well, if that is what you seek, the question is how much do you want it?” The children answered: “We will do anything!” “Oh, anything! Really?” the Owl said and laughed causing all the other beings to laugh as well. “Alright, solet’s assume you are indeed willing to do anything, then I am compelled to tell you that to seek that world might take your life, then what do you say?” The children thought for a moment and then said: “Yes we are ready!”

The next day the beings of the enchanted forest saw the children off and the wise owl told them what direction to go. “This will be a hard journey, one that will take all of your physical strength and mental capacity.  It will exhaust all of your food and water supplies leaving you with NOTHING. Are you ready?” The children said yes and started their journey! This time they walked through the dark part of the forest,… for a long time, many dangerous animals were peeking at them through the trees and some of them crawling, invisible at their feet.

Finally they noticed the forest getting thinner and thinner with more and more light shining in from the sun. They came out of the forest only to find a wasted, burned out land. Everything was dead. Some weird looking beings roaming around, that they did not recognize, perhaps partially human but mostly something else. The children grabbed each other’s arms for comfort and they remembered the lessons they had gotten from the beings of the forest and started using them to protect themselves and they continued their search for a better world.

After walking for some time they came to a waterfall and around it were many different beings, half human and half animal, some even part vegetation. They stood in a half circle around a pond that had formed at the bottom of the waterfall and they all looked into the waterfall, it seemed. The children stepped forward and got the attention of one of them asking what was going on. The large being, which was half a human and half a horse, said: “It’s the prophecy, they are coming, the beings that can end our world, end our time here in PARADISE”! The children got scared and contemplated if they should flee this place and perhaps go back home, or hide in the forest until this was over.

An enormously large Eagle sitting in a tree above them could hear their talk and flew down to the ground saying: “Many have fled, many have hid but none is safe! Those who chose to close their eyes to this catastrophic event will see the world become a better place but they will know that they had nothing to do with that. They will know, in time, they left the world alone when it needed them the most. Don’t be amongst those timid souls, you will never feel the same, for all time you would know you could have helped but you choose not to!”

The children decided to stay, not knowing what this was all about, but they chose to do what they could to help the world. They took their place in the circle of beings around the waterfall and they waited. Suddenly the wind changed, the water became cold and everyone around the pond felt a shiver. The Eagle above shouted: “Get ready!” The children tightened their grip of the wooden stick of power and crystal they had received through their training in the forest, not that they really understood what these tools did. The other beings took out other tools of power and some were carrying mighty swords.

Then the drums started and the land rumbled like in an earthquake, the water in the waterfall stopped and behind it we could see a gaping hole into the earth, black as the night and to the children, it was scary. They all started chanting magickal formulas and singing in angelic voices, summoning the best of this world against this gaping hole of darkness.  Darkness seemed to reach out to them and the others, pushing and pulling to get through.

The children and the others continued to do this work for a long time, non-stop and it seemed like time stood still. It was a difficult task as the children did not know what was going on, they felt like this might just be a waste of time. The boy leaned over to the girl and said: “What is this all about?”and the girl said “I don’t know but I feel it might be important,let’s just stay and finish!” A few hours later the boy leaned forward again and said: “I can feel something big here, I know we are doing something”! and the girl replied: “Yes and I can see them”! After hours of this work the water started to flow again and the beings slowly started walking away, tired and wounded from something unseen.

The children were happy that it was over but asked the Eagle above: “Now what?”

“Well,it’s over now. We rest and take time to heal.” They said: “But we don’t understand, what has happened, what has changed?!  We see nothing different!”The Eagle answered: “Go home, see the changes ineveryday life, if you need me just call me and I will come!”

So the children left the waterfall and went back home. When they left through the gate of the enchanted Forest the large half human and half bull looked at them and said: “Good job fair ones, you are truly blessed amongst humans”!

At home no one greeted them, no one cheered for their trials or learning. Most ignored them and some even pointed fingers and laughed sayiing “I told you so! Foolish children!”. They entered their old world with frustration and loneliness. They felt like their journey had been for nothing, and they got more and more depressed. They thought perhaps the others were RIGHT..maybe this was a foolish thing to have done.

Depressed and confused they called upon the Eagle who came instantly. “What does thou seek?” the Eagle said. “We seek more knowledge and understanding of what has happened, we don’t understand any of this” said the children.

The Eagle told them to follow him to a nearby creek. There he told them to sit down and he started talking. “Well,it’s like this you see, in the beginning we had balance and harmony in the universe, a place of perfect condition to learn about life. But this was disrupted by negative energies that sought to destroy our world, so we took up arms and we fought them. We said: “NO, you cannot take our world!”  This is what you were a part of at the waterfall. When we did this, we created a new energy in the universe, the energy of the people, we created a new power for this world, human power that can create anything. This is different from the energy that was here before which had been created by the Gods and was not human energy! Now, it’s your energy that rules this planet! Humans are now their own masters, for the first time ever!”

The children looked at the Eagle with deep questions and wondering and then asked: “But nothing has changed on earth since the waterfall, so what was it all for?” The Eagle laughed and then said: “No my children, EVERYTHING HAS CHANGED since that amazing day! The world is now YOURS and you are FREE to CREATE ANYTHING YOU WANT, that is the true UTOPIA! The world you are seeking is called Shamballah and this world will not be created by a specific group or person, it will ONLY and can ONLY be created by the people themselves. You want health, then the people need tochoose health, you want peace, then the people need tochoose peace, this world is now yours, what we chose as a collective is what we will create! But now we have the Great Task of Unifying all beings into one mind! Going toward one goal of a world of peace and health, love and compassion. This will be your work, dear children, you have been chosen as you are a part of a sacred lineage of special people, people that dare to stand up against negativity; Remember Your Lineage, it is what makes you special! You are the future of this planet and your work starts now!”

This little story is my answer to all of you who have doubt about what we did on May 5th. There is nothing else to say, you either are tuned into the task at hand, creating a new world, or you are NOT! You are either with us in the Great Task of creating a new world or you not.

For those who are, let’s move forward as a community and take care of our Spiritual Well-Being, grow our United Compassion and harness the Will to take on the Great Task at hand. It is a task filled with JOY and Inner Fulfillment that directs us to the path of our souls liberation and gives our lives a meaning. This is an amazing time in the evolution of the Mystery School tradition, a time where we get to express ourselves and truly be who we are. A time of community where we can become a true spiritual family and learn to take care of each other, learn to communicate truly from the inner soul and not pre-programmed sentences decided by tradition or dogma. This new community will focus on the Spiritual Well Being of all humans and animals and make sure that we learn how to take care of ourselves and others.

We now have the amazing opportunity to create anything we need on this planet, to re-create society in a new way, a way that supports the people. We are the ground-breakers for that and we can show others that pioneer spirit. Those timid souls who did not serve with us on May 5th are still our brothers and sisters and we will always love them and it is our task now to make them feel welcome in the school. Those who have turned against us for what we did on May 5th, well, OH WELL!!! I will not apologize for doing my job and to help make the world a better place, NEVER!  It is there choice to not serve and it is their choice and karma in how they act towards those who are STILL working to make this world a better place, on a globla scale!

Everyone has a choice to make.  To live as they wish, BUT the initiates in this lineage, those who take the work of making this world a better place seriously, those willing to set aside judgement and ego, these are the true Warriors of Light!  Those who KNOW we are far from done our GREAT TASK and who are never going to quit helping save souls and create Shamballah for EVERYONE!

Now we look forward with a smile and JOY in our hearts. Spiritual Well Being is an art and a science that has so many exciting components to it. These coming years we will be introducing many different programs both through Modern Mystery School and the NEW L.I.F.E. Programs (Living In Full Empowerment) that will help you understand these two components! These programs will bring us to the MASSES in a whole new way and we will show you all how our school will become a household name in a very short time.

Many books and booklets are coming out early next year and CD`s with both spiritual music and meditations etc. all going to be distributed through open channels such as music stores, etc. and we have formed good relationships and started collaboration with some amazing people, that will get our name out to the public in a new exciting ways.

We are also going to remember that we are a Global School, spanning now 44 countries with our teachings. In 2013 we will embrace that fact and focus on worldwide expansion and collaboration from exciting countries like South Africa, Brazil, Spain, England, Taiwan, Thailand and South Korea to name a few. We will send out many teachers to these areas and ask them to send us their uniqueness. Our collaboration will become worldwide!

In Spiritual Well Being we will focus on all aspects of the human condition, mind, body and soul. In addition to the regular curriculum, that will be made much easier and more fun to study, we will bring teachings that bring true abundance with support programs that help an individual to truly have great wealth. We will bring programs that help people to find their true soul-mate and live a romantic beautiful life with those they have now. Our programs will include working with your community to create peace, taking care of YOU in a Whole~istic Way, Understanding Your Emotions, the Mysteries of True Beauty; or Cleopatra’s Beauty Secrets, Crystals and Essential oils for everyday use, Tarot made simple, The Depth of the Well; or Dealing with Depression, Self Leadership, Understanding the Flow of Universal Energies, Spiritual Cooking, How to Make Music, Dancing, Spiritual Gardening, Juice Fasting and many more. We will engage in the popular themes of the people and bring them quality of the lineage teachings instead of teachings that leave students empty inside. Our lineage has the depth that the people want and need and we will serve them as is needed!

We will now create a new community inside the Modern Mystery School, based on people who are not afraid of expressing themselves and can stand up for their truth. A group of people who are willing to let go of judgment of each other and rumor spreading that serves no one! We have been hard on you in the last few years, yes, and I have been hard on you, for that one task of May 5th and to help you get rid of your EGO, but NOW it is time for you. This is YOUR SCHOOL and it is time for you to step in and show the world the glory that our sacred lineage holds.

This is my commitment to you, to allow YOU TO TAKE OVER!  I will now step down as “leader”, I have served you for the last 15 years and I have served from the heart, believing that I was doing what was best for you and the world. I love you all dearly and I have now chosen to just work, I am returning to my favorite teachings such as Kabbalah, Channeling School and others where I will show you a new and a better way to understand the sacred mysteries. I will remake all the programs into a much easier and better format but holding the sacred flame intact and the lineage strong. I am stepping back into the school to do what I do best, create new and exciting programs and to teach from spirit and JOY!

I hope that I will see you in the future of this amazing school, it is a journey worth taking!

I love you all,

Gudni GED Gudnason RM.
