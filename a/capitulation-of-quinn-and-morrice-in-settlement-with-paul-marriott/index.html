Capitulation of Quinn and Morrice in settlement with Paul Marriott

Tony Quinn

Quinn before this court case in Nevis was sending out personal data requests like confetti to anyone who might be a legal threat to him. His greatest fear beyond dying is that of appearing in court. He now uses oil money to keep out of those precints.

Though just like we would have liked to see a judgment against Quinn in the court, the settlement represents a considerable victory. Obviously we have no idea of the terms of the settlement, but if Quinn has anything to do with it there will be a non disclosure clause. Give Paul his due he refused to bow down and take the crap being shovelled out, and sought to get his shares and his oil drilling issues addressed.

Basically Quinn blinked and Paul got what he deserves.

Now what he said at the meeting at Dublin in March comes into play, “but warned shareholders they had to stand up for themselves.”

Many shareholders nearly bankrupted by Quinn were hoping to come in behind the Court Case by Marriott, but now that there has been a settlement they are left adrift. See our comment to this effect already in March in Italics below.

Mr Marriott said his legal action against the company was about principles rather than money. He said he would “keep going until the money runs out”, but warned shareholders they had to stand up for themselves.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/03/04/investors-in-new-move-to-oust-yoga-guru-quinn-as-oil-firm-director-by-p-cullen/

“Mr Marriott said his legal action against the company was about principles rather than money. He said he would “keep going until the money runs out”, but warned shareholders they had to stand up for themselves.”

Paul has put his finger on the weakness of the former Educoists. They have been taught to use other peoples’ money. They have unconsciously transferred this strategy to Paul. They want him to be the sugar daddy who takes away their pain. The lesson he has put to them is that he is involved with principle even to the point of the money running out. I can’t carry you on my shoulders; you need to get up of your knees. The indications are from a text I received late last night that this is what this meeting brought home to people. Also a source told me today that there is palpable sense of purpose as those present have rediscovered the original vision of what he oil company could be.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/03/04/statement-by-of-di-following-ine-shareholders-meeting-at-dublin-airport/

WE DO NOT MIX WHAT WE DO WITH OIL, QUINN KEEPS DOING IT.

We now realise that it is only as people disparage Quinn as the cultist leader and begin to address their own involvement with his organisation and control of their lives that will last the course. We use this term as we are constantly told by Quinn and Morrice that we are in the business of disparaging the directors of an oil company. We have no interest in the oil. Naturally it is only natural to concentrate on the shares, but  if one does not deal with the experience of involvement with the high level of conditioning resulting from being connected to Quinn there will be an increasing depression and loss of direction. Material benefits are important, but the psychological and emotional effects of involvement have deeper roots, and need professional treatment.

We will soon to report on the 40 anniversary of Quinn’s first Yoga class and where all the developments from Yoga Commune, the disappearance of two young men in that high octane commune who were often heard crying out as they tried to get in touch with the aura world of Quinn
