Welcome to the International Spiritual Care Conference website.  On this site you will find a report of the conference,  a range of resources and information to help you stay connected with  Rigpa’s Spiritual Care Education Programme

“Spiritual Care is not a luxury for a few; it is the essential right of every human being,

as essential as political liberty, medical assistance  and equality of opportunity.”
Sogyal Rinpoche, author of The Tibetan Book of Living and Dying

“Illness, suffering and death are part of the reality of human existence.  It would be foolish to pretend that we will not die or that we are not sick when we are. When caring for someone who is seriously ill, it is important to cultivate positive attitudes like compassion, love and tolerance.   Real care of the sick does not begin with costly procedures, but with the simple gift of affection and love.  In the practice of healing, a kind heart is as valuable as medical training, because it is the source of happiness for both oneself and others. People respond to kindness even when medicine is ineffective, and in turn cultivating a kind heart is a cause of our own good health.”


 His Holiness the Dalai Lama,
Message to the opening of the new Spiritual Care Centre, Dzogchen Beara Retreat Centre

Feedback from Speakers and Delegates

Feedback from speakers and delegates on the content and the organisation of the conference was extremely positive.
Dr Tony O’Brien, a consultant in palliative medicine who chaired the first day of the conference, said:

“People were constantly approaching me saying how much they had enjoyed the conference, how valuable it was and how we had achieved such a wonderful balance in the programme and a sense of inclusion. I believe that you achieved something particularly special and memorable and gave a voice to many who are striving in their own workplace to create a greater awareness of the spiritual needs of our patients and their families.

Dr Ciaran O’Boyle, who is Professor of Psychology at the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland, added: ”I found the mix of presentations and the interfacing of Buddhist and Western thinking absolutely absorbing, and it has given me a lot of food for thought. I also think that a lot of people at the conference, myself included, received not only academic and professional gifts, but personal gifts in the form of insights and reflections also. It was an honour to be there.”

The delegates themselves were nourished and amazed by the quality of the topics the speakers presented on as well as the common experience of their fellow delegates.  During the formal sessions, the speakers provided much food for thought and many delegates commented on how wonderful it was to be able to discuss these ideas with likeminded colleagues.

As one of the conference delegates, Nadine Uí Dhuibhgeanán, said: Míle buíochas do gach aon dhuine. Superb conference: excellently organised, beautiful setting, handpicked speakers who inspired, ignited passion, challenged paradigms and who were truly authentic. I got deep reflection space for myself – which I did not expect but this was facilitated through how Ally opened the conference and through the authenticity of each and every speaker. I truly and thoroughly enjoyed this conference in heart, mind and body. Congratulations on truly a superb gathering of authentic hearts, minds and bodies.”

Anne Grace CNN South Tipperary Hospice said:  “I’m working 16 years in Palliative care and this is the best conference I have attended. The organisation, speakers, setting etc were excellent. It is difficult to pick the high point as it was super from beginning to end.”

Thecla Finnan said “Energising; a gift; experiential but put into words areas that I recognise in my being and my life and now it feels like a springboard for returning to my work and my life renewed, invigorating and expanded. In exploring living and dying and being open to what is possible I feel I have got much more than I expected from the conference. The level of attention and care from the organisers was second to none. I felt honoured to be here and look forward to the next.”
