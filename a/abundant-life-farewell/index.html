Abundant Life Farewell

Those of you familiar to the ongoing controversies of Abundant Life will be aware of the newsletters that Kevin Sanford sends to his ministry partners on a monthly basis. Due to the publication by Dialogue Ireland of several of these newsletters available via his ministry website, Abundant Life implemented system changes to ensure that these newsletters would no longer be in the public domain. Obviously Kevin Sanford felt that there was inclusive material in his newsletters that he did not want the viewing public to access such as consistent begging, condescension of Irish society to name but a few of many inherent issues with his correspondence.  Some of these past newsletters can be viewed here:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/03/31/newsletters-from-abundant-life-the-archive/

As with all things Abundant Life related, the spin doctors have been out in force recently and Kevin is currently doing very best impersonation of Saddam Hussein’s information minister during the American incursion into Iraq, Comical Ali. Those who remember Comical Ali, will remember the consistent denial of the invasion, the boasting of the might of Saddam’s troops and the announcements by Ali of “near victory” as the US Marines had conquered Baghdad, and had him completely surrounded. Just like this, Kevin once again ignores the growing opposition, the pending litigation, the media incursion, the evaporation of financial and personnel support and the all pervading sense of bitterness towards his ministry from many survivors in his latest broadcast. The complete implosion of Abundant Life and destruction of the church is cleverly constructed by Kevin into a Glorious act of God in his most recent letter that he has sent to all his ministry partners and friends. Most of these would be based in the U.S. and unaware of the mounting issues for both the Sanfords and the church and clearly Kevin is doing his utmost to ensure that his financial backers remain unaware of the issues.

For those curious amongst you, here follows the letter in question. You will note that he mentions he has the support of every mature Christian he knows, a very pointed dig at those in opposition to him. It appears that he feels any Christian in disagreement with him is not a mature Christian. I suppose this makes most Christians and ministers in Galway immature by Kevin’s standard!

We can also see Kevin’s continued fascination with finances. Kevin takes the opportunity to boast of the financial means of the Hades with their “$20 million” facility. Kevin seems quite confident in the Hades ability to repair his damaged church considering they have passed his sole ministerial credential of having money. One has to ask though how much of the Hades “$20 million” facility is actually owned by the banks or even NAMA? This is not something that would bother Kevin though as he has shown time and time again that he confuses credit access with real actualized wealth and is unable to differentiate between the two.

It’s also interesting that Brendan in his use of the soldier analogy declares that the Sanfords have liberated many. Those who have been enslaved and in bondage to them over the years would most certainly dispute Brendan’s commentary.  ‘The Americans have sent their soldiers around the world to fight in other countries liberate them and then train up their own army.  They are gifted by God to do that.’  It’s also interesting that Hade shows a deep lack of understanding of US military engagements around the world. His views indicate a person totally out touch with reality.

Last but certainly not least as with all correspondence from Kevin, comes the postscript caveat from Kevin directly asking for financial contributions to support their move home. It just wouldn’t be a Kevin Sanford newsletter if there wasn’t a request for money somewhere in there!

“Hello, Everyone!

Heather and I have some really big news…surprising news:  After twenty years on the mission field, I’m bringing my family back home to Texas.  We have obviously laboured much in prayer over this decision and believe that we have the mind of God, the witness of the Spirit and 100% encouragement from every preacher and mature Christian that we know.  We have not entered into it lightly but, over time, believe with all of our hearts that the Lord is leading us home.

We will share the details of our future more as things reveal themselves.  Suffice it to say that a wonderful opportunity to start anew in the states has presented itself just as the doors in Ireland have been closing.  I would, however, like to give you the good report on the future of our wonderful Irish church.  Some dear friends of ours, Pastors Brendan and Sheila Hade from Dublin, will be taking over the leadership of the church here in Galway.  We are so very amazed that the Holy Spirit of God would encourage these pastors, at this stage in their lives and ministry, to personally take the helm of the church.  Why?  Well, these amazing and wonderful Irish pastors have only just recently finished building a €15 million church in the capitol (over $20 million).

The Hades have known us for many years.  They have also known our church and staff.  After praying about it, they decided to not only take the church, but to drive over every week and handle it themselves.  They were present with us last Sunday when we gave everybody the news.  It was teary, of course.  But if you have ever visited our church, you know that it is strong.  And they were, indeed, strong.  I was never so proud of them all, or of the work Heather and I have done here.  For fifteen years we have worked in this vineyard.  At times the ground was so hard and rocky that it nearly broke the plough!  But seeing God honour us by allowing the work to continue, and by honouring the church with such veteran, experienced new leaders of more than 30 years pastoring their own church, is humbling in the extreme.  We are thankful and amazed.  After handing the microphone to Pastor Brendan on Sunday, he was gracious and inspiring with his words to the congregation, and to us.  He said, ‘The Americans have sent their soldiers around the world to fight in other countries liberate them and then train up their own army.  They are gifted by God to do that.  Pastors Kevin and Heather have been exactly that kind of gift from God for this nation of Ireland…and now they are returning to their own land.’  Wow.  We really are leaving everyone in very good hands, indeed.  Thank God!

So we will be spending our last Christmas in Ireland with our four beautiful children.  We came here with none.  We are leaving Galway with many friends. We came here with none.  We leave with memories, a full heart and great hopes for the future. We pray that eternity will one day judge us faithful.

Knowing that it will be very expensive to bring my family home and to start again, we would like to ask everyone we know to consider helping us with the move.  There is so much to do, so much, indeed. Please pray about helping us.  God Bless you all!  I look forward to sharing more details soon. Thanks so much.

Love,
Kevin, Heather, Hannah, Abbey, Matthew and Lilly Beth”

Leaving Letter

We are also aware that Kevin and Heather are once again back in Texas currently, albeit temporarily as Heather continues her somewhat unsuccessful book signing tour. Thankfully Heather has been kind enough to provide us with her full itinerary via her Twitter feed and blog.

While Heather keeps herself busy with her book signings Kevin is once again hanging out with his Jesse Duplantis at Hallam’s annual “Campmeeting” conference. Kevin will also use the opportunity to set the contractual groundwork with Walter Hallam for his El Paso start up. Walter Hallam seems quite content to ignore the abuses and controversies that follow the Sanfords. A mitigated risk perhaps, knowing the potential for revenue generation that Kevin can bring to his ministry in El Paso. Another example of a purported “Minister of the Gospel” placing potential financial gain ahead of the welfare of individuals. Walter Hallam will have a lot to answer to if continues to allow Kevin and Heather to operate without intervention or question, turning a blind eye to all those who have been hurt. In conclusion Kevin states,  ‘We pray that eternity will one day judge us faithful.’ This sounds like some Confucian sage referencing Taoism. There is no sign of regret or an apology for harm done and a reputation destroyed. Rather we have an exit as evasive as any dud banker. There is not a sign of a Christian conscience informed by ethics. He has no sense of standing in the face of a Holy God and that he must appear before the judgement seat of Christ. That the Hades have taken this on with amnesia will finally reveal the paucity of  the length, depth and height of their imagination – Driving on empty!
