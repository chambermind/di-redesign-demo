Tony Quinn

QUINN: THE GURU WITH THE FEET OF CLAY

Quinn also has the cult leader’s typical persecution complex. His guilt makes him conjure up all kinds of conspiracies and plots against him. This paranoia based on guilt is part of the price the corrupt cult leader has to pay for his deplorable actions and intensified by living in the hermetically sealed world of the cult inner circle surrounded by yes men/ women and those dependent on him for their financial survival (in Quinn’s case the pittance they usually get from him and his “front” health businesses of shops, gyms, etc).

There is no interaction with the outside world other than stage managed seminars and rallies of the faithful. Here happy-clappy devotees can try to escape reality by buying into Quinn’s fantasy world where in Quinn’s mind your “success” is mine and my “success” is my own to borrow from an old saying. The phenomena of misattribution and misidentification as social psychologists call them are key cult dynamics here. Quinn never leaves Quinn-world to engage in real dialogue and debate as Dialogue Ireland and others have found despite many offers to Quinn on this. His tears, genuine or other, about how he is being criticised are all about playing a false victim role while really crying for himself as cry-babies do when they don’t get their own way.

The criticisms of Quinn, the false messiah, are based on huge numbers of reports about his unspiritual activities which is the main reason he will never debate with the critics but wheel out others to relentlessly and mechanically lie on his behalf. The Sunday World has been very instrumental in lifting the lid on the mucky messiah’s world and he tries to counter this by saying it’s just based on begrudgery and jealousy. He’s so far out of touch with reality at this point that he thinks this will convince as an argument and that he doesn’t have to even address the issues raised in the exposes. What an amazingly arrogant and self-deluded mindset. He also doesn’t challenge the criticisms because he doesn’t have a real defence. His sociopathic mindset can’t countenance the truth. The truth shall set you free spiritually and otherwise, including from Quinn manipulations. The Quinn-Yoga-Educo cult is steadily crumbling as the light reveals the guru with the feet of clay and his world to be a sordid place of lies, manipulations, sexual depravity, financial exploitations and other corruptions.
