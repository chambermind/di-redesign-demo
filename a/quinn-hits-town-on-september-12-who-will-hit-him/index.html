Further from Tony.

In all the excitement of telling you about Educo Formative Learning™ and urging

you to attend our meeting on Sunday, I neglected to tell you about our special guest.

Yes, we are going to have our very own X Factor, with you as the Judges. On the last

seminar there was a lovely young lady called Lorraine Nolan. When I learned that she

had a four and a half octave voice, I had to hear it. Your average pop singer may have

about 2 octaves. As something of a music buff, I can tell you that when Lorraine sang

I was so moved that I literally broke out in a sweat and had tears in my eyes. After

that, I so much wanted you to hear her and now she has agreed to sing on Sunday.

In case you missed my last letter to you, on the day I will give you a copy of our New

Brochure. We will go through this together. This will act as the map that will enable

you to bring you and your life up a few octaves, to sing your Song of Life better than

ever before. Yes, I am really saying that the Breakthrough information the Brochure

has to offer is that good.

Lots of Love,

Tony.
