ASSAULT ON THE PALMAR DE TROYA

Prison for the ex-pope of Palmar de Troya and his wife
Gregorio XVIII and his wife have refused to testify before the judge of Utrera
They are charged only with robbery with violence and not with attempted of homicide
FERNANDO PÉREZ ÁVILA
June 13, 2018 – 3:30 p.m.

The judge of First Instance and Instruction number 4 of Utrera sent this afternoon to prison to the former Pope of Palmar de Troya, Gregorio XVIII, and his wife, Nieves Triviño , after they tried to rob in the basilica of the order of the Carmelites of the Holy Face and seriously hurt one of the religious.

The ex-pope of the Palmar de Troya has passed this Wednesday to judicial disposition, after having received the medical discharge in the Hospital Virgen del Rocío . Gregorio XVIII has arrived at the courts of Utrera in the company of his wife, Nieves Triviño , to whom the judge extended his detention for 24 hours. He did so with the intention of taking a statement with his partner and check whether the two versions contradict each other. The Civil Guard charged them in principle with the crimes of attempted murder and robbery with violence and intimidation, although the court only investigates them for this last charge, although with aggravating circumstances.

Both entered the basilica of Palmar de Troya illegally on Sunday afternoon with the intention of stealing. They jumped the perimeter wall of the church on the lower part, next to the water tanks of the farm, hooded and armed with knives. But they met Father Silvestre, a young priest who was performing maintenance tasks. According to the first investigations, the ex-pope attacked Silvestre with a knife, but he defended himself and in the struggle stabbed Gregorio XVIII . The two men were seriously injured, while the ex-pope’s wife suffered minor injuries.

The former Palmarian Pope is taken to the courts by the Civil Guard. The former Palmarian Pope is taken to the courts by the Civil Guard. / ANTONIO PIZARRO

The ex-pope has remained from Sunday until the morning of this Wednesday at the Virgen del Rocío Hospital, where he was detained and in the custody of the Civil Guard. The doctors have given him the medical discharge at noon today and the Civil Guard has taken him directly to the court. The judge of Instruction 4 of Utrera has decreed the imprisonment of the two arrested. She will go to the women’s prison in Alcalá de Guadaíra and he will go to the prevention module in Seville-I.

The couple has arrived at the courts in a patrol car of the institute armed with the tinted windows. The two detainees were in the back seats of the car. He was handcuffed, dressed in a pink polo shirt and jeans, with a jacket that covered his head and sports shoes. She has repeated the model of yesterday, a white dress and a black vest over her face, although she has changed her shoes. Neither of the two inmates has said a single word to the informants who were waiting at the courthouse door.
