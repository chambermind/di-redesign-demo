ALCC: The Trials of internment 2 by Kenneth Sabourin

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Working on a chain gang……

Sometimes I would get a break from working at the church to do more intense work at the Sanford’s. In addition to work all staff are required to turn in a devotional sheet that shows that their workers are spending time with Jesus. The requirements were to read in the Old Testament, New Testament, Proverbs, worship, pray, speaking in tongues and record what God spoke to you at least 5 of the 7 days a week. In addition I had to listen to the sermon that I missed in the regular service (due to kid’s church) on a cd and then take a test and if I got any answers wrong id be in trouble. Getting by would be a minimum of 45 minutes a day and if you didn’t turn in your worksheet and test at 100% you’d get “talked to”. Work at the Sanford’s was the most discouraging. Mainly because Heather was always critiquing and bossing you around and making you feel like garbage. In my journal that I wrote in every day in Ireland, my 22nd day in Ireland I wrote, “…Pastor Heather is a little mean sometimes. I know she’s human but we have feelings. I think all I can do is just wait and see how it comes over the next few months”. I was blindsided by the experience and opportunity to be in another country and a fanatical church that it became a slow process of integrating my full being and not looking at red flags. At the Sanford’s “big and beautiful” home I would work twice as hard. I once had to paint a fence 3 times because it wasn’t painted to Heathers standards. I did the dishes after dinner once and didn’t wash a giant cast iron skillet because I remember there was a certain way to wash it being its metal type. I also didn’t change a light bulb that went out because I had trouble finding the correct bulb in the pantry. Heather commented that it wasn’t right of me to neglect the skillet and light bulb and that I need to be a harder worker. This was of course after babysitting her kids all day while she directs the other women of ALCC around her house doing chores such as cooking, cleaning, organizing and making her home look beautiful. I was stripped of my babysitting privileges because Heather was worried about me being alone with her kids. I was a wrestler in high school and so when I played with the kids I would pick them up, spin them around and pretend to be a monster. You know, fun kid stuff. They loved me. She pulled me aside one day and the first thing she said was, “I don’t know if you were raped as a kid…(paused) but I don’t know you and as a parent I just need to make sure my kids are safe. You’ll understand when you’re a parent”. She walked away and I felt so horrible. I had been there for months working and I didn’t know what I was doing wrong. These particular events happened around September and on the 2nd of that month both Kevin and Heather pulled me into the office and they asked me why I was there (in Ireland). I responded I want to serve Jesus. Kevin goes on to say that there are 2 areas in my life that he and his wife see as red flags and it was his job as my pastor (my God) to make sure I am in God’s favor. First Kevin noticed my devotional worksheets. He said last week I only did 4 of 7 days when 5 are minimum required. I simply said that I was just busy working I just didn’t get time and I am sorry, (considering I did 6/7 days on average every week). He made the notion that I need to improve or ill be sent home. Heather then directed my attention to the previous weeks work at her house where I didn’t wash her skillet and changed light bulb. She made the conclusion that I am not a hard worker and that I have a poor work ethic. She said the kingdom of God isn’t for lazy people and that they will make me an efficient worker if I choose to stay. I was freaked out and scared and so I asked to stay and I worked even harder than I did before. At this point the brainwashing really started to take over. It wasn’t about me having an opinion or option anymore, it was about being on the Sanford’s good side and having favor with them. I was rarely left in charge of answering phones because I still was new to navigating info to callers.

The next day after a day of watching the phones I get a warm welcome from Heather saying, “if you cant answer phones then your fired.” She was referring to me being “unprofessional” in a phone call the previous day to one of her vendors  because I didn’t have the answer to one of their questions. That was her reply when I asked her “how are you doing this morning pastor? Yet nothing I do is good enough. Even around Christmas time Heather had me wrap presents for her kids and when she saw my handy work her face had a look of discussed and she said my wrapping job was horrible and said it was disgraceful and an abomination. She always made me felt like the most pathetic human being in the world. I felt that I was never good enough and I always felt so convicted and that I could never be good enough for Jesus either. Some of my observations of Heather were this, she had a team of females cater to her every wish doing all the things a parent or pastor’s wife should do. The ladies even bathed and cleaned Heathers kids. The only type of work Heather did was boss people around. One day I got severely sick and I was fortunate enough to be sent home. But because of ALCC’s cult like doctrine Heather demanded I wake myself up from resting and pray to be healed in the name of Jesus and report back if I was feeling better so I could come back to work. What happened to just resting when your sick? One thing I noticed is that Kevin and Heather do not encourage relationships with anyone in their church. Most of the girls who work there have no significant other due to divorce or never dated. They can’t have any life outside of what Kevin and Heather’s planned for them. Kevin and Heather may get foreign support from America but they have no elders to keep them accountable. They answer to no one. If you stick your finger in their chest or disagree with what they believe you are banished. They give no explanations except stay away from those people and declare its Satan trying to temp you. Your not allowed to ask questions or really discover who God is. No it’s just whatever Kevin preaches and what Heather demands is what will happen. Why? Because they are ordained and inspired by God himself. More like narcissist who are power hungry, wanting control and treat people like commodities. And it’s all in the name of God.
