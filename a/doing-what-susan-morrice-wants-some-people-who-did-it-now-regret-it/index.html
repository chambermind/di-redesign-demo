DOING WHAT SUSAN MORRICE WANTS: SOME PEOPLE WHO DID IT NOW REGRET IT

Tony Quinn

Next Saturday, 29th September 2012, a crucial meeting will be held of the INE company.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/27/dialogue-ireland-trust-protest-saturday-september-29-2012/

Almost five years ago, on 13 December 2007, another crucial INE meeting took place, this time in Belize, attended by the people who had started up the company (less Mike Usher, who had died) – the Originators: Susan Morrice, Sheila McCaffrey, Jean Cornec and Paul Marriott. Also present was Caroline Williams, an Irish barrister. The purpose of the meeting was to get all present to sign an Operating Agreement to replace the original Operating Agreement which had governed the company since 2002, and under which all the Class B shareholders at the time had purchased their shares. The new 2007 Agreement not only “savagely” restricted the rights of the Class B shareholders, but also similarly restricted the rights of the Originators themselves – the Class A shareholders.

There are dire lessons to be learned from what happened at that meeting – Susan Morrice used two common persuasion techniques to induce the others to sign the Agreement (both often used in cults) – the results have been disastrous for all three of them. So we are drawing attention to these, in case she attempts to use the same techniques at the next meeting, to rush through measures designed to allow her and Tony Quinn to use the company as their personal property.

There were fierce arguments at the meeting – apart from Morrice; it appears the other Originators did not want to sign. Clause 40 of Judge Bannister’s Judgment describes how two of the Originators were induced to sign despite their doubts

Ms McCaffrey claims to have signed under duress, the pressure being the alleged threats to INE from Class B members or persons standing behind them and posing a threat to the company.

It’s a favourite tool of propaganda, to get people to believe in an enemy who doesn’t exist. Everyone had been perfectly happy with the 2002 Agreement until the oil was found and Tony Quinn was illegally made a member of the company – then paranoia set in. At a meeting in Hamhaugh Island, a new course was set – rather than being an openly operated commercial company, INE was to become a tightly controlled “cultist” entity where the Judgment states that Quinn and Morrice made no distinction between the company property (owned by all the shareholders) and their own. The company was under little, if any, threat from a hostile takeover as most of the Class B shareholders were loyal Educoists and in any case had to offer their shares first to the Board or other members before they could sell them to a third party.

And even supposing someone – another oil company, say, had bought up some or all of the Class B shares at a good price for the shareholders? The Class A shareholders still had a majority – according to the Judgment [Clause 41], the Class A shareholders had 637,037 voting units (each Class A share counted for 2 voting units, each Class B share counted for 1 voting unit) out of a total of 952,298 voting units.

But another oil company or commercial investor would have known the business, and known its rights. It would not have tolerated the running of the company as a private purse for Quinn and Morrice, and gone to Court to assert its rights as an oppressed minority shareholder. That is what Quinn and Morrice wanted to prevent – being held to account, being made to act like normal people running a normal business giving shareholders their due. Anyone who could do that was in their sights.

Susan Morrice was quite bare-faced about this, giving evidence in the Caribbean Court. While Tony Quinn came up with a story about how he had been asked to draw up a new Operating Agreement because there had been some confusion about the previous one, she said [Clause 34]

“it was to concentrate power in the hands of the Class A members in order to prevent a hostile takeover orchestrated by the buying up of Class B shares”

She at least was open about this – the Judge stated frankly that Quinn had lied because

“he was reluctant to admit that the true reason for the changes was to freeze out the B membership”

i.e. his loyal Educo followers, who had spent hundreds of thousands of Euro on his courses and had had the business judgment and the enterprise he lacked, to invest in the company before oil was found

The Class B shareholders have been treated as the enemy – as the saying goes “yesterday’s bread (or investment money) is soon forgotten”.

And as for the other Originators, how are they doing? Well, they all lived to regret the signatures they were pressured to put on that Agreement – they were all refused the right to sell their shares, and refused dividends under the new agreement – despite being technically rich, they could not get their hands on their actual money. Paul Marriott was even unpaid for services he had supplied under a contract.

Paul Marriott fought a Court case to get his contract paid and force the company to buy his shares – this was settled out of court, but he had previously stated he would not back down so the probability is he was paid his claim.

Sheila McCaffrey fought a Court case and succeeded in getting the 2007 Operating Agreement declared illegal.

Jean Cornec is still fighting a Court case to get the majority of his payment for his shares which he sold to Susan Morrice. Because of the “savage” restrictions, he had to sell to her under her terms which included a “gagging order” which she claims he broke, entitling her to take his shares with only part of the purchase money paid.

These are ruthless actions, and Susan Morrice has been at the heart of treating her former colleagues and collaborators this way.

Yet her second technique of persuasion is to be a nice, sweet person.

“Mr. Marriott said he signed because he could not bear seeing Ms Morrice in a state of what he described as near hysteria at the refusal of the others to sign” [Clause 34]

Susan Morrice has a tendency to present herself as some kind of oil saint – from her website; one would think that oil was a spiritual substance rather than a commercial mineral just like coal or iron ore. Yet the record of her expenses charged to the company is one of extreme extravagance paid for with other peoples’ money.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/08/22/will-the-real-susan-morrice-please-stand-up/

If any one of them had refused to sign, even if the others had signed and a majority was in favour, they could have petitioned the Commercial Court in the Caribbean for redress as an oppressed shareholder. They would have had a clear case, Judge Hogan in the Irish High Court agreed with Judge Bannister’s judgment that it was a clear case of oppression. But because Sheila McCaffrey had signed the agreement, Judge Bannister held her to blame for her own problems which were caused by the agreement, and did not order the company to be dissolved at her request, even though he thought it ought to be dissolved because of the scandalous way it was being run, because she had forfeited her right to relief by signing. He can only grant relief to people who are entitled to relief – that is why only the Class B shareholders, who were never given the opportunity to vote for or sign anything, can seek relief.

Susan Morrice is behaving just as Judge Bannister predicted, that is, doing what she wants regardless of the Judgment– issuing dividends (just before the meeting – it’s like a political election, being bribed with your own money!) even though Quinn’s shares are probably illegal, even though past payouts have not been issued equally to all shareholders, showing no remorse for her scandalous treatment of her colleagues and the Class B shareholders which was clearly recorded in the Judgment.

So if under pressure of some enemy that Susan Morrice tells you is at the gate, threatening the company, you vote for something, you may lose your right to relief. You don’t have to vote for anything. Susan Morrice is running from Court to Court, spending shareholders’ money, trying to cook up bogeymen just as she did at the Belize meeting. She claims that “negative remarks” are damaging the company – the company has oil coming out of the ground, which is worth money, no negative remarks are going to change that.

She claims that investors were put off by the negative remarks. The company has oil coming out of the ground; banks will lend it money to operate, with the profits it’s supposed to be making why does it even need to seek investors? Giving investors a new share in the company will only dilute the value of the existing shareholders. There is no guarantee that new explorations will find new oil – Mike Usher is no longer there. All shareholders deserve the chance to vote on whether they want their successful oil company to spend money and take risks on finding more oil, or whether that should happen in a separate company that Quinn, Morrice and some investors can invest in if they want to.

Let us remember that neither Quinn nor Morrice has ever been on record as putting a cent of their own money into the company. All they have ever done, or seem to want to do, is strip money out of it for their own purposes.

Hysterical and/or weepy is the technique of control!

So if, at the meeting, when the arguments rage, she attempts to present the company as in dire need of investors or protection from enemies so you must vote now for something that has been rushed at you, or then becomes hysterical and/or weepy about all the good she plans to do with the money and the projects that will be threatened if she can’t run the company in the way she wants, that you just don’t understand because she knows about the business and you don’t, and/or brings in a bunch of people you’ve never seen or heard of before who tell you that you must agree with her or dire consequences will ensue. You will remember her weepy presentation before Justice Bannister:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/09/26/unofficial-transcripts-of-the-case-before-justice-edward-bannister/

Remember

  • if you vote for something and then regret it later, you may lose your right to petition the Court for relief
  • the company is very successful, pumping oil, what’s the emergency? If there is an emergency, the management of the company must be seriously wrong
  • the sky is not going to fall in while you take impartial and expert advice and anybody who tries to persuade you otherwise does not have your interests at heart
  • the 2002 Agreement is currently the legal Operating Agreement of the company and doesn’t need changing as it gives excellent rights and protection both to the company and its shareholders
  • the Bogeyman does not exist

And the last people who did what she wanted at a meeting really, really wish they hadn’t.

Remember too that DI will be at the Bewley’s Airport Hotel to advise you. Not on your shareholding or about the INE. No rather about the power of influence and if you have already been a victim of the Quinn effect this leaves you susceptible to the Morrice crying or hysteria game which is her under the Quinn influence.
