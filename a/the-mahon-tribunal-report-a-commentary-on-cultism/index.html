The Mahon Tribunal Report a commentary on cultism

Conferences & CommentariesCultism

Dialogue Ireland has been concerned at the undue influence found in many cultist groups and we bring these to the attention of the public. However, for most of the period of the last administration we found that we could not get any support from our government for the issues that victims and survivors of cultism experienced. We now have some idea why!

This report helps us to evaluate how undue influence and a lack transparency can have terrible results  for the ordinary citizen.

http://www.rte.ie/news/2012/0322/mahon.html

The ghost estates, the planning process weighted not to give the majority of people a good lifestyle but rather to enrich a few with the inside knowledge won by chicanery resulting in a life of opulence for a few resulting  from the abuse of power. It seems now that Bertie Ahern used the Tribunal as a means of delaying the evil day so he could leave office without the full rigours of the law affecting him. The Tribunal cannot be used as a basis of prosecution, so it shows the principle that justice delayed is justice denied. If one considers the geography of Drumcondra, one notes that there are two Bank of Ireland branches within 10 minutes walk of St Luke’s. Then there is the AIB Bank nearly across the road. Just up from is the Irish Permanent. Then there was a First Active and an EBS about two minutes walk from there as well. Let us not forget the Irish Nationwide about 30 seconds away….

Here we see the reality of people going to O’Connell Street to do banking where it was impossible to park?  A Minister of Finance without a bank account? St Luke’s was for insiders for the true believers, not for anyone who raised any questions. Insiders who were placed in all kind of jobs and where the tentacles of power were spawned.

Using the lens of cultism can assist us to see how people were overwhelmed by an apparently unassuming indirect use of Charisma. A smoke and daggers, Apple Tart and Cart type humility. The cult of personality, the tendency to put the party interest above the public interest, and the denigration of opposing thought all lead to a cultist mentality. Commit suicide!

The speech at the graveside of Charles J Haughey represents his real views the rest was surface rhetoric. His primal theological views about the waters of Baptism will give him the possibility to reflect on his own mortality.

http://www.rte.ie/news/2006/0616/haughey.html#video
