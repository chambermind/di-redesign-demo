Urban Outreach has been struck off as a company.

Victory Outreach

You can read the accounts of Urban Outreach. We are not not yet clear whether Valdez has control of the company or whether those who have remained in VOI can outvote those who have left and have also been excommunicated for serious breaches of both Christian ethics and company law.

The addresses are not all correct at this time. But clearly the names of the directors are. If any of them have resigned it would have needed to be by resolution of the company. Anyone here is liable for the affairs of the company. If they do not address the issues connected to their strike off they may incur charges up to €100 a day.
