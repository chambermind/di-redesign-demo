Gardai say no progress yet in dog slaying case

Satanism

Gardai say no progress yet in dog slaying case

http://www.wicklowpeople.ie/news/gardai-say-no-progress-yet-in-dog-slaying-case-1197220.html

 Thursday September 07 2006

Gardai in Enniskerry have said that no progress has been made in the investigation into the cult-like slaying of a dog at Knocksink Woods last week.

A garda spokesman said on Monday that despite an appeal to the public for information, only one call had been received which amounted to ‘nothing substantial.’

News of the attack last week stunned the local community, and indeed the country, in both its ferocity and manner. The body of the dog was discovered by passers-by atop a concrete altar surrounded by stones. Both the gardai and the WSPCA undertook an investigation into the killing.

The news sparked debate about the possible existence of satanic activities taking place in this country, however, Mike Garde of Dialogue Ireland, an organisation which specialises in the emergence of new religions and cultism, said there is little evidence to suggest that such behaviour takes place on a frequent basis.

‘In general, Ireland does not have high number of occurrences of a Satanic nature. I think the last one that is on record was in the 1990s, in 1992, I think, again in Wicklow, when a number of youths came down from Dublin to Blessington, to a cemetery there, where they were looking for skulls and things like that,’ he said, speaking from his office in Dublin on Monday afternoon.

Mr Garde said that from what he had read and heard about the Knocksink killing, it would suggest to him an attention to detail and permanence unlike other such cases in Ireland.

‘There seems to be a deeper significance to this…the dog seems to have been aware of the danger it was in and either was known to its attackers or else primed for the purpose [of killing it].

He suggested that the Gardai should liaise with the FBI in the United States, which has a unit which focuses especially on such behaviour.

Meanwhile, Gardai have renewed their public appeal for information, and have invited calls from members of the public to Enniskerry Garda Station on (01) 6665750 or Bray at (01) 6665300.
