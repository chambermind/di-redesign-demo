From the archives – the Jim Jones tape

ChristianConferences & Commentaries

One of the difficulties in educating people regarding the field of cultism is due to the sheer inadequacy of language when it comes to describing that field. Often it is found that words, no matter how carefully chosen, simply fail to convey much of the nuance and subtlety of this topic. Crowd psychology, loaded language, redefinition of terms, the absolute authority of the leader, etc. are all concepts that are very easy to label, but incredibly difficult to describe and articulate.

To help illuminate the subject we present here the infamous Jim Jones tape, wherein Jones advocates mass suicide:

Jim Jones death tape (28Mb mp3, 44 minutes)

While this tape is quite long, it offers a rare insight into aspects of cultism that cannot be captured with mere words. Some aspects to note when listening to the tape include:

– The use of crowd psychology to control dissenters.

– The manner in which Jones reframes the act of suicide.

– The comments from some crowd members about how much their lives improved with Jones.

– The attitudes espoused towards the outside world.

It all adds up to 44 minutes of some of the most harrowing audio ever recorded.
