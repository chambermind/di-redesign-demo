Michael Meegan, described by the Irish Independent as a ‘charity worker’

Icross Charity

Michael Meegan, described by the Irish Independent as a ‘charity worker’ [http://www.independent.ie/national-news/courts/charity-worker-gets-settlement-and-apology-from-irish-daily-mail-over-sexual-misconduct-claims-3292894.html], has received a High Court apology for the Daily Mail’s 2010 publication of allegations of sexual misconduct, made by a number of people in affidavits submitted to the court [available here: https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/05/01/archive-following-the-mail-groups-success-in-the-courts-april-19-2010/].

The Irish Mail on Sunday has worked tirelessly to bring public scrutiny to bear on Meegan’s various activities, particularly those in Kenya. Meegan is said to have spent much of the last 30 years in ‘the bush’, but evidence for the work he claims to have carried out is often superficial. Meegan was a founder member of ICROSS (International Community for the Relief of Starvation and Suffering).

http://www.icrossinternational.org/

Dialogue Ireland has developed a publicly accessible repository of documents pertaining to Meegan and ICROSS. Much of the information available on the web is not reliable, so the Dialogue Ireland site is the best place to start. All the relevant articles published in the Mail on Sunday and other newspapers are available there.
