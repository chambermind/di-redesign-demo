Oil, Oil everywhere not a drop to drink?

Tony Quinn

Oil, Oil everywhere not a drop to drink?

It is imperative, despite legal threats, that this material is available for the public to read in the absence of the Irish media’s now two year silence in regard to theis issue of public interest. Dialogue Ireland received legal threats last year. We would value a visit to a court to have this issue clarified.

Having received advice on the company we publish this letter in the public interest.

Dear INE Member. Greetings.

I sent a ‘Very Special New Year Reflection’ to Ms. Morrice asking her to ensure that an email I sent to her was shared with all INE/BNE Directors.

I don’t know if she did this or not.

However, I do know that within a day I was in receipt of legal threats from her and INE from a Mr Brian Deeny of Elliott Duffy and Garrett, Commercial Lawyers in Belfast, basically saying that if I shared my email to her and the Directors with you then I would be held liable for damages for libel etc. etc.

I would love to share my response to her directly with you, which resulted out of her communication posted on the so-called ‘communications channel’, however to do so would be only asking for even more trouble.

So, I am instead writing to you to once again, as is my absolute legal right, to express some of my concerns of late, some of which are a direct result of what I consider direct propaganda posted on our members communication channel by Susan Morrice in support of one of our Directors (TQ’s) personal business interests Educo.

Anyway, in wishing you all a happy and prosperous new year I would like to remind you that Tony Quinn has created disharmony within our company and amongst investors and directors both before and since his involvement in INE, and remind you that he actively discouraged others from investing in the project in its early days when one could have bravely invested at $15 per share instead of the now $400. I hope he enjoys his loan from INE, which if he took would amount to $1.28M (64,000 shares X $20), which I and other non beneficiaries will be aiding repaying.

Perhaps the TQ memory of what he has said and done in the past is why he wants us all to forgive and forget the past and all make up and be friends?

As we enter a new decade 2010 I want to share and reflect with you upon how much profit has been paid to members or shared with members as a result of our exceptionally successful company. None!

If and when I/we see imminent 2009 accounts for BNE and INE I/we will be delighted to see that 2009 was a fantastic year, our best year ever, with our profits soaring to possibly beyond $50 million plus.

This is mainly due to the cessation of seismic payments! And we all of course know who the major players are in the company to which these seismic payments were made! The great profits for 2009 will also of course be due to the maintenance of a more stable but rising market price for oil at over $70 per barrel. (Currently $82+pb).

Our company’s financial success in 2009 is good news and should result in a healthy dividend of at least $35-$40 per member unit with plenty over for the development of the Never Delay oil field.

This news is also good news as never again should I/we have to feel that I or any other member should have to borrow our own money by way of a loan program. A dividend would at last create a yield on our investment from our company, create value to our membership and thus enable the development and creation of a market for our shares. (A share with no yield or potential for yield is of no interest to anyone). I hope from the bottom of my heart that this could never be the intention of anybody to do with our company?

Those members who have taken a loan I presume will know with whom they have actually taken out a loan and will be heartened to also know that those who didn’t, (and those who wouldn’t be accepted for the loan scheme, such as myself) will be sharing the burden of repaying that $10 Million and its interest to the banks out of their and my own future profits.

(50% of this $10M loaned, probably went to a few selected Class A members, who owing to their domiciles’ are possibly unlikely to be taxed on their loans unlike those in Ireland or the UK).

I presume Susan Morrice’s share of the loans was for her 64,000 shares as well as the 64,000 shares she purportedly purchased from Mr. Cornec. That possibly would have made a nice Christmas present of $2.56 Million to herself? Or perhaps she followed the advice of International lawyers and did not touch it with a barge pole! (‘It’ being the ‘loan release program’ to get us money in our pockets before Christmas in the most tax efficient way!)

I wonder what KPMG would consider to this inequitable apparent distribution and inequitable repayment scheme in which those that didn’t take loans pay repayments for those that did!? This seems really fair doesn’t it!?  It is also interesting that an implication from her recent ‘special new year reflection’ is that our company has realized $19 million in profits for investors. Our very successful company has paid nothing from its $100 M’s to Class B members over the past 7 years. All this $19M is money paid from one Educoist or aspirant member to another!

It is about time that we were treated by way of dividend and treated on an equitable basis otherwise the disharmony, mistrust and inequitable relations will only continue and probably increase.

I, like you, should be looking forward to receiving Accounts for 2009 for both BNE and INE quite soon.

Our long awaited and oft requested company’s Operating Agreement changed (without our consent or notification).

Our new Membership Certificates promised this month, (don’t forget to ask now for your old one back). (It would look good in a picture frame)!

Communication details of all members and our questions answered from numerous previous requests as promised by our company’s legal representative Mr. Clark Onstad. We have so much to look forward to.

I, like Ms. Morrice, also look forward to seeing you on the 10th of January and thank Ms. Morrice for the obvious open invitation to all INE Members to the Sunday meeting.

I am sure many non Educo members of our company both past and present will also enjoy attending to hear what Mr. Quinn has to say for himself to us his financial providers.

It is highly unlikely though that our attendance will increase the percentage of Educoists amongst INE members!

Are I and our ex directors included in Susan Morrice’s 90% statistic, of course we are!

It would be interesting though for us to really know and calculate how much support there is for the involvement of TQ and Educo in our company! I presume if one is not supportive of TQ then one is against our company and that anyone taking a loan has to be a supporter of TQ and Educo or they are in breach of their promissory notes?! What a mess!

The only promise INE Members want from our company very soon is a promise of a proper Dividend for all the years in profit, especially for our best year ever 2009. We surely don’t want inequitable loan schemes; to borrow without being given proper terms and conditions; and be denied to know to whom we will even be indebted to!

I am looking forward to an equitable and prosperous 2010 and of course a financial yield on my investment by way of a DIVIDEND.
