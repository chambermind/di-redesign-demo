Educo Health aka Tony Quinn tries to penetrate Tesco

Tony Quinn

Dialogue Ireland has been in touch with Tesco after they noted the attempts by Educo Health to gain undue influence with their products in Tesco Stores. Quinn’s company tries to send their personnel in to surreptitiously place brochures which are often in competition with products in store.

This brochure was found in Tesco Phibsborough on Thursday May 3, 2012. The brochures were also found in Tesco, Parnell St along with Falun Gong’s newspaper “New Epoch.”

Educo and Tesco

Not only that but they try to direct people to their own store. It is noteworthy that they advertise themselves under the more neutral name of Educo Health due to bad name Quinn is getting in Ireland due to his extremely immoral actions in making sure his own Educoists do not receive any dividends. This extraordinary when you consider that when the oil was discovered he was totally opposed to to it thinking it would effect his seminar business.  This a bit like Scientology talking about Dianetics rather than Scientology in the Irish context.

These attempts to misuse the stores is totally at variance with Tesco policy. Dialogue Ireland has received intelligence that a senior executive from a PR company which works with Tesco is linked with these efforts to place Educo Material in shops. She is known to be a fanatical devotee to Quinn and has lost her ability to keep the boundaries between her work and her private life in check.
