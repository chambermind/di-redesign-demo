Abuse by Church and Irish institutions

Statement of The Rt. Revd. Alan Winton Bishop of Thetford on ministry of Willi Stewart.

Dear Mike,

Thank you for sending the various letters, e-mails and notes for our attention.

We complied fully with our rigorous safeguarding procedures in appointing Willi Steward to the Chet Valley Benefice.  We always take any safeguarding information we receive seriously and the material you have sent has been reviewed by our safeguarding officer and the diocesan bishop.  Safeguarding issues are, in any case, regularly reviewed with all our clergy in the course of the oversight exercised by the bishops of the diocese.

With all good wishes

+ Alan

The Rt. Revd. Alan Winton

Bishop of Thetford

The Red House,

53, Norwich Road,

Stoke Holy Cross,

Norwich. NR14 8AB.

bishop.thetford@norwich.anglican.org
