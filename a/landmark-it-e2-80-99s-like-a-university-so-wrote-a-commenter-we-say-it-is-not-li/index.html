“It’s like a university.” so wrote a commenter. We say it is not like that at all.

Good documentary by French TV and hosted by an Australian cultwatch group:
http://www.culthelp.info/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=1243&Itemid=12url

The undercover footage says it all really. I will add that one of the aspects of this that will probably be missed by many is that the concepts Alain Roth is teaching are straight out of Scientology.
