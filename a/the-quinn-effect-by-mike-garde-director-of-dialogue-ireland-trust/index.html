THE QUINN EFFECT -By Mike Garde, Director of Dialogue Ireland Trust

Tony Quinn

THE QUINN EFFECT:

As another Educo seminar draws near on Sunday January 9, 2011. It is important to reflect on the attempts to bring Tony Quinn to justice and to make the public aware of his activities. Dialogue Ireland held a protest to alert the public to what Quinn was doing two years ago at the same event.

http://www.dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/video/tq%20protest.html

He has represented himself for nearly forty years as a putative Messiah, disciple of Aleister Crowley, http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleister_Crowley L Ron Hubbard the founder of Scientology. Then he claims to be the founder of Yoga in Ireland, his KI massage programme, the Health Food Shops, http://www.tonyquinn.com/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=43&Itemid=43

The use of Hypnosis is an important key to understanding his methodology and this well illustrated in the use of this discipline in the fight with Steve Collins and Chris Eubank,

http://www.dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/video/boxingclever.html

He appeared on the Late, Late Show attempting surgery without anaesthetic, his move into providing Educo seminars at foreign locations, and more recently his involvement with an Oil company in Belize. During this period he claimed to have obtained a Doctorate in Hypnotherapy, to be working towards a Master’s from the University of East London, a lower award, and then in recent years he claimed he was doing another doctorate. Neither his University nor his supervisor could ever provide evidence of any educational attainment?

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/01/18/the-tony-quinn-story-%E2%80%9Cfrom-butcher-to-oil-slick%E2%80%9D/

He has been a controversial figure since the early ‘70’s yet when invited to give an interview he declines and threatens any attempt to clarify what he is about with legal action. Generally the media keep away from him, and he rarely visits Ireland these days as he is the subject of many legal challenges

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/tony-quinn/

The legal challenge that frightens him most is that of Maire Lalor who has courageously left the organisation she was at the heart of and is now, having successfully thrown off his influence challenged him in the courts.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/01/12/tony-quinn-is-sued-for-%E2%80%98sex-assault%E2%80%99-by-nicola-tallant-sunday-world/

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/01/10/fitness-expert-quinn-accused-of-sex-assault

The current legal action by Paul Marriot in Nevis provides one of the first occasions Quinn will actually appear in a court room. This action if successful will hearten many, but we would be very stupid to confuse the internal affairs of a drilling Company and an oil company with striking a real blow against The Mighty Quinn’s Empire.

Quinn started as offering an alternative spiritual message in the early 70’s. He was anti-Catholic, but was viewed by his disciples as not been in conflict with the Church. Often his early meetings were held in Church halls. One of his early members remembers him being involved in a rite with a young woman which had him in the position of Jesus and the woman as Mary Magdalene. He saw Quinn after this Crowleyian rite the next morning rolled up with fear, believing the brothers of this woman would come and beat him up. The woman has not recovered to this day from this encounter.

There was then a move to holistic health, the shops which are his trade mark and Ki massage and body building. However, underlying this was a a cult like atmosphere where he had control over people. Reports about this go back to 1974.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2010/02/16/summary-of-media-coverage/

He always had seminars in Ireland, but it was not till the mid 90’s that Quinn started the International Educo seminars mainly on the Bahamas, but also in Monte Carlo, Egypt, Capri, Spain and the West coast of the States. Here there was an attempt to go into Life Coaching and how to become rich market. Even here he kept an esoteric level for the inner group of disciples who for instance included a big developer who was going to bed early to read the Aquarian Gospel of Jesus Christ. What was significant was that people who had real entrepreneurial gifts were spiritually castrated by involvement with Quinn.  Quinn claims he enhances material achievement, but the opposite occurs.

At the exoteric level it is about celebrity fame, going to the best restaurants, being seen in BT. In reality Quinn’s philosophy is UPM –“Use other peoples’ money.” This is very similar to the disease that afflicted Irish society during the Celtic twilight economy. That is exactly what the big developers did and the banks. So people would make comments like anyone who gets involved is an idiot and they are adults. They did not understand the spiritual and psychological programming that was taking place. Amazingly Quinn does not seem to be able to spend the money he has accumulated, he just holds onto it.

It was from this cohort of materialists that Quinn got involved with the oil. In fact at first when some Educoists suggested the purchase of oil shares to members of the Educo seminars Quinn was totally opposed. However, because some of these followers were also part of the inner religious circle he saw he could do UPM by bringing these high profile members under his influence. Obviously we do not know the inner working of this influence, but a person like Susan Morrice was totally under his control.

RTE shows how this process works: http://www.dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/video/Marcus.html

Over the years Dialogue Ireland has met hundreds of people who were involved with Quinn. It has tried to give advice and encouragement to people who feel like they have been literally and spiritually raped. As you can see from the comments on our blog there is a rage but yet a lack of clarity as how to react to their loss, dis-empowerment, and spiritual collapse. Some naturally want revenge, but tend to put too much faith in legal processes which can spin out of control and lead to no resolution. Also there is a fear to speak out against the Messiah as that can lead to eternal damnation or psychic attack. A level of darkness and depression surrounds them. Also they fear disparaging the oil company and thereby losing their shares, so are forced to abide by the crazy rules of the company. It is clear that Quinn’s game plan like the rest of the directors is to sell the company and literally fade away.

If that happens that would neatly happen around his 65 birthday.

So what then should we do:

  1. Continue to give advice and information.
  2. The internet is a massive force to bring to light what is going on. We have the largest archive in the world on this issue, and it is a helpful tool for ex members informing themselves and using our comment section to explore recovery. It is also a resource for lawyers to see the patterns they are encountering currently have been around since the early 70’s.
  3. Paul Marriott has taken the battle to Quinn, and others have cases pending. Paul’s case really goes for the jugular and is simple and direct.
  4. Maire Lalor’s case holds the key to breaking into the hermetically sealed Universe of the Quinnites. DI would be happy to administer a fighting Fund to support this case. Quinn’s strategy as was that of his lieutenant Vincent  Harford is to duck and weave, delay, confuse, attack and never defend. What we are saying as we do in regard to supporting the work of DI is we do not need people to give thousands, but use the donate button and if a lot give a little we can take on goliath.

5. Those Educoists, former Ki Massage and family members of those affected need to get organised. Ten years ago DI held a conference which    rattled Quinn’s cage. http://www.dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/conferences/2001conference.html

We need to hold a Conference where we would bring together legal, psychological and other cult experts to assist people as did the conference   in 2001.

6. It is difficult to confront Quinn at one of his foreign venues, but once it was clear he was back in Dublin it would be our aim to have more people protesting outside the RDS than are inside it. Now he will play the persecuted Christ inside, but gradually the penny will drop that the game is up. So even as he comes to these events surrounded by his former Irish Ranger security detail the roar from without will chill his soul.

7. We wish those people who will be going into the RDS well, they believe they have found the elixir of life. Cultism is the key not an attack on the person of Quinn. To misquote the Prophet Elijah, If Quinn is the Christ then follow him, If not all that is stopping you taking control of the solution is yourself.

Happy New Year and the hope of a Educoless future.
