HOLY GOD! ANOTHER JEWEL IN GALLAGHER’S CROWN

SEVENTH HEAVEN BY JIM GALLAGHER

MONEY-MAD House of Prayer guru Christina Gallagher has ANOTHER €2 million mansion home the Sunday World can reveal. This week we tracked down this astonishing property in England after it was put on the market. The fabulous house is registered to a member of Gallagher’s family and we can reveal today that it was bought WITHOUT a mortgage in 2009. The seven-bedroom and seven-bathroom home has its own lake, as well as an indoor swimming pool and snooker room.

The controversial leader of the House of Prayer on Achill Island who claims to speak regularly with the Virgin Mary, can also relax in six sumptuous reception rooms, a bar, steam room, gym, seven person hot tub and conservatory.  With its Italian marble floors, underfloor heating, CCTV, and walled gardens, it is fit for a queen. Gallagher is notorious for her luxurious tastes. When followers raised eyebrows  at her decision to fit a jacuzzi in the House of Prayer in Achill they were told: “Nothing is too good for Our Lady.”

Profit

The millionaire ‘mystic’ thought this astonishing property in the rolling hills of  Shropshire, England, would never come to light. It is not registered in her own name but that of her son Brendan, a plumber in Knockmore, Co Mayo, who could never afford such a home on his salary. The mansion, which has its own indoor lift, is now up for sale with a view to making a hefty profit. The sprawling five acre estate was snapped up for £1.8 million sterling in October 2009 but just 17 months later it is on the market for a whopping £2,250,000. This would make the owner a profit of £450,000 sterling, or well more than €500,000. The magnificent country house brings to SIX the number of luxury private properties the Sunday World has linked to Gallagher, a woman who insists she has no interest in materials things and would happily live in a tent. Three years ago we shocked the nation when we uncovered the fabulous €4 million mansion where Gallagher lives in plush Malahide, North Co Dublin. Her millionaires’ row home is close to that of wealthy pop star Ronan Keating and Westlife’s Nicky Byrne. We also uncovered the astonishing €1 million mansion Gallagher bought for her daughter Mary outside Ballina, the biggest house for miles around. We unlocked the secrets of another €1 million home just outside Newport, Co Mayo, which was filled with antiques, chandeliers and a four-poster bed Gallagher had imported specially from Harrods of London.

Income

We tracked down another house Gallagher bought for her son Brendan in her native Knockmore, Co Mayo. That was also bought without a mortgage. And Gallagher still partly owns the family home in Gortnadrehy, Foxford, she shared with estranged husband Paddy before they separated The unveiling of this secret English mansion will put more pressure on Gallagher to explain once and for all where all the money has come from. She does not work and has no formal income. We revealed three years ago how she was living like a pop star, driving a top of the range €120,000 BMW, while encouraging her elderly Catholic followers up and hand over their life savings to her “mission.”

We uncovered a memo to American followers in which it was explained that she wanted “cash only” from now on and no more cheques. And the money was to be sent directly to her home so she could “thank people personally.” Following our revelations about her wealthy private life, three former followers took legal action to get back €250,000 they had handed the Mayo mystic. Rather than face a public court case Gallagher quickly returned their money within a month. RTE’s Primetime and TV3, along with numerous newspapers, followed up the Sunday World’s shocking revelations about the House of Prayer leader. But the self-styled “humble housewife” has refused to give one word of explanation about her sumptuous lifestyle despite numerous requests. Accounts published just two months ago showed that the House of Prayer was sitting on profits of €1.72 million last year.

But former followers who have quit Gallagher’s cult-like organisation have asked what happened to the huge amounts of cash that poured in over 20 years. She always insisted on opening all mail herself. Nearly two years after our initial revelations we revealed that John Rooney, Gallagher’s main fundraiser in America where she has five Houses of Prayer, was an ex-convict. The 66-year-old fraudster was sentenced to 18 months in prison after he admitted taking part in a massive $15 million scam against banks across several states. Gallagher’s fabulous home in Malahide is registered in his name. Devotees were asked to sign cheques to John Rooney rather than the House of Prayer. The latest mansion, called Lower Hall, in the village of Knockin near Oswestry, Shropshire, ten miles from the Welsh border, is a magnificent modern home built just 11 years ago. Described in its publicity material as a “beautifully presented Manor House” with fine views, it offers “a very private & secure setting behind walls & electric gates.” The brochure says: “There is a luxurious master bedroom suite that extends across the southern facade to include the bedroom, bathroom, dressing room, sitting room & access to a sun room & balcony.

Mysterious

“The property is approached via a long drive lined with oak trees. The inner courtyard is flanked by lawns & evergreen oaks. “There is a decked terrace with awning and lower terrace with a water feature, sitting and BBQ area. Large wildlife pond.” The house, seven miles from Oswestry and 12 from Shrewsbury, has a massive 11,453sq ft of floor space or 1,064sq m. Its recently added solar panels on a south-facing roof produce electricity which is sold to the national grid. The wildlife pond with fountain is stocked with large Koi Carp and Orph fish. The gardens are stocked with ornamental trees, shrubs and double gates to the grounds beyond. This week neighbours in Knockin spoke of the mysterious “Irish woman” who had bought the house but who spent very little time there. Gallagher introduced herself to one local as “Bridget,” her second name which she also used on land register records for her Newport home. One friendly local called at the mansion with a cake to welcome the new owner to the village but didn’t get past the electronic gate. One neighbour said: “You never see her. She hasn’t been there since the freezing weather before Christmas. The House of Prayer had nobody available for comment when the Sunday World tried to contact Christina Gallagher there yesterday.
