VOI has failed to keep its word to respond to the issues surrounding the abuse of its members

Victory Outreach

Dialogue Ireland along with the general public was appalled by the revelations on the Prime Time documentary back in 2013.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/12/18/victory-outreach-prime-time-special-report/#more-12328

Towards the end of 2014 we embarked on a process of engagement with Paul Lloyd who had he claimed assumed the leadership of the Irish work and see if we could gain an understanding of what happened. We wanted to see if the abuse was as Paul suggested the result of a rotten apple in the barrel or a more systemic problem,  and to see if there is a difference between the ministry run by Andy Valdez and the restructured VOI. We met with Paul Lloyd in late December and hoped this statement would lead to a transparent and clear response. We expected a reply within a month, and hoped this earlier statement would lead to a transformed organisation.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2014/12/18/victory-outreach-international-statement-on-the-situation-in-dublin-ireland/

We accepted that Paul needed more time when he was ill soon after me met. However, whenever we requested him to honour his commitment we were told he was just getting onto a flight or coming back from some foreign destination.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2015/01/09/voi-dialogue-with-di-delayed-due-to-ill-health-of-paul-lloyd/

We are hereby giving notice that the report we produced for VOI will be published soon and we intend to give our considered opinion as to their current situation and interpret their failure to respond. We have formed the view they can’t respond because the the structure of VOI is in fact Papal Pentecostalism which explains why nothing can be done.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2014/12/23/victory-outreach-international-explains-their-structures/

We have also come to the conclusion that there was never an intention to reply. Finally in April we sent a paper outlining our understanding of the VOI structure worldwide, and some basic questions as to their current practice in Ireland. The former we asked Paul to forward to his HQ and the latter we asked him to respond to. We expected some of these points could be answered in a few days, and the other we asked him to take a month to get a response from his leadership. We also came to the conclusion that in the end Paul has no authority at all and in practice has no delegated authority to address any questions hence his ongoing excuses and and silence.
