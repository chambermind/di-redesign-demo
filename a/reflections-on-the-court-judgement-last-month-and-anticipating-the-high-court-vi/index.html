Reflections on the Court Judgement last month and anticipating the High Court visit next week.

Tony Quinn

The DI position in relation to being deposed is quite clear. We see it as a proxy attempt and strategy to destroy DI and protect Quinn based on a false conspiracy theory.  We clearly have no interest or shares in the oil company. Our only interests are charitable providing information and help to the public regarding cultist groups. We have been attacked by a bully using false legal premises and other people’s money.  DI is clearly not afraid to go to a court and give information and evidence at any time but does not accept it should willingly submit to the various false premises in the Quinn-Morrice actions without drawing attention to them by legal means and by the internet. Sheila McCaffrey is a Director of the company, Quinn is not. The only people who have been left out of the loop are the B Shareholders.

In summary, we have never and will never have any interest in the oil issue. Our concern is not to take out Quinn. We are not opposed to him as a person but to his actions and the devastation of so many lives.

We salute all the victims and all the survivors, and with this in mind we will now give you our sense of where we are at just before the High Court case next Tuesday.  When you are in a storm you do not see the calm and the sunshine which is waiting to appear. For all its importance these legal disputes will in the longer term have no major influence on human events. Looking at the big picture and standing back we can now see the outline of a solution.

1. Justice Bannister’s judgement last month has given us a road map for the future.

http://www.eccourts.org/judgments/decisions/2012/HC/stkitts/SMLifeVenturesvSusanMorriceetal.pdf

2. Even though Sheila did not obtain her desired end, she has paved the way for the recognition that without the B Shareholders there is                    no future for the company.  Are they up to the task, or are they still hiding behind the respective camps?

3. On the comment section we often get responses to the effect that Quinn is about to go down. These are naïve and wishful thinking. However, we have received numerous calls which indicate a tipping point has been reached. The Court Judgement is that point and not only the judgement but many seem to have transcripts of the case which are devastating for Quinn. We have never received the level of responses we are at present. These include the Yoga gang, the Jesus Christ brigade and the new materialist Celtic Tiger Educo cubs. We are getting intelligence from the very top even about the bins in Denver and the dog attacks. We must admit though we are finding it difficult to find any witness to Tony’s bumping into people in Penney’s. I have spent over 8 hours talking to people about their experiences. Some are still quite favourable to Quinn but they are within an inch of giving him the push. Remember the Berlin wall, things tend to be slow to change and then whoosh.

4. From sources in Jersey we have a deep throat who suggests that Quinn’s Empire is about €100m. His goal is not to enjoy his wealth but to become a billionaire. There is nothing worse than being rich and not being able to enjoy it.

5.  We will keep you posted and after the case on Tuesday we will publish all legal documents to give you an insight into the world of legal nirvana. If it was not for the fact that the B Shareholders are paying for all this nonsense I would send a legal response to their lawyers every couple of hours as I would Scientologists. Then they charge like the lawyers at the Tribunals. Bye bye money.
