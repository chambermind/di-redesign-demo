Abundant Life Limerick is not associated with Abundant Life /Victory Galway.

EvangelicalismVictory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

DI has had several phone calls from people who are confusing the Abundant Life church Limerick with what we knew as Abundant Life Galway which is now part of Victory Galway under the leadership of Brendan Hade and Thomas Cooney. You may recall that Kevin Sanford handed over Abundant Life to Brendan Hade who in turn merged Abundant Life Galway with The Living Word Church run by Thomas Cooney and his wife. The new church is now called Victory Galway and is under the control of Victory Dublin.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/faith-movement-abundant-life/

Kevin Sanford and Heather Sanford have fled back to Texas leaving massive debts and a line of people looking to be paid. Walter Hallam the pastor of Abundant Life Texas and pastor to Kevin Sanford/Brendan Hade had distanced himself from the Sanfords as it would appear has Brendan Hade.

Recently a very positive TV documentary showed Abundant Life Limerick and it was a very positive story where Church of Ireland Bishop Trevor Williams went undercover as part of an RTE Would You Believe programme.

http://www.rte.ie/player/#!v=1141097

http://www.rte.ie/player/#!v=1142080

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/04/01/bishop-undercover-series/

People unfortunately believe because of the close link with names that this church is one and the same.

Dialogue Ireland was in touch with Pastor Rev Kevin Fitzgibbon stated unequivocally that, “Abundant Life, Limerick has no relationship, and never had, with the church known as Abundant Life Galway.”
