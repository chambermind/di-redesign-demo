Who really controls Victory Galway? From a disgruntled member of Victory Galway!

EvangelicalismVictory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

Recently a disgruntled member of Victory Galway made contact with DI. They wanted to express their concerns of the behaviour of some of the church members.

This person was a member of Abundant Life, the church that collapsed as a result of the massive debts occurred by Pastors Kevin and Heather Sanford. For those of you who do not recall, the Sanfords were sent to Ireland by Walter Hallam from Abundant Life in Texas to start a church.

They then spent the next ten years manipulating people and leaving a trail of destruction in their wake. The doors finally closed on Abundant Life when Kevin lost a High Court Case for unpaid debts believed to be in the region of €100,000. The bulk of this debt was for unpaid rent on a building used by the church.

Kevin and Heather finally threw in the towel and before they left they handed the church over to Brendan and Sheila Hade who themselves are awaiting a day in the High Court for a pending court case against them. Brendan Hade spoke very highly of the Sanfords until an exposé in a tabloid newspaper highlighted the level of abuse that had taken place at the hands of the Sanfords. Prior to the exposé, Brendan Hade had committed to travelling to Galway regularly to steer the ship on a steady course. Unbeknown to Kevin Sanford, Brendan Hade brought in Pastor Thomas Cooney from another ailing church in Galway called the ‘Living Word Church’.

It is understood that Kevin and Thomas Cooney would not have been the best of friends and Kevin may have felt betrayed by the actions of Brendan Hade.

Kevin explained he was heading home to take up a new challenge, leading a new church in El Paso, Texas. DI understands and Kevin has confirmed this in his recent blog that this plan is no longer happening and it is believed this is as a result of the a withdrawal of support from Walter Hallam.

So where does this all lead now? Well you would have thought that Kevin and Heather would have washed their hands of the Church in Ireland but rather the opposite. In fact the reason why we were contacted by the church member was because they were approached to make a contribution to a fund for ‘Fathers Day’ that would be presented to Kevin in the USA by a member of the Victory Church, Galway. In fact DI has learned of a number of visits from Victory Galway church members to the Sanfords in Texas. These are members who still see Kevin and Heather as their pastors and the disgruntled member contacted us because they believe such actions are destructive in any future that Victory Galway are to have in Ireland under the pastoral leadership of Thomas Cooney and Brendan Hade.

DI really has a few points or questions regarding this matter.

 Is Thomas Cooney aware of the behaviour of some of his congregation as he tries to build and repair the damage caused over the years by the Sanfords?

If not, this means that Thomas Cooney who has been trying to raise much needed funds to address their own financial concerns is fighting a losing battle as the same people he has in leadership roles within the new church are actually acting and funding the Sanfords behind his back? So who really controls Victory Galway if this is happening?  If so this means that Thomas Cooney and Brendan Hade are actually supporting the Sanfords financially by allowing church members to do so.  Will they then look to pay the debts left behind by Kevin Sanford in the name of Abundant Life?

We would encourage people to log on to Kevin’s blog to have a read as we believe once this has been highlighted to him he is sure to edit or delete the postings as in previous cases.
