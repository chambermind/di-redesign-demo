Buddhaisation of Western Culture?

BuddhistTibetan Buddhism = Lamaism

As we look at Lamaism we need to understand that there is now a plausibility
structure which enables this view point to penetrate deeply into our culture.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/lamaism/
This photo is one which shows how where in Catholic Ireland Jesus, Mary and St. Francis of Assisi have been replaced by the Buddha. This was taken at a Hardware Shop in Drumcondra, Dublin, but the same can be found in Garden Centres. However, it is interesting to note that most of these statues are now on sale at a reduced price,  as even they are now under question. In other words, it is clear that under the severe pressure of the recession all beliefs and values are up for grabs, and it is likely that we will not be following  George Harrison but more likely Madonna.
