Victory or defeat, The show must go on?

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

“The prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests rule on their {own} authority; and My people love it so! But what will you do at the end of it?   “.   Jeremiah 5:31

 

 

As we watch with sadness what could be seen as another victim of the death of the so called Celtic tiger, we need not to let our collective anger at the banks, make us blind to the underlying issues in this case, as we often do these days. We can be sideswiped by the great good for example done by Sean Quinn in his home county, and yes he did incredible things, but he did so with chronic disregard for rules and regulatory controls. When the banks come calling he can cry foul and he can point to all his exploits but he cannot say I am without fault.  Similarly many will point to the good and the great good that the pastors and elders have been involved with at Victory Tallaght, and will paint the banks as the aggressor the enemy to be resisted, but is all as it should be with Victory Centre. We will have to wait the explanation for the collapse which was so swift. We have a good sense of what the cause was but for reasons of confidentiality can’t publish it yet.

Reports indicate that multiples of millions are owed by The Victory Trust that operates this church, the courts apparently also agree and have given the appointed receivers leave to reclaim their assets. Surprising then that Pastor Hade last Sunday gathered the faithful and told them that nothing is owed and that all will be well.   As Abraham Lincoln said, “you can fool all of the people some of the time, and some of the people all of the time but you can’t fool all of them all of the time.”

But this is a group of leaders who have held to the type of views on prosperity ever since they started. The Celtic Tiger gave them the ability with unlimited cash from banks to realise their wildest dreams in a tower of Babel type building scheme dedicated to the so called “prosperity gospel.”

The difference between the Sean Dunne’s of this world and the Victory Church is the one took his authority from the banks, whereas Victory claimed divine authorisation and blessing for their actions. We have pity on the former, but here we have to be more critical as when you couch your views in Sacred Scripture and claim divine authority for your actions it is not just an issue of a terrible bankruptcy, but an example of spiritual, moral and ethical failure. What you sow you reap.

 The prosperity gospel is the Evangelical* Christian’s Ponzi Scheme.

 It’s main exponents internationally came out of what was known as ‘The Word of Faith’ movement, teachers such as Kenneth Copeland, Creflo Dollar, Jesse Duplantis and Morris Cerullo. This sort of teaching has been prevalent in Victory.

 The basic premise of the Prosperity Gospel is that as we operate in faith, God responds with lavish material blessings. In essence as we invest in the kingdom we are told that there will be a positive return. The bible of course does speak about giving and blessing, but proponents of this gospel are using it to fleece the flock. Creflo dollar for example famously said ” if you want a 100.000 dollar salary, start paying the tithe ( 10%) of that salary now in faith.

Regular offers of special blessings for those who give special offerings usual specified amounts are a constant theme. As recently as 2011 Benny Hinn was in Victory telling people about how sad his divorce was, it was costing so much, and he was sure that God had a special blessing for anyone who would pledge €2000 to help him fight off the enemy. This year in a lavish ceremony Hinn remarried his wife because the impact it had was to expose him and funds were just not coming in.  Pastor Hade in his sermon “the church is for restoration” says. The Gospel is not just about going to heaven, but is about a good life here, in fact he says it will give us a life we couldn’t dream of, he says the good news for the poor is that they don’t have to be poor.

So the prosperity gospel says “life with God can be good, if you can just have enough faith and give us what you have them God will give it back multiplied to you.”  This emphasis on a better life here and now is very appealing especially in low income groups just as lottery sales are found to be. It is a gospel that rarely challenges addresses the fundamental issue that Evangelicals preach namely the sinful nature which estranges you from God, but instead proposes exactly what Everyman wants to hear.  “success and happiness.”  Appealing to this basic human desire thousands are lulled into sending money to televangelists, dubious missions, and are encouraged to buy books  DVDs and such like.   St Peter in his epistle warned that such teachers would come who “will make merchandise of you”.   But our quote from Jeremiah above says “the people love it so”.

As with any Ponzi scheme, the vehicle has to be good. In the international scheme prosperity preachers will point to their blessings. Creflo Dollar for example will talk of his poor upbringing and take you to his inventory of assets which includes two jetliners multiple cars and outrageous and luxurious homes. Others will speak of how miraculously loans were cleared and financial success just came.  Pastor Hade in a sermon on Restoration available on YouTube says ” if people see it works they will listen.”  So if prosperity is to be seen you build a large headquarters to give the feel good factor, you add a Starbucks and then you begin to peddle the product to the faithful.

The problem is it doesn’t work because it just is not true. Yes God blesses His people but His gospel and His kingdom are not of this world. Jesus says give it to the poor. He says seek to have your treasure in heaven. The bible also says the “the love of money is the root of evil,” and this love and pursuit of money is evident in Victory. Pending courts cases involve past members suing for money invested in crazy schemes which were used to raise money for what appear to be scams, past cases in the labour court for non payment of tax all point to an abuse of people.  The large investment in Firhouse/Tallaght is now looking like ” a bridge to far” needed a constant flow of people to feed the machine, but two problems arise, one not enough people and two a recession. For the prosperity gospel to work every time someone realises the truth and leaves you need someone new to walk in and take their place, just as in a Ponzi scheme new entrants provide the money to pay previous ones. Numbers at Victory Centre are just not working out. They have just run out of miracles or was it cash?

 But don’t be mistaken, these groups don’t pack up shop without a fight; the show really must go on. We saw this in 2011 year when Victory Tallaght made a ham fisted takeover of Abundant life Galway. At first Hade was delighted to be the new pastor, he got the 200 or so new people to be givers but had to do a quick exit when he discovers the level of outstanding debt.

So the people will be told “no problem here, keep coming, keep giving,” while in the background they work feverishly to keep the balls in the air.  The receiver will have to sell to someone, he will sell for way less than the loan amount, so enter a white knight who buys it back for Victory and the show is back on the road. But hey ! That’s how miracles happen.

  Evangelicalism

*In Ireland where there is a lot of confusion concerning Religion and its classification

it is important to clarify what we mean by the term Evangelical.

One often hears the question asked. What religion are you?  You will also hear from the media or in religious programmes comments about people being very religious. Also the census adds to this confusion by classifying denominations as Religions. So the Church of Ireland is viewed as a Religion rather than as a confession of or denomination of Christianity which is the religion. Due to the breakdown of the main Christian Churches you will find those who want to escape from religion will say they are spiritual rather than religious or increasingly atheist!

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/10/18/the-church-of-ireland-is-not-a-religion-rte-gets-it-wrong/

Evangelicals on the other hand are totally anti religion, and view religion as an enemy of faith. They view Christ in terms of a personal relationship rather than what often comes over from Catholics which is ‘The Church…’

 Up until recently though Evangelicals who had been native to Ireland since the Seventh Century were regarded by most Irish people as blow ins from America who came in with McDonalds.  However, during Cromwell’s time in Ireland Baptists were to be found in St Patrick’s Cathedral.

Dissenters also like Presbyterians, Baptists and Congregationalists were part of this mix. The 1904 Revival in Wales rather than the Pentecostal developments in Los Angles a few years later were a significant part of the story. The Elim Pentecostals had their origins here in Monaghan in 1915.

Pentecostals then are a branch of Evangelicalism which emphasise the role of the Person of the Holy Spirit in the life of the Christian. All Pentecostals are not into prosperity in fact those that are would be a minority.  All Pentecostals are Evangelicals but all Evangelicals are not Pentecostals.
