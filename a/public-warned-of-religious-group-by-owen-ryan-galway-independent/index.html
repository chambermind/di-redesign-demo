Public warned of religious group by Owen Ryan, Galway Independent

King of Kings Church Tuam

Galway Independent     Best Regional Newspaper — Irish Print Awards · Wed August 4, 2004· Vol 4 · Issue 31

Public warned of religious group  by Owen Ryan

The public are being warned to be wary of a Tuam religious group that has been accused controlling members and intimidating them into handing over money. Mr Mike Garde of Dialogue Ireland (an independent trust which provides information on religious groups) described the ‘King of Kings Foundation’ as “dysfunctional” when contacted by the Galway Independent this week.  “There is evidence that power has been misused and that people have been intimidated to give excessive money to the group. It seems that people have had their freedom removed and have been put under emotional and spiritual control,” said Mr Garde.  The group have been operating in Tuam for approximately 20 years according to Mr Garde, but in recent years it has changed its focus. “It started off trying to offer a genuine alternative, that tried to reach out to people. However in recent years it has been very concerned with money,” he claimed.  Local Catholic Priest, Father Stephen Farragher said that he believed the foundation had continually mistreated its members. “Much of what has gone on has been spiritual abuse. For example people who have left the group have been demonised, so that those left in the church will have no contact with them. Scripture was twisted to suit the people running it.”Both Mr Garde and Fr Farragher were anxious that other non-mainstream religious groups would not be branded as being guilty of the same behaviour as the King of Kings foundation.

Individuals who have left the group are reluctant to speak about their experiences, but in a statement they outlined why they chose to leave. “In the past five years it became ‘woman centred’ and finance purpose driven. Much of the money was for the leaders’ personal use as the standard of the building that the meetings are held in can verify. Fear tactics are used to manipulate and control members into parting with ‘offerings’ of money. It was also said by one of the leaders that if you don’t give you are open to financial debt and ill health. She has also claimed that anyone who comes against this church can die in a car accident as an early church member who left did. Members who leave the church are labelled with a demonic spirit, putting further fear into members so that they do not have contact with people who have left.”  Dialogue Ireland estimates that the group has about 15 members at the moment, as some have left recently. It holds its meetings in a prefab building in Tuam, which is dwarfed by the home of the husband and wife team that are in charge of the group.

The King of Kings foundation were contacted by the Galway Independent on Tuesday, but made no comment on the allegations.
