Tony Quinn

TONY QUINN’S

PAL IN TROUBLE

 FANS OF GOLDHAWK will recall the adventures of Tony Quinn devotee Tina Fearon, a beauty therapist who was inspired by Quinn’s mind control techniques to set up her own chain of beauty salons, the Free Spirit chain (see The Phoenix 29/1/10). Unfortunately, it’s all gone wrong for Fearon, who has been restricted from acting as company director for five years under Section 150 of the Companies Act. This restriction related to Fearon’s conduct with respect to one of her companies, Free Spirit Hair & Beauty Salon Ltd, which was behind a Free Spirit store in the IFSC, Dublin. The Revenue w wound this company up in 2010 along with four other companies in the chain – Free Spirit City West Ltd, Free Spirit Drogheda Ltd, Free Spirit Dundrum Ltd, and Free Spirit Sandyford Ltd. At the time the High Court heard that the Free Spirit group owed the Revenue more than €630,000 in unpaid VAT and PAYE/ PRSI.

The other director of all five companies is Philip Fearon (who gives a Dublin address with company records), and who has also been restricted under Section 150 of the Companies Act. Tina Fearon is the sole shareholder in each company and she was also a franchisee of Quinn’s Educo Gym. In 2008, she told the Sunday Business Post that she was “looking into” opening up Free Spirit salons in Dubai, London and Beverly Hills. It’s unclear if Tony Quinn was an investor in the Free Spirit group. However, in an interview on Educo’s website “Dr. Tony Quinn” said that he is “involved” with the Free Spirit companies, while on the Free Spirit website Fearon gave Quinn a gushing testimonial and explained that his Educo training seminar was the key to her financial success. Meanwhile, last May hair care products company Wella (UK) Ltd issued High Court proceedings personally against Tina Fearon
