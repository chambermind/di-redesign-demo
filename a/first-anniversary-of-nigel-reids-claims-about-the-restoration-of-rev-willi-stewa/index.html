First Anniversary of Nigel Reid’s claims about the Restoration of Rev Willi Stewart

Abuse by Church and Irish institutionsChristianConferences & CommentariesEvangelicalism

Nigel Reid reports his role in Willi Stewart’s return to ministry in 2011.

God’s blessing on CORE

Nigel Reid @ Sunday AM Service on 19 June 2011

Duration: 45:45

Hear Audio here:

Download 110619_19thJune2011.mp3

He was present to witness and support of Willi as he was placed as the Rector of five parishes in the Chet Valley in Norfolk. He was restored to being a Vicar there.

Three and half years ago he had a dream and God told him to go down in his car to Kilkenny and dig out Willi. We prayed together and started a journey of restoration. Is everything wonderful I can’t answer that today and it’s not appropriate? But I am absolutely convinced that Willi is rightly back in ministry.

The denial and continued resistance to addressing the issues that the horrendous events from 1994 till 2002 in Core represent is both tragic and an indictment. The refusal by the leadership of the Church of Ireland and the ongoing leadership of Core to address these legacy issues is soul destroying.

This a summary of comments heard over the past 10 years:

It was consensual…..

He was treated badly…

It was just a small issue..

We need to forgive Willi….

We need to move on….

Because Willi pleaded guilty to the charge of assault and there was no evidence given in court people have formed the opinion the whole thing was a storm in a tea cup. Also the two cases are confused. One involved a person who was being mentored and that was in the mid nineties. It was a totally inappropriate abuse by a mentor of a member, and an imbalance of power. This article which looks at this issue in regard to clergy relations with congregants is about Buddhists, but its relevance is absolute to the CORE situation. This is not just a failure of an individual but also no one in leadership took responsibility and no discipline ensued. Consequently the events of the early 2000’s followed logically as there was no means of warning further victims. Many of these people are praised in this sermon and continue in positions of responsibility in the Church of Ireland.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/06/12/violation-of-the-sacredwestern-psychological-perspectives-on-sexual-misconduct-in-the-clergy-and/

This is not an issue of Child Abuse, but inappropriate relations with Vulnerable adults.

There is also a confusion between issues related to Child Abuse which this was not, but it rather falls into the field of the Definition of Vulnerable Adults. This is the current law in N.I.

 http://www.nhssb.n-i.nhs.uk/publications/social_services/Safeguarding_Vulnerable_Adults.pdf

The second case is simply an assault after grooming.

The complete lack of awareness by Nigel of his responsibility of addressing the needs of the victim/s of assault and imbalance of power is hard to register. The total marginalisation of the family and the terrible sense of loss are hard to believe. Nigel was in a role at Core which has no bearing on what happened but it should lead him to seek out without having a dream the victim and not the perpetrator of these events. He will never be freed until he takes that road. Look at the web site of Core it is totally incoherent as it is in ostrich territory in the sand.

No one can say whether God can forgive the crimes committed but we do know that if we do not like Zacchaeus come out of the tree, repent and make amends there can be no true forgiveness and reconciliation.
