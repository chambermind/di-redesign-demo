Mike Garde talks about “The Complex”

Mike Garde from Dialogue Ireland talks about the background to John Duignan’s new book “The Complex”. This book is an expose of the cult of Scientology from someone with over 20 years of experience within its hierarchy.

The cult have issued legal proceedings to prevent it being sold outside Ireland and have successfully forced Amazon to yank it. This is the type of censorship that led to Anonymous taking action you think they aren’t learning their lesson?
