Scientology

Jonny Jacobsen, is a British expert on Scientology resident in Paris who has gathered a vast and impressive archive of material on the subject of Scientology. His site, Infinite Complacency is highly recommended for anyone wanting to learn about the background to the workings of Scientology.

http://infinitecomplacency.blogspot.com

Jonny Jacobsen is the author of the Infinite Complacency website tracking violence and abuse in Scientology. He trained as a journalist in the late 1980s with the Birmingham Post and Mail group and spent several years freelancing in Scotland before moving to Paris in 1994, where he is still based. His first investigative piece on Scientology was in 1996 as a reporter/producer with Radio France International.
