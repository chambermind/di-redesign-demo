Here is John in mortal combat as he tries to get shots of Quinn coming in through the fire Escape! Quinn is paranoid and thinks there is a conspiracy with people out to get him. He is wrong about the conspiracy but not about people looking for justice after the years of abuse. We have two Youtube videos.
Here you see the security going beyond their job description and trying to obstruct and interfere with a legitimate protest on the ground’s of a university. We had no problem of him renting the Helix, but he does not have the right to stop protests. We also accepted arbitrary boundaries between DCU and the Helix.

You will see how John tries to take a shot of Quinn leaving his vehicle. The Security man pursues him and tries to obstruct his line of vision. At the point he is using the mantra health and safety. John is near a railing with a massive drop. He has the railing in one hand and the camera in the other. The Security man holds a folder to obstruct the shot. He has a job of keeping Quinn safe, not trying to block John’s vision. He puts John at risk by his action. In other words he had a duty to stop anyone approaching Quinn.  John as you see is moving away from Quinn to his left, nowhere near him.
