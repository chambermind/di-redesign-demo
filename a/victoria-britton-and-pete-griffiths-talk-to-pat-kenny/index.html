Victoria Britton and Pete Griffiths talk to Pat Kenny

Scientology

Enough is enough for ex Scientologists
Show: The Pat Kenny Show

Embedded media (opens the original source)

Victoria Britton is the mother of Kyle Brennan (20) who died tragically on the evening of February 16, 2007 in the Clearwater, Florida, apartment of his Scientologist father, Tom Brennan. She makes it her mission to ensure her son’s passing is not forgotten. She has written extensively about his passing at The Truth for Kyle Brennan: https://vbreton2062.wordpress.com

Pete Griffiths, Former member of the Church of Scientology and Victoria Britton joined Pat in studio.

This is an exemplary interview with one of the greatest interviewers in the world. Very reasoned by both Pete and Victoria. Victoria was gentle and respectful to such a degree she was able to convey the reality of the how Scientology functions. Less is more. Winsome.
