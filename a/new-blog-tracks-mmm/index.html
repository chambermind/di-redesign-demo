New Blog tracks MMM

Magnificat Meal Movement
We have been contacted by Sam Hailey who runs the following blog.
http://helidon-truth.blogspot.com/

We will add it to our site and hope to be in touch with Sam by phone as soon as we get his contact details.

We notice that Sam has a question about the whereabouts of Debra? We have information that she is in Nouméa, New Caledonia in the Pacific about 1400kms NW of Brisbane and directly north East of the North Island NZ.

During the research for my thesis we found a number of her financial projects were based there.

It is also quite a handy location to hide away, and be back in Helidon when required.

Here in Ireland we anticipate a growing interest in the location from around

the world as familes begin to seek answers to a growing set of questions.

http://www.worldtravelguide.net/country/190/map/Australia-and-South-Pacific/New-Caledonia.html

During this period 6 years ago I saw Debra 2 when I tried to interview her in Helidon-

no one had seen her for a long time.

Please send us photos to upload. Surely the media in Australia must want to
give us the final shots before her retirement? The MMM community in Helidon likely

think she is evangelising….

The younger generation will soon be googling so the truth will come out.

You can also access a number of films about Debra and the MMM on the site via U tube from our Dialogue Site.
