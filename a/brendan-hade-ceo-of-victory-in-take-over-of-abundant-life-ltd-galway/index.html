19 November 2011

Brendan Hade CEO of Victory in take over of Abundant Life Ltd. Galway.

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

With all the current focus on Kevin and Heather Sanford with respect to the issues of Abundant Life, one could easily be forgiven for overlooking one of the chief protagonists in all of the proceedings, Brendan Hade. In Kevin’s most recent newsletter much is made by the Kevin of the supposed “wealth” of the Hades and their Dublin church. What Kevin seems to be unaware of is that Brendan is currently undergoing his own financial difficulties. While these are not as developed as those experienced by Kevin Sanford as he grapples on the point of bankruptcy, Brendan also is experiencing his own liquidity issues.

Note that two Elders of Victory were due in court last Monday November, 14, 2011

See additional sources in attached Word document.

The Hade Takeover V 4 Victory

Unlike Kevin who time and time again has shown his complete lack of business acumen with all his failed ministry and business ventures, Brendan has had some success during the boom times of the Celtic Tiger. Not content with the donations of an adoring congregation, Brendan Hade spread his wings in the blossoming economy of times past and invested church funds in many ventures. One such venture was the many asylum reception centres he setup for asylum seekers in Dublin. They received €2.5million in government funding for these in 2004. This figure fluctuated for other years but overall Brendan was in receipt of several multiples of this figure from the Government over the period he ran these centres. Brendan Hade has said their interest was “commercial” as well as charitable. This is very a very interesting position considering a large portion of their congregation are immigrants. At one point they owned 3 centres in Kilmacud House, Kilmarnock House in Killiney, and MILVERTON HOUSE, MONTGOMERY STREET, CARLOW . Due to the recession and the resulting changes to immigration policy these lucrative Government contracts have eroded for Brendan over time. Many have alluded to the prison like conditions of Abundant Life over the years under the stewardship of Kevin and Heather Sanford but the parallels can be seen with Brendan’s stewardship of the Asylum centres. In particular there is a very interesting report on the Killiney centre here  which was undertaken amid accusations of “prison-like conditions” by asylum-seeker occupants.

With the resulting loss of such lucrative contracts Brendan was forced to look at alternative methods of revenue generation. One of these ventures was to undertake a very large investment in a company called Global Mobile Vision (GMV) by means of a 26% shareholding held by both Brendan and his wife Sheila. What ensued in Global Mobile Vision was a financial and operational scandal and subsequent collapse not unlike that we have most recently seen in institutions such as Anglo Irish Bank. The scandal of GMV is documented in the following article.

http://www.indymedia.ie/article/70282

To summarise the events of the collapse of GMV, they received significant funds from the IDA, set up in Clondalkin, hired employees who did no work and ended up out of pocket for wages amid harassment and imprisonment accusations. GMV ended up in the courts owing over €1.4 million to creditors. Employees were owed more than €160,000 in wage arrears and other payments, according to the statement of affairs. Revenue was owed €150,633. Trade creditors total dues were €115,179, while directors loans were €1 million. Considering that Brendan and Sheila had a 26% equity stake in this company, they undertook very heavy losses. Much like Sean Quinn in his naivety with Anglo shareholdings which reduced him from Billionaire status to bankrupt, the same naivety proved costly to the Hades with GMV.

The owners of Global Mobile vision were an African couple called David and Ester Crownborne – who fled to Florida after the court judgement (bench warrants were issued after not appearing in court). David Crownborn at the time accused Minister Michael Martin of being part of a “Commie” conspiracy in Ireland to undermine GMV. The Crownbornes then proceeded to setup a church in Tampa Bay with a somewhat startling similarity to Victory Church. The Crownborne’s church also carries with it much controversy in Tampa Bay. http://www.tampabay.com/news/back-off-from-ybor-patrons-church-is-told/510953

There are very striking similarities between the Crownbornes and the Sanfords. They both have had failed, incompetently run ventures in Ireland, partnered by the Hades. Both had judgements against them and both fled Ireland rather than pay their dues and both setup controversial new churches in the U.S. (the Sanford’s are in the process of doing this in El Paso, Texas). The outstanding question from all of this is how could Brendan and Sheila Hade allow themselves to be associated with miscreants like the Crownbornes considering that the Hades as Pastor’s of a Christian church should be subject to the moral obligations and judgements that comes with the position of Pastorship. The simple answer is financial gain or in this case the potential for gain. Not deterred by the scandals of GMV or the lack of morals displayed by the Crownbournes, the Hades invested heavily in the hopes of financial increase. This as we now know backfired severely on them leaving a very large hole in their finances as of yet still not repaired. This coupled with the indebtedness the Hades undertook to build their “$20 million facility” that Kevin Sanford so proudly boasts of, has left the Hades in a very precarious financial position.

When all seemed lost to the Hades, opportunity has struck again.  With an alarming amount of amnesia from their experiences with GMV and the Crownbornes, the Hades were once again recently offered the opportunity for financial gain with Abundant Life. As the Sanfords flee the country with their church and reputation destroyed, the Hades have been offered the lifeline of approximately 100 new congregation members overnight and the potential of a successful Galway branch of Victory Church. One might expect that from the experiences with the Crownbornes and GMV, the Hades would exhibit some hesitance in jumping straight in to a similarly volatile and controversial relationship to that of GMV, but instead the Hades fuelled by a desire for increase and gain have once again chosen to conveniently develop amnesia and overlook the issues, abuses and controversies of Abundant Life in the hopes that it can help feed the hole in their finances. I’m sure they are well aware of the potential revenues that could be generated straight away from over 100 Abundant Life members through “tithes and offerings”. A very welcome turn of events for the Hades at a time of financial volatility for them. I’m sure if Sean Quinn himself was offered a Pastorship of Abundant Life, he would jump at the chance to earn a few more euro to stave off bankruptcy for as long as possible

In all of this we see ministers like the Hades, Sanfords and Crownbornes treat churches like businesses and members like clients and customers. With the current events in Abundant Life, the members are been treated by both the Hades and the Sanfords as not only paying customers but also as assets that can be traded at liberty. When ministers walk more like CEOs rather than their intended biblically defined role of a shepherd we see Pastors playing monopoly with their members tithes and lives. This approach to church is very much a central theme in the heretical prosperity teachings that these people permeate throughout their parishes. One has to ask themselves that if they preached the truth, that God’s ultimate purpose is bless you with abundant prosperity then why are these ministers, churches and incumbent congregations not only lacking prosperity but consistently lying at the point of poverty and bankruptcy. This rhetorical question needs no answer as a simple point can be derived. Either the Sanfords and the Hades preach a false self serving Gospel or God does not honour his biblical promises! Any true Christian involved in these churches must start to ask themselves why their experiences are consistently opposite to that which they have been taught to expect and if reality does not match up to the teaching they need to understand why and leave these places instead of enabling these ministers to trade their tithes and offerings like monopoly money.
