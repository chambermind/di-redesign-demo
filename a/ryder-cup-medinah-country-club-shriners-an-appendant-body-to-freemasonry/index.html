Ryder Cup, Medinah Country Club, Shriners an appendant body to Freemasonry ~

Conferences & Commentaries

Many golf lovers were so absorbed with watching the golf over the last few days they  probably did not reflect on the lovely building where the Medinah Country Club is based. It looks like a mosque, and the post September 11 world seems incongruous.

We are very conscious of Islamic architecture  as most countries have a mosque nearby. The story of how Medinah Country Club evolved is very interesting.

Shriners

The Ancient Arabic Order of the Nobles of the Mystic Shrine, also commonly known as Shriners and abbreviated A.A.O.N.M.S., established in 1870, is an appendant body to Freemasonry, based in the United States.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shriners

http://www.medinahcc.org/

Masonry and Shriners are about the human need for ritual and this phenomenon can be be an alternative to religion or held in conjunction with a major religious faith. The only reference to cult in this situation is that of the game of Golf!
