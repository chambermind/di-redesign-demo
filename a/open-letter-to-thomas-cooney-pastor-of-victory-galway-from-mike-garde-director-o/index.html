Open Letter to Thomas Cooney Pastor of Victory Galway from Mike Garde Director of DI.

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

Victory, Abundant Life and the new name is Victory Galway…………….

It was good to receive your call today and to be able to discuss the issues involved in the formation of Victory Galway.  Your position is that you are open to dialogue and to meet but only if we take down the posts that reference you which you regard as untrue. I am willing to meet you without any preconditions. I have also stressed that we will give you the right of reply without the need for your document to be edited by us. If there specific untruths we will immediately take them down when they are brought to our attention. You refuse to budge from your preconditions.

Fundamentally, we agreed to disagree. You did confirm that Abundant Life is being closed down. You also confirmed that your former Church was governed by a Trust which Like Victory in Dublin means that you do not have to give the public any idea where the money has gone. Companies have to publish accounts but Trusts do not.

Because you were resolute in your opinion that you did not wish to prepare a response I have gone some way to meet your request and have consulted my correspondent in regard to the articles written which reference you.

Dear XXXXX,
I was contacted by Thomas today. He claims that he is being misrepresented in regard to his position in the post you wrote about him. You could give evidence for these conclusions? Otherwise I could be ruining a possible avenue of dialogue. If you can’t stand over them I will amend them.

Thanks

Mike

Hi Mike,

My posts were based on the testimony of former members of Tom Cooney’s church and members of other local churches here that know Living Word far better than I do. I cannot verify the information as it based on the testimony of others and so is not first hand. This of course is also true regarding Hade as almost everything posted is not first hand info.

I will leave it to your discretion to edit or delete my articles as you feel necessary. It is true he is a business man but so is Hade and they will both point to that as the reason for their financial capability which surely must be taken with a pinch of salt.

I added some responses below too but as I have said, make whatever changes or deletions you want. I have no first hand knowledge of Tom Cooney but some of my sources would not lie regarding the issues as they are in some way sympathetic to Tom.

Another potential step could be the partnership with another local church. We know that Brendan Hade has close ministerial ties to Thomas Cooney, Pastor of another Pentecostal church in Galway called Living Word. Thomas shares the same ministerial roots and theological views of Brendan. Thomas and Brendan have enjoyed a good working relationship over the years and it is reported that surprisingly, Living Word has much closer connections to Victory than Abundant Life ever did. It is also reported that Living Word is undergoing its own struggles albeit on a much smaller scale to those of Abundant Life so a potential partnership between the newly branded Victory Church in Galway helmed by Brendan Hade and Living Word could be mutually beneficial. This would possibly involved the initial shared use of Living Word’s facility until Brendan could source his own facility and furnish it to a required standard. The question remains though would Thomas Cooney be willing to dilute his congregation with the toxic influence of Abundant Life members who still have not been vaccinated from the indoctrination of the Sanfords? It would be a risky move on Cooney’s part or indeed any other minister in Galway who would potentially entertain a contaminant like Abundant Life into their church, polluting and infecting their own congregation much like the Sanfords polluted and infected their followers…..Monkey See, Monkey Do!

Dialogue Ireland has thus far been right on the money with its recent predications related to ALCC so it will be interesting to see looking back months from now, how prophetic these thoughts really are. Finally, why would Hade wish to take on this poisoned chalice? Surely, his own financial problems would keep him out of town? He is a venture capitalist who sees the distressed assets and wishes to build his empire from the failure of the Sanfords. Mutton dressed up as lamb. Christians who advocate this view point seem totally off the wall in this current situation. This Nama or Celtic Tiger ideology is now shown for what it was all the time a diseased patient in the final stages of rigor mortis. Looking at Living Word’s web site it does not give the impression of being up to the challenge. Have we here a take over as well as an amalgamation? It is my intelligence from Galway that Living Word is also losing members. As I said they also represent the prosperity trajectory but with a quieter, Irish flavour and without the extreme control of members. Some from ALCC will find the transition to Living Word  easy.
