Susan Morrice seen wondering down Grafton Street suffering from amnesia

Her mental stability can surely be questioned when she starts comparing herself to Nelson Mandela and Invitus.  I guess she wants to see herself receiving international accolades.  Her ego being so big. This article is all about her and her brilliance.  Seems that the truth is so far removed from her mind with all the Educo brain washing that she really has lost the run of herself.  She knows well that she was no where near Belize when oil was found.  Safely tucked away in the grandeur and opulence of the Williamette Lane Ranch in Denver.  Strange too that she thinks by writing and re-writing the history she constantly trips herself up in lies.

Incidentally does she forget that she is divorcing the Husband Alex Cranberg, from this article you would think they are best buddies.  Is it true that Quinn the Mucky messiah and cult leader is named in the divorce??

Incidentally the team included the bankrupt Tina Fearon along with Dubai superhero Josh that cannot differentiate between perceived value and anything real.  Maybe he can help explain that one to the Irish Revenue.

Patricia Fitzpatrick haphazard organisational car crash skills for the Irish tour no doubt will lead her back to the North – most likely the Carryduff  Educogym, her Monaghan and Castleblaney Educogym base is now depleted with the closure of these two gyms as well.  Who knows Fitzpatrick may well be in the loop to answer some questions to the revenue herself.
