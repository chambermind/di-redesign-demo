The waters of Baptism the Irish way? Baptism Grosvenor Baptist Church, June 24, 2012

Uncategorized

Tania Rodriguez, Dermot Flanagan, Adrian Quinn & Tom Samuel were baptised on Sunday 24th June at Grosvenor Baptist Church.

Many people in Ireland would regard immersion and not baptising babies as strange. However, from the perspective of our Christian history in Ireland the Baptism of believers’ was the practice of the Christians in Ireland before the Normans came.

What was amazing was that had a Baptism Service the day after I brought a group to Moone.  Tania Rodriguez, Dermot Flanagan, Adrian Quinn & Tom Samuel gave expression to their commitment to Christ through the waters of Baptism.  The juxtaposition of these ancient images with the modern journey of faith entered into by  was very moving.

In November 2011 Mike Garde took John Samuel and the staff on an outing to Castledermot and Moone. We were given hospitality at Bolton Abbey Cistercian Monastery by Br Eoin De Bhaldraithe an expert on the High Crosses. He is also a scholar of early Irish Church history and he showed us some remarkable evidence which shows that a baptistery which suggests that Ireland was a Believer’s Church before the Normans came in 1169! The Baptistery was totally overgrown.   See below photographs taken on June 23 the day before the most recent baptisms in Grosvenor. Back in Castledermot:  He argues that the Normans brought the system of compulsory infant baptism to Ireland. This was linked to Peter’s pence which involved a tax on each baptised person going to Rome. Before that Irish Christians practised believers’ baptism. The Baptistery is at the edge of the Franciscan Abbey. It must precede it by about 400 years.

Note the baptistery which Bro Eoin has found.

Some work on making it look presentable has taken place since the visit in 2011.

Earlier Bro Eoin explained the history of the crosses at Castledermot.

Then we went to Moone where Bro Eoin gave a presentation on all the dimensions of the High Cross. Christians used to follow the example of the first centuries of the Christian Church in who prayed gathered around the cross.
