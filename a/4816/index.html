Deception & Incitement – Yet more distortions and Lies

House of Prayer

House of Prayer’s Response to the House of War article. They now realise they have to use the internet, though they depend on the fact that most of their members are not computer literate coming from the older generation. In other words these responses are an attempt to fight their battles in the medium that most people use. They know their dedicated members won’t read it, but it gives them 6 days before the next Sunday paper to try to divert people who do not know the full facts into believing their message.

Here is the Sunday World article alluded to in their response:

Statement 19th July 2011 Deception & Incitement

Tabloid Newspaper Continues Long-Running Vendetta against Christina Gallagher & the House of Prayer.

Yet more distortions and Lies

Once again, the way in which tabloid newspapers, seeking to feed their readers with lies and sensation, constantly distort truth and reality in doing so, this was evident in an article which appeared on July 17 , part of their long-running vendetta against Christina Gallagher and the House of Prayer, and amounted to more deception and incitement.

Every person attending the 18th Anniversary of the opening of the House of Prayer in Achill on Saturday – including the sensation seekers from the tabloid press – were fully aware of the many hundreds present for the prayers, filling not only the chapel and other interior areas of the House to overflowing , but also leaving an overspill using coaches in the car park and a few sitting on seats in front of the main building, despite the inclement weather.

The newspaper chose instead, to show just a picture of those few seated people outside, to suit their ridiculous claims that support was dwindling and to talk about “a few dozen of her last remaining followers.” What pathetic writing it is, ignoring truth time after time. Did not the newspaper’s “snapper” (photographer) actually say he spent all day outside the House of Prayer, so he could not but be aware of the number of coaches and the large numbers of pilgrims who attended.

Once again, we ask, is this newspaper and its journalist capable of truthfully reporting anything, considering these distortions and untruths? It is little wonder that the tabloid press is becoming more and more despised.

We see no reason to repeat aspects already addressed in response to previous distortions and lies, but the writer makes a false statement about Majella Meade, claiming she is …”now being blasted from the pulpit.” Majella Meade is not and has never been “blasted from the pulpit” and there are hundreds of weekly attendees during the prayers, all of whom can confound that particular lie. Majella Meade has always been appreciated and respected for the work she has done over the years, which she carried out in conjunction with the Fraternity.

The Sunday newspaper article also totally distorts and misrepresents Our Blessed Lady’s messages, and the reality of why Our Blessed Lady will no longer be giving public messages. It is well known to all who attend the House of Prayer that Our Blessed Lady on a number of occasions beginning some time ago had foretold to Christina that Her messages were coming to an end soon. She has not deserted Christina but She will not be giving public messages in the future. Again he gets it wrong when he says”….. Jesus guided her to the mansion…..” (the house in England). Christina was not “guided” by Our Lord to a house in England. It was her place of quiet and peaceful protection provided by people when that in Dublin was destroyed by The Sunday World. Jim Gallagher misleads people once again In his reference to the people signing over their donations. Christina requested professional advice from a solicitor to ensure that the confusion caused by media in relation to the house in Malahide would not be repeated. The advice was to put in place a declaration to be carefully read and signed providing proof to the public whom The Sunday World might try to mislead yet again with lies and twisted stories. It is not through any lack of trust in the unswerving good will of the people who were happy to provide the protection for Christina in the face of such malicious and relentless attack.

All who attend the House of Prayer know how false these distortions are. The entire Sunday World article is another pathetic mishmash and rehash, another attempt to create sensation and foster discontent – and is unworthy of any further comment.

ALLEGED INCIDENT

Material appears in the same edition of the newspaper about an alleged incident on Saturday, July 16, involving a photographer from the paper – an incident not connected in any way to the events at the House of Prayer on Saturday, July 16, and occurring on the public road.

On Christina’s return to her home on July 16th, it seemed her car had also been tampered with causing the alarm to go off continuously. So the harassment of Christina Gallagher continues. It is hard to understand how there is no law to protect against such an unjust campaign.
