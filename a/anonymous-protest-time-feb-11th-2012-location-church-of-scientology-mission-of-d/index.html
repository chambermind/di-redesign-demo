Anonymous Protest Time: Feb 11th, 2012 Location: ‘Church’ of Scientology Mission of Dublin Ltd.

ANONYMOUS Ireland. A lot of confusion revolves around the term Anonymous. It is is confused with all kinds of hacker and internet anarchism. There is nothing anarchic about this Chanology group. They are a generally highly ethical group committed to the protection of human rights and democratic freedoms which they see Scientology infringing. The article which was written by the Times of London really gets to the heart of what they are about. Also an very good paper on the the Anonymous issue.*

About Anonymous…

Anonymous is a grassroots collective comprised of ordinary people from all walks of life, from former Scientologists and long-time cult activists, to college students and corporate professionals, all united in recognition of the malign nature of the ‘Church’ of Scientology, the danger it poses to society, and the necessity of action.
