Commentary on the Quinn June Goodlife Con….ference

Tony Quinn

Our visiting editor’s column:

EDUCO cult leader, Tony Quinn, does not possess sufficient self awareness to realise that knowledge of his criminal behaviour will not disappear from the public arena. Apart from benefiting himself with sporadic appearances for Educoists to boost his ego and bank account, he hides from public view in the hope that the ‘noise’ will die a death. This is one request he won’t get as too many were damaged by ‘top brass’ foolish actions in the name of their guru’s money-making scam.

Ex-quinnites, ex-eduoists and INE B shareholders, who have vocalised a total loss faith in their ‘guru’ does not stop the self-proclaimed ‘messiah’ and ‘saviour’ of mankind from gathering his diminishing number of cult members. It would not surprise me if he convinces them, yet again, to give up their right to INE dividends with promises of leading them into ‘the land of milk and honey’; like a chameleon he can take on the role of Moses when it suits him. He promises to draw their energy out (inducing deep hypnosis); ‘life’ takes care of them and they have no need to concern themselves with trivialities such as their shares. He assures them of yearly ‘top-ups’, or has it changed to twice yearly, to enable access to the ‘switch in the mind,’ his abusive hypnotic techniques that turns them into a state of dependency. They have no real knowledge of what he is doing with their money or their mental health.

As Belize EDUCO cult oil business INE, still under the black shadow of Quinn, has taken a huge loss when Sheila McCaffrey, an original director, tenaciously held out for the big payoff and walked away with millions, Tony has consulted with himself (isn’t he the one with all the ‘brilliant’ ideas) and decided, following his bad publicity, that his ‘foolies’ needed reassuring. Mary obliges him by sending out a letter with promises of a new start, a new beginning to ‘making this world a better place.’ She asks educoists to bury their heads in the sand so they won’t hear the ‘noise’ of concern that causes chaos and puts a spoke in the machinations of EDUCO cult thinking.

The superficial ‘love’ that so many were taken in by has long disappeared and the ‘philosophy’ of Quinn far easier understood when you witness his behaviour. It’s all for one and NOT one for all in the self-serving EDUCO cult.

DI Commentary:

Your next Good Life Club Meeting with Tony is on Sunday June 9th at Clontarf Castle, this is instead of the September Meeting.

The likely rationale here is that the settlements now need to be built on, Justice Bannister ignored and September is a long time away. They want to rerun the EDUCO/INE meeting which was a bit of disaster for Susan as she did a runner from the Bewley’s Hotel. It is very important to understand the cultist notion that unites oil and Educo. In proper democratic organisations you have separation of powers. Here when talking about oil it is the same as talking about Educo. That is why the staff at Clontarf Castle told me earlier this year that they understood the seminar to be an oil meeting. Yet it had the feeling of a rock concert with groupies wearing evening wear at lunch time with fake tan and many seemed to have bought into Tony’s bust enhancement fantasy. They might try to do the INE meeting now they have bought silence from many. However, the Bannister judgement won’t go away.

Overwhelmingly the Castle seems to be our most popular venue.

 

It is also the most intimate place to observe the goings on as I did for two hours. From the point of view of the protest this time we are likely to have a double strategy. Protest at the Castle and outside Government Buildings to raise the awareness of the Irish people to the abuses of Quinn.

You can now go ahead with your travel arrangements.

We certainly are!

We hope you are getting the most out of your local meetings that Tony has designed for your benefit, where you practice both pressing the positive buttons and sharing the results with everyone in the group.

 

Pressing the positive buttons = recruitment to the next meeting that claims that this meeting is the greatest meeting since the sliced pan etc

Sharing the results with everyone in the group. Which results, what benefits. This the power of  influence where people are made to believe they are obtaining benefits whereas they are getting fleeced. The only thing that is free is this Tupperware party.

In turn this will enable you and them to get a better understanding and deepen the good programs, causing everyone to achieve a better life.

 Just rationally analyse the sentence above. Note the American spelling of programme!

Participation and sharing your good results and good memories is the key to success.

Which particular good results: Vague generalisations written without reference to any objective criteria of success. In fact whoever Quinn turns to turns out to lose it. Business people with great entrepreneurial gifts become infantilised.

This includes pressing the positive buttons in business, with family, friends, and anyone you know, then you are giving a better life to everyone, instead of problems and negativity. This is what everyone in the world wants and you can actually make it happen for all.

The party will deliver change and the Chairman Tony has decreed apple pie for all. What ever you do not think for yourselves. That is negative and guess what happens you will wake up!
