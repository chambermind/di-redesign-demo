2+2 does not equal 4

CultismTwo by Twos

The days of hiding in the long grass are over the 2X2S are going to be in headlights.

We have been engaged with the 2X2S a pre Christian group who have a theology which is still in that zone between the old and the new testaments.

The person we have the most contact with is Craig Fulton whose is what they call a Worker.

For over a year we have been in a confidential process not specifically related to the CSA issue but rather a case where we would suggest undue influence was exercised in regard the sale of Rigsdale House in Ballyhassig, Co Cork.

A person who grew up on the grounds of the house wanted to purchase the property as it has a long association with the family. Needless to say the 2X2S were not that happy with that idea as they hold their convention their annually and they believed if this person bought the property it would likely be lost for the 2X2S.

Here is my report of visiting a Convention in 2017.

We discovered that the aunt of this person who was extremely close to the potential buyer had agreed to to bypass him and sell it to a third party who were very active in the 2X2S. Taking legal advice he could not do anything as he did not have locus standi in relation to the sale and was therefore frustrated and snookered in relation to this and felt he was the victim of a lie and that Craig had obtained the property in an underhand manner. He had told the 2X2S if he purchased the property he would allow the Convention to continue.

In 2022 Craig Fulton categorically told Dialogue Ireland that he had no involvement with the transfer of the property. He seemed to have closed down any avenue for us to proceed with the person who had approached Dialogue Ireland. In the meantime it was not a question of he said and she said but rather we put it to Craig Fulton that we had a witness who would testify that he was intimately involved in the transaction. We can’t prove undue influence but we can in the context of Christian ethics show that Craig would have to deny Christ. Let your yes be yes and your no be no. His authority would be shattered and when the forces of truth confront him about an absence of structure he like Ananias and Sapphira would be unable to stand.

So we wrote to Craig Fulton again a few weeks and he repeated his earlier position. We had offered him the opportunity to meet us without legal liability. He could have without any implications restored the family to equity and helped them to find healing.

We are giving the outline of the story here we intend to publish this on our blog and hand the story to the media to investigate further.

We wrote to Craig Fulton telling him we were withdrawing our offer and we now regard ourselves at war.

When the pathetic child protection policy gets a proper evaluation who is going to be the able to withstand the tsunami that is coming? In a document made available to DI it appears the person responsible for taking care of CSA issues is the Headmaster of Ashton School in Cork. Which body is registered with Tusla and who are independent people to be contacted? They claim they are the truth but it could be that it is not the truth and is that Headmaster using his role in the school as a proxy for the 2X2S? Also where is the money held by the Workers, whose bank account does it go into? The money trail will in time be revealed.

We have plenty of information on the background to the 2X2S but there is a major problem which years of discussion can’t resolve. In order to help an Irish Catholic audience to understand what is happening I am going to illustrate the issue by reference to the Christian Brothers.

Those who are concerned by the two by Twos should read this very relevant article about the non incorporated Christian Brothers. Those affected by the 2X2S should read this article very carefully and refer it to their legal representative.

Obviously someone who is a victim or survivor can’t wait for these issues to be addressed but in reality unless the 2X2S create some kind of organisation they are going to hit a brick wall. Today an article was published in the the USA showing that the net is closing in on them.

Christian Brothers accused of tactics to deter and deny victims

8 Feb 2023

By Rita O’Reilly

Prime Time

The President of the Law Reform Commission and retired chief justice, Mr Justice Frank Clarke, has said it will recommend the law be changed to stop the use of a legal technicality by religious orders to deter and delay victims taking civil cases for compensation in the courts for historical child sexual abuse.

The Christian Brothers is accused of using a 2017 Supreme Court judgment to block child abuse victims successfully suing it in the courts. It is refusing to put forward someone to represent it as a defendant, leaving victims with no organisation to sue.

Here is the relevant section:

BECAUSE RELIGIOUS ORDERS ARE WHAT IS KNOWN AS UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS, WHICH CANNOT BE SUED BECAUSE THEY ARE NOT A CORPORATE BODY OR PERSON, THE APPROACH IS PERFECTLY LEGAL, AND IS THE CURRENT LAW AS IT STANDS.

However, while the courts cannot force an order to nominate a representative defendant, they have the choice to do so, as other unincorporated associations often do, but the Christian Brothers has chosen not to.
