The Quinn Cut and Run~His attempt at an oil bypass operation.

Tony Quinn

The Quinn Cut and Run

It appears that the noose around Tony Quinn’s neck is tightening even more in the last few days.  The Sunday Times article reviews the recent happenings in the Nevis court.

Article in Sunday Times, Business Section March 11, 2012

PDF below:

ST oilQuinn

This case has been brought by INE Director Sheila McCaffrey though her company SM Life Ventures LLC, this comes hot on the heels of the Maranco LLC case (Paul Marriot former Director) which concluded with a courthouse steps settlement for over $16 Million.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/07/11/capitulation-of-quinn-and-morrice-in-settlement-with-paul-marriott/

In the Nevis proceedings last week, it was unearthed that Quinn and Morrice had signed a secret deal in favour of Quinn to get Quinn paid out of the oil company.  The amounts involved is in excess of $23 Million.  This deal was signed in Aug 2011 and is a ‘sweetheart’ deal to the detriment of all other shareholders.  There is absolute outrage amongst shareholders currently that this had been about to be executed, had it not been for the Nevis proceedings by McCaffrey.

The deal was aiming at using INE Company funds to pay out Quinn instead of paying dividends to long suffering shareholders.  This flies in the face of the actual validity of Quinn’s shareholding as this is in dispute as part of the current Nevis action.  The sale agreement was clearly meant to bypass the legal process.

In addition to the $23 Million dollar pay-out the judgement cites that INE has already granted him a floating charge over its assets in order to secure payment of that sum.

Judge Bannister in Nevis said the following of the agreement before making the order to halt the deal:

“I will grant an order that no further step is to be taken in prosecution of the share sale agreement until after judgment in this action“

The order by the Judge went on to state a range of  wide ranging restraints put on the Company between now and the expedited  trial planned for June 2012.

Aine Coffey from the Sunday Times published this article.
