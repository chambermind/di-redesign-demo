Scientology promotional video

In January 2008, a video was leaked on the internet that included edited portions of an acceptance speech Cruise gave in 2004 when receiving the Freedom Medal from the International Association of Scientologists.[4] The video was published on YouTube, but was taken down after only a few hours due to legal threats and a copyright claim by the Church of Scientology. The church also forced several other websites to pull the video. One site, Gawker.com, has to date refused to pull the video, despite threats of a lawsuit by the church.[5]

The video clearly fits into fair use criteria, and RationalWiki stands in solidarity with other sites who are hosting the video. Here is the video as it originally appeared on YouTube.

For those who wish to examine his wisdom at length, read this transcript.[6]

The following is a transcript of the Tom Cruise Scientology Indoctrination Video that circulated on YouTube and various blogs this week. Reports say the Church of Scientology issued takedown orders to sites that chose to broadcast the video. For the sake of posterity (and as a cure for an hour of boredom), I threw on my Bose headphones and transcribed the video as seen HERE. While I have done my best to reproduce the video in its entirety, I’m sure I’ve missed a few “you know”s and, likely, some Scientology phrases I simply don’t understand. The following is offered without bias or comment.

Here is a quick glossary to help you through the transcript:
KSW: Keeping Scientology Working, a key “policy” in Scientology
SP: Suppressive Person, one who is antagonistic to Scientology
PTS: Potential Trouble Source, someone who is connected to an SP
Org: Organization. Any Scientology church, group or business is generally referred to as an org.
David Miscavige: Chairman of the Board of Religious Technology Center (RTC), a non-profit organization formed in 1982 to preserve, maintain and protect the Scientology religion. (From Scientology web site)
Out-Ethics: Immoral or unethical behavior, as defined by Scientology’s system of mores (called Ethics Tech).
LRH: L. Ron Hubbard, Founder of the Church of Scientology
Tech: Technology, the writings, policies and teachings of Scientology founder L. Ron Hubbard.
IAS: International Association of Scientologists, the official membership organization of Scientology “
