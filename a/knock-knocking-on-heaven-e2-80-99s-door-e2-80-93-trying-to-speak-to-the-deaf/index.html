….. the presenter Michael Ryan was unable to make contact with any of the people in these organisations! Yet at the end, the programme contradicted itself stating  “TV3 have been in ongoing discussions with the House of Prayer and hope to be able to broadcast their view on the matters raised on tonight’s programme in the near future”

Joanna and The Sage are mouthpieces for the H of P on the DI blog. At least they are unlike Tony Quinn who does not have a door bell, a post box or have the door nailed shut. When a summons was delivered to him they had to nail it to the door.

The facts you suggest are contradicted by the evidence of the programme. All the groups were approached on a number of occasions but they refused to communicate. Look at the documentary are you blind?

Also I was in Knockbridge and Fr McGinnity actually believes someone trying to interview him is entrapment. Poor Pat Coleman was trying to protect him.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/09/07/fr-gerard-mcginnity-is-parish-priest-of-knockmore-co-louth-2/ I saw the journalists deliver an invitation. They called but Fr M is afraid to stand up and communicate what he is about. So argument weak shout loud after the event.

Rather you realised that you had not taken up the opportunity to give an interview and when it was aired you had to pretend that you had engaged so you came up with the statement below on Tuesday. A little late in the day. You were phoning TV 3 since August in order to try to stop the programme going ahead. You have been resisting giving an interview for weeks. My impression from them was that they would give you an interview but you keep kicking for touch!

So because they could not nail you down this is all they could say: “TV3 have been in ongoing discussions with the House of Prayer and hope to be able to broadcast their view on the matters raised on tonight’s programme in the near future”

Even if they could agree with you about a statement to publish, they would not have published the one you tried to pretend they had agreed to but did not publish! I challenge Christina and Fr McGinnity to go in and give an interview explain the houses, the cheques etc. Get Fr McGinnity to explain how what he is doing is consistent with being a priest of the Archdiocese of Armagh.
