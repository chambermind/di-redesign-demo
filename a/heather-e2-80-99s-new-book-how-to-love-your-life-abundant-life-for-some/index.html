HEATHER’S NEW BOOK

Heather Sanford just sounds like an amazing person when you read the notes on the author and overview. Here at DI we were particularly amazed to see the note on enjoy our family and have a blessed home……. Well now Heather the reports we are getting is that your church has gone along way towards destroying homes and enslaving people so that they do not get to enjoy anything in life apart from serving you? Do you encourage young women to enjoy relationships in your church? Are they encouraged to experience the joys of meeting young men not just from your Church but amongst the other churches? Do you encourage those whose husbands are not believers to spend time with them, or are they expected to commit spiritual adultery by over doing their involvement in the work of the Church? I Cor 7?

I believe you encourage the break up of marriages and the encouragement devastating results in family life.

To the African members of this Church I believe you are waking up like others before you. As a South African I say to you do not take on the white man’s burden but only the yoke of Christ

Mother of 4, missionary, pastor – how about director of staff to ensure they school my children, feed them, wash them. Also under the guise of missionary work actually misdirect people into commercial directions.

Heather’s new book

 About the Author:

Missionary, mother of four, pastor, and much more, Heather Sanford left her native Texas to follow the call of God. She and her husband, Kevin, live in beautiful Galway, Ireland, where they pastor the church they pioneered fifteen years ago, after leaving a mission in Managua, Nicaragua, which they also founded. She has a tremendous grace to teach the ways of God, having learned to live them herself through many adventures of life and twenty years in ministry. With her contagious spirit, she ignites the heart and provokes a deep hunger to know God more. The profound truths she shares encourage all her listeners to settle for nothing less than heaven on Earth.

Overview:

This journal was created with you in mind! Since life certainly is a journey, the aim is to give you one more tool in this journey for loving your life. There’s no better way to put the truths from How to Love Your Life into action than to journal your heart and your plan.

We all want to love our lives, enjoy our family, and have a blessed home.

Heather Sanford’s book, How to Love Your Life, will help you to frame these inborn desires as targets and then assist you with your trajectory to make sure that the one life you have to live is a bull’s-eye! This journal, designed to accompany the book, will enable you to chronicle your journey day by day and step by step.

The pages of this journal are filled with expounded questions, as well as Scriptures and prayers from the book, with an inspiring new section “from God’s heart to yours.” Take time to meditate on the questions on a deeper level here than when you first read them in the book.
