Heather – The Sweet Briar!

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

In order to understand how Kevin and Heather treat people and why they expect things to be done a certain way, we first of all have to look at where they have come from. As part of our ongoing investigation as a result of information shared by present and former members of Abundant life, we wanted to share some of our findings with the wider public.

Heather is a graduated of ‘Sweet Briar College’ in Virgina, USA.

Its tuition and fees are $30,195 (2010-11). So as you can see Heather’s family were never afraid to spend money on the best education for her.

What is very interesting about this college is the male to female ratio.

Sweet Briar College has a total undergraduate enrollment of 745, with a gender distribution of 3.1 percent male students and 96.9 percent female students. We know Heather discourages her ladies in the church from dating or having a life outside of the church without her approval. Could this stem from her education at Briar College where she was predominantly surrounded by women? Below is a picture from the Briar College website – Heather Sanford AKA Heather A McGowen on the left and Kevin Sanford on the far right. We have reason to believe this photo was taken around Christmas time due to the lack of sun tans.

The Sweet Briar

The motto for Sweet Briar College is ‘Rosam Quae Meruit Ferat

“She who has earned the rose may bear it.”

Strange irony in this motto when we consider Heather’s involvement with Abundant Life we think.
