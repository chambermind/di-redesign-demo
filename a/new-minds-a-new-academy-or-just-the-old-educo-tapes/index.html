New Minds a new Academy or just the old Educo Tapes?

Tony Quinn

A person in contact with Dialogue Ireland involved in dealing with the effects of losing her husband due to his involvement with a variety of courses and groups found an advertisement about the New Minds Fitness Academy located in Glanmire, Co Cork in a magazine and asked us to look into it. She also happens to be a health professional.

http://newmindsfitness.com/


Open pdf below:

Educo Course Cork
As the Director of DI had grown up near Glanmire, in Co. Cork he made some enquiries about the course but no one had heard of it.

After doing some further research he discovered that the owner Damien Twohig
was also involved with the Educo Gyms which we know a lot about.


http://www.educogym.com/
He then phoned and asked to speak to the owner Damien Twohig.

http://rsvpmagazine.ie/rsvp-wedding-irene-walsh-and-damien-twohig/

In this article about the wedding of Damien Twohig and Irene Walsh it mentions he worked for David Kiely Kitchens in Carrigaline.

Damien Twohig worked with David for approximately four years in sales from 2004-2008. In 2005/2006 Damien Twohig went on a seminar to the Bahamas with Tony Quinn and from then on he became involved with Tony Quinn. This seminar cost approximately €20,000. Damien also attended several meetings with Tony Quinn. Damien left David Kiely kitchens in 2008 and then started his own kitchen company in Blackpool which subsequently closed soon after.

New Minds Fitness Academy is Cork’s most elite Personal Training & Fitness Academy. We specialise in Personal Training here at New Minds Fitness Academy.We have the best resources for our students and we want our students to be as focused on their results as we are in our own Personal Training.The New Minds Fitness Academy Certificate in Personal Training withFitness Instructing is a highly certified ITEC qualification that is accredited by REPS (Register of Exercise Professionals) and is on the National Framework of Qualifications.

Later that evening the owner phoned back and I had two concerns which he refused to address:

•”Experienced Course Tutors with over 20 years experience in Personal Training, Nutrition, Strength & Conditioning and Business.”

1. Who are the people that have 20 years of experience in regard to delivering these services and why are they not named on the brochure? It is all a bit vague.
2. The second question was related to the cost of these various courses?
The owner Damien Twohig informed me that they are ITEC accredited.


However, I respond that was not my question, rather who are the tutors and what is their training? I asked him was he linked to Tony Quinn and he avoided that question and stresses that though he owns the franchise for the two Educo Gyms in Cork,  New Minds is independent entity.  He also is adamant that New Minds Fitness Academy is totally separate from Educo. He owns all the businesses he tells me. They also sell the Quinn supplements sold in the Tony Quinn health shops.
(See attached document outlining his ownership?)
http://www.solocheck.ie/Irish-Company/New-Minds-Limited-432188

The document also has the course outlines and the links to all the services offered.

New Minds Cork

He then tells me to contact ITEC the body that certified them in the UK.
I protest the issue I am asking about is not certification but the names of those
delivering the lectures and their qualifications? He ends the discussion.

I immediately write to ITEC whose every response makes the plot thicken! We publish the full correspondence here for you to decide!

The next day I phoned and spoke to Nessa Cotter who also told me to contact ITEC. What was bizzare was that later I was told by ITEC that she was the owner?

Nessa Cotter (28) is the fashion stylist and blogger behind www.lifeslusthaves.com  . She lives in Rochestown with her fiancé, Munster rugby star Denis Hurley….

From Tuesdays to Thursdays I work in PR and marketing at Educogym so once I’m in the door, I’m onto emails and left to my own devices to develop and push the business.

http://www.networkcork.com/members/profile?cid=683

Back in Dublin I talked to a former Educo trainer who knows the organisation from top to bottom. This is his take on the situation:
Damien and his wife Irene Walsh are long term devotees of Quinn’s.  In the early days of the Educo Gyms their gyms were held out by Tony Quinn as the role models as how to run them.

My informant pointed out that those in other gyms did not want to follow their example which included getting those that called to sign up to pay €50 up front for a consultation so they would not leak before coming in. They also had a receptionist who got a bonus for recruiting  people, but the other gyms felt that would increase their costs, and rejected their advice.

So why is there no mention of Damien’s and Irene’s connection to Tony Quinn,
and Educo? He may have his reasons to give the impression that New Minds Limited is not connected to Educo but as far as DI is concerned we have a duty of care to the public whether they are connected or not to check their credentials. When we see advertisements for courses to be taken by transition students and costing over two grand more scrutiny is needed of what is going on.

Also with our forty years at researching issues related to Quinn we feel
it is important that the Cork public are informed?
