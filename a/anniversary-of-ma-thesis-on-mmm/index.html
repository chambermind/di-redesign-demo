Anniversary of MA Thesis on MMM

Magnificat Meal Movement

This time in May 2006 I had to have a cut off point for new material being part of my thesis.


So when people read it today it is important to realise it is based on very little inside information at the time. Certainly in Ireland people were frightened to talk, it was too recent. We had witness testimony of former members but we could not get an interview with Debra or people like Claire Murphy as we were threatened with stalking them. I must pay tribute here to the persistence of Wal Maggs who gave himself tirelessly to assisting people leaving the MMM and nearly single handedly taking on a person like Debra that most tried to avoid out of fear. My research was heavily dependent on people like Wal Maggs who had been an early leader in the MMM and who had produced two editions of “An End Times Tragedy,” his theological and cult analysis of the group. Before I came to Australia I was on the phone to him nearly every other day and his health was not as threatened as it is now. In fact he had invited me to stay with him while on my mission, but he had to go for tests, and Judi and Phil Bloomfield were great hosts and added to my knowledge of Helidon and the MMM.

The Ostrich is the bird that strikes me as summing up the general societal attitude in Helidon, put your head in the sand and hope it flies somewhere else! The Catholic Church seemed to be unwilling to assist in any real terms and during the writing of my thesis hardly ever responded to any of my communications. DI is dealing with the victims of this group in Australia, Europe, and the USA, and the church could have done more to assist. When the thesis was published I never even got the courtesy of an acknowledgement when I sent it to them.

See the pdf of the thesis and any new information will be added, but leave comments of any corrections or also add to our knowledge.

SPIRITUALITY AND CULTISM-1

http://www.dialogueireland.org/dicontent/resources/video/mmm.html

The MMM community goes on and Debra who was exiled in Vanuatu has been seen around  the area of Brisbane and Helidon. I remember when I visited in 2003 few people had seen Debra since the events of 1999. I am going to publish my archive and hopefully others will bring us new evidence!

I saw her twice, once at the boundary to the property of Mount St Mary’s with one of her Mercedes Benz cars with the famous number plate, CORMA on it. The first occasion she was with John Tonner in attendance and the second time in Twowoomba at a shopping centre where by complete accident I saw Debra purchasing clothes with John. I had been brought out of Helidon to see Fr Tom Keegan and Judi who gave me a lift gave me a few minutes to shop as all I had been doing was around Helidon. That evening I was cautioned by the police that I was stalking her.

At the grounds near the property I found a letter telling me to not come near the property. My only regret was that I did not get arrested as they had trespassed beyond their property and created an illegal entrance on what was Church property.

I would have loved to meet Fr John Ryan he was a Crocodile Dundee kind of priest. He would have got the bulldozers in!
