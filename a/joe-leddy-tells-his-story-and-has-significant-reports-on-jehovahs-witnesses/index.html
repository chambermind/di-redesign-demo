Jehovah's Witnesses

Joe Leddy is Dialogue Ireland’s Consultant on Jehovah’s Witnesses. His site has had problems recently, but we have been able to retrieve a lot of it.

He is currently very busy with a number of projects, and major health issues. So send any requests for information to DI for the present.

My Mission-Jehovah’s Witnesses

It is my mission or goal to educate people about the dangers of certain religious organizations. Regardless what the critics and be moaners say I believe that religion still pays a huge part in the day to day life people both hear in Europe and indeed through the world. It is easy to be cynical I would suggest that one is expected to by cynical when it comes to religion. I believe that people still believe in God however I believe the vast majority of God fearing people have lost faith in the churches of the world. When one looks at the actions of some of the major churches it is easy not to trust these churches, it is very easy to be cynical. This goes for Catholics, Protestants, Mormons, Born Again Christians, Jehovah Witnesses and even Muslims. Since 9/11 and the Middle Eastern wars we have witnesses huge gaps in the teachings of the Muslim religions. It is obvious to the observer that the Muslim faith is as fragmented and as misunderstood as the Catholic church was during the inquisitions.

At this juncture it is worth asking, do we all worship the same god? I don’t think anyone can answer that question without causing some kind of controversy. Jehovah Witnesses will have you believe that they are the only one true religion, the organization that God himself uses to speak to the world through their version of the bible and through their many publications. They would have you believe that the world is going to end soon, can we trust their version of events? I honestly don’t know, however in order to better understand these and many more unanswered questions it is my mission to get these questions out there and to try and stimulate debate. If one has a better understanding of the teachings of any organization it is always going to help them make a sound judgement.
