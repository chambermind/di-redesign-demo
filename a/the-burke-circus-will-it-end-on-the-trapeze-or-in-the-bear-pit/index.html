Submission to Governing Authorities:

13 Let everyone be subject to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except that which God has established. The authorities that exist have been established by God. 2 Consequently, whoever rebels against the authority is rebelling against what God has instituted, and those who do so will bring judgment on themselves. 3 For rulers hold no terror for those who do right, but for those who do wrong. Do you want to be free from fear of the one in authority? Then do what is right and you will be commended. 4 For the one in authority is God’s servant for your good. But if you do wrong, be afraid, for rulers do not bear the sword for no reason. They are God’s servants, agents of wrath to bring punishment on the wrongdoer. 5 Therefore, it is necessary to submit to the authorities, not only because of possible punishment but also as a matter of conscience.

You have been arriving at churches and agencies acting as if you had a divine mission as experts in transgenderism.

I think you, as a family, thought you had your gig nicely organised. Either you have a safe house near Wilson’s Hospital, or there is a nearly maniacal idea that you travel from Castlebar every morning. Then, even when Enoch knows he is trespassing (as he was sacked), you don’t show up and pretend you are an employed teacher. Wandering around the school to the extent that they had to employ security (paid for by the Department of Education).

So here, as a man of principle, he is taking money from the ‘Transgender state’ he hates.

Fundamentally, your little world came to an end when Justice Brian Cregan cut the crap. You don’t send people home for Christmas to consider how to plan more acts of trespass.

I had a less radical plan of having Enoch arrested every day and brought to Mullingar Garda station, and entering into a deradicalisation programme which involved the study of undue influence and biblical study with an Evangelical pastor. Enoch had written a couple of books, but in all this time, nothing on transgenderism?

Also, the climate is changing around transgenderism, but Enoch is breaking the law of trespass, so his voice on the subject is totally diluted by your family’s pride and arrogance. The family thought they would get into the Trump St Patrick’s Day circus, and your mother being thrown out and losing her shoes was frankly embarrassing.

You hoped to be part of the Trump and Musk show, but the judge has decided it is time for this circus to end.

I now hope you will realise the whole family might soon be in Mountjoy. The idea of you bowsies shouting at judges was rudely interrupted when the judge used a video link to stop you from interfering with the courts, which was a perfect response to your out-of-control behaviour. Also, the Independent calls the family a ‘well-known Evangelical family,’ or more recently, ‘fundamentalist Christians.’

My understanding is that your mother might have come from a Catholic family from Mayo and was involved with a group like Campus Crusade at Galway University. Another source suggested the Elim Pentecostal Church, but there seems to be no evidence that she is submitted to a church anywhere. Therefore, it is likely you are a home church, home school (that of a very high quality), and I believe a music school.

This means you have no counterbalance to your loud voices and confused shouting with the still small voice of the Holy Spirit. How can any of you form relationships in this environment to form families and obtain employment?

A few years ago, I offered to do an intervention at your home on behalf of the local churches, but they decided against it. In fact, even though there is an Evangelical Alliance, these churches do not have a regulatory framework. I had done this type of intervention in Tuam over 20 years ago and had been planning an intervention with the Moderator of the Presbyterian Church, Trevor Gribben, but to cut a long story short, he resigned last month.

Towards the end of 2022, I wrote to Enoch offering to come and engage with him, as I literally am within 500 metres of the prison. However, as you know, only a prisoner can invite a person in.

My concern is that you could take a very dark road forward; none of you can actually work here in Ireland, and your gifts could be used to great benefit to grow the church and add to the apologetics of the gospel. I could see you doing something like Charlie Kirk, but like me, you do not share his Christian nationalism (an impression I got from the interviews you gave during the Presidential election).

Anyway, I again offer to engage with you as a family, and we could either meet at the prison or at an acceptable venue.
