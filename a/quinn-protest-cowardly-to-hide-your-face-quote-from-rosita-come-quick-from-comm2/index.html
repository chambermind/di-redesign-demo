No Temptation Eve

Submitted on 2012/09/17 at 1:34 am
It was a cold windy rainy day with some sunshine to keep me warm. Mike Garde advised me to cover my face in the style of Nefertiti. She gives public talks about Scientology. A previous seminar and knowing about Quinn justifies covering up my identity.
TQ had 18 security staff trying to stop us protesting. He was afraid of a bigger turnout. I was told he wanted the area sealed off to prevent us from protesting. He arrived two and a half hours late because he is very nervous and afraid of being confronted. There is video footage of what happened to another protester.
A very poor attendance compared to previous years and mostly middle-aged men and women dressed to impress and a few long-term staff. I recognised some of them. Very few young faces so I suppose many B shareholders did not attend. Some women dressed like they were off out for a night on the town wearing sexy looking clothes and really high heels that would cripple you. A lot of them look like they have money to burn so I suppose that’s why Quinn makes an effort to keep them in EDUCO.
I took a good look at the Educoists when they were leaving the Helix after the seminar finished. Most of them had the same smug smile and some looked very angry and stupefied when they read the protest signs. Do none of them read the papers? One older lady said to her companion “Why do they bother?” A few asked me “Why are you covering your face?” and told me to “take it off” and some were hostile and calling me a coward. One of them stood in front of me staring and looked very angry. Overall, they were angrier leaving the seminar than they were arriving.
A few women laughed at us as they passed by and one woman laughed a lot. She did this deliberately. I think she thought she was great and better than us and we would be put off being there. She could not have been more wrong as this behaviour is exactly why I went there to observe.
Two older men, shareholders, were very willing to talk about positive results. They say exactly the same about their business doing really well and also they told us they received dividends. The first man to talk to us mentioned he told everyone about the great success he had.

He was quickly removed from our company by security before he had a chance to answer Mike Garde’s questions about how he got dividends when there were none received by other shareholders and the second man admitted to the fib of receiving dividends and said it was a loan. The conversation showed a lack of information about Tony Quinn’s court cases and Bannister’s findings. The second man said he did not want to know about it. He was happier not to know and he said that it was bad practice in a business to receive dividends. I don’t think they know about other Educoists being in debt because of many loans and the terrible things that happened to them on seminars. Maybe they don’t want to know about this either. Tony Quinn tells them not to read Dialogue Ireland or read the papers or watch the news. He controls them. I feel very uneasy to see how helpless they are to change this unless they are confronted with the truth.
Some people got very angry and threatening and wanted to complain to the police when Mike Garde was taking photos. The security men did not want anyone talking to us and removed them quickly before they had a chance to find out why we were there. They insisted that we stand at a distance from the Helix even though we have right of way as the space in front of the Helix is for public use. They also tried to stop our protest by standing in front of us to block our protest signs.

It is very strange to observe and talk to people who think they are in control of their lives and all the signs of their behaviour and how they speak are telling me they are not. I am glad I did not go on another seminar. My advice to anyone who reads this post is to tell everyone about how controlling he is and advise them to stay away from all Tony Quinn businesses.
As people arrived we could tell who were taking part by the expensive diaries and hardback journals they were coming in with to understand getting the deep insights of the Easy Way, or should it read Easy Rider?
