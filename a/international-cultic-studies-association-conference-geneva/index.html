International Cultic Studies Association Conference Geneva

Conferences & Commentaries

ICSA’s Annual Conference in Geneva, Switzerland will take place in two months.

If you haven’t yet registered and want to attend, please register now.  The longer you wait the more difficult it will become to obtain attractive lodging.

Please tell others about the conference if you believe they might be interested in attending.  Sending this information to your mail list, if you have one, can be very helpful.

Approximately 90 speakers are contributing to the conference.  There is an abundance of sessions for all of ICSA’s constituencies:

Former group members

Family members

Researchers

Helping professionals

Students

Others

The conference home pages can be found here:

English (http://icsahome.com/infoserv_conferences/conference_home_2009_geneva.asp)

On the home page, you will find links for the program, information on lodging, and other matters.

Thank you.

Michael D. Langone, Ph.D.
