TONY GOES THE EXTRA MILE BY NICOLA TALLANT SUNDAY WORLD

Tony Quinn

TONY GOES THE EXTRA MILE

BY NICOLA TALLANT

SUNDAY WORLD SEPTEMBER 12, 2010

SW Xfactor p7

Quinn getting desperate in bid for clients

MUCKY Messiah Tony Quinn is hoping to use the X Factor to get his business back on track. The guru will jet into Dublin this weekend to try to flog more of his mind-bending seminars and is hoping one of his latest recruits will help woo supporters to part with their hard-earned money. Quinn sent out letters this week to all ‘Educo’ followers to urge them to come and see him this Sunday in the RDS where he will make a rare appearance on stage in Ireland. But anyone who thinks that the 64 year

old with the perma-tan and blonde hair doesn’t move with the times – think again.

This time Tony, who has amassed a huge personal fortune through his Educo cult, is

promising to produce his very own X Factor sensation with singer Lorraine Nolan.

“Yes, we are going to have our very own X Factor, with you as the Judges,” he gushes. “On the last seminar there was a lovely young lady called Lorraine Nolan. When I learned that she had a four-and-a-half octave voice, I had to hear it.”

Dwindling

Quinn has booked out the Concert Hall at the RDS for his grand return to Ireland

where he will address his dwindling group of followers. “On the day I will give you a copy of our New Brochure. We will go through this together. “This will act as the map that will enable you to bring you and your life up a few octaves.” Quinn is trying to salvage his reputation in Ireland despite a new wave of controversy over his involvement in an oil company in Belize where shareholders are at war with Directors

who ‘gifted’ Quinn a large stake in the company after it struck oil. Many have complained that he holds ‘undue influence’ over one of it’s Directors Susan Morrice. To date court cases have been launched in the US, Belize and now Nevis all naming Quinn. The former butcher held his annual July seminars in Monte Carlo at bargain basement prices. Seats went for just €6000, a 66 per cent reduction on his usual prices. Quinn loves to associate himself with the famous. In the past he has acted as a

hypnotist for boxer Steve Collins, he tried to befriend film-maker Jim Sheridan and

now he is hoping singer Lorraine Nolan will hit the big time. She has been a lead

vocalist on Riverdance and held a concert tour of Songbird – the Eva Cassidy tour.
