Michelle’s is bringing new evidence to light on the Debra and the MMM

Magnificat Meal Movement

In 2003 I was in Helidon, Queensland and on one of the last days of my visit kindly put up by Phil and Judy Bloomfield I visited Michelle and Gordon. They talked freely but did not want to give me a full interview for my MA thesis. They were tired of the whole MMM saga and also were exhausted emotionally after all they went through. Debra had vilified them and they just wanted to get on with their lives.  When my thesis was published I did give them a look at it, but by then it was too late to change it. Later we will publish comments from Michelle from 2007. We have to balance the reality is that apostates do tend to see every aspect of their experience as negative. On the other hand we need to recognise that there is such a reality as brainwashing and people who escape do tend to bring a clear view as to what really is going on in the group. I am finding that my thesis’s conclusion that Debra had a genuine vision to begin with seems to be contradicted with the evidence of the witnesses who have come forward since 2006.

Apparently Michelle and Gordon have made a settlement in relation to Mary’s Mount the compound where Debra’s followers still reside. We have no idea what the terms of this settlement are as it is totally confidential.

Debra continues as a high flying refugee apparently living in a cave in Hawaii or hide out in California on a farm. Time we assume is also spent in French Caledonia which is where some of her transactions and the gold sales were directed from earlier in the decade.

Michelle’s Testimony

Dear Mike,

Gordon says that he was never declared bankrupt. So I don’t know what the facts are there and I don’t think you have much chance in involving Gordon in any more of this, he has had enough and so have I.

When people are in a sad place in their lives it is easy to get sucked in by someone like Debra. It happens all the time. I was unhappy in my marriage and was looking for something else. If I had not already been involved in a prayer group in my parish, I would never have got involved with her in the first place. Religion seems to be the easiest tool to use to manipulate people. My ex was obsessed with seers and visionaries and he was the one who was keen to sell our home. If you had any idea what type of person he was as a husband you would understand that I had little choice. He was much taken with Debra and she tried so very hard to suck me in, and the biggest mistake I made was to be desperate enough to want to believe her. People turn a blind eye to many things in life because they don’t want to be wrong. I was one of those people. I knew she was lying; I didn’t want to believe that she was. It is as simple as that. She also knew that I was second guessing, that is why she claimed I was her best friend. She needed to keep a close eye on me because she was always worried that I was going to leave. I stayed with the movement much longer than I wanted to, I was dying to get out. Having nowhere to go was the thing that stopped me. I tried many times to expose her while I was still a part of the group, but had little support from the others. The ones that promised to support me buckled under Debra’s power once she found out what I

Kind regards,

Michelle.

From what family members say, Gordon was not as aware of her lies as much as they were. Love being blind when you are young. Apparently her parents were very hard on her as a child. Dear Mike,

This is something I have been putting together for a while. This is why I believe that Debra was not genuine ……ever!

The day I met her she came to a prayer group in my parish church. I was one of the leaders in this prayer group. My husband was also there that day as he and another parishioner organised for her to be there.  After prayer we all gathered to have afternoon tea. There was only a small group of about 15 or so. Debra had most of the group standing around her, except me and a couple of others.  We were chatting away and I looked over at the other group to see Debra staring at me. I ignored her and kept on talking to my friends.  Out of the corner of my eye I could see Debra was getting annoyed that I was ignoring her until she couldn’t stand it any longer. She came over to me and pulled me aside.  She told me that God had just told her that I was chosen to be her special friend.  I was a little flattered I must admit, so I listened to what she had to say. She told me she was a visionary and she was gathering the people God has chosen to do his work, through her.

At this time I was in a very vulnerable state. My marriage was falling apart and I needed something to push me in a totally different direction. This seemed like the answer. Debra had only moved to Toowoomba a couple of weeks prior and just so happened to be renting a house a couple of blocks from my house. She invited me around to her house the next day. When I turned up she had another lady there from the neighbouring parish.  When Debra found out that I had a computer at home she was all over me. It turned out that the other lady also had a computer. I was asked to help start up a newsletter with this other lady. It wasn’t long after that I typed up Debra’s first published book, Diary 5.

While I was typing her diary I’d come across messages with lines and dates missing sometimes there was only half a message. I questioned her about this and she told me to fill in the blanks. I was surprised. I told her that it was not up to me and that I would not do something so dishonest.  She told me to give the pages to her and she would ask God to fill in the blanks. I thought this was a little off.

Debra moved to the other side of town three weeks after arriving in Toowoomba as the house they were renting was too small. I travelled 20 minutes across town regularly. Prayer meetings were held in Debra’s very large home. A modest group of very nice people all devout Catholics.  As long as Debra was the centre of attention at all times, things went smoothly. If a couple of us broke away from the gathering and talked amongst ourselves Debra would show a very different side. She showed a very dominant, jealous and paranoid side that stopped people in their tracks very quickly.

I was suspicious of Debra from the early stages, but I felt guilty for thinking negative things because I was afraid that God would punish me for not believing his prophet. When someone tells you that they are speaking to and actually seeing Jesus himself, it’s pretty overwhelming. There is a certain amount of fear one feels when one tries to confront a so called prophet.

There were so many things that were not right, things that did not ring true. Debra claimed that she had the stigmata. She told me on a number of occasions that Gordon had to clean up all the blood.  Out of curiosity I would talk to Gordon about it and he would say “What blood, I’ve never seen any blood”.  One day she showed me a large surgical patch in the centre of her chest and she told me that she had an open wound and how painful it was. I asked her why it was there and she said that she had the wound in the heart. I was so shocked I didn’t know what to think. I was thinking shouldn’t the wound be in the side like Jesus.  I asked Gordon about this thinking that he would definitely know if the wound was there, he was her husband for crying out loud! Lo and behold, he said that he had never seen any wound in her chest. I thought I was going mad, why would Debra tell me these outrageous lies knowing that I would probably find out the truth? Of course when I confronted her about the wound she told me that Gordon was telling me lies and that she was God’s prophet and how dare I disbelieve her.  On another day she had a large mosquito bite on the knuckle of one of her hands, she had scratched it until it was raw. As I was leaving her house that day she came running out to the car holding her hand, it was covered in blood. I instantly thought that she had scratched the bite to make it bleed and rubbed the blood on the rest of her hand. To her surprise I did not react at all I just kept on reversing out the driveway and drove home. The next day she called me over to her home and couldn’t wait to comment on the blood incident. I just brushed it off and commented that she had scratched the mosquito bite until it bled, she was furious with me for not believing that it was the stigmata.

As the months went by I found Debra’s possessiveness of me to be very overwhelming. I am a person who likes my own company and her demanding nature was too much. I made friends with everyone in the group much to her annoyance. It got to the stage that I would have my phone off the hook so that I could have some peace. I went shopping with my cousin once a week and she eventually put a stop to that by finding ways to get me to come to her home to do work such as photocopying newsletters and mailing out diaries. Eventually, I was doing the banking, mailing and typing.  She had other helpers but she seemed to want me there as much as possible.

Many times I thought of breaking away but she would always win me over with a message from God. She asked me to go to Medjudorje with her. I had never been overseas so I jumped at the chance. When we arrived I had to share a room with one of the women who later became one of the core group. This lady and I became close friends and Debra became so jealous that she said she could see large black rubber bands coming from this lady and the bands were wrapped around me like snakes. She said this lady had a demon in her and she was trying to corrupt my soul.  After this I had to keep my distance to placate Debra, her wrath was not pleasant.

I watched Debra very closely especially when friendships were made with different people in the group. She played one against the other on a regular basis. She talked about people behind their backs and used God and guilt tactics to keep people under her control. Nobody wants to hear that they have a demon inside them and certainly don’t want others to know about it.  People made the mistake of telling her their personal problems, things that one would expect a prophet of God to keep to herself. This was not the case she would get some kind of kick out of repeating what she had heard to anyone who wanted to listen.

I suppose I provoked her in many ways just to get her to react so that other people could see how badly she could behave. I would deliberately sit in the lounge room with Gordon and his daughter and chat to them while Debra and her little group would all be sitting around the dining room table. All eyes were on her because she would be the only one talking. I have never met anyone who could talk for hours on end like her. Her school teaching skills were put to good use. She would get so annoyed with me for not sitting at the table with the rest of them that she would talk louder and louder and in the end make some nasty comment about how rude some people are. I found her constant talking and bragging extremely tiring. The exact opposite to how a prophet should behave.

I am a dressmaker by trade and I made myself a dress and wore it to a gathering where Debra was giving a talk. She commented on my dress and asked me to make her one. I made her the dress and everyone commented how it made her look slim, as she was overweight. I made several of them to take to Spain in different colours. When Debra saw them she wanted some to take on the trip and one of the other ladies wanted them as well. So the three of us went over in these gowns and people were calling us the “Rainbow Ladies”.  When we came back home a couple of weeks later Debra said that she’d had a vision of Our Lady in a white gown and that all the women in our group should wear this gown in different colours. She described the gown to me and surprise, surprise it was the same gown I had made months ago. She told all the followers that it was Our Lady’s request and they believed her. It didn’t matter that it was originally my dress! I confronted Debra about this deception and she just brushed it off with the comment that God uses people for her to do his work. It was amazing how we all just turned a blind eye to this little discrepancy.

One day Debra showed me a gold cross that her father had given to her. It was two J’s forming the cross. She said she had not worn it even though she’d had it for years. At the next group gathering she said she had received a message from Jesus and a vision of a cross he wanted us to wear. The cross was to be called “The Victory Cross” the very same cross she showed me days before. I couldn’t believe it! I confronted her about this later in private and she said that God used her father for this special gift. I thought I was going crazy I asked myself “Why is this happening to me no one will believe me if I say anything”?

By this time Debra had pulled the wool over everyone’s eyes. It seemed she could lie as much as she liked and no one would ever challenge her. She told me that Satan had put a false screen over my eyes and I would continue to see things that were not really happening and that he wanted me to doubt her because God had chosen me for something special. I was supposedly being tested. I was so confused she managed to make me feel unworthy and very unholy.

I just could not shake the feeling that something was so very wrong. If Debra is a prophet would she be a liar? Would she have a lust for control? Would she be vindictive? Would she have fits of jealousy and rage? Why would God choose someone as mean and selfish as this person? There are many more humble people in the world you could have picked God! All these questions went round in my head every day. I thought “Maybe I have to endure all this for another purpose”. Debra was using mind control to make me and others believe that she was the only one who was right and everyone else was wrong. The confusing thing was there were times when Debra was really nice. She showed signs of humility and compassion but it was not consistent and as the years went by she became so skilled at pretending to be different things to different people it was hard to work out which personality was really her.

She told me that when she was a child at school she was the teacher’s pet because she had the nuns believing that she was almost perfect. She would play nasty tricks on someone in class and when the nun asked the culprit to own up she would be excluded because the nun automatically thought it couldn’t be Debra Burslem, she’s too good.

I wanted to believe her and that was my weakness. I wanted to believe that God had put a prophet right in our midst. I believed her when she told me I was chosen for something special my desire for this was stronger than reality. We all go through life making mistakes and we hopefully learn from them. Our faith is a large part of our lives and we all look for something that will make that faith stronger something out of the ordinary tends to make us stand up and take notice. Even though I knew there was something wrong I wanted to hang on in the hope of finding some truth.

When Debra told everyone that she was on a Eucharistic fast I really did think I was a bad person for doubting her. She started this while we were still in Toowoomba. I went to town with her one day and she pulled up in front of the shops and told me to wait in the car. I had a strong urge to follow her. She walked into a café and I waited to see what she was going to do. She asked for 6 dim sims.

I walked up behind her and asked her what she was doing and she was surprised to see me behind her. She made the excuse that she was buying them for Gordon. We drove back to her place and she put the food in the kitchen and we went to the dining room to do some paper work. It was getting late so I got ready to leave. I had only reached the front door and realised I had left something behind so I walked back into the room and saw Debra eating one of the dim sims. She did not hear me come in and was so shocked when I asked what she was doing she threw the dim sim and the packet into the kitchen pantry. My heart sank as I thought not another deception and why do I have to witness this? She used her usual explanation.  I was imagining things and she was only sniffing the food, she was allowed to smell the food to take away the temptation to eat.

I was so upset about this when I got home I told my husband but he didn’t want to know. The next day I told two group members and they seemed to believe me but one of them went and told Debra and she humiliated me and called me a liar. She was still on the fast when she moved to Helidon and at this stage she said as well as not eating she could no longer drink.  I had just finished making her another gown and she asked me to put it in her cupboard in the room she was using as a bedroom in the convent. She must have forgotten that she had a huge stash of food and drink in the cupboard where I hung the dress. I didn’t know what to do there was no point saying anything so I kept it to myself for the time being. Another day we both went to the Helidon café to buy groceries and she bought a cooked chicken. She said she was buying it for her daughter. When we returned to the convent she accidentally took one of my parcels into her living area.  I went back to get it and there she was eating a piece of the chicken. I thought O My God not again. She used the same line; you didn’t see anything the devil’s playing tricks.

By this time I was so sick of the lies and to make things worse one of the Irish visitors said that she’d witnessed Debra eating as well. I went to Debra and told her that I was not keeping quiet about this huge deception. She laughed in my face and told me that no one would believe me and that I was demonised. I was so distraught I had nowhere to turn I knew what she was going to do. She called a meeting and told everyone in the community that there was a Judas amongst us and it was her best friend. Everyone knew it was me. She told the group what I had confronted her with. I felt so humiliated I was treated like an outcast until everyone was instructed to speak to me after about a week.

Debra used the Bible as a weapon. She would flick through the pages until she found a scripture to fit in to the situation at the time. Most of my scriptures were about false prophets, but I did not flick through the Bible until I found them, I was not very familiar with the Bible at all.

There is one particular story that I will never forget. In 1994 my sister gave me a special gift for Christmas. She painted a portrait of Jesus. I was so surprised because she had only just started art classes. It is a very lovely painting and I cherish it.

I took the painting to show Debra and she asked me if I would let her have it in her room for the night. When I showed her the painting and she asked me to leave it with her I thought she was going to ask God to bless it or something. But when I collected it from her she didn’t even mention the painting. I guess I was a bit disappointed. While my sister was painting the portrait she said she could smell something like incense burning.

For weeks after, I could smell something in my home, similar to incense. I didn’t take much notice because I thought it might have been from candles that we sometimes lit at night. As I said I could smell it in my house for weeks before I realised it was coming from the painting. I don’t know why it smells like that or how but it does and it is only in the bottom part of the frame.

One morning two ladies from the movement came over to help me with some of the work I had to do for Debra. When I opened the front door they instantly commented on the beautiful smell. At that stage they had not seen the painting. When I took the painting down to show them the smell of incense was overwhelming. That’s when I discovered where the smell was coming from.

When Debra found out about this she told me that she could smell it when I left it at her house but she didn’t want to tell me until she asked God. So many people wanted to look at the painting so I took it to a few prayer gatherings.  Whilst at one of these gatherings Debra received a message from God and asked me to write it down. I was conveniently sitting right next to her. The message clearly said that God had anointed the painting for me alone and that I would understand the meaning in time.

After a while Debra asked me to stop bringing the painting to prayer gatherings because it was taking the attention away from the Icon of Mary. I was a bit hurt but I did what she asked. After we moved to Helidon I kept the painting in my private cottage.

I kept the original message Debra received that day at the prayer gathering so I knew exactly what was written. When it was published in one of the Diaries I was shocked to find that the words had been changed. I asked Debra about this and she said that I was just causing trouble and I was looking for glory. I was so annoyed I spoke to Ray Burke about it and actually showed him the original message. He went to Debra and was subsequently told not to question the prophet.

Debra was giving a talk to some overseas visitors and she told them that I had an anointed painting. Of course this caused the attention to be focused on the painting again so the visitors wanted to see it. This surprised me because I was of the impression that I was to keep the painting to myself.

Considering the fact that Debra opened her mouth about the painting she couldn’t stand people knowing that it belonged to me. She demanded that I give it to her because God said that the portrait was really painted for her and not me. I did not believe her. I did not believe that God would take away something that was given to me by my sister. Something that was very personal and special to me. I told her I was happy to hang the painting in the Oratory for everyone to share but she said that she wanted to hang it in her private area and that it was for her alone to see.

She was furious that I did not comply. Days after she told me that she prayed about the painting and God told her the smell was coming from the timber in the frame and that it was no longer anointed.  Whether the painting is anointed or the smell is from the timber frame, to this day it still smells of incense. I am very happy that I did not give it to her! In summary, the point is Debra claimed that she could smell it when she had it in her home, but she said nothing until the two women who came to my house noticed the smell and mentioned it to her. The other point is there was no smell coming from it when I first showed it to her. Why didn’t she tell me straight away if it was supposedly anointed by God? Why also would a prophet tell someone that something is anointed and later on tell them it wasn’t? Would God give that kind of gift and then suddenly take it away without reason? It was getting too much attention I guess.

There are so many more things I could say but this would turn into a book by the time I finished.

I strongly believe that Debra was from a very young age a disturbed person. The catalyst for her was when she lost her job as acting principal of the school she was teaching at. This sent her into deep depression and all this fantasy life she has been living is the only way she could get the power that she craves.

Someone who is genuinely gifted by God does not need to study books on every other seer and visionary around the world. If these people are for real each one has something uniquely special that other seers and visionaries don’t have that sets them apart from each other. Debra does not have anything that stands out. It is easy to see that she has mimicked certain characteristics from each seer and made it appear to be genuine.

I was with her when she met Myrna and Vassula neither of them showed any signs of recognising anything supernatural happening to her. In fact Debra was so annoyed that she was not given special treatment from them that she started speaking out against them. This was way back in the early stages. Is that how a humble prophet of God should behave? Maybe you should ask Vassula and Myrna what they thought of her. Going by what Claire told me Vassula wrote a message about Debra years and years ago, not long after she met her. The message was not favourable.

She fell out with every priest she came in contact with because she could not stand their authority. She could not stick to being a catholic because she needed to be the one in control. Therefore formed a religion of her own. She fought with priests long before she moved to Toowoomba. If she was experiencing the supernatural why didn’t her family ever witness these things? Why would someone claiming to be a prophet of God, want to learn about cults and make comments indicating that she wanted to turn the movement into one?  Too many questions don’t you think, Mike?

Well, I could go on because I am just getting started but I think this is enough for now.

Kind Regards,

Michelle.
