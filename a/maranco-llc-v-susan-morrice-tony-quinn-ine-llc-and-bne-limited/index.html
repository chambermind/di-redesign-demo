For Immediate Release 4 February 2011

Media Advisory

Maranco LLC v Susan Morrice, Tony Quinn, International Natural Energy LLC and Belize Natural Energy Limited

Claim No. NEVHCV 2010/0094

As a result of numerous inquiries from concerned shareholders of International National Energy INE and members of the media, Maranco issues this brief update on the litigation currently underway in Nevis, West Indies.

Maranco is a minority shareholder in INE, a Nevis company.  INE is the sole shareholder of Belize Natural Energy Limited BNE, the only oil producing company in Belize.  BNE has had significant revenues of over half a billion dollars US in the last few years, and is said to be a significant contributor to the annual revenue of the Government of Belize.

INE has never paid a dividend to its shareholders despite BNE’s success.

After being refused information about INE, Maranco as a last resort approached the courts in Nevis for assistance.

On 6 July 2010 Maranco filed a lawsuit in Nevis as an aggrieved minority shareholder, seeking certain relief against Susan Morrice, a director of INE, and Tony Quinn (who holds himself out as a director of INE), and also seeking relief as against INE and BNE.

INE defended the claim in the Nevis Courts.

On 11 January 2011 the Nevis Court struck out INE’s defence because it had failed to comply with an Order made by the Court.  INE immediately sought a stay of the Order striking out its defence and the Court of Appeal for Nevis granted that stay on an interim basis on 13 January 2011.  The Court of Appeal will hear arguments from Maranco and INE’s lawyers on 7 February 2011 as to whether that stay should remain in place, pending an appeal of the Order striking out INE’s defence.

On 31 January 2011 the Nevis Court granted judgment against Tony Quinn.  The Judgment stated, among other things, that Tony Quinn had breached his fiduciary duties to INE.  It is hoped that INE will now be guided by this judgment of the Nevis Court and disassociate itself from Tony Quinn and immediately seek to recover from him any and all damages suffered by its shareholders as a result of the breach of fiduciary duty to INE.

Maranco regrets that it has had to resort to the courts and the legal process to obtain relief.  It seeks only to enforce its legal rights.  Maranco is confident that it will obtain justice with the help of the judicial system.
