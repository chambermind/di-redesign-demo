A very helpful insight into the strange world of Educo and the Tony Quinn group.

Cultism

Investigation into Irish hypnotist Tony Quinn and his company Educo, and his influence and involvement in a Belize oil company.

Embedded media (opens the original source)


BBC One (NI) – Spotlight “Belize Oil”
⛶ Double-click the video for full-screen

The documentary above shows how Tony Quinn managed to get the shareholders in the oil company to hand them over to him on the basis of a promise. Many people found this experience was the straw that broke the camels back in relation to Quinn and his influence.

Tony Quinn and Susan Morrice managed to have the documentary taken down. Charles Colville speaks about the role of censorship in defamation cases which has direct relevance to this documentary. The Director of DI had a similar experience in the Irish courts.

The Hansard transcript names Charles Colville as Viscount Colville of Culross.


Wikipedia has a page about him: Charles Colville, 5th Viscount Colville of Culross

The Internet Movie Database names him as Charles Colville, Producer of TV’s “Elizabeth: Our Queen”, “The Windsors: A Royal Dynasty”, “Britain In Colour” etc.
