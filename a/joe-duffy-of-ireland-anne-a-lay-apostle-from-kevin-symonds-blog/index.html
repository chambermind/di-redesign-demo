Joe Duffy of Ireland & Anne a Lay Apostle from Kevin Symonds Blog

Direction for our Times

Joe Duffy of Ireland & Anne a Lay Apostle

http://d-rium.blogspot.com/2011/09/joe-duffy-of-ireland-anne-lay-apostle.html

Dear Readers,

I continue following the latest developments of Kathryn Ann Clarke/Anne a lay apostle.  I have learned that a Joe Duffy of the popular program “Live Line” in Ireland has recently interviewed Dr. Mark Miravalle and Fr. Darragh Connolly in two separate programs.

Miravalle was interviewed on September 23, 2011 and Fr. Connolly the 26th.  There are audio files available of the programs.  I encourage readers to listen to them.

The Miravalle Interview file is entitled: pod-v-23091110m19sliveline.mp3-pid0-619128.mp3

The Connolly Interview file is entitled: pod-v-26091118m14sliveline.mp3-pid0-1094040.mp3

The following are my comments to the first interview with Dr. Mark Miravalle and a man named “Joe Smith.”

The comments are issued at certain moments in the file.  In other words, if a comment is made at the 2:32 mark, for example, I’ll give the time and then the comment.  To provide some context if the reader cannot find the audio file, I will give some intro if necessary in [brackets and italics].

By way of introduction, Duffy is conducting this interview in the wake of an article in the publication The Irish Catholic.

Sr Briege removes endorsement for ‘Anne’

By

Michael Kelly

High-profile spiritual writer and healer Sr Briege McKenna has withdrawn her support for the alleged Co Cavan-based visionary known as Anne – a lay apostle’.

The housewife and mother of six claims to have been receiving messages from Christ, the Mother of God and various saints for years and is a frequent speaker both at conferences in Ireland and overseas and runs the organisation known as ‘Directions for our times’.

Bishop of Kilmore Diocese Leo O’Reilly, where the alleged visionary resides, has appointed a priest to act as full-time chaplain to Anne.

Now, Sr Briege, who along with Vincentian priest Fr Kevin Scallon are renewed for their international healing ministry, has published a note on her website saying: “it is with regret that I have discerned the need to withdraw my endorsement and support of Direction for our Times”.

Fr Scallon goes considerably further in his comments saying that “recent information has caused me to question the authenticity of ‘Direction for Our Times’.

“Regretfully I’ve come to discern that I can no longer support or encourage involvement in this organisation,” Fr Scallon adds.

Neither he nor Sr Briege were available for comment this week when contacted by The Irish Catholic however, both were previously known to have been firm supporters of both ‘Anne’ and the movement.

This article mentions the recent withdrawing of support for Anne by Sr. Briege McKenna and Fr. Kevin Scallon.

@0:33 [Duffy mentions Sr. Briege’s recent withdrawing of support of DFOT]–Sr. Briege and Fr. Kevin withdrew their support in early August, 2011, not in September.  Unless the radio show was pre-recorded, Duffy made a simple error.

@0:57–OK, so apparently, Duffy had some questions based upon Sr. Briege’s actions and contacted Direction For Our Times (DFOT) who then put Duffy in touch with Miravalle.

@1:22 [Miravalle states, but does not list at this point three “criteria” he says the Church uses to discern alleged private revelation] –In discussing these criteria in the past, Dr. Miravalle has referenced the Vatican’s document “Normae S. Congregationis” dated February, 1978.  He may be doing so here.

@1:58–Miravalle is stating his credentials.

@2:15–There is more in Normae Congregationis than “three categories” that Miravalle speaks about at this point in the interview.

@2:24 [Duffy claims Anne doesn’t give interviews] –Anne spoke with Deal Hudson in June, 2008.

@2:39 [Miravalle is talking about when he first met Anne] –Anne came to the University on October 12th, 2006 to speak, so the visit Miravalle here speaks of must have been earlier.

@2:45–There is some confusion here.  Did Miravalle speak with Kathryn after her visit (when she left)?  What was she doing in Steubenville prior to her October 2006 talk?

@4:14 [In response to Duffy’s raising the point about Sr. McKenna’s withdrawal of support, Miravalle says he can’t comment on their reasons] –Duffy didn’t ask Miravalle ‘why’ they removed their support.  He asked Miravalle if he was aware of their statements.

@4:32 [Miravalle mentions how Bishop O’Reilly’s support for Anne leads him, Miravalle, to believe Anne] –Upon what substance does O’Reilly base himself upon to grant this permission?

@5:07 [Duffy mistakenly says there are 10 Volumes] –In fact, there are only 8 Volumes available, not 10.  Volumes 5 & 8 have not been released because Anne has not been ‘told’ to publish them.

@5:14 [Duffy inquires about paying for the Volumes] –A good and fair question, to which Miravalle responds well.

@5:44 [Duffy inquires about the charity and taxes status of DFOT] –Is Duffy ‘leading’ Miravalle with this question?

@6:24–Though there is a connection between Medjugorje and Anne a lay apostle, Duffy inappropriately raises Medjugorje as a talking point to the detriment of the subject at hand.

@6:46 [Miravalle enumerates upon his “three critera”] –Miravalle is simply giving a cursory take on the Church’s teaching.  That might be fair given time constraints on a live radio talk show.  However, it must be noted that there is more to it than what Miravalle provides here.

@7:09 [Miravalle goes over good fruits of DFOT] –There are also some negative elements that Miravalle and others have not addressed.

Here there was a break in the program.  A Mr. Joe Smith joins the conversation.  Miravalle is in the background.

@7:20 [Duffy asks Smith if Smith thinks himself a follower of Anne] –Didn’t St. Paul address this very point of “following” in 1 Corinthians 3:4?

@7:43 [In response to Duffy’s above question] –Smith answers correctly and deftly.

@8:10 [Duffy brings the Sr. McKenna/Fr. Scallon matter to Smith for his comment] –Duffy’s throwback to the Sr. Briege/Fr. Kevin statements presents a difficulty that only Sr. Briege and Fr. Kevin can resolve.  To ask anyone else to respond who does not know their reasons is an invitation to disaster.

@8:30 [Smith responds similarly to Miravalle] –The reasons are known only to themselves, but that doesn’t mean we should dismiss the matter.

@8:41 [Duffy asks Smith how old Anne is] –This is a question coming out of the middle of nowhere.  Unless he was trying to establish how well Smith actually knew Anne, Duffy’s question is baffling.

@8:48 [Duffy asks Smith if he has heard Anne “preach”] –The verb “preach” is usually reserved to deacons, priests and bishops.  Smith did well to change the verb to “speak” as such is more fitting for a laywoman.

@9:21 [Duffy asks Smith if he knows anything about Kathryn’s early life such as a desire to enter religious life, etc.] –If the contents of Kathryn’s secular book “The Breakable Vow” are to be taken at face value, Kathryn has told us what her early life was like.

@9:37 [Smith provides some basic facts about Anne] –All but the remark on her age when she was married are true statements.

@9:53 [Duffy asks if Anne is Irish] –Kathryn was born in the United States and is, thus, a US citizen, unless she renounced it to live in Ireland.

End of recording.

My overall impression of this interview is that it basically turned into a waving of the pom-poms to Anne and DFOT.  The Fr. Connolly interview was even worse in this regard, though that interview did not go as smoothly.  More on that later.
