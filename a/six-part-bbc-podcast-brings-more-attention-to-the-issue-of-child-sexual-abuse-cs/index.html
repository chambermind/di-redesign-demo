Six-part BBC Podcast Brings More Attention to the Issue of Child Sexual Abuse (CSA) in the 2x2s.

ChristianCooneyitesTwo by TwosUncategorized


Since 2012, Dialogue Ireland has reported extensively on issues within the 2x2s, and in a recent post discussed how the organisational status (or lack of) enables CSA to go unpunished and unchecked in the “Church with No Name.”

(In Ireland, the name “The Cooneyites” is most associated with the group.)

At last the issue seems to be getting more media attention, and July of this year saw the release of the BBC Sounds six-part podcast Service and Saints podcast about the 2x2s, the abuse within the “church”, and its failure to prosecute the perpetrators of child abuse (CSA).

According to the podcast’s description:

“The ‘Church-With-No-Name’ has flown under the radar for more than a century but today is making headlines the world over. Why? Serious allegations of the worst kind and, according to former members, a failure from leadership to deal with the alleged perpetrators. There are hundreds of comments on message boards across multiple websites and social media platforms but no one wants to go on the record. So what are they afraid of? Do the members of this fellowship simply want to live a quiet and devoted life? Or are they hiding for a reason?”

Dialogue Ireland will be reviewing each episode, which you can listen to here for free.
