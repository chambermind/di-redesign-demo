Dossier sent to the Charities Section of the Revenue Commissioners in 2009

Tony Quinn

In 2008 two high level members of Quinn’s Educo programme contacted me. The first checked me out and did not get back to me for over a month, the other was still coming to terms with her decision to leave the organisation. In this case this did not only involve her leaving Educo, but also having lost her involvement with the oil company she had been a founder member of. In these circumstances I provided them with as much information as I could and they in fact gave me a lot of inside knowledge. This service was part of our mission.

In 2001 DI had tried to invite Tony Quinn to take part in a seminar we were holding on his Educo Programme. We got threatened and many of the Patrons of DI were sent letters doing what Quinn usually does, namely trying to get superiors to stop us fulfilling our mission.

http://dialogueireland.ie/dicontent/resources/conferences/2001conference.html

So following these defections I decided to engage in direct dialogue in confrontation with Quinn towards the end of 2008. As a seminar leader I felt he would have the intellectual stamina for these communications. However just before his seminar in early 2009 I received a letter from a solicitor in Dublin from the directors of an oil company saying I was interfering in the company.

Mail from Finn 12.1.09

This was rich coming from the Chairman of the company Susan Morrice who was selling oil shares at an Educo meeting in early January at the RDS. Here she was mixing oil and Tony Quinn’s seminars. All we are interested in is Quinn’s influence as we have not the slightest interest in the oil company.

Just before the event the DI web site www.dialogueireland.ie was taken out by a massive attack and my provider had to let me go as he was losing so many clients.

This then happened to my second provider. This was reported to the Gardai in Harcourt Square but they were not able to find the perpetrators. At the same time this was happening to us, Sheila McCaffrey a director of INE had her computer compromised all her personal mail was hacked into and worse yet this mail was modified and send out as if from her.  Again no one was caught for this crime.

The result was that concerned citizens from the Internet community helped us set up our blog: https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com

This WordPress blog has a lot more power to resist cyber attacks. I subsequently gave a statement to the Gardai at Mountjoy Station on the evidence of the defectors, who also gave statements to the Gardai, concerning, threats on my life by Quinn, the threat of taking out my web site heard in the presence of Quinn, and confirmation he was cooperating with Scientology in his campaigns against us.

DI Director statement to Gardai

The DPP did not take a case following this.

Returning to the dossier sent to Revenue we will now concentrate on this document. This dossier which included some of the private correspondence with Quinn plus the contents of hacked material and doctored sections where notes were added to implicate people in conspiracy. This is a very serious situation which only came to light when researching our files for a Personal Data request.
