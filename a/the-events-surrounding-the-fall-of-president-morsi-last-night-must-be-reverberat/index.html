4 July 2013

The events surrounding the fall of President Morsi last night must be reverberating around the Muslim Brotherhood Mosque in Clonskeagh.

Islamism

 For an understanding of developments in Ireland please go to:

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/category/islamism/

Egypt’s armed forces overthrew elected Islamist President Mohamed Morsi tonight and announced a political transition with the support of a wide range of political, religious and youth leaders.

This spells a major problem for the Muslim Brotherhood dominated Mosque in Dublin.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/11/20/the-cult-like-process-of-becoming-a-member-of-the-muslim-brotherhood/

 They were directly under the influence of the Qatar based cleric Dr Sheikh Yusuf al-Qaradawi an Egyptian based in Doha, Qatar.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2012/06/05/the-european-council-for-fatwa-and-research-ecfr-ceases-to-be-european/

Originally funded by the Maktoums of the UAE they became uneasy with their MB tenants and with the election of MB Morsi. His election would have led funding coming from Egypt. This development led someone in the UAE to embark on the support of Clongriffin to develop an alternative Islamic base in Dublin. This project with no named investor but has received planning permission, but has an An Bord Pleanála appeal launched by Tommy Broughan. This it should be noted has nothing to do with Islamism but around the appropriateness of the scale of the buildings and affect on the local area.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/04/24/appeal-against-clongriffin-mosque-tommy-broughan-td/

 In May a delegation from DI went to see Tommy Broughan to raise educational issues into the nature of the education proposed in the primary and secondary schools envisaged. Also after repeated requests to find out who was behind this project no one has stepped forward. The fact that there are a small group of Muslims in the area can’t explain the massive structure envisaged and the effect on the local community involved. It seems that the DCC has not given any consideration to these important issues.
