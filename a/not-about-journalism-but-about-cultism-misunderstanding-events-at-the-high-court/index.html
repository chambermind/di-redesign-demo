Not about journalism but about Cultism ~ Misunderstanding events at the High Court

Tony Quinn

It is natural that journalists will look at court cases through the lens of their own profession. Dialogue Ireland is seeking for the opportunity to explain our mission to the public. In most of the reports reporting on the court case DI is viewed as like say the Sunday World a media outlet, and that our main concern is to claim privilege as in the case of a journalist.

DI is becoming clearer that there is a general lack of understanding of our mission:

The goals of Dialogue Ireland

  • To work for contact and dialogue among people with different beliefs and philosophies; recognising the vast differences that exist among New Religious Movements, mainstream groups and other organisations active in Ireland.
  • To promote the study and understanding of cultism. To provide pastoral support to members and former members who have experienced cultism and their families according to their needs. To provide advice and information on cultism to those affected and to the media. We are in fact about protecting vulnerable adults ~  http://www.nhssb.n-i.nhs.uk/publications/social_services/Safeguarding_Vulnerable_Adults.pdf
  • To promote the study and understanding of World Religions when it is required as a context for understanding cultist and non-cultist New Religious Movements (NRMs). We hold conferences and seminars on some aspect of Cultism to further this aim.
  • To provide information and educational services to schools, vocational groups and other establishments. We cooperate with governmental and police bodies using intelligence collected from our international partners, and our own research, related to cultism. Rather than warn or raise fears, our strategy is to inform and empower people that are making enquiries.
  • To compile and assess current documentation and news reports on the topics we study and, by way of our web site and Blog, to provide the public access to up-to-date information as well as links to significant sites within the field. Our role is not journalism as such, but rather we retain the archive of articles from a variety of media for the benefit of those that use our site through the information we hold. We have no commercial objective and our aims are totally charitable.
  •  https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/about/

At the hearing on Tuesday Counsel for Mr Garde, Seamus Tuathail, said his client held himself out to be an expert on cultism and could not be forced to give evidence as an expert witness. Obviously whether he was involved as an expert witness or not he would have to be willing to be deposed if he had facts pertinent to the case. However, that is the reason we are opposing this request, not because we are against giving evidence but because we have no evidence to give! The Counsel for Susan Morrice, Jim O’Callaghan, told the court he required an adjournment to facilitate detailed briefing on Colorado law with regard to journalistic privilege. He seemed to imply that both cases were simply a matter of journalistic privilege. Mr Justice Gerard Hogan last month directed that  Mike Garde and Nicola Tallant should give evidence in the case but, since the application had been made in their absence, he allowed them to apply to have his orders set aside. They were considered by defendants in the case in Denver, Colorado, to have relevant evidence. He was clear before giving a new date to hear the case that Susan Morrice’s side had the burden of proof to show what evidence Mike Garde and Nicola Tallant had relevant to the case. It is noteworthy that Mike Garde was originally deposed in early June by Johnsons Solicitors. He wrote offering to assist them but requesting the evidence they had linking him to the case. There was a total silence till they sought the ex parte judgement from Justice Hogan at the end of July. It was strange to be sitting next to Nicola Tallant at the hearing who I have worked with on stories about Scientology, The International Church of Christ and more recently Tony Quinn when I had never met her in the flesh before. This all started in 1997, and here we are together in court where it is suggested we are part of a conspiracy about an oil company, how bizarre. Looking through my email correspondence with her, I might have sent her ten emails other than my TQ updates, but I have one reply from her about an article she wrote about the work of DI.
