Transcendental Meditation And Politics

Transcendental Meditation

By Peter Maarbjerg

When one tries to analyse the political attitudes inherent in TM, one soon finds out, that one has to cope with two aspects: a public, exoteric one and a secret, esoteric one.

The exoteric aspect is found in “The World Plan”, upon which The Age of Enlightenment is based. In “Celebrating Invincibility to Every Nation” TM says on the 21st of October 1977:

“The Establishment of The World Government of the Age of Enlightenment is the realization of the most sublime aspirations of the wise throughout the ages. The sovereignty of the World Government of the Age of Enlightenment is the domain of consciousness, the prime mover of life, the home of all the laws of nature, the field of all possibilities. The World Government is not based on territorial power. It does not usurp any of the functions of existing governments, nor does it replace them in any way. Invincible in the strength of wisdom, love, and joy of duty, the ‘World Government has come into being to raise world consciousness, thereby creating an atmosphere of harmony and positivity in which all governments can fulfil the noble aspirations of their constitutions. It exists to refine and strengthen the national consciousness of every country, which is the prime mover and unseen governor of every government. The World Government raises world consciousness through the activities of the 1500 Capitals of the Age of Enlightenment so far established, each Capital enriching the collective consciousness of one million people by raising and maintaining higher states of consciousness in the individual.

Inspired by the success of the Transcendental Meditation programme in improving all spheres of life, as validated by the personal experiences of thou-sands of individuals in all parts of the world and hundreds of scientific experiments in the fields of physiology, psychology, and sociology. His Holiness Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, founder of the Science of Creative Intelligence, in 1972 inaugurated the World Plan to solve the age-old problems of mankind in this generation. When it was further discovered that a powerful influence of orderliness and harmony in an entire city is produced when only one percent of the city population is growing in orderliness through the daily practice of Transcendental Meditation technique, a phenomenon known as ‘the Maharishi Effect’, Maharishi inaugurated the dawn of the Age of Enlightenment on 12 January 1975. When over 600 ci-ties on all continents reached one percent of their population practising the Transcendental Meditation technique and started to grow in the values of an ideal society – decreased sickness and suffering and in-creased positivity – on 12 January 1976 Maharishi established the World Government of the Age of Enlightenment to usher in and perpetuate the Age of Enlightenment for all mankind.

Through the activities of the ten ministries of the World Government, the field of infinite correlation at the basis of all creation is being enlivened. Society will soon be characterized by harmony and happiness. Through the Science of Creative Intelligence education will be ideal, producing fully developed citizens. Through the Transcendental Meditation programme health will be perfect. The increasing ability of man to act in accordance with the laws of nature will strengthen cultural integrity and promote fulfilling progress in every country.

The achievements of every government will be enhanced. Nations will rise to invincibility. Nature will be ba-lanced – the seasons will come on time, crops will be abundant. There will be peace in the family of nations. The whole human race will soon enjoy the full sunshine of the Age of Enlightenment.

The Ten Ministries of the World Government of the Age
of Enlightenment
1. Ministry for the Development of Consciousness
2. Ministry of Natural Law and Order
3. Ministry of Cultural Integrity, Invincibility, and World Harmony
4. Ministry of Education and Enlightenment
5. Ministry of Celebrations and Fulfilment
6. Ministry of Prosperity and Progress
7. Ministry of Information and Inspiration
8. Ministry of All Possibilities: Research and Development
9. Ministry for the Capitals of the Age of Enlightenment
10. Ministry of Health and Immortality.”

In appraising the effects of the TM programme, TM is certainly not belittling itself. Every positive event in foreign politics throughout the world is considered to be a result of the TM programme. In “World Government News” for February 1978 the following examples are men-tioned as results of “a dramatic wave of friendship and reconciliation among all nations”: Egypt-Israel, West-Germany-East Germany, Turkey-Greece, Thailand-Vietnam: One might ask: what effect has the TM programme had concerning the relations between Rhodesia-Mozambique, Vietnam-Cambodia and many others?

How can the Maharishi Effect be effective in closed countries such as those behind the iron curtain or China? Maharishi has an answer to this: the ‘Extended’ Maharishi Effect! An example is stated in the above-mentioned magazine: Outside Los Angeles lies San Bernadino, which is subject to very little local influence by the Maharishi Effect. “Using formulae for the extended Maharishi Effect TM’s own scientists estimated the Maharishi Effect ratio in San Berdino to be increased approximately ten-fold through the influence of Los Angeles. In accordance with these predictions San Berdino experienced the expected decline in crime, accidents, and hospital admissions. Even the smog driven inland from Los Angeles by westerly winds began to disappear.”

Maharishi expects the same to happen with China! From the same magazine: “At that time (1975) Maharishi analysed world events and concluded that the greatest danger to peace and harmony in the world was from chairman Mao’s philosofi of violent revolution. Maharishi send teams of teachers to Japan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Laos, Thailand, the Philippines, and Nepal. He said the measure would “soften the feeling in China and when national consciousness is more refined it will influence the head of state who will then act accordingly – the principle of violent revolution will be transformed to one of peaceful evolution.”

All this is laid forward to the public through highly illustrated brochures and magazines in glittering colours. It is very difficult to find a way through the candy floss to the truth, as this is hidden in the secret books of Maharishi and TM. Nevertheless one question arises naturally: what about the homeland of Maharishi? Millions of people are suffering from illness and starving. According to Maharishi’s “suffering is always the result of one’s action in the past” (Meditation by His Holiness Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, page 139).

Still a hungry man can be happy: “The hungry man can be a happy hungry man with meditation! If he takes to meditation he’ll be a happy hungry man: if he doesn’t take to meditation then he’ll suffer hunger and misery” (same, page 140).

In:other words: you should never react against injustice. All problems can be solved through meditation. In the Norwegian issue of “The Science of Being and Art of Living”, he says: “It is common knowledge among people what is good, and what is bad. And the laws of the country give you another basis, we should at least obey the laws in the country where we live.” That goes for Chile and South Africa as well!

The real person is made inhuman, turns into a smiling robot, as the essential is to remain unaffected by the relative world. The real person practises an attitude of non-involvement in which he/she pays no attention to suffering and evil.

“This is the art of seeing so that whatever you see brings joy, brings happiness, brings life. See that which is useful, do not see that which is miserable. This is the art of seeing. Art of speech, they say you should speak truth and you should speak the truth that is pleasant. Do not speak unpleasant truth. This is the teaching of the Vedas. It has gone all over the world, it will remain with humanity everywhere in every part of the globe, because this is the truth.” (Meditations of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, page 26).

This is a life on the surface, a superficial life. This is a life, which is blind to the suffering in the world. This meditation implies total subjection to present standards and authorities. Between the mind of a meditating person and the world, whose distress he is taught not to see is built up a wall according to the cosmic law, which is called “unity in the midst of diversity” (Meditations of Maharishi Mahesh Yogi, page 52). This means total dissolution of all ethics, of the difference between right and wrong, of justice and injustice, black and white. No real interest for the world is shown. The only action indicated to change a world in pain is meditation and still more meditation.
