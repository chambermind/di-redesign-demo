2x2s: Awards for Journalists, But No Still ‘No Organisation to Sue.’

CultismTwo by Twos

Prime Time journalist Rita Reilly is up for the Justice Media Award for her 2023 work covering the 2×2s, but still to this day, there’s no clarity from the Law Commission regarding the legal structure of the intinerant religious group.

Today I saw that Rita O’Reilly had entered the Justice Media Awards for her excellent work on the Christian Brothers.

Here is my comment on LinkedIn:

Congrats to you for both documentaries, one in 2023 and one broadcast last year?

One wonders if the Law Society keeps up with the Law Reform Commission? See my Facebook post, which shows the breakthrough Rita made, but as usual in Irish society, the meaning of the long arm of the law takes on a new meaning.

https://www.linkedin.com/school/law-society-of-ireland

It was her documentary that examined their property portfolio and was a follow-up to the one that raised the issue about the loophole in the law, whereby the Christian Brothers did not have to take responsibility for their crimes.

The Christian Brothers and the 2x2s are a case where the law gives permission for abuse to happen. What is new in Irish life?

It was that programme that gave me a light bulb moment in understanding how the 2x2S operated.

https://www.rte.ie/…/1354427-christian-brothers…/…

This is what Rita O’Reilly wrote in 2023.

“The President of the Law Reform Commission and retired chief justice, Mr Justice Frank Clarke, has said it will recommend the law be changed to stop the use of a legal technicality by religious orders to deter and delay victims taking civil cases for compensation in the courts for historical child sexual abuse.

The Christian Brothers is accused of using a 2017 Supreme Court judgment to block child abuse victims successfully suing it in the courts. It is refusing to put forward someone to represent it as a defendant, leaving victims with no organisation to sue.”

O’Reilly then produced a follow-up last year, and I noted that there seemed to be no progress with the law reform commission. I contacted O’Reilly, but she seemed unable to say whether there had been any developments with the Commission.

When I contacted the Commission myself a few months ago, they—at first—seemed unclear as to what I was referring to, but later confirmed they had just started gathering the material to write up what Justice Clarke referred to in February 2023.

As I was seeking to assist the victims, I was concerned about how the 2x2s (a group with no legal identity) obtained Garda clearance, which is only granted to organisations. One wonders what the Law Society makes of this as the sponsoring agency for the award?
