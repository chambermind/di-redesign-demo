The threats continue:

The shareholders have their voice and they will be heard.

The predictability of Susan Morrice, Tony Quinn and team is always the same, can they not learn from the freak show they put on in 2009 where they tried to divert from oil, and Educoise the shareholders and it did not work.  It will not work this time either.  Unlike many parts of the world the venue is a democratic country just like Nevis. Morrice and Quinn just do not respect the fact that the company is not their possession.
