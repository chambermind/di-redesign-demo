ANONYMOUS Ireland 33rd Monthly Protest

Conferences & CommentariesScientology

Dialogue Ireland is happy to make the public aware of this action. This is important as this week the BBC’s Panorama programme is making the public aware of what appeared to be a celebrity movement dedicated to the public is in reality a very dangerous political movement which is totally at odds with a democratic society. In Ireland this involves infiltrating all sectors of society. Dialogue Ireland has found that Anonymous is not only unmasking this activity but also has gained intelligence and is winning the war. They are nearly able at will to find out where they hold recruitment meetings, at which they are increasingly talking to themselves.

About Anonymous…

Anonymous is a grassroots collective comprised of ordinary people from all walks of life, from former Scientologists and long-time cult activists, to college students and corporate professionals, all united in recognition of the malign nature of the ‘Church’ of Scientology, the danger it poses to society, and the necessity of action.
