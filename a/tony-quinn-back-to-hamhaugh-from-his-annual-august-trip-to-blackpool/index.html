Tony Quinn back to Hamhaugh from his annual August trip to Blackpool

Tony Quinn

The more people who talk to me about Tony the more I am building up a picture of his life and his set ways. Do you remember going to Blackpool as a kid?

Unlikely if you are of the post Yoga generation and part of the Educo/Oil generation it will not feature as a place you aspire to visit. You will be visiting rather your Facebook page, and Blackpool will not feature as part of your itinerary. More likely it will be a Ryanair flight to Pisa, Paris or Nice.

However, Quinn is now a pensioner and has a thing about going there every year like clock work. Not sure if he can obtain a state pension as he was 66 on Feb 7 this year. Does he do PRSI? He can save on the security detail when he comes back for his seminar in September and use the Bus outside his door and then transfer to DCU.

However, this bit of trivia is a taster on a piece I mean to write to put the momentous changes the court Judgement in BVI produced in context. More of that later……
