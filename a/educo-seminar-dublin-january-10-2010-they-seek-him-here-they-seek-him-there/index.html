Educo Seminar Dublin January 10, 2010: They seek him here they seek him there

Tony Quinn

Educo Seminar Dublin January 10, 2010:

Obviously Dialogue Ireland was not present for the seminar, and we are really not concerned about what happened. Whether there were 50 persons present or 500 is really of no real interest to us. If anyone was there do feel free to leave a comment and your experience.  Also if Susan Morrice was crying or looked in terrible shape is not our concern. In fact if it was true I genuinely would be concerned for her as another human being. Why, because this is not how we are supposed to be. However, because I understand the source of her present state, I for one wish her well and hope that if the rumours of her marriage being over are correct hope she will find a way to restore it.

But in regard to the Seminar giver it is clear Quinn is trying to show a caring side as you will see in his message below. If you were at the seminar ask yourself the question? When you came away were you thinking to yourself that was really helpful and will really help in this current crisis? Or rather did you think to yourself Quinn was brilliant, Tony is a great speaker?

Below you can see how Quinn has responded to the challenge since this time last year. He has picked up a point which was made before that the Irish generally do not like people succeeding. So now Tony Quinn sees himself as being successful so as a result people are jealous so they want to bring him down. He is living so far from the real world that he forgets that the reason they are onto his case is that they feel he has abused persons and defrauded them. As we have written before he is not a successful entrepreneur, but rather has lived off the success of others and misattributed it to himself.

Also as he has lived away for so long does he not realise as Fintan O’Toole has shown in his book, A Ship of Fools how our society was run by those on the take, and where an aristocracy was created who paid no tax and where proper regulation was absent. It was in this culture that the Educo rip off took place. Just as these major business people can’t survive as their empires collapse, so there are young people who have zombie houses which they can no longer afford. So there a lot of people who paid out for zombie seminars who told their banks they were buying a car or such. The only tide Quinn is swimming against is that one coming from a sewerage pipe in Malahide.

Dialogue Ireland is not about personal attacks on Quinn but it is his organisation which is difficult to separate from him which has a 40 year record of a dubious nature. We want the truth to come out nothing more.

Is Success No Longer Politically Correct?

We are firmly in the grip of a recession and everybody knows this to be true.

Above all we are all looking for the light at the end of this extremely dark tunnel. Naturally, once we saw some light our whole mindset would change.

Then we would gain enthusiasm and courage and soon it would be business as usual. Have you considered how much of any recession is a mindset, that is, a mind set in a certain direction? If anyone were to offer us some light would we be prepared to go in that direction? The real danger is that success has become politically incorrect. Indeed, if you want to become unpopular fast then just admit to being a success. In our position we work with very successful people all of the time. Now they don’t want to admit it out loud. Think of it though, if people are now embarrassed, ashamed and made to feel bad by admitting to success, then in fact we’re not getting a true picture. In turn it’s going to take an awfully long time for things to change for the better with so few role models. Previously people had a mindset of moving toward success, now it’s not only acceptable but politically correct to talk about the difficulty and problems we are having.

Personally, I’ve had a Sunday newspaper devote its front page and two inside pages to criticising what they describe as my lavish lifestyle. This was an issue for them even though I reside in The Bahamas and have done so for 15 years. Well, I grew up in such an Ireland, where if you were a success people thought you must have done something wrong. Then along came what became known as the Celtic Tiger and roared onto the world stage and we became one of the most successful countries. We had a totally different mindset and our future looked so bright. Are we now falling back into the same old mindset that I for one thought we’d outgrown forever?

I Want You to Gain.

Once, the Blueprint Newspaper used to be distributed throughout Ireland three times a year. There hasn’t been one in nine years, so why now? It appears that for some reason it is possible to write articles about me, put them on the front page and this sells newspapers. If that is to be the case then it’s far better for people to read the truth – so here it is. In addition, the intention is that you the reader be uplifted and gain from reading the articles.

Personally I’ve always enjoyed swimming against the tide, and the recession and how it’s presented is a very nasty tide indeed. If you are the type of person who is also prepared to swim against this tide – and at this point that’s what it takes to succeed – then in this paper you’ll find only good news, inspiration, encouragement and hopefully above all, some helpful and useful information. People are so emotional, even passionate when they talk about the recession. In my business (as something of a success coach), when I can get people as emotional and passionate about their goals they usually come about. Read on and discover how with just a small switch in our minds we can begin to turn the tide, starting with our own lives.

Wishing you every success,

Tony Quinn.
