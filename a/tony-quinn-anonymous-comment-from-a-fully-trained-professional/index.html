Tony Quinn: Anonymous Comment from a fully trained Professional.

Tony Quinn

Anonymous Comment from a fully trained Professional.

Some very useful and important points have been made in recent posts about Quinn and his sordid activities.

Quinn was well known to laugh behind the backs of his followers, the “foolies” as he often called them, as they lied and disgraced themselves on his behalf and tried to defend his indefensible behaviour. Morrice is only one of many who have been proud to be Quinn’s defender. They fight for him and degrade themsleves for him but he is as disloyal as they come. You need only to think of the long line of youngsters selected from the group as lovers that the messiah has worked his way through and the all the followers he has exploited and abused. The “power” bestowed by the followers onto the leader in such cults lies mainly in the minds of the followers. Studies constantly show that the “charisma” of the leader comes mostly from the followers. The fear and adoration of the twisted Quinn by the “foolies” has kept him in business for almost.40 years. When they wake up from the cult conditioning and recognise the lies as thousands have over the years they fade into the background to lick their financial and emotional wounds but an increasing percentage are now honest, brave and socially minded enough to tell the truth about Quinn and warn others. Only a few die-hards still cling to him from the old days and with a few financially-minded close conspirators he’s gathered in recent times they are are the main support network that keeps trying to cover up his disgraceful behaviour while he hides out in the Bahamas and turns up in the RDS every January for a highly stage-managed rally. The main body of the “foolies” just come and go and are relieved of their cash and in quite a few cases their mental health. The shameful cover-ups aren’t working now and the orchestrated attacks on ex-Educoists who dare to criticise the twisted messiah are backfiring. The game is indeed over. Collette, all of your efforts to relaunch Quinn as a business guru and success coach have come unstuck. You’ve been trying to whitewash a dungheap for a long time but the stench still comes through. He lied to you and others about the emotional and sexual exploitation in the past and he hasn’t changed his character just got older and more decrepit. The cover-ups have failed and already some rats have prepared their ways to leave the sinking and stinking ship. The truth will always out however long it takes.
