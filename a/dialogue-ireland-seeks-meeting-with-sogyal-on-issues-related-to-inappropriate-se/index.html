Dialogue Ireland seeks meeting with Sogyal on issues related to inappropriate sexual contact…….

RIGPA

Sogyal Rinpoche

Sogyal Rinpoche is the spiritual director of Dzogchen Beara

and author of the widely acclaimed The Tibetan Book of Living

and Dying. With more than 2.8 million copies in print, available in 34

languages and 80 countries, Rinpoche’s teachings resonate with people

of any age, background and faith.

Rinpoche will lead the annual summer retreat at Dzogchen Beara from

29 June to 8 July 2012. For more details on this retreat please visit

http://www.dzogchenbeara.org

For more information on Sogyal Rinpoche and Rigpa (the international

network of Buddhist centres founded by Rinpoche) please visit

www.rigpa.org

For more information on Rigpa’s centres in Ireland please visit www.rigpa.ie


http://www.dzogchenbeara.org/programme/DB_programme_2012_screen.pdf

Dialogue Ireland at the request of one of our most senior Rigpa supporters on our blog has sought  a meeting with  Sogyal Rinpoche to discuss the issues which have concerned  people now for over 2 decades namely that  of inappropriate relations between teachers and students. This supporter  of Sogyal Rinpoche has been both commenting and posting in support of his innocence in regard to the issues we have posted here since 2009. In 2009 we sought to dialogue with the centre in West Cork but have not received a reply. So here publicly we are asking for Rigpa and Sogyal Rinpoche to receive a delegation to discuss the issues in detail.

In the meantime we must bring our archive on this issue to the attention of the public.
