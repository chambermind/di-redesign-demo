TONY RINGS IN THE NEW Y E A R

‘Messiah’ Quinn spotted shopping for sparkler

By NICOLA TALLANT

MUCKY Messiah Tony Quinn has been issued an ultimatum by his busty blonde

lover to ‘put a ring on it’. Quinn, who is home in Dublin for New Year, was

spotted in exclusive Weir’s Jewellers on Grafton Street on Christmas Eve looking at engagement rings with girlfriend Eve.

Insiders at his Educo cult say he is coming under increasing pressure from the South African beauty to make an honest woman of her despite their 39 year age gap.

The 62-year-old, whom followers are convinced is Jesus Christ, has remained a bachelor throughout his lucrative career as a ‘guru’. He has had a string of young lovers over the years but has been in a relationship with Eve for the past four

years since she attended one of his mind bending seminars in Morocco. She lives with him in the Bahamas where he has a luxury property on Paradise Island. She travels with him around the world when he hosts his sessions in the sun for €60,000 a head.

Tony rings in the New Year

Followers

The 23-year-old is held up as a poster girl for his Educogym network and has claimed that its workouts have ‘naturally’ shaped her figure. Although she shares his home in the Bahamas she is not allowed use his personal gold-plated bathroom and has her own guest room at the property. She behaves in slave-like fashion towards Quinn, as do all his followers. She carries his bags, cooks and caters to his every need and even

dresses the way he likes her to, in short and revealing dresses. She regularly polishes the six-foot statue of Jesus that stands on the stairwell to his bedroom. Those who knew her before she met Quinn say she came from a wealthy South African family. She was on the verge of becoming a golf pro when Quinn recruited her. They say she has totally changed her image to emulate his idol Marilyn Monroe. Quinn, Eve and his personal secretary Mary Power flew into Ireland on December 23 to spend Christmas at his Hick’s Tower compound in Malahide. He will host a meeting at the RDS for followers later this month and is expected to stay in Dublin until the end of this month when he will return to the Bahamas to host a seminar in the plush Hotel Riu on Paradise Island. He is hoping that the recession won’t hit followers who will dig deep to come up with the €10,000 to pay him for the seminar and then foot the bill for their flights, accommodation and food.
