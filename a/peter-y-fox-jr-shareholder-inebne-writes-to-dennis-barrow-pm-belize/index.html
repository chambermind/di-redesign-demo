22 February 2011

Peter Y. Fox, Jr Shareholder, INE/BNE writes to Dennis Barrow PM Belize

Tony Quinn

Dear Mr. Barrow,

I write as a former nine year resident and business owner in Belize, a shareholder in INE/BNE and neighbor and client of Dennis Barrow.

As a fellow stakeholder in INE/BNE I am certain you are as concerned as I am at the total lack of fiduciary responsibility and transparency in INE/BNE as recently confirmed by the Nevis Court in its judgment against the interloper Tony Quinn, an illegally appointed director of INE/BNE.

As a representative of the people of Belize, the rightful owners of the oil, and chief steward of the Belizean peoples’ share in INE/BNE, I urgently request that you take a position in opposition to the policies of the current board of directors – led by Tony Quinn and Susan Morrice.

The stakeholders, for the most part hard working middle class Irish families hoping to fund educations and retirements and former Belizeans like me who believed in the original goal to better the lives of Belizeans from oil profits, need your intercession. To date neither shareholders nor Belizeans have seen a single dime from the profits of INE/BNE.

Susan Morrice, in her latest letter to shareholders dated February 17, 2011 refers to the company as “—-a healthy profitable company”.  If the company is healthy and profitable why have the shareholders and the Belizeans not received a fair share of their profits?  Instead Quinn and Morrice offer up a bizarre scheme “allowing” shareholders to participate in what they call a Loan Release Program – in simple terms, the “opportunity” to borrow money from our own company.    However, if this wasn’t insane enough, shareholders cannot participate in the LRP unless Tony Quinn and Susan Morrice anoint them and deem them worthy.  Have the Belizean people been offered the opportunity to participate in the LRP?

The unholy alliance of Quinn and Morrice now boast that the First Lady of Belize will be visiting them in Ireland, presumably at the AGM being held on March 3 this year.  With great respect  Prime Minister Barrow, this proposed trip would seem to imply support by the GOB for the irresponsible activities of Quinn and Morrice and sends the  message that graft, corruption, mismanagement, malfeasance and non-transparency are OK in Belize.

Although the stakeholders brought this windfall to Belize, we need to keep in mind that the oil belongs to the Belizean people.  The oil is not an entitlement that is the private property of Quinn and Morrice.

To gain a perspective on the character of Tony Quinn, I would ask that you read the attached “Open Letter to Tony Quinn” that appeared last year in Dialogue Ireland.

Thanking you in advance for your attention to this matter,

I remain,

Peter Y. Fox, Jr

Shareholder, INE/BNE
