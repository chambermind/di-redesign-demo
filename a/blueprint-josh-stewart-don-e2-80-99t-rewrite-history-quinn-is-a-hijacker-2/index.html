Blueprint: Josh Stewart don’t rewrite history, Quinn is a hijacker (2)

Tony Quinn

Blueprint: Josh Stewart don’t rewrite history, Quinn is a hijacker (2)

From Comment2Post

Submitted on 2009/11/12 at 7:24pm

Dear John,

You have every right to be worried about your friend. I met Josh a few years ago when he started with Quinn. I suppose at the time he was fairly indebted to his cousin Susan and her husband for helping get his dream started as he describes himself in the interview. Once Susan was peddling Quinn’s worth – even to George Bush’s brother maybe Josh had no choice but to get involved. Now he is knee deep. He is supposedly a director in BNE having made a large investment but no such record of an investment exists. He was up on stage at the last AGM chastising people and making all kinds of accusations and leaving himself open to all kinds of things. Hundreds of people are handing over the share certificates to BNE in the hope of getting some money from them and all these certs are being sent to XJet’s address.

At the moment Quinn’s businesses are not doing well so his only claim to fame is all the credit Josh is giving him for advising him on business matters. In front of 100 people or so last January Josh hopped up on stage and gifted Quinn a further percentage of ownership in his business. He said it was a surprise for the delighted Quinn. He wasn’t half as surprised as the people witnessing this great gesture. I hear Susan, his cousin, is now going through divorce proceedings. It’s all very sad and I hope when your friend opens his mind again and realises how he has been manipulated, that you will be there for him. God knows he will need people who can explain to him that it is through no fault of his own that he has been entranced and manipulated by this sociopathic, manipulative, greedy man.
