KNOCKIN’ ON HEAVEN’S DOOR: By JIM GALLAGHER in KNOCK Sunday World

Joe Coleman

Earlier in October we had a massive invasion of Knock by Joe Coleman. Here we see his star is waning. The reality check of the Murphy Report has sent a shock wave through Irish Catholicism which left all these peripheral types of spirituality at the edge. Since the bright sunny day he was last in Knock where people exposed their eyes to damage by the sun we have had a biblical style flood which has made people face real issues. The kind of event represented by Coleman in a rainy cold December had lost his its gloss. Also his appearance on Live Line had shown up his street fighting Mariolatry for what it was hum bug masquerading as devotion. There was a threatening tone and not the humility one associates with a true devotee. Read Jim Gallagher’s piece in the Sunday World after you have read what we wrote a few weeks ago.

KNOCKIN’ ON

HEAVEN’S DOOR:

By JIM GALLAGHER in KNOCK

Our Lady fails to show as crowds stay away

December 6, 2009 23

ANOTHER EXCLUSIVE

THE car parks were nearly empty, the crowds were nowhere to be seen and

the Blessed Virgin, at least for most people, had a day off. The thousands of people

expected at Knock Basilica, Co Mayo yesterday to see the latest ‘apparition’ predicted by self-proclaimed clairvoyant Joe Coleman never appeared.

Doctors

And the good news for the doctors of Ireland was that no more sun-staring pilgrims damaged their eyes because the sun didn’t show either. Shrine manager Pat Lavelle said yesterday’s 800-strong crowd in the Basilica was normal for the weekend

before the Immaculate Conception holy day on December 8. “It falls in the week so people come here the previous weekend. There will be 5,000 people here on Monday night for an all-night vigil.” Mr Lavelle said that people were now beginning to see through clairvoyant Coleman. “People are beginning to see him for what he is. A lot of them are vulnerable people “Our Lady did not appear. He said his message would rock the Church. Now he says it will knock the Church.”

Travellers

Many of those who appeared yesterday were travellers. But they were not all there for Coleman. Hannah Mears (40) from Longford said: “I come here every year because

I believe in Our Lady. “Coleman is totally false. If you stare at the TV long enough you will see it move. Our Lady appeared here long before he came along. “I would like to give him a good kick.”

A month earlier there had been pandemonium when a staggering 10,000 people showed up hoping to see a vision of the Virgin Mary. Some may have permanently damaged their eyes by staring directly into the sun. But yesterday quiet and calm

reigned as the rain poured down on the Mayo town. Joe Coleman himself did appear but refused to speak claiming the media had accused him of lying. As he made his way into the church people reached out to touch him. He prayed quietly as his colleague Brenda Walsh led the crowd in a rosary.

Smiled

Then a few minutes after 3pm the 55-year-old Dubliner looked up to the roof and smiled and moved his lips as if talking to the Virgin Mary. Tears filled his eyes at one point and astonishingly he even blew the supposed apparition a kiss. His sidekick and ‘fellow visionary’ Keith Henderson knelt at his side with a massive grin. The local Archbishop of Tuam Dr Michael Neary had warned people that the Church was sceptical of such apparitions.
