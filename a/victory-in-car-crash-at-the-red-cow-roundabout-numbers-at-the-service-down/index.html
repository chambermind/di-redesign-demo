Victory in car crash at the Red Cow Roundabout. Numbers at the service down.

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant LifeVictory Galway now The Connect Church

There was only about 300-400 people at the Moran’s Red Cow Hotel on Sunday June 30 (including the children). Pastor Hade commented on the absence of such a large number of people. He also stressed the importance of tithing in his sermon which confirms that tithing is an essential part of their theology and methodology.



The Court case is on Tuesday July 2nd and they’re very hopeful that they will be allowed back into the Victory Centre next Sunday. If they are, it will seen as God’s vindication of their position.

On Friday June 14, it was not clear that Pastor Hade understood the nature of what July 2 represented. Instead of coming into the court and contesting the Justice’s interim order, they were  involved in frustrating the High Court. When they finally realised the game was up they still did not get it.


https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2013/06/16/victory-in-court-no-3-report-and-commentary-june-14/

Justice Sean Ryan had granted an ex parte injunction to the receivers to take over the assets.
Hade and Byrne did not seem to have understood that what this means is that the other party is given what they want pending an application of the Victory folks to contest this. They were not represented at the time and they seemed to think it was just a matter of coming in and protesting on July 2 and they can get a miracle reopening for Sunday. Hade was clearly told that the interim injunction would be made final on July 2 unless he got his act together. Gerry Byrne was salivating at the prospect… However, unless they come in and contest what the receivers have done they will be doing a tour of hotels with an ever increasing financial problem and an ever decreasing congregation.
