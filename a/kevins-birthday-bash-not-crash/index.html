Kevin’s birthday bash, not crash!

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

The 1968 MG car – A lovely present to get if you maintain, nurture it and appreciate it.

http://www.collectioncar.com/detailed.php?ad=4422&category_id=1

This is what a 1968 MG car looks like. DI would like to stress that the car in this photo is not the actual car given to Kevin Sanford for his 40th birthday. It is an MG car of the same year.

The car was a gift from the congregation of Abundant life to Kevin to celebrate his 40th birthday. The 1968 model was a significant gift as 1968 was the year of Heather’s birth.

DI is aware for sometime that the Sanford’s like their gifts and people in the congregation past and present have shared their disgust at the lavish gifts expected and given to the pastors on different dates throughout the year. Father’s day is another good date on the calendar for gifts for Kevin having received a ‘ i- pad’ presumably to be able to check the price of coffee beans! You would have to question why a pastor of a Christian church would be content receiving a gift on Father’s day from his subjects or should i say members when the ultimate father he believes in is our Father in heaven.

The car has not been seen for some time can anyone enlighten us to what has happened to it?
