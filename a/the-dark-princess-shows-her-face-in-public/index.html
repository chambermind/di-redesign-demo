The Dark Princess shows her face in public by Des Martin

An unusual title for a review of  a  promotional video? However, for those who have had personal experience of Claire Hoban and her methods the title would be a very mild caption.

There are several very interesting issues concerning this video. Firstly I have tried, on two occasions, to attached comments to this video, as one is invited to do on any YouTube video. On both occasions my comments were not published. As can be seen this video it is published by the Dublin City University Ryan Institute. Is it possible that this institution was unaware of  Hoban’s previous history? That may well be the case.

In a link from the video to Hoban’s CV she states that one of her previous employers was  Roebuck Consulting Ltd. A clever minimisation of the facts! What Hoban fails to mention is that Roebuck Consulting Ltd was/is a new Name for the Roebuck Counselling  Centre Ltd. Roebuck Consulting Ltd. did not exist in 2001 as claimed by Hoban in her CV. A minor mistake you might say.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/01/31/counsellor-who-got-e250000-from-clients-broke-ethics-code/

Here is another minor mistake in her CV. She states that she operated as Trainer and Psychotherapist in Private Practice. Nothing wrong with that! She fails to inform that all of this time she was working in The Roebuck Counselling Centre.

Minor mistakes? Not really! These are deliberate omissions by Hoban to hide the truth.

This is typical of my experience of Claire Hoban. There was always a modicum of truth in what she told you but the essence of her pontifications was untruths, fabrications, and exaggerations.

This is the woman that insisted that I sell my house. In her opinion I was too attached to this house. Was it stupid, mad, insane for me to sell my house? Of course it was and it was absolutely absurd when she manipulated me in to giving her the proceeds of the sale of my house.

Why would I give Claire Hoban 250,000 Euro? Well she was going to show me how to make millions! According to herself she had the knowledge to do this and she was going to impart this knowledge on me for a fee of 10%. So I was going to make 2.5 million within 2 years.

 Was this George Soros or one of his well trained assistants I was involved with? No!

Had Claire Hoban doctorate in Finance or an insiders information of the Stock Exchange. Not that I or any of the other unfortunate people that she has fleeced are aware of. Why would intelligent, articulate people give their hard earned money to Claire Hoban?

Do not forget that we are dealing with someone who was employed in a Counselling Centre. Claire Hoban used the information gleaned from counselling sessions, conducted by her and other counsellors working in the Roebuck Centre. This information was used to maintain clients of the centre in a dependent mind set. Clients were constantly encouraged to attend as many courses, group sessions as could be fitted into their week. Some clients were in the centre 5/6 days a week. As well as attending the centre there may well have been assignments to be prepared.

What was the effect of so much time spent in the centre? One became isolated from ones family, work colleagues, friends. There was very little opportunity for rational thought process to take place. In my own case there was a constant confusion between what I was being told in Roebuck and the truth. I was told by my family, by my accountant that giving these people money for a ponzi scheme was madness. I did not listen to their advice. I could not listen to their pleadings. To do so would be to betray Claire Hoban who had assumed a matriarchal position in my psyche. She knew what was best for me in only the way that a mother knows for her child.

At the same time I was also being told by Claire Hoban that I needed to take more responsibility for my life. How can you take responsibility for your life when you are being maintained in a child like state? How can you take responsibility when you are being constantly being made feel guilty and being shamed by your counsellor?

The majority of people who enter counselling are in crisis. Part of the precipitation of this crisis is the guilt and shame that has been heaped on them through out their lives. To have this shame and guilt worked to maintain the wounds of the counselee is an abomination of all that good respectful counselling can be.

A Counselling centre should be a place of respect and safety. This was never the case in the Roebuck Centre. Ultimately all that was important was your money and there main purpose was how to get their grubby little hands on your silver. This was a policy driven from the directors of Roebuck. It had to be so, as the money ended up in their bank accounts.

This does not absolve the behaviour and actions of Claire Hoban. She was a major player in bringing financial devastation, untold insecurity and mental illness into many people’s lives. Some achievement for someone who purports to have helped people in crisis.

 After reading this is the title of a Dark Princess not appropriate?
