Kevin’s news or lack of……..?

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

We cannot help but notice as many of you have so rightly pointed out, the deafening silence on a ‘newsletter’ front from Kevin Sanford. Now Pastor Kevin has been renowned in the past for covering everything from celebrations, St. Patrick’s Day and even the weather. So we like many of you are somewhat bemused at the lack of reporting on the recent updates. In fact no newsletter for two months is this because the Pastor is just too busy between holidaying in Spain and trips to the US or is it because some of the news he would have to share would be fairly bleak. We thought we share some ideas on what the next newsletter might cover. We welcome any other suggestions that might want to be included.

We have lost our court  case – Massive debts to be paid

Many members leaving – How could they have it so wrong about us

Funding issues – Please send Tithes

Update on house work –  who has been good

Future plans to help educate this nation

Business update on RELIV     from Heather – Sales Targets and performance of team members

Women’s conference – Judy     Jacobs and the story of the lady who is NOT cured

How to start a big church     and turn it into a small one – some pointers by the masters

Celtic cubs – We booked them but they did not come

CCA – Christian Camp of the Arts- Staff profiles – How training with horses helps us control your  children

http://kevinsanfordministries.blogspot.com/search?updated-min=2011-01-01T00%3A00%3A00-08%3A00&updated-max=2012-01-01T00%3A00%3A00-08%3A00&max-results

You can see from the above link that Kevin was very good last year of churning out the newsletters but seems to have gotten off the boil. I suppose No news is good news as opposed to the suggestions we have made above. One thing we can say though, Kevin seems to have taken the foot of the gas (maybe none in the tank) and seems to no longer request money by means of the newsletters?
