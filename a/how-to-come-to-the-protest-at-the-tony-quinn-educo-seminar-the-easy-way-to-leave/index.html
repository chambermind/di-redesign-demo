 Who is protesting?

Any one affected by Quinn, a family member, An ex member or Educoist.

People concerned about people who believe that not only that they above the law but beyond it. We want people who want to be under the law.

All welcome. The purpose is not frighten the members but to remind them that there are people willing to assist them to break free from this group. We also invite those assisted by DI from other groups and those from Scientology, the Moonies who have been like wise affected to come in solidarity with Quinn’s folk. Al for one and one for all.

This event is organised by Cult-Watch under the direction of John McGhee. Dialogue Ireland is happy to cooperate as part of its public affairs activity.

By Car

From City Centre

Follow the airport road which takes you through Drumcondra along the N1, towards the M1. Continue past the junction for Griffith Avenue until you reach the junction at Whitehall. There is a big Church at the right. At this crossroads turn left onto Collins Avenue. Continue along Collins Avenue until you pass St. Aidans School on your left hand side. Just after the school, there is a set of traffic lights, turn left into the entrance to DCU.

From M50 Northbound

Proceed southbound along the M50 and take the Ballymun exit (Junction 4). At the traffic lights on the roundabout, turn left and take the road to Ballymun. Drive through Ballymun and turn left at the Collins Avenue/Ballymun Road crossroads (approx 500 meters). DCU is located on Collins Avenue 500m on the right hand side.

From M50 Southbound

Drive through the Westlink toll bridge and continue along the M50 until the Ballymun exit (Junction 4). At the traffic lights on the roundabout, take the third exit and drive through Ballymun. Follow the directions for DCU as outlined above.
