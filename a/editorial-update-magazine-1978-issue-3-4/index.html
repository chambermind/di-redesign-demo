21 December 1978

Editorial – UpDate magazine, 1978 Issue 3 & 4

Books

In our last issue (Vol. II, issue 2) Ole Riis, the sociologist from Aarhus and Haderslev in Denmark started his analysis of “whatever happened to the Youth Rebellion”. In this issue he analyses some of the main characteristics of the new religious movements in the seventies.

The famous Danish journalist Jakob Andersen, who has been engaged in the study of and the confrontation with Scientology for a number of years has been in the US in order to study the US government charges against Scientology. In his ar­ticle he reports on this decisive battle and also refers to some of his own experiences on the Danish scene.

We have given Scientology an opening to respond to the questions implicit in Jakob Andersen’s article, and they have sent us the reply called “A Major Victory?”

In France, however, there has been a significant event. We are able to bring a translation of the record of a French court case under the heading Scientology loses a Case in France.

Again we have asked Scientology to respond which they do in an article called “The French Inquisition”. In both Scientology-answers the original Scientology-English has been preserved.

In a short notice we report on the latest development in the controversy between Scientology and Paulette Cooper, the author of the now famous and controversial book “The Scandal of Scientology”. The Author files $20-Million Suit against Scientology and we outline the main points of the case.

It may be of general interest to know that the Danish Scientology branch seems to be developing in a more peaceful direction than the rest of the movement. Whatever the reason may be, the fact is that Scientology has withdrawn the major part of its court cases against its critics in Denmark and seemingly wants to change is hitherto very aggressive attitude to other bodies and to individual critics. Time will show what is behind this conversion.

Transcendental Meditation has been the subject of this magazine a number of times (See Vol II, issue 1 April 1978 “TM and Politics” and “TM is a Religious Practice, Court Rules”.) Johannes Aagaard now gives a comprehensive analysis of the development of the movement in the last years and of its present crisis, so well hidden behind its smooth surface.. “It has been the game of our life” as one of the defectors expressed himself to the author, and surely it seems to be a high-life game, but a sort of game which reminds the critical student of Hans Andersen’s well-known story of “The Naked Emperor“.

The International Association for Mission Studies (IAMS) held a congress in Maryknoll, US in August 1978. One of its workshops produced a report on new religious movements, concentrating on TM and tantra. We publish this report for the general public, pointing to the importance of its recommendations, which should be studied carefully by the churches.

The broadly-based Scientology-sponsored bodies aiming to  promote tolerance and religious freedom which are now found in many countries, have also surfaced in Sweden. These people have managed to arouse attention on the general educational scene with a publication called “The Book of Religions“. We review this book as an expression of the general infiltration syncretistic attitude to life.

“Cults are coming, attracting Millions of the Nation’s Youth” is the alarming title of a report from the Bay area, US.

“The Left-hand-path of Tantra” is a letter from a reader of Up-date from Brazil entering into a. discussion of the reality of tantra – especially as related to the Ramakrishna-mission.
