The Palmarian Church – Palma De Troya near Seville, Spain

Palmarian Church

The Palmarian Church or Carmelites of The Holy Face Palmar de Troya

Dialogue Ireland is happy to make the public aware of this church which has a residence and chapel at 38,  Haddon Road, Clontarf. On August 2, 09 a group which has being going for about a year held their first protest outside this Palmarian centre. Following the example of Anonymous who protest outside Scientology’s centres worldwide they want to bring to the attention of the Irish public the extreme danger this church represents.

They wore masks to protect their anonymity and had attempted to deliver a letter to the priest from this sect without success.

They had informed the local Gardai of their intentions but they within 30 minutes the group were given the option of taking off the masks or having to stop. They chose the latter in order to not put themselves at risk.

They intend to contact the Department of Foreign Affairs and the Spanish Embassy to draw attention to the fact that up to 300 Irish citizens could be adversely affected by their involvement with this church. Problems include severe trauma, mental manipulation and the breakdown of family life. Some families are excluded if they do not adhere to rigid dress codes and many are going through the symptoms of Posttraumatic stress disorder.

The Catholic Church is aware of this sect, but has no pastoral role as this is a sect which claims it is the Catholic Church.
