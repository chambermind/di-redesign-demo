WELCOME TO COLAISTE BHRIDE CARNEW

Coláiste Bhríde is a modern second level school, equipped to the highest standard under the aegis of County Wicklow V.E.C..The school catchment area covers South Wicklow, North Wexford and parts of County Carlow. The school is committed to the academic and personal achievement of each of its students, in an atmosphere of mutual respect and goodwill.

We endeavour to help students to discover and develop personal talents and to set themselves high standards of achievement and behaviour. When the time comes for students to leave Coláiste Bhríde we hope that they will look back on their years in school with pride and satisfaction.
