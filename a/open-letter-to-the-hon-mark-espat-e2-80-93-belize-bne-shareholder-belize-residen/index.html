Open letter to the Hon. Mark Espat – Belize – BNE shareholder & Belize resident.

Tony Quinn

Open letter to the Hon. Mark Espat – Belize

Sir:

I have read and listened with interest your recent interview with Jules Vasquez on Chanel 7 News and also your Marlena Cuellar interview. It is reassuring after months of trying to get comment from the Belize media and politicians on what is happening in BNE / INE that finally (after some 20 plus requests) interviews are now happening. However, as a Belizean and a shareholder in this company I think that you should fully acquaint yourself with the facts before making statements. There is much of what you said that we as shareholders agree with but there is much that you have glossed over.

As a Belize resident I am amazed that you claim so much ignorance of the oil company. After all you were one of those of the government officials , ministers who along with the previous Prime Minister Said Musa attended the seminar on Caye Chapel organized by the same Susan Morrice Principal of BNE / INE and the controversial Guru Tony Quinn. Tony Quinn has since illegally been given a considerable amount of shares (valuing $24,000,000 – yes that’s right, 24 million) and also claims to have been placed on the board of INE. This has caused a major rift in our company. And they are still promoting Guru Quinn’s seminars within BNE – again at shareholders’ expense.

From the time of the Caye Chapel event of October 2006, this all expenses week long paid seminar on the Belize Caye (paid for by the investors I should add) up until now, you’ve had ample time to witness the blatant mis-use of investors’ money – none for the benefit of Belizeans or the investors. Nevertheless you and your Government cohorts had the time to work out some cozy deals with Susan Morrice and Guru Quinn – both voters and shareholders deserve to know these details.

And you mislead us yet again when you talk about the Government receiving 10% of the revenues from the oil company. We can only rely on audited accounts from the company which we understand the GOB oversees with their audit teams from the Commonwealth secretariat and which includes audit teams from Trinidad and the GOB.. so please review the financial reports, before making vague statements about how much the GOB earns from our Company.

For clarification, it is clear that when you take Royalties, Production Share, Taxes and the Government working interest of 10% and accumulate, that it is more like 50% plus of the revenue – this was realized through the agreement your Government reworked with Ms Morrice, Said Musa and Morrices’ ex- husband Alex Cranberg in 2006. The GOB gave an undertaking that this money was meant to be put into the Petroleum Fund for the people of Belize and reported in a way that it could be easily understood – showing where the income came from and how it was used to the benefit of the people of Belize. From your comments one can deduce that this is not happening and certainly we as Belizeans understand that in these tough economic times the rumors may be true that these revenues are being used to pay day to day running bills for the country – a little, or should I say a lot of transparency is demanded here, and not just by you Sir, by everyone who put their hard earned money into this company.

But the same omission (in terms of information ) has been experienced by shareholders, we have not – by this I mean none of the shareholders – been advised of the Never Delay field being a commercial discovery. We were told it was marginal and subject to production testing at a sham AGM in September 09 in Dublin. Until a recent article appeared in the Belfast Telegraph last we were not informed about its commercialese either, despite having communication from the company on January 1st, 2010. We as shareholders are just as annoyed as any Government officials and it begs the question: Was this a cover-up between the oil company and the Belize Government? So what we would like to see is an univocal statement from the GOB and BNE stating how commercial this discovery is and the reserve potential of same?

Moving on to the ‘Dividend’ – let’s get things very straight, there was NO $10 Million Dividend paid out to its shareholders. What happened is a highly controversial loan release programme, devised by your Government and Guru Tony Quinn in conjunction with Susan Morrice and her puppet board. This board includes her cousin Josh Stewart, Patty Usher from Belize – and party to this agreement and part of the announcement was Gilbert Canton CEO of BNE who was in Dublin in September for this meeting with shareholders.

This loan release programme was the brain child of Guru Quinn, Morrice and this phoney board to selectively pay out rightful monies due to shareholders. Shareholders who invested in the early stages of this company, in 2002/2003 raised a total of US$5M. Neither industry nor the GOB would back this venture. This $5M was hard earned cash from small investors who wanted to support an effort in this Country. This money was further matched by Joint Venture funds and further shareholder money that brought total investment to over US$11Million. This money was wisely used and carefully put to work under the stewardship of the original team from our company in Belize; this was Paul Marriott, Sheila McCaffrey and Jean Cornec. It did not involve any work from any of those who now purport to run this company and rewrite history.

The loan release programme of $US10 million payout that was so widely applauded in Belize and Ireland mostly went to four people who are now controlling our company with blatant disregard to us as shareholders. These are the so-named Baroness Susan Morrice, the Guru Tony Quinn and Josh Stewart, cousin of the Susan Morrice and Belizean Patty Usher- and let’s not forget the other little bonuses that will never appear on the books. These people received payouts of over half of the $US 10 million. However, not one shareholder received anything, except a loan that is repayable and these loans went to a chosen “few” supporters of this illegal board. In order for these cash strapped investors to get their loan they had to return their original share certificate – to date they have not received any other certificate showing they have shares, only a paper showing that they have an outstanding debt to the oil company (and remember this is their own money). Many of us took legal advice and were advised not to relinquish our original certificates

Not too long ago, one of our INE / BNE investors who is a resident of Belize had a letter drafted by a well known and established attorney in Belize requesting that the illegal operations of the oil company be investigated (we can supply names). As a courtesy, the attorney sent the letter to the then Government officials who advised the attorney not to send the letter to the oil company. Belize, a country we love, is well known to have a corrupt government, and that is why all of this deceit, chaos and robbery can survive – as evidenced by this decision, everyone knows the Government won’t do anything

Well, here’s your big chance Hon. Mark Espat, indeed it is time to be honorable and look after the people who put you in power, to say nothing of the money that is being frittered away by greedy officials. There’s plenty (or was) money to go around, but shareholders do need legal protection if the company is playing dirty – as it is. Their AGM’s are a sham, no Q&A but a big finale by Guru Tony Quinn about loving each other, as he laughs all the way to the bank. He’s the one who claims to have been responsible for finding the oil in Belize! And then let’s hear what the Government did with the millions it has already received.

By the way, if you want the other names of the attendees of Tony Quinn’s seminar (which again let me remind you, the shareholders footed the bill), we can provide them for you. You know of course who they are, as you were there, but we’ll give you an opportunity to show good faith and present all the facts when you look into this ‘cash cow’ known as INE/BNE.

And it’s time too to ask Gilbert Canton CEO of BNE, what was in that secret deal that illegally gave him oil shares (without the permisson of shareholders, or even their knowledge).

Right now the oil company is rushing to put out promotional data trying to put a spin on the facts to protect itself – but it’s too late. Let the AG of Belize make this company work honorably for the benefit of its shareholders and the people of Belize.

The ‘powers that be’ underestimate the power of the voter and the shareholder at their peril.

We really look forward to your next interview with Jules Vasquez and with Marlena Cuella.

BNE shareholder & Belize resident.
