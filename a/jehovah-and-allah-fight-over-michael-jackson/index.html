Jehovah and Allah fight over Michael Jackson

Jehovah's Witnesses

As we grapple with the reality of what happened to so many people in regard to abuse in institutional care we are aware

of the enormity of what the Ryan report has revealed.  http://www.childabusecommission.ie/

Last week we reported on alleged abuse at the highest levels in Scientology.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/06/22/the-truth-rundown-%E2%80%93-st-petersburg-times-21-23-june-2009-06-22/

The reality of what happened to Michael Jackson brought home to us the reality of childhood he experienced.

Last week we published a German report about the of childhood in the JW’s

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2009/06/24/jehovahs-witnesses-und-mental-abuse/

When Michael Jackson visited Cork in 1988 he was still a loyal JW, and joined them on door to door visits.

But gradually he was dabbling in all kinds of beliefs to find meaning. Now his mother has brought his children under the influence

of the very thing he was fleeing from.

“Katherine Jackson, the energetic matriarch, is believed to want all three children to move into her home in Encino, 50 miles from Palmdale, where she can raise them as young Jehovah’s Witnesses.

Born Kattie Screws, she limps after a childhood battle with polio and is famously tough, having routinely confronted and scared off women who approached her husband, Joe, when they were touring with the Jackson 5. The couple had 10 children, of which Michael was the seventh.”

http://entertainment.timesonline.co.uk/tol/arts_and_entertainment/music/article6591265.ece

Grace Rwaramba who cared for King of Pop and his children has shocking secrets of his addictions and bizarre nomadic life

http://entertainment.timesonline.co.uk/tol/arts_and_entertainment/music/article6591123.ece?token=null&offset=0&page=1

“Yes I am really distraught (sobs). Oh my God, the kids. Who has got the kids? He’s allowed this woman from the Nation of Islam . . . he has this woman . . . but she’s so cold and she is cold and doesn’t know how to hold them or how to hug them.”

After his last abuse trial he seemed to be swallowed up by the Nation of Islam and this was mentioned by his brother in the press conference where there was reference to Allah. If he was a JW there would have been a reference to Jehovah.
