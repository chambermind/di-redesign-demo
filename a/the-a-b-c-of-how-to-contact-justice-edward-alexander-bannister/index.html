Opinion on state of company at this stage. Position was clearer before recent meeting (though highly problematic for things to be functional in any normal company sense). The position is probably considerably more muddied by the recent meeting as attempts were made to reinstate the 2007 operating agreement (the one condemned by Justice Bannister). There would need to be evidence about documentation provided before that meeting, transactions re shares and payments, procedures followed and not followed prior to and during meeting, witnesses to these matters and to all events during the meeting. In any event, the likelihood is that Class B shareholders would be claiming that many of the decisions and actions taken by those mostly in actual control of the company are oppressive to them, in the past and currently, and they want this recorded, these activities ended and fair treatment implemented.

Given, Justice Bannister was judge in this case, he is keen to hear from the disadvantaged shareholders firstly and the barrister they might choose to represent them would be someone else. Barristers are usually engaged through a solicitor.
