Liberal elite’s double standard hard to bare ~ Kevin Meyers

Conferences & Commentaries
Liberal elite’s double standard hard to bare 

The Sunday Times / 12.05.13 / p. 11

Pdf of Article:

Kevin Meyers 1

 By Kevin Meyers

How much was the commercial failure of the Broadway production of Colm Tóibin’s The Testament of Mary due to the actress Fiona Shaw appearing completely naked on stage as the mother of Christ?

No reviewer seems to have criticised the nudity, which did not feature in Tóibin’s original script. But then, what reviewer’s credentials as a member of the liberal elite could survive such egregious prudishness? A publicly nude BVM is surely the height of modern, secular chic.

Well, it might be for critics. But while I’m all in favour of female nudity on the stage, I’d hate to be part of an audience watching a naked Mary. Such a display of nudity – especially in this, her traditional month of May – violates some deep taboo, not merely for an ex-Catholic like myself but even, apparently, for those cosmopolitan New Yorkers.

Needless to say, there’d be nothing to shun had the production been about Khadijah, Muharnmad’s wife. Since even a fleeting glimpse of her cheekbone might have merited a beheading or 10, and a Fiona full-frontal would probably have earned the  Islamic equivalent of a Tet Offensive, such a production would not have survived afirst reading by even the keenest of liberals. Rather appropriately, Salman Rushdie was present for Testaments opening night. He knows how Islamic censorship worksfrom the fatwa that caused him to go into hiding for nine years and took about 40 lives across the world, to the murderous absurdity of the Danish cartoons and theritualised butchery of Theo van Gogh. Curiously, such violence repeatedly causes our liberal elites to declare Islam is uniquely a religion of peace. Nobody who knows about Northern Ireland, Spain or the Balkans would say the same about Christianity. Ditto Buddhism and the religio-military junta that ran Burma for 40 years. Hinduism, with its Kshatriya warrior caste, halts such drivel from the outset. The foundation of the state of Israel, plus the unnerving frequency with which the term “sleweth” appears in the Old Testament, settles the issue for Jews.

Yet the only religion that is in conflict with its neighbours almost wherever it exists in sufficient numbers, even in such improbable places as Norway and Denmark, and  which gave the word “jihad” to every main language in the world, is also the only one our liberal elites proclaim to be one of peace. Such nonsense is ‘only possible with industrial quantities of that primary tool of the liberal mind, denial, and its cerebral cousin, cognitive dissonance. The Mary/Khadijah conundrum fits neatly into those conjoined areas of ideological hallucination. To show Mary naked is an expression of artistic liberty, whereas to show a divested Khadijah would be deeply offensive. And which of the following questions is least likely to get you into trouble with the semi-judicial equality quangos that guard the liberal ethos: “I hate Muslim practices”, “I hate homosexual practices”, or “I hate Catholic practices”?

The first is illegal Islamophobia, and the second is criminal bigotry – whereas the third is courageous free speech. Ed West, an English journalist, recently published The Diversity Illusion, a brilliant dissection of the dominant cultural heresy of our times. A tolerant community of mutually enriching fusions – ie, diversity – does not normally follow upon mass immigration. Instead, a generalised fracture into racial and cultural sub-units invariably results. And the English working classes who resented the transformation of their districts caused by mass immigration were swiftly labelled “bigots” by the liberal elite (who of course lived elsewhere), and thus could be safely ignored.

 A striking symbol of the transformations that can result was the renaming of St Mary’s Park, the site of the ancient “white chapel” of the Virgin from which the London borough takes its name, into Altab Ali Park, in honour of a murdered Bangladeshi youth. The name of St Mary – yes, she of Testament – outlasted the Reformation, Oliver Cromwell, Titus Oates, the Penal Laws and Goering’s Luftwaffe, but did not survive a single generation of immigration.

Ireland could have learnt from the British experience but, with a sublimely idiotic moral superiority, chose not to. Thus, north county Dublin is possibly about to undergo an irreversible demographic, cultural, racial and religious transformation, with the creation of a huge, Arab-funded mosque and Islamic centre at Clongriffin.

The history of Islamic migration to Europe has usually followed the same cycle of small communities being established, which soon justify the construction of a physically dominant mosque, often with outside funding, followed by Islamicisation of the immediate neighbourhood. You may argue this is a good thing for what is currently a ghost estate, or a bad thing, or a mixed thing, but what it is not is something to be ignored. Instead, the proposed Clongriffin project was approved almost on the nod a first step, perhaps, to Fingalistan. Alarmist? Maybe. But consider what has happened in England. Birmingham is about to become a majority-Asian city, with Leicester soon to follow, and the first public burning of Rushdie’s Satanic Verses anywhere was before a 7,000-strong mob in Bolton. Significantly, there is no balancing countermovement of Christians into Muslim countries; no flowering ofecclesiastical architecture in Pakistan or Indonesia to cope with the religious needs of immigrants. The only societies that permanently admit huge numbers of outsiders of entirely different racial, religious and cultural backgrounds are of Western European Christian stock, either in the EU or the US, where the power of liberal elites is deeply entrenched. China doesn’t do it. Nor Japan. Nor Russia. Nor India, Iran, nor any African or Arabic countries.

We have travelled a long way, surely, from Fiona Shaw’s nakedness? Indeed; but the fundamental issue is not her nudity, I but the cowardly and inconsistent ethos of the liberal elites, which would allow a theatrical presentation of the Virgin Mary’s pudenda, but never of Khadijah’s. So why is it acceptable, in the name of “freedom,”for artists to offend the traditional sensibilities of generations of Christians, who would have been scandalised by such a theatrical production, while at the same time invoking an entirely different set of values, based on “respect,” to protect the feelings of an incoming religion, such as Islam?

Ed West tellingly paraphrases Napoleon on this issue: “A nation that does not respect its own religion will soon learn to respect someone else’s.” Quite so. Hence the public park in Whitechapel, and the private parts on Broadway.
