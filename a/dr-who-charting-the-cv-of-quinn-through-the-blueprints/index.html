Dr Who? Charting the CV of Quinn through the Blueprints.

Tony Quinn

As you know we got TQ’s 100,000 delivered by some distribution company, though still not in our area. TQ has totally missed the boat  as in recessionary times one gets on average about 5 items of junk mail through the post. Generally those I have talked to put it straight in the green bin. Also it ignores the role of the internet in the world of today. He is caught in the Gutenburg galaxy.

I am gradually scanning through old editions of Blueprint. In early 1993 there is no mention of Dr Quinn. The first clear indication of his academic results is in the autumn of ’93.

Blueprint Autumn93

In this autumn edition he claims he has a doctorate in clinical Hypnotherapy and is curently working on a third degree with the oddly phrased expression, “with a London University?”

Doctored CV

In New Year 1994 he still has a doctorate in clinical Hypnotherapy, but in our first document in New Year 1998 a doctorate in clinical Hypnotherapy is mentioned. In a later post we will bring more evidence to your attention from the blueprints.

Blueprint TQCV

We are currently waiting to hear back from Professor Clifford about the issues raised in the September 09 Blueprint.

Blueprint

Why is Quinn trying to get a doctorate when he has one. Why sixteen years after mentioning a London University is he still trying to obtain a doctorate?

Also what is the role of Dr Andy Moore who lives in Quinn’s place in England and has been involved with Quinn for over 30 years?

How come he has a doctorate and Quinn claims he has authenticated his research. Is he really independent?

We wrote earlier:

Andrew Moore

“I went on the EducoT seminar in July ‘04.  I had been studying psychology for 10 years previous to my seminar and had recently finished my PHD.  I found the information that Tony Quinn gave on the seminar to be vastly superior to anything else I had studied.  The instructions on the programming was excellent and I started to apply it as soon as I came back.  My income increased by about 50% shortly afterwards and I have been very busy at work since.  I keep using the material and I feel going on the seminar is the best investment anyone can make.”

Andrew Moore (Doctor of Psychology),
London, UK

Testimony compromises his academic research? Is this peer reviewed and is it independent?

We are posting comments left on this section to give more background:

Andrew Moore has been with Tony Quinn for years. He used to live in one of the communes in Dublin before moving to Hamhaugh where he, like Vincent Harford, supplies free caretaking for the master. Andrew’s sister and her husband and two children were one of the family’s that Collette Millea turfed out of the house in Howth when instructed to do so by Tony Quinn. She decided to re invent the great guru so as a result families were left homeless and some of them jobless. Millea felt they didn’t fit Tony Quinn’s new profile. Andrew does a lot of the donkey work for Tony’s studies as did many others before him. He also sticks his name and qualifications on letters where needed to boost the man himself! Andrew is a generous and nice guy – what a shame he never got a life for himself away from the mind bending mucky messiah.
