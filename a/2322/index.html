THE RISE AND COLLAPSE OF THE QUINN-EDUCO EMPIRE

Tony Quinn

THE RISE AND COLLAPSE OF THE QUINN-EDUCO EMPIRE

While social psychology experts on cults acknowledge that cult leaders like Quinn are personally often like unsinkable rubber ducks that bounce along despite multiple exposes of their despicable activities they note that their empires have their phases of growth, peak and gradual or rapid decline in credibility, influence and power. The destructive cult leaders escape justice in present day society by masquerading as genuine spiritual movements or new religions, personal development systems or therapy movements when in reality they are nothing of the kind but quite the opposite.The laxity in our political and judicial systems, lack of scrutiny and lack of care for others has allowed Quinn to get away with his ripoffs and abuses for decades in Ireland and like the clerical sexual abuse revelations it’s taken a long time for the full and horrible reality of Quinn’s secretive world to be revealed.

Sometimes, however, as the cult empire collapses the mind-bending cult leader himself also collapses dramatically either by being convicted through the courts or terminating themselves, bringing manipulated and deluded fanatics with him, as with Jim Jones in the 1970s.

The Quinn-Educo empire is now in the decline / collapse phase as it is exposed as a sham that’s preached goodness, spirituality and “success” but has primarily practiced the opposite on a huge scale. The self-proclaimed messiah has lied, manipulated, bullied and exploited to get money, power and sexual domination of others and the lid has now been lifted on a 40 year old cesspool of sickening activity. The primarily illusory benefits created by this cult are far outweighed by the great costs incurred by abused members either financially, mentally or sexually, sometimes all of these. Despite intense efforts of his cronies, poodles and most devoted followers drafted in to protect him and lie on his behalf in classes, seminars or on the internet they have been dramatically unsuccessful to stem or counter the tide of criticisms and revelations. The time of reckoning comes for such cults eventually and the huge amounts of human suffering and misery Quinn has inflicted are being increasingly revealed. The snowball of exposes rolls on gaining momentum and with less fear of the sleazy “messiah” and more confidence and social conscience in the minds of the escapees from the cult these individuals act as “whistle-blowers” that terrify the cult leader and his inner circle. Evidence accrues along with more revelations so there’s nowhere left to hide but for the cult to decline and begin to implode. The hour of reckoning has indeed arrived for this cult.
