Tony Quinn

Susan, you send out a missile like this and you still don’t understand why shareholders are upset with you and the board do you?   What do you think you have achieved with this totally outrageous notice.

If you haven’t got it yet, here are a few clues as to why we shareholders want to change our irresponsible ‘so-called’ board:

1    no dividends – EVER from a company with USD sales fast approaching 3/4 BILLION TO DATE;

2    to get money we have to BORROW OUR OWN MONEY (unless you’re one of the privileged phoney board);

3    you Susan gave away our shares to Guru Quinn without our knowledge or approval;

4    you deem to change our Operating Agreement at your will, forgetting that shareholders have to approve any amendments;

5    you Susan appoint people to OUR board without shareholders’ approval;

6    you call farcical AGM’s without agenda and without a Q&A period;

7    you refuse to clarify questionable expenditures;

8    you evict shareholders from meetings because they want truthful answers;

9    you have high-priced attorneys sending threatening letters to shareholders when you fear the shareholders maybe getting too close to the truth about your corrupt management;

10   in 2011 you circulate 2008/9 financials as ‘consolidated’ – this is how unscrupulous people can hide expenses they would rather not explain….transparency dictates separate reporting for BNE;

11    you won’t give us 2010 financials;

12    … and please note, shareholders don’t need your permission to have a private meeting Susan – what you fear about such meetings is that IT WILL IN FACT REPRESENT THE INTERESTS OF ALL THE SHAREHOLDERS, maybe excepting you, Tony, Patricia, Josh and Gilbert;

13    the mind-controlling techniques, brought in by guru Quinn, no longer work – and what rubbish to say that attending a private meeting could be used against the company.

14    you’re still and attending and sending INE/NBE employees, AT OUR EXPENSE, to guru Quinn’s cult meetings – even though shareholders have not approved you’re using OUR money on your ‘pet’ projects;

15    you’re financed guru Quinn’s enterprises (OUR MONEY REMEMBER)without shareholder knowledge or approval – what has this to do with oil; you’re using OUR MONEY to support your personal interests and passion, and we STRENUOUSLY OBJECT (try as we might, we can’t find the bottom line figure of OUR investment in guru Quinn’s enterprises, nor can we see any profit coming our way);

16    you make false claims against honorable people to deflect attention away from the legal actions taken against you (see the numerous cases filed against you and Tony) and you use OUR MONEY to defend yourselves;

17    then there’s the real biggie – you remember those trusting shareholders who turned in their original share certificates at your request to be eligible for loans with the promise of a new certificate being issued to them – well guess what!  They never heard from you again Susan – so now they don’t have an original share certificate, or any share certificate and they DON’T HAVE THEIR SO CALLED LOAN;

18    finally rest assured, we all support INE/BNE, that’s why were objecting strenuously to all of your and Tony’s continued hypocrisy – it’s the current Board and its lack of transparency and dubious handling of our finances that we don’t support.

…..do you still not get it Susan et al.

P.S.  The next AGM will be a great opportunity for you, Tony, Josh, Patricia and Gilbert to face all of these issues – some things are inevitable. It’s called Karma.
