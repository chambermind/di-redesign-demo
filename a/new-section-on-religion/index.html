New section on “Religion.”

EducationHouse of PrayerJoe ColemanReligion

We are opening a new section on our site to reflect on Religion.

New Religious Movements, NRMs either benign or Cultist

can only be understood within a multi-disciplinary approach.

They arise out of a religious milieu or context.

It is clear that Ireland has a major problem in the area of religion.

Just over the last few months we have had the tree stump religion in

Co. Limerick, the realisation that the House of Prayer was more to

do with preying than praying.

Then we have had Joe Coleman claiming divine authority to force

his way into the Knock shrine. He claims to be more orthodox than

the bishops. He has now added Mary to the Trinity and openly worships her.

He can’t distinguish between devotion and worship.

He throws in a bit New Age Therapy and Clairvoyancy which

he used to charge for. Now he does not charge because Mary has

him on a lead. If he was a genuine Catholic he would submit to his

bishop to get his claims checked out. He should submit to lawful authority,

namely the Archbishop of Dublin. Archbishop Neary is getting all

the flack from Knock and Achill. It really isn’t his baby.

From a Catholic perspective he has proved himself to be a fake

by claiming to be more Catholic than the Pope and by his lack of humility.

If someone knocked on my door, (excuse the pun) and said Our Lady suggested

he should take over my wife as his, I would make two calls,

one to the Mater Hospital and the other to Mountjoy Garda Station.

Many Irish people are totally confused by death which is now called

passing on. We used to call it passing away. Many are confused as to

whether they die and are resurrected, or are reincarnated. Scientologists

drop the body, and view their current body as an overcoat like Gnostics of old.

Also if you observe what happens in frequent reports from Joe Dolan’s grave and other news reports,

Chinese Confucian folk religion seems to be alive and well.

The Christian doctrine of the Communion of Saints is confused with Ancestor worship. People go to graves to talk to loved ones.

The threat of Joe Coleman invading Knock again is upon us.

Due to the religious confusion among the Irish people and the threat this poses to our democracy the next time this invasion is proposed it is important that a senior member of the Gardai visits him and suggests he is inciting religious hatred against the Catholic Church.

He made this position clear on Liveline

http://www.rte.ie/podcasts/2009/pc/pod-v-021109-69m14s-livelinejoecoleman.mp3

He needs to be cautioned for trespass at the shrine, and if he persists needs to be arrested for causing an affray. Also the army must be on stand by to assist the Gardai if such crowds as came before assemble and attempt to take over the Basilica.

I would suggest that the Shrine rather than closing down might invite a Catholic Evangelist like Fr Pat Collins to address any genuine seekers seeking to understand the Catholic Faith at the Shrine. All attempts to high jack the Faith like we see in the case of Joe Coleman must be resisted in the first instance by a battle for the faithful, theologically, spiritually, and  by the civil authorities if this is not possible.
