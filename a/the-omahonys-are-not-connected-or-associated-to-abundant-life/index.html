“The O’Mahony’s are not connected or associated to Abundant Life.”

Victory (Faith Movement or Prosperity Gospel) Archive Abundant Life

Dialogue Ireland is happy to publish the following disclaimer on behalf of the O’Mahony family. We are happy to give them voice.

https://dialogueireland.wordpress.com/2011/05/27/connections-to-abundant-life-in-the-last-ten-years/

Jason came over to Ireland and Married one of Mark O’Mahony’s sisters, Rebecca. They both now have a business linked below. The O’Mahony’s originally led a faith based congregation in Greystones, Co. Wicklow. They moved to Galway and left Abundant Life some years ago now. There son Mark remained in Abundant Life and we now gather he is trying to form a church in Athlone.

“The O’Mahony’s are not connected or associated to Abundant Life.  The website venture was a life-coaching business by Jason which centred on goal-setting & life-planning.  It had absolutely no connection with any Church or any spiritual association and is not operating anymore.

The O’Mahony’s do not agree with any excessive financial gain being derived from ministry or church, and do not condone ministries which manipulate, coerce, or pressurize people to support them financially. They believe that the most important part of Christian life & belief must centre on loving God & loving people (as the great Command suggests in Matthew 22:37-39), and not on profiting financially, and are endeavouring to display a balanced Christian perspective & teaching in every way. They are also open to investigation by Mike Garde* or any other entity which seeks to ensure the safety of Christians within associations & Churches.”

 * Though I appreciate the openness this statement reveals, Dialogue Ireland does not see its role as investigation, the function of investigation is that of the Gardai. We provide a forum for people of different views and have offered Abundant Life the right of uncensored reply.
