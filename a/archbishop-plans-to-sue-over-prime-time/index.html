RTE is to face a second legal action arising out of A Mission to Prey, the Prime Time Investigates documentary that falsely accused Fr Kevin Reynolds of raping a teenager and fathering a child in Kenya, writes Colin Coyle.

It is understood that Archbishop Richard Burke is to sue the station for defamation over a claim he had sex with a minor, Dolores Atwood. Burke admits he had a sexual relationship with Atwood, but says this began when she was an adult.

Burke denies abusing Atwood and says an investigation by the St Patrick’s Missionary Society concluded there was “no corroborating evidence found to support the allegations of child sexual abuse made against [him]”.

In A Mission to Prey, it was alleged Burke began having sex with Atwood when she was 13. She said they first had sex when she was 14. Prime Time did include Burke’s claim that the relationship did not begin until Atwood was over 18.

RTE declined to comment.
