Angie’s comments made into a post to help Educoists break the pattern of control

Tony Quinn

Some of you may have noticed that Angie has been giving her experience for a number of months now. It started with the ‘Ex Educoist Dialogues.’

Some Educoists use our site to gather information about their shares.

They get little on that other than what we are learning about the activities of Sheila McCaffrey in handing over shares to Quinn and the unfortunate effects on Susan Morrice as a result of her conditioning by Quinn.

Some are only concerned about getting their money and have not addressed the terrible effects of being part of this mindset. What Angie assists us with is getting into the dark world of depression that result from not addressing this cultist world. What she is basically saying is until you do you will be stuck at all levels of your being.

People who attend their centres and seminars are encouraged to relax and let thoughts go. In other words they are invited to be in the NOW, ie not thinking about anything and suggestions are made to feel the energy around them. A ‘relationship’ is forged with those who comply and who talk about the great experience they had. The whole experience is very pleasant particularly for those who are vulnerable. They experience a feeling of being accepted, ‘special’ and that all of their worries will disappear. Those who attend the classes are encouraged to come twice a week with the veiled suggestion that it may not work as well for them if they don’t commit themselves to regular attendance.

What people don’t suspect is that it can become highly addictive; the brainwave pattern changes which then leads to experiencing a peaceful trance state; all of their worries have magically disappeared. Even now it astonishes me to think that the full credit for this ‘wonderful’ experience is credited to the person giving the relaxation class. They put themselves on a pedestal and accept all the credit. What their real goal is, is to prepare these people for seminars. They talk about Tony Quinn as if he is ‘god’; the answer to all their prayers; the one who will change their lives for the better; whatever they request ‘will come about’ and if this magical experience does not happen during the seminar or if they do not get their requests, they are told that this failure has nothing to do with Quinn or Cowman. In other words, the person who has been encouraged to take on the belief that ‘If you want something, really want it, it will happen for you’ is made to take on the belief that they must be doing something wrong if it does not happen; that they are not using Quinn’s formula in the right way to ‘bring it about’. This can have a devastating effect. Not only did they not get what they expected, or experienced what they were led to believe Tony Quinn could do effortlessly for them, they now find themselves in terrible debt with no means to pay back the loan unless they sell seminars to other gullible people. Some people have a great time and speak about the changes he has brought about in their lives. What I see are highly suggestible people who have experienced deep relaxation and trance and who are now hypnotised into thinking what a ‘wonderful’ guy Tony Quinn is. Their cognitive faculties are diminished, overtaken by the Quinn propaganda that is pushed ceaselessly on these seminars.

Furthermore, the more involved you become with this group the more they expect from you ie working for very little or nothing at all for long hours. They prefer workers who are highly susceptible to hypnotic suggestion, who act on whatever suggestion is made, no questions asked, and this includes getting money for personal seminars and selling seminars. The workers still have to pay for the classes and seminars, even if the cost is reduced, and this guarantees a constant money flow coming in by those who have committed themselves to the Quinn group. Those who resist change, even though it is no exaggeration to say that they have dedicated their lives to Tony Quinn, are left out in the cold and the ‘cold’ is something Quinn talks a lot about. In fact, the bullying orchestrated by him and Cowman leaves many out in the ‘cold’, a place that cuts them off from themselves and others. Isolation is a very cold place to find yourself in particularly when least expected. Too many have been terrified by the experiences that ensued due to ‘top brass’ subtle intimidation and orchestrated to continue by the ‘enlightened’ ones. The rejected are bullied as gossip infiltrates the whole group.

My life changed completely after undergoing hypnosis, which I had not agreed to, and the results of this opened my eyes to what was really going on and how far they were prepared to go, even if it caused physical injury and mental instability, to get what they wanted. When they did not get the results they wanted I was ‘rejected’. Their consistent efforts to isolate a person from family and friends, particularly if it involves gaining financially for the group, left me feeling very isolated and a strong belief that no-one would believe what I had experienced. I was desperately trying to disconnect from them ie Quinn, Cowman and the Forde’s. I was left with the belief that this would never happen and suffered Post Traumatic Stress for a number of years after it. Many things were said to me over the years of therapy that caused me to fear being attacked both physically and psychically. Of course, I now understand that all of it was embedded in my brain due to hypnosis. They had deliberately used my experiences in life which they knew about as I had attended psychotherapy during my time with this group. My ‘negative’ fears were used against me through hypnosis.

If you make a complaint about some-one’s behaviour, for instance, sexual misconduct or botched treatment received by their workers, or question the outcome of their behaviour and ensued damaged, which I did, or fail to sell seminars, or maintain strong individuality, you quickly come to realise that they don’t want you in the group; in other words you are not ‘chosen’. However, they also have the knack for ‘accepting’ anyone who looks like they have money regardless of whether they fall in with their philosophy and can act as sweet as sweet can be when they know they have the capability to seduce them into parting with it.

Attending the classes is like being on a roller coaster. The experience of being high in energy can be compared to taking a mood enhancing drug. It draws people back to the centre time and time again. When the ‘low’ hits them they are encouraged to return for a ‘top up’ and constant attendance brings about a dependency on the people who give these relaxation classes.

I also have reason to believe that Tony Quinn can manipulate this energy. If a person needs indoctrination he will talk about their tendency to be ‘negative’ and then somehow convinces them that he can change this. I urge you to read the blog that tells the stories of people who experienced his unscrupulous methods and his capacity to change a person’s life to serve him whether it is to seduce them sexually under the guise of ‘enlightenment’ or work for him in an area they had not previously decided on that guarantees him an income. Tony Quinn is an excellent hypnotist and those who are under his, so called, magical spell need to wake up!
