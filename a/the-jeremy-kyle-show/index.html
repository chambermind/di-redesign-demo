The Jeremy Kyle show

ChristianCultismThe Family COG

The Jeremy Kyle show is like the Bovril of daytime TV – either you love its format of bringing people of differing opinions onto the same stage or you hate it for being trash TV. Whatever your sentiments, there can be little doubt that the Jeremy Kyle format certainly allowed a rare look into the issue of cultism.

It is interesting that both audience members who had been victims of cultist abuse saw parallels between their group leader and David McKay.
