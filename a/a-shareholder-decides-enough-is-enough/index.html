A shareholder decides enough is enough!

Tony Quinn

St Patrick Missed a Few Snakes

Hello Susan:

Yes, of course it was necessary for you to cancel the AGM – but that doesn’t mean you can silence the shareholders any longer.

Imagine having to stand on that stage and face those shareholders whom you hold in such contempt.  Worse still, having to answer their Questions like:

–    If, in your own words, we have a ‘healthy and profitable company’, where are our dividends after six years of operation and 5,000 bbls/day?

–    Where are our financials for 2010?

–    What is the value of our shares?

–    Why was cult leader Tony Quinn gifted 64,000 shares without shareholder knowledge and approval?

–    Did cult leader Tony Quinn participate in the Loan Release Program?  Let’s do some math: $20/unit x 64,000 shares equals $1,280,000?

–    We’re hearing from CEO puppet Gilbert Canton how marvellously managed the company is – on what beach has he stuck his head in the sand?   Perhaps in front of your multi-million dollar beach house mansion in Placencia?  How many loan release dollars did he get?  How many did you get?

Word on the street is that if you were to have had an AGM, the cult leader Quinn would not have shown up.  How horrifying would it have been for you to stand on that stage, all alone, while your ‘Lord and Saviour’ and his goon disciples are hanging out in the Bahamas dodging the process servers and tax collectors?

The details are starting to add up – It’s becoming abundantly clear that you no longer serve the purposes of the cult leader Quinn and it’s just a matter of time before you go the way of his other disillusioned former devotees.  That’s just the way he operates.  Vince Lombardi once coined the phrase  “when the going gets tough the tough get going”.   In the cult leader’s case, let’s change that to “when the going gets tough the cult leader cuts and runs”.

And he’s not the only one who has taken this position Susan, in truth you longer serve any purpose, not only for the cult leader but also for our company.  You’re just too expensive and ineffective to keep around.

“ I fear we have awakened a dragon and filled him with a terrible resolve“.  Admiral Yamamoto – just after he bombed Pearl Harbor, December 7, 1941.

Sadly,

Your one time, friend and neighbor,

Peter Fox
