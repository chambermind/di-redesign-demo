Jehovah's Witnesses

THE JACKSON THREE THE SUNDAY TIMES MAGAZINE 13.09.09 (c)

Robert Sandall and John Harlow

The death of Michael Jackson surfaces the issue of child welfare and how his own experience of growing up in a  JW home has influenced his own development and that of his children. It is clear he was not close to his immediate family after the physical violence he experienced from Joe his father. Joe’s failure as a father should not be attributed to the JW’. However, the fact that he would have been told he might not live through Armaggedon would not have given him peace.

The authors’ talked to an educational consultant Tony Buzan who worked with Jackson. Buzan noticed that Jackson’s  children did not mention, “any grandparents, aunts, uncles or cousins- there were no family photographs of  of the extended Jackson clan. Nor did they ever mention religion.”

“Michael would talk to them about many great figures from the past, but I never heard him refer to Jesus.” Of course that would not be unusual as Jesus is the missing link in JW teaching. In fact his role is taken over by the Archangel Michael.

However, as you will read in the article what must be very difficult for them is that they are now firmly under JW influence!
