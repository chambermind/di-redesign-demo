About Tony Quinn leaving out the Voodoo and Merlquinn

Tony Quinn

Tony Quinn has had a lifelong interest in how we can get the most out of ourselves & life to bring about a better world

About Tony Quinn

Born in Dublin, Ireland and now living in The Bahamas, I have had a lifelong interest in how we can get the most out of ourselves and life and bring about a better world. I found the answer in Unconscious Attention®, the key to better results. For me The Thinker® became my way of life. I love applying ‘the more’ of my mind to life. Then it gets even better when I can offer this totally new approach to living to others and watch them and their lives change for the better. My main interest is in pioneering The Thinker®.

Pioneering The Thinker®

Obtaining the answer involved pursuing my own original research with a university and with Human Potential Research Ltd. and obtaining numerous qualifications, both English and American. Among these are a Master of Science Degree (University of East London) majoring in Psychotherapy and a Doctorate in Clinical Hypnotherapy (State Authorized, California, USA).

The background to all of this was my practice of yoga since the age of ten, particularly the mental aspects of meditation and concentration. In 1971 I introduced yoga to Ireland and it was a nationwide success that still continues today.

Designing Models and Structures

One of my biggest interests is in designing models and structures – for both fun and profit. Examples range from business, to running a country, productivity, education, med-icine, psychotherapy, human potential, etc. X Jet and Belize Natural Energy, both of which have won many awards, are run on my business structure.

Businesses

Since 1995 I have had an input at various levels into a great number of businesses. Some of the main ones are Belize Natural Energy Ltd. and International Natural Energy LLC (oil companies), X Jet LLC (an aviation company), as well as Human Potential Research Ltd., educogym® franchise, Tony Quinn Health Centres Ltd., The Irish Association of Holistic Medicine, The Irish Health Culture Association and the Irish Yoga Group. My other interests are writing, copywriting, sales, weight training, body sculpture, nutrition, food supplements and the art of living.

Track Record

Frankly, I would not want to go to a person’s seminar where the seminar itself was their sole means of making a living. I would want to see a track record demonstrating that what they were offering had worked for them and others in real life.
