Write to us — in confidence

A few words are enough. No name needed.

Prototype: this opens your email app addressed to info@dialogueireland.ie. The live site would use a proper form.

What happens next

  • Your message is read by a real person, not a bot.
  • Nothing is shared without your consent.
  • You decide how far to take it: one email or an ongoing conversation.
  • No pressure, no judgement, no cost to ask.
1

You write to us

Briefly, in your own words. Nothing is shared without your consent.

2

We listen and reply

A real person reads your message and answers.

3

We point you on

Information, a conversation, or the right specialist.

People have the right to believe in anything they like, but they also have the right to information which enables them to make an informed choice.
— Mike Garde, Director, Dialogue Ireland
